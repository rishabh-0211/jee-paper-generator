### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Two thin lenses have a combined power of +9 D. When they are separated by a distance of 20 cm , their equivalent power becomes $+\dfrac{27}{5} \mathrm{D}$. The power of both the lenses in dioptre are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4,5
- **(B)** 3,6
- **(C)** 2,7
- **(D)** 1,8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 3,6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the powers of the two lenses be $P_1$ and $P_2$.

When the lenses are in contact, their combined power is given by:
$P_1 + P_2 = 9$

When they are separated by a distance $d = 20 \text{ cm} = 0.2 \text{ m}$, the equivalent power is given by:
$P_{eq} = P_1 + P_2 - d P_1 P_2$

Substituting the given values:
$\dfrac{27}{5} = 9 - 0.2 P_1 P_2$

$\dfrac{1}{5} P_1 P_2 = 9 - \dfrac{27}{5}$

$\dfrac{1}{5} P_1 P_2 = \dfrac{18}{5}$

$P_1 P_2 = 18$

We have $P_1 + P_2 = 9$ and $P_1 P_2 = 18$. These are the sum and product of the roots of the quadratic equation:
$x^2 - 9x + 18 = 0$

$(x - 3)(x - 6) = 0$

Thus, the powers of the lenses are $3 \text{ D}$ and $6 \text{ D}$.

Answer: 3,6

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2020 (13 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A biconvex lens $\left(\mathrm{R}_{1}=\mathrm{R}_{2}=20 \mathrm{~cm}\right)$ has focal length equal to focal length of
concave mirror. The radius of curvature of concave mirror is
[R.I. of glass lens $=1 \cdot 5$ ]

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-40 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(B)** $-20 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(C)** $40 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(D)** $20 \mathrm{~cm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $-40 \mathrm{~cm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{1}{f}=(n-1)\left(\frac{1}{R_{1}}-\frac{1}{R_{2}}\right) \\
n=1.5, R_{1}=20 \mathrm{~cm}, R_{2}=-20 \mathrm{~cm} \\
\therefore f=20 \mathrm{~cm}
\end{array}
$$
for a concave mirror, $R=2 f$ and it is negative.
$$
\therefore R=-40 \mathrm{~cm}
$$

</details>
