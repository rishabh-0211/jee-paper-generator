### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel beam of light of intensity $I_0$ is incident on a glass plate, $25\%$ of light is reflected by upper surface ($I_1$) and $50\%$ of light is reflected from lower surface ($I_2$). The ratio $I_2$ to $I_1$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1:4$
- **(B)** $3:2$
- **(C)** $2:3$
- **(D)** $4:1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3:2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Intensity of incident light = $I_0$

Intensity of light reflected by the upper surface is $25\%$ of $I_0$:

$I_1 = \dfrac{25}{100} I_0 = \dfrac{I_0}{4}$

Intensity of light transmitted into the glass plate:

$I_t = I_0 - I_1 = I_0 - \dfrac{I_0}{4} = \dfrac{3I_0}{4}$

Intensity of light reflected from the lower surface is $50\%$ of the transmitted light:

$I_2 = \dfrac{50}{100} I_t = \dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{3I_0}{4} = \dfrac{3I_0}{8}$

The ratio of $I_2$ to $I_1$ is:

$\dfrac{I_2}{I_1} = \dfrac{\dfrac{3I_0}{8}}{\dfrac{I_0}{4}} = \dfrac{3}{8} \times 4 = \dfrac{3}{2}$

Answer: $3:2$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A plane mirror produces a magnification of

**Options:**

- **(A)** -1
- **(B)** +1
- **(C)** zero
- **(D)** +2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) +1**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a plane mirror, the image formed is virtual, erect, and of the same size as the object.

Magnification is given by $m = \dfrac{h_i}{h_o}$.

Since the height of the image $h_i$ is equal to the height of the object $h_o$ and both are erect, $h_i = h_o$.

Therefore, $m = +1$.

Answer: +1

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2023 (09 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If a ray of light in denser medium strikes a rarer medium at angle of incidence $i$, the angles of reflection and refraction are $r$ and $r^{\prime}$ respectively. If the reflected and refracted rays are at right angles to each other, the critical angle for the given pair of media is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sin ^{-1}\left(\tan r^{\prime}\right)$
- **(B)** $\tan ^{-1}(\sin \mathrm{i})$
- **(C)** $\sin ^{-1}(\tan r)$
- **(D)** $\cot ^{-1}(\tan \mathrm{i})$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\tan ^{-1}(\sin \mathrm{i})$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/mht_cet/_a3WSwRHk01mI-XN-iqJyaUkH8siPcr9X9IymVmzF88.original.fullsize.png">

According to Snell's Law,
$\begin{array}{ll} 
& \frac{\sin i}{\sin r}=\frac{1}{n} \\
& \text { But } i=r, \\
\therefore \quad & \frac{\sin r}{\sin r^{\prime}}=\frac{1}{n} \\
& \text { From figure } r^{\prime}+r+90=180^{\circ} \\
\therefore \quad & r^{\prime}=180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-r=90^{\circ}-r \\
\therefore \quad & \Rightarrow \frac{\sin r}{\sin \left(90^{\circ}-r\right)}=\frac{1}{n} \\
& \frac{\sin r}{\cos r}=\frac{1}{n}
\end{array}$
$\therefore \quad \tan \mathrm{r}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}$
Critical angle is given by $\sin i_c=\frac{1}{n}$
$\begin{array}{ll}
\therefore & \tan r=\sin i_c \\
\therefore & i_c=\sin ^{-1}(\tan r)
\end{array}$
