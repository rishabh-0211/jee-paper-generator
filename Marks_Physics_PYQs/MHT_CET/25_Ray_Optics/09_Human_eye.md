### Q1. MHT CET 2021 (24 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

'Circle of least confusion' refers to which one of the following defects occurring in images formed by mirrors or lenses?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Distortion
- **(B)** Coma
- **(C)** Astigmatism
- **(D)** Spherical aberration

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Spherical aberration**

**Detailed Solution:**

'Circle of least confusion' refers to Spherical aberration occurring in images formed by mirrors or lenses.

</details>
