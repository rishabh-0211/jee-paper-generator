### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A ray of light takes time '$t$' to travel a distance '$d$' in air and it travels a distance '$4d$', in time '$T$' in a certain medium. If $T = 8t$, the critical angle for the pair of media is
$\left[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) = 0.5, \sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) = 1\right]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{\pi^c}{2}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{\pi^c}{3}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{\pi^c}{4}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{\pi^c}{6}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\dfrac{\pi^c}{6}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Speed of light in air is $c = \dfrac{d}{t}$

Speed of light in the medium is $v = \dfrac{4d}{T}$

Given $T = 8t$, we get $v = \dfrac{4d}{8t} = \dfrac{d}{2t} = \dfrac{c}{2}$

The refractive index of the medium is $\mu = \dfrac{c}{v} = 2$

The critical angle $\theta_c$ is given by $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{1}{\mu} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

Therefore, $\theta_c = \dfrac{\pi^c}{6}$

Answer: $\dfrac{\pi^c}{6}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{t_1}{t_0}\right)$
- **(B)** $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4t_0}{t_1}\right)$
- **(C)** $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4t_1}{t_0}\right)$
- **(D)** $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{t_0}{4t_1}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4t_0}{t_1}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Speed of light in air is given by $v_1 = \dfrac{x}{t_0}$

Speed of light in the denser medium is given by $v_2 = \dfrac{4x}{t_1}$

The refractive index of the denser medium with respect to air is $\mu = \dfrac{v_1}{v_2}$

The critical angle $\theta_c$ for the medium is given by $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{1}{\mu} = \dfrac{v_2}{v_1}$

Substituting the values of $v_1$ and $v_2$:

$\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{\dfrac{4x}{t_1}}{\dfrac{x}{t_0}} = \dfrac{4t_0}{t_1}$

$\theta_c = \sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4t_0}{t_1}\right)$

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For a ray of light, the critical angle is minimum, when it travels from

**Options:**

- **(A)** glass to air
- **(B)** air to glass
- **(C)** glass to water
- **(D)** water to glass

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) glass to air**

**Detailed Solution:**

The critical angle $\theta_c$ is defined when light travels from an optically denser medium to an optically rarer medium. Thus, the options "air to glass" and "water to glass" are incorrect as light travels from rarer to denser medium in these cases.

The formula for the critical angle is given by $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{\mu_R}{\mu_D}$, where $\mu_R$ is the refractive index of the rarer medium and $\mu_D$ is the refractive index of the denser medium.

For the critical angle to be minimum, the ratio $\dfrac{\mu_R}{\mu_D}$ must be minimum.

For glass to air: $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{\mu_a}{\mu_g} = \dfrac{1}{1.5} = 0.67$

For glass to water: $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{\mu_w}{\mu_g} = \dfrac{1.33}{1.5} = 0.89$

Since $0.67 < 0.89$, the critical angle is minimum when light travels from glass to air.

Answer: glass to air

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

White light is incident on the interface of glass and air as shown in figure. If green light is just totally internally reflected, then reflected rays inside the glass contain
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/FI6JzEAvXxivsA20G6IS-tqKLiyGCXEX-CtQAL7ye7w.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** yellow, orange, red
- **(B)** violet, indigo, blue
- **(C)** green, yellow, orange
- **(D)** all colours except green

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) violet, indigo, blue**

**Detailed Solution:**

The critical angle $C$ for a medium is given by the relation $\sin C = \dfrac{1}{\mu}$, where $\mu$ is the refractive index of the medium.

According to Cauchy's equation, the refractive index $\mu$ depends on the wavelength $\lambda$ of light as $\mu = A + \dfrac{B}{\lambda^2}$. Thus, $\mu$ decreases as $\lambda$ increases.

The order of wavelengths for the visible spectrum is:
$\lambda_V < \lambda_I < \lambda_B < \lambda_G < \lambda_Y < \lambda_O < \lambda_R$

Consequently, the refractive indices follow the reverse order:
$\mu_V > \mu_I > \mu_B > \mu_G > \mu_Y > \mu_O > \mu_R$

Since $\sin C = \dfrac{1}{\mu}$, the critical angles for the respective colors will be in the order:
$C_V < C_I < C_B < C_G < C_Y < C_O < C_R$

The problem states that green light is just totally internally reflected, which means the angle of incidence $i$ is exactly equal to the critical angle for green light ($i = C_G$).

For total internal reflection to occur for a specific color, the angle of incidence $i$ must be greater than its critical angle ($i > C$).

Since $i = C_G$, we have:
$i > C_V$
$i > C_I$
$i > C_B$

Therefore, violet, indigo, and blue light will undergo total internal reflection and will be present in the reflected rays inside the glass. The colors yellow, orange, and red have critical angles greater than $i$, so they will be refracted into the air.

Answer: violet, indigo, blue

</details>
