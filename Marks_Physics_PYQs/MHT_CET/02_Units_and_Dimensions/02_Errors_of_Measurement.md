### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Mass and volume of a body are found to be $(6.00 \pm 0.03)$ kg and $(2.00 \pm 0.02)$ m$^3$ respectively. Then the density of the body is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 \pm 0.010$ kg/m$^3$
- **(B)** $3 \pm 0.015$ kg/m$^3$
- **(C)** $3 \pm 0.020$ kg/m$^3$
- **(D)** $3 \pm 0.045$ kg/m$^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $3 \pm 0.045$ kg/m$^3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $M = 6.00 \pm 0.03$ kg and $V = 2.00 \pm 0.02$ m$^3$.

Density $\rho = \dfrac{M}{V} = \dfrac{6.00}{2.00} = 3$ kg/m$^3$.

The maximum fractional error in density is given by

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} = \dfrac{\Delta M}{M} + \dfrac{\Delta V}{V}$

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{3} = \dfrac{0.03}{6.00} + \dfrac{0.02}{2.00}$

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{3} = 0.005 + 0.010 = 0.015$

$\Delta \rho = 3 \times 0.015 = 0.045$ kg/m$^3$.

Thus, the density of the body is $3 \pm 0.045$ kg/m$^3$.

Answer: $3 \pm 0.045$ kg/m$^3$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a certain system, the quantities A and B are measured as $8 \pm 0.08$ and $6 \pm 0.06$ respectively. Let $x = AB$, $y = A + B$ and $z = A - B$. The correct order of % error in $x$, $y$ and $z$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x = y > z$
- **(B)** $x < z < y$
- **(C)** $z > x > y$
- **(D)** $x > z > y$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $z > x > y$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $A = 8 \pm 0.08$ and $B = 6 \pm 0.06$.

Percentage error in $A = \dfrac{0.08}{8} \times 100 = 1\%$.

Percentage error in $B = \dfrac{0.06}{6} \times 100 = 1\%$.

For $x = AB$, the percentage error is the sum of the percentage errors of $A$ and $B$:

$\% \text{ error in } x = 1\% + 1\% = 2\%$.

For $y = A + B$, the absolute error is $\Delta y = \Delta A + \Delta B = 0.08 + 0.06 = 0.14$.

The value of $y = 8 + 6 = 14$.

$\% \text{ error in } y = \dfrac{0.14}{14} \times 100 = 1\%$.

For $z = A - B$, the absolute error is $\Delta z = \Delta A + \Delta B = 0.08 + 0.06 = 0.14$.

The value of $z = 8 - 6 = 2$.

$\% \text{ error in } z = \dfrac{0.14}{2} \times 100 = 7\%$.

Comparing the percentage errors, we get $7\% > 2\% > 1\%$, which means $z > x > y$.

Answer: $z > x > y$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A physical quantity $C$ is related to four other quantities $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ as $C = \dfrac{PQ^2}{R^3\sqrt{S}}$. The percentage error in the measurement of $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ are $1\%$, $2\%$, $3\%$ and $2\%$ respectively. The percentage error in the measurement of $C$ will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18\%$
- **(B)** $6\%$
- **(C)** $9\%$
- **(D)** $15\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $15\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $C = \dfrac{PQ^2}{R^3\sqrt{S}}$

The maximum percentage error in $C$ is given by

$\dfrac{\Delta C}{C} \times 100 = \dfrac{\Delta P}{P} \times 100 + 2 \dfrac{\Delta Q}{Q} \times 100 + 3 \dfrac{\Delta R}{R} \times 100 + \dfrac{1}{2} \dfrac{\Delta S}{S} \times 100$

Substituting the given percentage errors:

$\dfrac{\Delta C}{C} \times 100 = 1\% + 2(2\%) + 3(3\%) + \dfrac{1}{2}(2\%)$

$\dfrac{\Delta C}{C} \times 100 = 1\% + 4\% + 9\% + 1\% = 15\%$

Answer: $15\%$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the error in the measurement of volume of the sphere is $6\%$ then the error in the measurement of its surface area will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2\%$
- **(B)** $3\%$
- **(C)** $4\%$
- **(D)** $7.5\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The volume of a sphere is given by $V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

The percentage error in volume is $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V} \times 100 = 3 \left( \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \times 100 \right)$

Given that the error in volume is $6\%$, we have:

$3 \left( \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \times 100 \right) = 6\%$

$\dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \times 100 = 2\%$

The surface area of a sphere is given by $A = 4\pi r^2$

The percentage error in surface area is $\dfrac{\Delta A}{A} \times 100 = 2 \left( \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \times 100 \right)$

Substituting the percentage error in radius, we get:

$\dfrac{\Delta A}{A} \times 100 = 2 \times 2\% = 4\%$

Answer: $4\%$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A particular physical quantity is given by $X=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}$ and the measured value of 'a' is $40$ unit with $0.01$ unit accuracy. The value of 'b' is $\dfrac{98}{10}$ unit with $1$ unit accuracy. The % error in $X$ is nearly

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4\%$
- **(B)** $5\%$
- **(C)** $6\%$
- **(D)** $7\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given the physical quantity:

$X = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}$

From the given data:
Measured value of $a = 40$, accuracy $\Delta a = 0.01$
Measured value of $b = \dfrac{98}{10} = 9.8$, accuracy $\Delta b = 1$

The maximum permissible percentage error in $X$ is given by:

$\dfrac{\Delta X}{X} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( \dfrac{\Delta a}{a} + \dfrac{\Delta b}{b} \right) \times 100$

Substituting the given values:

$\dfrac{\Delta X}{X} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( \dfrac{0.01}{40} + \dfrac{1}{9.8} \right) \times 100$

$\dfrac{\Delta X}{X} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{2} \left( 0.00025 + 0.10204 \right) \times 100$

$\dfrac{\Delta X}{X} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{2} (0.10229) \times 100$

$\dfrac{\Delta X}{X} \times 100 \approx 5.11\%$

The percentage error in $X$ is nearly $5\%$.

Answer: $5\%$

</details>
