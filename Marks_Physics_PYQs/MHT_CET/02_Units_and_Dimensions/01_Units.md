### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

According to Boyle's law, the product PV remains constant. The SI unit of PV is same as that of

**Options:**

- **(A)** momentum
- **(B)** energy
- **(C)** force
- **(D)** impulse

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) energy**

**Detailed Solution:**

Pressure $P = \dfrac{\text{Force}}{\text{Area}}$

SI unit of $P = \text{N m}^{-2}$

SI unit of $V = \text{m}^3$

SI unit of $PV = \text{N m}^{-2} \times \text{m}^3 = \text{N m} = \text{Joule}$

Joule is the SI unit of energy.

Answer: energy

</details>
