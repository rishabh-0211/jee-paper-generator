### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In the circuit shown in the figure, the current through
<img style="width: 400px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/may/watermarked_images/19_may_shift_2__q24_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** the $3\Omega$ resistance is $0.50$ A.
- **(B)** the $3\Omega$ resistance is $0.25$ A.
- **(C)** the $4\Omega$ resistance is $0.50$ A.
- **(D)** the $4\Omega$ resistance is $0.25$ A.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) the $4\Omega$ resistance is $0.25$ A.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Simplifying the circuit from right to left.

In the rightmost loop, the $2\Omega$, $4\Omega$, and $2\Omega$ resistors are in series. Their equivalent resistance is:
$R_1 = 2 + 4 + 2 = 8\Omega$

This $8\Omega$ resistance is in parallel with the $8\Omega$ resistor in the middle branch. Their equivalent resistance is:
$R_2 = \dfrac{8 \times 8}{8 + 8} = 4\Omega$

This $4\Omega$ equivalent resistance is in series with the $2\Omega$ and $2\Omega$ resistors in the middle loop. Their equivalent resistance is:
$R_3 = 2 + 4 + 2 = 8\Omega$

This $8\Omega$ resistance is in parallel with the $8\Omega$ resistor in the left branch. Their equivalent resistance is:
$R_4 = \dfrac{8 \times 8}{8 + 8} = 4\Omega$

This $4\Omega$ equivalent resistance is in series with the $3\Omega$ and $2\Omega$ resistors in the main branch. The total equivalent resistance of the circuit is:
$R_{eq} = 3 + 4 + 2 = 9\Omega$

The total current drawn from the $9\text{ V}$ battery is:
$I = \dfrac{V}{R_{eq}} = \dfrac{9}{9} = 1\text{ A}$

This $1\text{ A}$ current flows through the $3\Omega$ resistor. Thus, the current through the $3\Omega$ resistance is $1\text{ A}$.

At the first junction, this $1\text{ A}$ current splits into two parallel branches. Since the resistance of the $8\Omega$ resistor and the equivalent resistance of the entire circuit to its right ($R_3 = 8\Omega$) are equal, the current divides equally. The current entering the middle loop is:
$I_1 = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 1\text{ A} = 0.5\text{ A}$

At the second junction, this $0.5\text{ A}$ current again splits into two parallel branches. Since the resistance of the second $8\Omega$ resistor and the equivalent resistance of the rightmost loop ($R_1 = 8\Omega$) are equal, the current divides equally again. The current entering the rightmost loop is:
$I_2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 0.5\text{ A} = 0.25\text{ A}$

This $0.25\text{ A}$ current flows through the $4\Omega$ resistor. Therefore, the current through the $4\Omega$ resistance is $0.25\text{ A}$.

Answer: the $4\Omega$ resistance is $0.25$ A.

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In the following circuit, the current in the $4\Omega$ resistance is $1.2 \text{ A}$. The total current ($I$) flowing in the circuit is
<img style="width: 400px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/may/watermarked_images/19_may_shift_1__q35_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.6 \text{ A}$
- **(B)** $2.4 \text{ A}$
- **(C)** $2.8 \text{ A}$
- **(D)** $3.2 \text{ A}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2.8 \text{ A}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In a parallel circuit, the potential difference across each branch is the same.

Let the current through the $3\Omega$ resistor be $I_2$.

Therefore, $I_1 \times 4\Omega = I_2 \times 3\Omega$

Substituting the given value of $I_1 = 1.2 \text{ A}$:

$1.2 \times 4 = I_2 \times 3$

$4.8 = 3 I_2$

$I_2 = 1.6 \text{ A}$

The total current $I$ in the circuit is the sum of the currents in the parallel branches:

$I = I_1 + I_2$

$I = 1.2 \text{ A} + 1.6 \text{ A} = 2.8 \text{ A}$

Answer: $2.8 \text{ A}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A resistor of $6\ \Omega$ with a tolerance of $10\%$ and another of $4\ \Omega$ with a tolerance of $10\%$ are connected in series. The tolerance of combination is about

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5\%$
- **(B)** $10\%$
- **(C)** $12\%$
- **(D)** $15\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $10\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $R_1 = 6\ \Omega$ with $10\%$ tolerance and $R_2 = 4\ \Omega$ with $10\%$ tolerance.

Absolute error in $R_1$ is $\Delta R_1 = \dfrac{10}{100} \times 6 = 0.6\ \Omega$

Absolute error in $R_2$ is $\Delta R_2 = \dfrac{10}{100} \times 4 = 0.4\ \Omega$

For series combination, the equivalent resistance is $R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 = 6 + 4 = 10\ \Omega$

The absolute error in the series combination is $\Delta R_{eq} = \Delta R_1 + \Delta R_2 = 0.6 + 0.4 = 1.0\ \Omega$

The tolerance of the combination is the percentage error, given by $\dfrac{\Delta R_{eq}}{R_{eq}} \times 100\%$

Tolerance $= \dfrac{1.0}{10} \times 100\% = 10\%$

Answer: $10\%$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A current of 9A enters point P of an equilateral triangle PQR having three wires of $3\Omega$ each and leaves by point R. The currents $I_1$ and $I_2$ are respectively
<img style="width: 280px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/april/watermarked_images/16_april_shift_2__q35_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2A , 7A$
- **(B)** $3A , 6A$
- **(C)** $5A , 4A$
- **(D)** $6A , 3A$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3A , 6A$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The current of $9\text{ A}$ entering at point P splits into two parallel paths to reach point R.

Path 1 is along P $\rightarrow$ Q $\rightarrow$ R. The equivalent resistance of this path is $R_1 = 3\Omega + 3\Omega = 6\Omega$. The current through this path is $I_1$.

Path 2 is directly from P $\rightarrow$ R. The resistance of this path is $R_2 = 3\Omega$. The current through this path is $I_2$.

Since the two paths are in parallel, the potential difference across them is equal:

$V_{PR} = I_1 R_1 = I_2 R_2$

$I_1(6) = I_2(3)$

$I_2 = 2I_1$

According to Kirchhoff's current law at junction P, the total current is the sum of the currents in the two branches:

$I_1 + I_2 = 9$

Substituting $I_2 = 2I_1$ into the above equation:

$I_1 + 2I_1 = 9$

$3I_1 = 9 \Rightarrow I_1 = 3\text{ A}$

Now, substituting the value of $I_1$ to find $I_2$:

$I_2 = 2(3) = 6\text{ A}$

Therefore, the currents $I_1$ and $I_2$ are $3\text{ A}$ and $6\text{ A}$ respectively.

Answer: $3A , 6A$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The current (I) drawn from the battery in the given circuit is
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/mhtcet2025/6rvIbssA6h9_PcF8HzcmCu1cBPETYbNMopng793B5ps.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.2 \mathrm{~A}$
- **(B)** $0.5 \mathrm{~A}$
- **(C)** $0.6 \mathrm{~A}$
- **(D)** $0.8 \mathrm{~A}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.5 \mathrm{~A}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the nodes of the circuit be labeled as A, B, C, and D, where A is connected to the positive terminal and D to the negative terminal of the battery.

From the circuit diagram, the resistances between the nodes are:
$R_{AB} = 5\ \Omega$
$R_{AC} = 10\ \Omega$
$R_{BD} = 10\ \Omega$
$R_{CD} = 20\ \Omega$
$R_{BC} = 10\ \Omega$

This forms a Wheatstone bridge. Checking the balance condition:
$\dfrac{R_{AB}}{R_{BD}} = \dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}$
$\dfrac{R_{AC}}{R_{CD}} = \dfrac{10}{20} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

Since $\dfrac{R_{AB}}{R_{BD}} = \dfrac{R_{AC}}{R_{CD}}$, the bridge is balanced. Thus, no current flows through the central resistor $R_{BC} = 10\ \Omega$, and it can be removed from the circuit.

The equivalent resistance $R_{eq}$ is the parallel combination of the series branches A-B-D and A-C-D:
$R_{ABD} = 5 + 10 = 15\ \Omega$
$R_{ACD} = 10 + 20 = 30\ \Omega$

$\dfrac{1}{R_{eq}} = \dfrac{1}{15} + \dfrac{1}{30} = \dfrac{3}{30} = \dfrac{1}{10}\ \Omega^{-1}$
$\Rightarrow R_{eq} = 10\ \Omega$

The current $I$ drawn from the battery is:
$I = \dfrac{V}{R_{eq}} = \dfrac{5}{10} = 0.5\text{ A}$

Answer: $0.5 \mathrm{~A}$

</details>
