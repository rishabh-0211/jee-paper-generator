### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The voltage across a lamp is $(6.0 \pm 0.3)$ volt and the current passing through it is $(4.0 \pm 0.1)$ ampere. The power consumed in watt will be (using percentage error)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(24.0 \pm 1.8)$
- **(B)** $(24.0 \pm 0.75)$
- **(C)** $(22.0 \pm 0.4)$
- **(D)** $(18.0 \pm 0.4)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $(24.0 \pm 1.8)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power is given by $P = VI$

Substituting the given values:

$P = 6.0 \times 4.0 = 24.0$ W

The relative error in power is given by:

$\dfrac{\Delta P}{P} = \dfrac{\Delta V}{V} + \dfrac{\Delta I}{I}$

$\dfrac{\Delta P}{24.0} = \dfrac{0.3}{6.0} + \dfrac{0.1}{4.0}$

$\dfrac{\Delta P}{24.0} = 0.05 + 0.025 = 0.075$

$\Delta P = 24.0 \times 0.075 = 1.8$ W

The power consumed is $(24.0 \pm 1.8)$ W.

Answer: $(24.0 \pm 1.8)$

</details>
