### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two wires $A$ and $B$ of equal lengths are connected in left and right gap of a metre bridge, null point is obtained at $40$ cm from left end. Diameters of the wires $A$ and $B$ are in the ratio $3:1$. The ratio of specific resistance of $A$ to that of $B$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4:1$
- **(B)** $3:1$
- **(C)** $6:1$
- **(D)** $8:1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $6:1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a metre bridge, the ratio of resistances in the left and right gaps is given by

$\dfrac{R_A}{R_B} = \dfrac{l}{100 - l}$

Given that the null point is at $40$ cm from the left end, $l = 40$ cm.

$\dfrac{R_A}{R_B} = \dfrac{40}{100 - 40} = \dfrac{40}{60} = \dfrac{2}{3}$

The resistance of a wire is given by $R = \rho \dfrac{L}{A} = \rho \dfrac{4L}{\pi d^2}$, where $\rho$ is the specific resistance, $L$ is the length, and $d$ is the diameter.

Since the lengths of wires $A$ and $B$ are equal, the ratio of their resistances is

$\dfrac{R_A}{R_B} = \dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B} \left(\dfrac{d_B}{d_A}\right)^2$

Given the ratio of diameters $d_A : d_B = 3 : 1$, substituting the values gives

$\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B} \left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2$

$\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B} \times \dfrac{1}{9}$

$\dfrac{\rho_A}{\rho_B} = \dfrac{2}{3} \times 9 = 6$

The ratio of specific resistance of $A$ to that of $B$ is $6:1$.

Answer: $6:1$

</details>

**Options:**

- **(D)** Parallel to $9\ \Omega$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Detailed Solution:**

Initially, $R_1 = x$, $R_2 = 9\ \Omega$, and $l = 40$ cm.

Substituting the values:

$\dfrac{x}{9} = \dfrac{40}{100 - 40}$

$\dfrac{x}{9} = \dfrac{40}{60} = \dfrac{2}{3}$

$x = 6\ \Omega$

To obtain the balance point at $50$ cm, the ratio of resistances in the left and right gaps must be $1:1$.

$\dfrac{R_1'}{R_2'} = \dfrac{50}{100 - 50} = 1$

The new balancing length is given as $1.6 l_1$.

$\dfrac{5}{\dfrac{R}{2}} = \dfrac{1.6 l_1}{100 - 1.6 l_1}$

$\dfrac{5}{R} = \dfrac{25}{100 - 25}$

**Detailed Solution:**

$\dfrac{X}{Y} = \dfrac{2}{3}$

$\dfrac{X + 30}{Y + 30} = \dfrac{5}{6}$

$X = 20\,\Omega$

$Y = \dfrac{3}{2}(20) = 30\,\Omega$
