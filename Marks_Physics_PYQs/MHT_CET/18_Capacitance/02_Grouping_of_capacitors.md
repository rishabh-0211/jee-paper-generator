### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two parallel plate air capacitors of same capacity '$C$' are connected in series to a battery of emf '$E$'. Then one of the capacitors is completely filled with dielectric material of constant '$K$'. The change in the effective capacity of the series combination is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{C}{2}\left[\dfrac{K-1}{K+1}\right]^2$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{C}{2}\left[\dfrac{K+1}{K-1}\right]^2$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{2}{C}\left[\dfrac{K-1}{K+1}\right]$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{C}{2}\left[\dfrac{K-1}{K+1}\right]$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\dfrac{C}{2}\left[\dfrac{K-1}{K+1}\right]$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Initial effective capacity of two identical capacitors of capacity $C$ connected in series is given by:

$C_{i} = \dfrac{C \times C}{C + C} = \dfrac{C}{2}$

When one of the capacitors is completely filled with a dielectric material of constant $K$, its new capacity becomes $KC$. The other capacitor remains unchanged with capacity $C$.

Final effective capacity of the series combination is:

$C_{f} = \dfrac{KC \times C}{KC + C} = \dfrac{KC}{K+1}$

The change in the effective capacity of the series combination is:

$\Delta C = C_{f} - C_{i}$

$\Delta C = \dfrac{KC}{K+1} - \dfrac{C}{2}$

$\Delta C = C \left[ \dfrac{K}{K+1} - \dfrac{1}{2} \right]$

$\Delta C = C \left[ \dfrac{2K - (K+1)}{2(K+1)} \right]$

$\Delta C = \dfrac{C}{2} \left[ \dfrac{K-1}{K+1} \right]$

Answer: $\dfrac{C}{2}\left[\dfrac{K-1}{K+1}\right]$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A capacitor of $20\ \mu$F is charged up to $500$ V is connected in parallel with another capacitor of $10\ \mu$F which is charged up to $200$ V. Then, the common potential will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $400$ V
- **(B)** $200$ V
- **(C)** $100$ V
- **(D)** $50$ V

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $400$ V**

**Detailed Solution:**

The common potential $V$ when two capacitors are connected in parallel is given by the ratio of total charge to total capacitance.

$V = \dfrac{C_1 V_1 + C_2 V_2}{C_1 + C_2}$

Given $C_1 = 20 \mu$F, $V_1 = 500$ V, $C_2 = 10 \mu$F, and $V_2 = 200$ V.

Substituting the values:

$V = \dfrac{20 \times 500 + 10 \times 200}{20 + 10}$

$V = \dfrac{10000 + 2000}{30}$

$V = \dfrac{12000}{30} = 400$ V

Answer: $400$ V

</details>

The final energy of the configuration is

- **(C)** $8.0\,CV^2$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

Final energy of the configuration is given by $U = \dfrac{Q^2}{2C_{eq}}$

Substituting the values, $U = \dfrac{(8CV)^2}{2(4C)} = \dfrac{64C^2V^2}{8C} = 8CV^2$

### Q7. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

Three identical capacitors connected in series have net capacitance '$x$'. Then they are connected in parallel. The ratio of energy stored in series configuration to that in parallel configuration is (if both configurations are connected to the same source)

**Options:**

- **(B)** $3:1$
- **(C)** $1:9$
- **(D)** $9:1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $1:9$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the capacitance of each identical capacitor be $C$.

In series configuration, the equivalent capacitance is $C_s = \dfrac{C}{3}$.

In parallel configuration, the equivalent capacitance is $C_p = 3C$.

The energy stored in a capacitor configuration connected to a voltage source $V$ is given by $U = \dfrac{1}{2} C_{eq} V^2$.

For the series configuration, the energy stored is $U_s = \dfrac{1}{2} C_s V^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \left(\dfrac{C}{3}\right) V^2$.

For the parallel configuration, the energy stored is $U_p = \dfrac{1}{2} C_p V^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} (3C) V^2$.

The ratio of energy stored in series to that in parallel is:

$\dfrac{U_s}{U_p} = \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2} \left(\dfrac{C}{3}\right) V^2}{\dfrac{1}{2} (3C) V^2} = \dfrac{\dfrac{C}{3}}{3C} = \dfrac{1}{9}$

Thus, the required ratio is $1:9$.

Answer: $1:9$

$U_s = \dfrac{1}{2} C_s V_s^2 = \dfrac{1}{2} \left(\dfrac{2C}{3}\right) V_s^2$

### Q63. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Three condenser of capacitance $C(\mu \mathrm{F})$ are connected in parallel to which a condenser of capacitance $C$ is connected in series. Effective capacitance is $3.75$, then capacity of each condenser is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 \mu \mathrm{F}$
- **(B)** $5 \mu \mathrm{F}$
- **(C)** $6 \mu \mathrm{F}$
- **(D)** $8 \mu \mathrm{F}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5 \mu \mathrm{F}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The effective capacitance of three condenser connected in parallel $=3 C$ When $3 C$ is connected in series to $C$
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\text {Result }} &=\frac{3 C \times C}{3 C+C}=3.75 \\
\Rightarrow \quad C &=5 \mu \mathrm{F}
\end{aligned}
$$
