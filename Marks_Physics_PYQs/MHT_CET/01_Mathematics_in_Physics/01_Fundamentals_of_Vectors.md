### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If a unit vector is represented by $\vec{U} = 0.9\,\hat{i} - 0.2\,\hat{j} + m\hat{k}$, then the value of m is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.85$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{0.15}$
- **(C)** $1$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{0.77}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sqrt{0.15}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\vec{U} = 0.9\hat{i} - 0.2\hat{j} + m\hat{k}$ is a unit vector.

The magnitude of a unit vector is $1$.

$|\vec{U}| = \sqrt{(0.9)^2 + (-0.2)^2 + m^2} = 1$

Squaring both sides:

$0.81 + 0.04 + m^2 = 1$

$0.85 + m^2 = 1$

$m^2 = 1 - 0.85$

$m^2 = 0.15$

$m = \sqrt{0.15}$

Answer: $\sqrt{0.15}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The angle made by vector $\vec{A} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}$ with x-axis and that with y-axis are respectively.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\tan^{-1} 2\sqrt{13}$ , $\tan^{-1} 3\sqrt{13}$
- **(B)** $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$ , $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}$
- **(C)** $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ , $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(D)** $\sin^{-1}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$ , $\sin^{-1}\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{3}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$ , $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given vector $\vec{A} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}$

The magnitude of the vector is $|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$

Let $\alpha$ be the angle made by the vector with the x-axis. Then,

$\cos \alpha = \dfrac{A_x}{|\vec{A}|} = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} \Rightarrow \alpha = \cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}\right)$

Let $\beta$ be the angle made by the vector with the y-axis. Then,

$\cos \beta = \dfrac{A_y}{|\vec{A}|} = \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}} \Rightarrow \beta = \cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}\right)$

Answer: $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$ , $\cos^{-1}\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

There are two vectors $\vec{A} = 6\hat{i} + 9\hat{j} - \hat{k}$ and $\vec{B} = 2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} - p\hat{k}$ which have the same direction. The value of 'p' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{1}{3}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{2}{3}$
- **(D)** $-\dfrac{1}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\dfrac{1}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since the vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ have the same direction, they are parallel. Their corresponding components must be proportional.

$\dfrac{A_x}{B_x} = \dfrac{A_y}{B_y} = \dfrac{A_z}{B_z}$

Substituting the given values:

$\dfrac{6}{2} = \dfrac{9}{3} = \dfrac{-1}{-p}$

$3 = 3 = \dfrac{1}{p}$

$p = \dfrac{1}{3}$

Answer: $\dfrac{1}{3}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A unit vector in the direction of resultant vector of $\vec{A}=-2 \hat{i}+3 \hat{j}+\hat{k}$ and $\vec{B}=\hat{i}+2 \hat{j}-4 \hat{k}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{-3 \hat{\mathrm{i}}+\hat{\mathrm{j}}+5 \hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{35}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\hat{i}+2 \hat{j}-4 \hat{k}}{\sqrt{35}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{-2 \hat{\mathrm{i}}+3 \hat{\mathrm{j}}+\hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{35}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{-\hat{\mathrm{i}}+5 \hat{\mathrm{j}}-3 \hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{35}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{-\hat{\mathrm{i}}+5 \hat{\mathrm{j}}-3 \hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{35}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The resultant vector $\vec{R}$ is given by the sum of vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$.

$\vec{R} = \vec{A} + \vec{B}$

$\vec{R} = (-2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + \hat{k}) + (\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 4\hat{k})$

$\vec{R} = -\hat{i} + 5\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}$

The magnitude of the resultant vector is:

$|\vec{R}| = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + (5)^2 + (-3)^2}$

$|\vec{R}| = \sqrt{1 + 25 + 9} = \sqrt{35}$

The unit vector in the direction of the resultant vector is:

$\hat{R} = \dfrac{\vec{R}}{|\vec{R}|} = \dfrac{-\hat{i} + 5\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}}{\sqrt{35}}$

Answer: $\frac{-\hat{\mathrm{i}}+5 \hat{\mathrm{j}}-3 \hat{\mathrm{k}}}{\sqrt{35}}$

</details>
