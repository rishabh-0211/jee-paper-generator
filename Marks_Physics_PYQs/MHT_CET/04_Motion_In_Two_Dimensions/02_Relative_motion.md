### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Airplanes A and B are flying with constant velocity in the same vertical plane at angles $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal as shown in the figure. The speed of A is $100\sqrt{3}$ m/s. At time $t = 0$ s, an observer in A finds B at a distance of $500$ m. This observer sees B moving with a constant velocity perpendicular to the line of motion of A. If at $t = t_0$, A just escapes being hit by B, $t_0$ in second is
$\{\sin(30)^\circ= 0.5, \cos(30)^\circ=3^{0.5}/2\}$
<img style="width: 300px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/may/watermarked_images/12_may_shift_1__q3_d1_v2.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the direction of motion of airplane A be along the x-axis. The velocity of A is $\vec{v}_A = 100\sqrt{3} \hat{i}$ m/s.

Since airplane A flies at $30^\circ$ and airplane B flies at $60^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal, the angle between their directions of motion is $60^\circ - 30^\circ = 30^\circ$.

The velocity of B can be written as:
$\vec{v}_B = v_B \cos(30^\circ) \hat{i} + v_B \sin(30^\circ) \hat{j}$

The relative velocity of B with respect to A is:
$\vec{v}_{BA} = \vec{v}_B - \vec{v}_A = (v_B \cos(30^\circ) - 100\sqrt{3}) \hat{i} + v_B \sin(30^\circ) \hat{j}$

It is given that the observer in A sees B moving perpendicular to the line of motion of A. This means the component of $\vec{v}_{BA}$ along the x-axis is zero:
$v_B \cos(30^\circ) - 100\sqrt{3} = 0$
$v_B \left(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = 100\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow v_B = 200$ m/s

The relative velocity of B with respect to A is then:
$\vec{v}_{BA} = 200 \sin(30^\circ) \hat{j} = 200 \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \hat{j} = 100 \hat{j}$ m/s

The magnitude of the relative velocity is $|\vec{v}_{BA}| = 100$ m/s.

For A to just escape being hit by B, the initial relative distance of $500$ m must be covered by B in the frame of A. The time taken for this is:
$t_0 = \dfrac{500}{100} = 5$ s

Answer: $5$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two boys are standing at points A and B on ground where distance AB = a. The boy at point B starts running perpendicular to line AB with velocity '$V_1$'. The boy at point A starts running simultaneously with velocity 'V' and catches the other boy in time 't'. The value of 't' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V^2 - V_1^2)}\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- **(B)** $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V_1^2 - V^2)}\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- **(C)** $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V^2 - V_1^2)}\right]$
- **(D)** $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V_1^2 - V^2)}\right]$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V^2 - V_1^2)}\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the meeting point be C.

In the right-angled triangle ABC, the initial distance AB is $a$.

Since the boy at B runs perpendicular to AB, the angle at B is $90^{\circ}$.

The distance travelled by the boy from B is $BC = V_1 t$.

The distance travelled by the boy from A is $AC = V t$.

Using Pythagoras theorem in $\triangle ABC$:

$(AC)^2 = (AB)^2 + (BC)^2$

$(V t)^2 = a^2 + (V_1 t)^2$

$V^2 t^2 - V_1^2 t^2 = a^2$

$t^2 (V^2 - V_1^2) = a^2$

$t = \left[\dfrac{a^2}{V^2 - V_1^2}\right]^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$

Answer: $\left[\dfrac{a^2}{(V^2 - V_1^2)}\right]^{\frac{1}{2}}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two girls are standing at the ends 'A' and 'B' of a ground where $A B=b$. The girl at ' $B$ ' starts running in a direction perpendicular to ' AB ' with velocity ' $\mathrm{V}_1$ '. The girl at 'A' starts running simultaneously with velocity ' $\mathrm{V}_2$ ' and in shortest distance meets the other girl in time ' t '. The value of ' $t$ ' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{b}{\sqrt{V_1{ }^2+V_2{ }^2}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{b}{V_1+V_2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{b}{V_2-V_1}$
- **(D)** $\frac{b}{\sqrt{V_2{ }^2-V_1{ }^2}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{b}{\sqrt{V_2{ }^2-V_1{ }^2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the initial positions of the two girls be A and B, with $AB = b$.

The girl at B runs in a direction perpendicular to AB with velocity $V_1$. Let the meeting point be C. The distance covered by her in time $t$ is $BC = V_1 t$.

The girl at A runs with velocity $V_2$ and meets the other girl at C in time $t$. The distance covered by her is $AC = V_2 t$.

Since the path of the girl at B is perpendicular to AB, $\triangle ABC$ is a right-angled triangle with the right angle at B.

Using Pythagoras theorem in $\triangle ABC$:

$AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2$

$(V_2 t)^2 = b^2 + (V_1 t)^2$

$V_2^2 t^2 - V_1^2 t^2 = b^2$

$t^2 (V_2^2 - V_1^2) = b^2$

$t = \dfrac{b}{\sqrt{V_2^2 - V_1^2}}$

Answer: $\frac{b}{\sqrt{V_2{ }^2-V_1{ }^2}}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two boys are standing at points $A$ and $B$ on ground, where distance $A B=x$. The boy at $B$ stars running perpendicular to $A B$ with velocity $V_1$. The boy at A starts running simultaneously with velocity v and meets the other boy in time $t$. The value of $t$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left[\frac{x}{v-v_1}\right]^{1 / 2}$
- **(B)** $\left[\frac{x}{v_1-v}\right]^{1 / 2}$
- **(C)** $\left[\frac{x^2}{v^2-v_1^2}\right]^{1 / 2}$
- **(D)** $\left[\frac{x^2}{v_1^2-v^2}\right]^{1 / 2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\left[\frac{x^2}{v^2-v_1^2}\right]^{1 / 2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the meeting point be $C$.

Since boy $B$ runs perpendicular to $AB$, $\triangle ABC$ is a right-angled triangle at $B$.

Distance covered by boy $B$ in time $t$ is $BC = v_1 t$.

Distance covered by boy $A$ in time $t$ is $AC = v t$.

Using Pythagoras theorem in $\triangle ABC$:

$AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2$

$(v t)^2 = x^2 + (v_1 t)^2$

$v^2 t^2 - v_1^2 t^2 = x^2$

$t^2 (v^2 - v_1^2) = x^2$

$t = \left[\dfrac{x^2}{v^2 - v_1^2}\right]^{1/2}$

Answer: $\left[\frac{x^2}{v^2-v_1^2}\right]^{1 / 2}$

</details>
