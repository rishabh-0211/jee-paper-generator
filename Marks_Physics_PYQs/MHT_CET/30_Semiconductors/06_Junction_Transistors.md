### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In an n-p-n transistor in common emitter configuration when $V_{BE}$ is increased by small amount then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $I_B$ increases and $I_C$ increases proportionally
- **(B)** $I_B$ increases and $I_C$ remain constant
- **(C)** $I_B$ remain constant and $I_C$ increases
- **(D)** both $I_B$ and $I_C$ remain nearly constant

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $I_B$ increases and $I_C$ increases proportionally**

**Detailed Solution:**

In a common emitter configuration of an n-p-n transistor, the base-emitter junction is forward biased.

When the base-emitter voltage $V_{BE}$ is increased, the forward bias increases, which leads to an increase in the base current $I_B$.

The collector current $I_C$ is related to the base current $I_B$ by the relation $I_C = \beta I_B$, where $\beta$ is the common emitter current gain.

Since $\beta$ is nearly constant for a given transistor, an increase in $I_B$ results in a proportional increase in $I_C$.

Therefore, $I_B$ increases and $I_C$ increases proportionally.

Answer: $I_B$ increases and $I_C$ increases proportionally

</details>

$A_p = \beta^2 \left(\dfrac{R_L}{r}\right)$

### Q70. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A change of $8.0 \mathrm{~mA}$ in the emitter current brings a change of $7.9 \mathrm{~mA}$ in the collector current. The values of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.99,90$
- **(B)** $0.96,79$
- **(C)** $0.97,99$
- **(D)** $0.99,79$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $0.99,79$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that, change in emitter current, $\Delta I_{E}=8 \mathrm{~mA}$
and change in collector current, $\Delta I_{C}=7.9 \mathrm{~mA}$ We know that,
$$
\alpha=\frac{\Delta I_{C}}{\Delta I_{E}} \Rightarrow \alpha=\frac{7.9}{8} \Rightarrow \alpha \simeq 0.99
$$
Also we know that
$$
\begin{array}{c}
\quad \beta=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha} \\
\Rightarrow \quad \beta=\frac{\frac{7.9}{8}}{1-\frac{7.9}{8}}=\frac{7.9}{8-7.9}
\end{array}
$$
or $\quad \beta=\frac{7.9}{0.1}=79$
Hence, the required answer is $\alpha=0.99$ and $\beta=79$

</details>
