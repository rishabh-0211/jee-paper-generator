### Q1. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following while in motion cannot be deflected by magnetic field?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Protons
- **(B)** Cathode rays
- **(C)** Alpha particles
- **(D)** Neutrons

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Neutrons**

**Detailed Solution:**

The protons, cathode rays and the alpha particles are charged particles, so these are deflected by the magnetic field. But neutrons have not any charge, so these are not deflected by magnetic field.

</details>
