### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A wire of Young's modulus $1 \cdot 6 \times 10^{12} \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^{2}$ is stretched by a force so as to produce a strain of $2 \times 10^{-4}$. The energy density of the wire is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 \cdot 2 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$
- **(B)** $3 \cdot 2 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$
- **(C)** $1 \cdot 6 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$
- **(D)** $6 \cdot 4 \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3 \cdot 2 \times 10^{4} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{m}^{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Energy $=1 / 2 \times Y \times(\text { Strain })^{2}$
$=1 / 2 \times 1.6 \times 10^{12} \times 4^{2} \times 10^{-8}$
$=3.2 \times 10^{4}$.

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Young's modulus of the material of a wire is $Y$. If it is under a stress $S$, the energy stored per unit volume is given by

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{S}{Y}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{S^{2}}{Y}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{S}{Y^{2}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{S^{2}}{Y^{2}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{2} \frac{S^{2}}{Y}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here $u=\frac{1}{2}$ stress $\times$ strain
also $Y=$ stress $/$ strain
Hence
$$
\begin{array}{l}
u=\frac{1}{2}(\text { stress })^{2} / Y \\
u=\frac{1}{2} \frac{S^{2}}{Y}
\end{array}
$$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A couple produces

**Options:**

- **(A)** no motion
- **(B)** linear and rotational motion
- **(C)** purely rotational motion
- **(D)** purely linear motion

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) purely rotational motion**

**Detailed Solution:**

A couple consists of two equal and opposite forces acting at a separation, so that net force becomes zero. When a couple acts on a body it rotates the body but does not produce any translatory motion. Hence, only rotational motion is produced.

</details>
