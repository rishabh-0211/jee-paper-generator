### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The compressibility of water is $6 \times 10^{-10} \mathrm{~m}^{2} / \mathrm{N}$. If one litre of water is subjected to a pressure of $4 \times 10^{7} \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^{2}$, then the decrease in its volume in millilitre will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** 10
- **(B)** 20
- **(C)** 24
- **(D)** 15

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 24**

**Detailed Solution:**

Bulk modulus $B=-\frac{P}{\Delta V / V}$
$-$ ve sign shows that with an increase in pressure, a decrease in volume occurs
Compressibility $\mathrm{K}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{~B}}=-\frac{\Delta \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{PV}}$
Decrease in volume,
$\Delta \mathrm{V}=\mathrm{PVK}=4 \times 10^{7} \times 1 \times 6 \times 10^{-10}$
$=24 \times 10^{-3}$ litre $=24 \times 10^{-3} \times 10^{3} \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$
$=24 \mathrm{~cc}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2020 (16 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The density of a metal at normal pressure $\mathrm{P}$ is $\varrho .$ When it is subjected to an excess pressure, the density becomes $\varrho^{\prime}$. If $\mathrm{K}$ is the bulk modulus of the metal, then the ratio $\frac{\varrho^{\prime}}{\varrho}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1+\frac{K}{P}$
- **(B)** $1+\frac{\mathrm{P}}{K}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{1-\frac{K}{P}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{1-\frac{P}{K}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{1}{1-\frac{P}{K}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{B}=-\mathrm{V} \frac{\mathrm{dP}}{\mathrm{dV}}$
Thus, $\Delta \mathrm{V}=-\frac{\mathrm{pV}}{\mathrm{B}}$ or $\mathrm{V}^{\prime}-\mathrm{V}=-\frac{\mathrm{pV}}{\mathrm{B}}$
$\mathrm{Or}, \mathrm{V}^{\prime}=\mathrm{V}\left(1-\frac{\mathrm{p}}{\mathrm{B}}\right)$
Now, $\rho^{\prime}=\frac{m}{V^{\prime}}=\frac{m}{V\left(1-\frac{p}{B}\right)}=\frac{\rho}{\left(1-\frac{p}{B}\right)}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 2)

### Q5. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The density and bulk modulus of a metal bar is ' $\mathrm{e}$ ' and ' $\mathrm{K}$ ' respectively. When pressure 'P' is applied from all sides to that metal bar, the increase in its density is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{Q P}{(K+P)}$
- **(B)** $\frac{Q P}{(K-P)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{K-P}{Q}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{K}+\mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{Q}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{Q P}{(K-P)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\rho=\mathrm{M} / \mathrm{V}$
$\frac{\mathrm{d} \rho}{\rho}=-\frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{V}}$
$\mathrm{k}=-\frac{\mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{V}}$
$-\frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{V}}=\frac{\mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{k}}$
$\frac{\mathrm{d} \rho}{\rho}=\frac{\mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{k}}$
$\Rightarrow \mathrm{d} \rho=\frac{\rho \mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{k}}$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The increase in pressure required to decrease the $200 \mathrm{~L}$ volume of a liquid by $0.008 \%$ in $\mathrm{kPa}$ is (Bulk modulus of the liquid $=2100 \mathrm{MPa}$ is )

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8.4$
- **(B)** 84
- **(C)** $92.4$
- **(D)** 168

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 84**

**Detailed Solution:**

Bulk modulus $K=\frac{\Delta p}{\Delta V} \cdot V$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\Delta p=\frac{K \Delta V}{V} \\
\Delta p=\frac{2100 \times 10^{6} \times 0.008}{200}
\end{array}
$$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For a given material, the Young's modulus is $2.4$ times that of rigidity modulus. Its poisson's ratio is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2.4$
- **(B)** $1.2$
- **(C)** $0.4$
- **(D)** $0.2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $0.2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$Y=2 \eta(1+\sigma)$
$\Rightarrow \quad 2.4 \eta=2 \eta(1+\sigma)$
$\Rightarrow \quad 1.2=1+\sigma$
$\Rightarrow \quad \sigma=0.2$

</details>
