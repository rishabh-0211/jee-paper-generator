### Q1. MHT CET 2022 (10 Aug Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which one of the following statements is not the property of light?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Light involves transportation of energy.
- **(B)** Light can travel through vacuum.
- **(C)** Light requires material medium.
- **(D)** Light has finite speed.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Light requires material medium.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Light is electromagnetic in nature it does not require any material medium for its propagation.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In which layer of the atmosphere, the water vapour is present?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text { Troposphere }$
- **(B)** Ionosphere
- **(C)** Mesosphere
- **(D)** Stratosphere

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text { Troposphere }$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Water vapour in the atmosphere is confined only to its lower layer, i.e. troposphere. This is the reason all weather phenomena occur only in this layer.

</details>
