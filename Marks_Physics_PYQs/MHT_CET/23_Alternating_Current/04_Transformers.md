### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A transformer having an efficiency 80% is working on $200 \text{ V}$ and $4 \text{ kW}$ power supply. If the current in the secondary coil is $5 \text{ A}$, the voltage across the secondary coil and the current in the primary coil, respectively, are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $10 \text{ A}, 600 \text{ V}$
- **(B)** $20 \text{ A}, 640 \text{ V}$
- **(C)** $25 \text{ A}, 680 \text{ V}$
- **(D)** $40 \text{ A}, 700 \text{ V}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $20 \text{ A}, 640 \text{ V}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given input power $P_p = 4 \text{ kW} = 4000 \text{ W}$ and primary voltage $V_p = 200 \text{ V}$.

The current in the primary coil is $I_p = \dfrac{P_p}{V_p} = \dfrac{4000}{200} = 20 \text{ A}$.

The efficiency of the transformer is $\eta = \dfrac{P_s}{P_p} = \dfrac{V_s I_s}{P_p}$.

Substituting the given values, $0.8 = \dfrac{V_s \times 5}{4000}$.

$V_s = \dfrac{0.8 \times 4000}{5} = \dfrac{3200}{5} = 640 \text{ V}$.

The current in the primary coil is $20 \text{ A}$ and the voltage across the secondary coil is $640 \text{ V}$.

Answer: $20 \text{ A}, 640 \text{ V}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If a transformer of an amplifier has output impedance $8000\ \Omega$ and the speaker has input impedance of $8\ \Omega$, the primary and secondary turns of this transformer connected between the output of amplifier and to loudspeaker should have the ratio

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1000:1$
- **(B)** $100:1$
- **(C)** $1:32$
- **(D)** $32:1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $32:1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a transformer used for impedance matching, the relationship between the primary impedance $Z_p$, secondary impedance $Z_s$, primary turns $N_p$, and secondary turns $N_s$ is given by:

$\dfrac{Z_p}{Z_s} = \left(\dfrac{N_p}{N_s}\right)^2$

Given:
$Z_p = 8000\ \Omega$
$Z_s = 8\ \Omega$

Substituting the values into the formula:

$\left(\dfrac{N_p}{N_s}\right)^2 = \dfrac{8000}{8} = 1000$

$\dfrac{N_p}{N_s} = \sqrt{1000} \approx 31.62$

The closest integer ratio among the given options is $32:1$.

Answer: $32:1$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Alternating current of peak value $\left(\dfrac{2}{\pi}\right)$ A flows through the primary coil of a transformer. The coefficient of mutual inductance between primary and secondary coils is 1H. The peak value of induced e.m.f. in the secondary coil is
(Frequency of a.c. = 50 Hz)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $100\text{V}$
- **(B)** $300\text{V}$
- **(C)** $200\text{V}$
- **(D)** $400\text{V}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $200\text{V}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The current in the primary coil is $I = I_0 \sin(\omega t)$.

The induced e.m.f. in the secondary coil is given by $e = -M \dfrac{dI}{dt}$.

$e = -M \dfrac{d}{dt} (I_0 \sin(\omega t)) = -M I_0 \omega \cos(\omega t)$

The peak value of the induced e.m.f. is $e_0 = M I_0 \omega$.

Given $M = 1$ H, $I_0 = \dfrac{2}{\pi}$ A, and $f = 50$ Hz.

The angular frequency is $\omega = 2\pi f = 2\pi \times 50 = 100\pi$ rad/s.

Substituting the values, we get:

$e_0 = 1 \times \left(\dfrac{2}{\pi}\right) \times 100\pi = 200$ V.

Answer: $200\text{V}$

</details>

### Q16. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The core used in transformers are laminated to

**Options:**

- **(A)** increase the magnetic field.
- **(B)** increase the level of the magnetic saturation of the core.
- **(C)** reduce the residual magnetism in the core.
- **(D)** reduce eddy current losses in the core.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) reduce eddy current losses in the core.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The core of a transformer is laminated to minimize eddy current losses. When the magnetic flux linked with the core changes, it induces eddy currents within the core material. These currents cause energy loss in the form of heat. By using a laminated core consisting of thin sheets insulated from each other, the resistance of the path for eddy currents is significantly increased, which reduces the magnitude of the eddy currents and thereby minimizes the power loss.

Answer: reduce eddy current losses in the core.

</details>

**Options:**

- **(D)** $250 \mathrm{~mA}$

### Q42. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A transformer is used to light a $100 \mathrm{~W}$ and $110 \mathrm{~V}$ lamp from a $220 \mathrm{~V}$ mains. If the main current is $0.5 \mathrm{~A}$, the efficiency of the transformer is approximately

**Options:**

- **(A)** $30 \%$
- **(B)** $50 \%$
- **(C)** $90 \%$
- **(D)** $10 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $90 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The efficiency of transformer $=\frac{\text { Energy obtained from the secondary coil }}{\text { Energy given to the primary coil }}$
Or $\eta=\frac{\text { Output power }}{\text { Input power }}$
or
$$
\eta=\frac{V_{s} I_{s}}{V_{p} I_{p}}
$$
Given, $\quad V_{s} I_{s}=100 \mathrm{~W}, V_{p}=220 \mathrm{~V}, I_{p}=0.5 \mathrm{~A}$
$$
\text { Hence, } \quad \eta=\frac{100}{220 \times 0.5}=0.90=90 \%
$$
Note The efficiency of an ideal transformer is 1 (or $100 \%$ ). But in practice due to loss in energy, the efficiency of transformer is always less than 1 (or less than $100 \%$ ).

</details>
