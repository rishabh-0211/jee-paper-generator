### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}$ is the mass of an oxygen isotope ${ }_8 \mathrm{O}^{17}$ and $M_p$ and $M_N$ are the mass of proton and mass of neutron respectively, then the nucleus binding energy of the isotope is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\quad \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{o}} \mathrm{C}^2$
- **(B)** $\quad\left(\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}-8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{p}}\right) \mathrm{C}^2$
- **(C)** $\left(\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}-17 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{N}}\right) \mathrm{C}^2$
- **(D)** $\left(8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{p}}+9 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{N}}-\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}\right) \mathrm{C}^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{p}}+9 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{N}}-\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}\right) \mathrm{C}^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The nucleus of the oxygen isotope ${}_8\mathrm{O}^{17}$ contains $8$ protons and $17 - 8 = 9$ neutrons.

The mass defect $\Delta M$ is given by the difference between the sum of the masses of the individual nucleons and the actual mass of the nucleus.

$\Delta M = Z \mathrm{M}_p + (A - Z) \mathrm{M}_N - \mathrm{M}_O$

Substituting $Z = 8$ and $A = 17$:

$\Delta M = 8 \mathrm{M}_p + 9 \mathrm{M}_N - \mathrm{M}_O$

The binding energy is given by $\Delta M \mathrm{C}^2$:

Binding Energy $= (8 \mathrm{M}_p + 9 \mathrm{M}_N - \mathrm{M}_O) \mathrm{C}^2$

Answer: $\left(8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{p}}+9 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{N}}-\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}}\right) \mathrm{C}^2$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $M_{O}$ is the mass of an oxygen isotope ${ }_{8} \mathrm{O}^{17}$, $M_{p}$ and $M_{n}$ are the masses of a proton and a neutron, respectively, the nuclear binding energy of the isotope is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(M_{O}-8 M_{p}\right) c^{2}$
- **(B)** $\left(M_{O}-8 M_{p}-9 M_{n}\right) c^{2}$
- **(C)** $M_{O} c^{2}$
- **(D)** $\left(M_{O}-17 M_{n}\right) c^{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left(M_{O}-8 M_{p}-9 M_{n}\right) c^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Binding energy
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{BE} &=\left(M_{\text {nucleus }}-M_{\text {nucleons }}\right) c^{2} \\
&=\left(M_{O}-8 M_{p}-9 M_{n}\right) c^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
