### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A set of $8$ tuning forks is arranged in ascending order of frequencies. Each fork produces $4$ beats with the next one and frequency of last fork is octave of first. The frequency of $5^{\text{th}}$ fork is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $22$ Hz
- **(B)** $33$ Hz
- **(C)** $44$ Hz
- **(D)** $55$ Hz

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $44$ Hz**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the frequency of the first tuning fork be $f_1$.

Since each tuning fork produces $4$ beats with the next one and they are arranged in ascending order of frequencies, their frequencies form an arithmetic progression with a common difference $d = 4$ Hz.

The frequency of the $n^{\text{th}}$ tuning fork is given by $f_n = f_1 + (n - 1)d$.

For the $8^{\text{th}}$ tuning fork, $f_8 = f_1 + (8 - 1) \times 4 = f_1 + 28$.

It is given that the frequency of the last fork is the octave of the first, which means $f_8 = 2f_1$.

Substituting the value of $f_8$, we get:

$2f_1 = f_1 + 28$

$f_1 = 28$ Hz

The frequency of the $5^{\text{th}}$ tuning fork is:

$f_5 = f_1 + (5 - 1) \times 4$

$f_5 = 28 + 16 = 44$ Hz.

Answer: $44$ Hz

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A man standing between two parallel cliffs fires a gun. He hear two echos one after $3$ second and the other after $5$ second. The separation between the two cliffs is $1360$ m, the speed of sound is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $320$ m/s
- **(B)** $330$ m/s
- **(C)** $340$ m/s
- **(D)** $350$ m/s

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $340$ m/s**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the distance of the man from the first cliff be $d_1$ and from the second cliff be $d_2$.

Total distance between the cliffs is $d_1 + d_2 = 1360$ m.

Time taken for the first echo, $t_1 = 3$ s.

Time taken for the second echo, $t_2 = 5$ s.

Let the speed of sound be $v$.

Distance traveled by sound for the first echo is $2d_1 = v t_1 = 3v$.

Distance traveled by sound for the second echo is $2d_2 = v t_2 = 5v$.

Adding the two equations:

$2d_1 + 2d_2 = 3v + 5v$

$2(d_1 + d_2) = 8v$

Substituting $d_1 + d_2 = 1360$ m:

$2 \times 1360 = 8v$

$2720 = 8v$

$v = \dfrac{2720}{8} = 340$ m/s.

Answer: $340$ m/s

Two sources of sound are emitting progressive waves $y_1 = 4\sin 708\,\pi t$ and $y_2 = 3\sin 700\,\pi t$. The sources are placed close to each other. The number of beats heard per second and intensity ratio between waxing and wanning are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4$ , $16 : 9$
- **(B)** $8$ , $16 : 9$
- **(C)** $8$ , $49 : 1$
- **(D)** $4$ , $49 : 1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4$ , $49 : 1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given progressive waves are $y_1 = 4\sin 708\pi t$ and $y_2 = 3\sin 700\pi t$.

The angular frequencies are $\omega_1 = 708\pi$ and $\omega_2 = 700\pi$.

The frequencies of the two sources are:
$f_1 = \dfrac{\omega_1}{2\pi} = \dfrac{708\pi}{2\pi} = 354$ Hz
$f_2 = \dfrac{\omega_2}{2\pi} = \dfrac{700\pi}{2\pi} = 350$ Hz

The number of beats heard per second is:
$n = f_1 - f_2 = 354 - 350 = 4$

The amplitudes of the two waves are $A_1 = 4$ and $A_2 = 3$.

The maximum amplitude during waxing is $A_{max} = A_1 + A_2 = 4 + 3 = 7$.
The minimum amplitude during waning is $A_{min} = A_1 - A_2 = 4 - 3 = 1$.

The intensity of a wave is directly proportional to the square of its amplitude. The intensity ratio between waxing and waning is:
$\dfrac{I_{max}}{I_{min}} = \left(\dfrac{A_{max}}{A_{min}}\right)^2 = \left(\dfrac{7}{1}\right)^2 = \dfrac{49}{1}$

The number of beats is $4$ and the intensity ratio is $49 : 1$.

Answer: $4$ , $49 : 1$

Since initially $n_2 = n_1 - 6$, we get $n_2' > n_2$.
For the frequency to increase, the tension $T_2$ must be increased.

### Q42. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Beats are produced by two waves given by $y_{1}=a \sin 2000 \pi t$ and $y_{2}=a \sin 2008 \pi t$. The
number of beats heard per second is

**Options:**

- **(A)** zero
- **(B)** one
- **(C)** four
- **(D)** eight

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) four**

**Detailed Solution:**

Number of beats per second $=n_{1} \sim n_{2}$ $\omega_{1}=2000 \pi=2 \pi n_{1}$ $$
\begin{array}{lc}
\Rightarrow & n_{1}=1000 \\
\text { and } & \omega_{2}=2008 \pi=2 \pi n_{2} \\
\Rightarrow & n_{2}=1004
\end{array}
$$
Number of beats heard per second
$$
=1004-1000=4
$$

</details>
