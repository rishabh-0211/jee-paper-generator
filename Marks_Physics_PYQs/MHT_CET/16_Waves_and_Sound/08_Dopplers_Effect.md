### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

An observer moves towards a stationary source of sound with a velocity $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{th}$ of the velocity of sound. The percentage increase in the apparent frequency is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.5\%$
- **(B)** $0\%$
- **(C)** $20\%$
- **(D)** $5\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $20\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the velocity of sound be $v$ and the actual frequency of the source be $f$.

Velocity of the observer, $v_o = \dfrac{v}{5}$.

Velocity of the source, $v_s = 0$.

According to the Doppler effect, the apparent frequency $f'$ is given by:

$f' = f \left( \dfrac{v + v_o}{v} \right)$

Substituting the value of $v_o$:

$f' = f \left( \dfrac{v + \dfrac{v}{5}}{v} \right) = f \left( 1 + \dfrac{1}{5} \right) = \dfrac{6}{5} f$

The percentage increase in the apparent frequency is:

$\dfrac{f' - f}{f} \times 100 = \dfrac{\dfrac{6}{5} f - f}{f} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20\%$

Answer: $20\%$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two sirens situated one km apart are producing sound of frequency $330$ Hz. The observer starts moving from one siren to the other with a speed of $2$ m/s. If the speed of sound is $330$ m/s, What will be the beat frequency heard by the observer?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8$ Hz
- **(B)** $4$ Hz
- **(C)** $6$ Hz
- **(D)** $1$ Hz

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $4$ Hz**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the frequency of the sirens be $f = 330$ Hz and the speed of sound be $v = 330$ m/s.

The observer moves away from the first siren with speed $v_o = 2$ m/s. The apparent frequency heard from the first siren is:

$f_1 = f \left( \dfrac{v - v_o}{v} \right)$

The observer moves towards the second siren with speed $v_o = 2$ m/s. The apparent frequency heard from the second siren is:

$f_2 = f \left( \dfrac{v + v_o}{v} \right)$

The beat frequency heard by the observer is the difference between the two apparent frequencies:

$f_{beat} = f_2 - f_1 = f \left( \dfrac{v + v_o}{v} \right) - f \left( \dfrac{v - v_o}{v} \right) = f \left( \dfrac{2v_o}{v} \right)$

Substituting the given values:

$f_{beat} = 330 \left( \dfrac{2 \times 2}{330} \right) = 4$ Hz.

Answer: $4$ Hz

</details>
