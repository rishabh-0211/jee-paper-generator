### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The velocity time graph of a body moving in a straight line is shown in a figure. The ratio of displacement to distance travelled by the body in time $0$ to $10$ s is
<img style="width: 400px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/april/watermarked_images/13_april_shift_2__q31_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1:1$
- **(B)** $1:4$
- **(C)** $1:2$
- **(D)** $1:3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $1:3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Displacement is the algebraic sum of the areas under the velocity-time graph, and distance is the sum of their absolute values.

Calculating the areas for each time interval:
Area for $0$ to $2$ s: $A_1 = 8 \times 2 = 16$ m
Area for $2$ to $4$ s: $A_2 = -4 \times 2 = -8$ m
Area for $4$ to $8$ s: $A_3 = 4 \times 4 = 16$ m
Area for $8$ to $10$ s: $A_4 = -4 \times 2 = -8$ m

Total displacement $= A_1 + A_2 + A_3 + A_4 = 16 - 8 + 16 - 8 = 16$ m

Total distance $= |A_1| + |A_2| + |A_3| + |A_4| = 16 + 8 + 16 + 8 = 48$ m

Ratio of displacement to distance $= \dfrac{16}{48} = \dfrac{1}{3}$

Thus, the required ratio is $1:3$.

Answer: $1:3$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For the velocity-time graph shown in the figure below, the distance covered by the body in last two second of its motion is ' $\mathrm{S}_1$ '. What is the ratio of ' $S_1$ ' to the total distance covered by it
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/uuW-ddp9PTeGPo6oxHRfa837gavlTQAGPQAy6XyPHLM.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{4}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given velocity-time graph, the motion starts at $t = 1$ s and ends at $t = 7$ s.

The distance covered in the last two seconds ($t = 5$ s to $t = 7$ s) is given by the area of $\triangle CDE$:

$S_1 = \dfrac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times (7 - 5) \times 10 = 10$ m

The total distance covered is the area of the trapezium $ABCD$:

$S_{\text{total}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times (AD + BC) \times \text{height}$

$S_{\text{total}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times ((7 - 1) + (5 - 3)) \times 10$

$S_{\text{total}} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times (6 + 2) \times 10 = 40$ m

The required ratio is:

$\dfrac{S_1}{S_{\text{total}}} = \dfrac{10}{40} = \dfrac{1}{4}$

Answer: $\frac{1}{4}$

For figure (b), the displacement $X$ increases linearly with time $t$. The graph is a straight line with a positive constant slope. This indicates that the object is moving with a uniform (constant) velocity in the positive $x$-direction.

For figure (d), the slope alternates between positive and negative constant values, indicating oscillatory motion with constant speed.

Thus, figure (a) shows an object at rest, and figure (b) shows an object moving with uniform velocity in the positive $x$-direction.

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A velocity - time graph of a body is shown below. The distance covered by the body from 6 second to 9 second is
<img width=300px src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/mht_cet/e5xw16ezoGH9oM_-_8gN6s2Cp6yOTkbixu_cUxX-j2A.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** 22.5 m
- **(B)** 60.0 m
- **(C)** 82.5 m
- **(D)** 120.0 m

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 82.5 m**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given velocity-time graph, the velocity at $t = 6$ s is $20$ m/s and at $t = 10$ s is $40$ m/s.

The acceleration in this interval is constant and is given by the slope of the graph:

$a = \dfrac{40 - 20}{10 - 6} = 5 \text{ m/s}^2$

The velocity at $t = 9$ s is:

$v = u + at = 20 + 5(9 - 6) = 35$ m/s

The distance covered from $t = 6$ s to $t = 9$ s is the area under the $v-t$ graph in this time interval, which forms a trapezium:

$\text{Distance} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times (\text{sum of parallel sides}) \times \text{height}$

$\text{Distance} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times (20 + 35) \times (9 - 6)$

$\text{Distance} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 55 \times 3 = 82.5$ m

Answer: 82.5 m

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The acceleration of a moving body can be found from

**Options:**

- **(A)** area under velocity - time graph.
- **(B)** area under distance - time graph.
- **(C)** slope of the velocity - time graph.
- **(D)** slope of the distance - time graph.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) slope of the velocity - time graph.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acceleration is defined as the rate of change of velocity with respect to time, which is mathematically given by $a = \dfrac{dv}{dt}$.

In a velocity-time graph, the slope of the curve at any point is equal to $\dfrac{dv}{dt}$.

Therefore, the acceleration of a moving body can be found from the slope of the velocity-time graph.

Answer: slope of the velocity - time graph.

</details>
