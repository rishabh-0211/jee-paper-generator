### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The periodic time of a magnet in vibration magnetometer is $T_0$. If a magnet is replaced by another magnet whose moment of inertia is three times and magnetic moment is one-third of the original magnet, then time period of new magnet will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{T_0}{3}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{T_0}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(C)** $3\,T_0$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{3}\,T_0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $3\,T_0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The time period of a magnet in a vibration magnetometer is given by $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{I}{MB_H}}$

For the original magnet, $T_0 = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{I}{MB_H}}$

For the new magnet, the moment of inertia is $I' = 3I$ and the magnetic moment is $M' = \dfrac{M}{3}$

The new time period is $T' = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{I'}{M'B_H}}$

Substituting the values, $T' = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{3I}{\left(\dfrac{M}{3}\right)B_H}}$

$T' = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{9I}{MB_H}}$

$T' = 3 \times 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{I}{MB_H}}$

$T' = 3T_0$

Answer: $3\,T_0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The frequency of oscillation of a small magnet in a magnetic field of induction 'B' is '$n$'. If the frequency of oscillations of the same magnet in a field of induction 'X' falls to $\left(\dfrac{n}{3}\right)$, the value of 'X' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3B$
- **(B)** $9B$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{B}{3}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{B}{9}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\dfrac{B}{9}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The frequency of oscillation of a magnet in a magnetic field is given by

$n = \dfrac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\dfrac{MB}{I}}$

Since the same magnet is used, $M$ and $I$ are constant. Thus, $n \propto \sqrt{B}$, which implies $B \propto n^2$.

Given $n_1 = n$ at $B_1 = B$, and $n_2 = \dfrac{n}{3}$ at $B_2 = X$.

$\dfrac{B_1}{B_2} = \left(\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\right)^2$

$\dfrac{B}{X} = \left(\dfrac{n}{n/3}\right)^2 = 3^2 = 9$

$X = \dfrac{B}{9}$

Answer: $\dfrac{B}{9}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2021 (24 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two bar magnets ' $\mathrm{P}$ ' and ' $\mathrm{Q}$ ' are kept in uniform magnetic field 'B' with magnetic moments ' $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}$ ' and ' $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}$ ' respectively. Magnet ' $\mathrm{P}$ ' is oscillating with frequency twice that of magnet ' $\mathrm{Q}$ '. If the moment of inertia of the magnet ' $\mathrm{P}$ ' is twice that of magnet ' $\mathrm{Q}$ ' then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}=2 \mathrm{MP}_{\mathrm{P}}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}=2 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}=8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}=8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}=8 \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The frequency of oscillation given by
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{p}}=\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{p}} \mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{I}_P}} \text { and } \mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{Q}}=\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}} \mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{Q}}}} \\
& \mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{p}}=2 \mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{Q}} \\
& \frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}} \mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{P}}}}=2 \cdot \frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}} \mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{Q}}}} \\
& \frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{P}}}=4 \frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{Q}}} \\
& \therefore \frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{P}}}{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{Q}}}=4 \frac{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{P}}}{\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{Q}}}=8 \quad\left[\because \mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{P}}=2 \mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{Q}}\right]
\end{aligned}$

</details>
