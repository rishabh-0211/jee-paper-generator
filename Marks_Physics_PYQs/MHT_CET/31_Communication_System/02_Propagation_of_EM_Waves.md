### Q2. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

According to theoretical study of radiation from a same linear antenna, the power radiated is proportional to $[\lambda=$ wavelength $]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\lambda^{-2}$
- **(B)** $\lambda^{-1}$
- **(C)** $\lambda^{2}$
- **(D)** $\lambda$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\lambda^{-2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

A linear antenna radiates power proportional to $\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}\right)^{2}$.
Hence a longer antenna emits more power. Also power radiated by a long wavelength base would be small.

</details>
