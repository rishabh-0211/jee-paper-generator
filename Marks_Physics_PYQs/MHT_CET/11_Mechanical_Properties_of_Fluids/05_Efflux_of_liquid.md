### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A water tank of height $'h'$ has a hole at the bottom. It is completely filled with water. The total pressure at the bottom is $4H$ and atmospheric pressure is $H$. The velocity of water flowing out of the hole is ($\rho$ = density of water)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\dfrac{6H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- **(B)** $\left(\dfrac{H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- **(C)** $\left(\dfrac{3H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- **(D)** $\left(\dfrac{5H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left(\dfrac{6H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total pressure at the bottom of the tank is given by the sum of atmospheric pressure and the pressure due to the water column.

$P_{total} = P_{atm} + \rho g h$

Given $P_{total} = 4H$ and $P_{atm} = H$.

$4H = H + \rho g h$

$\rho g h = 3H$

Applying Bernoulli's principle between the top surface of the water and the hole at the bottom:

$P_{atm} + \rho g h + 0 = P_{atm} + 0 + \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$

$\rho g h = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$

Substituting the value of $\rho g h$:

$3H = \dfrac{1}{2}\rho v^2$

$v^2 = \dfrac{6H}{\rho}$

$v = \left(\dfrac{6H}{\rho}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$

Answer: $\left(\dfrac{6H}{\rho}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

On opposite sides of a wide vertical vessel filled with water (density $\rho$), two identical holes are drilled, each having cross-section area $A$. The height difference between holes is $x$. The resultant force of reaction of water flowing out of vessel is ($g$ = acceleration due to gravity)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\rho A g x$
- **(B)** $2\rho A g x$
- **(C)** $3\rho A g x$
- **(D)** $4\rho A g x$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2\rho A g x$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the depth of the upper hole from the free surface of water be $h_1$ and the depth of the lower hole be $h_2$.

The velocity of efflux from the upper hole is $v_1 = \sqrt{2gh_1}$ and from the lower hole is $v_2 = \sqrt{2gh_2}$.

The force of reaction due to water flowing out from a hole is given by the rate of change of momentum, $F = v \dfrac{dm}{dt} = \rho A v^2$.

The reaction force from the upper hole is $F_1 = \rho A (2gh_1) = 2\rho A g h_1$.

The reaction force from the lower hole is $F_2 = \rho A (2gh_2) = 2\rho A g h_2$.

Since the holes are on opposite sides, the water jets emerge in opposite directions, meaning the reaction forces also act in opposite directions.

The resultant force of reaction is $F_{net} = F_2 - F_1 = 2\rho A g (h_2 - h_1)$.

Given that the height difference between the holes is $x$, we have $h_2 - h_1 = x$.

Therefore, $F_{net} = 2\rho A g x$.

Answer: $2\rho A g x$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (5 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A vessel completely filled with water has two holes 'P' and 'Q' at depths ' 2 h ' and ' 8 h ' from the top respectively. Hole ' P ' is square of side ' a ' and hole ' $Q$ ' is a circle of radius ' $r$ '. The water flowing out per second from both the holes is same, then side ' $a$ ' of hole ' $P$ ' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{2 \pi r}$
- **(B)** $r \sqrt{2 \pi}$
- **(C)** $2 \sqrt{\pi r}$
- **(D)** $2 \pi r$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $r \sqrt{2 \pi}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Velocity of efflux from hole P at depth $2h$ is $v_P = \sqrt{2g(2h)} = 2\sqrt{gh}$

Velocity of efflux from hole Q at depth $8h$ is $v_Q = \sqrt{2g(8h)} = 4\sqrt{gh}$

Area of square hole P is $A_P = a^2$

Area of circular hole Q is $A_Q = \pi r^2$

Since the volume of water flowing out per second is the same for both holes:

$A_P v_P = A_Q v_Q$

$a^2 (2\sqrt{gh}) = (\pi r^2) (4\sqrt{gh})$

$a^2 = 2\pi r^2$

$a = r\sqrt{2\pi}$

Answer: $r \sqrt{2 \pi}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

There are two identical small holes on the opposite side of a tank containing full of a liquid. The tank is open at the top. The difference in height between the two holes is ' $h$ '. As the liquid comes out of the two holes, the tank will experience a net horizontal force proportional to
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/mhtcet2025/Dt9YM_clXvs84t6KEAHgfi73x1ImGdlJqZ4EFDiT5qQ.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $h^{3 / 2}$
- **(B)** $h^2$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\mathrm{h}}$
- **(D)** h

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) h**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the area of each hole be $A$ and the density of the liquid be $ho$.

Let the depth of the upper hole from the free surface of the liquid be $h_1$ and that of the lower hole be $h_2$.

According to Torricelli's law, the velocity of efflux from the upper hole is $v_1 = \sqrt{2gh_1}$ and from the lower hole is $v_2 = \sqrt{2gh_2}$.

The horizontal force exerted on the tank due to the liquid coming out of a hole is equal to the rate of change of momentum of the escaping liquid:
$F = v \dfrac{dm}{dt} = v (\rho A v) = \rho A v^2$

Force due to the upper hole:
$F_1 = \rho A (\sqrt{2gh_1})^2 = 2\rho A g h_1$

Force due to the lower hole:
$F_2 = \rho A (\sqrt{2gh_2})^2 = 2\rho A g h_2$

Since the holes are on opposite sides, the liquid jets emerge in opposite directions, causing the forces on the tank to act in opposite directions.

The net horizontal force on the tank is:
$F_{\text{net}} = F_2 - F_1 = 2\rho A g h_2 - 2\rho A g h_1 = 2\rho A g (h_2 - h_1)$

Given that the difference in height between the two holes is $h$, we have $h_2 - h_1 = h$.

Therefore, $F_{\text{net}} = 2\rho A g h$

Thus, the net horizontal force is directly proportional to $h$.

Answer: h

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2020 (16 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A large open tank containing water has two holes to its wall. A square hole of side 'a' is made at a depth 'y' and a circular hole of radius 'r' is made at a depth '16y'from the surface of water. If equal amount of water comes out through both the holes per second, then the relation between 'r' and 'a' will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $r=\frac{2 a}{\pi}$
- **(B)** $r=\frac{a}{2 \sqrt{\pi}}$
- **(C)** $r=\frac{a}{2 \pi}$
- **(D)** $r=\frac{2 a}{\sqrt{\pi}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $r=\frac{a}{2 \sqrt{\pi}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

(A)
$v_{1} A_{1}=v_{2} A_{2}$
$\sqrt{2 g h_{1}} A_{1}=\sqrt{2 g h_{2}} A_{2}$
$\sqrt{y} a^{2}=\sqrt{16} y \pi r^{2}$

$r=\frac{a}{2 \sqrt{\pi}}$

### Q6. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A large vessel completely filled with water has two holes ' $A$ ' and ' $B$ ' at depths 'h' and '4h' from the top. Hole 'A' is a square of side 'L' and hole 'B' is circle of radius 'R'. If from both the holes same quanitity of water is flowing per second, then side of square hole is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \pi R$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{2 \pi \mathbf{R}}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{2 \pi} \cdot \mathrm{R}$
- **(D)** $\frac{R}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{2 \pi} \cdot \mathrm{R}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The ratio of velocities of water is given by
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{V_{A}}{V_{B}}=\sqrt{\frac{h}{4 h}}=\frac{1}{2} \\
\therefore & V_{B}=2 V_{A}
\end{aligned}$
Quantity of water flowing is same
$\begin{aligned}
\therefore \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{A}} \times \mathrm{L}^{2} &=\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{B}} \times \pi \mathrm{R}^{2} \\
\therefore \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{L}^{2} &=2 \mathrm{~V}_{\mathrm{A}} \times \pi \mathrm{R}^{2} \\
\therefore \quad \mathrm{L}^{2} &=2 \pi \mathrm{R}^{2} \\
\mathrm{~L} &=\sqrt{2 \pi} \cdot \mathrm{R}
\end{aligned}$

</details>
