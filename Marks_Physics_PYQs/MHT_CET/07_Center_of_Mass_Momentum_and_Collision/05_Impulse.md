### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A cricket ball of mass $120 \text{ g}$ is moving with a velocity of $8 \text{ m/s}$ and is hit by a bat so that the ball gets turned back with a velocity of $12 \text{ m/s}$. If the duration of contact between the ball and bat is $0.02 \text{ s}$, the impulse (in $\text{Ns}$) given to the ball by the bat is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.48$
- **(B)** $1.2$
- **(C)** $2.4$
- **(D)** $3.6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2.4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mass of the ball, $m = 120 \text{ g} = 0.12 \text{ kg}$

Taking the direction of the ball after being hit as positive, the initial velocity is $u = -8 \text{ m/s}$ and the final velocity is $v = 12 \text{ m/s}$.

Impulse is equal to the change in momentum of the ball.

$J = \Delta p = m(v - u)$

$J = 0.12(12 - (-8))$

$J = 0.12 \times 20 = 2.4 \text{ Ns}$

Answer: $2.4$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A bullet is fired from a gun. The force on the bullet is $F = (600 - 2 \times 10^5 t)$ N where $t$ is in second. The force on the bullet becomes zero as soon as it leaves the barrel. The average impulse imparted to the bullet is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $9$ N-s
- **(B)** zero
- **(C)** $0.9$ N-s
- **(D)** $1.8$ N-s

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $0.9$ N-s**

**Detailed Solution:**

The force on the bullet becomes zero when it leaves the barrel.

$F = 600 - 2 \times 10^5 t = 0$

$t = \dfrac{600}{2 \times 10^5} = 3 \times 10^{-3}$ s

The impulse imparted to the bullet is given by:

$J = \int_{0}^{t} F dt$

$J = \int_{0}^{3 \times 10^{-3}} (600 - 2 \times 10^5 t) dt$

$J = \left[ 600t - 10^5 t^2 \right]_{0}^{3 \times 10^{-3}}$

$J = 600(3 \times 10^{-3}) - 10^5 (3 \times 10^{-3})^2$

$J = 1.8 - 10^5 (9 \times 10^{-6})$

$J = 1.8 - 0.9 = 0.9$ N-s

Answer: $0.9$ N-s

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Force is applied to a body of mass 3 kg at rest on a frictionless horizontal surface as shown in the force against time (F - t) graph. The speed of the body after 1 s is
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/mhtcet2025/MZzfiTBuvWNmVuk68D58adTQ_dGLDk3ezUctRliwB4k.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $6 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The area under the Force-time ($F-t$) graph gives the impulse, which is equal to the change in momentum of the body.

Impulse $J = \text{Area under the } F-t \text{ graph}$

$J = (8 \times 0.5) + (4 \times 0.5)$

$J = 4 + 2 = 6 \text{ N s}$

According to the impulse-momentum theorem:

$J = \Delta p = m(v - u)$

Given that the body is initially at rest, $u = 0$, and mass $m = 3 \text{ kg}$.

$6 = 3(v - 0)$

$v = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2 \text{ m/s}$

Answer: $2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2022 (05 Aug Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A wall is hit elastically and normally by ' $n$ ' balls per second. All the balls have the same mass ' $m$ ' and are moving with the same velocity ' $u$ '. The force exerted by the balls on the wall is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \mathrm{mnu}^2$
- **(B)** $2 \mathrm{mnu}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{mnu}^2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{mnu}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2 \mathrm{mnu}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The correct option is (B).
Force is related to momentum change by following relation,
$\mathrm{F}=\frac{\mathrm{dp}}{\mathrm{dt}}$
Momentum change per second, $\frac{\mathrm{dp}}{\mathrm{dt}}=\mathrm{n}(\mathrm{mu}-(-\mathrm{mu}))=2 \mathrm{nmu}$
Therefore,
$\mathrm{F}=2 \mathrm{nmu}$

</details>

### Q9. MHT CET 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A ball of mass $0.1 \mathrm{~kg}$ strikes a wall normally with a speed of $30 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ and rebounds with a speed of $20 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. The impulse of the force exerted by the wall on the ball is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $5 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $2 \mathrm{~N}$ -s
- **(D)** $3 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Impulse $=$ Change is momentum $p_{i}-p_{f}$ direction of $p_{f}$ and $p_{i}$ apposite to each other.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \quad & \text { Impulse }=m u-(-m v) \\
&=m u+m v=m(u+v)=0.1(30+20) \\
&=0.1 \times 50=5 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
