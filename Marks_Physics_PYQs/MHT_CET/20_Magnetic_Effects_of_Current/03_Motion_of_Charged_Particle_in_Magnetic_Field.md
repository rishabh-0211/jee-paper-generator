### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two particles X and Y of masses '$m_1$' and '$m_2$' respectively, having equal charges after being accelerated through the same potential difference enter a region of uniform magnetic field and describe circular paths of radii '$r_1$' and '$r_2$' respectively. The ratio of '$r_1$' and '$r_2$' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}\right)^2$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_2}{m_1}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Kinetic energy gained by a charged particle accelerated through a potential difference $V$ is $K = qV$.

The momentum of the particle is $p = \sqrt{2mK} = \sqrt{2mqV}$.

When the particle enters a uniform magnetic field $B$, the radius of the circular path is given by $r = \dfrac{p}{qB}$.

Substituting the value of $p$, we get $r = \dfrac{\sqrt{2mqV}}{qB} = \sqrt{\dfrac{2mV}{qB^2}}$.

Since $V$, $q$, and $B$ are the same for both particles, $r \propto \sqrt{m}$.

Therefore, the ratio of the radii is $\dfrac{r_1}{r_2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}}$.

Answer: $\sqrt{\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}}$

</details>

### Q50. MHT CET 2008

- **(D)** $R$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{R^{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The radius of the orbit in which ions moving is determined by the relation as given below
$$
\frac{m v^{2}}{R}=q v B
$$
where $m$ is the mass, $v$ is velocity, $q$ is charge of ion and $B$ is the flux density of the magnetic field, so that $q v B$ is the magnetic force acting on the ion, and $\frac{m v^{2}}{R}$ is the centripetal force on the ion moving in a curved path of radius $R$.

The angular frequency of rotation of the ions about the vertical field $B$ is given by
$\omega=\frac{v}{R}=\frac{q B}{m}=2 \pi v$
where $v$ is frequency. Energy of ion is given by
or
$$
\begin{aligned}
E &=\frac{1}{2} m v^{2}=\frac{1}{2} m(R \omega)^{2} \\
&=\frac{1}{2} m R^{2} B^{2} \frac{q^{2}}{m^{2}} \\
E &=\frac{1}{2} \frac{R^{2} B^{2} q^{2}}{m}
\end{aligned} 
$$ ... (i)
If ions are accelerated by electric potential $V$, then energy attained by ions
$$
E=q V ...(ii)
$$
From Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$$
q V=\frac{1}{2} \frac{R^{2} B^{2} q^{2}}{m}
$$
or
$$
\frac{q}{m}=\frac{2 V}{R^{2} B^{2}}
$$
If $V$ and $B$ are kept constant, then
$$
\frac{q}{m} \propto \frac{1}{R^{2}}
$$
