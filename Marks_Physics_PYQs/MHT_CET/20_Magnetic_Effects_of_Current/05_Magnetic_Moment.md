### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A uniform conducting wire of length $'12a'$ and resistance $R$ is wound up as a current carrying coil in the shape of an equilateral triangle of side $'a'$ and then a square of side $'a'$. The ratio of magnetic dipole moments of triangular to square coil is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1:\sqrt{3}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{3}:1$
- **(C)** $1:3$
- **(D)** $3:1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $1:\sqrt{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Length of the wire $L = 12a$.

For the equilateral triangular coil of side $a$:
Perimeter of one turn $= 3a$
Number of turns $N_t = \dfrac{12a}{3a} = 4$
Area of the triangular coil $A_t = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2$
Magnetic dipole moment $M_t = N_t I A_t = 4 \times I \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 = \sqrt{3}Ia^2$

For the square coil of side $a$:
Perimeter of one turn $= 4a$
Number of turns $N_s = \dfrac{12a}{4a} = 3$
Area of the square coil $A_s = a^2$
Magnetic dipole moment $M_s = N_s I A_s = 3 \times I \times a^2 = 3Ia^2$

Ratio of magnetic dipole moments:
$\dfrac{M_t}{M_s} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}Ia^2}{3Ia^2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

Answer: $1:\sqrt{3}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A charge $q$ is circulating at a constant speed $v$ with a semicircular loop of radius $r$. The magnetic moment of this loop is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(qv\pi r)/(\pi+2)$
- **(B)** $qvr/3$
- **(C)** $(\pi rqv)/2(\pi+2)$
- **(D)** $qvr$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $(\pi rqv)/2(\pi+2)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The area of the semicircular loop is $A = \dfrac{\pi r^2}{2}$.

The magnetic moment of the loop is $M = I A = \left( \dfrac{qv}{r(\pi + 2)} \right) \left( \dfrac{\pi r^2}{2} \right) = \dfrac{\pi r q v}{2(\pi + 2)}$.

Answer: $(\pi rqv)/2(\pi+2)$

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A thin circular wire carrying current $I$ has magnetic moment $M$. The shape of the wire is changed to a square and it carries the same current. It will have magnetic moment $M_1$. The ratio $M$ to $M_1$ is

**Options:**

- **(B)** $\dfrac{4}{\pi}$

- **(D)** $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

Let the length of the wire be $L$.
