### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In case of 'free expansion', which one of the following statements is 'WRONG'?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is an instantaneous change.
- **(B)** The system is not in thermodynamic equilibrium.
- **(C)** Free expansion can be plotted in a p-v diagram.
- **(D)** It is an uncontrolled change.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Free expansion can be plotted in a p-v diagram.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Free expansion is an irreversible process in which a gas expands into a vacuum. During this process, the gas is not in thermodynamic equilibrium, and its intermediate states are undefined.

Since a p-V diagram can only represent states that are in thermodynamic equilibrium, free expansion cannot be plotted as a continuous curve on a p-V diagram.

The process is also instantaneous and uncontrolled. Therefore, the statement that free expansion can be plotted in a p-V diagram is incorrect.

Answer: Free expansion can be plotted in a p-v diagram.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

At a pressure P and temperature T, 7 gram of oxygen occupies a volume V. The equation of state will be (Molecular weight of Oxygen $= 32$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $PV = \left(\dfrac{2}{7}\right)RT$
- **(B)** $PV = \left(\dfrac{7}{2}\right)RT$
- **(C)** $PV = \left(\dfrac{7}{16}\right)RT$
- **(D)** $PV = \left(\dfrac{7}{32}\right)RT$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $PV = \left(\dfrac{7}{32}\right)RT$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The ideal gas equation is given by $PV = nRT$.

The number of moles $n$ is calculated as $n = \dfrac{m}{M}$.

Given mass of oxygen $m = 7$ g and molecular weight $M = 32$ g/mol.

Therefore, $n = \dfrac{7}{32}$.

Substituting the value of $n$ into the ideal gas equation, we get $PV = \left(\dfrac{7}{32}\right)RT$.

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A balloon is filled at $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and 1 atmospheric pressure by volume $500 \mathrm{~m}^3$ helium gas. At $-3^0 \mathrm{C}$ and 0.5 atmospheric pressure, the volume of helium gas will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $500 \mathrm{~m}^3$
- **(B)** $700 \mathrm{~m}^3$
- **(C)** $900 \mathrm{~m}^3$
- **(D)** $1000 \mathrm{~m}^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $900 \mathrm{~m}^3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the ideal gas equation, $\dfrac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$

Given:
$P_1 = 1 \text{ atm}$, $V_1 = 500 \text{ m}^3$, $T_1 = 27^{\circ}\text{C} = 300 \text{ K}$
$P_2 = 0.5 \text{ atm}$, $T_2 = -3^{\circ}\text{C} = 270 \text{ K}$

Substituting the values:
$\dfrac{1 \times 500}{300} = \dfrac{0.5 \times V_2}{270}$

$V_2 = \dfrac{500 \times 270}{300 \times 0.5}$

$V_2 = \dfrac{5 \times 90}{0.5} = 900 \text{ m}^3$

Answer: $900 \mathrm{~m}^3$

</details>
