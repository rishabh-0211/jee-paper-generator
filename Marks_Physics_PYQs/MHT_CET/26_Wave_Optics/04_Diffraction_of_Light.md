In the diffraction pattern due to a single slit of width '$a$' with incident light of wavelength '$\lambda$', at an angle of diffraction '$\theta$', the condition for the second maximum is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a \sin\theta = 2\lambda$
- **(B)** $2\lambda \sin\theta = 3a$
- **(C)** $5\lambda \sin\theta = 3a$
- **(D)** $2a \sin\theta = 5\lambda$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2a \sin\theta = 5\lambda$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a single slit diffraction pattern, the path difference is given by $\Delta x = a \sin\theta$.

The condition for the $n$-th secondary maximum is $\Delta x = (2n + 1)\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$, where $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$

For the second maximum, $n = 2$.

Substituting $n = 2$, we get $a \sin\theta = (2(2) + 1)\dfrac{\lambda}{2} = \dfrac{5\lambda}{2}$.

This can be rearranged as $2a \sin\theta = 5\lambda$.

Answer: $2a \sin\theta = 5\lambda$

</details>

$\text{Ratio} = \dfrac{\dfrac{\theta}{2}}{\dfrac{q\theta}{2}} = \dfrac{1}{q}$
