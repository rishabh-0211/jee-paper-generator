### Q1. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

An object placed on the ground is in stable equilibrium. If the object is given a slight push, then initially the position of centre of gravity

**Options:**

- **(A)** moves nearer to ground
- **(B)** rises higher above the ground
- **(C)** remains as such
- **(D)** may remain at same level

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) rises higher above the ground**

**Detailed Solution:**

In stable equilibrium, the centre of gravity of object, lies at minimum height from ground. As the object is given a slight push, its centre of gravity rises because it comes in unstable equilibrium.
