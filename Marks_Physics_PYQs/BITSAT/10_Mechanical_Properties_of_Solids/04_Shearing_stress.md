### Q1. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The upper end of a wire of diameter $12 \mathrm{~mm}$ and length $1 \mathrm{~m}$ is clamped and its other end is twisted through an angle of $30^{\circ} .$ The angle of shear is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $0.18^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $36^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $0.36^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.18^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$r \theta=\ell \phi \Rightarrow \phi=\frac{r \theta}{\ell}=\frac{6 \mathrm{~mm} \times 30^{\circ}}{1 \mathrm{~m}}=0.18^{\circ}$

</details>
