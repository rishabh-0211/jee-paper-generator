### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Two wires of the same material (Young's modulus $=Y$ ) and same length $L$ but radii $R$ and $2 R$ respectively, are joined end to end and a weight $w$ is suspended from the combination as shown in the figure. The elastic potential energy in the system is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/2n_SL8fc1mmhV9VeX7B6Vc9oP6D6cgL4XanpcBQnsdc.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3 w^2 L}{4 \pi R^2 Y}$
- **(B)** $\frac{3 w^2 L}{8 \pi R^2 Y}$
- **(C)** $\frac{5 w^2 L}{8 \pi R^2 Y}$
- **(D)** $\frac{w^2 L}{\pi R^2 Y}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{5 w^2 L}{8 \pi R^2 Y}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \Delta l_1=\frac{w L}{\left(4 \pi R^2\right) Y}, \Delta l_2=\frac{w L}{\pi R^2 Y} \\ & \therefore U=\frac{1}{2} K_1\left(\Delta l_1\right)^2+\frac{1}{2} K_2\left(\Delta l_2\right)^2 \\ &=\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{Y\left(4 \pi R^2\right)}{L} \times\left[\frac{w L}{4 \pi R^2 Y}\right]^2+\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{Y\left(\pi R^2\right)}{L} \\ & \times\left[\frac{w L}{\pi R^2 Y}\right]^2\left(\because K=\frac{Y A}{L}\right) \\ &=\frac{5 w^2 L}{8 \pi R^2 Y}\end{aligned}$

</details>
