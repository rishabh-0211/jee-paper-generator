### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

One main scale division of a vernier caliper is equal to $m$ units. If ${m}^{\text {th }}$ division of main scale coincides with $(n+1)^{\text {th }}$ division of vernier scale, the least count of the vernier caliper is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{n}{(n+1)}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{(\mathrm{n}+1)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{(\mathrm{n}+1)}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{n}(\mathrm{n}+1)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{(\mathrm{n}+1)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given 1 Main scale division = m
$\mathrm{n} M S D=(\mathrm{n}+1) \mathrm{VSD}$
$\Rightarrow \quad 1 \mathrm{VSD}=\frac{\mathrm{n}}{\mathrm{n}+1} \mathrm{MSD}$
Least count of vernier caliper $=1 \mathrm{MSD}-1 \mathrm{VSD}$
$\Rightarrow \quad L . C=m-m\left(\frac{n}{n+1}\right)=\left(1-\frac{n}{n+1}\right) m$
$=m\left(\frac{n+1-n}{n+1}\right)=\left(\frac{1}{n+1}\right) m \Rightarrow L \cdot C=\left(\frac{m}{n+1}\right)$

</details>

- **(B)** $x / 4$

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At null point $\frac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}=\frac{R_{3}}{R_{4}}=\frac{x}{100-x}$
