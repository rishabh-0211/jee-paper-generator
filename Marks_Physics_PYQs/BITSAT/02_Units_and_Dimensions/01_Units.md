### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

On a temperature scale ' $X$ '. The boiling point of water is $65^{\circ} \mathrm{X}$ and the freezing point is $-15^{\circ} \mathrm{X}$. Assume that the $\mathrm{X}$ scale is linear. The equivalent temperature corresponding to $-95^{\circ} \mathrm{X}$ on the Farenheit scale would be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-63^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(B)** $-112^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(C)** $-48^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(D)** $-148^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-148^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Boiling point of water, $=$ UFP $65^{\circ}$
Freezing point of water, $=\mathrm{LFP}-15^{\circ}$
$\frac{\mathrm{X}-\mathrm{LFP}}{\mathrm{UFP}-\mathrm{LFP}}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{F}}-32}{212-32}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{-95-(-15)}{65-(-15)}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{F}}-32}{180}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{-80}{80}=\frac{T_F-32}{180} \Rightarrow T_F=-180+32$
$\Rightarrow \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{F}}=-148^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Electric field in a certain region is given by $\vec{E}=\left(\frac{A}{x^2} \hat{i}+\frac{B}{y^3} \hat{j}\right)$. The SI unit of $A$ and $B$ are:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C}^{-1} ; \mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C}^{-1} ; \mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C} ; \mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C} ; \mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C}^{-1} ; \mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electric field in a certain region is given by,
$\vec{E}=\frac{A}{x^2} \hat{i}+\frac{B}{y^3} \hat{j}$
$\left[\frac{\mathrm{A}}{\mathrm{x}^2}\right]=\mathrm{NC}^{-1} \Rightarrow[\mathrm{A}]=\mathrm{Nm}^2 \mathrm{C}^{-1}$
$\left[\frac{\mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{y}^3}\right]=\mathrm{NC}^{-1} \Rightarrow[\mathrm{B}]=\mathrm{Nm}^3 \mathrm{C}^{-1}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The engine of a truck moving along a straight road delivers constant power. The distance travelled by the truck in time $t$ is proportional to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t$
- **(B)** $t^{2}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\mathrm{t}}$
- **(D)** $t^{3 / 2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $t^{3 / 2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{\mathrm{mv}^{2}}{\mathrm{t}}=\frac{\mathrm{ms}^{2}}{\mathrm{t}^{3}}$

since both $\mathrm{P}$ and $\mathrm{m}$ are constants

$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{s}^{2}}{\mathrm{t}^{3}}=$ constant

</details>
