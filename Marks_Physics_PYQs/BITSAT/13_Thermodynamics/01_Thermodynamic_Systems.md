### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

On celcius scale the temperature of body increases by $40^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The increase in temperature on Fahrenheit scale is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $70^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(B)** $68^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(C)** $72^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$
- **(D)** $75^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $72^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since $\frac{F-32}{9}=\frac{C}{5}$
$\Rightarrow \Delta \mathrm{C}=\frac{5}{9} \Delta \mathrm{~F}$
$\Rightarrow 40=\frac{5}{9} \Delta \mathrm{~F} \Rightarrow \Delta \mathrm{~F}=72^{\circ} \mathrm{F}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The compressibility of water is $4 \times 10^{-5}$ per unit atmospheric pressure. The decrease in volume of $100 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$ of water under a pressure of 100 atmosphere will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.4 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$
- **(B)** $4 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$
- **(C)** $0.025 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$
- **(D)** $0.004 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $0.4 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Compressibility $=\frac{1}{\mathrm{~B}}=\frac{\Delta \mathrm{V} / \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{P}}$.

Here, $\mathrm{P}=100 \mathrm{~atm}$

Compressibility $=4 \times 10^{-5}$ and $\mathrm{V}=100 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$

Hence, $\Delta \mathrm{V}=0.4 \mathrm{~cm}^{3}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A solid body of constant heat capacity $1 \mathrm{~J} /{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is being heated by keeping it in contact with reservoirs in two ways:
(i) Sequentially keeping in contact with 2 reservoirs such that each reservoir supplies same amount of heat.
(ii) Sequentially keeping in contact with 8 reservoirs such that each reservoir supplies same amount of heat. 
In both the cases body is brought from initial temperature $100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to final temperature $200^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Entropy change of the body in the two cases respectively is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\ln 2,2 \ln 2$
- **(B)** $2 \ln 2,8 \ln 2$
- **(C)** $\ln 2,4 \ln 2$
- **(D)** $\ln 2, \ln 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\ln 2, \ln 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The entropy change of the body in the two cases is same as entropy is a state function.

</details>
