### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A gas can be taken from A to B via two different processes $A C B$ and $A D B$. When path $A C B$ is used 60 J of heat flows into the system and 30J of work is done by the system. If path ADB is used work done by the system is 10 J . The heat Flow into the system in path ADB is :
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/BTgnsQiDXhMQKUjqaE8rwJ3EgYJiO9S8_MGxWHRyZVw.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** 40 J
- **(B)** 80 J
- **(C)** 100 J
- **(D)** 20 J

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 40 J**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\triangle \mathrm{U}$ remains same for both paths ACB and ADB
$\Delta Q_{A C B}=\Delta W_{A C B}+\Delta U_{A C B}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/cfGnB_R9pifjj0rf8sfOj_3iHZgf3N86v-T3w80prTw.original.fullsize.png"/>
$\Rightarrow 60 \mathrm{~J}=30 \mathrm{~J}+\Delta \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{ACB}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{ACB}}=30 \mathrm{~J}$
As change in internal energy depends only on initial and final point
$\therefore \Delta \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{ADB}}=\Delta \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{ACB}}=30 \mathrm{~J}$
$\Delta \mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{ADB}}=\Delta \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{ADB}}+\Delta \mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{ADB}}=10 \mathrm{~J}+30 \mathrm{~J}=40 \mathrm{~J}$

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A source supplies heat to a system at the rate of 1000 W . If the system performs work at a rate of 200 W . The rate at which internal energy of the system increases

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1200 W
- **(B)** 600 W
- **(C)** 500 W
- **(D)** 800 W

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 800 W**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given,
Rate of heat supplied, $\frac{\mathrm{dQ}}{\mathrm{dt}}=1000 \mathrm{~W}$
Rate of work performed, $\frac{\mathrm{dw}}{\mathrm{dt}}=200 \mathrm{~W}$
Using first law of thermodynamics
$d Q=d U+d w$
\(\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dU}}{\mathrm{dt}}=\frac{\mathrm{dQ}}{\mathrm{dt}}-\frac{\mathrm{dw}}{\mathrm{dt}} \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dU}}{\mathrm{dt}}=1000-200=800\)

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In a thermodynamic process, the pressure of a fixed mass of a gas is changed in such a manner that the gas releases $20 \mathrm{~J}$ of heat and $8 \mathrm{~J}$ of work is done on the gas. If the initial internal energy of the gas was $30 \mathrm{~J}$, then the final internal energy will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(B)** $42 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(C)** $18 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(D)** $58 \mathrm{~J}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $18 \mathrm{~J}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\Delta \mathrm{Q}=-20 \mathrm{~J}, \mathrm{~W}=-8 \mathrm{~J}$

Using Ist law $\Delta \mathrm{Q}=\Delta \mathrm{U}+\Delta \mathrm{W}$

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow \Delta Q=-20+8=-12 \mathrm{~J} \\

\mathrm{Uf}=-12+30=18 \mathrm{~J}

\end{array}

$$

</details>
