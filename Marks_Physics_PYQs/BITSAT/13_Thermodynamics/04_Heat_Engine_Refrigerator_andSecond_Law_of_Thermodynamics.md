### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A Carnot's heat engine works between the temperatures $427^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. What amount of heat should it consume per second to deliver mechanical work at the rate of $1.0 \mathrm{~kW} ?$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.417 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $4.17 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $41.7 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $0.212 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $0.417 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The efficiency of the heat engine is

$$

\eta=1-\frac{T_{2}}{T_{1}}=1-\left(\frac{273+27 K}{273+427 K}\right)=\frac{4}{7}

$$

But $\quad \eta=\frac{\mathrm{W}}{\mathrm{Q}_{1}}$

$$

\therefore \mathrm{Q}_{1}=\frac{\mathrm{W}}{\eta}=\frac{1.0 \mathrm{~kW}}{4 / 7}=1.75 \mathrm{~kW}=0.417 \mathrm{kcal} / \mathrm{s}

$$

Thus, the engine would require 417 cal of heat per second, to deliver the requisite amount of work.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

An ideal refrigerator has a freezer at a temperature of $13^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The coefficient of performance of the engine is $5 .$ The temperature of the air (to which heat is rejected) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $320^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $39^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $325 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(D)** $325^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $39^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{T}_{2}=273-13=260$,
$$
\mathrm{K}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_{2}}{\mathrm{~T}_{1}-\mathrm{T}_{2}} ; \quad 5=\frac{260}{\mathrm{~T}_{1}-260}
$$
or $\mathrm{T}_{1}-260=52 ; \quad \mathrm{T}_{1}=312 \mathrm{~K}$
$\mathrm{T}_{2}=312-273=39^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

</details>
