### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A bowl filled with very hot soup cools from $98^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $86^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in 2 minutes when the room temperature is $22^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. How long it will take to cool from $75^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $69^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2 minutes
- **(B)** 1.4 minutes
- **(C)** 0.5 minute
- **(D)** 1 minute

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1.4 minutes**

**Detailed Solution:**

The rate of heat flow is given by
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}=-K\left(T-T_0\right) \Rightarrow \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}=-K\left(T_{a v g}-T_0\right) \\
& \text { Initially } T_{a v g}=\frac{T_1+T_2}{2} \\
& \text { Here, } T_1=98^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\
& T_2=86^{\circ} \mathrm{C}
\end{aligned}$
$\begin{aligned} & \text { (i) } \frac{\mathrm{ms} \times 12}{2}=-\mathrm{K}\left(\frac{98+86}{2}-22\right) \\ & 6=-\frac{\mathrm{K}}{\mathrm{ms}}\left[\frac{98+86}{2}-22\right] \quad(\because \Delta \mathrm{Q}=\mathrm{ms} \Delta \mathrm{T})\end{aligned}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/m-qxoSp3pUGTs9hNmklcIiM0y5wsfF4lw7tCcyRjGXc.original.fullsize.png">

Now, cool from $\mathrm{T}_1^{\prime}=75^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{T}_2=69^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\
& \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_1^{\prime}+\mathrm{T}_2^{\prime}}{2}=\frac{75+69}{2}
\end{aligned}$
(ii) $\frac{\mathrm{ms} \times 6}{\Delta \mathrm{t}}=-\mathrm{K}\left(\frac{75+69}{2}-22\right)$<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/bQrJV0HZxZIN7yecVk6HwfeL4MYFknI9xPA7NGCSfTw.original.fullsize.png">

Divide equation (ii) by (i), we have
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{6}{\Delta t(6)}=\frac{50}{70} \\
& \Delta t=\frac{7}{5}=1.4 \mathrm{~min}
\end{aligned}$

### Q4. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the radius of a star is $R$ and it acts as a black body, what would be the temperature of the star, in which the rate of energy production is $Q$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $Q / 4 \pi R^{2} \sigma$
- **(B)** $\left(\mathrm{Q} / 4 \pi R^{2} \sigma\right)^{-1 / 2}$
- **(C)** $\left(4 \pi R^{2} Q / \sigma\right)^{1 / 4}$
- **(D)** $\left(\mathrm{Q} / 4 \pi R^{2} \sigma\right)^{1 / 4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(\mathrm{Q} / 4 \pi R^{2} \sigma\right)^{1 / 4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Stefan's law for black bodyradiation $Q=\sigma e A T^{4}$

$$

T=\left[\frac{Q}{\sigma\left(4 \pi R^{2}\right)}\right]^{1 / 4}

$$

Here $e=1$

$$

A=4 \pi R^{2}

$$

### Q5. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A copper sphere cools from $62^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in 10 minutes and to $42^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in the next 10 minutes. Calculate the temperature of the surroundings.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18.01^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $26^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $10.6^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $20^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $26^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

By Newton's law of cooling, $\frac{\theta_{1}-\theta_{2}}{t}=-k\left[\frac{\theta_{1}+\theta_{2}}{2}-\theta_{0}\right]$ ....(1)

A sphere cools from $62^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in $10 \mathrm{~min}$. $\frac{62-50}{10}=-\mathrm{k}\left[\frac{62+50}{2}-\theta_{0}\right]$ ....(2)
Now, sphere cools from $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $42^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in next $10 \mathrm{~min} .$
$$
\frac{50-42}{10}=-\mathrm{k}\left[\frac{50+42}{2}-\theta_{0}\right] ....(3)
$$
Dividing eq $^{\text {n }}$. (2) by (3) we get, $\frac{56-\theta_{0}}{46-\theta_{0}}=\frac{1.2}{0.8}$ or $0.4 \theta_{0}=10.4$
Hence $\theta_{0}=26^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

</details>
