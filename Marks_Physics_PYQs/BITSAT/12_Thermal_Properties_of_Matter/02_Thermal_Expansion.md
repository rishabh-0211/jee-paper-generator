### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A solid metallic cube having total surface area $24 \mathrm{~m}^2$ is uniformly heated. If its temperature is increased by $10^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, calculate the increase in volume of the cube.
$\left(\right.$ Given : $\left.\alpha=5.0 \times 10^{-4}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}^{-1}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2.4 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~cm}^3$
- **(B)** $1.2 \times 10^5 \mathrm{~cm}^3$
- **(C)** $6.0 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~cm}^3$
- **(D)** $4.8 \times 10^5 \mathrm{~cm}^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $1.2 \times 10^5 \mathrm{~cm}^3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $\Delta V=V_0 \gamma \Delta T$
$\Delta V=a^3,(3 \alpha) \Delta T$
Now, $6 a^2=24 \quad[\because$ Total surface area of cube $\left.=6 a^2\right]$
$\Rightarrow a^2=4 \Rightarrow a=2$
So, $\Delta V=2^3\left(3 \times 5 \times 10^{-4}\right) \times 10=1200 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~m}^3$
$=1200 \times 10^2 \mathrm{~cm}^3=1.2 \times 10^5 \mathrm{~cm}^3$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A crystal has a coefficient of expansion $13 \times 10^{-7}$ in one direction and $231 \times 10^{-7}$ in every direction at right angles to it. Then the cubical coefficient of expansion is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $462 \times 10^{-7}$
- **(B)** $244 \times 10^{-7}$
- **(C)** $475 \times 10^{-7}$
- **(D)** $257 \times 10^{-7}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $475 \times 10^{-7}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \gamma &=\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3} \\ &=13 \times 10^{-7}+231 \times 10^{-7}+231 \times 10^{-7} \\ &=475 \times 10^{-7} \end{aligned}$

</details>
