### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A long straight wire along the $Z$ -axis carries a current I in the negative $Z$ -direction. The

magnetic vector field $\vec{B}$ at a point having coordinates $(x, y)$ in the $Z=0$ plane is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mu_{0} I(y \hat{i}-x \hat{j})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mu_{0} I(x \hat{i}+y \hat{j})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mu_{0} I(x \hat{j}-y \hat{i})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mu_{0} I(x \hat{i}-y \hat{j})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{\mu_{0} I(y \hat{i}-x \hat{j})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The wire carries a current $I$ in the negative $z$ -direction. We have to consider the

magnetic vector field $\vec{B}$ at $(x, y)$ in the $z=0$ plane.

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/USmKK_7_XG-H5CPMkrNdWUbIbHIBnjlAwdJk7tBK1OY.original.fullsize.png">


Magnetic field $\vec{B}$ is perpendicular to $O P$ $\therefore \vec{B}=B \sin \theta \hat{i}-B \cos \theta \hat{j}$

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\sin \theta=\frac{y}{r}, \cos \theta=\frac{x}{r} B=\frac{\mu_{0} I}{2 \pi r} \\

\therefore \quad \vec{B}=\frac{\mu_{0} I}{2 \pi r^{2}}(y \hat{i}-x \hat{j})

\end{array}

$$

or $\quad \vec{B}=\frac{\mu_{0} I(y \hat{i}-x \hat{j})}{2 \pi\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}$

</details>
