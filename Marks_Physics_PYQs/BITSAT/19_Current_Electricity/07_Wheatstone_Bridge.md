### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Calculate the effective resistance between A and B in following network.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/YJpQE-eDGbLQZckszdzWZVTi0105gSwFyaai0-oGDk0.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 \Omega$
- **(B)** $10 \Omega$
- **(C)** $20 \Omega$
- **(D)** $30 \Omega$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $20 \Omega$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equivalent resistance
$$
\begin{array}{l}
=(5+10+15) \|(10+20+30) \\
\text { So, } R_{\text {eq }}=\frac{30 \times 60}{30+60}=20 \Omega
\end{array}
$$
