### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In the given circuit, $E_1=E_2=E_3=2 \mathrm{~V}$ and $R_1=R_2=4 \Omega$, then current flowing through the branch $A B$ is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/ezyKE-IOTRn0fQtjMrQqefXc54BO8ateC8lfj65ElqU.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** zero
- **(B)** $2 A$ from $A$ and $B$
- **(C)** $2 A$ from $B$ to $A$
- **(D)** $5 A$ from $B$ to $B$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2 A$ from $A$ and $B$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given circuit can be redrawn as,
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/XnqLnlC7H6t4kzVS6Mk9vAr4QceBEtSCi9RQdPqWDO8.original.fullsize.png"/>
where, equivalent emf of the combination,
$\begin{aligned}
E_{\text {eq }} & =\frac{E_1 R_1+E_2 R_2}{R_1+R_2} \\
& =\frac{2 \times 4+2 \times 4}{4+4}=2 \mathrm{~V}
\end{aligned}$
and $R_{e q}=\frac{R_1 R_2}{R_1+R_2}=\frac{4 \times 4}{4+4}=\frac{16}{8}=2 \Omega$
Net emf of the circuit,
$\begin{aligned}
E^{\prime} & =E_2+E_{\mathrm{eq}} \\
& =2+2 \\
& =4 \mathrm{~V}
\end{aligned}$
$\therefore$ Current flowing through branch $A B$,
$I=\frac{E^{\prime}}{R_{e q}}=\frac{4}{2}=2 \mathrm{~A}$
Clearly from figure, it is clear that direction of current is from point $A$ to $B$ clockwise is loop.

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Five cells each of emf $E$ and internal resistance $r$ send the same amount of current through an external resistance $R$ whether the cells are connected in parallel or in series. Then the ratio $\left(\frac{R}{r}\right)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{5}$
- **(D)** $1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given : Number of cells, $n=5$, emf of each cell $=E$
Internal resistance of each cell $=r$
In series, current through resistance $R$
$I=\frac{n E}{n r+R}=\frac{5 E}{5 r+R}$
In parallel, current through resistance $R$
$I^{\prime}=\frac{E}{\frac{r}{n}+R}=\frac{n E}{r+n R}=\frac{5 E}{r+5 R}$
According to question, $I=I^{\prime}$
$\therefore \frac{5 E}{5 r+5 R}=\frac{5 E}{r+5 R} \Rightarrow 5 r+R=r+5 R$
or $R=r \quad \therefore \frac{R}{r}=1$

</details>
