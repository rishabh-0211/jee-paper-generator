### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

When a light ray incidents on the surface of a medium, the reflected ray is completely polarized. Then the angle between reflected and refracted rays is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $45^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $90^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $120^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $180^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $90^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Brewester's law if the reflected ray is completely polarized, then the reflected and refracted ray is perpendicular to each other.
Thus, $\theta=90^{\circ}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Find the final intensity of light (I"), if the angle between the axes of two polaroids is $60^{\circ}$.

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/F85G7OkNKzViqzZ2bu3CPqzm_WQWtzvg3XO4_m1fGKU.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3 \mathrm{I}_{0}}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{4}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{8}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{8}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the first polaroid

$$

\mathrm{I}^{\prime}=\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{2}

$$

From second polaroid

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{I}^{\prime \prime} &=\mathrm{I}^{\prime} \cos ^{2} \theta=\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{2} \cos ^{2}\left(60^{\circ}\right) \\

&=\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{2} \times\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}=\frac{\mathrm{I}_{0}}{8}

\end{aligned}

$$

</details>
