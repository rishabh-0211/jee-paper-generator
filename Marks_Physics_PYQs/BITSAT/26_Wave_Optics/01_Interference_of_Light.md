### Q1. BITSAT 2019

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two monochromatic light waves are travelling with same frequency and constant phase difference. If both waves interfere, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** there is a gain of energy
- **(B)** there is a loss of energy
- **(C)** the energy is redistributed and the distribution remains constant in time
- **(D)** the energy is redistributed and the distribution changes with time

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) the energy is redistributed and the distribution remains constant in time**

**Detailed Solution:**

When two waves with same frequency and constant phase difference interfere to each other, then after interference, intensity of resultant wave is distributed such that it is maximum at some points [constructive interference or bright fringes] and minimum at another points. [destructive interference or dark fringes]. Hence, energy is distributed and remains constant [in the form of bright and dark fringes]

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

When a mica sheet of thickness 7 microns and $\mu=1.6$ is placed in the path of one of interfering beams in the biprism experiment then the central fringe gets at the position of seventh bright fringe. The wavelength of light used will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4000 \mathrm{~A}$
- **(B)** $5000 \AA$
- **(C)** $6000 \mathrm{~A}$
- **(D)** $7000 \AA$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $6000 \mathrm{~A}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\lambda=\frac{(\mu-1) \mathrm{t}}{\mathrm{n}}$
According to question
$$
\mathrm{n}=7, \mu=1.6, \mathrm{t}=7 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~meter}
$$
From eqs. (1) and $(2), \lambda=6 \times 10^{-7}$ meter

</details>
