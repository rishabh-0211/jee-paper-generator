### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Three bricks each of length $L$ and mass $M$ are arranged as shown from the wall. The distance of the centre of mass of the system from the wall is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/qMdxJfwjUdkLzDUsPKM0YdQXmw-xVZisZyfqigQuYY8.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{L}}{4}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{L}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\left(\frac{3}{2}\right) \mathrm{L}$
- **(D)** $\left(\frac{11}{12}\right) \mathrm{L}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(\frac{11}{12}\right) \mathrm{L}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/u7DJxTmuY-PRriQ_WqzP3NpTSxNbz31Yer_aINc-e1s.original.fullsize.png">


$x_{1}=\frac{\mathrm{L}}{2}, x_{2}=\mathrm{L}, x_{3}=\frac{5 \mathrm{~L}}{4}$

$\therefore x_{\mathrm{CM}}=\frac{m_{1} x_{1}+m_{2} x_{2}+m_{3} x_{3}}{m_{1}+m_{2}+m_{3}}$

$=\frac{\mathrm{M} \times \frac{\mathrm{L}}{2}+\mathrm{M} \times \mathrm{L}+\mathrm{M} \times \frac{5 \mathrm{~L}}{4}}{\mathrm{M}+\mathrm{M}+\mathrm{M}}=\frac{11 \mathrm{~L}}{12}$

</details>
