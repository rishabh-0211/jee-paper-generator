### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Two bodies are having kinetic energies in the ratio $16: 9$. If they have same linear momentum, the ratio of their masses respectively is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4 : 3
- **(B)** 3 : 4
- **(C)** 16 : 9
- **(D)** 9 : 16

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 9 : 16**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \text { K.E. }=\frac{\mathrm{p}^2}{2 \mathrm{~m}} \\ & \therefore \frac{\mathrm{K}_1}{\mathrm{~K}_2}=\frac{\mathrm{p}_1^2}{2 \mathrm{~m}_1} \times \frac{2 \mathrm{~m}_2}{\mathrm{p}_2^2}=\frac{\mathrm{m}_2}{\mathrm{~m}_1}=\frac{16}{9} \\ & \therefore \frac{\mathrm{m}_1}{\mathrm{~m}_2}=\frac{9}{16}\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A nucleus splits into two nuclear parts which have their velocity ratio equal to $2: 1 .$ What will be the ratio of their nuclear radius?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2^{1 / 3}: 1$
- **(B)** $1: 2^{1 / 3}$
- **(C)** $3^{1 / 2}: 1$
- **(D)** $1: 3^{1 / 2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $1: 2^{1 / 3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

As momentum is conserved, therefore

$\frac{\mathrm{m}_{1}}{\mathrm{~m}_{2}}=\frac{\mathrm{A}_{1}}{\mathrm{~A}_{2}}=\frac{\mathrm{v}_{2}}{\mathrm{v}_{1}}=\frac{1}{2}$

$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{R}_{1}}{\mathrm{R}_{2}}=\left(\frac{\mathrm{A}_{1}}{\mathrm{~A}_{2}}\right)^{1 / 3}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{1 / 3}=1: 2^{1 / 3}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A rifle man, who together with his rifle has a mass of $100 \mathrm{~kg}$, stands on a smooth surface and fires 10 shots horizontally. Each bullet has a mass $10 \mathrm{~g}$ and a muzzle velocity of $800 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. The velocity which the rifle man attains after firing 10 shots is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $0.8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $0.08 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $-0.8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to law of conservation of momentum,

$$

\begin{array}{l}

100 \mathrm{v}=-\frac{10}{1000} \times 10 \times 800 \\

\text { i.e., } \mathrm{v}=0.8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}

\end{array}

$$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A man of mass $100 \mathrm{~kg}$. is standing on a platform of mass $200 \mathrm{~kg}$. which is kept on a smooth ice surface. If the man starts moving on the platform with a speed $30 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$ relative to the platform then calculate with what velocity relative to the ice the platform will recoil?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$
- **(B)** $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$
- **(C)** $15 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$
- **(D)** $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Consider the situation shown in figure.Suppose the man moves at a speed w towards right and the the platform recoils at a speed \(\mathrm{V}\) towards left, both relative to the ice. Hence, the speed of the man relative to the platform is
\(\mathrm{V}+\mathrm{w}\). By the question,
\(\mathrm{V}+\mathrm{w}=\mathrm{v}\), or \(\mathrm{w}=\mathrm{v}-\mathrm{V} \ldots\) (i)
Taking the platform and the man to be the system, there is no external horizontal force on the system. The linear momentum of the system remains constant. Initially both the man and the platform were at rest. Thus,
\(0=\mathrm{MV}-\underset{m v}{\operatorname{mor}} \mathrm{MV}=\mathrm{m}(\mathrm{v}-\mathrm{V})[\mathrm{Using}(\mathrm{i})]\)
or, \(V=\frac{m v}{M+m}\)
Hence, the velocity relative to the ice is \(V=\frac{m v}{M+m}\).

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A spring is compressed between two toy carts of mass $\mathrm{m}_{1}$ and $\mathrm{m}_{2}$. When the toy carts are released, the springs exert equal and opposite average forces for the same time on each toy cart. If $v_{1}$ and $\mathrm{v}_{2}$ are the velocities of the toy carts and there is no friction between the toy carts and the ground, then :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{v}_{1} / \mathrm{v}_{2}=\mathrm{m}_{1} / \mathrm{m}_{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{v}_{1} / \mathrm{v}_{2}=\mathrm{m}_{2} / \mathrm{m}_{1}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{v}_{1} / \mathrm{v}_{2}=-\mathrm{m}_{2} / \mathrm{m}_{1}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{v}_{1} / \mathrm{v}_{2}=-\mathrm{m}_{1} / \mathrm{m}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{v}_{1} / \mathrm{v}_{2}=-\mathrm{m}_{2} / \mathrm{m}_{1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Applying law of conservation of linear momentum
$\mathrm{m}_{1} \mathrm{v}_{1}+\mathrm{m}_{2} \mathrm{v}_{2}=0$
$\frac{\mathrm{m}_{1}}{\mathrm{~m}_{2}}=-\frac{\mathrm{v}_{2}}{\mathrm{v}_{1}}$ or $\frac{\mathrm{v}_{1}}{\mathrm{v}_{2}}=-\frac{\mathrm{m}_{2}}{\mathrm{~m}_{1}}$

</details>
