### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The effect of increase in temperature on the number of electrons in conduction band $\left(\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{e}}\right)$ and resistance of a semiconductor will be as:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both $\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{e}}$ and resistance decrease
- **(B)** Both $\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{e}}$ and resistance increase
- **(C)** $\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{e}}$ increases, resistance decreases
- **(D)** $n_e$ decreases, resistance increases

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{e}}$ increases, resistance decreases**

**Detailed Solution:**

When temperature increases, more electrons excite to conduction band and hence conductivity increases, therefore resistance decreases.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2018

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

At a certain temperature, the number density of charge carriers in a semiconductor is <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math>. When an electric field is applied to it, the charge carriers drift with an average speed <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math>. If the temperature of the semiconductor is raised.

**Options:**

- **(A)** <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> will increase but <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math> will decrease
- **(B)** <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> will decrease but <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math> will increase
- **(C)** Both <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> and <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math> will increase
- **(D)** Both <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> and <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math> will decrease

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> will increase but <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math> will decrease**

**Detailed Solution:**

On raising the temperature of the semiconductor, covalent bonds start breaking up and thus more charge carriers release. This will increase the value of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>n</mi></math> and as a result the rate of collision of charge carriers will increase. This will decrease the drift speed <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>v</mi></math>.

</details>
