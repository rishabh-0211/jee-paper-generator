### Q1. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

For a CE transistor amplifier, the audio signal voltage across the collector resistance of $2.0 \mathrm{k} \Omega$ is $2.0 \mathrm{~V}$. Suppose the current amplification factor of the transistor is 100 , What should be the value of $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{B}}$ in series with $\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{BB}}$ supply of $2.0 \mathrm{~V}$ if the dc base current has to be 10 times the signal current?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $14 \mathrm{k} \Omega$
- **(B)** $18 \mathrm{k} \Omega$
- **(C)** $10 \mathrm{k} \Omega$
- **(D)** $5 \mathrm{k} \Omega$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $14 \mathrm{k} \Omega$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The output ac voltage is $2.0 \mathrm{~V}$. So, the ac collector current $\mathrm{i}_{\mathrm{C}}=2.0 / 2000=1.0 \mathrm{~mA}$.

The signal current through the base is, therefore given by $\mathrm{i}_{\mathrm{B}}=\mathrm{i}_{\mathrm{C}} / \beta=1.0 \mathrm{~mA} / 100=0.010 \mathrm{~mA}$

The dc base current has to be $10 \times 0.010$ $=0.10 \mathrm{~mA}$

$$

\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{B}}=\left(\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{BB}}-\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{BE}}\right) / \mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{B}}

$$

Assuming $\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{BE}}=0.6 \mathrm{~V}, \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{B}}=(2.0 .0 .6) / 0.10$

$=14 \mathrm{k} \Omega$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

An oscillator is nothing but an amplifer with

**Options:**

- **(A)** positive feedback
- **(B)** negative feedback
- **(C)** large gain
- **(D)** no feedback

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) positive feedback**

**Detailed Solution:**

A positive feedback from output to input in an amplifier provides oscillations of constant amplitude.

</details>
