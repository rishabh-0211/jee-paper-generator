### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For an insulator, the forbidden energy gap is

**Options:**

- **(A)** Zero
- **(B)** $1 \mathrm{eV}$
- **(C)** $2 \mathrm{eV}$
- **(D)** $5 \mathrm{eV}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $5 \mathrm{eV}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For an insulator, $E_{\mathrm{g}}>3 \mathrm{eV}$, that is why electron transition from valence band to conduction band is not possible. For semiconductor $E_{\mathrm{g}}$ is $0.2 \mathrm{eV}$ to $0.3 \mathrm{eV}$, while for metals $E_g>5 \mathrm{eV}$.

</details>
