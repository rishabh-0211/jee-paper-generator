### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A body which is initially at rest at a height $R$ above the surface of the Earth of radius R, falls freely towards the Earth, then its velocity on reaching the surface of the Earth is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{(2 g R)}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{(g R)}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\frac{3}{2} g R}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{(4 g R)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sqrt{(g R)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Increase in kinetic energy = Decrease in potential energy
$\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \quad \frac{1}{2} m v^2=\frac{m g R}{1+\frac{R}{R}}=\frac{m g R}{2} \quad\left(\Delta U=\frac{m g h}{1+\frac{h}{R}}, h=R\right) \\
& \Rightarrow \quad m v^2=m g R \Rightarrow v=\sqrt{g R}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The kinetic energy of a satellite in its orbit around the earth is E . What should be the kinetic energy of the satellite so as to enable it to escape from the gravitational pull of the earth?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 E$
- **(B)** 2 E
- **(C)** $\sqrt{2} \mathrm{E}$
- **(D)** E

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 2 E**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know that $v_e=\sqrt{2} v_0$, where $\mathrm{v}_0$ is orbital velocity.
K.E. in the orbit, $E=\frac{1}{2} M v_0{ }^2$
K.E. to escape $E=\frac{1}{2} M v_e^2=\frac{1}{2} M\left(2 v_0{ }^2\right)$
$=\frac{1}{2} M v_0^2 \times 2=2 E$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\mathrm{V}$ is the gravitational potential due to sphere of uniform density on it's surface, then it's value at the center of sphere will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3 V}{2}$
- **(B)** V
- **(C)** $\frac{4}{3} \mathrm{~V}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{V}}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{3 V}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Potential due to sphere
$\mathrm{V}=\frac{\mathrm{GM}}{2 \mathrm{R}^3}\left(3 \mathrm{R}^2-\mathrm{r}^2\right)$
At surface $r=R \Rightarrow V=\left(\frac{G M}{R}\right)$
At centre, $r=0$
$\therefore \mathrm{V}_0=\frac{3 \mathrm{GM}}{2 \mathrm{R}}=\left(\frac{3 \mathrm{~V}}{2}\right)$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The potential energy of a satellite of mass $\mathrm{m}$ and revolving at a height $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}$ above the surface of earth where $\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}=$ radius of earth, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-\mathrm{m} \mathrm{g} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{-\mathrm{mg} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{-\mathrm{mg} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{-\mathrm{mg} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{-\mathrm{mg} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At a height h above the surface of earth the gravitational potential energy of the particle of mass m is $\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{h}}=-\frac{\mathrm{GM}_{\mathrm{e}} \mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}+\mathrm{h}}$

Where $M_{e} \& R_{e}$ are the mass $\&$ radius of earth respectively. In this question, since $\mathrm{h}=\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{e}}$ 

So $U_{h-R_{e}}=-\frac{G M_{e} m}{2 R_{e}}=\frac{-m g R_{e}}{2}$

</details>

$=\frac{\mathrm{R} 2 \pi}{2 \pi \sqrt{\mathrm{R} / \mathrm{g}}}=\sqrt{\mathrm{gR}}=\frac{\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{e}}}{\sqrt{2}}$

</details>
