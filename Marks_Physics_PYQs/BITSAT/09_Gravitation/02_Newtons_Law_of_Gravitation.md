### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two identical particles each of mass ' $m$ ' go round a circle of radius $a$ under the action of their mutual gravitational attraction. The angular speed of each particle will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{\frac{G m}{2 a^3}}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{Gm}}{8 \mathrm{a}^3}}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\frac{G m}{4 a^3}}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{\frac{G m}{a^3}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{\frac{G m}{4 a^3}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Radius of circle, $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{a}$
Centrifugal force, $F=m \omega^2 r$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{Gmm}}{(2 \mathrm{a})^2}=\mathrm{m} \omega^2 \mathrm{a} \\
& \left(\because \mathrm{F}=\frac{\mathrm{G} \mathrm{M} \mathrm{M}_2}{\mathrm{~d}}\right)
\end{aligned}$
Here $M_1=m ; M_2=m$ and $d=2 a$
$\Rightarrow$ angular speed, $\omega=\sqrt{\frac{G m}{4 a^3}}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Two planets $A$ and $B$ of radii $R$ and $1.5 R$ have densities $\rho$ and $\rho / 2$ respectively. The ratio of acceleration due to gravity at the surface of $B$ to A is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2: 3$
- **(B)** $2: 1$
- **(C)** $3: 4$
- **(D)** $4: 3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $3: 4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acceleration due to gravity,
$\mathrm{g}=\frac{\mathrm{GM}}{\mathrm{R}^2}=\frac{4}{3} \pi \mathrm{G} \rho \mathrm{R}$
$\therefore \frac{g_2}{g_1}=\frac{\rho_2}{\rho_1} \times \frac{R_2}{R_1}=\frac{1}{2} \times 1.5=\frac{3}{4}$

</details>
