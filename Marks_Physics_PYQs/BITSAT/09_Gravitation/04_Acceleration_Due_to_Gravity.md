### Q1. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the radius of the earth were to shrink by one per cent, its mass remaining the same, the value of g on the earth's surface would

**Options:**

- **(A)** increase by $0.5 \%$
- **(B)** increase by $2 \%$
- **(C)** decrease by $0.5 \%$
- **(D)** decrease by $2 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) increase by $2 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\quad \mathrm{g}=\frac{\mathrm{GM}}{\mathrm{R}^{2}} \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dg}}{\mathrm{g}}=-2 \frac{\mathrm{dR}}{\mathrm{R}}$
$\frac{\mathrm{dR}}{\mathrm{R}}=-1 \% \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dg}}{\mathrm{g}}=2 \%$

</details>
