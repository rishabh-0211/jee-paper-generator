### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

An oil drop of radius $1 \mu \mathrm{~m}$ is held stationary under a constant electric field of $3.65 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{C}$ due to some excess electrons presence on it. If the density of oil drop is $1.26 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3$, then number of excess electrons on the oil drop approximately are
[Take, $g=10 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$ ]

**Options:**

- **(A)** 7
- **(B)** 12
- **(C)** 9
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 9**

**Detailed Solution:**

$E=3.65 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{C}, r=1 \mu \mathrm{~m}=10^{-6} \mathrm{~m}$
$\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\text {oil }} & =1.26 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{cm}^3 \\
& =1.26 \times 10^3 \mathrm{~kg} / \mathrm{m}^3
\end{aligned}$
Since, droplet is stationary hence weight of droplet $=$ force due to electric field
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 \cdot \rho_{\text {oil }} \cdot g=q E...(i)$
If $n$ be the number of excess electrons in the oil drop, then
$q=n e$
Hence, from Eq. (i), $\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 \rho_{\text {oil }} \cdot g=n e E$
$\begin{aligned}
\Rightarrow n & =\frac{4 \pi r^3 \rho_{\text {oill }} g}{3 e E} \\
& =\frac{4 \times 3.14 \times\left(10^{-6}\right)^3 \times 1.26 \times 10^3 \times 10}{3 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \times 3.65 \times 10^4} \\
& =9.03 \simeq 9
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If two charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ are separated with distance ' $d$ ' and placed in a medium of dielectric constant $K$. What will be the equivalent distance between charges in air for the same electrostatic force ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $d \sqrt{K}$
- **(B)** $K \sqrt{d}$
- **(C)** $1.5 d \sqrt{K}$
- **(D)** $2 d \sqrt{K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $d \sqrt{K}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

As. $F$(air)=$F$(medium)
$\Rightarrow \frac{1}{4 \pi \epsilon_0} \frac{\mathrm{q}_1 \mathrm{q}_2}{\mathrm{~d}_{\text {air }}^2}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{~K}} \frac{1}{4 \pi \epsilon_0} \frac{\mathrm{q}_1 \mathrm{q}_2}{\mathrm{~d}^2} \Rightarrow \mathrm{~d}_{\text {air }}=\mathrm{d} \sqrt{\mathrm{K}}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The force between two small charged spheres having charges of $1 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{C}$ and $2 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{C}$ placed $20 \mathrm{~cm}$ apart in air is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4.5 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~N}$
- **(B)** $5.4 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~N}$
- **(C)** $4.5 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $5.4 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~N}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4.5 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~N}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here, $q_1=1 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{C}, q_2$ and $2 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{C}$, $r=20 \mathrm{~cm}=20 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~m}$
$F=\frac{q_1 q_2}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0 r^2}=\frac{9 \times 10^9 \times 1 \times 10^{-7} \times 2 \times 10^{-7}}{\left(20 \times 10^{-2}\right)^2}$
$=4.5 \times 10^{-3} N$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2020

Three charge q, Q and 4q are placed in a straight line of length $l$ at points distant $0, \frac{1}{2}$ and $l$ respectively from one end. In order to make the net froce on q zero, the charge Q must be equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-\mathrm{q}$
- **(B)** $-2 q$
- **(C)** $\frac{-\mathrm{q}}{2}$
- **(D)** q

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $-\mathrm{q}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left(\mathrm{F}_{\text {net }}\right)_{\mathrm{q}}=0$

$\Rightarrow \quad \mathrm{k} \frac{\mathrm{Qq}}{\left(\frac{\ell}{2}\right)^{2}}+\mathrm{k} \frac{4 \mathrm{q}^{2}}{\ell^{2}}=0$

\text { where } \mathrm{k}=\frac{1}{4 \pi \varepsilon_{0}} \\
