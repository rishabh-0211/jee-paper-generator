### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

Five charges $+q,+5 q,-2 q,+3 q$ and $-4 q$ are situated as shown in the figure, The electric flux due to this configuration through the surface S is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{5 q}{\epsilon_0}$
- **(B)** $\frac{4 q}{\epsilon_0}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3 q}{\epsilon_0}$
- **(D)** $\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{4 q}{\epsilon_0}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using Gauss's law, $\phi=\frac{q}{\epsilon_0}$
Here, $\mathrm{q}=$ charge inside the closed surface
$\therefore \phi=\frac{q+(-2 q)+5 q}{\epsilon_0}$
$\Rightarrow \phi=\frac{4 q}{\epsilon_0}$

### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A charge $+q$ is at a distance $L / 2$ above a square of side $\mathrm{L}$. Then what is the flux linked with the surface?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{q}}{4 \varepsilon_{0}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{2 q}{3 \varepsilon_{0}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{q}}{6 \varepsilon_{0}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{6 \mathrm{q}}{\varepsilon_{0}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\mathrm{q}}{6 \varepsilon_{0}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/yVVO3LpdmZ20CqO6ICa0d7KACe8LFNfQG2cK4diHrEs.original.fullsize.png">
m{L}

The given square of side $L$ may be considered as one of the faces of a cube with edge $L$. Then given charge q will be considered to be placed at the centre of the cube. Then according to Gauss's theorem, the magnitude of the electric flux through the faces (six) of the cube is given by

$\phi=\mathrm{q} / \varepsilon_{0}$

Hence, electric flux through one face of the cube for the given square will be

$\phi^{\prime}=\frac{1}{6} \phi=\frac{\mathrm{q}}{6 \varepsilon_{0}}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a region of space having a uniform electric field $\mathrm{E}$, a hemispherical bowl of radius $\mathrm{r}$ is placed. The electric flux $\phi$ through the bowl is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \pi \mathrm{RE}$
- **(B)** $4 \pi R^{2} E$
- **(C)** $2 \pi R^{2} E$
- **(D)** $\pi R^{2} E$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2 \pi R^{2} E$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\phi=\mathrm{E}(\mathrm{ds}) \cos \theta=\mathrm{E}\left(2 \pi \mathrm{r}^{2}\right) \cos 0^{\circ}=2 \pi \mathrm{r}^{2} \mathrm{E}$

</details>
