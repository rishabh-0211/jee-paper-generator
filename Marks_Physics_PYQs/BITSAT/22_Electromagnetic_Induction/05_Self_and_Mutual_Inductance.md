### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

There are two long co-axial solenoids of same length $l$. The inner and outer coils have radii $r_1$ and $r_2$ and number of turns per unit length $n_1$ and $n_2$, respectively. The ratio of mutual inductance to the self-inductance of the inner-coil is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{n_1}{n_2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{n_2}{n_1} \cdot \frac{n_1}{r_2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{n_2}{n_1} \cdot \frac{r_2^2}{r_1^2}$
- **(D)** $\frac{n_2}{n_1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{n_2}{n_1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The rate of mutual inductance is given by
$\mathrm{M}=\mu_0 \mathrm{n}_1 \mathrm{n}_2 \pi \mathrm{~m}_1^2$     ....(i)
The rate of self inductance is given by
$\mathrm{L}=\mu_0 \mathrm{n}_1^2 \pi \mathrm{r}_1^2$    .....(ii)
Dividing (i) by (ii)
$\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{M}}{\mathrm{L}}=\frac{\mathrm{n}_2}{\mathrm{n}_1}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two coaxial solenoids are made by winding thin insulated wire over a pipe of cross-sectional area $\mathrm{A}=10 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ and length $=20 \mathrm{~cm} .$ If one of the solenoid has 300 turns and the other 400 turns, their mutual inductance is $\left(\mu_{0}=4 \pi \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{TmA}^{-1}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2.4 \pi \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{H}$
- **(B)** $4.8 \pi \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{H}$
- **(C)** $4.8 \pi \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{H}$
- **(D)** $2.4 \pi \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{H}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2.4 \pi \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{H}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} M &=\frac{\mu_{0} \mathrm{~N}_{1} \mathrm{~N}_{2} \mathrm{~A}}{\ell} \\ &=\frac{4 \pi \times 10^{-7} \times 300 \times 400 \times 100 \times 10^{-4}}{0.2} \\ &=2.4 \pi \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{H} \end{aligned}$

### Q3. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two solenoids of same cross-sectional area have their lengths and number of turns in ratio of 1: 2 . The ratio of self-inductance of two solenoids is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1: 1
- **(B)** 1: 2
- **(C)** 2: 1
- **(D)** 1: 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1: 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

From $L=\frac{\mu_{0} N^{2} A}{1} \alpha \frac{N^{2}}{1}$
we get, $\frac{\mathrm{L}_{1}}{\mathrm{~L}_{2}}=\frac{(1 / 2)^{2}}{1 / 2}=\frac{1}{2}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The back emf induced in a coil, when current changes from 1 ampere to zero in one milli-second, is 4 volts, the self inductance of the coil is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1 henry
- **(B)** 4 henry
- **(C)** $10^{-3}$ henry
- **(D)** $4 \times 10^{-3}$ henry

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4 \times 10^{-3}$ henry**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{e}=-\mathrm{L} \frac{\mathrm{di}}{\mathrm{dt}}$
But $\mathrm{e}=4 \mathrm{~V}$ and $\frac{\mathrm{di}}{\mathrm{dt}}=\frac{0-1}{10^{-3}}=-1 / 10^{-3}$
$\therefore \frac{-1}{10^{-3}}(-\mathrm{L})=4 \Rightarrow \mathrm{L}=4 \times 10^{-3}$ henry

</details>
