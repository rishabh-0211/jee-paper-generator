### Q1. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A $2 \mathrm{~m}$ wide truck is moving with a uniform speed $v_{0}=8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ along a straight horizontal road. $\mathrm{A}$ pedestrain starts to cross the road with a uniform speed $v$ when the truck is 4 m away from him. The minimum value of $v$ so that he can cross the road safely is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/Z3fJLaj1dg6v6X8HIQc2GSDfUqbDQP6LBa9-0HbyYFE.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2.62 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $4.6 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $3.57 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $1.414 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $3.57 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the man starts crossing the road at an angle $\theta$ as shown in figure. For safe crossing the condition is that the man must cross the road by the time the truck describes the distance 4 । $A C$ or 4 । $2 \cot \theta$. $\therefore \frac{4+2 \cot \theta}{8}=\frac{2 / \sin \theta}{v}$ or $v=\frac{8}{2 \sin \theta+\cos \theta}$

For minimum $v, \frac{d v}{d \theta}=0$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/0Fx5hXOh-RW30FnSsjhaGPlflHon3wkCwFFKFHGt6w4.original.fullsize.png">


or $\frac{-8(2 \cos \theta-\sin \theta)}{(2 \sin \theta+\cos \theta)^{2}}=0$ or $2 \cos \theta-$

$\sin \theta=0$

or $\tan \theta=2$

From equation (i),

$$

v_{\min }=\frac{8}{2\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\right)+\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}}=\frac{8}{\sqrt{5}}=3.57 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}

$$

### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A passenger in a open car travelling at $30 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ throws a ball out over the bonnet. Relative to the car the initial velocity of the ball is $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ at $60^{\circ}$ to the horizontal. The angle of projection of the ball with respect to the horizontal road will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)$
- **(B)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$
- **(C)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$
- **(D)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$\begin{array}{l}
\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{bc}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}}=\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}}-\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{c}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}} \\
20 \cos 60^{\circ}=\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}}-30 \\
\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}}=40 ;\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{bc}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}}=\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}}-\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{c}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}} \\
20 \sin 60^{\circ}=\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}}-0 \\
\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}}=10 \sqrt{3} ; \tan \theta=\frac{\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{y}}}{\left(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{\mathrm{b}}\right)_{\mathrm{x}}}=\frac{10 \sqrt{3}}{40}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}
\end{array}
$$

</details>
