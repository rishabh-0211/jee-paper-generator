### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the distance between object and its two times magnified virtual image produced by a curved mirror is 15 cm , the focal length of the mirror must be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{10}{3} \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(B)** $-12 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(C)** $-10 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(D)** 15 cm

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $-10 \mathrm{~cm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since, image is magnified. So, it is a concave mirror.
$\therefore \mathrm{m}=2=\frac{-\mathrm{v}}{\mathrm{u}}$
$2=\frac{-(15-u)}{-u}$
$2 u=15-u$
$3 \mathrm{u}=15 \Rightarrow \mathrm{u}=5 \mathrm{~cm}$
$\mathrm{v}=15-\mathrm{u}=15-5=10 \mathrm{~cm}$
Bymirror formula,
$\frac{1}{\mathrm{f}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{v}}+\frac{1}{\mathrm{u}}$
$=\frac{1}{10}+\frac{1}{(-5)}=\frac{1-2}{10}=\frac{-1}{10}$
$\mathrm{f}=-10 \mathrm{~cm}$

### Q2. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{R}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{R}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2 \sqrt{R}-R}{\sqrt{2}}$
- **(D)** $R\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$

**Correct Option:** **(D) $R\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From similar triangles,

\frac{Q C}{\sin 30^{\circ}}=\frac{R}{\sin 120^{\circ}}

Thus $P Q=P C-Q C=R-\frac{R}{\sqrt{3}}=R\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)$
