### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A thin prism $P_1$ with an angle $6^{\circ}$ and made of glass of refractive index 1.54 is combined with another prism $\mathrm{P}_2$ made from glass of refractive index 1.72 to produce dispersion without average deviation. The angle of prism $\mathrm{P}_2$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $1.3^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $7.8^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $4.5^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4.5^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Angle of deviation for first prism
$\delta_1=A_1\left(\mu_1-1\right)=6(1.54-1)$
Angle of deviation for second prism
$\begin{aligned}
& \delta_2=A_2\left(\mu_2-1\right) \\
& =A_2(1.72-1)
\end{aligned}$
For dispersion without deviation
$\begin{aligned}
& \delta_1=\delta_2 \\
& \Rightarrow 6^{\circ}(1.54-1)=\mathrm{A}_2(1.72-1) \\
& \Rightarrow \mathrm{A}_2=\frac{6^{\circ} \times 0.54}{0.72}=\frac{18^{\circ}}{4}=4.5^{\circ}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

For the angle of minimum deviation of a prism to be equal to its refracting angle, the prism must be made of a material whose refractive index:

**Options:**

- **(A)** lies between $\sqrt{2}$ and 1
- **(B)** lies between 2 and $\sqrt{2}$
- **(C)** is less than 1
- **(D)** is greater than 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) lies between 2 and $\sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/vBf9AydqoolnOnr1k-AKjQXgtl9K0dyNQLyAHlqZttw.original.fullsize.png">


The angle of minimum deviation is given as

$$

\delta_{\min }=i+e-A

$$

for minimum deviation

$$

\begin{aligned}

\delta_{\min } &=A \text { then } \\

2 A &=i+e

\end{aligned}

$$

in case of $\delta_{\min } i=e$

$$

\begin{aligned}

2 A &=2 i \quad r_{1}=r_{2}=\frac{A}{2} \\

i &=A=90^{\circ}

\end{aligned}

$$

from smell's law

$$

\begin{array}{l}

1 \sin i=n \sin r_{1} \\

\sin A=n \sin \frac{A}{2} \\

2 \sin \frac{A}{2} \cos \frac{A}{2}=n \sin \frac{A}{2} \\

2 \cos \frac{A}{2}=n

\end{array}

$$

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\text { when } \quad A=90^{\circ}=i_{\min } \\

\text { then } \quad n_{\min }=\sqrt{2} \\

i=A=0 \quad n_{\max }=2

\end{array}

$$

</details>
