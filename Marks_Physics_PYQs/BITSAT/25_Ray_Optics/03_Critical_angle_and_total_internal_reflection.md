### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Green light of wavelength $5460 Å$ is incident on an airglass interface. If the refractive index of glass is $1.5$, the wavelength of light in glass would be $\left(\mathrm{c}=3 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3640 Å$
- **(B)** $5460 Å$
- **(C)** $4861 Å$
- **(D)** None of the above

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3640 Å$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{a}^{\lambda} \mathrm{g}=\frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{a}}}{\mu}=\frac{5460}{1.5}=3640 Å$

</details>
