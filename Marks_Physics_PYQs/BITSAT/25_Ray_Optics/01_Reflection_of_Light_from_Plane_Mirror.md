### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A mosquito $O$ is sitting infront of a glass rod having spherical end of radius of curvature $40 \mathrm{~cm}$. The image would be formed at
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/weLGNMYc7iY8HKVyLvwJjxS4kZCrETIc0bLDHgLJXiY.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $40 \mathrm{~cm}$ left
- **(B)** infinity
- **(C)** $20 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the right
- **(D)** $15 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the left

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $40 \mathrm{~cm}$ left**

**Detailed Solution:**

As, we know, $\frac{\mu_2}{v}-\frac{\mu_1}{u}=\frac{\mu_2-\mu_1}{R}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad \frac{1.5}{v}-\frac{1}{(-20)}=\frac{1.5-1}{+40} \\
& v=-40 \mathrm{~cm} \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
Negative sign shows that image is obtained on the same side of object i.e., towards left.

</details>
