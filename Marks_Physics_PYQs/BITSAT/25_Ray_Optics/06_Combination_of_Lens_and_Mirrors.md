### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An achromatic convergent doublet of two lense in contact has a power of $+5 \mathrm{D}$. The power of converging lens is $+6 \mathrm{D}$. The ratio of the dispersive power of the convergent and divergent lenses is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3: 7$
- **(B)** $2: 3$
- **(C)** $1: 5$
- **(D)** $5: 3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $1: 5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

- The condition of achromatism is $W_1 P_1+W_2 P_2=0$
$$
\begin{array}{rlrl}
\Rightarrow & W_1 P_1 & =-W_2 P_2 \\
\Rightarrow & & \frac{W_1}{W_2} & =-\frac{P_2}{P_1}
\end{array}
$$
Now, $\quad P_1+P_2=4 \mathrm{D}$
but, Power of converging lens,
$$
P_1=5 \mathrm{D}
$$
$\because$ Power of diverging lens
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_2 & =4 \mathrm{D}-P_1 \\
& =4 \mathrm{D}-5 \mathrm{D}=-\mathrm{D}
\end{aligned}
$$
[From ii]
$\therefore$ From Eq. (i), we have
$$
\frac{W_1}{W_2}=-\frac{P_2}{P_1}=\frac{-(-D)}{5 D}=\frac{1}{5} \Rightarrow \frac{W_1}{W_2}=\frac{1}{5}
$$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A planoconvex lens fits exactly into a planoconcave lens. Their plane surface are parallel to each other. If the lenses are made of different materials of refractive indices $\mu_{1} \& \mu_{2}$ and $R$ is the radius of curvature of the curved surface of the lenses, then focal length of combination is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{R}{\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{2 R}{\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{R}{2\left(\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}\right)}$
- **(D)** $\frac{R}{2-\left(\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}\right)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{R}{\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

If F be the equivalent focal length, then

$$

\begin{aligned}

\frac{1}{F}=\frac{1}{f_{1}}+\frac{1}{f_{2}} \quad \frac{1}{F}=\left(\mu_{1}-1\right)\left(\frac{1}{\infty}+\frac{1}{R}\right) \\

+\left(\mu_{2}-1\right)\left(\frac{1}{-R}-\frac{1}{\infty}\right)=\frac{\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}}{R} \\

F=\frac{R}{\mu_{1}-\mu_{2}}

\end{aligned}

$$

</details>
