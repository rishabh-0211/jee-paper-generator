### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A vessel of depth $2 \mathrm{~d} \mathrm{~cm}$ is half filled with a liquid of refractive index $\mu_{1}$ and the upper half with a liquid of refractive index $\mu_{2}$. The apparent depth of the vessel seen perpendicularly is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\frac{\mu_{1} \mu_{2}}{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}}\right) \mathrm{d}$
- **(B)** $\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right) \mathrm{d}$
- **(C)** $\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right) 2 \mathrm{~d}$
- **(D)** $\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1} \mu_{2}}\right) 2 \mathrm{~d}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right) \mathrm{d}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Apparent depth $=\mathrm{d} / \mu_{1}+\mathrm{d} / \mu_{2}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

### Q5. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A vessel of depth $2 \mathrm{~d} \mathrm{~cm}$. is halffilled with a liquid of refractive index $\mu_{1}$ and the upper half with a liquid of refractive index $\mu_{2} .$ The apparent depth of the vessel seen perpendicularly is -

**Options:**

- **(A)** $d\left(\frac{\mu_{1} \mu_{2}}{\mu_{1}+\mu_{2}}\right)$
- **(B)** $d\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right)$
- **(C)** $2 d\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right)$
- **(D)** $2 d\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1} \mu_{2}}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $d\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{h}^{\prime}=\frac{\mathrm{d}_{1}}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{\mathrm{d}_{2}}{\mu_{2}}=\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{1}{\mu_{1}}+\frac{1}{\mu_{2}}\right)$

</details>
