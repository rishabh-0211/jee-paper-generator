### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A body of weight $2 \mathrm{~kg}$ is suspended as shown in the figure. The tension $\mathrm{T}_{1}$ in the horizontal string (in $\mathrm{kg}$ wt ) is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/M1AX_eSftPIY0bQ28YXsaE8NAl_9yaLTo_jffdjtHu4.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(C)** $2 \sqrt{3}$
- **(D)** $2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2 \sqrt{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The tension in the above string can be divided into two components where the component opposite to weight will balance the weight while the component of this tension opposite to \(\mathrm{T}_{1}\) will be equal to \(\mathrm{T}_{1}\).

\(\mathrm{T} \sin \theta=2 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{wt}\)

\(\mathrm{T} \cos \theta=\mathrm{T}_{1}\)

The first equation gives

\(\mathrm{T}=4 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{wt}\)

So \(\mathrm{T}_{1}\) will be,

\(\mathrm{T}_{1}=2 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{wt}\)

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Block A of mass $\mathrm{m}$ and block $\mathrm{B}$ of mass $2 \mathrm{~m}$ are placed on a fixed triangular wedge by means of a massless, inextensible string and a frictionless pulley as shown in figure.

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/bOXlG9AeXs25Cicn-CH4Vcykc50hKlssLX3EyMgyDAA.original.fullsize.png">


The wedge is inclined at $45^{\circ}$ to the horizontal on both the sides. If the coefficient of friction between the block $\mathrm{A}$ and the wedge is $2 / 3$ and that between the block $\mathrm{B}$ and the wedge is $1 / 3$ and both the blocks $\mathrm{A}$ and $\mathrm{B}$ are released from rest, the acceleration of A will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-1 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$
- **(B)** $1.2 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$
- **(C)** $0.2 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$
- **(D)** zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) zero**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total maximum friction force,\(f_{t}=f_{A}+f_{B}\)

\(\mu_{\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{mg} \cos 45^{\circ}+\mu_{\mathrm{B}} 2 \mathrm{mg} \cos 45^{\circ}\)

or, \(f_{t}=m g\left(\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{3} \times 2 \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\)

\(=\frac{4}{3 \sqrt{2}} \mathrm{mg}\)

The net pulling force, \(F=2 m g \sin 45^{\circ}-m g \sin 45^{\circ}\) \(=\mathrm{mg} \sin 45^{\circ}\)

\(=\frac{m g}{\sqrt{2}}\)

\(\because \mathrm{F}<\mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{t}}\)

\(\therefore\) The blocks will not move.

So, acceleration of block A is Zero.

\(\therefore\) option (D) is correct.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A block of mass m is placed on a smooth inclined wedge $A B C$ of inclination $\theta$ as shown in the figure. The wedge is given an acceleration 'a' towards the right. The relation between a and $\theta$ for the block to remain stationary on the wedge is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/6elWAXz6q9osdRlGR5hP02iRBVuwHR0J8DkFfiRalJA.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a=\frac{g}{\operatorname{cosec} \theta}$
- **(B)** $a=\frac{g}{\sin \theta}$
- **(C)** $a=g \tan \theta$
- **(D)** $a=g \cos \theta$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $a=g \tan \theta$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the mass of block is m. It will remains stationary if forces acting on it are in equilibrium. i.e., $m a \cos \theta=m g \sin \theta$

$\Rightarrow a=g \tan \theta$

Here $\mathrm{ma}=$ Pseudo force on block,
