### Q1. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A load of mass m falls from a height h on to the scale pan hung from the spring as shown in the figure. If the spring constant is $\mathrm{k}$ and mass of the scale pan is zero and the mass $\mathrm{m}$ does not bounce relative to the pan, then the amplitude of vibration is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/MII3jxvI459IXuFrqsqf4QInRZ3xrVDaHO7VyWKbHHU.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{mg} / \mathrm{d}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \sqrt{\left(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{hk}}{\mathrm{mg}}\right)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}}+\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \sqrt{\left(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{hk}}{\mathrm{mg}}\right)}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \sqrt{\left(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{hk}}{\mathrm{mg}}-\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}}\right)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \sqrt{\left(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{hk}}{\mathrm{mg}}\right)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to energy conservation principle, If, $\mathrm{x}_{1}$ is maximum elongation in the spring when the particle is in its lowest extreme position. Then,

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\mathrm{mgh}=\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{kx}_{1}^{2}-\mathrm{mgx}_{1} \\

\Rightarrow \frac{1}{2} \mathrm{kx}_{1}^{2}-\mathrm{mgx}_{1}-\mathrm{mgh}=0 \\

\text { or, } \mathrm{x}_{1}^{2}-\frac{2 \mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \mathrm{x}_{1}-\frac{2 \mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \cdot \mathrm{h}=0 \\

\therefore \mathrm{x}_{1}=\frac{\frac{2 \mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \pm \sqrt{\left.\left(\frac{2 \mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}}\right)^{2}+4 \times \frac{2 \mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \mathrm{h}\right]}}{2}

\end{array}

$$

Amplitude $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{X}_{1}-\mathrm{X}_{0}$ (elongation in spring for equilibrium position)

$$

\mathrm{A}=\frac{\mathrm{mg}}{\mathrm{k}} \sqrt{\left(1+\frac{2 \mathrm{hk}}{\mathrm{mg}}\right)}

$$

</details>
