### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The coefficient of friction between the rubber tyres and the road wayis $0.25 .$ The maximum speed with which a car can be driven round a curve of radius $20 \mathrm{~m}$ without skidding is $\left(\mathrm{g}=9.8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $14 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mu \mathrm{mg}=\mathrm{mv}^{2} / \mathrm{r}$ or $\mathrm{v}=\sqrt{\mu \mathrm{g} \mathrm{r}}$

or $v=\sqrt{(0.25 \times 9.8 \times 20)}=7 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A person with his hand in his pocket is skating on ice at the rate of $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ and describes a circle of radius $50 \mathrm{~m}$. What is his inclination to vertical:

$\left(\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{sec}^{2}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\tan ^{-1}(1 / 2)$
- **(B)** $\tan ^{-1}(1 / 5)$
- **(C)** $\tan ^{-1}(3 / 5)$
- **(D)** $\tan ^{-1}(1 / 10)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\tan ^{-1}(1 / 5)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since surface (ice) is frictionless, so the centripetal force required for skating will be provided by inclination of boy with the vertical and that angle is given as $\tan \theta=\frac{\mathrm{v}^{2}}{\mathrm{rg}}$ where $\mathrm{v}$ is speed of skating $\&$

i is radius of circle in which he moves.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The minimum velocity (in $\mathrm{ms}^{-1}$ ) with which a car driver must traverse a flat curve of radius $150 \mathrm{~m}$ and coefficient of friction 0.6 to avoid skidding is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 60
- **(B)** 30
- **(C)** 15
- **(D)** 25

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 30**

**Detailed Solution:**

The condition to avoid skidding,

$$

\mathrm{v}=\sqrt{\mu \mathrm{rg}}=\sqrt{0.6 \times 150 \times 10}=30 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}

$$

</details>
