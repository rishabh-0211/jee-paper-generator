### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A pendulum of mass 1 kg and length $l=1 \mathrm{~m}$ is released from rest at angle $\theta=60^{\circ}$. The power delivered by all the forces acting on the bob at angle $\theta=30^{\circ}$ will be $\left(\right.$ Take, $\left.g=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 13.4 W
- **(B)** 20.4 W
- **(C)** 24.6 W
- **(D)** zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 13.4 W**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power of tension $=0$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/7fC1iQG-mfCO6VTmOOZdWZaybHRy1oo3SE9jZQncHj8.original.fullsize.png"/>
$\begin{aligned}
& \text { Power of } m g=(m g)(v) \cos 60^{\circ} \\
& \text { Here, } \quad v=\sqrt{2 g h} \\
& \text { and } \quad h=l\left(\cos 30^{\circ}-\cos 60^{\circ}\right)=0.36 \mathrm{~m} \\
& v=\sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 0.36} \\
& =2.68 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \text { Power }=(m g)(v) \cos 60^{\circ} \\
& =(1 \times 10)(2.68) \times \frac{1}{2} \\
& =1.34 \times 10=13.4 \mathrm{~W}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following must be known in order to determine the power output of an automobile?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Final velocity and height
- **(B)** Mass and amount of work performed
- **(C)** Force exerted and distance of motion
- **(D)** Work performed and elapsed time of work

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Work performed and elapsed time of work**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power is defined as the rate of doing work. For the automobile, the power output is the amount of work done (overcoming friction) divided by the length of time in which the work was done.

</details>
