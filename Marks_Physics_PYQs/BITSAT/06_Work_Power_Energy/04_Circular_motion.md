### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A particle of mass 2 kg is on a smooth horizontal table and moves in a circular path of radius 0.6 m . The height of the table from the ground is 0.8 m . If the angular speed of the particle is $12 \mathrm{rad} \mathrm{s}^{-1}$, the magnitude of its angular momentum about a point on the ground right under the centre of the circle is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $14.4 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^2 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $8.64 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^2 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $20.16 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^2 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $11.52 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^2 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $14.4 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^2 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Angular momentum
$L_0=\mathrm{mvr} \sin 90^{\circ}$
$=2 \times 0.6 \times 12 \times 1 \times 1$
$\left[\right.$ As $V=r \omega$, $\left.\operatorname{Sin} 90^{\circ}=1\right]$
So, $L_0=14.4 \mathrm{kgm}^2 / \mathrm{s}$
