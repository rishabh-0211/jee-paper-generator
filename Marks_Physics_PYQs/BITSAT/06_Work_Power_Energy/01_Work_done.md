### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A force of $F - 0.5 \mathrm{~N}$ is applied on lower block as shown in figure. The work done by lower block
on upper block for a displacement of 3 m of the upper block with respect to ground is (Take, $\left.g=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2\right)$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/uJGfAVfcrT-8sjVhrwrd2IVRLals33Hu6O6zl4jYNSU.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** -0.5 J
- **(B)** 0.5 J
- **(C)** 2 J
- **(D)** -2 J

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 0.5 J**

**Detailed Solution:**

Maximum acceleration of 1 kg block may be
$a_{\max }=\mu g=1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2$
Common acceleration without relative motion between two blocks may be,
$a=\frac{0.5}{3} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2$
Since, $a \lt a_{\max }$
There will be no relative motion and blocks will move with acceleration $\frac{0.5}{3} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2$.
Force of friction by lower block on upper block,
$\begin{aligned}
f & =m a=(1)\left(\frac{0.5}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{6} \mathrm{~N}(\text { towards right }) \\
\therefore \quad W & =f \times s \\
& =\frac{1}{6} \times 3=0.5 \mathrm{~J}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A machine gun fires 300 bullets per $\mathrm{min}$ if the mass of each bullet is $10 \mathrm{~g}$ and the velocity of the bullets is $600 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$, the power (in $\mathrm{kW}$ ) of the gun is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $43200$
- **(B)** $9$
- **(C)** $72$
- **(D)** $7.2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $9$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Work done by the gun $=$ total kinetic energy of the bullets
$$
\begin{aligned}
& =n \frac{1}{2} m v^2=300 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 10^{-3} \times(600)^2 \\
& =150 \times 10 \times 10^{-3} \times 600 \times 600
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text { Power of gun } & =\frac{\text { work done }}{\text { Time taken }} \\
& =\frac{150 \times 10 \times 10^{-3} \times 600 \times 600}{60 \mathrm{~s}} \\
& =9 \mathrm{~kW} \quad(\because 1 \text { minute }=60 \text { seconds })
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A boy pushes a toy box $2.0 \mathrm{~m}$ along the floor by means of a force of $10 \mathrm{~N}$ directed downward at an angle of $60^{\circ}$ to the horizontal. The work done by the boy is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(B)** $8 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(C)** $10 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(D)** $12 \mathrm{~J}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $10 \mathrm{~J}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{W}=\mathrm{F} \mathrm{s} \cos \theta=10 \times 2 \cos 60^{\circ}=10 \mathrm{~J}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A small block of mass $\mathrm{m}$ is kept on a rough inclined surface of inclination $\theta$ fixed in a elevator. The elevator goes up with a uniform velocity $\mathrm{v}$ and the block does not slide on the wedge. The work done by the force of friction on the block in time t will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** zero
- **(B)** $\mathrm{mgvt} \cos ^{2} \theta$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{mgvt} \sin ^{2} \theta$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{mgvt} \sin 2 \theta$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{mgvt} \sin ^{2} \theta$**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\mathrm{W}_{\mathrm{f}}=\mathrm{f} \sin \theta \mathrm{S}\)

\(=(\mathrm{mg} \sin \theta)(\sin \theta)(\mathrm{vt})\)

\(=\mathrm{mg} \sin ^{2} \theta \mathrm{vt}\)

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A light inextensible string that goes over a smooth fixed pulley as shown in the figure connects two blocks of masses $0.36 \mathrm{~kg}$ and $0.72 \mathrm{~kg}$. Taking $\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2},$ find the work done $(\mathrm{in}$

joules) by the string on the block of mass $0.36 \mathrm{~kg}$ during the first second after the system is released from rest.

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/i7gKSnXmc1MN1Q3bNmoSV1vXdMYrIOUkiYHPtD9sntw.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(B)** $2 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(C)** $8 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(D)** $10 \mathrm{~J}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $8 \mathrm{~J}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $m=0.36 \mathrm{~kg}, M=0.72 \mathrm{~kg}$. The figure shows the forces on $m$ and $M$. When the system is released, let the acceleration be $a$. Then

$T-m g=m a$

$M g-T=M a$

$$

a=\frac{(M-m) g}{M+m}=g / 3

$$

and $T=4 \mathrm{mg} / 3$

For block m:

$$

u=0, a=g / 3, t=1, s=?

$$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/_LD6QYhzhQSmiBzK3EKnfY0Rr12eqtvfzy_3HP0QUNg.original.fullsize.png">


$$

s=u t+\frac{1}{2} a t^{2}=0+\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{g}{3} \times 1^{2}=g / 6

$$

Work done by the string on $m$ is

$$

\bar{T} \bar{s}=T s=4 \frac{m g}{3} \times \frac{g}{6}=\frac{4 \times 0.36 \times 10 \times 10}{3 \times 6}=8 \mathrm{~J}

$$

</details>
