### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Two vessels $A$ and $B$ are of the same size and are at same temperature. A contains 1 g of hydrogen and $B$ contains 1 g of oxygen. $\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{A}}$ and $P_B$ are the pressures of the gases in $A$ and $B$ respectively, then $\frac{P_A}{P_B}$ is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8$
- **(B)** $16$
- **(C)** $32$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $16$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{A}}=\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{~mol}, \mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{1}{32} \mathrm{~mol}$
By ideal gas equation,
$P V=n R T \Rightarrow P \propto n$
$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{A}}}{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{A}}}{\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{32}{2}=16$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A bicycle tyre is filled with air having pressure of $270 \mathrm{kPa}$ at $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The approximate pressure of the air in the tyre when the temperature increases to $36^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $270 \mathrm{kPa}$
- **(B)** $262 \mathrm{KPa}$
- **(C)** $278 \mathrm{kPa}$
- **(D)** $360 \mathrm{kPa}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $278 \mathrm{kPa}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the ideal gas equation $\mathrm{PV}=\mathrm{nRT}$ Here, volume is constant $\therefore \frac{\mathrm{P}_1}{\mathrm{~T}_1}=\frac{\mathrm{P}_2}{\mathrm{~T}_2}$
Here, $T_1=27+273=300 \mathrm{~K}$
$P_1=270 \mathrm{kPa}$
$\mathrm{T}_2=36+273=309 \mathrm{~K}$
$\Rightarrow P_2=\frac{P_1}{T_1} \times T_2=\frac{270 \times(309)}{300}=278 \mathrm{kPa}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

One mole of an ideal diatomic gas undergoes a process as shown in the figure. The molar specific heat of the gas in the process is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 R / 2$
- **(B)** $R / 2$
- **(C)** $5 R / 2$
- **(D)** $7 R / 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3 R / 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In the curve, $V \propto \frac{1}{T}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow V=m \frac{1}{T} \text { where, } m=\text { slope of curve. } \\
& \Rightarrow V T=\text { constant } \\
& \therefore \quad T d V+V d T=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad d V=-\frac{V}{T} d T \\
& \text { As, } Q=\Delta U+W \\
& C d T=C_V d T+p d V \\
& C d T=C_V d T-\frac{p}{T} V d T \\
& \Rightarrow \quad C=C_V-\frac{p V}{T} \\
& =C_V-R \quad\left(\because \frac{p V}{T}=R\right) \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
For diatomic gas, $C_V=\frac{5}{2} R$
$$
\therefore \quad C=C_V-R=\frac{5}{2} R-R=\frac{3 R}{2}
$$
