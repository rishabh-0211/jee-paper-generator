### Q2. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For a gas if ratio of specific heats at constant pressure and volume is $\gamma$ then value of degrees of freedom is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3 \gamma-1}{2 \gamma-1}$
- **(B)** $\frac{2}{\gamma-1}$
- **(C)** $\frac{9}{2}(\gamma-1)$
- **(D)** $\frac{25}{2}(\gamma-1)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{2}{\gamma-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \gamma=1+\frac{2}{f}, \Rightarrow \gamma-1=\frac{2}{f} \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{f}{2}=\frac{1}{\gamma-1} \Rightarrow f=\frac{2}{\gamma-1} \end{aligned}$

</details>
