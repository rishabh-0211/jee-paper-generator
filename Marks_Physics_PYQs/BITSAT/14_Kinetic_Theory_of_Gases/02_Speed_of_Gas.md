### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a mixture of gases, the average number of degree of freedoms per molecule is 6 . The rms speed of the molecule of the gas is $c$, then the velocity of sound in the gas is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{c}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{c}{\sqrt{2}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2 c}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{3 c}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{2 c}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

($v_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{\frac{3 R T}{M}}$ and $v_{\text {sound }}=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma R T}{M}}$ $\frac{v_{\text {sound }}}{v_{\text {rms }}}=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{3}}$
Degree of freedom is 6 .
$\begin{array}{lr}
\therefore & \gamma=1+\frac{2}{f}=1+\frac{2}{6}=\frac{4}{3} \\
\therefore & v_{\text {sound }}=\sqrt{\frac{4 / 3}{3}} v_{\text {rms }}=\frac{2}{3} v_{\text {rms }}=\frac{2 c}{3}
\end{array}$

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The temperature of an ideal gas is increased from 200 K to 800 K . If r.m.s. speed of gas at 200 K is $v_0$. Then, r.m.s. speed of the gas at 800 K will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{v}_0$
- **(B)** $4 v_0$
- **(C)** $\frac{v_0}{4}$
- **(D)** $2 \mathrm{v}_0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2 \mathrm{v}_0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

RMS speed,
$\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{rms}}=\sqrt{\frac{3 \mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{M}}}$
$\Rightarrow \quad \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{mm}} \propto \sqrt{\mathrm{T}}$
Here, $\mathrm{T}_{\text {initiol }}=200 \mathrm{~K}$
$\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{fing1}}=800 \mathrm{~K}$
Initial RMS speed $=\mathrm{v}_0$
$\therefore \frac{v_0}{v_{\mathrm{rms}}}=\sqrt{\frac{200}{800}} \Rightarrow v_{\mathrm{ms}}=2 v_0$

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A monoatomic gas of molar mass $m$ is kept in a insulated container. Container is moving with velocity $v$. If the container is suddenly stopped, then the change in the temperature of the gas is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $m v^2 / 4 R$
- **(B)** $m v^2 / 2 R$
- **(C)** $m v^2 / R$
- **(D)** $m v^2 / 3 R$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $m v^2 / 3 R$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the insulated container has $n$ moles of the monoatomic gas. The loss of kinetic energy of the gas,
$$
\Delta E_K=\frac{1}{2}(m n) v^2
$$
If the change in temperature of the gas is $\Delta T$, then heat gained by the gas is,
$$
\Delta Q=\frac{3}{2} n R \Delta T
$$
Now, according to question,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta Q & =\Delta E_K \\
\frac{3}{2} n R \Delta T & =\frac{1}{2} m n v^2 \\
\Rightarrow \quad \Delta T & =\frac{m v^2}{3 R}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2016

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow \quad v=\frac{200 \times 2}{\sqrt{3}} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\

\Rightarrow \quad \mathrm{v}=\frac{400}{\sqrt{3}} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}

### Q8. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The temperature of an ideal gas is increased from $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $127^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, then percentage increase in $\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{rms}}$

is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $37 \%$
- **(B)** $11 \%$
- **(C)** $33 \%$
- **(D)** $15.5 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $15.5 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know, $\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{mms}}=\sqrt{\frac{3 \mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{M}}}$

$\Rightarrow \%$ increase in

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{rms}}=\frac{\sqrt{\frac{3 \mathrm{R} \mathrm{T}_{2}}{\mathrm{M}}}-\sqrt{\frac{3 \mathrm{RT}_{1}}{\mathrm{M}}}}{\sqrt{\frac{3 \mathrm{RT}_{1}}{\mathrm{M}}}} \times 100 \\

=\frac{\sqrt{\mathrm{T}_{2}}-\sqrt{\mathrm{T}_{1}}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{T}_{1}}} \times 100 \\

=\frac{\sqrt{400}-\sqrt{300}}{\sqrt{300}} \times 100 \\

=\frac{20-17.32}{17.32} \times 100=15.5 \%

\end{array}

$$

</details>
