### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A flask contains hydrogen and oxygen in the ratio of $2: 1$ by mass at temperature $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The ratio of average kinetic energy per molecule of hydrogen and oxygen respectively is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2 : 1
- **(B)** 1 : 1
- **(C)** 1 : 4
- **(D)** 4 : 1

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1 : 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

Average kinetic energy for diatomic gases
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{av}}=\frac{5}{2} \mathrm{kT} \\
& \therefore \frac{\left(\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{av}}\right)_{\mathrm{H}}}{\left(\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{av}}\right)_{\mathrm{O}}}=\frac{(27+273)}{(27+273)}=1
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The average translational kinetic energy of $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ (molar mass 32) molecules at a particular temperature is $0.048 \mathrm{eV}$. The translational kinetic energy of $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ (molar mass 28 ) molecules in $\mathrm{eV}$ at the same temperature is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.0015
- **(B)** 0.003
- **(C)** 0.048
- **(D)** 0.768

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 0.048**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given,
\(\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}=0.048 \mathrm{eV}\)
We know that,
Translational kinetic energy,
\(\mathrm{E}=\frac{3}{2} \mathrm{KT}\)
where, \(\mathrm{k}=\) Boltzmann constant
\(\mathrm{T}=\) Temperature
\(\therefore \mathrm{E} \propto \mathrm{T}\)
Since, the temperature is same for both oxygen and nitrogen
therefore, \(\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{O}_{2}}=\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}\)
\(\therefore \mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{N}_{2}}=0.048 \mathrm{eV}\)

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

3 moles of an ideal gas at a temperature of $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ are mixed with 2 moles of an ideal gas at a temperature $227^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, determine the equilibrium temperature of the mixture, assuming no loss of energy.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $327^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $107^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $318^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $410^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $107^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Energy possessed by the ideal gas at $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is
$$
E_{1}=3\left(\frac{3}{2} R \times 300\right)=\frac{2700 R}{2}
$$
Energy possessed by the ideal gas at $227^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ is
$$
\mathrm{E}_{2}=2\left(\frac{3 \mathrm{R}}{2} \times 500\right)=1500 \mathrm{R}
$$
If $\mathrm{T}$ be the equilibrium temperature, of the mixture, then its energy will be
$$
\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{m}}=5\left(\frac{3 \mathrm{R} \mathrm{T}}{2}\right)
$$
Since, energy remains conserved,
$$
\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{m}}=\mathrm{E}_{1}+\mathrm{E}_{2}
$$
or $\quad 5\left(\frac{3 R T}{2}\right)=\frac{2700 R}{2}+1500 R$
or $\quad \mathrm{T}=380 \mathrm{~K}$ or $107^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

</details>
