### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find the average value of current shown graphically from $t=0$ to $t=2 \mathrm{~s}$.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/iUWimKZhttquCKNCq2-3k6pbi9tKdoQFJjAziDKZ1HE.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3 A
- **(B)** 5 A
- **(C)** 10 A
- **(D)** 4 A

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 5 A**

**Detailed Solution:**

From $i-t$ graph, area from $t=0$ to $t=2 \mathrm{~s}$, we get
$\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{1}{2} \times 1 \times 10+\frac{1}{2} \times(2-1) \times 10 \\
& =5+5=10 \mathrm{~A} \\
\therefore \text { Area current, } i_{\mathrm{av}} & =\frac{\text { Area }(i-t) \text { graph }}{\text { time interval }}=\frac{10}{2}=5 \mathrm{~A}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An alternating voltage $V(t)=220 \sin 100 \pi t$ volt is applied to a purely resistive load of $50 \Omega$. The time taken for the current to rise from half of the peak value to the peak value is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 5 ms
- **(B)** 3.3 ms
- **(C)** 7.2 ms
- **(D)** 2.2 ms

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 3.3 ms**

**Detailed Solution:**

Rising half to peak
$t=T / 6$
$\mathrm{t}=\frac{2 \pi}{6 \omega}=\frac{\pi}{3 \omega}=\frac{\pi}{300 \pi}=\frac{1}{300}=3.3 \mathrm{~ms}$
Here $\omega=100 \pi \mathrm{rad} / \mathrm{s}$

### Q3. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An alternating voltage $\mathrm{V}=\mathrm{V}_{0} \sin \omega \mathrm{t}$ is applied across a circuit. As a result, a current $\mathrm{I}=\mathrm{I}_{0} \sin \left(\omega \mathrm{t}-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ flows in it. The power consumed per cycle is

**Options:**

- **(A)** zero
- **(B)** $0.5 \mathrm{~V}_{0} \mathrm{I}_{0}$
- **(C)** $0.707 \mathrm{~V}_{0} \mathrm{I}_{0}$
- **(D)** $1.414 \mathrm{~V}_{0} \mathrm{I}_{0}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) zero**

**Detailed Solution:**

The phase angle between voltage $V$ and current $I$ is $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

Therefore, power factor $\cos \phi=\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=0$.

Hence the power consumed is zero.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A direct current of $5 \mathrm{~A}$ is superposed on an alternating current $I=10 \sin \omega t$ flowing through the wire. The effective value of the resulting current will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(15 / 2) \mathrm{A}$
- **(B)** $5 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~A}$
- **(C)** $5 \sqrt{5} \mathrm{~A}$
- **(D)** $15 \mathrm{~A}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~A}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total carrent, $1=(5+10 \sin \omega \mathrm{t})$

$$

\begin{aligned}

\Rightarrow I_{\text {eff }} &=\left[\frac{\int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}} \mathrm{I}^{2} \mathrm{dt}}{\int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}} \mathrm{dt}}\right]^{1 / 2} \\

&=\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{~T}} \int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}}(5+10 \sin \omega \mathrm{t})^{2} \mathrm{dt}\right]^{1 / 2} \\

&=\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{~T}} \int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}}\left(25+100 \sin \omega \mathrm{t}+100 \sin ^{2} \omega \mathrm{t}\right)\right]^{1 / 2} \\

\text { But, } \quad \frac{1}{\mathrm{~T}} \int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}} \sin \omega \mathrm{t} \mathrm{dt}=0

\end{aligned}

$$

and

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\frac{1}{\mathrm{~T}} \int_{0}^{\mathrm{T}} \sin ^{2} \omega \mathrm{t} \mathrm{dt}=\frac{1}{2} \\

\text { So, } \mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{eff}}=\left[25+\frac{1}{2} \times 100\right]^{1 / 2}=5 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~A}

\end{array}

$$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Voltage $V$ and current $i$ in $A C$ circuit are given by $\mathrm{V}=50 \sin (50 \mathrm{t})$ volt, $\mathrm{i}=50 \sin \left(50 \mathrm{t}+\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \mathrm{mA}$

The power dissipated in the circuit is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5.0 \mathrm{~W}$
- **(B)** $2.5 \mathrm{~W}$
- **(C)** $1.25 \mathrm{~W}$
- **(D)** zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $1.25 \mathrm{~W}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $V=50 \sin (50$ t) $V$ Maximum voltage, $\mathrm{V}_{0}=50 \mathrm{~V}$

$$

i=\left(50 t+\frac{\pi}{3}\right) \mathrm{mA}

$$

Maximum current, $i_{0}=50 \mathrm{~mA}=50 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~A}$

Power dissipated, $\mathrm{P}=\frac{\mathrm{i}_{0}}{\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{\mathrm{V}_{0}}{\sqrt{2}}$

$=\frac{50 \times 50 \times 10^{-3}}{2}=\frac{2500 \times 10^{-3}}{2}=1.25 \mathrm{~W}$

</details>
