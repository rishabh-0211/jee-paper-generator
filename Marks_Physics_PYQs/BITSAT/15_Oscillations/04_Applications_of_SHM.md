### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A simple pendulum doing small oscillations at a place R height above earth surface has time period of $T_1=4 \mathrm{~s}$. $T_2$ would be it's time period if it is brought to a point which is at a height $2 R$ from earth surface. Choose the correct relation $[\mathrm{R}=$ radius of Earth $]:$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $T_1=T_2$
- **(B)** $2 \mathrm{~T}_1=3 \mathrm{~T}_2$
- **(C)** $3 \mathrm{~T}_1=2 \mathrm{~T}_2$
- **(D)** $2 \mathrm{~T}_1=\mathrm{T}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $3 \mathrm{~T}_1=2 \mathrm{~T}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Time period of simple pendulum,
$\mathrm{T}=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{l}{\mathrm{~g}}} \& \mathrm{~g}=\frac{\mathrm{GM}}{(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{h})^2}$
$\therefore \mathrm{T} \propto(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{h})$
$\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{T}_1}{\mathrm{~T}_2}=\frac{\left(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{h}_1\right)}{\left(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{h}_2\right)}=\frac{(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{R})}{(\mathrm{R}+2 \mathrm{R})}=\frac{2}{3} \Rightarrow 3 \mathrm{~T}_1=2 \mathrm{~T}_2$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A tunnel has been dug through the centre of the earth and a ball is released in it. It executes S.H.M. with time period

**Options:**

- **(A)** 42 minutes
- **(B)** 1 day
- **(C)** 1 hour
- **(D)** 84.6 minutes

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 84.6 minutes**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{T}=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{g}}}=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{64 \times 10^{6}}{9.8}}$

$=2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{8 \times 10^{3}}{7 \times \sqrt{2}}$

$=\frac{\sqrt{2} \times 22 \times 8 \times 1000}{49 \times 60} \min =84.6 \mathrm{~min}$

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{k}}}$

- **(C)** $\pi \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{m}}{3 \mathrm{k} / 2}}$

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A simple pendulum has time period 't'. Its time period in a lift which is moving upwards with acceleration $3 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{12.8}}$
- **(B)** $t \sqrt{\frac{12.8}{9.8}}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{t} \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{6.8}}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{t} \sqrt{\frac{6.8}{9.8}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $t \sqrt{\frac{9.8}{12.8}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{t} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{9.8}}, \mathrm{t}^{\prime} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{12.8}}$
$\left(\because \mathrm{g}^{\prime}=9.8+3=12.8\right)$
$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{t}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{t}}=\sqrt{\frac{9.8}{12.8}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{t}^{\prime}=\sqrt{\frac{9.8}{12.8}} \mathrm{t}$

</details>
