### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The distance travelled by a particle starting from rest and moving with an acceleration $\frac{4}{3} \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$, in the third second is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6 m
- **(B)** 4 m
- **(C)** $\frac{10}{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(D)** $\frac{19}{3} \mathrm{~m}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{10}{3} \mathrm{~m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Distance travelled in the nth second is given by
$\mathrm{t}_{\mathrm{n}}=\mathrm{u}+\frac{\mathrm{a}}{2}(2 \mathrm{n}-1)$
put $\mathrm{u}=0, \mathrm{a}=\frac{4}{3} \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}, \mathrm{n}=3$
$\therefore \quad d=0+\frac{4}{3 \times 2}(2 \times 3-1)=\frac{4}{6} \times 5=\frac{10}{3} m$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2018

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Consider the acceleration, velocity and displacement of a tennis ball as its falls to the ground and bounces back. Directions of which of these change in the process?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Velocity only
- **(B)** Displacement and velocity
- **(C)** Acceleration, velocity and displacement
- **(D)** Displacement and acceleration

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Displacement and velocity**

**Detailed Solution:**

When a tennis ball falls on the ground and bounces back, its velocity and displacement changes in reverse direction while direction of acceleration remains same.

</details>
