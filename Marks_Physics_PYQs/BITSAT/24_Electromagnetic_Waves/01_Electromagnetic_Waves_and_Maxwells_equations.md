### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A parallel plate capacitor consists of two circular plates of radius $R=0.1 \mathrm{~m}$. They are separated by a short distance. If electric field between the capacitor plates changes as $\frac{d E}{d t}=6 \times 10^{13} \frac{\mathrm{~V}}{\mathrm{~m} \times \mathrm{s}}$, then the value of displacement current is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 15.25 A
- **(B)** 6.25 A
- **(C)** 16.67 A
- **(D)** 4.69 A

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 16.67 A**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, area of plates, $A=\pi R^2=3.14 \times(0.1)^2 \mathrm{~m}^2$ and $\frac{d E}{d t}=6 \times 10^{13} \frac{\mathrm{~V}}{\mathrm{~m} \times \mathrm{s}}$
Displacement current between the plates,
$\begin{aligned}
I_d & =\varepsilon_0 \frac{d \phi}{d t} \\
\Rightarrow \quad I_d & =\varepsilon_0 A\left(\frac{d E}{d t}\right) \quad\left[\because \frac{d \phi}{d t}=A \frac{d E}{d t}\right] \\
& =8.85 \times 10^{-12} \times 3.14 \times(0.1)^2 \times 6 \times 10^{13} \\
\Rightarrow \quad I_d & =16.67 \mathrm{~A}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Electromagnetic waves travel in a medium with speed of $1.5 \times 10^8 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. The relative permeability of the medium is 2.0 . The relative permittivity will be :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5$
- **(B)** $1$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$v=\frac{C}{\sqrt{\mu_{\mathrm{r}} \epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}}}$
$\Rightarrow 1.5 \times 10^8=\frac{3 \times 10^8}{\sqrt{2 \times \epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}}}$
$\Rightarrow 2 \epsilon_{\mathrm{T}}=4 \Rightarrow \epsilon_{\mathrm{T}}=2$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

**Options:**

$\left[\because \mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{E}}=\mathrm{U}_{\mathrm{B}}=\frac{1}{2} \in_0 \mathrm{E}_0^2=\frac{\mathrm{B}_0^2}{2 \mu_0}\right]$

### Q9. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The magnetic field in a travelling electromagnetic wave has a peak value of $20 \mathrm{nT}$. The peak value of electric field strength is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$
- **(B)** $6 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$
- **(C)** $9 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$
- **(D)** $12 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $6 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\overrightarrow{\mathrm{E}}_{0}=\overrightarrow{\mathrm{B}}_{0} \times \overrightarrow{\mathrm{C}}$
$$
\left|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{E}}_{0}\right|=\left|\mathrm{B}_{0}\right| \cdot|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{C}}|=20 \times 10^{-9} \times 3 \times 10^{8}=6 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}
$$

</details>
