### Q2. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Given that $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{A}}+\overrightarrow{\mathrm{B}}=\overrightarrow{\mathrm{R}}$ and $\mathrm{A}^{2}+\mathrm{B}^{2}=\mathrm{R}^{2}$. The angle between $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{A}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{B}}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** $\pi / 4$
- **(C)** $\pi / 2$
- **(D)** $\pi$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\pi / 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\cos \theta=\frac{\mathrm{R}^{2}-\mathrm{A}^{2}-\mathrm{B}^{2}}{2 \mathrm{~A} \mathrm{~B}}=\frac{\mathrm{R}^{2}-\mathrm{R}^{2}}{2 \mathrm{AB}}=0$
$\therefore \theta=\pi / 2$

</details>
