### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The free space inside a current carrying toroid is filled with a material of susceptibility $2 \times 10^{-2}$. The percentage increase in the value of magnetic field inside the toroid will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \%$
- **(B)** $0.2 \%$
- **(C)** $0.1 \%$
- **(D)** $1 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $2 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given,
Susceptibility of material, $\chi_m=2 \times 10^{-2}$
Using $\mu_{\mathrm{r}}=1+\chi_{\mathrm{m}}=1+0.02=1.02$
$\mathrm{B}_{\text {final }}=\mu_{\mathrm{r}} \mathrm{B}_0$ (here, $\mathrm{B}_0=$ initial magnetic field)
$\%$ increase in magnetic field
$=\frac{\mathrm{B}_{\text {final }}-\mathrm{B}_0}{\mathrm{~B}_0} \times 100=\frac{\mu_{\mathrm{r}} \mathrm{B}_0-\mathrm{B}_0 \times 100}{\mathrm{~B}_0}$
$=\frac{(\chi+1)-1 \times 100}{1}=0.02 \times 100=2 \%$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The susceptibility of a magnetism at $300 \mathrm{~K}$ is $1.2 \times 10^{-5}$. The temperature at which the susceptibility increases to $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $150 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(B)** $200 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(C)** $250 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(D)** $20 \mathrm{~K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $200 \mathrm{~K}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \chi=\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{T}} \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{\chi_{1}}{\chi_{2}}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_{2}}{\mathrm{~T}_{1}} \Rightarrow \frac{1.2 \times 10^{-5}}{1.8 \times 10^{-5}}=\frac{\mathrm{T}_{2}}{300} \\ & \Rightarrow \mathrm{T}_{2} \Rightarrow \frac{12}{18} \times 300=200 \mathrm{~K} \end{aligned}$

</details>
