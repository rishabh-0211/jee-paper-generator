### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The atomic mass of ${ }_6 \mathrm{C}^{12}$ is 12.000000 u and that of ${ }_6 \mathrm{C}^{13}$ is 13.003354 u . The required energy to remove a neutron from ${ }_6 \mathrm{C}^{13}$, if mass of neutron is 1.008665 u , will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 62.5 MeV
- **(B)** 6.25 MeV
- **(C)** 4.95 MeV
- **(D)** 49.5 MeV

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 4.95 MeV**

**Detailed Solution:**

${ }_6 \mathrm{C}^{13}+$ Energy $\rightarrow{ }_6 \mathrm{C}^{12}+{ }_0 \mathrm{n}^1$
Mass defect, $\Delta \mathrm{m}=(12.000000+1.008665)-$ 13.003354
$=-0.00531 \mathrm{u}$
$\therefore$ Energyrequired $=\Delta \mathrm{m} \times 931.5=0.00531 \times 931.5$
$\mathrm{MeV}=4.95 \mathrm{MeV}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The mass of $_{7} \mathrm{~N}^{15}$ is $15.00011 \mathrm{amu}$, mass of $_{8} \mathrm{O}^{16}$ is 15.99492 amu and $m_{p}=1.00783$ amu. Determine binding energy of last proton of ${ }_{8} \mathrm{O}^{16}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2.13 \mathrm{MeV}$
- **(B)** $0.13 \mathrm{MeV}$
- **(C)** $10 \mathrm{MeV}$
- **(D)** $12.13 \mathrm{MeV}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $12.13 \mathrm{MeV}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{M}\left({ }_{8} \mathrm{O}^{16}\right)=\mathrm{M}\left({ }_{7} \mathrm{~N}^{15}\right)+1 \mathrm{~m}_{\mathrm{p}}$

binding energy of last proton $=M\left(N^{15}\right)+m_{P}-M\left({ }_{1} O^{16}\right)$

$=15.00011+1.00783-15.99492$

$=0.01302 \mathrm{amu}=12.13 \mathrm{MeV}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let binding energy per nucleon of nucleus is denoted by $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bn}}$ and radius of nucleus is denoted as $r$. If mass number of nuclei $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}$ and 64 and 125 respectively then

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(r_{A} < r_{B}, E_{b n A} < E_{b n B}\)
- **(B)** \(r_{A} < r_{B}, E_{b n A} < E_{b n A}\)
- **(C)** \(r_{A}=\frac{4}{5} r_{B}, E_{b n A} < E_{b n B}\)
- **(D)** \(r_{A} < r_{B}, E_{b n A} > E_{b n B}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) \(r_{A} < r_{B}, E_{b n A} > E_{b n B}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{r}_{0}(\mathrm{~A})^{1 / 3}$

r increases with increasing A mass number So, $r_{A}<r_{B}$ as mass number of $A$ is smaller 

$\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bn}}$ decreases with increasing $\mathrm{A}$ for $\mathrm{A}>56$ ${ }^{56} \mathrm{Fe}$ has highest $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bn}}$ value. So, $E_{b n}$ for $A=64$ is larger as compared to $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bn}}$ for nucleus with $\mathrm{A}=125$

$\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bnA}}>\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{bnB}}$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If the total binding energies of ${ }_{1}^{2} \mathrm{H},{ }_{2}^{4} \mathrm{He},$ ${ }_{26}^{56} \mathrm{Fe} \&{ }_{92}^{235} \mathrm{U}$ nuclei are 2.22,28.3,492 and 1786 MeV respectively, identify the most stable nucleus of the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** ${ }_{26}^{56} \mathrm{Fe}$
- **(B)** ${ }_{1}^{2} \mathrm{H}$
- **(C)** ${ }_{92}^{235} \mathrm{U}$
- **(D)** ${ }_{2}^{4} \mathrm{He}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) ${ }_{26}^{56} \mathrm{Fe}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{B.E}_{\mathrm{H}} &=\frac{2.22}{2}=1.11 \\ \mathrm{B.E}_{\mathrm{He}} &=\frac{28.3}{4}=7.08 \\ \mathrm{B.E}_{\mathrm{Fe}} &=\frac{492}{56}=8.78=\text { maximum } \\ \mathrm{B.E}_{\mathrm{U}} &=\frac{1786}{235}=7.6 \end{aligned}$
$\frac{56}{26} \mathrm{Fe}$ is most stable as it has maximum binding energy per nucleon.

</details>
