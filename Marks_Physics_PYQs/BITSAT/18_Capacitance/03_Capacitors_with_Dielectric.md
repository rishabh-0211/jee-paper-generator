### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A parallel plate capacitor with plate area A and plate separation $d=2 \mathrm{~m}$ has a capacitance of $4 \mu \mathrm{~F}$.
The new capacitance of the system if half of the space between them is filled with a dielectric material of dielectric constant $\mathrm{K}=3$ (as shown in figure) will be:
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/gnNODDb46rlSYm79fYRjnQTl5L8pmrwgZaF9dr7NDfc.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \mu \mathrm{~F}$
- **(B)** $32 \mu \mathrm{~F}$
- **(C)** $6 \mu \mathrm{~F}$
- **(D)** $8 \mu \mathrm{~F}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $6 \mu \mathrm{~F}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $C_i=\frac{A \epsilon_0}{d}=4 \mu F$
$C_f=\frac{A \epsilon_0}{d-t+\frac{t}{k}}=\frac{A \epsilon_0}{d-\frac{d}{2}+\frac{d}{2 \times 3}}$
$=\frac{A \in_0}{d\left(1-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{6}\right)}$
$=\frac{4 \mu F}{\frac{2}{3}}=6 \mu F$

</details>

- **(A)** $\frac{3 K \varepsilon_{0} A}{4 d}$
- **(B)** $\frac{4 K \varepsilon_{0} A}{3 d}$
- **(C)** $\frac{(K+1) \varepsilon_{0} A}{2 d}$
- **(D)** $\frac{K(K+3) \varepsilon_{0} A}{2(K+1) d}$

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{K(K+3) \varepsilon_{0} A}{2(K+1) d}$**

$$

$$

\therefore \quad c_{e q .}=\frac{c_{1} \times c_{2}}{c_{1}+c_{2}}+c_{3}=\frac{(3+\mathrm{K}) \mathrm{K} A \varepsilon_{0}}{2 d(\mathrm{~K}+1)}
