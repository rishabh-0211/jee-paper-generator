### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A capacitor of capacitance $C$ is charged to a potential V. The flux of the electric field through a closed surface enclosing the positive plate of the capacitor is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{C V}{2 \varepsilon_0}$
- **(B)** $\frac{2 \mathrm{CV}}{\varepsilon_0}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{CV}}{\varepsilon_0}$
- **(D)** Zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\mathrm{CV}}{\varepsilon_0}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the Gauss's law
$\begin{aligned}
& \phi=\frac{\mathrm{q}_{\text {in }}}{\epsilon_0}=\frac{\mathrm{Q}}{\epsilon_0} \quad \because \mathrm{Q}=\mathrm{CV} \\
& \therefore \phi=\frac{\mathrm{CV}}{\epsilon_0}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Calculate the area of the plates of a one farad parallel plate capacitor if separation between plates is $1 \mathrm{~mm}$ and plates are in vacuum

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
- **(B)** $0.3 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
- **(C)** $1.3 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}$
- **(D)** $1.13 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $1.13 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a parallel plate capacitor $\mathrm{C}=\frac{\varepsilon_{0} \mathrm{~A}}{\mathrm{~d}}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \quad & \mathrm{A}=\frac{\mathrm{Cd}}{\varepsilon_{0}}=\frac{1 \times 10^{-3}}{8.85 \times 10^{-12}} \\
&=1.13 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
This corresponds to area of square of side $10.6 \mathrm{~km}$ which shows that one farad is very large unit of capacitance.

</details>
