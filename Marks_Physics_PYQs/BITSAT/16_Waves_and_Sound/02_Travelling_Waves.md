### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A plane progressive wave is given by $y=2 \cos$ $2 \pi(330 t-x) \mathrm{m}$. The frequency of the wave is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 165 Hz
- **(B)** 330 Hz
- **(C)** 660 Hz
- **(D)** 340 Hz

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 330 Hz**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of wave is, $y=2 \cos 2 \pi(330 t-x)$ m
$y=A \cos (\omega t-k x)$
On comparing $\omega=2 \pi \times 330$
$2 \pi \mathrm{f}=2 \pi \times 330 \Rightarrow \mathrm{f}=330 \mathrm{~Hz}$
$[\because \omega=2 \pi \mathrm{~F}]$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If equation of transverse wave is $y=x_{0} \cos$ $2 \pi\left(\mathrm{nt}-\frac{\mathrm{x}}{\lambda}\right) .$ Maximum velocity of particle is twice of wave velocity, if $\lambda$ is-

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pi / 2 \mathrm{x}_{0}$
- **(B)** $2 \pi x_{0}$
- **(C)** $\pi$
- **(D)** $\pi x_{0}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\pi x_{0}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$y=x_{0} \cos 2 \pi\left(\right.$ nt $\left.-\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)$

$\mathrm{y}=\mathrm{x}_{0} \cos \frac{2 \pi}{\lambda}(\mathrm{vt}-\mathrm{x}) \quad[\because \mathrm{v}=\mathrm{n} \lambda]$

$\left(\frac{\mathrm{dy}}{\mathrm{dt}}\right)_{\max }=\mathrm{x}_{0} \times \frac{2 \pi}{\lambda} \mathrm{v}=2 \mathrm{v}($ given $) \therefore \lambda=\pi \mathrm{x}_{0}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The transverse displacement $y(x, t)$ of a wave on a string is given by 
$y(x, t)=e^{-\left(a x^{2}+b t^{2}+2 \sqrt{a b}\right.}$ xt $)$
This represents a

**Options:**

- **(A)** wave moving in $-x$ direction, speed $\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}$
- **(B)** standing wave of frequency $\sqrt{b}$
- **(C)** standing wave of frequency $\frac{1}{\sqrt{b}}$
- **(D)** wave moving in $+x$ direction, speed $\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) wave moving in $-x$ direction, speed $\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$y(x, t)=e^{-\left(a x^{2}+b t^{2}+2 \sqrt{a b} x t\right)}=e^{-(\sqrt{a x}+\sqrt{b t})^{2}}$
It is a function of type $y=f(\omega t+k x)$ $\therefore y(x, t)$ represents wave travelling along $-x$ direction.
$\text { Speed of wave }=\frac{\omega}{k}=\frac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{a}}=\sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}$

</details>
