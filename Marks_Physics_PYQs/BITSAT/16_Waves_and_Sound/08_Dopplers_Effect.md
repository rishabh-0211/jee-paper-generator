### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The engine of a train moving with speed $10 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ towards a platform sounds a whistle at frequency $400 \mathrm{~Hz}$. The frequency heard by a passenger inside the train is (neglect air speed. Speed of sound in air $330 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ )

**Options:**

- **(A)** $200 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(B)** $400 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(C)** $412 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(D)** $388 \mathrm{~Hz}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $400 \mathrm{~Hz}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The relative velocity of a passenger with respect to source of sound (engine of train) is 0 . So there will be no Doppler's effect. So frequency heard is $400 \mathrm{~Hz}$.

</details>
