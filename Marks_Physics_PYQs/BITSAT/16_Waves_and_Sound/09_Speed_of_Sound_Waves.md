### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The speed of sound in oxygen at S.T.P. will be approximately:
(Given, $\left.\mathrm{R}=8.3 \mathrm{JK}^{-1}, \gamma=1.4\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $315 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $333 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $341 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $325 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $315 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At STP
Temperature, $T=273 \mathrm{~K}$
Molecular mass of oxygen, $\mathrm{M}=32 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~kg}$
Speed of sound is given by
$\mathrm{v}=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma \mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{M}}}=\sqrt{\frac{1.4 \times 8.3 \times 273}{32 \times 10^{-3}}}$
$=314.8541 \simeq 315 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The ratio of speed of sound in hydrogen gas to the speed of sound in oxygen gas at the same temperature is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4: 1$
- **(B)** $1: 2$
- **(C)** $1: 4$
- **(D)** $1: 1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $4: 1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{H}_2}=2 ; \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}_2}=32$
Speed of sound, $\mathrm{v}=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma \mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{M}}}$
$\Rightarrow \quad v \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{M}}$
$\therefore \quad \frac{\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{H}_2}}{\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{O}_2}}=\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{O}_2}}{\mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{H}_2}}}=\sqrt{\frac{32}{2}}=4: 1$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Transverse waves of the same frequency are generated in two steel wires $A$ and $B$. The diameter of $A$ is twice that of $B$ and the tension in $A$ is half that in $B$. The ratio of the velocities of the waves in $A$ and $B$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1: 2$
- **(B)** $1: \sqrt{2}$
- **(C)** $1: 2 \sqrt{2}$
- **(D)** $3: 2 \sqrt{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $1: 2 \sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The velocity of transverse waves is given by $v=\sqrt{\frac{T}{m}}$ where, $T=$ Tension and $m=$ mass per unit length of the wire. If $r$ is the radius of the wire and $\rho$ its density then,
$m=\pi r^2 \rho$
$\therefore \quad v=\sqrt{\frac{T}{m}}=\sqrt{\frac{T}{\pi r^2 \rho}}$
$\therefore \quad v_A=\frac{\sqrt{T_A}}{r_A \sqrt{\pi \rho}}$
and $\quad v_B=\frac{\sqrt{T_B}}{r_B \sqrt{\pi \rho}}$
Now, $\quad \frac{v_A}{v_B}=\sqrt{\frac{T_A}{T_B}} \cdot \frac{r_B}{r_A}$
$\because \quad r_A=2 r_B$ and $T_A=\frac{1}{2} T_B$
Hence, $\quad \frac{v_A}{v_B}=\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{2}}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A submarine $A$ travelling at $17 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ is being chased along the line of its velocity by another submarine $B$ travelling at $34 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. B sends a sonar signal of $600 \mathrm{~Hz}$ to detect $A$ and receives a reflected sound of frequency $v$. The of $v$ is
[Speed of sound in water $=1500 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$ ]

**Options:**

- **(A)** $613.7 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(B)** $6137 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(C)** $62 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(D)** $539 \mathrm{~Hz}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $613.7 \mathrm{~Hz}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\therefore$ Given, velocity of submarine $A, v_A=17 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
Velocity of submarine $B, v_B=34 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
Signal sent by submarine $B$ is detected by submarine $A$ can be shown as
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/Jq4hW_ynZTPjtqs3fQMJASQbe7HfYMizbGu9xW3UZps.original.fullsize.png">

Freqency of the signal, $f_0=600 \mathrm{~Hz}$
Speed of sound in water $v_s=1500 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
Frequency received by submarine $A$ is
$$
f_1=\left(\frac{v_{\mathrm{S}}-v_A}{v_{\mathrm{S}}-v_B}\right) f_0=\left[\frac{1500-17}{1500-34}\right] \times 600 \mathrm{~Hz}
$$
$$
f_1=\frac{1483}{1466} \times 600
$$
Frequency received by submarine $B$ is
$$
f_2=\left(\frac{v_{\mathrm{S}}+v_B}{v_s+v_A}\right) f_1
$$
Substituting given values and $f_1$ value from Eq. (i), we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_2 & =\left(\frac{1500+34}{1500+17}\right) \times\left[\frac{1483}{1466} \times 600\right] \\
f_2 & =1.0112 \times 1.0115 \times 600 \\
f_2 & =613.7 \mathrm{~Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Q5. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The displacement y of a particle in a medium can be expressed as, $y=10^{-6} \sin \left(100 t+20 x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) m$ where $t$ is in second and $x$ in meter. The speed of the wave is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $2000 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $5 \pi \mathrm{m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From equation given,

$\begin{array}{l}

\omega=100 \\

\text { and } \mathrm{k}=20 \\

\mathrm{v}=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{k}}=\frac{100}{20}=5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}

\end{array}$

</details>
