### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An organ pipe, open from both end produces 5 beats per second when vibrated with a source of frequency $200 \mathrm{~Hz}$. The second harmonic of the same pipes produces 10 beats per second with a source of frequency $420 \mathrm{~Hz}$. The fundamental frequency of organ pipe is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $195 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(B)** $205 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(C)** $190 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(D)** $210 \mathrm{~Hz}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $205 \mathrm{~Hz}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the fundamental frequency of organ pipe be $\mathrm{f}$

Case I : $f=200 \pm 5=205 \mathrm{~Hz}$ or $195 \mathrm{~Hz}$

Case II : frequency of 2 nd harmonic of organ pipe $=2 \mathrm{f}$ (as is clear from the second figure) $2 \mathrm{f}=420 \pm 10$ or $\mathrm{f}=210 \pm 5$ or $\mathrm{f}=205$ or 215

Hence fundamental frequency oforgan pipe $=205 \mathrm{~Hz}$

### Q3. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The fundamental frequency of an open organ pipe is $300 \mathrm{~Hz}$. The first overtone of this pipe has same frequency as first overtone of a closed organ pipe. If speed of sound is $330 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$, then the length of closed organ pipe is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $41 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(B)** $37 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(C)** $31 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(D)** $80 \mathrm{~cm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $41 \mathrm{~cm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For open pipe, $\mathrm{n}=\frac{\mathrm{v}}{2 \ell},$ where $\mathrm{n}_{0}$ is the
fundamental frequency of open pipe. $\therefore \ell=\frac{\mathrm{v}}{2 \mathrm{n}}=\frac{330}{2 \times 300}=\frac{11}{20}$
As freq. of 1 st overtone of open pipe $=$ freq. of 1 st overtone of closed pipe
$\begin{aligned} \therefore \quad 2 \frac{\mathrm{v}}{2 \ell}=3 \frac{\mathrm{v}}{4 \ell^{\prime}} \Rightarrow \ell^{\prime} &=\frac{3 \ell}{4}=\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{11}{20} \\ &=41.25 \mathrm{~cm} \end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two tuning forks $A$ and $B$ sounded together give 6 beats per second. With an air resonance tube closed at one end, the two forks give resonance when the two air columns are $24 \mathrm{~cm}$ and 25 $\mathrm{cm}$ respectively. Calculate the frequencies of forks.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $120 \mathrm{~Hz}, 124 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(B)** $110 \mathrm{~Hz}, 114 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(C)** $150 \mathrm{~Hz}, 144 \mathrm{~Hz}$
- **(D)** $170 \mathrm{~Hz}, 118 \mathrm{~Hz}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $150 \mathrm{~Hz}, 144 \mathrm{~Hz}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the frequency of the first fork be $\mathrm{f}_{1}$ and that of second be $\mathrm{f}_{2}$
We then have, $\mathrm{f}_{1}=\frac{\mathrm{v}}{4 \times 24}$ and $\mathrm{f}_{2}=\frac{\mathrm{v}}{4 \times 25}$
We also see that $\mathrm{f}_{1}>\mathrm{f}_{2}$
$$
\therefore \mathrm{f}_{1}-\mathrm{f}_{2}=6
$$
and $\frac{\mathrm{f}_{1}}{\mathrm{f}_{2}}=\frac{24}{25}$
Solving (i) and (ii), we get $\mathrm{f}_{1}=150 \mathrm{~Hz}$ and $\quad \mathrm{f}_{2}=144 \mathrm{~Hz}$

</details>
