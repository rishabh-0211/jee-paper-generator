### Q3. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Electrical resistance of certain materials, known as superconductors, changes abruptly from a non-zero value to zero as their temperature is lowered below a critical temperature $T_C(0)$. An interesting property of superconductors is that their critical temperature becomes smaller than $T_C(0)$ if they are placed in a magnetic field i.e., the critical temperature $T_C(B)$ is a function of the magnetic field strength $B$. The dependence of $T_C(B)$ on $B$ is shown in the figure.<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/_FYhQfph9ZU_3U1GDvqFWoqKuV9LURP8FOgdidn3QG8.original.fullsize.png">
<strong>Question:</strong>
A superconductor has $T_C(0)=100 \mathrm{~K}$. When a magnetic field of $7.5$ Tesla is applied, its $T_C$ decreases to $75 \mathrm{~K}$. For this material one can definitely say that when (Note : $\mathrm{T}$ = Tesla)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $B=5 \mathrm{~T}, T_C(B)=80 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(B)** $B=5 \mathrm{~T}, 75 \mathrm{~K} < T_C(B) < 100 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(C)** $B=10 \mathrm{~T}, 75 \mathrm{~K} < T_C(B) < 100 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(D)** $B=10 \mathrm{~T}, T_C(B)=70 \mathrm{~K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $B=5 \mathrm{~T}, 75 \mathrm{~K} < T_C(B) < 100 \mathrm{~K}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

With increase in temperature, $T_C$ is decreasing.
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_C(0) & =100 \mathrm{~K} \\
T_C & =75 \mathrm{~K} \text { at } B=7.5 \mathrm{~T}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, at $B=5 \mathrm{~T}, \quad T_C$ should lie between $75 \mathrm{~K}$ and $100 \mathrm{~K}$. Hence, the correct option should be (b).

</details>

### Q5. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Consider a thin square sheet of side $L$ and thickness $t$, made of a material of resistivity $\rho$. The resistance between two opposite faces, shown by the shaded areas in the figure is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/jQWT8TUcKYNLj76kfHeUfDLbThIKMquc23PlGL0NDCw.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** directly proportional to $L$
- **(B)** directly proportional to $t$
- **(C)** independent of $L$
- **(D)** independent of $t$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) independent of $L$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$R=\frac{\rho(L)}{A}=\frac{\rho L}{t L}=\frac{\rho}{t}$ ie, $R$ is independent of $L$. Hence the correct option is (c).
