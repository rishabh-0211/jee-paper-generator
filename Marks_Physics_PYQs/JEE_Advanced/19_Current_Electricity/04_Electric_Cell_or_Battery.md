### Q1. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

Two batteries of different emfs and different internal resistances are connected as shown. The voltage across $A B$ in volt is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/W2cdpJKmwYYphVd0NTggoakyZDVjRGpVU8m5_97fO6Y.original.fullsize.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **5**

**Detailed Solution:**

$V_{A B}=$ Equivalent emf of two batteries in parallel.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{E_1 / r_1+E_1 / r_2}{1 / r_1+1 / r_2} \\
& =\frac{(6 / 1)+(3 / 2)}{(1 / 1)+(1 / 2)}=5 \mathrm{~V}
\end{aligned}
$$
$\therefore$ Answer is 5 .
Analysis of Question
(i) In my opinion this is the simplest question of JEE 2011.
(ii) When no current is drawn from this equivalent battery, then
$$
V_{A B}=V=E
$$
Otherwise, $V=E \pm$ ir
(iii) Similar type of question was asked in JEE 1981 and cancelled paper of JEE 1997.
