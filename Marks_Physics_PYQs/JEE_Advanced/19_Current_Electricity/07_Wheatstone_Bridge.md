### Q4. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A meter bridge is set up as shown in figure, to determine an unknown resistance $X$ using a standard $10 \Omega$ resistor. The galvanometer shows null point when tapping key is at $52 \mathrm{~cm}$ mark. The end-corrections are $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ and $2 \mathrm{~cm}$ respectively for the ends $A$ and $B$. The determined value of $X$ is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/-5wAQXPr0ogSu-GX8RP1V0KuFgaPpJl2ZFwXoZMVR7U.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $10.2 \Omega$
- **(B)** $10.6 \Omega$
- **(C)** $10.8 \Omega$
- **(D)** $11.1 \Omega$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $10.6 \Omega$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the concept of balanced Wheatstone bridge, we have
$$
\begin{array}{rlrl} 
& \frac{P}{Q} & =\frac{R}{S} \\
\therefore & & \frac{X}{(52+1)} & =\frac{10}{(48+2)} \\
\therefore & & X & =\frac{10 \times 53}{50}=10.6 \Omega
\end{array}
$$
$\therefore$ Correct option is (b).
Analysis of Question
Question is moderately tough, because normally end corrections are not taught in the coaching/schools in this type of problem.

### Q5. JEE Advanced 2008 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Statement $1 \mathrm{In}$ a Meter Bridge experiment, null point for an unknown resistance is measured. Now, the unknown resistance is put inside an enclosure maintained at a higher temperature. The null point can be obtained at the same point as before by decreasing the value of the standard resistance.
and Statement 2 Resistance of a metal increase with increase in temperature.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement 1 is true, Statement 2 is true, Statement 2 is a correct explanation for Statement 1.
- **(B)** Statement 1 is true, Statement 2 is true, Statement 2 is not a correct explanation for Statement 1.
- **(C)** Statement 1 is true, Statement 2 is false.
- **(D)** Statement 1 is false, Statement 2 is true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Statement 1 is false, Statement 2 is true**

**Detailed Solution:**

With increase in temperature, the value of unknown resistance will increase.
In balanced Wheatstone bridge condition, $\frac{R}{X}=\frac{l_1}{l_2}$
Here, $R=$ value of standard resistance,
$X=$ value of unknown resistance .
To take null point at same point or $\frac{l_1}{l_2}$ to remain unchanged, $\frac{R}{X}$ should also remain unchanged. Therefore, if $X$ is increasing $R$ should also increase.
$\therefore$ correct option is (d).

</details>

### Q6. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A resistance of $2 \Omega$ is connected across one gap of a meter-bridge (the length of the wire is $100 \mathrm{~cm}$ ) and an unknown resistance, greater than $2 \Omega$, is connected across the other gap. When these resistances are interchanged, the balance point shifts by $20 \mathrm{~cm}$. Neglecting any corrections, the unknown resistance is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 \Omega$
- **(B)** $4 \Omega$
- **(C)** $5 \Omega$
- **(D)** $6 \Omega$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3 \Omega$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& R & >2 \Omega \\
\therefore & 100-x & >x \\
\text { Applying } & \frac{P}{Q} & =\frac{R}{S}
\end{aligned}
$$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/bj5wXDgKnTfN4Vg1PIwLyg-p-M4Vw5b76zBexKtpjvI.original.fullsize.png">
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/k0HDe0hHZT2EmygS2hd4fIRONvnb06ZJCeC2L7TIA9I.original.fullsize.png">

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{2}{R}=\frac{x}{100-x} \\
& \frac{R}{2}=\frac{x+20}{80-x}
\end{aligned}
$$
Solving Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$$
R=3 \Omega
$$
$\therefore$ Answer is (a).

</details>
