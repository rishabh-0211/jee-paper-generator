### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A metal wire of cross-sectional area $0.5$ mm$^2$ and length $100$ m is connected across a battery of e.m.f. $2$ V and internal resistance $1\ \Omega$. The density, atomic mass and electrical conductivity of the metal are $6.35 \times 10^3$ kg m$^{-3}$, $63.5$ gm/mole and $2 \times 10^8$ mho m$^{-1}$, respectively. Assuming one conduction electron per atom of the metal, the drift velocity (in mm s$^{-1}$) of the electrons in the wire is:
[Take Avogadro's number as $6 \times 10^{23}$ and charge of the electron as $1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C.]

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.052$
- **(B)** $0.104$
- **(C)** $0.208$
- **(D)** $0.156$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $0.208$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The number density of conduction electrons $n$ is given by the number of atoms per unit volume, since there is one conduction electron per atom:

$n = \dfrac{d \times N_A}{M}$

Substituting the given values:

$n = \dfrac{6.35 \times 10^3 \times 6 \times 10^{23}}{63.5 \times 10^{-3}} = 6 \times 10^{28} \text{ m}^{-3}$

The resistance of the wire $R$ is:

$R = \dfrac{L}{\sigma A}$

$R = \dfrac{100}{2 \times 10^8 \times 0.5 \times 10^{-6}} = \dfrac{100}{10^2} = 1\ \Omega$

The total resistance of the circuit is $R_{total} = R + r = 1 + 1 = 2\ \Omega$.

The current in the circuit is:

$I = \dfrac{E}{R_{total}} = \dfrac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ A}$

The drift velocity $v_d$ is given by the relation $I = n e A v_d$:

$v_d = \dfrac{I}{n e A}$

$v_d = \dfrac{1}{6 \times 10^{28} \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \times 0.5 \times 10^{-6}}$

$v_d = \dfrac{1}{4.8 \times 10^3} \text{ m s}^{-1}$

Converting to mm s$^{-1}$:

$v_d = \dfrac{1000}{4800} \text{ mm s}^{-1} = \dfrac{5}{24} \text{ mm s}^{-1} \approx 0.208 \text{ mm s}^{-1}$

Answer: $0.208$

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** Heat produced in bar $B C$ is 4 times the heat produced in bar $A B$
- **(B)** Electric field in both halves is equal
- **(C)** Current density across $A B$ is doubled that of across $B C$
- **(D)** Potential difference across $A B$ is 4 times that of across $B C$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Heat produced in bar $B C$ is 4 times the heat produced in bar $A B$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Current flowing through both the bars is equal. Now, the heat produced is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
H & =I^2 R t \text { or } H \propto R \text { or } \frac{H_{A B}}{H_{B C}}=\frac{R_{A B}}{R_{B C}} \\
& =\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2 r}\right)^2}{\left(\frac{1}{r}\right)^2} \\
& =\frac{1}{4} \text { or } H_{B C}=4 H_{A B}
\end{aligned}
$$
