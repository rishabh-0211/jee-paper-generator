### Q2. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the resultant of all the external forces acting on a system of particles is zero, then from an inertial frame, one can surely say that

**Options:**

- **(A)** linear momentum of the system does not change in time
- **(B)** kinetic energy of the system does not change in time
- **(C)** angular momentum of the system does not change in time
- **(D)** potential energy of the system does not change in time

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) linear momentum of the system does not change in time**

**Detailed Solution:**

On a system of particles if,
$$
\Sigma \overrightarrow{\mathbf{F}}_{\mathrm{ext} .}=0
$$
then $\quad \overrightarrow{\mathbf{P}}_{\text {system }}=$ constant
No other conclusions can be drawn.

</details>

### Q3. JEE Advanced 2008 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Two balls, having linear momenta $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1=p \hat{\mathbf{i}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2=-p \hat{\mathbf{i}}$, undergo a collision in free space. There is no external force acting on the balls. Let $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}$ be their final momenta. The following option(s) is/are NOT ALLOWED for any non-zero value of $p, a_1, a_2, b_1, b_2, c_1$ and $c_2$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}=a_1 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}+c_1 \hat{\mathbf{k}}, \overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=a_2 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_2 \hat{\mathbf{j}}$
- **(B)** $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}=c_1 \hat{\mathbf{k}}, \overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=c_2 \hat{\mathbf{k}}$
- **(C)** $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}=a_1 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}+c_1 \hat{\mathbf{k}}$
- **(D)** $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}=a_1 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}$
$\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=a_2 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_2 \hat{\mathbf{j}}-c_1 \hat{\mathbf{k}}$
$\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=a_2 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_1^{\prime}=a_1 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}$
$\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=a_2 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_2 \hat{\mathbf{j}}-c_1 \hat{\mathbf{k}}$
$\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}}_2^{\prime}=a_2 \hat{\mathbf{i}}+b_1 \hat{\mathbf{j}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Initial momentum of the system $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_1}+\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_2}=0$
$\therefore$ Final momentum $\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_1^{\prime}}+\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_2^{\prime}}$ should also be zero.
Option (b) is allowed because if we putc $c_1=-c_2 \neq 0, \overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_1^{\prime}}+\overrightarrow{\mathbf{p}_2^{\prime}}$ will be zero. Similarly, we can check other options.
$\therefore$ correct options are (a) and (d).

</details>
