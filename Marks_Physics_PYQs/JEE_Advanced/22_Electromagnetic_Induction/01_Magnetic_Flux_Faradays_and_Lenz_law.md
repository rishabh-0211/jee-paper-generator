### Q1. JEE Advanced 2020 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A light disc made of aluminum (a nonmagnetic material) is kept horizontally and is free to rotate about its axis as shown in the figure. A strong magnet is held vertically at a point above the disc away from its axis. On revolving the magnet about the axis of the disc, the disc will (figure is schematic and not drawn to scale)

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/6676a6e9-bfbc-4e76-9694-e6cc096fb1f3-image.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** rotate in the direction opposite to the direction of magnet&#39;s motion
- **(B)** rotate in the same direction as the direction of magnet&#39;s motion
- **(C)** not rotate and its temperature will remain unchanged
- **(D)** not rotate but its temperature will slowly rise

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) rotate in the same direction as the direction of magnet&#39;s motion**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>When we start rotating the magnet above the disc, eddy currents will be generated in the disc. Now we know from Lenz&#39;s law that the induced emf will drive the current such that it opposes the cause of the production of induced emf. Hence the disc will rotate in the same direction as the direction of magnet&#39;s motion</p>

</details>

### Q2. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A current carrying infinitely long wire is kept along the diameter of a circular wire loop, without touching it, the correct statement(s) is(are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** The emf induced in the loop is zero if the current is constant.
- **(B)** The emf induced in the loop is finite if the current is constant.
- **(C)** The emf induced in the loop is zero if the current decreases at a steady rate.
- **(D)** The emf induced in the loop is infinite if the current decreases at a steady rate.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) The emf induced in the loop is zero if the current decreases at a steady rate.**

**Detailed Solution:**

If the current is constant, the emf induced in the loop zero. Emf will be induced in the circular wire loop when flux through it changes with time.

$\mathrm{e}=-\frac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta \mathrm{t}}$

when the current is constant, the flux changing through it will be zero.

Also, if the current decreases at steady rate, the emf. induced in the loop is zero.

When the current is decreasing at a steady rate then the change in the flux (decreasing inwards) on the right half of the wire is equal to the change in flux (decreasing outwards) on the left half of the wire such that $\Delta \phi$ through the circular loop is zero.

- **(A)** $I_1>I_2$

- **(D)** $I_1$ is in the direction $a b$ and $I_2$ is in the direction $d c$
