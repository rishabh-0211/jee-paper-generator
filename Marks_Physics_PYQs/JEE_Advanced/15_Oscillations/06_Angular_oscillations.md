### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

A tank contains two immiscible liquids of densities $6\rho$ and $2\rho$. The higher density liquid is filled up to a height $L/2$ from the bottom. A thin rod of density $\rho$ and length $L$ is fully immersed and hinged at the bottom so that it can oscillate freely, as shown in the figure. If the rod is slightly disturbed from its equilibrium, the time period of small oscillations is $\dfrac{2\pi}{n}\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$, where $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity. The value of $n$ is:
<img style="width: 300px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q25_d1.png" />

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **1.73**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $A$ be the cross-sectional area of the rod.

Mass of the rod, $M = \rho A L$.

Weight of the rod, $W = \rho A L g$, acting at a distance $L/2$ from the hinge.

When the rod is displaced by a small angle $\theta$, the restoring torque is provided by the buoyant forces from the two liquids.

Buoyant force due to the lower liquid, $F_{B1} = (6\rho) A (L/2) g = 3\rho A L g$.

This force acts at the center of the immersed part in the lower liquid, which is at a distance $L/4$ from the hinge.

Torque due to $F_{B1}$ is $\tau_{B1} = F_{B1} (L/4) \theta = (3\rho A L g) (L/4) \theta = \dfrac{3}{4} \rho A L^2 g \theta$.

Buoyant force due to the upper liquid, $F_{B2} = (2\rho) A (L/2) g = \rho A L g$.

This force acts at the center of the immersed part in the upper liquid, which is at a distance $L/2 + L/4 = 3L/4$ from the hinge.

Torque due to $F_{B2}$ is $\tau_{B2} = F_{B2} (3L/4) \theta = (\rho A L g) (3L/4) \theta = \dfrac{3}{4} \rho A L^2 g \theta$.

Destabilizing torque due to gravity is $\tau_g = W (L/2) \theta = (\rho A L g) (L/2) \theta = \dfrac{1}{2} \rho A L^2 g \theta$.

Net restoring torque, $\tau_{net} = \tau_{B1} + \tau_{B2} - \tau_g = \left(\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{2}\right) \rho A L^2 g \theta = \rho A L^2 g \theta$.

Moment of inertia of the rod about the hinge, $I = \dfrac{M L^2}{3} = \dfrac{\rho A L^3}{3}$.

The equation of motion is $I \alpha = -\tau_{net}$

$\dfrac{\rho A L^3}{3} \alpha = -\rho A L^2 g \theta$

$\alpha = -\dfrac{3g}{L} \theta$

This represents simple harmonic motion with $\omega^2 = \dfrac{3g}{L}$.

Time period, $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega} = \dfrac{2\pi}{\sqrt{3}} \sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}$.

Answer: $\sqrt{3} = 1.73$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A metal rod of length $L$ and mass $m$ is pivoted at one end. A thin disc of mass $M$ and radius $R( < L)$ is attached at its centre to the free end of the rod. Consider two ways the disc is attached. Case A-the disc is not free to rotate about its centre and case $B$-the disc is free to rotate about its centre. The rod-disc system performs
SHM in vertical plane after being released from the same displaced position. Which of the following statement(s) is/are true?<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/2Z9bbEPubSFDgbkK7f83upg2RB4bCyUHjrCj_uu9Q6U.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** Restoring torque in case $A=$ Restoring torque in case $B$
- **(B)** Restoring torque in case $A < $ Restoring torque in case $B$
- **(C)** Angular frequency for case $A>$ Angular frequency for case $B$
- **(D)** Angular frequency for case $A < $ Angular frequency for case $B$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Restoring torque in case $A < $ Restoring torque in case $B$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tau_A=\tau_B=m g \frac{L}{2} \sin \theta+\operatorname{MgL} \sin \theta$
$=$ Restoring torque about point $O$.
In case $A$, moment of inertia will be more. Hence, angular acceleration $(\alpha=\tau / I)$ will be less. Therefore, angular frequency will be less.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/1s5RswcuRs38mBLtlc7w0I0vX4MgAYvlJTzjP3tzLLk.original.fullsize.png">

$\therefore$ Correct options are (a) and (d).
