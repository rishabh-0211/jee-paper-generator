### Q2. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A uniform rod of length $L$ and mass $M$ is pivoted at the centre. Its two ends are attached to two springs of equal spring constants $k$. The springs are fixed to rigid supports as shown in the figure, and rod is free to oscillate in the horizontal plane. The rod is gently pushed through a small angle $\theta$ in one direction and released. The frequency of oscillation is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/zEebOkNjPBbVUWGAOGgVtZ1z2igySczwe95j7PSisvY.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{2 k}{M}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{k}{M}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{6 k}{M}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{24 k}{M}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{6 k}{M}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/DrdMlLTmdrM4vZuHYllhDZoQTWGug-jp8kdYa7DO4KM.original.fullsize.png">

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \text { Restoring torque }=-(2 k x) \cdot \frac{L}{2} \\
& \qquad \alpha=-\frac{k L(L / 2 \theta)}{I}=-\left[\frac{k L^2 / 2}{M L^2 / 12}\right] \cdot \theta
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& =-\left(\frac{6 k}{M}\right) \theta \\
\therefore \quad f & =\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\left|\frac{\alpha}{\theta}\right|}=\frac{1}{2 \pi} \sqrt{\frac{6 k}{M}}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
