For <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>a</mi><mo>&#8810;</mo><mi>R</mi></math> the magnitude of the work done in the process is given by <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mfenced><mrow><mn>4</mn><mi>&#960;</mi><msub><mi>P</mi><mn>0</mn></msub><mi>R</mi><msup><mi>a</mi><mn>2</mn></msup></mrow></mfenced><mi>X</mi><mo>,</mo></math> where <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>X</mi></math> is a constant and <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>&#947;</mi><mo>=</mo><msub><mi>C</mi><mi>p</mi></msub><mo>/</mo><msub><mi>C</mi><mi>V</mi></msub><mo>=</mo><mn>41</mn><mo>/</mo><mn>30</mn></math>. The value of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi>X</mi></math> is______

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **2.05**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{W}=(\Delta \mathrm{P})_{\text {avg }} \times 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a} \\
&=\left|\frac{\mathrm{dP}}{2} \cdot 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a}\right|
\end{aligned}\)
(for small change \((\Delta \mathrm{P})_{\text {avg }}<\mathrm{P}>\) arithmetic mean)
\(=\mathrm{PV} \text { gamma }=\mathrm{c} \Rightarrow \mathrm{dP}=-\gamma \frac{\mathrm{P}}{\mathrm{V}} \mathrm{dV}=-\frac{\gamma \mathrm{P}_0}{\mathrm{~V}} 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a}\)
\(=\frac{\gamma \mathrm{P}_0}{2 \mathrm{~V}} \times 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a} \times 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a}\)
\(=\frac{\gamma \mathrm{P}_0}{2 \times 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^3} 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a} \times 4 \pi \mathrm{R}^2 \mathrm{a}\)
\(=\left(4 \mathrm{pRP} \times \mathrm{a}^2\right) \frac{3 \gamma}{2}\)
\(\therefore \mathrm{x}=2.05\)

</details>

### Q9. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
$P_{14-16}$ : Paragraph for Questions Nos. 14 to 16 A fixed thermally conducting cylinder has a radius $R$ and height $L_0$. The cylinder is open at its bottom and has a small hole at its top. A piston of mass $M$ is held at a distance $L$ from the top surface as shown in the figure. The atmospheric pressure is $p_0$.<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/4uUwDqNsXDagR3met7Ief3ZxT31gi025zIfyrEsgmZk.original.fullsize.png">
<strong>Question:</strong>
The piston is taken completely out of the cylinder. The hole at the top is sealed. A water tank is brought below the cylinder and put in a position so that the water surface in the tank is at the same level as the top of the cylinder as shown in the figure. The density of the water is $\rho$. In equilibrium, the height $H$ of the water column in the cylinder satisfies<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/u6L69mqUQ4YOY0tduE71-0P8gZFsbvh9f1xHSgJEuBU.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2+p_0\left(L_0-H\right)+L_0 p_0=0$
- **(B)** $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2-p_0\left(L_0-H\right)-L_0 p_0=0$
- **(C)** $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2+p_0\left(L_0-H\right)-L_0 p_0=0$
- **(D)** $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2-p_0\left(L_0-H\right)+L_0 p_0=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2+p_0\left(L_0-H\right)-L_0 p_0=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{gathered}
p_1=p_2 \\
p_0+\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)=p
\end{gathered}
$$
Now, applying $p_1 V_1=p_2 V_2$
for the air inside the cylinder, we have
$$
p_0\left(L_0\right)=p\left(L_0-H\right)
$$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/7oQA8KNhtTUSHFHvt9EQSIUJcVfMbFX9a2U9386X-5c.original.fullsize.png">

$\therefore \quad p=\frac{p_0 L_0}{L_0-H}$
Substituting in Eq. (i), we have
$$
p_0+\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)=\frac{p_0 L_0}{L_0-H}
$$
or $\rho g\left(L_0-H\right)^2+p_0\left(L_0-H\right)-p_0 L_0=0$
$\therefore$ Option (c) is correct.

</details>

### Q10. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
$P_{14-16}$ : Paragraph for Questions Nos. 14 to 16 A fixed thermally conducting cylinder has a radius $R$ and height $L_0$. The cylinder is open at its bottom and has a small hole at its top. A piston of mass $M$ is held at a distance $L$ from the top surface as shown in the figure. The atmospheric pressure is $p_0$.<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/4uUwDqNsXDagR3met7Ief3ZxT31gi025zIfyrEsgmZk.original.fullsize.png">
<strong>Question:</strong>
While the piston is at a distance $2 L$ from the top, the hole at the top is sealed. The piston is then released, to a position where it can stay in equilibrium. In this condition, the distance of the piston from the top is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\frac{2 p_0 \pi R^2}{\pi R^2 p_0+M g}\right)(2 L)$
- **(B)** $\left(\frac{p_0 \pi R^2-M g}{\pi R^2 p_0}\right)(2 L)$
- **(C)** $\left(\frac{p_0 \pi R^2+M g}{\pi R^2 p_0}\right)(2 L)$
- **(D)** $\left(\frac{p_0 \pi R^2}{\pi R^2 p_0-M g}\right)(2 L)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(\frac{p_0 \pi R^2}{\pi R^2 p_0-M g}\right)(2 L)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $p$ be the pressure in equilibrium
Then,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \quad p=p_0-\frac{M g}{A}=p_0-\frac{M g}{\pi R^2} \\
& \text { Applying } \quad p_1 V_1=p_2 V_2 \\
& p_0(2 A L)=(p)\left(A L^{\prime}\right) \\
& L^{\prime}=\frac{2 p_0 L}{p}=\left(\frac{p_0}{p_0-\frac{M g}{\pi R^2}}\right) \text { (2L) } \\
& =\left(\frac{p_0 \pi R^2}{\pi R^2 p_0-M g}\right)(2 L) \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
$\therefore$ Option (d) is correct.

</details>
