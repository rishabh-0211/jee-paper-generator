### Q2. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A real gas behaves like an ideal gas if its

**Options:**

- **(A)** pressure and temperature are both high
- **(B)** pressure and temperature are both low
- **(C)** pressure is high and temperature is low
- **(D)** pressure is low and temperature is high

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) pressure is low and temperature is high**

**Detailed Solution:**

A real gas behaves like an ideal gas at low pressure and high temperature. $\therefore$ correct option is (d).

</details>
