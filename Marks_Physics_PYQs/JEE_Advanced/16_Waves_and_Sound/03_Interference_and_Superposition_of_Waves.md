### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

List-I shows four configurations made of straight and semi-circular narrow tubes containing air. A sound wave of wavelength $\lambda = 0.29$ m enters these structures at the point $S$ and a sound detector is placed at $D$. Between the points $S$ and $D$, the sound travels only through the tubes. List-II contains the possible smallest values of $l$ (refer to the figures) for which the detector $D$ records maximum amplitude. Ignore effects of sharp corners. [Given $\cos(15^\circ) = 0.97$]
Choose the option that best describes the match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II.
<table style="border: 1px solid black; border-collapse: collapse; width: auto;"><tr><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">List-I</th><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">List-II</th></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(P) <img style="width: 200px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q29_d1.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(1) $1.32$ m</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(Q) <img style="width: 220px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q29_d2.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(2) $1.19$ m</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(R) <img style="width: 190px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q29_d3.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(3) $0.51$ m</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(S) <img style="width: 220px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q29_d4.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(4) $0.29$ m</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;"></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(5) $0.13$ m</td></tr></table>

**Options:**

- **(A)** P$\rightarrow$4, Q$\rightarrow$3, R$\rightarrow$5, S$\rightarrow$1
- **(B)** P$\rightarrow$4, Q$\rightarrow$3, R$\rightarrow$1, S$\rightarrow$5
- **(C)** P$\rightarrow$3, Q$\rightarrow$4, R$\rightarrow$1, S$\rightarrow$2
- **(D)** P$\rightarrow$3, Q$\rightarrow$4, R$\rightarrow$5, S$\rightarrow$2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) P$\rightarrow$3, Q$\rightarrow$4, R$\rightarrow$5, S$\rightarrow$2**

**Detailed Solution:**

For maximum amplitude at the detector $D$, the path difference $\Delta x$ between the two paths must be an integer multiple of the wavelength $\lambda$. For the smallest value of $l$, we take $\Delta x = \lambda = 0.29$ m.

For configuration (P):
The sound travels through a straight tube of length $l$ and a semi-circular tube of diameter $l$.
Path difference $\Delta x = \dfrac{\pi l}{2} - l = l\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - 1\right)$.
Equating to $\lambda$: $l\left(\dfrac{3.1416}{2} - 1\right) = 0.29 \Rightarrow l(0.5708) = 0.29 \Rightarrow l \approx 0.51$ m.
Thus, P $\rightarrow$ 3.

For configuration (Q):
The sound travels through a straight tube of length $l$ and a rectangular path of length $0.5l + l + 0.5l = 2l$.
Path difference $\Delta x = 2l - l = l$.
Equating to $\lambda$: $l = 0.29$ m.
Thus, Q $\rightarrow$ 4.

For configuration (R):
The sound travels through a straight horizontal tube of length $l$ and a path consisting of a vertical tube of length $l$ followed by a semi-circular tube.
The diameter of the semi-circle is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed by the tubes, which is $\sqrt{l^2 + l^2} = l\sqrt{2}$.
The length of the semi-circular part is $\dfrac{\pi (l\sqrt{2})}{2} = \dfrac{\pi l}{\sqrt{2}}$.
Path difference $\Delta x = \left(l + \dfrac{\pi l}{\sqrt{2}}\right) - l = \dfrac{\pi l}{\sqrt{2}}$.
Equating to $\lambda$: $\dfrac{\pi l}{\sqrt{2}} = 0.29 \Rightarrow l = \dfrac{0.29 \sqrt{2}}{\pi} \approx \dfrac{0.29 \times 1.414}{3.1416} \approx 0.13$ m.
Thus, R $\rightarrow$ 5.

For configuration (S):
The sound travels through a straight tube of length $l$ and a triangular path.
The angles of the triangle are $45^\circ$, $105^\circ$, and $180^\circ - (45^\circ + 105^\circ) = 30^\circ$.
Using the sine rule, the lengths of the other two sides are $l \dfrac{\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 105^\circ}$ and $l \dfrac{\sin 45^\circ}{\sin 105^\circ}$.
Path difference $\Delta x = l \dfrac{\sin 30^\circ + \sin 45^\circ}{\sin 105^\circ} - l$.
Given $\sin 105^\circ = \cos 15^\circ = 0.97$.
$\Delta x = l \left( \dfrac{0.5 + 1/\sqrt{2}}{0.97} - 1 \right) \approx l \left( \dfrac{0.5 + 0.707}{0.97} - 1 \right) = l \left( \dfrac{1.207}{0.97} - 1 \right) \approx 0.244 l$.
Equating to $\lambda$: $0.244 l = 0.29 \Rightarrow l \approx 1.19$ m.
Thus, S $\rightarrow$ 2.

Matching the results, we get P $\rightarrow$ 3, Q $\rightarrow$ 4, R $\rightarrow$ 5, S $\rightarrow$ 2.

Answer: P$\rightarrow$3, Q$\rightarrow$4, R$\rightarrow$5, S$\rightarrow$2

</details>

### Q5. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Two plane harmonic sound waves are expressed by the equations.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& y_1(x, t)=A \cos (0.5 \pi x-100 \pi t) \\
& y_2(x, t)=A \cos (0.46 \pi x-92 \pi t)
\end{aligned}
$$
(All parameters are in MKS)<strong>Question:</strong>
At $x=0$ how many times the amplitude of $y_1+y_2$ is zero in one second at $x=0$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 192
- **(B)** 48
- **(C)** 100
- **(D)** 96

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 96**

**Detailed Solution:**

At $x=0, y=y_1+y_2=2 A \cos 96 \pi t \cos 4 \pi t$
Frequency of $\cos (96 \pi t)$ function is $48 \mathrm{~Hz}$ and that of $\cos (4 \pi t)$ function is $2 \mathrm{~Hz}$. In one second cos function becomes zero at $2 f$ times, where $f$ is the frequency. Therefore, first function will become zero at 96 times and the second at 4 times. But second overlaps with first. Hence, net $y$ will become zero 96 times in 1 second.

</details>
