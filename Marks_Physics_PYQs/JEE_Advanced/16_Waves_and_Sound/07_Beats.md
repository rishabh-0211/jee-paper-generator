### Q2. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Two plane harmonic sound waves are expressed by the equations.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& y_1(x, t)=A \cos (0.5 \pi x-100 \pi t) \\
& y_2(x, t)=A \cos (0.46 \pi x-92 \pi t)
\end{aligned}
$$
(All parameters are in MKS)<strong>Question:</strong>
How many times does an observer hear maximum intensity in one second?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4
- **(B)** 10
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 4**

**Detailed Solution:**

In one second number of maximas is called the beat frequency. Hence,
$$
f_b=f_1-f_2=\frac{100 \pi}{2 \pi}-\frac{92 \pi}{2 \pi}=4 \mathrm{~Hz}
$$

</details>
