& =\left\{\frac{1}{2} I(2 \omega)^2+\frac{1}{2}(2 I)(\omega)^2\right\}-\frac{1}{2}(3 I)\left(\frac{4}{3} \omega\right)^2 \\
& =\frac{1}{3} I \omega^2
\end{aligned}
$$
$\therefore$ Option (b) is correct.

</details>

\frac{1}{2}(2 I)(\omega)^2 & =\frac{1}{2} k x_2^2

$$
\frac{x_1}{x_2}=\sqrt{2}
$$
$\therefore$ Option (c) is correct.
