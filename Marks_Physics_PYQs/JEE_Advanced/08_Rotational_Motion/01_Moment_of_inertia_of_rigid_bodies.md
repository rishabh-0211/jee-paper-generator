### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

List-I shows four planar structures made of uniform solid rods each of mass $m$ and length $l$. In the List-II the possible moment of inertia of these structures about an axis $OCO'$, which lies in the plane of the structures, are given.
Choose the option that describes the correct match between the entries in List-I to those in List-II.
<table style="border: 1px solid black; border-collapse: collapse; width: auto;"><tr><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">List-I</th><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">List-II</th></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(P) <img style="width: 200px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q32_d1.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(1) $\dfrac{5}{4} m l^2$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(Q) <img style="width: 220px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q32_d2.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(2) $\dfrac{1}{6} m l^2$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(R) <img style="width: 150px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q32_d3.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(3) $\dfrac{1}{12} m l^2$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(S) <img style="width: 180px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q32_d4.png" /></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(4) $\dfrac{2}{3} m l^2$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;"></td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 10px;">(5) $\dfrac{1}{3} m l^2$</td></tr></table>

**Options:**

- **(A)** P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 1, R$\rightarrow$ 4, S$\rightarrow$ 2
- **(B)** P$\rightarrow$ 1, Q$\rightarrow$ 3, R$\rightarrow$ 4, S$\rightarrow$ 2
- **(C)** P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 3, R$\rightarrow$ 2, S$\rightarrow$ 1
- **(D)** P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 4, R$\rightarrow$ 2, S$\rightarrow$ 1

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 1, R$\rightarrow$ 4, S$\rightarrow$ 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

The moment of inertia of a uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $l$ about an axis passing through one end and making an angle $\theta$ with the rod is given by $I = \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2\theta$.

For structure (P):
The axis $OO'$ makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with both rods $CA$ and $CB$.
$I_P = I_{CA} + I_{CB} = \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2(45^\circ) + \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2(45^\circ) = 2 \times \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{m l^2}{3}$.
This matches with (5).

For structure (Q):
The axis $OO'$ makes an angle of $60^\circ$ with rods $CA$ and $CB$. Rod $AB$ is parallel to the axis at a distance $d = l \sin(60^\circ) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} l$.
$I_Q = I_{CA} + I_{CB} + I_{AB} = 2 \times \left( \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2(60^\circ) \right) + m d^2$
$I_Q = 2 \times \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \times \dfrac{3}{4} + m \left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} l \right)^2 = \dfrac{m l^2}{2} + \dfrac{3 m l^2}{4} = \dfrac{5 m l^2}{4}$.
This matches with (1).

For structure (R):
The axis $OO'$ is the diagonal of the square, making an angle of $45^\circ$ with all four rods.
$I_R = 4 \times \left( \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2(45^\circ) \right) = 4 \times \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{2 m l^2}{3}$.
This matches with (4).

For structure (S):
The axis $OO'$ makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with both rods $CA$ and $CB$.
$I_S = 2 \times \left( \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \sin^2(30^\circ) \right) = 2 \times \dfrac{m l^2}{3} \times \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{m l^2}{6}$.
This matches with (2).

Therefore, the correct matching is P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 1, R$\rightarrow$ 4, S$\rightarrow$ 2.

Answer: P$\rightarrow$ 5, Q$\rightarrow$ 1, R$\rightarrow$ 4, S$\rightarrow$ 2

</details>

### Q3. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A lamina is made by removing a small disc of diameter $2 R$ from a bigger disc of uniform mass density and radius $2 R$, as shown in the figure. The moment of inertia of this lamina about axes passing though $O$ and $P$ is $I_{O}$ and $I_{P}$ respectively. Both these axes are perpendicular to the plane of the lamina. The ratio $I_{P} / I_{O}$ to the nearest integer is

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/FCGosuZSs9bvX9TAOyMX0Db910xOh_xEi9Gnk8hO45Q.original.fullsize.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **3**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\sigma$ be the surface mass density.

Moment of inertia of the lamina about axes passing through 'O'

$\begin{array}{l}

I_{O}=\frac{1}{2} \sigma\left[\pi(2 R)^{2}\right] \times(2 R)^{2}- \\

=\frac{13}{2} \pi \sigma R^{4} \quad\left[\frac{1}{2}\left(\sigma \pi R^{2}\right)^{2}+\sigma\left(\pi R^{2}\right) \times R^{2}\right]

\end{array}$

Moment of inertia of the lamina about axes passing through 'P'

$\begin{array}{l}

I_{P}=8 \pi \sigma \mathrm{R}^{4}+\sigma \pi(2 \mathrm{R})^{2} \times(2 \mathrm{R})^{2} \\

{\left[\frac{1}{2} \sigma\left(\pi R^{2}\right) R^{2}+\sigma\left(\pi R^{2}\right)\left(\sqrt{(2 R)^{2}+R^{2}}\right)^{2}\right]}

\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{l}

=24 \pi \sigma \mathrm{R}^{4}-5.5 \sigma \pi \mathrm{R}^{4}=18.5 \pi \sigma \mathrm{R}^{4} \\

\therefore \quad \frac{I_{P}}{I_{O}}=\frac{18.5 \pi \sigma R^{4}}{\frac{13}{2} \pi \sigma R^{4}}=\frac{37}{13} \approx 3

\end{array}$

</details>
