### Q1. JEE Advanced 2024 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Four identical thin, square metal sheets, $S_1, S_2, S_3$ and $S_4$, each of side $a$ are kept parallel to each other with equal distance $d(\ll a)$ between them, as shown in the figure. Let $C_0=\varepsilon_0 a^2 / d$, where $\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/wrVTJvFmbkH-cpCDFPq8t7MWcliNkKMHSUGq3QODPwI.original.fullsize.png">
Match the quantities mentioned in List-I with their values in List-II and choose the correct option.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/G0RPj7Bb_okyw7CrMC7Lv-b191S6Emkx_BZg-tF6d-w.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{P} \rightarrow 3 ; \mathrm{Q} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{R} \rightarrow 4 ; \mathrm{S} \rightarrow 5$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{P} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{Q} \rightarrow 3 ; \mathrm{R} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{S} \rightarrow 1$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{P} \rightarrow 3 ; \mathrm{Q} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{R} \rightarrow 4 ; \mathrm{S} \rightarrow 1$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{P} \rightarrow 3$; $\mathrm{Q} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{R} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{S} \rightarrow 5$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{P} \rightarrow 3 ; \mathrm{Q} \rightarrow 2 ; \mathrm{R} \rightarrow 4 ; \mathrm{S} \rightarrow 1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For P
<img src="images/hf8c375_chfcr47r348.png">
All are in series
\(\begin{gathered}
C_{e q}=\frac{C_0}{3} \\
P \rightarrow(3)
\end{gathered}\)
For Q
<img src="images/hf8c375_chfcr47r3481.png">
\(\begin{gathered}
C_{e q}=\frac{C_0}{2} \\
Q \rightarrow(2)
\end{gathered}\)
For R
<img src="images/hf8c375_chfcr47r3482.png">
\(\begin{gathered}
C_{e q}=\frac{2 C}{3} \\
R \rightarrow(4)
\end{gathered}\)
For S
<img src="images/hf8c375_chfcr47r3483.png">
\(\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow C_{e q}=3 C_0 \\
& S \rightarrow(1)
\end{aligned}\)
