- **(A)** $60 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the left of the system
- **(B)** $60 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the right of the system
- **(C)** $12 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the left of the system
- **(D)** $12 \mathrm{~cm}$ to the right of the system
