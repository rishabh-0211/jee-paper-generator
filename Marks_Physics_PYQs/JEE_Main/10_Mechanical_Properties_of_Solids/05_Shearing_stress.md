### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

A cube has side length $5$ cm and modulus of rigidity $10^5$ N/m$^2$. The displacement produced by a force of $10$ N in the upper face of cube is _____ mm.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given:
Side length of the cube, $L = 5$ cm $= 0.05$ m
Modulus of rigidity, $\eta = 10^5$ N/m$^2$
Force applied on the upper face, $F = 10$ N

Area of the upper face, $A = L^2 = (0.05)^2 = 2.5 \times 10^{-3}$ m$^2$

Shear stress is given by:
$\text{Stress} = \dfrac{F}{A} = \dfrac{10}{2.5 \times 10^{-3}} = 4000$ N/m$^2$

Shear strain is given by:
$\text{Strain} = \dfrac{\Delta x}{L}$

Modulus of rigidity is defined as the ratio of shear stress to shear strain:
$\eta = \dfrac{\text{Stress}}{\text{Strain}} = \dfrac{F/A}{\Delta x/L}$

Rearranging for displacement $\Delta x$:
$\Delta x = \dfrac{F \cdot L}{A \cdot \eta}$

Substituting the values:
$\Delta x = \dfrac{10 \times 0.05}{2.5 \times 10^{-3} \times 10^5}$

$\Delta x = \dfrac{0.5}{250} = 0.002$ m

Converting to millimeters:
$\Delta x = 0.002 \times 1000 = 2$ mm

Answer: $2$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2025 (4 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

Two slabs with square cross section of different materials $(1,2)$ with equal sides $(l)$ and thickness $\mathrm{d}_1$ and $\mathrm{d}_2$ such that $\mathrm{d}_2=2 \mathrm{~d}_1$ and $l \gt \mathrm{d}_2$. Considering lower edges of these slabs are fixed to the floor, we apply equal shearing force on the narrow faces. The angle of deformation is $\theta_2=2 \theta_1$. If the shear moduli of material 1 is $4 \times 10^9 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$, then shear moduli of material 2 is $\mathrm{x} \times 10^9 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$, where value of $x$ is ________.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **1**

**Detailed Solution:**

Deformation angle
$\begin{aligned}
& 2 \theta_1=\theta_2 \\
& \Rightarrow 2 \frac{\sigma_1}{\eta_1}=\frac{\sigma_2}{\eta_2}
\end{aligned}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/ug-nihWUV__s_XEm2wZ0lcdHnq7CdJTwLfTwUhH2He0.original.fullsize.png"/>
$\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow 2\left(\frac{\mathrm{~F}}{\ell \mathrm{~d}_1 \eta_1}\right)=\frac{\mathrm{F}}{\ell \mathrm{d}_2 \eta_2} \\ & \Rightarrow \eta_2=\frac{\eta_1}{4}=1 \times 10^9 \Rightarrow x=1\end{aligned}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2014 (12 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Steel ruptures when a shear of $3.5 \times 10^8 \mathrm{~N} \mathrm{~m}^{-2}$ is applied. The force needed to punch a $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ diameter hole in a steel sheet $0.3 \mathrm{~cm}$ thick is nearly:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.4 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(B)** $2.7 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(C)** $3.3 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $1.1 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $3.3 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/R-rkVCvPGH5HFN1BuWa7GoNVoOLYjw5gOgkACFrQEAQ.original.fullsize.png">

Shearing strain is created along the side surface of the punched disk. Note that the forces exerted on the disk are exerted along
the circumference of the disk, and the total force exerted on its center only.
Let us assume that the shearing stress along the side surface of the disk is uniform, then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{F}>& \int_{\text {surface }} \mathrm{dF}_{\max }=\int_{\text {surface }} \sigma_{\max } \mathrm{dA}=\sigma_{\max } \int_{\text {surface }} \mathrm{dA} \\
&=\int \sigma_{\max } \cdot \mathrm{A}=\sigma_{\max } \cdot 2 \pi\left(\frac{\mathrm{D}}{2}\right) \mathrm{h} \\
&=3.5 \times 10^8 \times\left(\frac{1}{2} \times 10^{-2}\right) \times 0.3 \times 10^{-2} \times 2 \pi \\
&=3.297 \times 10^4 \simeq 3.3 \times 10^4 \mathrm{~N}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
