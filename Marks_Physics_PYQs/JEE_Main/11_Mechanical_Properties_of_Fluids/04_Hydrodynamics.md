### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A liquid of density $600$ kg/m$^3$ flowing steadily in a tube of varying cross-section. The cross-section at a point $A$ is $1.0$ cm$^2$ and that at $B$ is $20$ mm$^2$. Both the points $A$ and $B$ are in same horizontal plane, the speed of the liquid at $A$ is $10$ cm/s. The difference in pressures at $A$ and $B$ points is ________ Pa.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18$
- **(B)** $144$
- **(C)** $36$
- **(D)** $72$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $72$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the equation of continuity:

$A_A v_A = A_B v_B$

$(1.0 \times 10^{-4}) \times 0.1 = (20 \times 10^{-6}) \times v_B$

$v_B = \dfrac{10^{-5}}{2 \times 10^{-5}} = 0.5$ m/s

Using Bernoulli's equation for a horizontal tube:

$P_A + \dfrac{1}{2} \rho v_A^2 = P_B + \dfrac{1}{2} \rho v_B^2$

$P_A - P_B = \dfrac{1}{2} \rho (v_B^2 - v_A^2)$

Substituting the given values:

$P_A - P_B = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 600 \times (0.5^2 - 0.1^2)$

$P_A - P_B = 300 \times (0.25 - 0.01)$

$P_A - P_B = 300 \times 0.24 = 72$ Pa

Answer: $72$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A water spray gun is attached to a hose of cross sectional area $30$ cm$^2$. The gun comprises of $10$ perforations each of cross sectional area $15$ mm$^2$. If the water flows in the hose with the speed of $50$ cm/s, calculate the speed at which the water flows out from each perforation. (Neglect any edge effects)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $100$ m/s
- **(B)** $10$ m/s
- **(C)** $1000$ m/s
- **(D)** $15 \times 10^2$ m/s

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $10$ m/s**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the equation of continuity:

$A_1 v_1 = n A_2 v_2$

Here,
$A_1 = 30 \text{ cm}^2 = 30 \times 10^{-4} \text{ m}^2$
$v_1 = 50 \text{ cm/s} = 0.5 \text{ m/s}$
$n = 10$
$A_2 = 15 \text{ mm}^2 = 15 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}^2$

Substituting the given values:

$(30 \times 10^{-4}) \times 0.5 = 10 \times (15 \times 10^{-6}) \times v_2$

$15 \times 10^{-4} = 150 \times 10^{-6} \times v_2$

$15 \times 10^{-4} = 1.5 \times 10^{-4} \times v_2$

$v_2 = \dfrac{15 \times 10^{-4}}{1.5 \times 10^{-4}} = 10 \text{ m/s}$

Answer: $10$ m/s

</details>

$\rho gh = \frac{1}{2}\rho(4v_1^2 - v_1^2) = \frac{3\rho v_1^2}{2}$
$v_1 = \sqrt{\frac{2gh}{3}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 1000 \times 5}{3}} = \frac{100}{\sqrt{3}}$ cm/s
Rate of flow $Q = A_1v_1 = 6 \times \frac{100}{\sqrt{3}} = 200\sqrt{3}$ cm³/s

Net force due to stopped liquid, $\mathrm{F}_{2}=\frac{1}{4}$ pav $^{2}$ Total force,

$\mathrm{F}=\mathrm{F}_{1}+\mathrm{F}_{2}=\frac{1}{2} \rho \mathrm{av}^{2}+\frac{1}{4} \mathrm{pav}^{2}=\frac{3}{4} \mathrm{pav}^{2}$

### Q28. JEE Main 2014 (12 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A cylindrical vessel of cross-section A contains water to a height $\mathrm{h}$. There is a hole in the bottom of radius ' $a$ '. The time in which it will be emptied is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{2 \mathrm{~A}}{\pi \mathrm{a}^2} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{g}}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\sqrt{2} \mathrm{~A}}{\pi \mathrm{a}^2} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{g}}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~A}}{\pi \mathrm{a}^2} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{g}}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{A}}{\sqrt{2} \pi \mathrm{a}^2} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{g}}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{\sqrt{2} \mathrm{~A}}{\pi \mathrm{a}^2} \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{g}}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\text { Let the rate of falling water level be }-\frac{d h}{d t}
$$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/slu-xQFM6L0BkrR0Kq2COyH9gyrJ7xks2DxIwxeWjp8.original.fullsize.png">

Initially at $t=0 ; h=h$
$$
t=t ; h=0
$$
Then, $A\left(-\frac{d h}{d t}\right)=\pi a^2 \cdot v$
$$
d t=-\frac{A}{\pi a^2 \sqrt{2 g h}} d h
$$
$[\because$ velocity of efflux of liquid $v=\sqrt{2 g h}]$ Integrating both sides
$$
\int_0^t d t=-\frac{A}{\sqrt{2 g} \pi a^2} \int_h^0 h^{-1 / 2} d h
$$
$$
[t]_0^t=-\frac{A}{\sqrt{2 g} \pi a^2} \cdot\left[\frac{h^{1 / 2}}{1 / 2}\right]_h^0
$$

$$
t=\frac{\sqrt{2} A}{\pi a^2} \sqrt{\frac{h}{g}}
$$
