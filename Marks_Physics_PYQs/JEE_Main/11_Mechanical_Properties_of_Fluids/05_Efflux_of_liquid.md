### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A cylindrical vessel of $40$ cm radius is completely filled with water and its capacity is $528$ dm$^3$ (dm : decimeter). The vessel is placed on a solid block of exactly same height as vessel. If a small hole is made at $70$ cm below the top of water level, then horizontal range of water falling on the ground in the beginning is __________ cm.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $120\sqrt{2}$
- **(B)** $140\sqrt{2}$
- **(C)** $140\sqrt{3}$
- **(D)** $120\sqrt{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $140\sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Volume of the cylindrical vessel, $V = 528$ dm$^3 = 528000$ cm$^3$

Radius of the vessel, $r = 40$ cm

Let the height of the vessel be $H$.

$V = \pi r^2 H$

$528000 = \dfrac{22}{7} \times (40)^2 \times H$

$H = \dfrac{528000 \times 7}{22 \times 1600} = 105$ cm

The vessel is placed on a block of height equal to the vessel, so the height of the block is $105$ cm.

Depth of the hole from the top water level, $h = 70$ cm

Height of the hole from the ground, $y = \text{Height of block} + (H - h)$

$y = 105 + (105 - 70) = 140$ cm

Velocity of efflux, $v = \sqrt{2gh}$

Time of flight of water, $t = \sqrt{\dfrac{2y}{g}}$

Horizontal range, $R = v \times t = \sqrt{2gh} \times \sqrt{\dfrac{2y}{g}} = 2\sqrt{hy}$

$R = 2\sqrt{70 \times 140} = 2\sqrt{70 \times 70 \times 2} = 140\sqrt{2}$ cm

Answer: $140\sqrt{2}$

</details>

### Q7. JEE Main 2014 (11 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A tank with a small hole at the bottom has been filled with water and kerosene (specific gravity $0.8$ ). The height of water is $3 \mathrm{~m}$ and that of kerosene $2 \mathrm{~m}$. When the hole is opened the velocity of fluid coming out from it is nearly: (take $\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~ms}^{-2}$ and density of water $\left.=10^3 \mathrm{~kg} \mathrm{~m}^{-3}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $10.7 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $9.6 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $8.5 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $7.6 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $9.6 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&h_1 \rho_1 g+h_2 \rho_2 g=\frac{1}{2} \rho_1 \mathrm{v}^2 \\
&\Rightarrow 1000 \times 10 \times 3+800 \times 10 \times 2 \\
&=\frac{1}{2} \times 1000 \times \mathrm{v}^2 \\
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{v}^2=2 \times \frac{46000}{1000} \\
&\Rightarrow \mathrm{v}=\sqrt{92}=9.6 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Q8. JEE Main 2012 (26 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

In a cylindrical water tank, there are two small holes $A$ and $B$ on the wall at a depth of $h_1$, from the surface of water and at a height of $h_2$ from the bottom of water tank. Surface of water is at height of $h_2$ from the bottom of water tank. Surface of water is at heigh $H$ from the bottom of water tank. Water coming out from both holes strikes the ground at the same point $S$. Find the ratio of $h_1$ and $h_2$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/WVgqWaqg_ppmNPWND79rH1C-IH8XtLqijJMCMu5K8A8.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** Depends on $H$
- **(B)** $1: 1$
- **(C)** $2: 2$
- **(D)** $1: 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Depends on $H$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Range is same for both holes
$$
\therefore 2 \sqrt{\left(\mathrm{H}-h_1\right) h_1}=2 \sqrt{\left(\mathrm{H}-h_2\right) h_2}
$$
Squaring both sides,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 4\left(H-h_1\right) \mathrm{h}_1=4\left(H-h_2\right) h_2 \\
& H h_1-h_1^2=H h_2-h_2^2
\end{aligned}
$$
On solving we get,
$$
H=h_1+h_2
$$
Hence, the ratio of $\frac{h_1}{h_2}$ depends on $H$.
