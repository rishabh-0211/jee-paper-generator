### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An insulated wire is wound so that it forms a flat coil with $N = 200$ turns. The radius of the innermost turn is $r_1 = 3$ cm, and of the outermost turn $r_2 = 6$ cm. If $20$ mA current flows in it then the magnetic moment will be $\alpha \times 10^{-2}$ A.m$^2$. The value of $\alpha$ is _____.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4.4$
- **(B)** $2.64$
- **(C)** $3.25$
- **(D)** $1.2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2.64$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the number of turns per unit radial width be $n = \dfrac{N}{r_2 - r_1}$.

Consider an elemental ring of radius $r$ and width $dr$. The number of turns in this element is $dN = n \, dr$.

The magnetic moment of this elemental ring is $dM = I \, dN \, A = I \left( \dfrac{N}{r_2 - r_1} \, dr \right) (\pi r^2)$.

Integrating from $r_1$ to $r_2$ to find the total magnetic moment $M$:

$M = \int_{r_1}^{r_2} \dfrac{I \pi N}{r_2 - r_1} r^2 \, dr = \dfrac{I \pi N}{3(r_2 - r_1)} (r_2^3 - r_1^3)$

Substituting the given values: $I = 20 \times 10^{-3}$ A, $N = 200$, $r_1 = 0.03$ m, and $r_2 = 0.06$ m:

$M = \dfrac{20 \times 10^{-3} \times \pi \times 200}{3(0.06 - 0.03)} \left( (0.06)^3 - (0.03)^3 \right)$

$M = \dfrac{4\pi}{0.09} (216 \times 10^{-6} - 27 \times 10^{-6})$

$M = \dfrac{4\pi}{0.09} \times 189 \times 10^{-6} = 4\pi \times 2100 \times 10^{-6}$

$M = 84\pi \times 10^{-4} \approx 84 \times 3.14 \times 10^{-4} = 263.76 \times 10^{-4} \approx 2.64 \times 10^{-2}$ A.m$^2$

Comparing with $\alpha \times 10^{-2}$ A.m$^2$, we get $\alpha = 2.64$.

Answer: $2.64$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2024 (06 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

A coil having 100 turns, area of $5 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~m}^2$, carrying current of $1 \mathrm{~mA}$ is placed in uniform magnetic field of $0.20 \mathrm{~T}$ such a way that plane of coil is perpendicular to the magnetic field. The work done in turning the coil through $90^{\circ}$ is _______ $\mu \mathrm{J}$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **100**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & W=\Delta U=U_f-U_i \\ & W=(-\vec{\mu} . \vec{B})_f-(-\vec{\mu} . \vec{B})_i \\ & =0+(\vec{\mu} \cdot \vec{B})_i \\ & =\left(100 \times 5 \times 10^{-3} \times 1 \times 10^{-3}\right) \times 0.2 \mathrm{~J} \\ & =1 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~J}=100 \mu \mathrm{J}\end{aligned}$

</details>
