### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A current carrying circular loop of radius $2$ cm with unit normal $\hat{n} = \dfrac{\hat{k}+\hat{i}}{\sqrt{2}}$ is placed in a magnetic field, $\vec{B} = B_0(3\hat{i}+2\hat{k})$. If $B_0 = 4\times 10^{-3}$ T and current $I=100\sqrt{2}$ A, the torque experienced by the loop is ________ Wb·A. ($\pi=3.14$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $16\times 10^{-5}\,\hat{k}$
- **(B)** $5024\times 10^{-7}\,\hat{k}$
- **(C)** $5024\times 10^{-7}\,\hat{i}$
- **(D)** $5024\times 10^{-7}\,\hat{j}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $5024\times 10^{-7}\,\hat{j}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The magnetic dipole moment of the current carrying loop is given by $\vec{M} = I A \hat{n}$.

Given radius $r = 2$ cm $= 2 \times 10^{-2}$ m, the area of the loop is:
$A = \pi r^2 = \pi (2 \times 10^{-2})^2 = 4\pi \times 10^{-4}$ m$^2$

Substituting the given values $I = 100\sqrt{2}$ A and $\hat{n} = \dfrac{\hat{i}+\hat{k}}{\sqrt{2}}$:
$\vec{M} = (100\sqrt{2}) \times (4\pi \times 10^{-4}) \times \left(\dfrac{\hat{i}+\hat{k}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)$
$\vec{M} = 4\pi \times 10^{-2} (\hat{i}+\hat{k})$ A$\cdot$m$^2$

The torque experienced by the loop in the magnetic field is $\vec{\tau} = \vec{M} \times \vec{B}$.
Given $\vec{B} = 4 \times 10^{-3} (3\hat{i}+2\hat{k})$ T, we have:
$\vec{\tau} = [4\pi \times 10^{-2} (\hat{i}+\hat{k})] \times [4 \times 10^{-3} (3\hat{i}+2\hat{k})]$
$\vec{\tau} = 16\pi \times 10^{-5} [(\hat{i}+\hat{k}) \times (3\hat{i}+2\hat{k})]$

Evaluating the cross product:
$(\hat{i}+\hat{k}) \times (3\hat{i}+2\hat{k}) = (\hat{i} \times 3\hat{i}) + (\hat{i} \times 2\hat{k}) + (\hat{k} \times 3\hat{i}) + (\hat{k} \times 2\hat{k})$
$= 0 + 2(-\hat{j}) + 3(\hat{j}) + 0 = \hat{j}$

Therefore, the torque is:
$\vec{\tau} = 16\pi \times 10^{-5} \hat{j}$

Substituting $\pi = 3.14$:
$\vec{\tau} = 16 \times 3.14 \times 10^{-5} \hat{j} = 50.24 \times 10^{-5} \hat{j} = 5024 \times 10^{-7} \hat{j}$ Wb$\cdot$A

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A circular coil of radius $2$ cm and $125$ turns carries a current of $1$ A. The coil is placed in a uniform magnetic field of magnitude $0.4$ T. The axis of the coil makes an angle of $30°$ with the direction of the magnetic field. The torque acting on the coil is $\alpha \times 10^{-4}$ N.m. The value of $\alpha$ is ______.
($\pi=3.14$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **314**

**Detailed Solution:**

The magnetic moment of the coil is given by $M = NIA$.

The torque acting on the coil is $\tau = MB \sin\theta = NIAB \sin\theta$.

Substituting the given values:

$\tau = 125 \times 1 \times (\pi \times (0.02)^2) \times 0.4 \times \sin 30^\circ$

$\tau = 125 \times 1 \times 4\pi \times 10^{-4} \times 0.4 \times 0.5$

$\tau = 100\pi \times 10^{-4}$

$\tau = 100 \times 3.14 \times 10^{-4} = 314 \times 10^{-4}$ N.m

Comparing with $\alpha \times 10^{-4}$ N.m, we get $\alpha = 314$.

Answer: $314$

</details>

 ($\mu_{\mathrm{o}}=4 \pi \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~T}. \mathrm{m} / \mathrm{A}$)

- **(B)** $6 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~N}$ towards $P$
- **(C)** $6 \times 10^{-7} \mathrm{~N}$ towards $R$
- **(D)** $6 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~N}$ towards $R$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $6 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~N}$ towards $R$**

Total force per unit length towards R: $4 \times 10^{-5}$ N/m. 
For 0.15 m length: $F = 4 \times 10^{-5} \times 0.15 = 6 \times 10^{-6}$ N towards R.
