### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A short bar magnet placed with its axis at $30^{\circ}$ with an external field of 800 Gauss, experiences a torque of $0.016 \mathrm{~N}. \mathrm{m}$. The work done in moving it from most stable to most unstable position is $\alpha \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{~J}$. The value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **64**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a magnetic dipole in an external field, torque is $\tau = mB\sin\theta$
At 30°: $0.016 = m \times 0.08 \times \sin 30°$
$0.016 = m \times 0.08 \times 0.5$
$m = 0.4$ A·m$^2$
Work done from most stable (aligned, 0°) to most unstable (anti-aligned, 180°) position equals the change in potential energy:
$W = U_0° - U_{180°} = -mB\cos 0° - (-mB\cos 180°) = -mB - mB = 2mB$
$W = 2 \times 0.4 \times 0.08 = 0.064$ J $= 64 \times 10^{-3}$ J
Therefore, $\alpha = 64$

</details>

A magnetic dipole experiences a torque of $80 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~N} \mathrm{~m}$ when placed in uniform magnetic field in such a way that dipole moment makes angle of $60^{\circ}$ with magnetic field. The potential energy of the dipole is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $80 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(B)** $-40 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~J}$
- **(C)** $-60 \mathrm{~J}$
- **(D)** $-80 \mathrm{~J}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-80 \mathrm{~J}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tau=\mathrm{M} \times \mathrm{B}=\mathrm{MB} \sin 60=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \mathrm{MB}=80 \sqrt{3}$
$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{MB}=160 \\ & \mathrm{U}=-\mathrm{M} \cdot \mathrm{B}=-\mathrm{MB} \cos 60 \\ & \mathrm{U}=-160 \times 1 / 2=-80 \mathrm{~J}\end{aligned}$
