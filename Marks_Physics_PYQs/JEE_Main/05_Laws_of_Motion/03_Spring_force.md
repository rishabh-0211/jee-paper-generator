### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A spring of force constant $15 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}$ is cut into two pieces. If the ratio of their length is $1: 3$, then the force constant of smaller piece is $\_\_\_\_$ $\mathrm{N} / \mathrm{m}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 60
- **(B)** 15
- **(C)** 20
- **(D)** 45

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 60**

**Detailed Solution:**

Spring force constant is inversely proportional to length: $k \propto \frac{1}{L}$ 
So $kL = $ constant for a given spring material and structure.
Original spring: $k = 15$ N/m, length $= L$.
Smaller piece has length $\frac{L}{4}$ (since length ratio is 1:3, smaller is $\frac{1}{4}$ of total).
For smaller piece: $k_1 \times \frac{L}{4} = 15 \times L$
$k_1 = 15 \times 4 = 60$ N/m

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (2 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

The length of a light string is 1.4 m when the tension on it is 5 N. If the tension increases to 7 N , the length of the string is 1.56 m. The original length of the string is _____ m.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **1**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{T}=\mathrm{K}\left(\ell-\ell_0\right) \\ & \Rightarrow 5=\mathrm{K}\left(1.4-\ell_0\right) \\ & \Rightarrow 7=\mathrm{K}\left(1.56-\ell_0\right) \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{5}{1.4-\ell_0}=\frac{7}{1.56-\ell_0} \\ & \therefore \ell_0=1 \mathrm{~m}\end{aligned}$

</details>

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Initially the blocks are at rest and the spring is unstretched. Then a constant force '$\mathrm{F}$' starts acting on the block of mass '$\mathrm{M}$' to pull it. Find the force on the block of mass '$\mathrm{m}$'

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{mF}}{\mathrm{M}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{(M+m) F}}{\mathrm{m}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{m F}}{\mathrm{(m+M)}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{MF}}{(\mathrm{m}+\mathrm{M})}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\mathrm{m F}}{\mathrm{(m+M)}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Kx}=\mathrm{ma}=\frac{\mathrm{mF}}{\mathrm{m}+\mathrm{M}}$
