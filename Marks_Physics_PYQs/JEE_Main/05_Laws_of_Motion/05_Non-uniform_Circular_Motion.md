### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A car of mass ' $m$ ' moves on a banked road having radius ' $r$ ' and banking angle $\theta$. To avoid slipping from banked road, the maximum permissible speed of the car is $v_0$. The coefficient of friction $\mu$ between the wheels of the car and the banked road is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mu=\frac{\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{o}}^2+\mathrm{rg} \tan \theta}{\mathrm{rg}+\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{o}}^2 \tan \theta}$
- **(B)** $\mu=\frac{v_0^2-r g \tan \theta}{r g-v_0^2 \tan \theta}$
- **(C)** $\mu=\frac{v_0^2-r g \tan \theta}{r g+v_0^2 \tan \theta}$
- **(D)** $\mu=\frac{v_o^2+r g \tan \theta}{\mathrm{rg}-\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{o}}^2 \tan \theta}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mu=\frac{v_0^2-r g \tan \theta}{r g+v_0^2 \tan \theta}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/H0D_W8zBZE6QUzYkJ7JmTebfsrJwleY7i9iANRAXpDw.original.fullsize.png">

So, $N=m g \cos \theta+\frac{m v_0^2}{r} \sin \theta$
$f_r=\mu m g \cos \theta+\frac{\mu m v_0^2}{r} \sin \theta$
$\begin{aligned}
& \text { And } \frac{m v_0^2}{r} \cos \theta=m g \sin \theta+f_r \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{m v_0^2}{r} \cos \theta-m g \sin \theta=\mu\left(m g \cos \theta+\frac{m v_0^2}{r} \sin \theta\right) \\
& \Rightarrow\left(v_0^2-g r \tan \theta\right)=\mu\left(v_0^2 \tan \theta+g r\right) \\
& \Rightarrow \mu=\frac{v_0^2-g r \tan \theta}{g r+v_0^2 \tan \theta}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2024 (06 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A car of $800 \mathrm{~kg}$ is taking turn on a banked road of radius $300 \mathrm{~m}$ and angle of banking $30^{\circ}$. If coefficient of static friction is 0.2 then the maximum speed with which car can negotiate the turn safely: $\left(\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2, \sqrt{3}=1.73\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $264 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $51.4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $70.4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $102.8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $51.4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{m}=800 \mathrm{~kg} \\ & \mathrm{r}=300 \mathrm{~m} \\ & \theta=30^{\circ} \\ & \mu_{\mathrm{s}}=0.2 \\ & \mathrm{~V}_{\max }=\sqrt{\operatorname{Rg}\left[\frac{\tan \theta+\mu}{1-\mu \tan \theta}\right]} \\ & =\sqrt{300 \times \mathrm{g} \times\left[\frac{\tan 30^{\circ}+0.2}{1-0.2 \times \tan 30^{\circ}}\right]} \\ & =\sqrt{300 \times 10 \times\left[\frac{0.57+0.2}{1-0.2 \times 0.57}\right]} \\ & \mathrm{V}_{\max }=51.4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}\end{aligned}$

</details>
