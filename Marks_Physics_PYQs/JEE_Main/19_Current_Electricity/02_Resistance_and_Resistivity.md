### Q5. JEE Main 2024 (08 Apr Shift 1)

$\begin{aligned} & 0{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \rightarrow 100^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\ & \frac{10.2-10}{10}=\alpha(100-0)\end{aligned}$   ...(1)

$0^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \rightarrow \mathrm{t}^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
$\frac{10.95-10}{10}=\alpha(\mathrm{t}-0)$   ...(2)
$\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{t}}{100}=\frac{0.95}{0.2}=475^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\ & \mathrm{t}=475+273=748 \mathrm{~K}\end{aligned}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2024 (06 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

A wire of resistance $R$ and radius $r$ is stretched till its radius became $r / 2$. If new resistance of the stretched wire is $x R$, then value of $x$ is _______

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **16**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know $\mathrm{R}=\frac{\rho l}{\mathrm{~A}}, \mathrm{R} \propto \frac{l}{\mathrm{r}^2}$
As we starch the wire, its length will increase but its radius will decrease keeping the volume constant
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{i}}=\mathrm{V}_{\mathrm{f}} \\
& \pi \mathrm{r}^2 l=\pi \frac{\mathrm{r}^2}{4} l_{\mathrm{f}} \\
& l_{\mathrm{f}}=4 l \\
& \frac{\mathrm{R}_{\text {new }}}{\mathrm{R}_{\text {old }}}=\left(\frac{4 l}{\frac{\mathrm{r}^2}{4}}\right) \frac{\mathrm{r}^2}{l}=16 \\
& \mathrm{R}_{\text {new }}=16 \mathrm{R} \\
\therefore & \mathrm{x}=16
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q46. JEE Main 2006

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A material ' $B$ ' has twice the specific resistance of ' $A$ '. A circular wire made of ' $B$ ' has twice the diameter of a wire made of ' $A$ '. Then for the two wires to have the same resistance, the ratio $\ell_A / \ell_B$ of their respective lengths must be

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{R}_1=\frac{\rho_{\mathrm{A}} \ell_{\mathrm{A}}}{\pi \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{A}}^2} \quad \mathrm{R}_2=\frac{\rho_{\mathrm{B}} \ell_{\mathrm{B}}}{\pi \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{B}}^2}$
$\frac{\ell_{\mathrm{A}}}{\ell_{\mathrm{B}}}=\frac{\rho_{\mathrm{B}} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{A}}^2}{\rho_{\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{B}}^2}=\frac{2 \rho_{\mathrm{A}} \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{A}}^2}{\rho_{\mathrm{A}} \cdot 4 \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{A}}^2} \Rightarrow \frac{\ell_{\mathrm{B}}}{\ell_{\mathrm{A}}}=2$

</details>

### Q48. JEE Main 2003

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The length of a given cylindrical wire is increased by $100 \%$. Due to the consequent decrease in diameter the change in the resistance of the wire will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $200 \%$
- **(B)** $100 \%$
- **(C)** $50 \%$
- **(D)** $300 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $300 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{L}_1=2 \mathrm{l}$ or $\left(\pi \mathrm{r}^2 \mathrm{l}\right)=\left(\pi \mathrm{r}_2^2\right)(2 \mathrm{l})$
$\Rightarrow \mathrm{r}_2=\frac{\mathrm{r}}{\sqrt{2}}$$;$               $\mathrm{R}=\rho \frac{1}{\pi^2}$
$\mathrm{R}_{\text {new }}=(\rho) \frac{21}{(\pi)(\mathrm{r} / \sqrt{2})^2}=\frac{(\rho) 4 \mathrm{l}}{(\pi) \mathrm{r}^2}=4 \times \mathrm{R}$
$\therefore \Delta \mathrm{R}=4 \mathrm{R}-\mathrm{R}=3 \mathrm{R}$
$\frac{\Delta \mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{R}} \%=\frac{3 \mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{R}} \times 100=300 \%$

</details>
