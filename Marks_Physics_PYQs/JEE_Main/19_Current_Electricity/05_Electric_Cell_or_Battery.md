### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

Two cells of emfs $1$ V and $2$ V and internal resistance $2\,\Omega$ and $1\,\Omega$, respectively connected in parallel, gave a current of $1$ A through an external resistance. If the polarity of one cell is reversed, then value of current through the external resistance will be $\dfrac{\alpha}{5}$ A. The value of $\alpha$ is __________.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **3**

**Detailed Solution:**

For two cells connected in parallel with the same polarity, the equivalent emf $E_{eq}$ and equivalent internal resistance $r_{eq}$ are given by:

$E_{eq} = \dfrac{E_1 r_2 + E_2 r_1}{r_1 + r_2}$

$r_{eq} = \dfrac{r_1 r_2}{r_1 + r_2}$

Substituting the given values ($E_1 = 1$ V, $r_1 = 2\,\Omega$, $E_2 = 2$ V, $r_2 = 1\,\Omega$):

$E_{eq} = \dfrac{1 \times 1 + 2 \times 2}{2 + 1} = \dfrac{5}{3}$ V

$r_{eq} = \dfrac{2 \times 1}{2 + 1} = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\Omega$

The current $I$ through the external resistance $R$ is $1$ A:

$I = \dfrac{E_{eq}}{R + r_{eq}}$

$1 = \dfrac{\dfrac{5}{3}}{R + \dfrac{2}{3}}$

$R + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3} \Rightarrow R = 1\,\Omega$

When the polarity of one cell is reversed, the new equivalent emf $E'_{eq}$ becomes:

$E'_{eq} = \dfrac{|E_1 r_2 - E_2 r_1|}{r_1 + r_2} = \dfrac{|1 \times 1 - 2 \times 2|}{3} = \dfrac{3}{3} = 1$ V

The equivalent internal resistance remains the same, $r_{eq} = \dfrac{2}{3}\,\Omega$.

The new current $I'$ through the external resistance $R$ is:

$I' = \dfrac{E'_{eq}}{R + r_{eq}} = \dfrac{1}{1 + \dfrac{2}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{5}{3}} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ A

Given that the new current is $\dfrac{\alpha}{5}$ A, we have:

$\dfrac{\alpha}{5} = \dfrac{3}{5} \Rightarrow \alpha = 3$

Answer: $3$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

When an external resistance of $5\ \Omega$ is connected across terminals of a cell, a current of $0.25\text{ A}$ flows through it. When the $5\ \Omega$ resistor is replaced by a $2\ \Omega$ resistor, a current of $0.5\text{ A}$ flows through it. The internal resistance of the cell is _______ $\Omega$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **1**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the EMF of the cell be $E$ and its internal resistance be $r$.

The current $I$ in the circuit is given by $I = \dfrac{E}{R + r}$.

For the first case, $R_1 = 5\ \Omega$ and $I_1 = 0.25\text{ A}$:

$0.25 = \dfrac{E}{5 + r} \Rightarrow E = 0.25(5 + r)$

For the second case, $R_2 = 2\ \Omega$ and $I_2 = 0.5\text{ A}$:

$0.5 = \dfrac{E}{2 + r} \Rightarrow E = 0.5(2 + r)$

Equating the expressions for $E$:

$0.25(5 + r) = 0.5(2 + r)$

$5 + r = 2(2 + r)$

$5 + r = 4 + 2r$

$r = 1\ \Omega$

Answer: $1$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For the two cells having same EMF $E$ and internal resistance $r$, the current passing through the external resistor $6 \Omega$ is same when both the cells are connected either in parallel or in series. The value of internal resistance $r$ is $\_\_\_\_$ $\Omega$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6
- **(B)** 3
- **(C)** 4
- **(D)** 9

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 6**

**Detailed Solution:**

For series connection of two cells: $I_s = \frac{2E}{2r + 6}$.
For parallel connection: $I_p = \frac{E}{r/2 + 6} = \frac{2E}{r + 12}$.
 Given $I_s = I_p$: $\frac{2E}{2r + 6} = \frac{2E}{r + 12}$.
 This gives $2r + 6 = r + 12$, so $r = 6\Omega$.

</details>

### Q6. JEE Main 2025 (22 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

- **(A)** Both Statement-I and Statement-II are false
- **(B)** Statement-I is false but Statement-II is true
- **(C)** Both Statement-I and Statement-II are true

If $E_1=E_2$ and $r_1=r_2, E_{\text {eq }}=E_1=E_2$
$\therefore$ Statement 1 is false.
$r_{\text {eq }}$ is less then both $r_1$ and $r_2$
