**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{t}}=\mathrm{v} \\
&\mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{B}}=2150 \mathrm{~Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$
Reflected wave frequency received by $\mathrm{A}$, $\mathrm{f}_{\mathrm{A}}{ }^{\prime}=$ ?

\end{aligned}
$$
