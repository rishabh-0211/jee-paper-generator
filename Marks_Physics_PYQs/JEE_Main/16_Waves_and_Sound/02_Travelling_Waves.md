### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

A transverse wave on a string is described by $y = 3\sin(36t + 0.018x + \pi/4)$, where $x, y$ are in cm and $t$ in seconds. The least distance between the two successive crests in the wave is _____ cm. (Nearest integer)
($\pi = 3.14$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **349**

**Detailed Solution:**

The standard equation of a transverse wave is given by $y = A\sin(\omega t + kx + \phi)$

Comparing this with the given equation $y = 3\sin(36t + 0.018x + \pi/4)$, we get the wave number $k = 0.018 \text{ cm}^{-1}$.

The least distance between two successive crests is the wavelength $\lambda$ of the wave.

We know that $k = \dfrac{2\pi}{\lambda}$

$\lambda = \dfrac{2\pi}{k} = \dfrac{2 \times 3.14}{0.018}$

$\lambda = \dfrac{6.28}{0.018} = \dfrac{6280}{18} = 348.88 \text{ cm}$

Rounding to the nearest integer, we get $349 \text{ cm}$.

Answer: $349$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The equation of a plane progressive wave is given by $y = 5\cos\pi\left(200t - \dfrac{x}{150}\right)$ where $x$ and $y$ are in cm and $t$ is in second. The velocity of the wave is _______ m/s.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $120$
- **(B)** $150$
- **(C)** $200$
- **(D)** $300$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $300$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the wave is $y = 5\cos\pi\left(200t - \dfrac{x}{150}\right)$

Rewriting the equation, we get $y = 5\cos\left(200\pi t - \dfrac{\pi x}{150}\right)$

Comparing this with the standard wave equation $y = A\cos(\omega t - kx)$, we have:

$\omega = 200\pi$ rad/s

$k = \dfrac{\pi}{150}$ rad/cm

The velocity of the wave is given by $v = \dfrac{\omega}{k}$

$v = \dfrac{200\pi}{\dfrac{\pi}{150}} = 30000$ cm/s

Converting to m/s, we get $v = \dfrac{30000}{100} = 300$ m/s

Answer: $300$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A point source is kept at the center of a spherically enclosed detector. If the volume of the detector increased by 8 times, the intensity will

**Options:**

- **(A)** increase by 64 times
- **(B)** increase by 8 times
- **(C)** decrease by 4 times
- **(D)** decrease by 8 times

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) decrease by 4 times**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a point source, intensity is given by $I = \frac{P}{4\pi r^2}$ where $P$ is power and $r$ is distance.
If the volume of the spherical detector increases by 8 times, then $\frac{4}{3}\pi r_{new}^3 = 8 \cdot \frac{4}{3}\pi r_{old}^3$, giving $r_{new} = 2r_{old}$.
The new intensity is $I_{new} = \frac{P}{4\pi (2r_{old})^2} = \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{P}{4\pi r_{old}^2} = \frac{I_{old}}{4}$.
Therefore intensity decreases by 4 times.

</details>

### Q6. JEE Main 2025 (2 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

**Options:**

- **(A)** $y=2 \cos (0.83 x-3.35 t) \mathrm{cm}$
- **(B)** $y=2 \sin (0.83 x-3.5 t) \mathrm{cm}$
- **(C)** $y=2 \cos (3.35 x-0.83 t) \mathrm{cm}$
- **(D)** $y=2 \cos (0.13 x-0.5 t) \mathrm{cm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $y=2 \cos (0.83 x-3.35 t) \mathrm{cm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{v}=\frac{\text { distance }}{\text { time }} \\
& \mathrm{v}=\frac{12}{0.3}=4 \mathrm{~cm} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \mathrm{k}=\frac{2 \pi}{\lambda}=\frac{2 \pi}{7.5}=\frac{4 \pi}{15}=0.83 \\
& \mathrm{v}=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{k}} \Rightarrow \omega=\mathrm{vk}=4 \times \frac{4 \pi}{15}=3.35
\end{aligned}$
So $y=A \cos (k x-\omega t)$
