### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A car moving with a speed of $54$ km/h takes a turn of radius $20$ m. A simple pendulum is suspended from the ceiling of the car. Determine the angle made by the string of the pendulum with the vertical during the turning. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\tan^{-1}(0.5)$
- **(B)** $\tan^{-1}(0.75)$
- **(C)** $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$
- **(D)** $\tan^{-1}(0.25)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Speed of the car, $v = 54 \times \dfrac{5}{18} = 15$ m/s.

In the frame of the car, the pendulum bob experiences a pseudo force (centrifugal force) horizontally outwards and the gravitational force downwards.

Let $T$ be the tension in the string and $\theta$ be the angle it makes with the vertical. For equilibrium in the car's frame, the horizontal and vertical forces balance:

$T \sin \theta = \dfrac{mv^2}{r}$

$T \cos \theta = mg$

Dividing the two equations, we get:

$\tan \theta = \dfrac{v^2}{rg}$

Substituting the given values $v = 15$ m/s, $r = 20$ m, and $g = 10$ m/s$^2$:

$\tan \theta = \dfrac{(15)^2}{20 \times 10} = \dfrac{225}{200} = 1.125$

$\theta = \tan^{-1}(1.125)$

Answer: $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$

</details>

### Q26. JEE Main 2019 (11 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A particle is moving along a circular path with a constant speed of $10 \mathrm{~ms}^{-1}$. What is the magnitude of the change in velocity of the particle, when it moves through an angle of $60^{\circ}$ around the centre of the circle?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $10 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** zero
- **(C)** $10 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/chjAiJkT9Uqb1dmt7Hnxw21eZvUekpkO7tU--70NiJo.original.fullsize.png">
Change in velocity,
$$
\begin{array}{l}
|\Delta \overline{\mathrm{v}}|=\sqrt{\mathrm{v}_{1}^{2}+\mathrm{v}_{2}^{2}+2 \mathrm{v}_{1} \mathrm{v}_{2} \cos (\pi-\theta)} \\
=2 \mathrm{v} \sin \frac{\theta}{2} \quad\left(\because\left|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{1}\right|=\left|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}_{2}\right|\right)=\mathrm{v} \\
=(2 \times 10) \times \sin \left(30^{\circ}\right)=2 \times 10 \times \frac{1}{2} \\
=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}
\end{array}
$$
