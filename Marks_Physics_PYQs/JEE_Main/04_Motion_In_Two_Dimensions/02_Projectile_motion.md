### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two identical bodies, projected with the same speed at two different angles cover the same horizontal range $R$. If the time of flight of these bodies are $5$ s and $10$ s, respectively, then the value of $R$ is ________ m. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $250$
- **(B)** $25$
- **(C)** $500$
- **(D)** $125$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $250$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For two projectiles to have the same horizontal range $R$ with the same initial speed $u$, their angles of projection must be complementary, i.e., $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$.

The times of flight for these two angles are given by:
$t_1 = \dfrac{2u \sin \theta}{g}$
$t_2 = \dfrac{2u \cos \theta}{g}$

Multiplying $t_1$ and $t_2$, we get:
$t_1 t_2 = \left(\dfrac{2u \sin \theta}{g}\right) \left(\dfrac{2u \cos \theta}{g}\right) = \dfrac{2}{g} \left(\dfrac{u^2 (2 \sin \theta \cos \theta)}{g}\right)$

Since the horizontal range is $R = \dfrac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$, we can write:
$t_1 t_2 = \dfrac{2R}{g}$

Given $t_1 = 5$ s, $t_2 = 10$ s, and $g = 10$ m/s$^2$, substituting these values:
$5 \times 10 = \dfrac{2R}{10}$

$50 = \dfrac{2R}{10}$

$2R = 500 \Rightarrow R = 250$ m

Answer: $250$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $x$ and $y$ coordinates of a projectile as a function of time $(t)$ are given as $24t$ and $43.6t-4.9t^2$, respectively, then the angle (in degrees) made by the projectile with horizontal when $t=2$ s is ______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $60$
- **(B)** $45$
- **(C)** $30$
- **(D)** $75$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $45$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The velocity components of the projectile are given by the derivatives of the position coordinates with respect to time.

$v_x = \dfrac{dx}{dt} = \dfrac{d}{dt}(24t) = 24$

$v_y = \dfrac{dy}{dt} = \dfrac{d}{dt}(43.6t - 4.9t^2) = 43.6 - 9.8t$

At $t = 2$ s, the velocity components are:

$v_x = 24$

$v_y = 43.6 - 9.8(2) = 43.6 - 19.6 = 24$

The angle $\theta$ made by the velocity vector with the horizontal is given by:

$\tan \theta = \dfrac{v_y}{v_x} = \dfrac{24}{24} = 1$

$\theta = 45^{\circ}$

Answer: $45$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two projectiles are projected with the same initial velocities at the $15^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal. The ratio of their ranges is $1:x$. The value of $x$ is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{2}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(C)** $2\sqrt{3}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sqrt{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The horizontal range of a projectile is given by the formula $R = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$.

For the first projectile, the angle of projection is $\theta_1 = 15^\circ$.

$R_1 = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(2 \times 15^\circ)}{g} = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(30^\circ)}{g} = \dfrac{u^2}{2g}$

For the second projectile, the angle of projection is $\theta_2 = 30^\circ$.

$R_2 = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(2 \times 30^\circ)}{g} = \dfrac{u^2 \sin(60^\circ)}{g} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}u^2}{2g}$

The ratio of their ranges is:

$\dfrac{R_1}{R_2} = \dfrac{\dfrac{u^2}{2g}}{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}u^2}{2g}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

Given that the ratio is $1:x$, we get $x = \sqrt{3}$.

Answer: $\sqrt{3}$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A boy throws a ball into air at $45^{\circ}$ from the horizontal to land it on a roof of a building of height $H$. If the ball attains maximum height in 2 s and lands on the building in 3 s after launch, then value of $H$ is $\_\_\_\_$ m. 
 $\left(\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 15
- **(B)** 20
- **(C)** 25
- **(D)** 10

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 15**

**Detailed Solution:**

For projectile motion at 45°, vertical component of initial velocity:
$v_{0y} = v_0 \sin 45° = \frac{v_0}{\sqrt{2}}$
Maximum height occurs at $t = 2$ s when $v_y = 0$:
$0 = v_{0y} - gt$
$\Rightarrow v_{0y} = 10 \times 2 = 20$ m/s
Thus $\frac{v_0}{\sqrt{2}} = 20$, so $v_0 = 20\sqrt{2}$ m/s
Height at landing time $t = 3$ s:
$H = v_{0y}t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2 = 20 \times 3 - \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 9$
$= 60 - 45 = 15$ m

</details>
