### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A river of width 200 m is flowing from west to east with a speed of $18 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$. A boat, moving with speed of $36 \mathrm{~km} / \mathrm{h}$ in still water, is made to travel one-round trip (bank to bank of the river). Minimum time taken by the boat for this journey and also the displacement along the river bank are $\_\_\_\_$ and $\_\_\_\_$ respectively.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 20 s and 100 m
- **(B)** 40 s and 0 m
- **(C)** 40 s and 200 m
- **(D)** 40 s and 100 m

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 40 s and 200 m**

**Detailed Solution:**

Width of the river $d = 200$ m.
Speed of river $v_r = 18$ km/h $= 18 \times \frac{5}{18} = 5$ m/s.
Speed of boat in still water $v_b = 36$ km/h $= 36 \times \frac{5}{18} = 10$ m/s.
For minimum time to cross the river, the boat must head perpendicular to the river flow (at $90^\circ$ to the bank).
Time taken to cross once $t = \frac{d}{v_b} = \frac{200}{10} = 20$ s.
For a round trip (bank to bank and back), the total minimum time is $T = 2t = 2 \times 20 = 40$ s.
During the first crossing, the drift (displacement along the bank) is $x_1 = v_r \times t = 5 \times 20 = 100$ m.
During the return trip, to minimize time, the boat again heads perpendicular to the flow relative to the water. The river continues to flow in the same direction, so the drift is again in the same direction as the first trip.
Return drift $x_2 = v_r \times t = 5 \times 20 = 100$ m.
Total displacement along the river bank $= x_1 + x_2 = 100 + 100 = 200$ m.
Thus, minimum time is $40$ s and displacement is $200$ m.

</details>

### Q16. JEE Main 2018 (15 Apr Shift 2 Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A man in a car at location Q on a straight highway is moving with speed $\mathrm{v}$. He decides to reach a point $P$ in a field at a distance $d$ from highway (point $M$ ) as shown in the figure.Speed of the car in the field is half to that on the highway. What should be the distance RM, so that the time taken to reach $P$ is minimum?
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/KAO7CMAojPHVxeDrpH3zszTdEUpI7RzGaJ04e_umNxo.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\sqrt{2}}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{d}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\sqrt{3}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the car turn of the highway at a distance ' $x$ ' from the point $\mathrm{M}$. So, $\mathrm{RM}=x$ And if speed of car in field is $v$, then time taken by the car to cover the distance $Q R=$ $Q M-x$ on the highway,
$$
t_1=\frac{Q M-x}{2 v}
$$
Time taken to travel the distance ' $R P^{\prime}$ in the field
$$
t_2=\frac{\sqrt{d^2+x^2}}{v}
$$
Total time elapsed to move the car from $Q$ to $P$
$$
t=t_1+t_2=\frac{Q M-x}{2 v}+\frac{\sqrt{d^2+x^2}}{v}
$$

$$
\text { or } x=\frac{d}{\sqrt{2^2-1}}=\frac{d}{\sqrt{3}}
$$
