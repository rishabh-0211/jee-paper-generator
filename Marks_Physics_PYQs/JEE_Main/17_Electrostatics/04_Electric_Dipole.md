### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A rigid dipole undergoes a simple harmonic motion about its centre in the presence of an electric field $\vec{E}_1=E_0\hat{x}$. If another electric field $\vec{E}_2=2E_0(\hat{y}+\hat{z})$ is introduced to the system, what will be the percentage change in the frequency of the oscillation (approximate)?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $73\%$
- **(B)** $63\%$
- **(C)** $83\%$
- **(D)** $53\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $73\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The frequency of oscillation of a rigid dipole in an electric field is given by $f = \dfrac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\dfrac{pE}{I}}$, where $p$ is the dipole moment, $E$ is the magnitude of the electric field, and $I$ is the moment of inertia.

This implies $f \propto \sqrt{E}$.

The initial electric field is $\vec{E}_1 = E_0 \hat{x}$, so its magnitude is $E_1 = E_0$.

The initial frequency is $f_1 \propto \sqrt{E_0}$.

When the second electric field $\vec{E}_2 = 2E_0(\hat{y} + \hat{z})$ is introduced, the net electric field becomes $\vec{E}_{net} = \vec{E}_1 + \vec{E}_2 = E_0 \hat{x} + 2E_0 \hat{y} + 2E_0 \hat{z}$.

The magnitude of the net electric field is $E_{net} = \sqrt{E_0^2 + (2E_0)^2 + (2E_0)^2} = \sqrt{E_0^2 + 4E_0^2 + 4E_0^2} = 3E_0$.

The new frequency is $f_2 \propto \sqrt{3E_0}$.

The percentage change in the frequency is given by $\dfrac{f_2 - f_1}{f_1} \times 100\%$.

Substituting the values, we get $\left( \dfrac{\sqrt{3E_0}}{\sqrt{E_0}} - 1 \right) \times 100\% = (\sqrt{3} - 1) \times 100\%$.

Using $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$, the percentage change is $(1.732 - 1) \times 100\% = 73.2\%$.

The approximate percentage change is $73\%$.

Answer: $73\%$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two short electric dipoles $A$ and $B$ having dipole moment $p_1$ and $p_2$ respectively are placed with their axis mutually perpendicular as shown in the figure. The resultant electric field at a point $x$ is making an angle of $60°$ with the line joining points $O$ and $x$. The ratio of the dipole moments $p_2/p_1$ is _______.
<img style="width:300px;"src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/02APRS1__q36_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(B)** $2\sqrt{3}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2\sqrt{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the distance of point $x$ from the origin $O$ be $r$.

Point $x$ lies on the axial line of dipole A. The electric field at $x$ due to dipole A is directed along the horizontal axis and its magnitude is given by:
$E_A = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \dfrac{2p_1}{r^3}$

Point $x$ lies on the equatorial line of dipole B. The electric field at $x$ due to dipole B is directed perpendicular to the horizontal axis and its magnitude is given by:
$E_B = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \dfrac{p_2}{r^3}$

The resultant electric field makes an angle of $60^{\circ}$ with the line $Ox$ (the horizontal axis). Therefore, the tangent of this angle is the ratio of the perpendicular component of the electric field to the parallel component:
$\tan 60^{\circ} = \dfrac{E_B}{E_A}$

Substituting the expressions for $E_A$ and $E_B$:
$\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \dfrac{p_2}{r^3}}{\dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \dfrac{2p_1}{r^3}}$

$\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{p_2}{2p_1}$

$\dfrac{p_2}{p_1} = 2\sqrt{3}$

Answer: $2\sqrt{3}$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

Three charges $+2 q,+3 q$ and $-4 q$ are situated at $(0,-3 a),(2 a, 0)$ and $(-2 a, 0)$ respectively in the $x y$ plane. The resultant dipole moment about origin is $\_\_\_\_$.

- **(C)** $2 q a(3 \hat{i}-7 \hat{j})$

<details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2025 (7 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A dipole with two electric charges of $2 \mu \mathrm{C}$ magnitude each, with separation distance $0.5 \mu \mathrm{~m}$, is placed between the plates of a capacitor such that its axis is parallel to an electric field established between the plates when a potential difference of 5 V is applied. **Options:**

- **(A)** $5 \times 10^{-9} \mathrm{Nm}$
- **(B)** $5 \times 10^{-3} \mathrm{Nm}$
- **(C)** $2.5 \times 10^{-12} \mathrm{Nm}$

$\begin{aligned}

### Q9. JEE Main 2025 (28 Jan Shift 2)

An electric dipole of dipole moment $6 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{Cm}$ is placed in uniform electric field of magnitude $10^6 \mathrm{~V} / \mathrm{m}$. Initially, the dipole moment is parallel to electric field. The work that needs to be done on the dipole to make its dipole moment opposite to the field, will be ____ J.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **12**

**Detailed Solution:**

Work done in rotating a dipole $=\Delta U$
$\text { or } \begin{aligned}
W & =\left(-P E \cos \theta_f\right)-\left(-P E \cos \theta_i\right) \\

\end{aligned}$
