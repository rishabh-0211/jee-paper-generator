### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A monochromatic source of light operating at $15$ kW emits $2.5\times 10^{22}$ photons/s. The region of an electromagnetic spectrum to which the emitted electromagnetic radiation belongs to ________. (Take $h=6.6\times 10^{-34}$ J·s and $c=3\times 10^8$ m/s).

**Options:**

- **(A)** Microwave
- **(B)** Infrared
- **(C)** Visible
- **(D)** Ultraviolet

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Ultraviolet**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power of the source, $P = 15 \text{ kW} = 15 \times 10^3 \text{ J/s}$

Number of photons emitted per second, $n = 2.5 \times 10^{22} \text{ s}^{-1}$

Energy of one photon, $E = \dfrac{P}{n} = \dfrac{15 \times 10^3}{2.5 \times 10^{22}} = 6 \times 10^{-19} \text{ J}$

Using $E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$, we get $\lambda = \dfrac{hc}{E}$

$\lambda = \dfrac{6.6 \times 10^{-34} \times 3 \times 10^8}{6 \times 10^{-19}} = 3.3 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m} = 330 \text{ nm}$

The wavelength $330 \text{ nm}$ lies in the ultraviolet region of the electromagnetic spectrum.

Answer: Ultraviolet

</details>

### Q17. JEE Main 2013 (09 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Photons of an electromagnetic radiation has an energy $11 \mathrm{keV}$ each. To which region of electromagnetic spectrum does it belong ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** X-ray region
- **(B)** Ultra violet region
- **(C)** Infrared region
- **(D)** Visible region

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) X-ray region**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{E}=\frac{\mathrm{hc}}{\lambda} \Rightarrow \lambda=\frac{\mathrm{hc}}{\mathrm{E}}$
$\Rightarrow \lambda=\frac{6.6 \times 10^{-34} \times 3 \times 10^8}{11 \times 1000 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19}}$
$=12.4 Å$

### Q18. JEE Main 2012 (26 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The frequency of $X$-rays; $\gamma$-rays and ultraviolet rays are respectively $a, b$ and $c$ then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a < b ; b>c$
- **(B)** $a>b ; b>c$
- **(C)** $a < b < c$
- **(D)** $a=b=c$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $a < b ; b>c$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Frequency range of $\gamma$-ray, $b=10^{18}-10^{23} \mathrm{~Hz}$
Frequency range of X-ray, $a=10^{16}-10^{20} \mathrm{~Hz}$
Frequency range of ultraviolet ray, $c=10^{15}-10^{17} \mathrm{~Hz}$
$\therefore a < b ; b>c$

</details>

### Q19. JEE Main 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If a source of power $4 \mathrm{~kW}$ produces $10^{20}$ photons/second, the radiation belong to a part of the spectrum called

**Options:**

- **(A)** X-rays
- **(B)** ultraviolet rays
- **(C)** microwaves
- **(D)** $\gamma$-rays

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) X-rays**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 4 \times 10^3=10^{20} \mathrm{xhf} \\
& f=\frac{4 \times 10^3}{10^{20} \times 6.023 \times 10^{-34}} \\
& f=6.03 \times 10^{16} \mathrm{~Hz}
\end{aligned}
$$
The obtained frequency lies in the band of X-rays.

</details>
