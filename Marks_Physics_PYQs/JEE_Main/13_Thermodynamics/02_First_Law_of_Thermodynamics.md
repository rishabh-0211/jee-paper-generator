### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Initial pressure and volume of a monoatomic ideal gas are $P$ and $V$. The change in internal energy of this gas in adiabatic expansion to volume $V_{final}=27V$ is ________ J.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-2PV(3\sqrt{3}-1)$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{4}{3}PV$
- **(C)** $-\dfrac{4}{3}PV$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{3}{4}PV$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $-\dfrac{4}{3}PV$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a monoatomic ideal gas, the ratio of specific heats is $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$.

In an adiabatic process, $PV^{\gamma} = \text{constant}$.

$P_1 V_1^{\gamma} = P_2 V_2^{\gamma}$

$P V^{5/3} = P_2 (27V)^{5/3}$

$P_2 = P \left(\dfrac{1}{27}\right)^{5/3} = \dfrac{P}{243}$

The change in internal energy is given by:

$\Delta U = \dfrac{P_2 V_2 - P_1 V_1}{\gamma - 1}$

Substituting the values:

$\Delta U = \dfrac{\left(\dfrac{P}{243}\right)(27V) - PV}{\dfrac{5}{3} - 1}$

$\Delta U = \dfrac{\dfrac{PV}{9} - PV}{\dfrac{2}{3}}$

$\Delta U = \dfrac{-\dfrac{8}{9}PV}{\dfrac{2}{3}} = -\dfrac{4}{3}PV$

Answer: $-\dfrac{4}{3}PV$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

One mole of diatomic gas having rotational modes only is kept in a cylinder with a piston system. The cross-section area of the cylinder is $4$ cm$^2$. The gas is heated slowly to raise the temperature by $1.2\,^\circ$C during which the piston moves by $25$ mm. The amount of heat supplied to the gas is ________ J. (Atmospheric pressure $=100$ kPa, $R=8.3$ J/mol·K) (Neglect mass of the piston)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $24.8$
- **(B)** $25$
- **(C)** $15.04$
- **(D)** $29.98$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $25$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the first law of thermodynamics:
$Q = \Delta U + W$

Since the gas is diatomic with rotational modes only, the degrees of freedom are $f = 5$, so:
$C_V = \dfrac{5}{2}R$

For $n = 1\text{ mole}$ and $\Delta T = 1.2\text{ K}$:
$\Delta U = nC_V\Delta T = 1 \times \dfrac{5}{2} \times 8.3 \times 1.2 = 24.9\text{ J}$

Work done by the gas:
$W = P\,\Delta V$

Cross-sectional area of the piston:
$A = 4\text{ cm}^2 = 4 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m}^2$

Piston displacement:
$x = 25\text{ mm} = 2.5 \times 10^{-2}\text{ m}$

Change in volume:
$\Delta V = A \cdot x = (4 \times 10^{-4})(2.5 \times 10^{-2}) = 1 \times 10^{-5}\text{ m}^3$

With $P = 100\text{ kPa} = 10^5\text{ Pa}$:
$W = (10^5)(1 \times 10^{-5}) = 1\text{ J}$

Therefore, the heat supplied is:
$Q = \Delta U + W = 24.9 + 1 = 25.9\text{ J}$

Hence, the heat supplied to the gas is $25.9\text{ J}$.

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

$5$ moles of unknown gas is heated at constant volume from $10 \, °C$ to $20 \, °C$. The molar specific heat of this gas at constant pressure $c_p = 8$ cal/mol.°C and $R = 8.36$ J/mol.°C. The change in the internal energy of the gas is _______ calorie.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **300**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $n = 5$ moles, $\Delta T = 20^{\circ}\text{C} - 10^{\circ}\text{C} = 10^{\circ}\text{C}$.

The molar specific heat at constant pressure is $c_p = 8 \text{ cal/mol}\cdot^{\circ}\text{C}$.

The universal gas constant is $R = 8.36 \text{ J/mol}\cdot^{\circ}\text{C}$.

Converting $R$ into calories, we use $1 \text{ cal} = 4.18 \text{ J}$:

$R = \dfrac{8.36}{4.18} \text{ cal/mol}\cdot^{\circ}\text{C} = 2 \text{ cal/mol}\cdot^{\circ}\text{C}$

Using Mayer's relation, the molar specific heat at constant volume is:

$c_v = c_p - R = 8 - 2 = 6 \text{ cal/mol}\cdot^{\circ}\text{C}$

The change in internal energy of the gas is given by:

$\Delta U = n c_v \Delta T$

$\Delta U = 5 \times 6 \times 10 = 300 \text{ cal}$

Answer: $300$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Heat is supplied to a diatomic gas at constant pressure. Then the ratio of $\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W$ is _______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 : 3 : 5$
- **(B)** $5 : 3 : 2$
- **(C)** $2 : 5 : 7$
- **(D)** $7 : 5 : 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $7 : 5 : 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a diatomic gas, the number of degrees of freedom is $f = 5$.

The molar heat capacity at constant volume is $C_v = \dfrac{f}{2}R = \dfrac{5}{2}R$.

The molar heat capacity at constant pressure is $C_p = C_v + R = \dfrac{7}{2}R$.

At constant pressure, the heat supplied is $\Delta Q = nC_p\Delta T = n\left(\dfrac{7}{2}R\right)\Delta T$.

The change in internal energy is $\Delta U = nC_v\Delta T = n\left(\dfrac{5}{2}R\right)\Delta T$.

The work done by the gas is $\Delta W = nR\Delta T = n\left(\dfrac{2}{2}R\right)\Delta T$.

The ratio $\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W$ is $\dfrac{7}{2} : \dfrac{5}{2} : \dfrac{2}{2} = 7 : 5 : 2$.

Answer: $7 : 5 : 2$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

10 mole of an ideal gas is undergoing the process shown in the figure. The heat involved in the process from $P_{1}$ to $P_{2}$ is $\alpha$ Joule ($P_{1}=21.7 \mathrm{~Pa}$ and $\left.P_{2}=30 \mathrm{~Pa}, \mathrm{C}_{v}=21 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K}. \mathrm{mol}, R=8.3 \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{mol}. \mathrm{K}\right)$. The value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$. 
 <img style="width:300px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/24S2_q_30_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** 21
- **(B)** 15
- **(C)** 28
- **(D)** 24

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 21**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given $P-V$ diagram, the process from $P_1$ to $P_2$ is an isochoric process because the volume remains constant at $V = 1\text{ m}^3$.
For an isochoric process, the work done $W = 0$.
According to the first law of thermodynamics, the heat involved is $Q = \Delta U + W = \Delta U$.
The change in internal energy is given by $\Delta U = n C_v \Delta T$.
Using the ideal gas equation $PV = nRT$, we have $n\Delta T = \frac{V\Delta P}{R}$.
Substituting this into the expression for $Q$:
$Q = C_v \left( \frac{V(P_2 - P_1)}{R} \right)$
Given values: $n = 10$ moles, $V = 1\text{ m}^3$, $P_1 = 21.7\text{ Pa}$, $P_2 = 30\text{ Pa}$, $C_v = 21\text{ J/mol}\cdot\text{K}$, and $R = 8.3\text{ J/mol}\cdot\text{K}$.
$Q = 21 \times \frac{1 \times (30 - 21.7)}{8.3}$
$Q = 21 \times \frac{8.3}{8.3} = 21\text{ J}$.
Thus, the value of $\alpha$ is 21.

</details>

### Q7. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The internal energy of a monoatomic gas is 3 nRT. One mole of helium is kept in a cylinder having internal cross section area of $17 \mathrm{~cm}^{2}$ and fitted with a light movable frictionless piston. The gas is heated slowly by suppling 126 J heat. If the temperature rises by $4^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, then the piston will move $\_\_\_\_$ cm. 
 (atmospheric pressure $=10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1.45
- **(B)** 15.5
- **(C)** 1.55
- **(D)** 14.5

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 15.5**

**Detailed Solution:**

The question states the internal energy of the gas is $U = 3nRT$.
The change in internal energy is $\Delta U = 3nR\Delta T$.
Given: $n = 1$ mole, $\Delta T = 4^{\circ}C = 4$ K, and $R \approx 8.314$ J/mol·K.
$\Delta U = 3 \times 1 \times 8.314 \times 4 = 99.768$ J.
According to the first law of thermodynamics, $\Delta Q = \Delta U + W$.
Given heat supplied $\Delta Q = 126$ J.
Work done by the gas $W = \Delta Q - \Delta U = 126 - 99.768 = 26.232$ J.
Since the piston is light and movable, the process occurs at constant atmospheric pressure $P = 10^5$ Pa.
Work done $W = P \Delta V = P(A \times d)$, where $A$ is the cross-section area and $d$ is the displacement of the piston.
$A = 17 \text{ cm}^2 = 17 \times 10^{-4} \text{ m}^2$.
$26.232 = 10^5 \times (17 \times 10^{-4}) \times d$.
$26.232 = 170 \times d$.
$d = \frac{26.232}{170} \approx 0.1543$ m.
Converting to cm: $d \approx 15.43$ cm.
Rounding to the nearest option, we get $15.5$ cm.

</details>
