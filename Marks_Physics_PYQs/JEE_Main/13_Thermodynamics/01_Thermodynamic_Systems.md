### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Consider the following statements:
A. Zeroth law of thermodynamics gives concept of temperature
B. First law of thermodynamics gives concept of internal energy
C. In isothermal expansion of ideal gas, $\Delta Q \neq \Delta W$
D. Product of intensive and extensive variables is extensive
E. The ratio of any extensive variable to mass will be an extensive variable
Choose the correct combination of statements from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** C, D and E Only
- **(B)** A, B and C Only
- **(C)** A, B and D Only
- **(D)** B, C and D Only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) A, B and D Only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement A is correct because the zeroth law of thermodynamics defines temperature and thermal equilibrium.

Statement B is correct because the first law of thermodynamics introduces the concept of internal energy through the equation $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$.

Statement C is incorrect because for an isothermal expansion of an ideal gas, the change in internal energy $\Delta U = 0$. According to the first law of thermodynamics, $\Delta Q = \Delta W$.

Statement D is correct because if an intensive variable (which is independent of the size of the system) is multiplied by an extensive variable (which is directly proportional to the size of the system), the product will be directly proportional to the size of the system, making it an extensive variable.

Statement E is incorrect because the ratio of two extensive variables (like any extensive variable and mass) is independent of the size of the system, making it an intensive variable.

Therefore, only statements A, B, and D are correct.

Answer: A, B and D Only

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (4 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

There are two vessels filled with an ideal gas where volume of one is double the volume of other. The large vessel contains the gas at 8 kPa at 1000 K while the smaller vessel contains the gas at 7 kPa at 500 K. If the vessels are connected to each other by a thin tube allowing the gas to flow and the temperature of both vessels is maintained at 600 K , at steady state the pressure in the vessels will be (in kPa).

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4.4$
- **(B)** $6$
- **(C)** $24$
- **(D)** $18$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/fvEA_8lzZ3cGyjkIEaxwsDTH_zQUGL0cGe6xBdb_b2E.original.fullsize.png"/>
Number of masses will remain constant
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{n}_1+\mathrm{n}_2=\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{f}} \\
& \frac{\mathrm{P}_1 \mathrm{~V}_1}{\mathrm{RT}_1}+\frac{\mathrm{P}_2 \mathrm{~V}_2}{\mathrm{RT}_2}=\frac{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{f}} \mathrm{~V}_{\mathrm{f}}}{\mathrm{RT}_{\mathrm{f}}} \\
& \frac{8 \times 2 \mathrm{~V}}{\mathrm{R} \times 1000}+\frac{7 \times \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{R} \times 500}=\frac{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{f}}(3 \mathrm{~V})}{\mathrm{R} \times 600} \\
& \frac{16}{1000}+\frac{14}{1000}=\frac{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{f}}}{\mathrm{R} \times 600} \\
& \frac{30}{1000}=\frac{\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{f}}}{200} \\
& \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{f}}=6 \mathrm{kPa}
\end{aligned}$
