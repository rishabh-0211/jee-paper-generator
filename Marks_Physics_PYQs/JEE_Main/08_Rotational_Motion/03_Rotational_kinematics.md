### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A wheel initially at rest is subjected to a uniform angular acceleration about its axis. In the first $2\text{ s}$ it rotates through an angle $\theta_1$ and in the next $2\text{ s}$ it rotates through an angle $\theta_2$. The ratio $\dfrac{\theta_2}{\theta_1}$ is _______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{1}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the equation of rotational kinematics $\theta = \omega_0 t + \dfrac{1}{2} \alpha t^2$.

Since the wheel is initially at rest, $\omega_0 = 0$.

Angle rotated in the first $2\text{ s}$:

$\theta_1 = \dfrac{1}{2} \alpha (2)^2 = 2\alpha$

Angle rotated in the first $4\text{ s}$:

$\theta_{total} = \dfrac{1}{2} \alpha (4)^2 = 8\alpha$

Angle rotated in the next $2\text{ s}$:

$\theta_2 = \theta_{total} - \theta_1 = 8\alpha - 2\alpha = 6\alpha$

The ratio is:

$\dfrac{\theta_2}{\theta_1} = \dfrac{6\alpha}{2\alpha} = 3$

Answer: $3$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A particle is rotating in a circular path and at any instant its motion can be described as $\theta = \dfrac{5t^4}{40} - \dfrac{t^3}{3}$. The angular acceleration of the particle after $10$ seconds is _______ rad/s$^2$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $150$
- **(B)** $120$
- **(C)** $130$
- **(D)** $170$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $130$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\theta = \dfrac{5t^4}{40} - \dfrac{t^3}{3} = \dfrac{t^4}{8} - \dfrac{t^3}{3}$

Angular velocity $\omega = \dfrac{d\theta}{dt} = \dfrac{4t^3}{8} - \dfrac{3t^2}{3} = \dfrac{t^3}{2} - t^2$

Angular acceleration $\alpha = \dfrac{d\omega}{dt} = \dfrac{3t^2}{2} - 2t$

At $t = 10$ s, $\alpha = \dfrac{3(10)^2}{2} - 2(10)$

$\alpha = 150 - 20 = 130$ rad/s$^2$

Answer: $130$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A fly wheel having mass 3 kg and radius 5 m is free to rotate about a horizontal axis. A string having negligible mass is wound around the wheel and the loose end of the string is connected to 3 kg mass. The mass is kept at rest initially and released. Kinetic energy of the wheel when the mass descends by 3 m is $\_\_\_\_$ $\mathrm{J}.\left(\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **30**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the mass of the flywheel be $M = 3$ kg and its radius be $R = 5$ m.
Let the hanging mass be $m = 3$ kg and the distance descended be $h = 3$ m.
The flywheel is a disk, so its moment of inertia is $I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$.
By conservation of energy, the loss in potential energy of the hanging mass equals the gain in kinetic energy of the system.
$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$
Since the string does not slip, $v = R\omega$, so $\omega = \frac{v}{R}$.
$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{2}MR^2)(\frac{v}{R})^2$
$mgh = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{4}Mv^2$
Substituting the values: $3 \times 10 \times 3 = \frac{1}{2}(3)v^2 + \frac{1}{4}(3)v^2$
$90 = \frac{3}{2}v^2 + \frac{3}{4}v^2 = \frac{9}{4}v^2$
$v^2 = \frac{90 \times 4}{9} = 40$ m$^2$/s$^2$.
The kinetic energy of the wheel is $K_{wheel} = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2 = \frac{1}{4}Mv^2$.
$K_{wheel} = \frac{1}{4} \times 3 \times 40 = 30$ J.

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A uniform rod of mass $m$ and length $l$ suspended by means of two identical inextensible light strings as shown in figure. Tension in one string immediately after the other string is cut, is $\_\_\_\_$. $(g$ acceleration due to gravity) 
 <img style="width:200px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/21S1_q_45_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $m g / 4$
- **(B)** $m g / 2$
- **(C)** $m g / 3$
- **(D)** mg

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $m g / 4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

When string at B is cut, rod rotates about A.
Torque about A: $\tau = mg \times \frac{l}{2}$
Moment of inertia about A: $I_A = \frac{ml^2}{3}$
Angular acceleration: $\alpha = \frac{mg \cdot l/2}{ml^2/3} = \frac{3g}{2l}$
Acceleration of COM: $a_{cm} = \alpha \times \frac{l}{2} = \frac{3g}{4}$
Newton's second law: $mg - T = ma_{cm}$
$T = mg - \frac{3mg}{4} = \frac{mg}{4}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2025 (8 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A thin solid disk of 1 kg is rotating along its diameter axis at the speed of 1800 rpm. By applying an external torque of $25 \pi \mathrm{Nm}$ for 40 s, the speed increases to 2100 rpm. The diameter of the disk is ________ m.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **40**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, $\mathrm{m}=1 \mathrm{~kg}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \omega_{\mathrm{i}}=1800 \mathrm{rpm}=1800 \times \frac{2 \pi}{60}=60 \pi \frac{\mathrm{rad}}{\mathrm{sec}} \\
& \omega_{\mathrm{f}}=2100 \mathrm{rpm}=2100 \times \frac{2 \pi}{60}=70 \pi \frac{\mathrm{rad}}{\mathrm{sec}} \\
& \tau_{e x t}=25 \pi \mathrm{Nm} \\
& \mathrm{t}=40 \mathrm{sec}
\end{aligned}$
Using equation of motion
$\begin{aligned}
& \omega_{\mathrm{f}}=\omega_{\mathrm{i}}+\alpha \mathrm{t} \\
& 70 \pi=60 \pi+\alpha(40) \\
& \alpha=\frac{\pi}{4} \mathrm{rad} / \mathrm{sec}^2
\end{aligned}$
Also, $\tau=\mathrm{I} \alpha$
$\tau=\frac{\mathrm{mR}^2}{4} \alpha$
$\begin{aligned}
& 25 \pi=\frac{1 \times \mathrm{R}^2}{4} \times \frac{\pi}{4} \\
& \mathrm{R}=20 \mathrm{~m}
\end{aligned}$
Hence, diameter of disk $=2 \mathrm{R}=2 \times 20=40 \mathrm{~m}$

</details>
