### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Two masses 400 g and 350 g are suspended from the ends of a light string passing over a heavy pulley of radius 2 cm. When released from rest the heavier mass is observed to fall 81 cm in 9 s. The rotational inertia of the pulley is $\_\_\_\_$ $\mathrm{kg} \cdot \mathrm{m}^{2}$. $\left(\mathrm{g}=9.8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4.75 \times 10^{-3}$
- **(B)** $9.5 \times 10^{-3}$
- **(C)** $1.86 \times 10^{-2}$
- **(D)** $8.3 \times 10^{-3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $9.5 \times 10^{-3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From kinematics with $s = 0.81$ m and $t = 9$ s: $a = 2s/t^2 = 0.02$ m/s². 
For the pulley system, applying Newton's second law to both masses and torque equation: $(m_1 - m_2)g - (m_1 + m_2)a = Ia/R^2$. 
Substituting values: $0.05 \times 9.8 - 0.75 \times 0.02 = I \times 50$ gives $0.49 - 0.015 = 50I$. 
Therefore $I = 9.5 \times 10^{-3}$ kg·m²

</details>
