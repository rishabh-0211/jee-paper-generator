### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (4 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A wheel is rolling on a plane surface. The speed of a particle on the highest point of the rim is $8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. The speed of the particle on the rim of the wheel at the same level as the centre of wheel, will be :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(B)** $8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(C)** $4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
- **(D)** $8 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $4 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/mz_gGMlbrsWBKdkIu06fyDZjEBO1DhMUgtQd3ofUpGY.original.fullsize.png"/>
If $V_B=2 V$
Point A is instantaneous center of rotation
Given $V_B=8 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{V}=4 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \mathrm{~V}_{\mathrm{p}}=\sqrt{2} \mathrm{v} \Rightarrow \mathrm{~V}_{\mathrm{p}}=4 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \operatorname{correct}(1)
\end{aligned}$

### Q5. JEE Main 2012 (12 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A solid sphere is rolling on a surface as shown in figure, with a translational velocity $v \mathrm{~m} \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$. If it is to climb the inclined surface continuing to roll without slipping, then minimum velocity for this to happen is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/A_BCkEX2ZTzTq-FIU9NtxqTpXWypyw5HEL67ejIFpkw.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{2 g h}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{\frac{7}{5} g h}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\frac{7}{2} g h}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{\frac{10}{7} g h}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\sqrt{\frac{10}{7} g h}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Minimum velocity for a body rolling without slipping
$$
v=\sqrt{\frac{2 g h}{1+\frac{K^2}{R^2}}}
$$
For solid sphere, $\frac{K^2}{R^2}=\frac{2}{5}$
$$
\therefore \quad v=\sqrt{\frac{2 g h}{1+\frac{K^2}{R^2}}}=\sqrt{\frac{10}{7} g h}
$$

</details>
