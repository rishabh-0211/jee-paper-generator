### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two identical bodies $A$ and $B$ of equal masses have initial velocities $\vec{v_1} = 4\hat{i}$ m/s and $\vec{v_2} = 4\hat{j}$ m/s respectively. The body $A$ has acceleration $\vec{a_1} = 6\hat{i} + 6\hat{j}$ m/s$^2$ while the acceleration of the other body $B$ is zero. The centre of mass of the two bodies moves in __________ path.

**Options:**

- **(A)** circular
- **(B)** parabolic
- **(C)** straight line
- **(D)** elliptical

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) straight line**

**Detailed Solution:**

Velocity of the centre of mass is given by:

$\vec{v}_{cm} = \dfrac{m_1\vec{v}_1 + m_2\vec{v}_2}{m_1 + m_2}$

Since $m_1 = m_2 = m$, we get:

$\vec{v}_{cm} = \dfrac{m(4\hat{i}) + m(4\hat{j})}{2m} = 2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j}$ m/s

Acceleration of the centre of mass is given by:

$\vec{a}_{cm} = \dfrac{m_1\vec{a}_1 + m_2\vec{a}_2}{m_1 + m_2}$

$\vec{a}_{cm} = \dfrac{m(6\hat{i} + 6\hat{j}) + 0}{2m} = 3\hat{i} + 3\hat{j}$ m/s$^2$

Here, $\vec{v}_{cm} = 2(\hat{i} + \hat{j})$ and $\vec{a}_{cm} = 3(\hat{i} + \hat{j})$.

Since the initial velocity and acceleration of the centre of mass are parallel to each other, the centre of mass moves in a straight line path.

Answer: straight line

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two blocks of masses $2$ kg and $1$ kg respectively, are tied to the ends of a string which passes over a light frictionless pulley as shown in the figure below. The masses are held at rest at the same horizontal level and then released. The distance traversed by the centre of mass in $2$ s is _______ m. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)
<img style="width:300px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/02APRS2__q28_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3.33$
- **(B)** $3.12$
- **(C)** $2.22$
- **(D)** $1.42$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2.22$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The acceleration of the blocks in the Atwood machine is given by:

$a = \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2} g$

Substituting the given values ($m_1 = 2$ kg, $m_2 = 1$ kg):

$a = \dfrac{2 - 1}{2 + 1} g = \dfrac{g}{3}$

Taking the downward direction as positive, the acceleration of the $2$ kg block is $a_1 = \dfrac{g}{3}$ and the acceleration of the $1$ kg block is $a_2 = -\dfrac{g}{3}$.

The acceleration of the centre of mass is:

$a_{cm} = \dfrac{m_1 a_1 + m_2 a_2}{m_1 + m_2}$

$a_{cm} = \dfrac{2 \left(\dfrac{g}{3}\right) + 1 \left(-\dfrac{g}{3}\right)}{2 + 1} = \dfrac{\dfrac{g}{3}}{3} = \dfrac{g}{9}$

Since the system is released from rest, the initial velocity of the centre of mass is zero.

The distance traversed by the centre of mass in $t = 2$ s is:

$S_{cm} = \dfrac{1}{2} a_{cm} t^2$

$S_{cm} = \dfrac{1}{2} \left(\dfrac{10}{9}\right) (2)^2 = \dfrac{20}{9} \approx 2.22 \text{ m}$

Answer: $2.22$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A body $A$ of mass $M$ while falling vertically downwards under gravity breaks into two parts; a body B of mass $1 / 3 \mathrm{M}$ and a body $\mathrm{C}$ of mass $2 / 3 \mathrm{M}$. The centre of mass of bodies $\mathrm{B}$ and $\mathrm{C}$ taken together shifts compared to that of body $\mathrm{A}$ towards

**Options:**

- **(A)** depends on height of breaking
- **(B)** does not shift
- **(C)** body C
- **(D)** body B

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) does not shift**

**Detailed Solution:**

No horizontal external force is acting
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{cm}}=0 \\
& \text { since } \mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{cm}}=0 \\
& \therefore \Delta \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{cm}}=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2002

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two identical particles move towards each other with velocity $2 v$ and $v$ respectively. The velocity of centre of mass is

**Options:**

- **(A)** v
- **(B)** v/3
- **(C)** v/2
- **(D)** zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) v/2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Conserving Linear Momentum
$$
2 \mathrm{Mv}_{\mathrm{c}}=2 \mathrm{Mv}-\mathrm{Mv} \Rightarrow \mathrm{v}_{\mathrm{c}}=\mathrm{v} / 2
$$

</details>
