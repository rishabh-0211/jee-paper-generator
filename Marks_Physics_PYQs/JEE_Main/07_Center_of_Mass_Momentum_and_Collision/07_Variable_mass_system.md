### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (28 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A balloon and its content having mass $M$ is moving up with an acceleration ' $a$ '. The mass that must be released from the content so that the balloon starts moving up with an acceleration ' $3 a^{\prime}$ will be
(Take ' g ' as acceleration due to gravity)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{2 \mathrm{Ma}}{3 \mathrm{a}+\mathrm{g}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{3 \mathrm{Ma}}{2 \mathrm{a}-\mathrm{g}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3 \mathrm{Ma}}{2 \mathrm{a}+\mathrm{g}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2 \mathrm{Ma}}{3 \mathrm{a}-\mathrm{g}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{2 \mathrm{Ma}}{3 \mathrm{a}+\mathrm{g}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/pzfaRVzhOXPJGRDRlOOdi-JRXuf8-leiVkag2Uo_R60.original.fullsize.png">


$\begin{aligned}
& F-m g=m a \\
& F=m a+m g \\
& F-(m-x) g=(m-x) 3 a
\end{aligned}$

Put F
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{Ma}+\mathrm{mg}-\mathrm{mg}+\mathrm{xg}=3 \mathrm{ma}-3 \mathrm{xa} \\
& \mathrm{x}=\frac{2 \mathrm{ma}}{\mathrm{~g}+3 \mathrm{a}}
\end{aligned}$
