### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (08 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A player caught a cricket ball of mass $150 \mathrm{~g}$ moving at a speed of $20 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$. If the catching process is completed in $0.1 \mathrm{~s}$, the magnitude of force exerted by the ball on the hand of the player is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(B)** $300 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(C)** $150 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $30 \mathrm{~N}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $30 \mathrm{~N}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{F}=\frac{\Delta \mathrm{P}}{\Delta \mathrm{t}}=\frac{\mathrm{mv}-0}{0.1} \\ & =\frac{150 \times 10^{-3} \times 20}{0.1}=30 \mathrm{~N}\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q11. JEE Main 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The figure shows the position - time $(x-t)$ graph of one-dimensional motion of a body of mass $0.4 \mathrm{~kg}$. The magnitude of each impulse is 
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/cqAjxDb2-bp7snL99-w3cbEdG5O7muCCt8W80BAneHk.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.4 \mathrm{Ns}$
- **(B)** $0.8 \mathrm{Ns}$
- **(C)** $1.6 \mathrm{Ns}$
- **(D)** $0.2 \mathrm{Ns}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.8 \mathrm{Ns}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the graph, it is a straight line so, uniform motion. Because of impulse direction of velocity changes as can be seen from the slope of the graph.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \text { Initial velocity }=\frac{2}{2}=1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \text { Final velocity }=-\frac{2}{2}=-1 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s} \\
& \qquad \bar{P}_{\mathrm{i}}=0.4 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s} \\
& \qquad \overline{\mathrm{P}}_{\mathrm{i}}=-0.4 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s} \\
& \overrightarrow{\mathrm{J}}=\overline{\mathrm{P}}_{\mathrm{i}}-\overline{\mathrm{P}}_{\mathrm{i}}=-0.4-0.4=-0.8 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}(\overrightarrow{\mathrm{J}}=\text { impulse }) \\
& |\overrightarrow{\mathrm{J}}|=0.8 \mathrm{~N}-\mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
