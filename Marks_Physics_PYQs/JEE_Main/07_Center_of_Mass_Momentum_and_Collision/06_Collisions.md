### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a perfectly inelastic collision, two spheres made of the same material with masses 15 kg and 25 kg, moving in opposite directions with speeds of $10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ and $30 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$, respectively, strike each other and stick together. The rise in temperature (in ${ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$), if all the heat produced during the collision is retained by these spheres, is : (specific heat of sphere material $31 \mathrm{cal} / \mathrm{kg}.{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and $1 \mathrm{cal}=4.2 \mathrm{~J}$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.15$
- **(B)** $1.75$
- **(C)** 1.44
- **(D)** 1.95

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 1.44**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using conservation of momentum:
$m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2 = (m_1 + m_2)v_f$ where $v_2 = -30$ m/s (opposite direction).
$15(10) + 25(-30) = 40v_f \Rightarrow v_f = -15$ m/s
Initial kinetic energy:
$KE_i = \frac{1}{2}(15)(10)^2 + \frac{1}{2}(25)(30)^2 = 750 + 11250 = 12000$ J
Final kinetic energy:
$KE_f = \frac{1}{2}(40)(15)^2 = 4500$ J
Heat produced:
$Q = 12000 - 4500 = 7500$ J $= \frac{7500}{4.2} = 1785.71$ cal
Temperature rise:
$\Delta T = \frac{Q}{m_{total} \cdot c} = \frac{1785.71}{40 \times 31} = 1.44°C$

</details>

m u &=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(m v_1+M v_2\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
Along $y$-direction,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&o=m v_1 \sin 45^{\circ}-M v_2 \sin 45^{\circ} \\
&o=\left(m v_1-M v_2\right) \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
