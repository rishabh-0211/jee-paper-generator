### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Angle of minimum deviation is equal to the half of the angle of prism in an equilateral prism. The refractive index of the prism is __________.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.5$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{2}$
- **(D)** $1.65$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For an equilateral prism, the angle of the prism is $A = 60^{\circ}$.

Given that the angle of minimum deviation is half of the angle of the prism:

$\delta_{m} = \dfrac{A}{2} = \dfrac{60^{\circ}}{2} = 30^{\circ}$

The refractive index of the prism is given by:

$\mu = \dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{A + \delta_{m}}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\dfrac{A}{2}\right)}$

Substituting the values of $A$ and $\delta_{m}$:

$\mu = \dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{60^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\dfrac{60^{\circ}}{2}\right)}$

$\mu = \dfrac{\sin(45^{\circ})}{\sin(30^{\circ})}$

$\mu = \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}{\dfrac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{2}$

Answer: $\sqrt{2}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A ray of light passing through an equilateral prism is having velocity $2.12 \times 10^8$ m/s in the prism material, then the minimum angle of deviation is _________ degrees.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $45$
- **(B)** $30$
- **(C)** $28$
- **(D)** $58$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $30$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Velocity of light in vacuum, $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s

Velocity of light in the prism, $v = 2.12 \times 10^8$ m/s

Refractive index of the prism material, $\mu = \dfrac{c}{v} = \dfrac{3 \times 10^8}{2.12 \times 10^8} \approx 1.414 \approx \sqrt{2}$

For an equilateral prism, the angle of the prism $A = 60^\circ$.

Using the prism formula:

$\mu = \dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{A + \delta_m}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\dfrac{A}{2}\right)}$

$\sqrt{2} = \dfrac{\sin\left(\dfrac{60^\circ + \delta_m}{2}\right)}{\sin(30^\circ)}$

$\sqrt{2} = \dfrac{\sin\left(30^\circ + \dfrac{\delta_m}{2}\right)}{\dfrac{1}{2}}$

$\sin\left(30^\circ + \dfrac{\delta_m}{2}\right) = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$

$30^\circ + \dfrac{\delta_m}{2} = 45^\circ$

$\dfrac{\delta_m}{2} = 15^\circ$

$\delta_m = 30^\circ$

Answer: $30$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For a thin symmetric prism made of glass (refractive index $1.5$), the ratio of incident angle and minimum deviation will be _______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 : 4$
- **(B)** $3 : 2$
- **(C)** $2 : 1$
- **(D)** $1 : 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3 : 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a thin prism, the angle of minimum deviation is given by $\delta_m = (\mu - 1)A$.

At the position of minimum deviation, the angle of incidence $i$ and angle of emergence $e$ are equal, and the angle of refraction is $r = \dfrac{A}{2}$.

Using Snell's law for small angles, $i = \mu r = \mu \left(\dfrac{A}{2}\right)$.

The ratio of the incident angle to the minimum deviation is:

$\dfrac{i}{\delta_m} = \dfrac{\dfrac{\mu A}{2}}{(\mu - 1)A} = \dfrac{\mu}{2(\mu - 1)}$

Given $\mu = 1.5$, substituting the value:

$\dfrac{i}{\delta_m} = \dfrac{1.5}{2(1.5 - 1)} = \dfrac{1.5}{2(0.5)} = \dfrac{1.5}{1} = \dfrac{3}{2}$

Answer: $3 : 2$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For a transparent prism, if the angle of minimum deviation is equal to its refracting angle, the refractive index $n$ of the prism satisfies.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1 < n < 2$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{2} < n < 2 \sqrt{2}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{2} < n < 2$
- **(D)** $n \geqslant 2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{2} < n < 2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At minimum deviation, $\delta_m = A$ (angle of minimum deviation equals prism angle). The refractive index formula becomes:
$n = \frac{\sin\left(\frac{A + \delta_m}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)} = \frac{\sin\left(\frac{2A}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)} = \frac{\sin A}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)}$
Using $\sin A = 2\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)$:
$n = \frac{2\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)} = 2\cos\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)$
For a physical prism, $0 < A < 90°$, so $45° < \frac{A}{2} < 90°$, giving $0 < \cos\left(\frac{A}{2}\right) < \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.
Therefore, $1 < n < \sqrt{2} \cdot 2 = 2\sqrt{2}$. However, more precisely, $\sqrt{2} < n < 2$.

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The exit surface of a prism with refractive index $n$ is coated with a material having refractive index $\frac{n}{2}$. When this prism is set for minimum angle of deviation, it exactly meets the condition of critical angle. The prism angle is $\_\_\_\_$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $30^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $15^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $45^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $60^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $60^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the refractive index of the prism be $n_1 = n$ and the refractive index of the coating on the exit surface be $n_2 = \frac{n}{2}$.
At the condition of minimum deviation, the angle of refraction at the first surface $r_1$ and the angle of incidence at the second surface $r_2$ are equal, i.e., $r_1 = r_2 = \frac{A}{2}$, where $A$ is the prism angle.
The problem states that at the exit surface, the light meets the condition of critical angle. Therefore, $r_2 = \theta_c$.
The critical angle $\theta_c$ for the interface between the prism and the coating is given by $\sin \theta_c = \frac{n_2}{n_1}$.
Substituting the values, $\sin \theta_c = \frac{n/2}{n} = \frac{1}{2}$.
Thus, $\theta_c = 30^\circ$.
Since $r_2 = \theta_c$, we have $r_2 = 30^\circ$.
Using the relation $r_1 = r_2 = \frac{A}{2}$ for minimum deviation, we get $\frac{A}{2} = 30^\circ$.
Therefore, $A = 60^\circ$.

</details>

### Q8. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 1)

Consider an equilateral prism (refractive index $\sqrt{2}$). A ray of light is incident on its one surface at a certain angle $i$. If the emergent ray is found to graze along the other surface then the angle of refraction at the incident surface is close to $\_\_\_\_$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $20^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $15^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $40^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $30^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $15^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given an equilateral prism, the angle of the prism is $A = 60^{\circ}$.
The refractive index of the prism is $\mu = \sqrt{2}$.
The emergent ray grazes the second surface, which means the angle of emergence is $e = 90^{\circ}$.
Applying Snell's law at the second surface: $\mu \sin(r_2) = 1 \cdot \sin(e)$.
$\sqrt{2} \sin(r_2) = \sin(90^{\circ}) = 1$.
$\sin(r_2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, which gives $r_2 = 45^{\circ}$.
For a prism, we know the relation $r_1 + r_2 = A$.
Substituting the values: $r_1 + 45^{\circ} = 60^{\circ}$.
$r_1 = 60^{\circ} - 45^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$.
The angle of refraction at the incident surface is $r_1 = 15^{\circ}$.

### Q9. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 2)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $60^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the geometry of the prism, the angle of the prism is $A = 180^\circ - (45^\circ + \theta)$.
The incident ray is parallel to the base, so the angle of incidence $i$ at the first surface is equal to the base angle $45^\circ$. Thus, $i = 45^\circ$.
Applying Snell's law at the first surface: $1 \cdot \sin(45^\circ) = \mu \sin(r_1)$.

$75^\circ = 135^\circ - \theta \implies \theta = 135^\circ - 75^\circ = 60^\circ$.
