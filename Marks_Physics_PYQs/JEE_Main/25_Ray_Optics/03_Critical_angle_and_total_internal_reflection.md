### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

One side of an equilateral prism is painted by a transparent material of refractive index $n_2$. The refractive index of prism is $1.6$. The minimum value of $n_2$ required for total internal reflection from painted face is _______.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/02APRS2__q41_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3\sqrt{3}/1.6$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(C)** $3.2/\sqrt{3}$
- **(D)** $4\sqrt{3}/5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4\sqrt{3}/5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that the prism is an equilateral triangle, all its refracting angles are $60^\circ$.

The incident ray falls normally on one of the faces of the prism. Therefore, the angle of incidence is $0^\circ$ and the ray enters the prism undeviated.

From the geometry of the figure, the ray strikes the bottom painted face. In the right-angled triangle formed by the ray and the two faces of the prism, the angle between the left face and the bottom face is $60^\circ$.

The angle the refracted ray makes with the bottom face is $90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ$.

The angle of incidence $i$ at the bottom painted face is the angle made with the normal to that face:
$i = 90^\circ - 30^\circ = 60^\circ$

For total internal reflection to occur at the painted face, the angle of incidence must be greater than or equal to the critical angle $\theta_c$ for the prism-paint interface:
$i \ge \theta_c$
$\sin i \ge \sin \theta_c$

The critical angle is given by $\sin \theta_c = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1}$, where $n_1 = 1.6$ is the refractive index of the prism and $n_2$ is the refractive index of the paint.

Substituting the values:
$\sin 60^\circ \ge \dfrac{n_2}{1.6}$
$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \ge \dfrac{n_2}{1.6}$
$n_2 \le 1.6 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
$n_2 \le 0.8\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{5}$

The limiting value of $n_2$ required for total internal reflection is $\dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{5}$.

Answer: $4\sqrt{3}/5$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Consider light travelling from a medium $A$ to medium $B$ separated by a plane interface. If the light undergoes total internal reflection during its travel from medium $A$ to $B$ and the speed of light in media $A$ and $B$ are $2.4 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$ and $2.7 \times 10^{8} \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}$, respectively, then the value of critical angle is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{9}\right)$
- **(B)** $\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{9}{8}\right)$
- **(C)** $\cot ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{\sqrt{13}}\right)$
- **(D)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{\sqrt{17}}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{\sqrt{17}}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The refractive index of a medium is inversely proportional to the speed of light in that medium, given by $n = \frac{c}{v}$.
For light travelling from medium $A$ to medium $B$, the critical angle $\theta_c$ is defined when light travels from a denser to a rarer medium.
Given speeds are $v_A = 2.4 \times 10^8$ m/s and $v_B = 2.7 \times 10^8$ m/s.
The refractive indices are $n_A = \frac{c}{v_A}$ and $n_B = \frac{c}{v_B}$.
The critical angle $\theta_c$ is given by $\sin \theta_c = \frac{n_B}{n_A} = \frac{v_A}{v_B}$.
Substituting the values: $\sin \theta_c = \frac{2.4 \times 10^8}{2.7 \times 10^8} = \frac{24}{27} = \frac{8}{9}$.
To find the value in terms of $\tan^{-1}$, we use the trigonometric identity for a right-angled triangle where perpendicular $p = 8$ and hypotenuse $h = 9$.
The base $b = \sqrt{h^2 - p^2} = \sqrt{9^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{81 - 64} = \sqrt{17}$.
Thus, $\tan \theta_c = \frac{p}{b} = \frac{8}{\sqrt{17}}$.
Therefore, $\theta_c = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{8}{\sqrt{17}}\right)$.

</details>

### Q20. JEE Main 2013 (09 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Light is incident from a medium into air at two possible angles of incidence (A) $20^{\circ}$ and (B) $40^{\circ}$. In the medium light travels $3.0 \mathrm{~cm}$ in $0.2 \mathrm{~ns}$. The ray will :

**Options:**

- **(A)** suffer total internal reflection in both cases (A) and (B)
- **(B)** suffer total internal reflection in case (B) only
- **(C)** have partial reflection and partial transmission in case (B)
- **(D)** have $100 \%$ transmission in case (A)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) suffer total internal reflection in case (B) only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Velocity of light in medium
$$
\mathrm{V}_{\text {med }}=\frac{3 \mathrm{~cm}}{0.2 \mathrm{~ns}}=\frac{3 \times 10^{-2} \mathrm{~m}}{0.2 \times 10^{-9} \mathrm{~s}}=1.5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}
$$
Refractive index of the medium
$$
\mu=\frac{V_{\text {air }}}{V_{\text {med }}}=\frac{3 \times 10^8}{1.5}=2 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}
$$
As $\mu=\frac{1}{\sin C}$
$$
\therefore \sin \mathrm{C}=\frac{1}{\mu}=\frac{1}{2}=30^{\circ}
$$
Condition of TIR is angle of incidence i must be greater than critical angle. Hence ray will suffer TIR in case of $(B)\left(i=40^{\circ}>30^{\circ}\right)$ only.
