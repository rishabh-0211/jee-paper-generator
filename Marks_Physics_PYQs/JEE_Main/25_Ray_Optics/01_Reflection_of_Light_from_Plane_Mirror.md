To get three images of a single object, one should have two plane mirrors at an angle of

- **(C)** $120^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $30^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $90^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Image formula

$\Rightarrow \frac{360}{\theta}=4 \Rightarrow \theta=\frac{360}{4}=90$
