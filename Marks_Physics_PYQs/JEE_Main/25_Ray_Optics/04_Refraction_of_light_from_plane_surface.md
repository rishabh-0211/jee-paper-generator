### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Light ray incident along a vector $\vec{AO}$ $(\vec{AO} = 2\hat{i}-3\hat{j})$ emerges out along vector $\vec{OB}$ $(\vec{OB} = C\hat{i}-4\hat{j})$ as shown in the figure below. The value of $C$ is ________.
<img style="width: 300px" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/08APRS2__q42_d1.jpg" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.6$
- **(B)** $0.16$
- **(C)** $11.6$
- **(D)** $16$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $1.6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given figure, the interface is horizontal and the normal is along the y-axis.

The incident ray is along the vector $\vec{AO} = 2\hat{i} - 3\hat{j}$.
The angle of incidence $\alpha$ is the angle between the incident ray and the normal.
$\sin \alpha = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-3)^2}} = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$

The refracted ray is along the vector $\vec{OB} = C\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}$.
The angle of refraction $\beta$ is the angle between the refracted ray and the normal.
$\sin \beta = \dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2 + (-4)^2}} = \dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2 + 16}}$

Applying Snell's law at the interface:
$\mu_1 \sin \alpha = \mu_2 \sin \beta$
$1 \times \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} = 1.5 \times \dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2 + 16}}$
$\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} = \dfrac{3}{2} \dfrac{C}{\sqrt{C^2 + 16}}$

Squaring both sides:
$\dfrac{4}{13} = \dfrac{9}{4} \left( \dfrac{C^2}{C^2 + 16} \right)$
$16(C^2 + 16) = 117 C^2$
$16 C^2 + 256 = 117 C^2$
$101 C^2 = 256$
$C^2 = \dfrac{256}{101}$
$C = \dfrac{16}{\sqrt{101}} \approx 1.592$

Approximating $\sqrt{101} \approx 10$, we get $C \approx 1.6$.

Answer: $1.6$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The wavelength of light, while it is passing through water is 540 nm. The refractive index of water is $4 / 3$. The wavelength of the same light when it is passing through a transparent medium having refractive index of $3 / 2$ is $\_\_\_\_$ nm.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 540
- **(B)** 480
- **(C)** 840
- **(D)** 380

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 480**

**Detailed Solution:**

Frequency of light is constant in all media.
In water with refractive index $4/3$, wavelength is 540 nm.
The wavelength in vacuum is $\lambda_0 = 540 \times \frac{4}{3} = 720$ nm.
In a medium with refractive index $3/2$, the wavelength is $\lambda = \frac{720}{3/2} = 720 \times \frac{2}{3} = 480$ nm.

</details>

A printed page is pressed by a glass of water. The refractive index of the glass and water is $1.5$ and $1.33$, respectively. If the thickness of the bottom of glass is $1 \mathrm{~cm}$ and depth of water is $5 \mathrm{~cm}$, how much the page will appear to be shifted if viewed from the top ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.033 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(B)** $3.581 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(C)** $1.3533 \mathrm{~cm}$
- **(D)** $1.90 \mathrm{~cm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Detailed Solution:**
