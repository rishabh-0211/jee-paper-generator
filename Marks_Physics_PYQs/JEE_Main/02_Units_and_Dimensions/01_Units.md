### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A new unit $(\alpha)$ of length is chosen such that it is equal to the speed of light in vacuum. What is the distance between Venus and Earth in terms of $\alpha$ units if light takes $6$ min. $40$ s to cover this distance?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $200\,\alpha$
- **(B)** $400\,\alpha$
- **(C)** $300\,\alpha$
- **(D)** $500\,\alpha$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $400\,\alpha$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that the new unit of length $\alpha$ is chosen such that the speed of light in vacuum is $1\,\alpha/\text{s}$.

Time taken by light to cover the distance, $t = 6\text{ min } 40\text{ s}$

$t = 6 \times 60\text{ s} + 40\text{ s} = 400\text{ s}$

Distance between Venus and Earth is given by $d = c \times t$

$d = 1\,\alpha/\text{s} \times 400\text{ s} = 400\,\alpha$

Answer: $400\,\alpha$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Keeping the significant figures in view, the sum of the physical quantities $52.01 \mathrm{~m}, 153.2 \mathrm{~m}$ and 0.123 m is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 205.3 m
- **(B)** 205.333 m
- **(C)** 205.33 m
- **(D)** 205 m

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 205.3 m**

**Detailed Solution:**

When adding measurements, the result must be limited to the least number of decimal places among all quantities.
The measurements are: 52.01 m (2 decimal places), 153.2 m (1 decimal place), and 0.123 m (3 decimal places).
The limiting factor is 1 decimal place.
Calculating the sum: 52.01 + 153.2 + 0.123 = 205.333 m.
Rounding to 1 decimal place gives 205.3 m, which is option (1).

</details>

- **(C)** $5.32 \mathrm{~cm}$

The least count (L.C.) of a screw guage is the smallest length which can be measured accurately with it.
