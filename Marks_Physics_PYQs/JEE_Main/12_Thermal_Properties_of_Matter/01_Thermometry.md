### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The temperature of a body in air falls from $40^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to $24^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ in 4 minutes. The temperature of the air is $16^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The temperature of the body in the next 4 minutes will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{14}{3}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $\frac{42}{3}^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $\frac{28}{3}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $\frac{56}{3}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{56}{3}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& \Delta T=T_2-T_1=16^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\
& \text { And } T_0=16^{\circ} \mathrm{C} \\
& \frac{\Delta T}{t_1}=-k\left(32-16^{\circ}\right)...(i) \\
& \frac{\left(24-T_3\right)}{4}=-k\left(\frac{24+T_3}{2}-16\right) ....(ii) \\
& \frac{16}{4}=-k(16) \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{\left(24-T_3\right)}{4}=-k\left(12+\frac{T_3}{2}-16\right) \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{16}{24-T_3}=\frac{16}{T_3} T_2 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{T_3}{2}-4=24-T_3 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{3 T_3}{2}=28 \\
& \Rightarrow T_3=\frac{56}{3}{ }^{\circ} \mathrm{C}
\end{aligned}$

</details>
