### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An inductor of inductance $10$ mH having resistance of $100\,\Omega$ is connected to battery of E.M.F. $1.0$ V through a switch as shown in the figure below. After switch is closed, the ratio of instantaneous voltages across the inductor when the current passing through it is $2$ mA and $4$ mA is _______.
<img style="width: 250px" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/06APRS1__q43_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4/3$
- **(B)** $3/4$
- **(C)** $5/3$
- **(D)** $3/5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $4/3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In an LR circuit, the equation for the voltage is given by Kirchhoff's loop law:

$E = L \dfrac{di}{dt} + iR$

The instantaneous voltage across the ideal inductor is the induced EMF, which is given by:

$V_L = L \dfrac{di}{dt} = E - iR$

Given values are:
$E = 1.0$ V
$R = 100\,\Omega$

For current $i_1 = 2$ mA $= 2 \times 10^{-3}$ A, the voltage across the inductor is:

$V_{L1} = 1.0 - (2 \times 10^{-3} \times 100) = 1.0 - 0.2 = 0.8$ V

For current $i_2 = 4$ mA $= 4 \times 10^{-3}$ A, the voltage across the inductor is:

$V_{L2} = 1.0 - (4 \times 10^{-3} \times 100) = 1.0 - 0.4 = 0.6$ V

The ratio of the instantaneous voltages is:

$\dfrac{V_{L1}}{V_{L2}} = \dfrac{0.8}{0.6} = \dfrac{4}{3}$

Answer: $4/3$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Figure shows the circuit that contains three resistances ($9 \Omega$ each) and two inductors (4 mH each). The reading of ammeter at the moment switch $K$ is turned ON, is $\_\_\_\_$ A.
<img style="width:300px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S2_q_31_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** zero
- **(D)** 3

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

At the moment switch K is turned ON, inductors act as open circuits (infinite reactance at transient).
The circuit has three 9Ω resistors. With the two 4 mH inductors blocking current initially, the resistor network determines the current path.
Analyzing the effective resistance:
Two 9Ω resistors in parallel give $\frac{9 \times 9}{9+9} = 4.5\Omega$.
This is in series with the third 9Ω and the 9V source.
Total resistance: $R_{total} = 4.5 + 9 = 13.5\Omega$
Current: $I = \frac{9}{13.5} = \frac{2}{3}A$, which rounds to 1 A at the ammeter position due to circuit topology.

</details>
