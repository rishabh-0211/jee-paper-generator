### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For an experimental expression $y=\frac{32.3 \times 1125}{27.4}$, where all the digits are significant. Then to report the value of $y$ we should write

**Options:**

- **(A)** $y=1326.19$
- **(B)** $y=1330$
- **(C)** $y=1326.186$
- **(D)** $y=1326.2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $y=1330$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$y=\frac{32.3 \times 1125}{27.4}=1326.18$
So we need to report to three significant digit.
So, $y=1330$

</details>
