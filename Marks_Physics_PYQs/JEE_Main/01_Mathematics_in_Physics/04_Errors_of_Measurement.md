### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The density $\rho$ of a uniform cylinder is determined by measuring its mass $m$, length $l$ and diameter $d$. The measured values of $m$, $l$ and $d$ are $97.42 \pm 0.02$ g, $8.35 \pm 0.05$ mm and $20.20 \pm 0.02$ mm, respectively. Calculated percentage fractional error in $\rho$ is _______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.63\%$
- **(B)** $0.82\%$
- **(C)** $0.72\%$
- **(D)** $0.25\%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.82\%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The density of a uniform cylinder is given by $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V} = \dfrac{4m}{\pi d^2 l}$

The percentage fractional error in density is given by:

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} \times 100 = \left( \dfrac{\Delta m}{m} + 2\dfrac{\Delta d}{d} + \dfrac{\Delta l}{l} \right) \times 100$

Substituting the given values:

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} \times 100 = \left( \dfrac{0.02}{97.42} + 2 \times \dfrac{0.02}{20.20} + \dfrac{0.05}{8.35} \right) \times 100$

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} \times 100 = \left( 0.000205 + 0.00198 + 0.005988 \right) \times 100$

$\dfrac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} \times 100 = 0.008173 \times 100 = 0.8173\%$

Rounding off to two decimal places, the percentage error is $0.82\%$.

Answer: $0.82\%$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Four persons measure the length of a rod as $20.00 \mathrm{~cm}, 19.75 \mathrm{~cm}, 17.01 \mathrm{~cm}$ and 18.25 cm. The relative error in the measurement of average length of the rod is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.08
- **(B)** 0.06
- **(C)** 0.18
- **(D)** 0.24

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 0.06**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mean $= \frac{20.00+19.75+17.01+18.25}{4} = \frac{75.01}{4} = 18.7525$ cm.
Absolute errors: $|20.00-18.7525|=1.2475$, $|19.75-18.7525|=0.9975$, $|17.01-18.7525|=1.7425$, $|18.25-18.7525|=0.5025$.
Mean absolute error $= \frac{1.2475+0.9975+1.7425+0.5025}{4} = \frac{4.49}{4} = 1.1225$.
Relative error $= \frac{1.1225}{18.7525} \approx 0.06$.

</details>

### Q63. JEE Main 2012 (19 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The amount of heat produced in an electric circuit depends upon the current $(I)$, resistance $(R)$ and time $(t)$. If the error made in the measurements of the above quantities are $2 \%, 1 \%$ and $1 \%$ respectively then the maximum possible error in the total heat produced will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1 \%$
- **(B)** $2 \%$
- **(C)** $6 \%$
- **(D)** $3 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $6 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given : $\frac{\Delta I}{I} \times 100=2 \%$;
$\frac{\Delta R}{R} \times 100=1 \%$ and $\frac{\Delta t}{t} \times 100=1 \%$
Heat produced, $H=I^2 R t$
Maximum possible error in heat produced,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{\Delta H}{H} \times 100 \\
& =2\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I} \times 100\right)+\frac{\Delta R}{R} \times 100+\frac{\Delta t}{t} \times 100 \\
& =2 \times 2 \%+1 \%+1 \%=6 \%
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q64. JEE Main 2012 (Offline)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Resistance of a given wire is obtained by measuring the current flowing in it and the voltage difference applied across it. If the percentage errors in the measurement of the current and the voltage difference are $3 \%$ each, then error in the value of resistance of the wire is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6 \%$
- **(B)** zero
- **(C)** $1 \%$
- **(D)** $3 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $6 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{R}=\frac{\mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{i}}$
$\Rightarrow \quad\left|\frac{\Delta \mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{R}}\right|=\left|\frac{\Delta \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{V}}\right|+\left|\frac{\Delta \mathrm{i}}{\mathrm{i}}\right|$
$\frac{\Delta \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{V}} \times 100=3$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{\Delta \mathrm{V}}{\mathrm{V}}=0.03$
Similarly, $\frac{\Delta i}{i}=0.03$
Hence $\frac{\Delta \mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{R}}=0.06$
So percentage error is $\frac{\Delta \mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{R}} \times 100=6 \%$

</details>

If a wire is stretched to make it $0.1 \%$ longer, its resistance will :

- **(D)** increases by $0.05 \%$

$$

Thus when wire is stretched by $0.1 \%$ resistance increases by $0.2 \%$

### Q67. JEE Main 2003

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The length of a simple pendulum executing simple harmonic motion is increased by $21 \%$. The percentage increase in the time period of the pendulum of increased length is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $11 \%$
- **(B)** $21 \%$
- **(C)** $42 \%$
- **(D)** $10 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $10 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{T}=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{l}}{\mathrm{g}}} ; \log \mathrm{T}=\log (2 \pi)+\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{\mathrm{l}}{\mathrm{g}}\right) \Rightarrow \log \mathrm{T}=\log (2 \pi)+\frac{1}{2} \log (1)-\frac{1}{2} \log (\mathrm{g})$
Differentiating


$\frac{\Delta \mathrm{T}}{\mathrm{T}}=0+\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\Delta \mathrm{l}}{\mathrm{l}}-0 \Rightarrow \frac{\Delta \mathrm{T}}{\mathrm{T}} \times 100=\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\Delta \mathrm{l}}{\mathrm{l}} \times 100$                           $=\frac{1}{2} \times 21=10.5 \approx 10 \%$
Note: In this method, the $\%$ error obtained is an approximate value on the higher side. Exact value is less than the obtained one.

</details>
