### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

One gas of $n_1$ mole of molecules at temperature $T_1$, volume $V_1$, and pressure $P_1$, and another gas of $n_2$ mole of molecules at temperature $T_2$, volume $V_2$, and pressure $P_2$, are mixed resulting in pressure $P$ and volume $V$ of the mixture. The temperature of the mixture is _____.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(T_1 + T_2)/2$
- **(B)** $T_1 T_2 PV/(T_2 P_1 V_1 + T_1 P_2 V_2)$
- **(C)** $(T_2 P_1 V_1 + T_1 P_2 V_2)/(T_1 T_2 PV)$
- **(D)** $|T_1 - T_2|/2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $T_1 T_2 PV/(T_2 P_1 V_1 + T_1 P_2 V_2)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the ideal gas equation, the number of moles of the first gas is $n_1 = \dfrac{P_1 V_1}{R T_1}$ and the number of moles of the second gas is $n_2 = \dfrac{P_2 V_2}{R T_2}$.

For the mixture, the total number of moles is $n = n_1 + n_2$.

Applying the ideal gas equation for the mixture:

$P V = n R T$

$T = \dfrac{P V}{n R}$

Substituting the values of $n_1$ and $n_2$:

$T = \dfrac{P V}{\left( \dfrac{P_1 V_1}{R T_1} + \dfrac{P_2 V_2}{R T_2} \right) R}$

$T = \dfrac{P V}{\dfrac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} + \dfrac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}}$

$T = \dfrac{T_1 T_2 P V}{P_1 V_1 T_2 + P_2 V_2 T_1}$

Answer: $T_1 T_2 PV/(T_2 P_1 V_1 + T_1 P_2 V_2)$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A mixture of carbon dioxide and oxygen has volume $8310$ cm$^3$, temperature $300$ K, pressure $100$ kPa and mass $13.2$ g. The number of moles of carbon dioxide and oxygen gases in the mixture respectively are _______.
(Assume both carbon dioxide and oxygen gases behave like ideal gases) $[R = 8.31$ J/mol.K$]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.15$ and $0.18$
- **(B)** $0.25$ and $0.08$
- **(C)** $0.21$ and $0.12$
- **(D)** $0.13$ and $0.20$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $0.21$ and $0.12$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the ideal gas equation, the total number of moles of the mixture is given by:

$n = \dfrac{PV}{RT}$

Substituting the given values:
$P = 100 \text{ kPa} = 10^5 \text{ Pa}$
$V = 8310 \text{ cm}^3 = 8.31 \times 10^{-3} \text{ m}^3$
$T = 300 \text{ K}$
$R = 8.31 \text{ J/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$

$n = \dfrac{10^5 \times 8.31 \times 10^{-3}}{8.31 \times 300} = \dfrac{100}{300} = \dfrac{1}{3} \text{ mol}$

Let $n_1$ and $n_2$ be the number of moles of $CO_2$ and $O_2$ respectively.

$n_1 + n_2 = \dfrac{1}{3}$

The total mass of the mixture is $13.2 \text{ g}$. Using the molar masses of $CO_2$ ($44 \text{ g/mol}$) and $O_2$ ($32 \text{ g/mol}$):

$44n_1 + 32n_2 = 13.2$

Substituting $n_2 = \dfrac{1}{3} - n_1$ into the mass equation:

$44n_1 + 32\left(\dfrac{1}{3} - n_1\right) = 13.2$

$12n_1 + \dfrac{32}{3} = 13.2$

$12n_1 = \dfrac{39.6 - 32}{3} = \dfrac{7.6}{3}$

$n_1 = \dfrac{7.6}{36} = \dfrac{19}{90} \approx 0.21 \text{ mol}$

The number of moles of $O_2$ is:

$n_2 = \dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{19}{90} = \dfrac{11}{90} \approx 0.12 \text{ mol}$

The number of moles of $CO_2$ and $O_2$ are $0.21$ and $0.12$ respectively.

Answer: $0.21$ and $0.12$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The mean free path of a molecule of diameter $5 \times 10^{-10} \mathrm{~m}$ at the temperature $41^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ and pressure $1.38 \times 10^{5} \mathrm{~Pa}$, is given as $\_\_\_\_$ m.
(Given $k_{B}=1.38 \times 10^{-23} \mathrm{~J} / \mathrm{K}$).

**Options:**

- **(A)** $10 \sqrt{2} \times 10^{-8}$
- **(B)** $2 \sqrt{2} \times 10^{-8}$
- **(C)** $2 \sqrt{2} \times 10^{-10}$
- **(D)** $2 \times 10^{-8}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2 \sqrt{2} \times 10^{-8}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mean free path is calculated using $\lambda = \frac{k_B T}{\sqrt{2} \pi d^2 P}$
Given: $d = 5 \times 10^{-10}$ m, $T = 41°C = 314$ K, $P = 1.38 \times 10^5$ Pa, $k_B = 1.38 \times 10^{-23}$ J/K
$\lambda = \frac{1.38 \times 10^{-23} \times 314}{\sqrt{2} \times \pi \times (5 \times 10^{-10})^2 \times 1.38 \times 10^5}$
$= \frac{1.38 \times 10^{-23} \times 314}{\sqrt{2} \times \pi \times 25 \times 10^{-20} \times 1.38 \times 10^5}$
Numerator: $433.32 \times 10^{-23}$
Denominator: $1.414 \times 3.14159 \times 34.5 \times 10^{-15} = 153.18 \times 10^{-15}$
$\lambda = \frac{433.32 \times 10^{-23}}{153.18 \times 10^{-15}} = 2.83 \times 10^{-8}$ m = $2\sqrt{2} \times 10^{-8}$ m

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

A gas of certain mass filled in a closed cylinder at a pressure of 3.23 kPa has temperature $50^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. The gas is now heated to double its temperature. The modified pressure is $\_\_\_\_$ Pa. Note: Volume is constant.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **3730**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since its given that volume is constant, $P \propto T$.

$T_i = 50°C = 323$ K
$T_f = 2 \times 50 = 100°C = 373$ K

$\frac{P_f}{P_i} = \frac{T_f}{T_i}$

$\frac{P_f}{3.23 \text{ kPa}} = \frac{373}{323}$

$P_f = 3.23 \times \frac{373}{323} = 3.73$ kPa $= 3730$ Pa

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Consider two boxes containing ideal gases $A$ and $B$ such that their temperatures, pressures and number densities are same. The molecular size of $A$ is half of that of $B$ and mass of molecule $A$ is four times that of $B$. If the collision frequency in gas $B$ is $32 \times 10^{18} / \mathrm{s}$ then collision frequency in gas $A$ is $\_\_\_\_$ $/ \mathrm{s}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \times 10^{8}$
- **(B)** $8 \times 10^{8}$
- **(C)** $4 \times 10^{8}$
- **(D)** $32 \times 10^{8}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4 \times 10^{8}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Collision frequency $(z) = \sqrt{2} \pi d^2 N \sqrt{\dfrac{8RT}{\pi M}}$
Temp, N are same
$Z \propto \dfrac{d^2}{\sqrt{M}}$
$d_A = \dfrac{d_B}{2}$
$M_A = 4M_B$
$\dfrac{Z_A}{Z_B} = \dfrac{d_A^2}{d_B^2} \times \dfrac{\sqrt{M_B}}{\sqrt{M_A}} = \left(\sqrt{\dfrac{M_B}{M_A}}\right) \left(\dfrac{d_A}{d_B}\right)^2$
$= \left(\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{4}}\right) \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2$
$\dfrac{Z_A}{Z_B} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{8}$
$\Rightarrow Z_A = \dfrac{32 \times 10^8}{8} = 4 \times 10^8 /s$

</details>

### Q17. JEE Main 2013 (23 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

This question has Statement-1 and Statement-2. Of the four choices given after the Statements, choose the one that best describes the two Statements.
Statement 1: The internal energy of a perfect gas is entirely kinetic and depends only on absolute temperature of the gas and not on its pressure or volume.
Statement 2: A perfect gas is heated keeping pressure constant and later at constant volume. For the same amount of heat the temperature of the gas at constant pressure is lower than that at constant volume.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement-1 is true, Statement- 2 is true and Statement $-2$ is the correct explanation of Statement-1.
- **(B)** Statement-1 is true, Statement-2 is false.
- **(C)** Statement-1 is true, Statement-2 is true but Statement-2 is not the correct explanation of Statement-1.
- **(D)** Statement-1 is false, Statement-2 is true.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement-1 is true, Statement-2 is true but Statement-2 is not the correct explanation of Statement-1.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Internal energy of a system is the energy possessed by the system due to molecular motion and molecular configuration. Internal energy $u=u_k+u_p$ For an ideal gas there is no molecular attraction $\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{p}}=0$
$\therefore$ Internal energy $\mathrm{u}=\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{k}}$ (i.e., internal kinetic energy)
and $\mathrm{u}=\mathrm{u}_{\mathrm{k}}=\frac{3}{2} \mu \mathrm{RT}$
At constant pressure $(\Delta \mathrm{Q})_{\mathrm{P}}=\mu \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{p}} \Delta \mathrm{T}$
At constant volume $(\Delta \mathrm{Q})_{\mathrm{v}}=\mu \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{v}} \Delta \mathrm{T}$
