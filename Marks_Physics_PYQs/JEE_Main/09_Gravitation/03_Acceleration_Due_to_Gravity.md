### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

When one moves from a point $16$ km below the earth's surface to a point $16$ km above the earth's surface. The change in $g$ is approximately $\alpha$ %. The value of $\alpha$ is _____.
(Take radius of the earth $= 6400$ km.)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.12$
- **(B)** $0.25$
- **(C)** $0.50$
- **(D)** $0.75$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $0.25$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The acceleration due to gravity at a depth $d$ below the earth's surface is given by:

$g_d = g\left(1 - \dfrac{d}{R}\right)$

The acceleration due to gravity at a height $h$ above the earth's surface (for $h \ll R$) is given by:

$g_h = g\left(1 - \dfrac{2h}{R}\right)$

Given $d = 16$ km and $h = 16$ km, we can find the values of $g_d$ and $g_h$:

$g_d = g\left(1 - \dfrac{16}{6400}\right) = g\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{400}\right)$

$g_h = g\left(1 - \dfrac{2 \times 16}{6400}\right) = g\left(1 - \dfrac{2}{400}\right)$

The magnitude of the change in acceleration due to gravity when moving from the depth $d$ to the height $h$ is:

$\Delta g = g_d - g_h = g\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{400}\right) - g\left(1 - \dfrac{2}{400}\right)$

$\Delta g = g\left(\dfrac{2}{400} - \dfrac{1}{400}\right) = \dfrac{g}{400}$

The percentage change in $g$ is approximately:

$\dfrac{\Delta g}{g} \times 100 = \dfrac{\dfrac{g}{400}}{g} \times 100 = \dfrac{100}{400} = 0.25\%$

Thus, the value of $\alpha$ is $0.25$.

Answer: $0.25$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The height in terms of radius of the earth $(R)$, at which the acceleration due to gravity becomes $\dfrac{g}{9}$, where $g$ is acceleration due to gravity on earth's surface, is ______.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{3}R$
- **(B)** $2\sqrt{2}R$
- **(C)** $2R$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{4}{9}R$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2R$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The acceleration due to gravity at a height $h$ above the surface of the earth is given by

$g' = \dfrac{g}{\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2}$

Given that $g' = \dfrac{g}{9}$, we get

$\dfrac{g}{9} = \dfrac{g}{\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2}$

$\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2 = 9$

$1 + \dfrac{h}{R} = 3$

$\dfrac{h}{R} = 2$

$h = 2R$

Answer: $2R$

</details>

Assuming the earth to be a sphere of uniform mass density, a body weighed $300 \mathrm{~N}$ on the surface of earth. How much it would weigh at R/4 depth under surface of earth ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $75 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(B)** $300 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(C)** $375 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $225 \mathrm{~N}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $225 \mathrm{~N}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

weight at depth $\quad=\mathrm{m} \times \mathrm{g}_{\mathrm{d}}$
$\begin{aligned} & =\mathrm{m} \times \frac{3 \mathrm{~g}_{\mathrm{s}}}{4} \\ & =\frac{3}{4} \times 300 \\ & =225 \mathrm{~N}\end{aligned}$

### Q50. JEE Main 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The height at which the acceleration due to gravity becomes $\frac{\mathrm{g}}{9}$ (where $\mathrm{g}=$ the acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the earth) in terms of $R$, the radius of the earth is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 R$
- **(B)** $\frac{R}{\sqrt{2}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{R}{2}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{2} \mathrm{R}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $2 R$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$g^{\prime}=\frac{G M}{(R+h)^2}$, acceleration due to gravity at height $h$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \frac{g}{9}=\frac{G M}{R^2} \cdot \frac{R^2}{(R+h)^2}=g\left(\frac{R}{R+h}\right)^2 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{1}{9}=\left(\frac{R}{R+h}\right)^2 \Rightarrow \frac{R}{R+h}=\frac{1}{3} \\
& \Rightarrow 3 R=R+h \Rightarrow 2 R=h
\end{aligned}
$$
