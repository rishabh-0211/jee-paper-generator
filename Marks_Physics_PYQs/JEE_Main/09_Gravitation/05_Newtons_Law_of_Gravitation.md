### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Net gravitational force at the center of a square is found to be $F_{1}$ when four particles having mass $M, 2 M, 3 M$ and $4 M$ are placed at the four corners of the square as shown in figure and it is $F_{2}$ when the positions of $3 M$ and $4 M$ are interchanged. The ratio $\frac{F_{1}}{F_{2}}$ is $\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{5}}$. The value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$.
<img style="width:200px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_q_34_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \sqrt{5}$
- **(B)** 3
- **(C)** 1
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $k = \frac{G}{r^2\sqrt{2}}$ where $r = a/\sqrt{2}$.
Net field at center has components along $x$ and $y$.
Config 1 (original):
$g_x = k(-M+2M+3M-4M) = 0$,
$g_y = k(-M-2M+3M+4M) = 4kM$.
$F_1 = 4kM$.
Config 2 (3M and 4M swapped):
$g_x = k(-M+2M+4M-3M) = 2kM$,
$g_y = k(-M-2M+4M+3M) = 4kM$.
$F_2 = kM\sqrt{4+16} = 2\sqrt{5}\,kM$.
$\frac{F_1}{F_2} = \frac{4}{2\sqrt{5}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} \Rightarrow \alpha = 2$.

</details>
