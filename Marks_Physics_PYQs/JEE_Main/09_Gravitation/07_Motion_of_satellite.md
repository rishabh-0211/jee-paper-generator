### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Given below are two statements: 
 <b>Statement I</b>: A satellite is moving around earth in the orbit very close to the earth surface. The time period of revolution of satellite depends upon the density of earth. 
 <b>Statement II</b>: The time period of revolution of the satellite is $T=2 \pi \sqrt{\frac{R_{e}}{g}}$ (for satellite very close to the earth surface), where $R_{\mathrm{e}}$ radius of earth and $g$ acceleration due to gravity. 
 In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true
- **(B)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true
- **(C)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(D)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Both Statement I and Statement II are true**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement I: For a satellite very close to Earth's surface, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R_e}{g}}$ where $g = \frac{GM}{R_e^2} = \frac{4\pi G \rho R_e}{3}$.
Substituting: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{3}{4\pi G \rho}}$.
This shows period depends on Earth's density $\rho$. Statement I is TRUE.
Statement II: Given directly as $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R_e}{g}}$, which is the standard formula.
Statement II is TRUE.
Both statements are true.

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Initially a satellite of 100 kg is in a circular orbit of radius $1.5 \mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{E}}$. This satellite can be moved to a circular orbit of radius $3 R_{E}$ by supplying $\alpha \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~J}$ of energy. The value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$. (Take Radius of Earth $R_{E}=6 \times 10^{6} \mathrm{~m}$ and $\mathrm{g}=10 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^{2}$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 150
- **(B)** 1000
- **(C)** 500
- **(D)** 100

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1000**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total energy of satellite in orbit: $E = -\frac{GMm}{2r} = -\frac{gR_E^2 m}{2r}$
$E_1 = -\frac{gR_E^2 m}{2(1.5R_E)} = -\frac{gR_E m}{3}$
$E_2 = -\frac{gR_E^2 m}{2(3R_E)} = -\frac{gR_E m}{6}$
Energy supplied $= E_2 - E_1 = -\frac{gR_E m}{6} + \frac{gR_E m}{3} = \frac{gR_E m}{6}$
$= \frac{10 \times 6 \times 10^6 \times 100}{6} = 10^9$ J $= 1000 \times 10^6$ J
$\alpha = 1000$

</details>

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/GegVCRd2ndZdMP0Llu5HR5VMH0yO8-KWj4a3KrgCaqI.original.fullsize.png">

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{v}=(\mathrm{R}+\mathrm{h}) \frac{2 \pi}{\mathrm{T}}$ ....(2)

### Q34. JEE Main 2003

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{T}_2=8 \times \mathrm{T}_1=8 \times 5=40=40$ hours
