### Q2. JEE Main 2024 (06 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The acceptor level of a p-type semiconductor is $6 \mathrm{eV}$. The maximum wavelength of light which can create a hole would be : Given $\mathrm{hc}=1242 \mathrm{eV} \mathrm{nm}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $414 \mathrm{~nm}$
- **(B)** $103.5 \mathrm{~nm}$
- **(C)** $207 \mathrm{~nm}$
- **(D)** $407 \mathrm{~nm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $207 \mathrm{~nm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \text { Energy }=\frac{\mathrm{hc}}{\lambda} \\ & \mathrm{E}=\frac{1240}{\lambda(\mathrm{nm})} \mathrm{eV} \\ & 6=\frac{1240}{\lambda(\mathrm{nm})} \\ & \lambda=\frac{1240}{6}=207 \mathrm{~nm}\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2023 (31 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The effect of increase in temperature on the number of electrons in conduction band <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mo>(</mo><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub><mo>)</mo></math> and resistance of a semiconductor will be as:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> and resistance decrease
- **(B)** Both <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> and resistance increase
- **(C)** <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> increases, resistance decreases
- **(D)** <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> decreases, resistance increases

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> increases, resistance decreases**

**Detailed Solution:**

In case of semiconductors as the temperature increases the electrons in the valence band get excited and jump into the conduction band and hence the conductance increases resulting in the decrease of resistance. Thus, as temperature increases, <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi></msub></math> increases and resistance decreases.
