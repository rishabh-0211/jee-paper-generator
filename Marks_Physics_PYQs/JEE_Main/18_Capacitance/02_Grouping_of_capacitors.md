### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A sphere of capacitance $100$ pF is charged to a potential of $100$ V. Another identical uncharged metal sphere is brought in contact with the charged sphere, then the change in the total energy stored on these spheres, when they touch is $\alpha \times 10^{-7}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (combined capacitance of spheres is $200$ pF)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{9}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\dfrac{5}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Initial energy of the charged sphere is given by:

$U_i = \dfrac{1}{2} C V^2$

$U_i = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 100 \times 10^{-12} \times (100)^2 = 5 \times 10^{-7}$ J

When the identical uncharged sphere is brought in contact, the charge is shared equally and the common potential becomes:

$V' = \dfrac{V}{2} = 50$ V

Final total energy of the two spheres is:

$U_f = 2 \times \left( \dfrac{1}{2} C V'^2 \right) = C V'^2$

$U_f = 100 \times 10^{-12} \times (50)^2 = 2.5 \times 10^{-7}$ J

Change in energy is the loss of energy during charge sharing:

$\Delta U = U_i - U_f = 5 \times 10^{-7} - 2.5 \times 10^{-7} = 2.5 \times 10^{-7}$ J

$\Delta U = \dfrac{5}{2} \times 10^{-7}$ J

Comparing with $\alpha \times 10^{-7}$ J, we get $\alpha = \dfrac{5}{2}$.

Answer: $\dfrac{5}{2}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

From the circuit given below, the capacitance between terminals $A$ and $B$ shown in the circuit is ______ $\mu$F.
(take $C_1=C_2=C_3=1$ $\mu$F and $C_4=2$ $\mu$F.)
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/04APRS2__q37_d1.jpg" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2$
- **(B)** $7/2$
- **(C)** $7/3$
- **(D)** $5/2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the nodes in the circuit be labeled to identify the connections:
- Let the input terminal be Node A.
- Let the junction between $C_1$ and $C_2$ be Node X.
- The top wire connects Node A to the junction between $C_2$ and $C_3$. Thus, this junction is also at the potential of Node A.
- The bottom wire connects Node X to the output terminal Node B, with capacitor $C_4$ in this path.

Based on these nodes, the connections of each capacitor are as follows:
- $C_1$ is connected between Node A and Node X.
- $C_2$ is connected between Node X and Node A.
- $C_3$ is connected between Node A and Node B.
- $C_4$ is connected between Node X and Node B.

We can now simplify the circuit step-by-step:
Capacitors $C_1$ and $C_2$ are in parallel between nodes A and X. Their equivalent capacitance is:
$C_{12} = C_1 + C_2 = 1\,\mu\text{F} + 1\,\mu\text{F} = 2\,\mu\text{F}$

This equivalent capacitor $C_{12}$ is in series with $C_4$ (which is connected between X and B). The equivalent capacitance of this branch is:
$C_{AXB} = \dfrac{C_{12} \times C_4}{C_{12} + C_4} = \dfrac{2 \times 2}{2 + 2} = 1\,\mu\text{F}$

Finally, this branch is in parallel with $C_3$ (which is connected directly between A and B). The total equivalent capacitance between A and B is:
$C_{AB} = C_{AXB} + C_3 = 1\,\mu\text{F} + 1\,\mu\text{F} = 2\,\mu\text{F}$

Answer: $2$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A capacitor $P$ with capacitance $10 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~F}$ is fully charged with a potential difference of 6.0 V and disconnected from the battery. The charged capacitor $P$ is connected across another capacitor $Q$ with capacitance $20 \times 10^{-6} \mathrm{~F}$. The charge on capacitor $Q$ when equilibrium is established will be $\alpha \times 10^{-5} C$ (assume capacitor $Q$ does not have any charge initially), the value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$。

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **4**

**Detailed Solution:**

Capacitor P (10 × 10⁻⁶ F) is charged to 6.0 V.
Initial charge: $Q_P = 10 \times 10^{-6} \times 6 = 60 \times 10^{-6}$ C.
When connected to uncharged capacitor Q (20 × 10⁻⁶ F), charge redistributes until both reach same potential.
Final voltage: $V_f = \frac{Q_{total}}{C_{total}} = \frac{60 \times 10^{-6}}{30 \times 10^{-6}} = 2$ V.
Charge on Q: $Q_Q = 20 \times 10^{-6} \times 2 = 40 \times 10^{-6}$ C = $4 \times 10^{-5}$ C.
Thus $\alpha = 4$.

</details>

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{4 \varepsilon_1 \varepsilon_2}{\left(\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2\right)^2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/bmXH4QbDrI-aItIUp4anwIg-DNXHTwAKa9iB4zJsCFM.original.fullsize.png"/>

$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{C}=\frac{\varepsilon_2 \varepsilon_0 \mathrm{~A}}{\mathrm{~d} / 2}=\frac{2 \varepsilon_2 \varepsilon_0 \mathrm{~A}}{\mathrm{~d}} \\

$\mathrm{C}_1=\frac{\mathrm{CC}^{\prime}}{\mathrm{C}+\mathrm{C}^{\prime}}=\frac{4 \varepsilon_2 \varepsilon_1 \mathrm{C}_0^2}{2 \mathrm{C}_0\left(\varepsilon_2+\varepsilon_1\right)}$
