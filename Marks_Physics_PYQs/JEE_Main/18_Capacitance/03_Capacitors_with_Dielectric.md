### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel plate capacitor is having separation between plates $0.885$ mm. It has a capacitance of $1\,\mu$F when the space between the plates is filled with an insulating material of resistivity $1\times 10^{13}\,\Omega$m and resistance $17.7\times 10^{14}\,\Omega$. Relative permittivity of the insulating material is $\alpha\times 10^7$. The value of $\alpha$ is ________. (Take permittivity of free space $=8.85\times 10^{-12}$ F/m)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **2**

**Detailed Solution:**

The resistance of the insulating material is given by $R = \dfrac{\rho d}{A}$.

The capacitance of the parallel plate capacitor is given by $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 \epsilon_r A}{d}$.

Multiplying the expressions for $R$ and $C$, we get:

$RC = \rho \epsilon_0 \epsilon_r$

Rearranging for relative permittivity $\epsilon_r$:

$\epsilon_r = \dfrac{RC}{\rho \epsilon_0}$

Substituting the given values:

$\epsilon_r = \dfrac{17.7 \times 10^{14} \times 10^{-6}}{10^{13} \times 8.85 \times 10^{-12}}$

$\epsilon_r = \dfrac{17.7 \times 10^8}{8.85 \times 10^1}$

$\epsilon_r = 2 \times 10^7$

Comparing this with the given expression $\epsilon_r = \alpha \times 10^7$, we get:

$\alpha = 2$

Answer: $2$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel plate air capacitor has a capacitance $C$. When it is half filled as show in figure with a dielectric constant $K = 5$, the percentage increase in the capacitance is _______.
<img style="width:400px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/02APRS1__q32_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $33.34$
- **(B)** $66.67$
- **(C)** $200$
- **(D)** $400$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $66.67$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Initial capacitance of the air capacitor is given by:

$C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$

When the capacitor is half-filled with a dielectric of thickness $d/2$, it can be considered as two capacitors connected in series.

Capacitance of the dielectric-filled part:

$C_1 = \dfrac{K \epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = \dfrac{2K \epsilon_0 A}{d} = 2KC$

Capacitance of the air-filled part:

$C_2 = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = \dfrac{2 \epsilon_0 A}{d} = 2C$

The equivalent capacitance $C_{eq}$ of the series combination is:

$C_{eq} = \dfrac{C_1 C_2}{C_1 + C_2} = \dfrac{(2KC)(2C)}{2KC + 2C} = \dfrac{2K}{K+1} C$

Substituting $K = 5$:

$C_{eq} = \dfrac{2(5)}{5+1} C = \dfrac{10}{6} C = \dfrac{5}{3} C$

The increase in capacitance is:

$\Delta C = C_{eq} - C = \dfrac{5}{3} C - C = \dfrac{2}{3} C$

The percentage increase in capacitance is:

$\% \text{ increase} = \dfrac{\Delta C}{C} \times 100 = \dfrac{2}{3} \times 100 \approx 66.67\%$

Answer: $66.67$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel plate capacitor with plate separation 5 mm is charged by a battery. On introducing a mica sheet of 2 mm and maintaining the connections of the plates with the terminals of the battery, it is found that it draws $25 \%$ more charge from the battery. The dielectric constant of mica is $\_\_\_\_$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1.0
- **(B)** 2.5
- **(C)** 2.0
- **(D)** 1.5

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 2.0**

**Detailed Solution:**

Initial capacitor has plate separation 5 mm with capacitance $C_1 = \epsilon_0 A / 5$.
After inserting 2 mm mica sheet, the gap remaining is 3 mm of air. The effective separation becomes: $d_{eff} = 3 + 2/k$ where $k$ is the dielectric constant of mica.
New capacitance: $C_2 = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{3 + 2/k}$
Since battery remains connected, voltage is constant. The charge is: $Q = CV$
Ratio of charges: $\frac{Q_2}{Q_1} = \frac{C_2}{C_1} = \frac{\epsilon_0 A/(3 + 2/k)}{\epsilon_0 A/5} = \frac{5}{3 + 2/k}$
Given that charge increases by 25%: $Q_2 = 1.25 Q_1$
$\frac{5}{3 + 2/k} = 1.25$
$5 = 1.25(3 + 2/k)$
$5 = 3.75 + 2.5/k$
$1.25 = 2.5/k$
$k = 2$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

The space between the plates of a parallel plate capacitor of capacitance C (without any dielectric) is now filled with three dielectric slabs of dielectric constants $K_{1}=2, K_{2}=3$ and $K_{3}=5$ (as shown in figure). If new capacitance is $\frac{n}{3} C$ then the value of $n$ is $\_\_\_\_$. 
 <img style="width:300px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_q_48_1.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **8**

**Detailed Solution:**

The initial capacitance is $C = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d}$.
From the figure, the capacitor is divided into three parts.
The bottom half is divided into two capacitors $C_2$ and $C_3$ in parallel, each with area $A/2$ and thickness $d/2$.
$C_2 = \frac{K_2 \epsilon_0 (A/2)}{d/2} = K_2 \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d} = 3C$
$C_3 = \frac{K_3 \epsilon_0 (A/2)}{d/2} = K_3 \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d} = 5C$
The equivalent capacitance of the bottom part is $C_{eq, bottom} = C_2 + C_3 = 3C + 5C = 8C$.
The top half is a single capacitor $C_1$ with area $A$ and thickness $d/2$.
$C_1 = \frac{K_1 \epsilon_0 A}{d/2} = 2K_1 \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{d} = 2(2)C = 4C$.
The total capacitance $C'$ is the series combination of $C_1$ and $C_{eq, bottom}$.
$\frac{1}{C'} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_{eq, bottom}} = \frac{1}{4C} + \frac{1}{8C} = \frac{2+1}{8C} = \frac{3}{8C}$
$C' = \frac{8}{3}C$
Comparing with $C' = \frac{n}{3}C$, we get $n = 8$.

</details>

### Q6. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel plate capacitor has capacitance $C$, when there is vacuum within the parallel plates. A sheet having thickness $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{\mathrm{rd}}$ of the separation between the plates and relative permittivity $K$ is introduced between the plates. The new capacitance of the system is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{4 K C}{3 K-1}$
- **(B)** $\frac{3 C K^{2}}{(2 K+1)^{2}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3 K C}{2 K+1}$
- **(D)** $\frac{C K}{2+K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{3 K C}{2 K+1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Original capacitance: $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$
After inserting dielectric of thickness $\frac{d}{3}$, system becomes two capacitors in series.
Vacuum region: $C_1 = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{2d/3} = \frac{3C}{2}$
Dielectric region: $C_2 = \frac{K\varepsilon_0 A}{d/3} = 3KC$
Equivalent capacitance: $\frac{1}{C_{new}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2} = \frac{2}{3C} + \frac{1}{3KC} = \frac{2K+1}{3KC}$
$C_{new} = \frac{3KC}{2K+1}$

</details>

### Q12. JEE Main 2024 (04 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

A parallel plate capacitor of capacitance $12.5 \mathrm{pF}$ is charged by a battery connected between its plates to potential difference of $12.0 \mathrm{~V}$. The battery is now disconnected and a dielectric slab $\left(\epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}=6\right)$ is inserted between the plates. The change in its potential energy after inserting the dielectric slab is _____ $10^{-12} \mathrm{~J}$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **750**

**Detailed Solution:**

Before inserting dielectric capacitance is given $\mathrm{C}_0=12.5 \mathrm{pF}$ and charge on the capacitor $\mathrm{Q}=\mathrm{C}_0 \mathrm{~V}$ After inserting dielectric capacitance will become $\epsilon_{\mathrm{r}} \mathrm{C}_0$.
Change in potential energy of the capacitor
$\begin{aligned}
& =\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{i}}-\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{f}} \\
& =\frac{\mathrm{Q}^2}{2 \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{i}}}-\frac{\mathrm{Q}^2}{2 \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{f}}}=\frac{\mathrm{Q}^2}{2 \mathrm{C}_0}\left[1-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}}\right] \\
& =\frac{\left(\mathrm{C}_0 \mathrm{~V}\right)^2}{2 \mathrm{C}_0}\left[1-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}}\right]=\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{C}_0 \mathrm{~V}^2\left[1-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}}\right]
\end{aligned}$
Using $\mathrm{C}_0=12.5 \mathrm{pF}, \mathrm{V}=12 \mathrm{~V}, \epsilon_{\mathrm{r}}=6$
$\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{1}{2}(12.5) \times 12^2\left[1-\frac{1}{6}\right]=\frac{1}{2}(12.5) \times 12^2 \times \frac{5}{6} \\
& =750 \mathrm{pJ}=750 \times 10^{-12} \mathrm{~J}
\end{aligned}$
