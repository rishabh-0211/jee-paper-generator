### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A parallel plate air capacitor is connected to a battery. The plates are pulled apart at uniform speed $v$. If $x$ is the separation between the plates at any instant, then the time rate of change of electrostatic energy of the capacitor is proportional to $x^\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is _____.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-2$
- **(B)** $1$
- **(C)** $-1$
- **(D)** $2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $-2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The electrostatic energy of a parallel plate capacitor is given by $U = \dfrac{1}{2} C V^2$.

For a parallel plate capacitor, the capacitance is $C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A}{x}$, where $x$ is the separation between the plates.

Since the capacitor remains connected to the battery, the potential difference $V$ across the plates is constant.

Substituting $C$ into the energy equation gives:

$U = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A V^2}{2x}$

Differentiating $U$ with respect to time $t$ to find the time rate of change of electrostatic energy:

$\dfrac{dU}{dt} = \dfrac{d}{dt} \left( \dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A V^2}{2x} \right) = -\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A V^2}{2x^2} \dfrac{dx}{dt}$

Given that the plates are pulled apart at a uniform speed $v$, we have $\dfrac{dx}{dt} = v$.

$\dfrac{dU}{dt} = -\dfrac{\varepsilon_0 A V^2 v}{2x^2}$

From the above expression, it is clear that $\dfrac{dU}{dt} \propto x^{-2}$.

Comparing this with $x^\alpha$, we get $\alpha = -2$.

Answer: $-2$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the correct statements : 
 A. Effective capacitance of a series combination of capacitors is always smaller than the smallest capacitance of the capacitor in the combination. 
 B. When a dielectric medium is placed between the charged plates of a capacitor, displacement of charges cannot occur due to insulation property of dielectric. 
 C. Increasing of area of capacitor plate or decreasing of thickness of dielectric is an alternate method to increase the capacitance. 
 D. For a point charge, concentric spherical shells centered at the location of the charge are equipotential surfaces. 
 Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** B and D Only
- **(B)** A, B and C Only
- **(C)** C and D Only
- **(D)** A, C and D Only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) A, C and D Only**

**Detailed Solution:**

A. Effective capacitance of series combination is always smaller than the smallest capacitance - TRUE. For series: $\frac{1}{C_{eff}} = \frac{1}{C_1} + \frac{1}{C_2} + \ldots$, therefore $C_{eff} < C_{min}$
B. Dielectric prevents charge displacement - FALSE. A dielectric allows polarization (displacement of bound charges) but prevents free charge movement. It does not prevent displacement of bound charges.
C. Increasing area or decreasing thickness increases capacitance - TRUE. From $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r A}{d}$, increasing $A$ or decreasing $d$ increases $C$.
D. Concentric spherical shells centered at a point charge location are equipotential surfaces - TRUE. For a point charge, the potential at distance $r$ is $V = \frac{kq}{r}$, which depends only on distance, making concentric spheres equipotential surfaces.
Correct statements: A, C, D

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2025 (28 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two capacitors $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected in parallel to a battery. Charge-time graph is shown below for the two capacitors. The energy stored with them are $U_1$ and $U_2$, respectively. Which of the given statements is true?
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/D32wjMmBXT43HqZNd6PJ9mZ_Vn9-qWtVO-NRgBTtrVo.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{C}_2\gt\mathrm{C}_1, \mathrm{U}_2 \lt \mathrm{U}_1$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{C}_1\gt\mathrm{C}_2, \mathrm{U}_1\gt\mathrm{U}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}_1\gt\mathrm{C}_2, \mathrm{U}_1 \lt \mathrm{U}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{C}_2\gt\mathrm{C}_1, \mathrm{U}_2\gt\mathrm{U}_1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{C}_2\gt\mathrm{C}_1, \mathrm{U}_2\gt\mathrm{U}_1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a capacitor at steady state
$q=C V \text { and } U=\frac{1}{2} C V^2$

Since $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected in parallel, $V_1=V_2$.
Also from graph $q_1 < q_2$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad C_1 V_1 < C_2 V_2 \\
& \text { i.e. } C_1 < C_2 \text { or } C_2>C_1 \\
& \qquad \frac{U_1}{U_2}=\frac{C_1 V_1^2}{C_2 V_2^2}=\frac{C_1}{C_2} < 1
\end{aligned}$
or $U_1 < U_2$ or $U_2>U_1$

</details>

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/FF6xH_UbcWErgxqmV1AYHz-YXoM9PWysQku5xB47pgE.original.fullsize.png">

$=\frac{b \ell \varepsilon_0}{d}$
