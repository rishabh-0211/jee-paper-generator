### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For the series $L C R$ circuit connected with $220 \mathrm{~V}, 50 \mathrm{~Hz}$ a.c source as shown in the figure, the power factor is $\frac{\alpha}{10}$. The value of $\alpha$ is $\_\_\_\_$. 
 <img style="width:300px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/24S1_q_30_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** 10
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Net reactance: $X = X_C - X_L = 150 - 70 = 80Ω$. 
Impedance: $Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} = \sqrt{60^2 + 80^2} = \sqrt{10000} = 100Ω$. 
Power factor: $\cos\phi = R/Z = 60/100 = 0.6$. 
Since power factor is $\alpha/10$, we have $0.6 = \alpha/10$, so $\alpha = 6$

</details>
