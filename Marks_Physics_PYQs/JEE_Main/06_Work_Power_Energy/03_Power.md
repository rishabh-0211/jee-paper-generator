### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A body of mass $2$ kg begins to move under the influence of time dependent force $\vec{F} = (2t\hat{i} + 6t^2\hat{j})$ N, where $\hat{i}$ and $\hat{j}$ are unit vectors along $x$ and $y$-axis respectively. The power produced by the force at $t = 2$ s is _____ W.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **200**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\vec{F} = (2t\hat{i} + 6t^2\hat{j})$ N and $m = 2$ kg.

Acceleration of the body is $\vec{a} = \dfrac{\vec{F}}{m} = \dfrac{2t\hat{i} + 6t^2\hat{j}}{2} = t\hat{i} + 3t^2\hat{j}$ m/s$^2$.

Since the body begins to move from rest, its velocity at time $t$ is given by $\vec{v} = \int \vec{a} dt = \int (t\hat{i} + 3t^2\hat{j}) dt = \dfrac{t^2}{2}\hat{i} + t^3\hat{j}$ m/s.

At $t = 2$ s, the force is $\vec{F} = 2(2)\hat{i} + 6(2)^2\hat{j} = 4\hat{i} + 24\hat{j}$ N.

At $t = 2$ s, the velocity is $\vec{v} = \dfrac{(2)^2}{2}\hat{i} + (2)^3\hat{j} = 2\hat{i} + 8\hat{j}$ m/s.

Power produced by the force is $P = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v} = (4\hat{i} + 24\hat{j}) \cdot (2\hat{i} + 8\hat{j})$.

$P = (4 \times 2) + (24 \times 8) = 8 + 192 = 200$ W.

Answer: $200$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (7 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An object of mass 1000 g experiences a time dependent force $\vec{F}=\left(2 t \hat{i}+3 t^2 \hat{j}\right) N$. The power generated by the force at time $t$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(2 t^2+3 t^3\right) W$
- **(B)** $\left(2 t^2+18 t^3\right) W$
- **(C)** $\left(3 t^3+5 t^5\right) W$
- **(D)** $\left(2 t^3+3 t^5\right) W$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(2 t^3+3 t^5\right) W$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}}=(2 t \hat{\mathrm{i}}+3 \mathrm{t} \hat{\mathrm{j}}) \mathrm{N} \\ & \mathrm{m}=1000 \mathrm{gm}=1 \mathrm{~kg} \\ & \overrightarrow{\mathrm{~F}}=m \overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}}, \overrightarrow{\mathrm{a}}=2 \mathrm{t} \hat{\mathrm{i}}+3 \mathrm{t}^2 \hat{j} \\ & \frac{\mathrm{~d} \overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}}{\mathrm{dt}}=2 t \hat{\mathrm{i}}+3 \mathrm{t}^2 \hat{\mathrm{j}}\end{aligned}$
$\vec{v}=t^2 \hat{i}+t^3 \hat{j}$
Power, $\mathrm{P}=\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{v}}$
$\begin{aligned} & P=\left(2 t \hat{i}+3 t^2 \hat{j}\right) \cdot\left(t^2 \hat{i}+t^3 \hat{j}\right) \\ & P=\left(2 t^3+3 t^5\right) W\end{aligned}$

### Q3. JEE Main 2025 (29 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A sand dropper drops sand of mass $m(t)$ on a conveyer belt at a rate proportional to the square root of speed $(v)$ of the belt, i.e. $\frac{\mathrm{dm}}{\mathrm{dt}} \propto \sqrt{v}$. If P is the power delivered to run the belt at constant speed then which of the following relationship is true?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{P} \propto \sqrt{v}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{P} \propto v$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{P}^2 \propto v^5$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{P}^2 \propto v^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{P}^2 \propto v^5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power $=\vec{F} \cdot \vec{V}$
and $F=\frac{d p}{d t}=$ Rate of change of linear momentum
$F=V \cdot \frac{d m}{d t}=K_1 V^{\frac{3}{2}}, K$ is constant
Power $(P)=\left(K V^{\frac{3}{2}}\right) \cdot(V)$
$=K V^{\frac{5}{2}}$

So, $P^2 \propto V^5$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2024 (05 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A body is moving unidirectionally under the influence of a constant power source. Its displacement in time $\mathrm{t}$ is proportional to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t$
- **(B)** $t^{3 / 2}$
- **(C)** $t^2$
- **(D)** $t^{2 / 3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $t^{3 / 2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & P=\operatorname{constant} \Rightarrow \mathrm{FV}=\text { constant } \\ & \Rightarrow \mathrm{m} \frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{dt}} \mathrm{V}=\text { constant } \\ & \int_0^{\mathrm{V}} \mathrm{VdV}=(\mathrm{C}) \int_0^{\mathrm{t}} \mathrm{dt} \\ & \left(\frac{\mathrm{V}^2}{2}\right)=\mathrm{Ct} \\ & \mathrm{V} \propto \mathrm{t}^{1 / 2} \\ & \frac{\mathrm{ds}}{\mathrm{dt}} \propto \mathrm{t}^{1 / 2} \\ & \int_0^{\mathrm{S}} \mathrm{ds}=\mathrm{K} \int_0^{\mathrm{t}} \mathrm{t}^{1 / 2} \mathrm{dt} \\ & \mathrm{S}=\mathrm{K} \times \frac{2}{3} \mathrm{t}^{3 / 2} \\ & \mathrm{~S} \propto \mathrm{t}^{3 / 2}\end{aligned}$
$\therefore$ displacement is proportional to $(\mathrm{t})^{3 / 2}$

</details>

A body of mass $m$ is accelerated uniformly from rest to a speed $v$ in a time $T$. The instantaneous power delivered to the body as a function time is given by

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{mv}^2}{\mathrm{~T}^2} \cdot \mathrm{t}$
- **(B)** $\frac{m v^2}{T^2} \cdot t^2$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{\mathrm{mv}^2}{\mathrm{~T}^2} \cdot \mathrm{t}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2} \frac{\mathrm{mv}^2}{\mathrm{~T}^2} \cdot \mathrm{t}^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{\mathrm{mv}^2}{\mathrm{~T}^2} \cdot \mathrm{t}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{P}=(\mathrm{ma}) \cdot \mathrm{v}$
$=m a^2 t$
$=m \frac{V^2}{T^2} t$

</details>

### Q24. JEE Main 2004

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A body of mass $m$, accelerates uniformly from rest to $v_1$ in time $t_1$. The instantaneous power delivered to the body as a function of time $t$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{mv}_1 \mathrm{t}}{\mathrm{t}_1}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{mv}_1^2 \mathrm{t}}{\mathrm{t}_1^2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{m v_1 t^2}{t_1}$
- **(D)** $\frac{m v_1^2 t}{t_1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{\mathrm{mv}_1^2 \mathrm{t}}{\mathrm{t}_1^2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power $P=\vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}=\operatorname{mav}=m\left(\frac{v_1}{t_1}\right)\left(\frac{v_1}{t_1} t\right)=\frac{m_1^2 t}{t_1^2}$

</details>

### Q25. JEE Main 2003

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A body is moved along a straight line by a machine delivering a constant power. The distance moved by the body in time ' $t$ ' is proportional to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t^{3 / 4}$
- **(B)** $t^{3 / 2}$
- **(C)** $t^{1 / 4}$
- **(D)** $t^{1 / 2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $t^{3 / 2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Power $=$ F. V
$\mathrm{F}=\mathrm{m}\left(\frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{dt}}\right) \Rightarrow \mathrm{m} \cdot \mathrm{v} \cdot \frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{dt}}=$ constant $=\mathrm{C}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dV}}{\mathrm{dt}}=\frac{\mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{m}}=\mathrm{k} \Rightarrow \mathrm{vdv}=\mathrm{kdt} \quad \Rightarrow \int \mathrm{vdv}=\int \mathrm{kdt} \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{V}^2}{2}=\mathrm{kt}+\mathrm{c}$
$\Rightarrow \mathrm{v} \propto(\mathrm{t})^{1 / 2}$        $\frac{\mathrm{ds}}{\mathrm{dt}}=\mathrm{c} \cdot \mathrm{t}^{1 / 2}$
$\Rightarrow \int \mathrm{ds}=\int\left(\mathrm{c} \cdot \mathrm{t}^{1 / 2}\right) \mathrm{dt} \Rightarrow \mathrm{S}=\mathrm{C} \cdot \frac{2}{3} \mathrm{t}^{3 / 2}$     $\Rightarrow \mathrm{S}=\frac{\mathrm{ct}^{3 / 2}}{3 / 2} \Rightarrow \mathrm{s} \propto \mathrm{t}^{3 / 2}$

</details>
