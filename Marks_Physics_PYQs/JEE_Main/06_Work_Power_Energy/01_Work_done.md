### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The rain drop of mass $1$ g, starts with zero velocity from a height of $1$ km. It hits the ground with a speed of $5$ m/s. The work done by the unknown resistive force is _______ J. (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-8.75$
- **(B)** $-8.35$
- **(C)** $-9.55$
- **(D)** $-9.98$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-9.98$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the work-energy theorem, the total work done by all forces equals the change in kinetic energy:

$W_{g} + W_{r} = \Delta K$

$mgh + W_{r} = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 - 0$

Substituting the given values ($m = 10^{-3}$ kg, $g = 10$ m/s$^2$, $h = 1000$ m, $v = 5$ m/s):

$(10^{-3})(10)(1000) + W_{r} = \dfrac{1}{2}(10^{-3})(5)^2$

$10 + W_{r} = 0.0125$

$W_{r} = 0.0125 - 10 = -9.9875$ J

$W_{r} \approx -9.98$ J

Answer: $-9.98$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

A $1$ kg block subjected to two simultaneous forces $(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 4\hat{k})$ N and $(3\hat{i} - \hat{j} - 2\hat{k})$ N is moved a distance of $25$ m along $(3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j})$ direction. The work done in this process is _____ J.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **35**

**Detailed Solution:**

The net force acting on the block is the vector sum of the two forces:

$\vec{F} = \vec{F}_1 + \vec{F}_2 = (2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}) + (3\hat{i} - \hat{j} - 2\hat{k}) = 5\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$ N

The block is moved a distance of $25$ m along the direction of the vector $3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}$. The unit vector along this direction is:

$\hat{n} = \dfrac{3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}}{\sqrt{3^2 + (-4)^2}} = \dfrac{3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}}{5}$

The displacement vector $\vec{s}$ is the magnitude multiplied by the unit vector:

$\vec{s} = 25 \hat{n} = 25 \left( \dfrac{3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}}{5} \right) = 15\hat{i} - 20\hat{j}$ m

The work done $W$ is the dot product of the net force and the displacement vector:

$W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = (5\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) \cdot (15\hat{i} - 20\hat{j})$

$W = (5 \times 15) + (2 \times -20) + (2 \times 0)$

$W = 75 - 40 = 35$ J

Answer: $35$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A force $\mathrm{F}=\alpha+\beta \mathrm{x}^2$ acts on an object in the x -direction. The work done by the force is 5 J when the object is displaced by 1 m . If the constant $\alpha=1 \mathrm{~N}$ then $\beta$ will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $15 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$
- **(B)** $12 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$
- **(C)** $8 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$
- **(D)** $10 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $12 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$F=\alpha+\beta x^2$
Work done $\int d w=\int F \cdot d x$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad \Delta W=\int F \cdot d x=\int\left(\alpha+\beta x^2\right) d x \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \Delta W=\left|\alpha x+\frac{\beta x^3}{3}\right|_0^1=\alpha+\frac{\beta}{3}=5
\end{aligned}$
Given $\alpha=1$
So, $\frac{\beta}{3}=4$
$\Rightarrow \beta=12 \mathrm{~N} / \mathrm{m}^2$

</details>

### Q31. JEE Main 2004
