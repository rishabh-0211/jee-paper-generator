### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In case of vertical circular motion of a particle by a thread of length $r$ if the tension in the thread is zero at an angle $30^{\circ}$ shown in figure, the velocity at the bottom point $(A)$ of the circular path is ($g=$ gravitational acceleration) 
 <img style="width:150px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/24S2_q_45_1.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{\frac{7}{2} g r}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{5 g r}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{4 g r}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{\frac{5}{2} g r}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\sqrt{\frac{7}{2} g r}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At the point where tension $T = 0$ and angle from vertical is $60°$ (i.e., $30°$ from horizontal):
$T + mg\cos 60° = \frac{mV^2}{\ell}$
Since $T = 0$:
$V^2 = \frac{g\ell}{2}$
Using mechanical energy conservation from bottom (A) to the point where $T = 0$:
$\frac{1}{2}mu^2 = mg(\ell + \ell\cos 60°) + \frac{1}{2}mV^2$
$u^2 = 2g\ell\left(1 + \frac{1}{2}\right) + \frac{g\ell}{2} = 3g\ell + \frac{g\ell}{2} = \frac{7g\ell}{2}$
$u = \sqrt{\frac{7g\ell}{2}}$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2024 (08 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

A circular table is rotating with an angular velocity of $\omega \mathrm{rad} / \mathrm{s}$ about its axis (see figure). There is a smooth groove along a radial direction on the table. A steel ball is gently placed at a distance of $1 \mathrm{~m}$ on the groove. All the surfaces are smooth. If the radius of the table is $3 \mathrm{~m}$, the radial velocity of the ball w.r.t. the table at the time ball leaves the table is $x \sqrt{2} \omega \mathrm{m} / \mathrm{s}$, where the value of $x$ is _____.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/5jxdEQWJyDonHJm3ClguEqHfRU17MfS8aV9ZwgaHpe8.original.fullsize.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & a_c=\omega^2 x \\ & \frac{v d v}{d x}=\omega^2 x \\ & \int_0^v v d v=\int_1^3 \omega^2 x d x \\ & \frac{v^2}{2}=\omega^2\left[\frac{x^2}{2}\right] \\ & \frac{v^2}{2}=\frac{\omega^2}{2}\left[3^2-1^2\right] \\ & v=2 \sqrt{2} \omega \\ & x=2\end{aligned}$

</details>
