### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two radioactive substances A and B of mass numbers $200$ and $212$ respectively, shows spontaneous $\alpha$-decay with same $Q$ value of $1$ MeV. The ratio of energies of $\alpha$-rays produced by A and B is ________.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{2548}{2650}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{2706}{2646}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{2597}{2600}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{2862}{2499}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\dfrac{2597}{2600}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In an $\alpha$-decay, the kinetic energy of the emitted $\alpha$-particle is given by the conservation of momentum and energy as:

$K_{\alpha} = \dfrac{A - 4}{A} Q$

where $A$ is the mass number of the parent nucleus.

For substance A ($A = 200$):

$K_{\alpha, A} = \dfrac{200 - 4}{200} Q = \dfrac{196}{200} Q$

For substance B ($A = 212$):

$K_{\alpha, B} = \dfrac{212 - 4}{212} Q = \dfrac{208}{212} Q$

The ratio of the energies of the $\alpha$-rays produced by A and B is:

$\dfrac{K_{\alpha, A}}{K_{\alpha, B}} = \dfrac{\dfrac{196}{200} Q}{\dfrac{208}{212} Q} = \dfrac{196}{200} \times \dfrac{212}{208}$

Simplifying the fractions:

$\dfrac{K_{\alpha, A}}{K_{\alpha, B}} = \dfrac{49}{50} \times \dfrac{53}{52} = \dfrac{2597}{2600}$

Answer: $\dfrac{2597}{2600}$

</details>

$$

&\frac{d N}{d t}=100-\lambda N \\

$\therefore n=4$, Therefore, in 8 seconds 4 half life had occurred in which counting rate reduces to 100 counts $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$.
$\therefore$ Half life, $\frac{T_1}{2}=2 \mathrm{sec}$
In $6 \mathrm{sec}, 3$ half life will occur
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore\left[\frac{\frac{d \mathrm{~N}}{d t}}{1600}\right]=\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]^3 \\
