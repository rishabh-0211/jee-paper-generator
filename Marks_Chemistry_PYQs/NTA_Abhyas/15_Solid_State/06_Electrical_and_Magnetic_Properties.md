### Q2. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

On application of pressure certain crystals can produce electric signals. This phenomenon is known as

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ferrielectricity
- **(B)** Ferroelectricity
- **(C)** Peizoelectricity
- **(D)** Pyroelectricity

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Peizoelectricity**

**Detailed Solution:**

When polar crystal is subjected to a mechanical stress, electricity is produced - a case of piezoelectricity. Reversely, if electric field is applied, mechanical stress is developed. Piezoelectric crystal acts as a mechanical electrical transductor.
