### Q3. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

How physical adsorption will be effected by increase in temperature

**Options:**

- **(A)** It will decrease
- **(B)** It will increase
- **(C)** First increase then decrease
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) It will decrease**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since adsorption is an exothermic process (taking place with the evolution of heat) therefore in accordance with Lechatelier's principle, the magnitude of physical adsorption will decrease with the increase in temperature. In case of chemisorption the adsorption first increase and then decreases with increase in temperature.

</details>
