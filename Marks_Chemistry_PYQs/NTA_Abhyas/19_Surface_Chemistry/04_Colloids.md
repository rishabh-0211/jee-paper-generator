### Q2. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements is correct for a lyophilic sol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is not easily solvated
- **(B)** The coagulation of this sol is irreversible in nature
- **(C)** It is unstable
- **(D)** It is quite stable in a solvent

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) It is quite stable in a solvent**

**Detailed Solution:**

In lyophilic sols, the dispersed phase particles have great affinity for the dispersion medium. These sols are reversible. These are quite stable. Examples include gum, gelatin, starch, proteins and rubber etc.
