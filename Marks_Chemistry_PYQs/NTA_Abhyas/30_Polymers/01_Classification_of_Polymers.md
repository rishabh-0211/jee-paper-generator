### Q7. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Arrange the following polymers in an increasing order of intermolecular forces; fibre, plastic, elastomer.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Elastomer < Fibre < Plastic
- **(B)** Elastomer < Plastic < Fibre
- **(C)** Plastic < Elastomer < Fibore
- **(D)** Fibre < Elastomer < Plastic

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Elastomer < Plastic < Fibre**

**Detailed Solution:**

Elastomers or rubbers have the weakest intermolecular forces of attraction followed by plastics while fibres have the strongest forces of attraction. Thus, the increasing intermolecular forces of attraction follows the order

elastomer < plastic < fibre.
