<math><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>2</mn></mrow></msub><mo>→</mo><mi mathvariant="normal"> </mi><msup><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">O</mi></mrow><mrow><mo>-</mo></mrow></msup><mi mathvariant="normal"> </mi><mo>+</mo><mi mathvariant="normal"> </mi><msup><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi></mrow><mrow><mo>-</mo></mrow></msup></math>

**Options:**

- **(A)** Disproportionation
- **(B)** Intramolecular redox
- **(C)** Intermolecular redox
- **(D)** None of the above

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Disproportionation**

**Detailed Solution:**

Auto-redox or disproportionation is the one in which same substance is oxidized and reduced as well.

Intramolecular redox change is the one in which one element of a compound is oxidized and the other element is reduced.

Intermolecular redox is the one in which one of the two reactants is oxidized and the other is reduced.
