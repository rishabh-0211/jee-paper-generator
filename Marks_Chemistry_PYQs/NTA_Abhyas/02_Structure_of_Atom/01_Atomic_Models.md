### Q1. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Amongst the following statements, select the set having statements which was proposed by Dalton.
(1) All the atoms of a given element have identical properties including identical mass. Atoms of different elements differ in mass.
(2) When gases combine or reproduced in a chemical reaction they do so in a simple ratio by volume provided all gases are at the same T &amp; P
(3) Chemical reactions involve reorganization of atoms. These are neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.
(4) Matter consists of indivisible atoms

**Options:**

- **(A)** (1), (2), (3)
- **(B)** (1), (3), (4)
- **(C)** (1), (2), (4)
- **(D)** (1), (2), (3), (4)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) (1), (3), (4)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statements (1), (3) and (4) are of Dalton's theory.

Statement (2) - When gases combine or reproduced in a chemical reaction they do so in a simple ratio by volume provided all gases are at the same T & P represents Avogadro law.

</details>
