### Q16. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Consider the following

<img alt="" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/nta_abhyas/jee_main/images/635632.JPG" style="width: 499px; height: 199px;" />

- **(A)** Mutarotation
- **(B)** Epimerisation
