### Q2. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

RNA and DNA are chiral molecules, their chirality is due to

**Options:**

- **(A)** chiral bases
- **(B)** chiral phosphate ester units
- **(C)** D-sugar component
- **(D)** L-sugar component

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) D-sugar component**

**Detailed Solution:**

The constituents of nucleic acids are nitrogenous bases, sugar and phosphoric acid. The sugar present in DNA is D(-)-2-deoxyribose  and the sugar present in RNA is D(-) ribose. Due to these D(-)-sugar components, DNA and RNA molecules are chiral molecules.

### Q4. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The sequence of an mRNA molecule produced from a DNA template strand with the composition 

5'-AGCTACACT-3' is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 5'-AGUGUAGCU-3'
- **(B)** 5'-UCGAUGUGA-3'
- **(C)** 5'-AGTGTAGCT-3'
- **(D)** 5'-TCGATGTGA-3'

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 5'-UCGAUGUGA-3'**

**Detailed Solution:**

The two stands of DNA are complementary to each other and the bases of one stand are paired with the bases of other strand by means of hydrogen bonding, i.e., A = T and  <math><mi mathvariant="normal">G</mi><mo>≡</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi></math> . But uracil (U) instead of thymine (T) is present in case of RNA. Thus, the sequence of base on m-RNA molecule is

DNA strand      5'-AGCTACACT-3'

m-RNA           5'-UCGAUGUCA-3'

</details>
