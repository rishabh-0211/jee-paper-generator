### Q5. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The helical structure of protein is stabilized by

**Options:**

- **(A)** Dipeptide bonds
- **(B)** Hydrogen bonds
- **(C)** Ether bonds
- **(D)** Peptide bonds

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Hydrogen bonds**

**Detailed Solution:**

The helical structure of protein is stabilized by hydrogen bonds between amide group of the same peptide chain.  These bonds are formed by <math><mo>–</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">N</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">H</mi><mo>-</mo></math> group of one unit and oxygen of carbonyl group of the third unit.
