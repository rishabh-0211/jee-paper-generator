Mark the correct order of decreasing acid strength of the following compounds.

**Options:**

- **(A)** (V) &gt; (IV) &gt; (II) &gt; (I) &gt; (III)
- **(B)** (II) &gt; (IV) &gt; (I) &gt; (III) &gt; (V)
- **(C)** (IV) &gt; (V) &gt; (III) &gt; (II) &gt; (I)
- **(D)** (V) &gt; (IV) &gt; (III) &gt; (II) &gt; (I)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) (II) &gt; (IV) &gt; (I) &gt; (III) &gt; (V)**

**Detailed Solution:**

The electron withdrawing group <math><mfenced separators="|"><mrow><mo>-</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">N</mi><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">O</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>2</mn></mrow></msub></mrow></mfenced></math> increases the acidity of phenols and the electron donating group <math><mfenced separators="|"><mrow><mo>-</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">O</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">H</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>3</mn></mrow></msub></mrow></mfenced></math> decreases the acidity of phenols. The effect oat p-position is greater than at m-position. 
Hence the order of acidic nature is (II) &gt; (IV) &gt; (I) &gt; (III) &gt; (V).

### Q4. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What will be the correct decreasing order of acid strength of the hydroxybenzoic acids? (Symbols and notations carry their usual meanings)

**Options:**

- **(A)** p-Hydroxybenzoic acid > benzoic acid > m-hydroxybenzoic acid > o-hydroxybenzoic acid
- **(B)** o-Hydroxybenzoic acid &gt; m-hydroxybenzoic acid &gt; benzoic acid &gt; p-hydroxybenzoic acid
- **(C)** o-Hydroxybenzoic acid > benzoic acid > m-hydroxybenzoic acid > p-hydroxybenzoic acid
- **(D)** m-Hydroxybenzoic acid > benzoic acid > o-hydroxybenzoic acid > p-hydroxybenzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) o-Hydroxybenzoic acid &gt; m-hydroxybenzoic acid &gt; benzoic acid &gt; p-hydroxybenzoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

In case of hydroxybenzoic acids, they display both kind of effect and there is a decrease in electron density at all positions due to inductive effect of -OH group, but increase in electron density at o- and p-positions due to resonance effect by -OH group. o-hydroxy benzoic acid is strongest among the given acids due to intramolecular hydrogen bonding in its  conjugate base
