### Q1. NTA Abhyas (JEE Main) 2020

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

How many of the following acids will show higher reactivity towards esterification reaction as compared to acetic acid?
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/nta_abhyas/jee_main/images/de512d97-7514-4f37-8036-d0d4456078b9.png" style="height: 150px; width: 481px;" />

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **1**

**Detailed Solution:**

The order of reactivity of carboxylic acids towards esterification reaction is, <math><mtext>HCOOH</mtext><mo>&gt;</mo><msub><mrow><mtext>CH</mtext></mrow><mrow><mn>3</mn></mrow></msub><mtext>COOH</mtext><mo>&gt;</mo><msub><mrow><mtext>RCH</mtext></mrow><mrow><mn>2</mn></mrow></msub><mtext>COOH</mtext><mo>&gt;</mo><msub><mrow><mtext>R</mtext></mrow><mrow><mn>2</mn></mrow></msub><mtext>CHCOOH</mtext><mo>&gt;</mo><msub><mrow><mtext>R</mtext></mrow><mrow><mn>3</mn></mrow></msub><msub><mrow><mtext>CCOOH</mtext></mrow></msub></math>
Thus, only methanoic acid will show higher reactivity towards esterification reaction as compared to acetic acid.
