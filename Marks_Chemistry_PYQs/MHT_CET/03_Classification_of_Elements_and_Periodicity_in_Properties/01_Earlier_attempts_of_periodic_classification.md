### Q2. MHT CET 2021 (22 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which block element from following are known as transition elements?

**Options:**

- **(A)** f-block
- **(B)** s-block
- **(C)** p-block
- **(D)** d-block

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) d-block**

**Detailed Solution:**

Transition elements (also known as transition metals) are elements that have partially filled $d$ orbitals. IUPAC defines transition elements as an element having a $\mathrm{d}$ subshell that is partially filled with electrons, or an element that has the ability to form stable cations with an incompletely filled d orbital. In general, any element which corresponds to the d-block of the modern periodic table (which consists of groups 3-12) is considered to be a transition element.

</details>
