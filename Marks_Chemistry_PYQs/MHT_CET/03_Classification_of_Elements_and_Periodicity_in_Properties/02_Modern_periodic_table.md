### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following elements has highest electronegativity?

**Options:**

- **(A)** K
- **(B)** Na
- **(C)** Li
- **(D)** Cs

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Li**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electronegativity is the tendency of an atom to attract a shared pair of electrons towards itself.

In a group, electronegativity decreases from top to bottom due to an increase in atomic size and shielding effect.

The given elements belong to Group 1 (alkali metals). Their order from top to bottom is Li, Na, K, Cs.

Therefore, Li has the highest electronegativity among the given elements.

Answer: Li

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the total number of unpaired electrons in an element placed at period-4 and group-12 either in its oxidation state or at ground state?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Zero
- **(B)** One
- **(C)** Two
- **(D)** Three

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Zero**

**Detailed Solution:**

The element present in period 4 and group 12 of the periodic table is Zinc (Zn).

The atomic number of Zn is 30.

The electronic configuration of Zn in its ground state is $[Ar] 3d^{10} 4s^{2}$. Since all the subshells are completely filled, there are no unpaired electrons in the ground state.

The most common and stable oxidation state of Zinc is $+2$.

The electronic configuration of $Zn^{2+}$ is $[Ar] 3d^{10}$. Here as well, the $d$-subshell is completely filled, meaning there are no unpaired electrons.

Therefore, the total number of unpaired electrons in either its ground state or its oxidation state is zero.

Answer: Zero

</details>
