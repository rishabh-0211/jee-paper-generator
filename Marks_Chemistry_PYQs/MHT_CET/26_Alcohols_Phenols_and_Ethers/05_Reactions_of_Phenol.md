### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

Identify the reason for acidic nature of phenol.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Presence of hydrogen bonding.
- **(B)** Phenoxide ion is least stable.
- **(C)** Presence of strong and stable O-H bond.
- **(D)** Due to resonance stabilization of its anion.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Due to resonance stabilization of its anion.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Phenol is acidic in nature because it can easily lose a proton to form a phenoxide ion. The phenoxide ion formed is highly stabilized by resonance, as the negative charge on the oxygen atom is delocalized over the benzene ring. This resonance stabilization of the conjugate base (phenoxide anion) is the primary reason for the acidic nature of phenol.

Answer: Due to resonance stabilization of its anion.

</details>
