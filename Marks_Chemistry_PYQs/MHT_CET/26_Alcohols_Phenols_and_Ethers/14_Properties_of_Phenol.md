### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following reagents is used in the conversion of phenol into picric acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** dil. $\mathrm{HNO}_3$
- **(B)** dil. $\mathrm{HNO}_2$
- **(C)** conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_3+$ conc. $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$
- **(D)** conc. $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_3+$ conc. $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Phenol reacts with a nitrating mixture containing concentrated nitric acid and concentrated sulfuric acid to form 2,4,6-trinitrophenol, commonly known as picric acid.

The reaction involves electrophilic aromatic substitution where the $-\mathrm{OH}$ group strongly activates the benzene ring, directing the incoming nitro groups to the ortho and para positions.

Dilute $\mathrm{HNO}_3$ yields a mixture of ortho and para nitrophenols, not picric acid.

Answer: conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_3+$ conc. $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following compounds has lowest solubility in water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phenol
- **(B)** p-Cresol
- **(C)** o-Nitrophenol
- **(D)** p-Nitrophenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) o-Nitrophenol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Solubility of phenols in water is primarily due to their ability to form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water molecules.

In o-nitrophenol, the $-\text{OH}$ and $-\text{NO}_2$ groups are adjacent to each other, leading to strong intramolecular hydrogen bonding (chelation). This restricts the molecule from forming intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water.

In contrast, phenol, p-cresol, and p-nitrophenol do not exhibit intramolecular hydrogen bonding and can readily form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water, making them more soluble.

Therefore, o-nitrophenol has the lowest solubility in water among the given compounds.

Answer: o-Nitrophenol

</details>

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

### Q11. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which among the following has the highest melting point?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phenol
- **(B)** p-Nitrophenol
- **(C)** p-Cresol
- **(D)** o-Nitrophenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) p-Nitrophenol**

**Detailed Solution:**

p-Nitrophenol has the highest melting point among the given compounds.

In p-nitrophenol, the -OH and -NO$_2$ groups are at the para position, which allows for strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding. This leads to the association of molecules and a highly symmetrical structure that packs efficiently in the crystal lattice, resulting in a high melting point (around $114^\circ$C).

In contrast, o-nitrophenol forms intramolecular hydrogen bonds, which restricts intermolecular association, giving it a lower melting point (around $45^\circ$C). Phenol and p-cresol also have lower melting points (around $41^\circ$C and $35^\circ$C respectively) due to less efficient packing and weaker intermolecular forces compared to p-nitrophenol.

Answer: p-Nitrophenol

</details>

### Q12. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the compound having highest solubility in water from the following?

**Options:**

- **(A)** tert-Butyl alcohol
- **(B)** Phenol
- **(C)** o-Nitrophenol
- **(D)** p-Nitrophenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) tert-Butyl alcohol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Solubility of organic compounds in water depends on their ability to form hydrogen bonds with water molecules and the size of the hydrophobic hydrocarbon part.

tert-Butyl alcohol has a small hydrocarbon part (4 carbon atoms) and a highly polar $-OH$ group, which forms strong intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water. It is completely miscible with water in all proportions.

Phenol has a larger hydrophobic phenyl ring (6 carbon atoms), which decreases its solubility in water compared to lower aliphatic alcohols.

o-Nitrophenol exhibits intramolecular hydrogen bonding, which reduces its ability to form hydrogen bonds with water, making it sparingly soluble.

p-Nitrophenol can form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water, making it more soluble than o-nitrophenol, but its large hydrophobic aromatic ring still limits its solubility compared to tert-butyl alcohol.

Therefore, tert-butyl alcohol has the highest solubility in water.

Answer: tert-Butyl alcohol

</details>
