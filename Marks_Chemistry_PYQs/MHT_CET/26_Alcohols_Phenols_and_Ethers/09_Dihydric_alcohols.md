### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is a dihydric alcohol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Glycerol
- **(B)** Ethylene glycol
- **(C)** Sorbitol
- **(D)** Methanol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ethylene glycol**

**Detailed Solution:**

A dihydric alcohol contains two hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups in its molecule.

Glycerol is a trihydric alcohol (propane-$1,2,3$-triol) containing three $-OH$ groups.

Ethylene glycol is a dihydric alcohol (ethane-$1,2$-diol) containing two $-OH$ groups.

Sorbitol is a hexahydric alcohol containing six $-OH$ groups.

Methanol is a monohydric alcohol containing one $-OH$ group.

Answer: Ethylene glycol

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is NOT dihydric alcohol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Catechol
- **(B)** Resorcinol
- **(C)** Phloroglucinol
- **(D)** Hydroquinone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Phloroglucinol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Catechol is benzene-1,2-diol, which contains two hydroxyl groups and is a dihydric phenol.

Resorcinol is benzene-1,3-diol, which contains two hydroxyl groups and is a dihydric phenol.

Hydroquinone is benzene-1,4-diol, which contains two hydroxyl groups and is a dihydric phenol.

Phloroglucinol is benzene-1,3,5-triol, which contains three hydroxyl groups and is a trihydric phenol.

Therefore, phloroglucinol is not a dihydric alcohol.

Answer: Phloroglucinol

</details>
