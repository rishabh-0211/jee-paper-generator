### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product obtained when alkenes react with cold and dilute alkaline potassium permanganate.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alkanols
- **(B)** Glycols
- **(C)** Glycerols
- **(D)** Alkanoic acids

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Glycols**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cold and dilute alkaline potassium permanganate is known as Baeyer's reagent.

When alkenes react with Baeyer's reagent, they undergo hydroxylation, which means two hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups are added across the carbon-carbon double bond.

The resulting products are vicinal diols, which are commonly known as glycols.

For example, ethene reacts with cold, dilute alkaline $KMnO_4$ to form ethane-1,2-diol (ethylene glycol).

Answer: Glycols
