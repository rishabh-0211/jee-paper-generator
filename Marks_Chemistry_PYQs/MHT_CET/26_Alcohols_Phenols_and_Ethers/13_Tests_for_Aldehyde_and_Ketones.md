### Q1. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds on reaction with ammonical silver nitrate solution forms precipitate of silver?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethanal
- **(B)** Ethanoic acid
- **(C)** Ethanol
- **(D)** Ethoxy ethane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Ethanal**

**Detailed Solution:**

Silver mirror test or Tollen's test is given by only aldehydes. Hence, ethanal on reaction with ammonical silver nitrate solution forms precipitate of silver.

</details>
