### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Aldol condensation reaction is

**Options:**

- **(A)** Nucleophilic addition-elimination reaction
- **(B)** Electrophilic substitution
- **(C)** Nucleophilic substitution
- **(D)** Electrophilic addition-elimination reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Nucleophilic addition-elimination reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

Aldol condensation involves two main steps.

First, an enolate ion acts as a nucleophile and attacks the carbonyl carbon of another molecule, which is a nucleophilic addition reaction.

Second, the resulting aldol undergoes dehydration, which is an elimination reaction, to form an $\alpha,\beta$-unsaturated carbonyl compound.

Therefore, the overall process is a nucleophilic addition-elimination reaction.

Answer: Nucleophilic addition-elimination reaction

</details>

**Options:**

- **(C)** HCHO
- **(D)** $\mathrm{C}_{6} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{CHO}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CHO}$**

**Detailed Solution:**
