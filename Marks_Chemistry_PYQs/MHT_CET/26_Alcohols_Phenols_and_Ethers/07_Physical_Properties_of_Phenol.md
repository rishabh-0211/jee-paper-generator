### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds is NOT a dihydric phenol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Quinol
- **(B)** Resorcinol
- **(C)** Catechol
- **(D)** Phloroglucinol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Phloroglucinol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Dihydric phenols are compounds that contain two hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups attached directly to a benzene ring.

Quinol (hydroquinone) is benzene-$1,4$-diol, which has two $-OH$ groups.

Resorcinol is benzene-$1,3$-diol, which has two $-OH$ groups.

Catechol is benzene-$1,2$-diol, which has two $-OH$ groups.

Phloroglucinol is benzene-$1,3,5$-triol, which contains three $-OH$ groups and is therefore a trihydric phenol.

Thus, phloroglucinol is not a dihydric phenol.

Answer: Phloroglucinol

</details>

Catechol is benzene-$1,2$-diol, which contains two $-OH$ groups. It is a dihydric phenol.

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which among the following compounds has highest solubility in water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phenol
- **(B)** p-resol
- **(C)** o-Nitrophenol
- **(D)** p-Nitrophenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Phenol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Solubility of organic compounds in water depends on their ability to form hydrogen bonds with water molecules, the size of the hydrophobic part, and the lattice energy of the solid.

1. Phenol forms strong intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water, making it quite soluble (solubility $\approx 8.3 \text{ g/100 mL}$).

2. p-Cresol contains an additional hydrophobic methyl ($-CH_3$) group, which increases the non-polar character and decreases its solubility compared to phenol (solubility $\approx 1.9 \text{ g/100 mL}$).

3. o-Nitrophenol exhibits intramolecular hydrogen bonding (chelation). This restricts its ability to form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water, resulting in very low solubility (solubility $\approx 0.2 \text{ g/100 mL}$).

4. p-Nitrophenol can form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water. However, due to its higher molecular mass and strong intermolecular interactions in the solid state (high lattice energy), its solubility is lower than that of phenol (solubility $\approx 1.6 \text{ g/100 mL}$).

Therefore, phenol has the highest solubility in water among the given compounds.

Answer: Phenol

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which among the following has highest melting point ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phenol
- **(B)** o-Nitrophenol
- **(C)** p-Nitrophenol
- **(D)** p-Cresol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) p-Nitrophenol**

**Detailed Solution:**

p-Nitrophenol has the highest melting point among the given compounds.

In p-nitrophenol, there is strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding between the -OH group of one molecule and the -NO$_2$ group of another molecule. This leads to the association of molecules, requiring more thermal energy to break the crystal lattice.

In contrast, o-nitrophenol exhibits intramolecular hydrogen bonding (chelation), which restricts molecular association and lowers its melting point.

Phenol and p-cresol also exhibit intermolecular hydrogen bonding, but the interactions are weaker compared to p-nitrophenol. Additionally, the highly symmetrical structure of p-nitrophenol allows it to pack more efficiently in the solid state, further increasing its melting point.

Answer: p-Nitrophenol

</details>
