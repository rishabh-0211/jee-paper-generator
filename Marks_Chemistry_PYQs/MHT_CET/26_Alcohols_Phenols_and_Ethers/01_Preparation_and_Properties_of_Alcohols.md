### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Water solubility of alcohol, amines and alkanes of comparable molar mass are in order?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alcohol $>$ alkane $>$ amine
- **(B)** Amine $>$ Alcohol $>$ alkane
- **(C)** Alcohol $>$ amines $>$ alkanes
- **(D)** Alkane $>$ Alcohol $>$ amine

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Alcohol $>$ amines $>$ alkanes**

**Detailed Solution:**

Water solubility of organic compounds depends primarily on their ability to form hydrogen bonds with water molecules.

Oxygen is more electronegative than nitrogen, which makes the O-H bond in alcohols more polar than the N-H bond in amines. As a result, alcohols form stronger hydrogen bonds with water compared to amines, leading to higher solubility.

Alkanes are non-polar hydrocarbons and cannot form hydrogen bonds with water, making them essentially insoluble (hydrophobic).

Therefore, the order of water solubility for compounds of comparable molar mass is Alcohol $>$ amines $>$ alkanes.

Answer: Alcohol $>$ amines $>$ alkanes

</details>

### Q95. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The correct order of boiling point for primary $\left(1^{\circ}\right)$, secondary $\left(2^{\circ}\right)$ and tertiary $\left(3^{\circ}\right)$ alcohols is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1^{\circ}>2^{\circ}>3^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $3^{\circ}>2^{\circ}>1^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $2^{\circ}>1^{\circ}>3^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $2^{\circ}>3^{\circ}>1^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $1^{\circ}>2^{\circ}>3^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alcohols with same molecular weight are expected to have almost same boiling point however two more factors other than molecular weight are important, they are namely H-bonding and surface area of molecule. Both these factors are least in $3^{\circ}$ alcohols and maximum in $1^{\circ}$ alcohols. Hence, $3^{\circ}$ alcohols have least boiling point while $1^{\circ}$ alcohols have maximum boiling point.

</details>
