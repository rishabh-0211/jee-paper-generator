### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following functional groups is functional isomer of Ethers?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Aldehydes
- **(B)** Carboxylic acids
- **(C)** Alcohols
- **(D)** Ketones

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Alcohols**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ethers and alcohols have the same general molecular formula $C_n H_{2n+2}O$ for saturated non-cyclic compounds.

They differ in the nature of the functional group. Ethers have an alkoxy group ($-O-$) while alcohols have a hydroxyl group ($-OH$).

Thus, ethers and alcohols are functional isomers.

Aldehydes and ketones have the general formula $C_n H_{2n}O$, while carboxylic acids have the general formula $C_n H_{2n}O_2$.

Answer: Alcohols

</details>
