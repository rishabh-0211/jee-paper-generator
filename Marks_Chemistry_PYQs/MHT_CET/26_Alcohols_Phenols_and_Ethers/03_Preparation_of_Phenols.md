### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the substrate ' X ' in the following reaction.
$\mathrm{X}+\underset{\text { (air) }}{\mathrm{O}_2} \xrightarrow[\text { ii) dil } \mathrm{HCl} \Delta]{\text { i) Co-naphthenate } 423 \mathrm{~K}}$ phenol + Acetone

**Options:**

- **(A)** Chlorobenzene
- **(B)** Benzene sulphonic acid
- **(C)** Benzenamine
- **(D)** Isopropyl benzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Isopropyl benzene**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is the commercial method for the preparation of phenol from cumene.

Cumene is isopropyl benzene. When isopropyl benzene is oxidized by air in the presence of cobalt naphthenate catalyst at $423 \text{ K}$, it forms cumene hydroperoxide.

Cumene hydroperoxide on heating with dilute $\text{HCl}$ undergoes decomposition to yield phenol and acetone as a byproduct.

Therefore, the substrate 'X' is isopropyl benzene.

Answer: Isopropyl benzene

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product ' B ' in the following reaction.
\(\text {Toluene } \xrightarrow[\mathrm{CS}_2]{\text { Chromylchloride }} \mathrm{A} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{O}^{+}} \mathrm{B}\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzal chloride
- **(B)** Benzaldehyde
- **(C)** Benzyl alcohol
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Benzaldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is the Etard reaction.

Toluene reacts with chromyl chloride ($\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2}$) in a non-polar solvent like $\mathrm{CS_2}$ to form a brown chromium complex, which is intermediate $\mathrm{A}$.

$\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_3} + 2\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CS_2}} \mathrm{C_6H_5CH(OCrOHCl_2)_2}$ (Complex $\mathrm{A}$)

Hydrolysis of the chromium complex $\mathrm{A}$ with acidic water ($\mathrm{H_3O^+}$) yields benzaldehyde as the final product $\mathrm{B}$.

$\mathrm{C_6H_5CH(OCrOHCl_2)_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_3O^+}} \mathrm{C_6H_5CHO}$ (Benzaldehyde)

Therefore, product $\mathrm{B}$ is benzaldehyde.

Answer: Benzaldehyde

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the substrate ' S ' in the following reaction.
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/L88TxUY0V_ZComcTUleNSabaLCdzaHisVocQa3Sz0g4.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** p-Nitrochlorobenzene
- **(B)** o-Nitrochlorobenzene
- **(C)** 2,4,6 -trichloronitrobenzene
- **(D)** 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is a nucleophilic aromatic substitution where a leaving group on the benzene ring is replaced by a hydroxyl ($-OH$) group from water to form 2,4,6-trinitrophenol (picric acid).

For an aryl halide to undergo nucleophilic substitution under very mild conditions (like warming with water), the ring must be strongly activated by the presence of multiple powerful electron-withdrawing groups at the ortho and para positions relative to the leaving group.

Among the given options, 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene has three strongly electron-withdrawing nitro ($-NO_2$) groups at the ortho and para positions with respect to the chlorine atom. These groups highly stabilize the intermediate carbanion (Meisenheimer complex) through resonance and inductive effects, making the $C-Cl$ bond extremely susceptible to nucleophilic attack.

Thus, 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene reacts readily with warm water to yield 2,4,6-trinitrophenol.

Reaction:
2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene + $H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{Warm}}$ 2,4,6-trinitrophenol + $HCl$

Answer: 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

What is the name of tert-butyl alcohol according to carbinol system?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Methyl carbinol
- **(B)** Ethyl carbinol
- **(C)** Propyl carbinol
- **(D)** Trimethyl carbinol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Trimethyl carbinol**

**Detailed Solution:**

In the carbinol system of nomenclature, the simplest alcohol, methanol ($CH_3OH$), is named as carbinol.

Other alcohols are named as alkyl derivatives of carbinol. The alkyl groups attached to the carbon atom bearing the hydroxyl group (the carbinol carbon) are listed in alphabetical order as prefixes to the word carbinol.

The structure of tert-butyl alcohol is $(CH_3)_3C-OH$.

Here, the carbinol carbon is attached to three methyl groups. Therefore, its name in the carbinol system is trimethyl carbinol.

Answer: Trimethyl carbinol

</details>
