### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the type of compounds obtained when methanol is reacted with R-Mg-X.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alkane
- **(B)** Alkene
- **(C)** Ether
- **(D)** Ester

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Alkane**

**Detailed Solution:**

Methanol ($CH_3OH$) contains an acidic hydrogen atom attached to the highly electronegative oxygen atom.

Grignard reagents ($R-Mg-X$) act as strong bases. When a Grignard reagent reacts with a compound containing an active or acidic hydrogen, an acid-base reaction takes place.

The alkyl group ($R^-$) from the Grignard reagent abstracts the acidic proton ($H^+$) from methanol to form an alkane ($R-H$).

The chemical reaction is given by:
$CH_3OH + R-Mg-X \rightarrow R-H + CH_3OMgX$

Thus, the compound obtained is an alkane.

Answer: Alkane

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 2)

Which of the following is the correct order of acidic strength of following compounds?
i. $\text{CH}_3\text{OH}$,
ii. $(\text{CH}_3)_3\text{C.OH}$,
iii. $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{OH}$,
iv. $\text{p-NO}_2\text{-C}_6\text{H}_4\text{OH}$

- **(B)** iv $>$ iii $>$ ii $>$ i
- **(C)** iii $>$ iv $>$ i $>$ ii
- **(D)** iii $>$ iv $>$ ii $>$ i

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) iv $>$ iii $>$ i $>$ ii**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acidic strength depends on the stability of the conjugate base formed after the removal of an $\text{H}^+$ ion.

Phenols (iii and iv) are more acidic than aliphatic alcohols (i and ii) because the phenoxide ion is stabilized by resonance.

In aliphatic alcohols, electron-donating alkyl groups ($+I$ effect) destabilize the alkoxide ion, decreasing acidity. The tert-butyl group in (ii) has a much stronger $+I$ effect than the methyl group in (i), making tert-butyl alcohol less acidic than methanol.

Therefore, the correct order of acidic strength is iv $>$ iii $>$ i $>$ ii.

**Correct Option:** **(C) 2-hydroxy-2-methyl propane**
