### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The main function of a salt bridge in an electrochemical cell is to:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Increase cell potential
- **(B)** Decreases the reaction rate
- **(C)** Maintain electrical neutrality in half-cells
- **(D)** Increase the reaction rate

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Maintain electrical neutrality in half-cells**

**Detailed Solution:**

A salt bridge is a U-shaped tube containing an inert electrolyte like $KCl$, $KNO_3$, or $NH_4NO_3$ in agar-agar or gelatin.

Its primary functions in an electrochemical cell are to complete the inner electrical circuit by allowing the movement of ions and to maintain the electrical neutrality of the electrolytes in the two half-cells.

As oxidation occurs at the anode, positive ions accumulate in the anodic half-cell. Similarly, as reduction occurs at the cathode, negative ions accumulate in the cathodic half-cell. The salt bridge provides anions to the anodic compartment and cations to the cathodic compartment to neutralize this charge buildup.

Answer: Maintain electrical neutrality in half-cells

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the function of salt bridge.

**Options:**

- **(A)** As cathode
- **(B)** As anode
- **(C)** To connect two half cells.
- **(D)** To measure electrode potential.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) To connect two half cells.**

**Detailed Solution:**

A salt bridge is a U-shaped tube containing an inert electrolyte in a gel-like substance.

Its primary functions are to complete the electrical circuit by connecting the two half-cells and to maintain the electrical neutrality of the solutions in the two half-cells.

Therefore, its function is to connect two half cells.

Answer: To connect two half cells.

</details>
