### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Identify the product $X$ in the following reaction.
Sodium propanoate $\xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text { soda-lime }} X+$ sodium carbonate

**Options:**

- **(A)** Propane
- **(B)** Ethane
- **(C)** Methane
- **(D)** Butane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ethane**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of sodium propanoate with soda-lime is a decarboxylation reaction.

$CH_3CH_2COONa + NaOH \xrightarrow{CaO, \Delta} CH_3CH_3 + Na_2CO_3$

The alkyl group attached to the carboxylate group is an ethyl group ($CH_3CH_2-$). Upon decarboxylation, it gains a hydrogen atom to form ethane ($C_2H_6$).

Therefore, the product $X$ is ethane.

Answer: Ethane

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2023 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following is dicarboxylic acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Valeric acid
- **(B)** Caproic acid
- **(C)** Glutaric acid
- **(D)** Butyric acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Glutaric acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>To identify the dicarboxylic acid, we examine the carboxyl group count for each compound.</p><p>Valeric acid (pentanoic acid) has formula $\text{CH}_3(\text{CH}_2)_3\text{COOH}$, containing one carboxyl group and is thus monocarboxylic.</p><p>Caproic acid (hexanoic acid) is $\text{CH}_3(\text{CH}_2)_4\text{COOH}$, also monocarboxylic.</p><p>Glutaric acid (pentanedioic acid) has structure $\text{HOOC}(\text{CH}_2)_3\text{COOH}$, with two carboxyl groups, making it dicarboxylic.</p><p>Butyric acid (butanoic acid) is $\text{CH}_3(\text{CH}_2)_2\text{COOH}$, again monocarboxylic.</p><p><strong>The dicarboxylic acid is glutaric acid.</strong></p>

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2020 (19 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following reactions involves $\alpha$ -halogenation of carboxylic acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Gattermann reaction
- **(B)** Riemer - Tiemann reaction
- **(C)** Sandmeyer reaction
- **(D)** Hell-Vohlard-Zelinsky reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Hell-Vohlard-Zelinsky reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hell Volhard Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction:
This reaction involves alpha bromination of carboxylic acids. Carboxylic acids react with chlorine or bromine in presence of small amount of red phosphorous to give alpha halo carboxylic acids.
$\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}-\mathrm{COOH} \stackrel{\mathrm{Br}_{2} / \mathrm{P}}{\rightarrow} \mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CHBr}-\mathrm{COOH} \stackrel{\mathrm{Br}_{2} / \mathrm{P}}{\rightarrow} \mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CBr}_{2}-$
$\mathrm{COOH} \stackrel{\mathrm{Br}_{2} / \mathrm{P}}{\longrightarrow}$ No further reaction.

</details>
