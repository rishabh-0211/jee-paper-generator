### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (27 Apr Shift 2)

**Options:**

- **(A)** Carboxylic acids
- **(B)** Alcohols
- **(C)** Aldehydes
- **(D)** Esters

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify ' B ' in the following sequence of reactions.
Acetic acid $\xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text { Thionyl chloride }} \mathrm{A} \xrightarrow{\text { Ammonia }} \mathrm{B}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyl amine
- **(B)** Ammonium acetate
- **(C)** Acetyl chloride
- **(D)** Acetamide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Acetamide**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetic acid reacts with thionyl chloride ($\mathrm{SOCl_2}$) upon heating to form acetyl chloride as the major product.

$\mathrm{CH_3COOH} + \mathrm{SOCl_2} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{CH_3COCl} + \mathrm{SO_2} + \mathrm{HCl}$

Thus, compound A is acetyl chloride.

Acetyl chloride then reacts with ammonia ($\mathrm{NH_3}$) to form acetamide.

$\mathrm{CH_3COCl} + 2\mathrm{NH_3} \rightarrow \mathrm{CH_3CONH_2} + \mathrm{NH_4Cl}$

Thus, compound B is acetamide.

Answer: Acetamide

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COCH}_3$

- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{OH}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

$(\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CO})_2\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{COOH}$

Thus, the product formed is acetic acid, $\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{COOH}$.

### Q7. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If salicylic acid (138 u) reacts with acetic anhydride (102 u) to from aspirin (180 u) calculate % atom economy

**Options:**

- **(A)** $25 \%$
- **(B)** $50 \%$
- **(C)** $65 \%$
- **(D)** $75 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $75 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The formula for percentage atom economy is given by:

$\% \text{ atom economy} = \dfrac{\text{Mass of desired product}}{\text{Sum of mass of all reactants}} \times 100$

The desired product is aspirin, with a mass of $180 \text{ u}$.

The reactants are salicylic acid and acetic anhydride. The sum of their masses is:

$\text{Total mass of reactants} = 138 \text{ u} + 102 \text{ u} = 240 \text{ u}$

Substituting these values into the formula:

$\% \text{ atom economy} = \dfrac{180}{240} \times 100$

$\% \text{ atom economy} = \dfrac{3}{4} \times 100 = 75 \%$

Answer: $75 \%$

</details>

### Q9. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product obtained when excess of benzoyl chloride is treated with dimethyl cadmium.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetophenone
- **(B)** Benzoic acid
- **(C)** Benzophenone
- **(D)** Benzaldehyde

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Acetophenone**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of an acid chloride with a dialkyl cadmium yields a ketone. Dialkyl cadmium is a less reactive organometallic reagent and does not react further with the ketone produced.

When benzoyl chloride is treated with dimethyl cadmium, the methyl group from dimethyl cadmium replaces the chlorine atom of benzoyl chloride to form acetophenone.

$2C_6H_5COCl + (CH_3)_2Cd \rightarrow 2C_6H_5COCH_3 + CdCl_2$

The product obtained is acetophenone.

Answer: Acetophenone

</details>

### Q10. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which among the following is a pair of monocarboxylic acids?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Malonic acid and propionic acid
- **(B)** Valeric acid and succinic acid
- **(C)** Acetic acid and adipic acid
- **(D)** Butyric acid and caproic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Butyric acid and caproic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Monocarboxylic acids contain only one carboxylic acid group ($-COOH$).

Malonic acid is a dicarboxylic acid ($HOOC-CH_2-COOH$).

Succinic acid is a dicarboxylic acid ($HOOC-(CH_2)_2-COOH$).

Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid ($HOOC-(CH_2)_4-COOH$).

Butyric acid ($CH_3-(CH_2)_2-COOH$) and caproic acid ($CH_3-(CH_2)_4-COOH$) both contain only one $-COOH$ group and are therefore monocarboxylic acids.

Answer: Butyric acid and caproic acid

</details>

### Q11. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

Which from following compounds is NOT a mono carboxylic acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phthalic acid
- **(B)** Salicylic acid
- **(C)** o-toluic acid
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Phthalic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Phthalic acid is benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid, which contains two carboxyl (-COOH) groups. Thus, it is a dicarboxylic acid.

Salicylic acid is 2-hydroxybenzoic acid, containing one -COOH group.

o-toluic acid is 2-methylbenzoic acid, containing one -COOH group.

Therefore, phthalic acid is not a mono carboxylic acid.

Answer: Phthalic acid

</details>
