### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following reactions is used to prepare acid anhydride?

**Options:**

- **(A)** By heating sodium salt of carboxylic acid with NaOH in the presence of CaO.
- **(B)** By heating carboxylic acid in excess with $\text{P}_2\text{O}_5$.
- **(C)** By reacting carboxylic acids with $\text{LiAlH}_4$.
- **(D)** By heating Carboxylic acids with $\text{SOCl}_2$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) By heating carboxylic acid in excess with $\text{P}_2\text{O}_5$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Heating a sodium salt of a carboxylic acid with NaOH and CaO (soda lime) results in decarboxylation, yielding an alkane.

Heating a carboxylic acid with a strong dehydrating agent like $\text{P}_2\text{O}_5$ or concentrated $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ removes a molecule of water from two molecules of the acid, forming an acid anhydride. The reaction is: $2\text{RCOOH} \xrightarrow{\text{P}_2\text{O}_5, \Delta} (\text{RCO})_2\text{O} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$.

Reacting a carboxylic acid with $\text{LiAlH}_4$ reduces it to a primary alcohol.

Heating a carboxylic acid with $\text{SOCl}_2$ converts it into an acid chloride.

Answer: By heating carboxylic acid in excess with $\text{P}_2\text{O}_5$.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the product obtained when acetic acid reacts with $\text{PCl}_5$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{CH}_2\text{ClCOOH}$
- **(B)** $\text{CH}_3\text{COOCl}$
- **(C)** $\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$
- **(D)** $\text{CHCl}_2\text{COOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of acetic acid with phosphorus pentachloride yields acetyl chloride, phosphoryl chloride, and hydrogen chloride.

$\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} + \text{PCl}_5 \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{COCl} + \text{POCl}_3 + \text{HCl}$

The main product obtained is acetyl chloride ($\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$).

Answer: $\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the reagent used in following reaction.
$\text{Benzoic acid} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\large{\text{Reagent}}} \text{Benzoyl chloride} + \text{phosphorous oxychloride} + \text{Hydrogen chloride}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{PCl}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{HCl}$
- **(C)** $\text{PCl}_5$
- **(D)** $\text{SOCl}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{PCl}_5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of benzoic acid with phosphorus pentachloride ($\text{PCl}_5$) yields benzoyl chloride, phosphorus oxychloride ($\text{POCl}_3$), and hydrogen chloride ($\text{HCl}$).

$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COOH} + \text{PCl}_5 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COCl} + \text{POCl}_3 + \text{HCl}$

Thus, the reagent used is $\text{PCl}_5$.

Answer: $\text{PCl}_5$

</details>
