### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds is obtained in the saponification process?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{R-CHO}$
- **(B)** $\text{R-COONa}$
- **(C)** $\text{R-CO-R}$
- **(D)** $\text{R-COCl}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{R-COONa}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Saponification is the process of alkaline hydrolysis of an ester (such as fats and oils) with an alkali like $\text{NaOH}$ or $\text{KOH}$.

The general reaction is given by:
$\text{R-COOR'} + \text{NaOH} \rightarrow \text{R-COONa} + \text{R'OH}$

The products formed are sodium or potassium salts of carboxylic acids (soap) and alcohol.

Thus, $\text{R-COONa}$ is obtained in the saponification process.

Answer: $\text{R-COONa}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find the number of moles of glycerol produced when n mole of triglyceride undergoes saponification.

**Options:**

- **(A)** n
- **(B)** 2n
- **(C)** 3n
- **(D)** $\frac{3}{2} \mathrm{n}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) n**

**Detailed Solution:**

Saponification of a triglyceride is the hydrolysis of the ester with an alkali. The general reaction is:

Triglyceride + $3$NaOH $\rightarrow$ Glycerol + $3$Soap

From the stoichiometry of the balanced chemical equation, $1$ mole of triglyceride produces $1$ mole of glycerol and $3$ moles of soap.

Therefore, $n$ moles of triglyceride will produce $n$ moles of glycerol.

Answer: n

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product ' B ' in the following sequence of reactions.
Methylpropanoate $\underset{\text { dil. } \mathrm{NaOH}}{\stackrel{\Delta}{\longrightarrow}} \mathrm{A} \xrightarrow[\text { Conc. } \mathrm{HCl}]{\mathrm{H}^{+}} \mathrm{B}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** sodium propanoate
- **(B)** propanone
- **(C)** propanal
- **(D)** propanoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) propanoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Methylpropanoate is an ester with the chemical formula $\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COOCH}_3$.

When methylpropanoate is heated with dilute $\text{NaOH}$, it undergoes alkaline hydrolysis (saponification) to form the sodium salt of the carboxylic acid and an alcohol. Thus, product A is sodium propanoate.

$\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COOCH}_3 + \text{NaOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COONa} (\text{A}) + \text{CH}_3\text{OH}$

When sodium propanoate (A) is treated with concentrated $\text{HCl}$, it undergoes acidification to yield the corresponding carboxylic acid. Thus, product B is propanoic acid.

$\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COONa} + \text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COOH} (\text{B}) + \text{NaCl}$

Therefore, the final product B is propanoic acid.

Answer: propanoic acid

</details>
