### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the common name for the simplest carboxylic acid, HCOOH ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetic acid
- **(B)** Formic acid
- **(C)** Propanoic acid
- **(D)** Butanoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Formic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

The simplest carboxylic acid is $HCOOH$.

Its IUPAC name is methanoic acid.

Its common name is formic acid, derived from the Latin word 'formica' meaning ant, as it was first isolated from the destructive distillation of ants.

Answer: Formic acid

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Corrosive sublimate $\left(\mathrm{HgCl}_{2}\right)$ can be used to distinguish between

**Options:**

- **(A)** formic acid and acetic acid
- **(B)** acetaldehyde and butanone
- **(C)** formaldehyde and propanone
- **(D)** All of the above

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) formic acid and acetic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Corrosive sublimate gives black ppt of calomel with formic acid but it does not react with acetic acid.
$$
2 \mathrm{HCOOH}+2 \mathrm{HgCl}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Hg}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}_{2}+\mathrm{CO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}
$$
$\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}+\mathrm{HgCl}_{2} \longrightarrow$ No reaction

</details>
