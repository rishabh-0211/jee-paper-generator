### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is obtained when dihydrogen reacts with the alkali metals at high temperature?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alkoxides
- **(B)** Metal hydrides
- **(C)** Peroxides
- **(D)** Metal nitrites

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Metal hydrides**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkali metals react with dihydrogen at high temperatures to form the corresponding metal hydrides.

$2M + H_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2M^{+}H^{-}$

where $M$ represents an alkali metal (Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs). These hydrides are ionic (salt-like) in nature.

Answer: Metal hydrides

</details>
