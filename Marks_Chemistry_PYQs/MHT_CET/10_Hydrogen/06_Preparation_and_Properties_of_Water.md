### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following ions are responsible for hardness of water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Ag}^+, \text{Au}^{3+}$ ions
- **(B)** $\text{Hg}^{2+}, \text{Pb}^{2+}$ ions
- **(C)** $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions.
- **(D)** $\text{As}^{3+}, \text{CN}^-$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hardness of water is primarily caused by the presence of soluble salts of calcium and magnesium, such as their bicarbonates, chlorides, and sulfates.

Therefore, $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions are responsible for the hardness of water.

Answer: $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions.

</details>
