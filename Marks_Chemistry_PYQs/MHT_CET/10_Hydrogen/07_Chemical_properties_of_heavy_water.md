### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (12 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the boiling point of heavy water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $100 \cdot 4^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $101 \cdot 4^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $273^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $101 \cdot 4^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Correct answer is $101.4^{0} \mathrm{C}$
The boiling point of heavy water is $101.4^{0} \mathrm{C}$ or $374.4 \mathrm{~K}$. Its melting point is $3.8^{0} \mathrm{C}$ or $276.8 \mathrm{~K}$.

</details>
