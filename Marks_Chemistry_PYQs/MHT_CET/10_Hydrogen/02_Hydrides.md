### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following metals does not form $\text{MH}_2$ type hydride when heated with hydrogen under normal conditions?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Be
- **(B)** Mg
- **(C)** Ca
- **(D)** Sr

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Be**

**Detailed Solution:**

All alkaline earth metals except beryllium combine directly with hydrogen upon heating to form their hydrides, $\text{MH}_2$.

Beryllium hydride ($\text{BeH}_2$) cannot be prepared by direct heating of beryllium with hydrogen under normal conditions. It is prepared indirectly by the reduction of beryllium chloride with lithium aluminium hydride.

$2\text{BeCl}_2 + \text{LiAlH}_4 \rightarrow 2\text{BeH}_2 + \text{LiCl} + \text{AlCl}_3$

Answer: Be

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (5 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the correct order of acidity of hydrides of $16^{\text {th }}$ group elements from the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The hydrides of Group 16 elements are $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$, $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}$, $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se}$, and $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te}$.

As we move down the group from oxygen to tellurium, the atomic size of the central atom increases.

This results in an increase in the $\mathrm{H}-\mathrm{E}$ bond length and a corresponding decrease in the bond dissociation enthalpy.

Consequently, the tendency to release an $\mathrm{H}^+$ ion increases down the group.

Thus, the acidic character increases in the order: $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te}$.

The correct decreasing order of acidity is $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$.

Answer: $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}>\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$

</details>
