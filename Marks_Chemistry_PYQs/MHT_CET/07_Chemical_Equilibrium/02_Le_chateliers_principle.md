### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify from following salts so that the solubility of salt in water decreases with increase in temperature.

**Options:**

- **(A)** NaBr
- **(B)** NaCl
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NaNO}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The solubility of most solid solutes in water increases with an increase in temperature.

For salts like $\mathrm{NaNO}_3$ and $\mathrm{NaBr}$, solubility increases significantly with temperature.

For $\mathrm{NaCl}$, solubility increases only slightly with temperature.

For $\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{SO}_4$, the solubility decreases with an increase in temperature.

Hence, $\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{SO}_4$ is the salt whose solubility decreases with an increase in temperature.

Answer: $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$

</details>
