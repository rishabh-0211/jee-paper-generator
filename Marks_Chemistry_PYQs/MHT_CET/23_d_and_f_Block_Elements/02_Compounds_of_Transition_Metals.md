### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the oxidation state of chromium in the final product when KI reacts with acidified potassium dichromate solution?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $+2$
- **(B)** $+3$
- **(C)** $+6$
- **(D)** $+4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $+3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Potassium dichromate ($K_2Cr_2O_7$) acts as a strong oxidizing agent in an acidic medium.

It oxidizes iodide ions ($I^-$) from KI to iodine ($I_2$) and itself gets reduced to chromium(III) ions ($Cr^{3+}$).

The ionic equation for the reaction is:

$Cr_2O_7^{2-} + 14H^+ + 6I^- \rightarrow 2Cr^{3+} + 3I_2 + 7H_2O$

In the final product, chromium is present as $Cr^{3+}$.

Therefore, the oxidation state of chromium in the final product is $+3$.

Answer: $+3$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Calculate the oxidation number of Cr in $\mathrm{CrO}_4^{2-}$ ion and $\mathrm{K}_2 \mathrm{Cr}_2 \mathrm{O}_7$ respectively.

**Options:**

- **(A)** +4 and +6
- **(B)** +3 and +2
- **(C)** +6 and +6
- **(D)** +8 and +2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) +6 and +6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the oxidation number of Cr in $\mathrm{CrO}_4^{2-}$ be $x$.

$x + 4(-2) = -2$

$x - 8 = -2$

$x = +6$

Let the oxidation number of Cr in $\mathrm{K}_2 \mathrm{Cr}_2 \mathrm{O}_7$ be $y$.

$2(+1) + 2y + 7(-2) = 0$

$2 + 2y - 14 = 0$

$2y = 12$

$y = +6$

The oxidation numbers are $+6$ and $+6$.

Answer: +6 and +6

</details>
