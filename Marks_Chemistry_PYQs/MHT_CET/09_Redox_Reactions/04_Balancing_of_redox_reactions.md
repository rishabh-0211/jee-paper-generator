### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the value of $x$ and $y$ in order to balance following redox reaction?
$\mathrm{xCuO}+\mathrm{yNH}_3 \longrightarrow \mathrm{xCu}+\mathrm{N}_2+\mathrm{xH}_2 \mathrm{O}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{x}=2, \mathrm{y}=1$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{x}=1, \mathrm{y}=2$
- **(C)** $x=3, y=2$
- **(D)** $x=2, y=3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $x=3, y=2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given chemical equation is $x\mathrm{CuO} + y\mathrm{NH}_3 \longrightarrow x\mathrm{Cu} + \mathrm{N}_2 + x\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$.

Balancing the nitrogen atoms on both sides, there are 2 nitrogen atoms in $\mathrm{N}_2$ on the product side. Thus, $y = 2$.

Substituting $y = 2$, the number of hydrogen atoms on the reactant side becomes $2 \times 3 = 6$.

To balance the hydrogen atoms on the product side, we must have $2x = 6$, which gives $x = 3$.

Substituting $x = 3$ and $y = 2$ into the equation gives $3\mathrm{CuO} + 2\mathrm{NH}_3 \longrightarrow 3\mathrm{Cu} + \mathrm{N}_2 + 3\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$.

Checking the oxygen and copper atoms, there are 3 Cu and 3 O atoms on both sides. The equation is completely balanced.

Therefore, $x = 3$ and $y = 2$.

Answer: $x=3, y=2$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2023 (10 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the value of $x$ in order to balance following redox reaction?
$\mathrm{Mn}_{(\mathrm{aq})}^{2+}+x \mathrm{ClO}_{3(\mathrm{aq})}^{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{MnO}_{2(\mathrm{~s})}+x \mathrm{ClO}_{2(\mathrm{aq})}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x=1$
- **(B)** $x=2$
- **(C)** $x=3$
- **(D)** $x=4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $x=2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The balanced equation is:
$\mathrm{Mn}_{(\mathrm{aq})}^{2+}+2 \mathrm{ClO}_{3(\mathrm{aq})}^{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{MnO}_{2(\mathrm{~s})}+2 \mathrm{ClO}_{2(\mathrm{aq})}$

### Q3. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the value of ' $x$ ' in order to balance the following redox reaction by ion electron method?
$$
\mathrm{x} \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_4 \rightarrow \mathrm{x} \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_2+2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}
$$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 1
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\mathrm{x} \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_4^{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{x} \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_2^{-}+2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}
$$
Reduction Half Reaction
$$
\mathrm{ClO}_4^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ClO}_2^{-}
$$
$$
4 \mathrm{e}^{-}+4 \mathrm{H}^{+}+\mathrm{ClO}_4^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ClO}_2^{-}+2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}
$$
Oxidation Half Reaction,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{O}_2 \\
& \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{O}_2+2 \mathrm{H}^{+}+2 \mathrm{e}^{-}
\end{aligned}
$$
Net Redox reaction
$$
\begin{aligned}
& {\left[4 \mathrm{e}^{-} 4 \mathrm{H}^{+}+\mathrm{ClO}_4^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ClO}_2^{-}+2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}\right] \times 1} \\
& {\left[2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{O}_2+2 \mathrm{H}^{+}+2 \mathrm{e}^{-}\right] \times 2} \\
& 2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_4^{-} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{O}_2+\mathrm{ClO}_2^{-}+2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \\
& \mathrm{x}=2
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
