### Q1. MHT CET 2023 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

According to reaction,
$\mathrm{Mg}_{(\mathrm{s})}+2 \mathrm{HCl}_{(\mathrm{aq})} \longrightarrow \mathrm{MgCl}_{2(\mathrm{aq})}+\mathrm{H}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \uparrow$
Calculate the mass of $\mathrm{Mg}$ required to liberate
$4.48 \mathrm{dm}^3 \mathrm{H}_2$ at STP.
$\left(\right.$ Molar mass of $\mathrm{Mg}=24 \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$ )

**Options:**

- **(A)** $12 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(B)** $4.8 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(C)** $6 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(D)** $2.4 \mathrm{~g}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $4.8 \mathrm{~g}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Number of moles of a gas (n)
$=\frac{\text { Volume of gas at STP }}{22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3 \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}}$
$\therefore \quad \mathrm{n}=\frac{4.48 \mathrm{dm}^3}{22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3 \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}}=0.2 \mathrm{~mol}$
$\mathrm{Mg}_{(\mathrm{s})}+2 \mathrm{HCl}_{(\mathrm{aq})} \longrightarrow \mathrm{MgCl}_2+\mathrm{H}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \uparrow$
$1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{Mg} \equiv 1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{H}_2$ gas
$\therefore \quad$ Mg required to liberate $0.2 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{H}_2$ gas
$=0.2 \mathrm{~mol}=0.2 \times 24=4.8 \mathrm{~g}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2023 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the mass of $\mathrm{KClO}_{3(\mathrm{~s})}$ required to liberate 22. $4 \mathrm{dm}^3$ oxygen at STP during thermal decomposition?
(Molar Mass of $\mathrm{KClO}_{3(\mathrm{~s})}=122.5 \mathrm{~g} / \mathrm{mol}$ )

**Options:**

- **(A)** $122.5 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(B)** $81.67 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(C)** $10.25 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(D)** $8.16 \mathrm{~g}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $81.67 \mathrm{~g}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& 2 \mathrm{KClO}_3 \\
& {[2 \mathrm{moles}]}
\end{aligned} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{KCl}+\underset{[3 \mathrm{moles}]}{3 \mathrm{O}_2 \uparrow}
$$
[3 moles]
$$
2 \text { moles of } \mathrm{KClO}_3=2 \times 122.5=245 \mathrm{~g}
$$
3 moles of $\mathrm{O}_2$ at STP occupy $=\left(3 \times 22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3\right)$.
Thus, $245 \mathrm{~g}$ of potassium chlorate will liberate $67.2 \mathrm{dm}^3$ of oxygen gas.

Let ' $x$ ' gram of $\mathrm{KClO}_3$ liberate $22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3$ of oxygen gas at S.T.P.
$$
\therefore \quad x=\frac{245 \times 22.4}{3 \times 22.4}=81.67 \mathrm{~g}
$$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2023 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Calculate amount of methane formed by liberation of $149.6 \mathrm{~kJ}$ of heat using following equation.
$$
\mathrm{C}_{(\mathrm{s})}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{4(\mathrm{~g})} \quad \Delta \mathrm{H}=-74.8 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}
$$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $16 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(B)** $24 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(C)** $32 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(D)** $48 \mathrm{~g}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $32 \mathrm{~g}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the given reaction, $74.8 \mathrm{~kJ}$ of heat is evolved when 1 mol of methane is formed.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 74.8 \mathrm{~kJ} \equiv 1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{CH}_4=16 \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{CH}_4 \\
& 149.6 \mathrm{~kJ} \equiv x \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{CH}_4 \\
& x=\frac{149.6 \times 16}{74.8}=32 \mathrm{~g}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2023 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What volume of $\mathrm{CO}_{2(\mathrm{~g})}$ at STP is obtained by complete combustion of $6 \mathrm{~g}$ carbon?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3$
- **(B)** $11.2 \mathrm{dm}^3$
- **(C)** $5.6 \mathrm{dm}^3$
- **(D)** $2.24 \mathrm{dm}^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $11.2 \mathrm{dm}^3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{array}{ll} & \mathrm{C}_{(\mathrm{g})}+\mathrm{O}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \\ & 1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{C} \equiv 1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{CO}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \\ & 6 \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{C}=0.5 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{C} \\ \therefore \quad & 0.5 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{C} \equiv 0.5 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{CO}_{2(\mathrm{~g})} \\ & \text { At STP, } 1 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{CO}_{2(\mathrm{~g})}=22.4 \mathrm{dm}^3 \\ \therefore \quad & 0.5 \mathrm{~mol} \mathrm{CO} 2(\mathrm{~g})=11.2 \mathrm{dm}^3\end{array}$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Copper is a divalent metal. The value of its electrochemical equivalent is $3.29 \times 10^{-4} \mathrm{~g} .$ Its atomic mass is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $31.74 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(B)** $63.5 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(C)** $126.9 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(D)** $15.87 \mathrm{~g}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $63.5 \mathrm{~g}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electrochemical equivalent, $Z=\frac{E}{96500}$ $\begin{array}{lr}\text { or } & 3.29 \times 10^{-4}=\frac{E}{96500} \\ \therefore \quad E=31.74\end{array}$
Atomic mass $=2 \times E$ $[\because$ Cu is divalent.]
$$
=2 \times 31.74=63.49 \mathrm{~g}
$$

</details>
