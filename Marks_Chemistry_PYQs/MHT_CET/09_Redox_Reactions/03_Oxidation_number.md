Find out the oxidation state of oxygen in $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-1$
- **(B)** $-2$
- **(C)** $+1$
- **(D)** $+2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $-1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the oxidation state of oxygen in $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ be $x$.

$2 + 2x = 0$

The oxidation state of oxygen in $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ is $-1$.

Answer: $-1$

$2(+1) + 2(x) + 6(-2) + 2(-1) = 0$

$2 + 2x - 12 - 2 = 0$

$2x = 12$

$x = +6$

Alternatively, the calculated oxidation state without considering the peroxy linkage is $+7$, which exceeds the maximum possible oxidation state of sulfur ($+6$). Hence, the oxidation state of sulfur is $+6$.

Answer: $+6$

### Q25. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 1)

In the equation,
$\mathrm{BiO}_3^{-}+6 \mathrm{H}^{+}+x ~\mathrm{e}^{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{Bi}^{3+}+3 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$

What is the value of $x$ ?

- **(B)** 3
- **(C)** 4
- **(D)** 6

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given half-reaction is:

$\mathrm{BiO}_3^{-} + 6 \mathrm{H}^{+} + x \mathrm{e}^{-} \rightarrow \mathrm{Bi}^{3+} + 3 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$

To find the value of $x$, we balance the total charge on both sides of the equation.

Total charge on the reactant side $= -1 + 6(+1) + x(-1) = 5 - x$

Total charge on the product side $= +3 + 3(0) = +3$

Equating the charges on both sides:

$5 - x = 3$

$x = 2$

Alternatively, the oxidation state of $\mathrm{Bi}$ in $\mathrm{BiO}_3^{-}$ is $+5$. It gets reduced to $\mathrm{Bi}^{3+}$ (oxidation state $+3$). The decrease in oxidation state is $5 - 3 = 2$, which corresponds to the gain of $2$ electrons. Thus, $x = 2$.

Answer: 2

### Q84. MHT CET 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The oxidation number of $\mathrm{C}$ atom in $\mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{CCl}_{4}$ are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-2$ and $-4$
- **(B)** 0 and $-4$
- **(C)** 0 and 4
- **(D)** 2 and 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 0 and 4**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the oxidation number of $C$ is $x$
$\mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}_{2}$
$x+2(+1)+2(-1)=0$
$x+2-2=0$
$x=0$
$\mathrm{CCl}_{4}$
$x+4(-1)=0$
$x-4=0$
$x=4$

</details>
