### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What type of oxide the $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ is?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acidic oxide
- **(B)** Basic oxide
- **(C)** Amphoteric oxide
- **(D)** Neutral oxide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Amphoteric oxide**

**Detailed Solution:**

Aluminium oxide ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$) reacts with both acids and bases to form salt and water. Therefore, it exhibits both acidic and basic properties.

Reaction with acid:
$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 6\text{HCl} \rightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$

Reaction with base:
$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 2\text{NaOH} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 2\text{Na}[\text{Al}(\text{OH})_4]$

Hence, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ is an amphoteric oxide.

Answer: Amphoteric oxide

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following oxides is amphoteric in nature?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{BaO}$
- **(B)** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_5$
- **(C)** $\text{CO}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\text{BaO}$ is an oxide of an alkaline earth metal and is basic in nature.

$\text{N}_2\text{O}_5$ and $\text{CO}_2$ are oxides of non-metals and are acidic in nature.

$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ reacts with both acids and bases to form salt and water, exhibiting both acidic and basic properties. Hence, it is an amphoteric oxide.

Reaction with acid: $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 6\text{HCl} \rightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$

Reaction with base: $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 2\text{NaOH} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 2\text{Na}[\text{Al}(\text{OH})_4]$

Answer: $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$

</details>
