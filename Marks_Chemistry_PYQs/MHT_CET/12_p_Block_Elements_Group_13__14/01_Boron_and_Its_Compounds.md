### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is bond angle $\mathrm{F}-\mathrm{B}-\mathrm{F}$ in $\mathrm{BF}_3$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $107^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $104.5^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $120^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $109.5^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $120^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In $\mathrm{BF}_3$, the central boron atom has $3$ valence electrons and forms $3$ single bonds with fluorine atoms.

There are no lone pairs on the central boron atom. The steric number is $3$ (bond pairs) $+ 0$ (lone pairs) $= 3$.

This corresponds to $sp^2$ hybridization.

The molecular geometry is trigonal planar, which results in an $\mathrm{F}-\mathrm{B}-\mathrm{F}$ bond angle of exactly $120^{\circ}$.

Answer: $120^{\circ}$
