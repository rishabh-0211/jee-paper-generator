### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from the following is NOT polymorphic form of silica ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Cristobalite
- **(B)** $\alpha$ - quartz
- **(C)** $\beta$ - quartz
- **(D)** Aragonite

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Aragonite**

**Detailed Solution:**

Silica ($SiO_2$) exists in various polymorphic forms such as quartz ($\alpha$-quartz and $\beta$-quartz), cristobalite, and tridymite.

Aragonite is a naturally occurring crystalline form of calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$), not silica.

Therefore, Aragonite is not a polymorphic form of silica.

Answer: Aragonite

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The common basic structural unit in silicates and silica is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Si}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{6}^{4-}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{SiO}_{3}^{2-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{SiO}_{4}^{4-}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Si}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{7}^{6-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{SiO}_{4}^{4-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Silica $\left(\mathrm{SiO}_{2}\right)$ and silicates contain a common structural unit $\left(\mathrm{SiO}_{4}\right)^{4-}$ in which each Si is surrounded tetrahedrally by four oxygen atoms.

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Carborundum is

**Options:**

- **(A)** SiC
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Al}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3} \cdot \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Al}_{2}\left(\mathrm{SO}_{4}\right)_{3}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{AlCl}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) SiC**

**Detailed Solution:**

When silica is heated with carbon in electric furnace, it is reduced to carborundum or silicon carbide.
$$
\mathrm{SiO}_{2}+3 \mathrm{C} \longrightarrow \mathrm{SiC}+2 \mathrm{CO}
$$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Monosilane on coming in contact with air burns with a luminous flame producing vortex rings. These rings are of

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$
- **(B)** SiO
- **(C)** Si
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{SiO}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Monosilane $\left(e g, S i H_{4}\right)$ on coming in contact with air burns with a luminous flame producing vortex ring. These rings are of silica.
$$
\mathrm{SiH}_{4}+3 \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{SiO}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}
$$

</details>
