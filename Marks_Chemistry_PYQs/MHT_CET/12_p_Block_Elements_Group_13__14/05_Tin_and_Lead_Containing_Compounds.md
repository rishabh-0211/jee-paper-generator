### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the formula of Tin (IV) oxide?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Sn}_2\text{O}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{SnO}_2$
- **(C)** $\text{SnO}_4$
- **(D)** $\text{SnO}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{SnO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The oxidation state of Tin (Sn) in Tin (IV) oxide is $+4$.

The oxidation state of Oxygen (O) in an oxide ion is $-2$.

To form a neutral compound, the total positive charge must balance the total negative charge. Two oxide ions are required to balance one Tin (IV) ion.

Using the criss-cross method, the formula is initially $\text{Sn}_2\text{O}_4$, which simplifies to the empirical formula $\text{SnO}_2$.

Answer: $\text{SnO}_2$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following compounds is most covalent?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\quad \mathrm{SbCl}_3$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{PbCl}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{SnCl}_4$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{SnCl}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{SnCl}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Fajan's rules, the covalent character of a compound increases with an increase in the charge on the cation and a decrease in the size of the cation.

The oxidation states of the central metal ions in the given compounds are:
$\mathrm{SbCl}_3$: $\mathrm{Sb}^{3+}$
$\mathrm{PbCl}_2$: $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$
$\mathrm{SnCl}_4$: $\mathrm{Sn}^{4+}$
$\mathrm{SnCl}_2$: $\mathrm{Sn}^{2+}$

Among these, $\mathrm{Sn}^{4+}$ has the highest positive charge ($+4$) and the smallest size. Therefore, it has the highest polarizing power, making $\mathrm{SnCl}_4$ the most covalent compound among the given options.

Answer: $\mathrm{SnCl}_4$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A solution contains $\mathrm{Cl}^{-}, \mathrm{I}^{-}$ and $\mathrm{SO}_{4}^{3-}$ ions in it. Which of the following ion is capable to precipitate all of the above when added in this solution?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Hg}^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$ ion is capable to precipitate $-\mathrm{Cl},-\mathrm{I}$ and $-\mathrm{SO}_{4}^{2-}$ ions.
$\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}+2 \mathrm{Cl}^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{BaCl}_{2} \downarrow$
$\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}+2 \mathrm{I}^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{BaI}_{2} \downarrow$
$\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}+\mathrm{SO}_{4}^{2-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{BaSO}_{4} \downarrow$

</details>
