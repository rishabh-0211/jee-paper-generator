### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the catalyst used to transform carbon monoxide from water gas into carbon dioxide

**Options:**

- **(A)** Potassium dichromate
- **(B)** Sodium chromate
- **(C)** Iron chromate
- **(D)** Nickel

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Iron chromate**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of carbon monoxide of water gas (syngas) with steam to form carbon dioxide and dihydrogen is called the water-gas shift reaction.

$\text{CO(g)} + \text{H}_2\text{O(g)} \xrightarrow{\text{Iron chromate, } 673\text{ K}} \text{CO}_2\text{(g)} + \text{H}_2\text{(g)}$

The catalyst used in this process is iron chromate.

Answer: Iron chromate

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the catalyst used in following reaction at $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ?
$\mathrm{CO}+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{CO}_2+\mathrm{H}_2$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Fe}-\mathrm{Cr}$
- **(B)** Ni
- **(C)** Co-Th
- **(D)** Platinised asbestos

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Fe}-\mathrm{Cr}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is the water-gas shift reaction, which is used to increase the yield of hydrogen gas from syngas.

$\mathrm{CO} + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{CO}_2 + \mathrm{H}_2$

This reaction is carried out at around $500^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ (or $673 \text{ K}$) in the presence of iron(III) oxide ($\mathrm{Fe}_2\mathrm{O}_3$) promoted by chromium(III) oxide ($\mathrm{Cr}_2\mathrm{O}_3$) as a catalyst.

Therefore, the catalyst used is $\mathrm{Fe}-\mathrm{Cr}$.

Answer: $\mathrm{Fe}-\mathrm{Cr}$

</details>
