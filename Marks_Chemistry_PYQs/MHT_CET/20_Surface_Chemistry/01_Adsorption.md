### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify false statement among the following regarding adsorption.

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is a surface phenomenon.
- **(B)** It is an exothermic process.
- **(C)** Gases having low critical temperature can be readily adsorbed by solid adsorbent.
- **(D)** Colloidal substances acts as good adsorbents.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Gases having low critical temperature can be readily adsorbed by solid adsorbent.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Adsorption is a surface phenomenon where molecules accumulate at the surface rather than in the bulk.

During adsorption, there is always a decrease in residual forces of the surface, which leads to a decrease in surface energy. This energy is released as heat, making adsorption an exothermic process.

The extent of adsorption of a gas on a solid depends on the nature of the gas. Easily liquefiable gases, which have higher critical temperatures, are adsorbed more readily because the van der Waals forces are stronger. Therefore, gases with low critical temperatures are not readily adsorbed.

Colloidal substances have a very large surface area per unit mass, which makes them excellent adsorbents.

Thus, the statement that gases having low critical temperature can be readily adsorbed is false.

Answer: Gases having low critical temperature can be readily adsorbed by solid adsorbent.

</details>

Which of the following statements is NOT True with respect to physisorption?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is reversible
- **(B)** It is multimolecular.
- **(C)** $\Delta H < 0$
- **(D)** $\Delta S > 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\Delta S > 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Physisorption involves weak van der Waals forces and is a reversible process.

It can form multimolecular layers on the adsorbent surface at higher pressures.

Adsorption is an exothermic process, meaning the enthalpy change is negative ($\Delta H < 0$).

During adsorption, the gas molecules are restricted to the surface of the solid, which decreases their randomness or entropy. Therefore, the entropy change is negative ($\Delta S < 0$).

Thus, the statement $\Delta S > 0$ is not true.

Answer: $\Delta S > 0$

</details>

- **(B)** Adsorption
- **(C)** Desorption
- **(D)** Deposition

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Sorption**

**Detailed Solution:**

The phenomenon in which both adsorption and absorption take place simultaneously is known as sorption.

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following statements is correct for the spontaneous adsorption of a gas?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\Delta S$ is negative and, therefore $\Delta H$ should be highly positive
- **(B)** $\Delta S$ is negative and therefore, $\Delta H$ should be highly negative.
- **(C)** $\Delta S$ is positive and therefore, $\Delta H$ should be negative.
- **(D)** $\Delta S$ is positive and therefore, $\Delta H$ should also be highly positive.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\Delta S$ is negative and therefore, $\Delta H$ should be highly negative.**

**Detailed Solution:**

During the adsorption of a gas, the gas molecules are restricted to the surface of the solid, which decreases their randomness or entropy. Thus, the change in entropy, $\Delta S$, is negative.

For a process to be spontaneous, the change in Gibbs free energy, $\Delta G$, must be negative.

According to the Gibbs-Helmholtz equation:
$\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$

Since $\Delta S$ is negative, the term $-T\Delta S$ becomes positive. For $\Delta G$ to be negative, the enthalpy change, $\Delta H$, must be negative and its magnitude must be large enough to overcome the positive $-T\Delta S$ term.

Therefore, $\Delta S$ is negative and $\Delta H$ should be highly negative.

Answer: $\Delta S$ is negative and therefore, $\Delta H$ should be highly negative.

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following assertions about the extent of physisorption is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Increases with increase in temperature
- **(B)** Decreases with increase in surface area
- **(C)** Decreases with increase in the strength of Van der Waals forces
- **(D)** Decreases with increase in temperature

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Decreases with increase in temperature**

**Detailed Solution:**

Physisorption is an exothermic process. According to Le Chatelier's principle, an increase in temperature shifts the equilibrium in the backward direction, leading to desorption. Thus, the extent of physisorption decreases with an increase in temperature.

Additionally, the extent of physisorption increases with an increase in the surface area of the adsorbent and with an increase in the strength of Van der Waals forces between the adsorbate and adsorbent.

Answer: Decreases with increase in temperature

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2025 (5 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is NOT a characterstic of chemisorption?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is specific.
- **(B)** Heat released is in the range of $40-200 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$.
- **(C)** Formation of multimolecular layer of adsorbate.
- **(D)** It is irreversible.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Formation of multimolecular layer of adsorbate.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Chemisorption involves the formation of chemical bonds between the adsorbate molecules and the surface of the adsorbent.

Because chemical bonds can only be formed directly with the surface molecules, chemisorption results in the formation of a unimolecular layer (monolayer) of the adsorbate.

Physisorption, which involves weak van der Waals forces, can form multimolecular layers.

Other characteristics of chemisorption include being highly specific, irreversible, and having a high heat of adsorption (typically in the range of $40-200 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$ or $80-240 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$).

Therefore, the formation of a multimolecular layer is not a characteristic of chemisorption.

Answer: Formation of multimolecular layer of adsorbate.

</details>

### Q14. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following statements is NOT true about absorption?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Concentration of absorbate is uniform throughout the bulk of the absorbent.
- **(B)** It is independent of temperature and pressure.
- **(C)** It is not accompanied by evolution or absorption of heat.
- **(D)** It depends on surface area.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) It depends on surface area.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Absorption is a bulk phenomenon where the absorbed matter is uniformly distributed throughout the bulk of the absorbent.

According to the standard differences between adsorption and absorption:
1. Absorption is a bulk phenomenon, whereas adsorption is a surface phenomenon.
2. Absorption is independent of surface area, whereas adsorption depends on the surface area of the adsorbent.
3. Absorption is generally considered independent of temperature and pressure in this context, whereas adsorption depends on them.
4. Absorption is not accompanied by the evolution or absorption of heat, whereas adsorption is an exothermic process.

Therefore, the statement "It depends on surface area" is true for adsorption but NOT true for absorption.

Answer: It depends on surface area.

</details>
