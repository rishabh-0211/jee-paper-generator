### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify sequential reactions involved in Sol-Gel process .

**Options:**

- **(A)** Poly condensation and hydorlysis
- **(B)** Hydrolysis and poly condensation
- **(C)** Poly condensation and oxidation
- **(D)** Oxidation and hydrolysis

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Hydrolysis and poly condensation**

**Detailed Solution:**

The sol-gel process is a wet-chemical technique used for the synthesis of nanomaterials, glassy, and ceramic materials.

The process involves the conversion of monomers into a colloidal solution (sol) that acts as the precursor for an integrated network (gel).

The sequential reactions involved in the sol-gel process are:
1. Hydrolysis of the precursor (usually metal alkoxides) to form hydroxyl groups.
2. Polycondensation of the resulting hydroxyl groups to form a three-dimensional network (gel) with the elimination of water or alcohol.

Therefore, the correct sequence of reactions is hydrolysis followed by polycondensation.

Answer: Hydrolysis and poly condensation

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Match column I (process) with column II (application)
$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|} \hline & \text{Column I} & & \text{Column II} \\ \hline \text{A)} & \text{Dialysis} & \text{a)} & \text{Cleansing action of soap} \\ \hline \text{B)} & \text{Peptization} & \text{b)} & \text{Coagulation} \\ \hline \text{C)} & \text{Emulsification} & \text{c)} & \text{Colloidal Solution Preparation} \\ \hline \text{D)} & \text{Electrophoresis} & \text{d)} & \text{Purification of colloidal solution} \\ \hline \end{array}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{d}, \mathrm{B}=\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{D}=\mathrm{b}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{d}, \mathrm{B}=\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{D}=\mathrm{b}$
- **(C)** $A=c, B=b, C=d, D=a$
- **(D)** $A=b, B=c, C=a, D=d$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{d}, \mathrm{B}=\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{D}=\mathrm{b}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Dialysis is a process used for the purification of colloidal solutions by removing dissolved impurities using a semipermeable membrane. Thus, A matches with d.

Peptization is the process of converting a fresh precipitate into colloidal particles by adding a suitable electrolyte. It is a method for the preparation of colloidal solutions. Thus, B matches with c.

Emulsification is the process of forming an emulsion. The cleansing action of soap is based on the emulsification of grease and dirt. Thus, C matches with a.

Electrophoresis involves the movement of colloidal particles under the influence of an electric field. When these particles reach the oppositely charged electrode, they lose their charge and undergo coagulation. Thus, D matches with b.

The correct matching is A=d, B=c, C=a, D=b.

Answer: $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{d}, \mathrm{B}=\mathrm{c}, \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{D}=\mathrm{b}$

</details>
