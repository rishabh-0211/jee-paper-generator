### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following represents the Freundlich's empirical equation for adsorption of gas on solid (for $\mathrm{n}>1$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{x}{\mathrm{~m}}=\mathrm{kp}^{\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{x}=\mathrm{kp}^{\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{x}{\mathrm{~m}}=\mathrm{kp}^{\mathrm{n}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{x}=\mathrm{kp}^{\mathrm{n}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{x}{\mathrm{~m}}=\mathrm{kp}^{\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Freundlich adsorption isotherm gives the empirical relationship between the quantity of gas adsorbed by a unit mass of solid adsorbent and pressure at a particular temperature.

The equation is expressed as:

$\dfrac{x}{m} = k p^{\dfrac{1}{n}}$

where $x$ is the mass of the gas adsorbed on mass $m$ of the adsorbent at pressure $p$, and $k$ and $n$ are constants which depend on the nature of the adsorbent and the gas at a particular temperature, with $n > 1$.

Answer: $\frac{x}{\mathrm{~m}}=\mathrm{kp}^{\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the value of slope in Freundlich adsorption isotherm $\log \frac{\mathrm{x}}{\mathrm{m}}$ against $\log \mathrm{C}$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}$
- **(B)** n
- **(C)** K
- **(D)** $\quad \log \mathrm{K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Freundlich adsorption isotherm is given by the equation:

$\dfrac{x}{m} = K C^{1/n}$

Taking the logarithm on both sides, we get:

$\log \left(\dfrac{x}{m}\right) = \log K + \dfrac{1}{n} \log C$

This equation is of the form of a straight line, $y = mx + c$, where:

$y = \log \left(\dfrac{x}{m}\right)$

$x = \log C$

Slope ($m$) = $\dfrac{1}{n}$

y-intercept ($c$) = $\log K$

Therefore, the value of the slope in the plot of $\log \left(\dfrac{x}{m}\right)$ against $\log C$ is $\dfrac{1}{n}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}}$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2007

Freundlich adsorption isotherm is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{x}{m}=k p^{1 / n}$
- **(B)** $x=m k p^{1 / n}$

- **(D)** All of these

**Detailed Solution:**

Freundlich adsorption isotherm is
