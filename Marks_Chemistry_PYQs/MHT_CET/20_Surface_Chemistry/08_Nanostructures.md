### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following types of nanostructures, the thin film is?

**Options:**

- **(A)** zero dimensional nano structure
- **(B)** one dimensional nano structure
- **(C)** two dimensional nano structure
- **(D)** three dimensional nano structure

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) two dimensional nano structure**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nanostructures are classified based on the number of dimensions that are not confined to the nanoscale.

Zero-dimensional (0D) nanostructures have all three dimensions in the nanoscale (e.g., quantum dots, nanoparticles).

One-dimensional (1D) nanostructures have two dimensions in the nanoscale and one macroscopic dimension (e.g., nanowires, nanotubes).

Two-dimensional (2D) nanostructures have one dimension in the nanoscale (thickness) and two macroscopic dimensions (length and width). Examples include thin films, graphene, and surface coatings.

Three-dimensional (3D) nanostructures have no dimensions confined to the nanoscale, but possess nanoscale features (e.g., bulk nanomaterials).

Since a thin film has only its thickness in the nanometer range while its length and width are macroscopic, it is classified as a two-dimensional nanostructure.

Answer: two dimensional nano structure

</details>

### Q11. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which instrument is used for determination of particle size?

**Options:**

- **(A)** SEM
- **(B)** TEM
- **(C)** FTIR
- **(D)** UV visible spectroscopy

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) TEM**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the analytical techniques used for characterizing nanomaterials:

Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) is used to determine the structure and morphology of the particles.

Transmission Electron Microscope (TEM) is specifically used to determine the particle size.

Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) is used to determine the absorption of functional groups and binding nature.

UV-visible spectroscopy is used for the preliminary assessment of optical properties.

Therefore, TEM is the instrument used for the determination of particle size.

Answer: TEM

</details>

- **(B)** TEM
- **(C)** XRD
- **(D)** FTIR

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) FTIR**

**Detailed Solution:**
