### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following anions has greater power for coagulation of positive sol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Cl}^-$
- **(B)** $[\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-}$
- **(C)** $\text{PO}_4^{2-}$
- **(D)** $\text{SO}_4^{2-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $[\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulating power of an ion is directly proportional to the magnitude of its charge (valency). For the coagulation of a positive sol, an anion is required.

The given anions and their charge magnitudes are:
$\text{Cl}^-$ : $1$
$\text{SO}_4^{2-}$ : $2$
$\text{PO}_4^{3-}$ (given as $\text{PO}_4^{2-}$ in options) : $3$
$[\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-}$ : $4$

Since $[\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-}$ has the highest negative charge, it possesses the maximum coagulating power among the given options.

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following exhibits the minimum coagulating power for precipitating of positively charged ferric oxide sol ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{KNO}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{K}_2\text{SO}_4$
- **(C)** $\text{K}_3\text{PO}_4$
- **(D)** $\text{K}_4\text{Fe(CN)}_6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{KNO}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulating power of an ion is directly proportional to the magnitude of its charge.

Since the ferric oxide sol is positively charged, the anions of the added electrolyte are responsible for its coagulation.

The anions provided by the given electrolytes are:
$\text{KNO}_3 \rightarrow \text{NO}_3^{-}$ (charge magnitude = $1$)
$\text{K}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow \text{SO}_4^{2-}$ (charge magnitude = $2$)
$\text{K}_3\text{PO}_4 \rightarrow \text{PO}_4^{3-}$ (charge magnitude = $3$)
$\text{K}_4[\text{Fe(CN)}_6] \rightarrow [\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-}$ (charge magnitude = $4$)

The order of coagulating power is $[\text{Fe(CN)}_6]^{4-} > \text{PO}_4^{3-} > \text{SO}_4^{2-} > \text{NO}_3^{-}$.

Thus, $\text{KNO}_3$ exhibits the minimum coagulating power.

Answer: $\text{KNO}_3$

</details>

- **(D)** Haemoglobin

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

Since $\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]^{4-}$ has the maximum negative charge, it exhibits the maximum power of coagulation for a positively charged sol.

### Q7. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following process does NOT results in coagulation?

**Options:**

- **(A)** electrophoresis
- **(B)** by adding excess solvent
- **(C)** by mixing two oppositely charged sols
- **(D)** by boiling

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) by adding excess solvent**

**Detailed Solution:**

Coagulation of a colloidal sol can be achieved by methods that neutralize the charge on the colloidal particles or disturb their stability.

Electrophoresis causes coagulation because colloidal particles move towards the oppositely charged electrode, lose their charge, and precipitate.

Boiling causes coagulation because increased collisions between particles and the dispersion medium disturb the adsorbed ion layer, reducing the charge on the particles.

Mixing two oppositely charged sols results in mutual coagulation as they neutralize each other's charge.

Adding excess solvent merely dilutes the sol and does not neutralize the charge or cause the particles to aggregate. Therefore, it does not result in coagulation.

Answer: by adding excess solvent

</details>

### Q10. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the number of electrons present in antibonding orbitals of \(\mathrm{N}_2\) molecule according to molecular orbital theory?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 14
- **(B)** 04
- **(C)** 10
- **(D)** 06

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 04**

**Detailed Solution:**

The total number of electrons in the $N_2$ molecule is $7 + 7 = 14$.

The molecular orbital electronic configuration of $N_2$ is:

$\sigma_{1s}^{2} \sigma^{*}_{1s}^{2} \sigma_{2s}^{2} \sigma^{*}_{2s}^{2} \pi_{2p_x}^{2} \pi_{2p_y}^{2} \sigma_{2p_z}^{2}$

The antibonding molecular orbitals are denoted by an asterisk ($^{*}$).

The electrons present in antibonding orbitals are in $\sigma^{*}_{1s}$ and $\sigma^{*}_{2s}$.

Number of electrons in antibonding orbitals = $2 + 2 = 4$.

Answer: 04

</details>
