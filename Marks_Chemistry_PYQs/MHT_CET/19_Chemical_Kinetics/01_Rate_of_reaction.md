### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements is NOT true for the reaction, $4A + B \rightarrow 2C + 2D$?

**Options:**

- **(A)** The rate of consumption of B is $(1/4)^{\text{th}}$ the rate of consumption of A.
- **(B)** The rate of formation of D is half the rate of consumption of A.
- **(C)** The rate of formation of C is half the rate of consumption of B.
- **(D)** The rate of formation of C and D are equal.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) The rate of formation of C is half the rate of consumption of B.**

**Detailed Solution:**

For the reaction $4A + B \rightarrow 2C + 2D$, the rate of reaction is given by:

$\text{Rate} = -\dfrac{1}{4}\dfrac{d[A]}{dt} = -\dfrac{d[B]}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{d[C]}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{d[D]}{dt}$

From this relation, we can analyze the given options:

$-\dfrac{d[B]}{dt} = \dfrac{1}{4}\left(-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}\right)$, so the rate of consumption of B is one-fourth the rate of consumption of A.

$\dfrac{d[D]}{dt} = \dfrac{2}{4}\left(-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(-\dfrac{d[A]}{dt}\right)$, so the rate of formation of D is half the rate of consumption of A.

$\dfrac{d[C]}{dt} = 2\left(-\dfrac{d[B]}{dt}\right)$, so the rate of formation of C is twice the rate of consumption of B. The statement says it is half, which is incorrect.

$\dfrac{d[C]}{dt} = \dfrac{d[D]}{dt}$, so the rates of formation of C and D are equal.

Thus, the incorrect statement is that the rate of formation of C is half the rate of consumption of B.

Answer: The rate of formation of C is half the rate of consumption of B.

</details>
