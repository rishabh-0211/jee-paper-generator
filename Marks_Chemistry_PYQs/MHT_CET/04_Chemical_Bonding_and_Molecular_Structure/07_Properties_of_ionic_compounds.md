### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify a pair of isomorphous substances from following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Diamond and Fullerene
- **(B)** $\beta$ - Quartz and Cristobalite
- **(C)** Sodium fluoride and Magnesium oxide
- **(D)** Calcite and Aragonite

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Sodium fluoride and Magnesium oxide**

**Detailed Solution:**

Isomorphous substances are those that have the same crystal structure and similar chemical composition.

Sodium fluoride (NaF) and Magnesium oxide (MgO) both crystallize in the face-centered cubic (fcc) rock salt structure. They have the same atomic ratio ($1:1$) and their constituent ions are isoelectronic ($Na^+$ and $Mg^{2+}$, $F^-$ and $O^{2-}$). Thus, they form an isomorphous pair.

Diamond and Fullerene are allotropes of carbon with different structures.

$\beta$-Quartz and Cristobalite are polymorphs of silica with different structures.

Calcite and Aragonite are polymorphs of calcium carbonate with different structures.

Answer: Sodium fluoride and Magnesium oxide

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following pair of compounds exhibits isomorphism?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Calcite and aragonite
- **(B)** $\alpha$-quartz and cristobalite
- **(C)** Sodium nitrate and calcium carbonate
- **(D)** Diamond and fullerene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Sodium nitrate and calcium carbonate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Isomorphism is the phenomenon in which two or more different substances have the same crystal structure. They typically have the same atomic ratio.

Sodium nitrate ($NaNO_3$) and calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) have the same atomic ratio ($1:1:3$) and crystallize in the same rhombohedral structure. Therefore, they exhibit isomorphism.

Calcite and aragonite are polymorphs of calcium carbonate.

$\alpha$-quartz and cristobalite are polymorphs of silica.

Diamond and fullerene are allotropes (polymorphs) of carbon.

Answer: Sodium nitrate and calcium carbonate

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Calculate radius of fourth orbit of $\mathrm{B}^{4+}$ ion.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 169.3 pm
- **(B)** 211.6 pm
- **(C)** 380.8 pm
- **(D)** 413.2 pm

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 169.3 pm**

**Detailed Solution:**

The radius of the $n$-th Bohr orbit for a hydrogen-like species is given by the formula:

$r_n = 52.9 \times \dfrac{n^2}{Z} \text{ pm}$

For the $\mathrm{B}^{4+}$ ion, the atomic number $Z = 5$ and the orbit number $n = 4$.

Substituting these values into the formula:

$r_4 = 52.9 \times \dfrac{4^2}{5} \text{ pm}$

$r_4 = 52.9 \times \dfrac{16}{5} \text{ pm}$

$r_4 = 52.9 \times 3.2 \text{ pm}$

$r_4 = 169.28 \text{ pm} \approx 169.3 \text{ pm}$

Answer: 169.3 pm

</details>
