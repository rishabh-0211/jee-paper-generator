### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is formal charge on sulphur in sulphuric acid molecule?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-2$
- **(B)** $+2$
- **(C)** $-1$
- **(D)** $0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The most stable Lewis structure of sulphuric acid ($H_2SO_4$) minimizes the formal charge by expanding the octet of the central sulphur atom. In this structure, sulphur forms two double bonds with two oxygen atoms and two single bonds with two hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups.

The formula for formal charge is:
Formal charge = (Valence electrons) - (Non-bonding electrons) - $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (Bonding electrons)

For the sulphur atom in $H_2SO_4$:
Number of valence electrons = $6$
Number of non-bonding electrons = $0$
Number of bonding electrons = $12$ (since it forms $6$ bonds)

Formal charge = $6 - 0 - \dfrac{12}{2} = 0$

Answer: $0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements regarding Pi $(\pi)$ bond is NOT true ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is weaker than sigma bond.
- **(B)** It is formed by lateral overlapping of two half filled P orbitals.
- **(C)** It is formed after the formation of sigma bond.
- **(D)** The presence of Pi $(\pi)$ bond increases the bond length of covalent bond.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) The presence of Pi $(\pi)$ bond increases the bond length of covalent bond.**

**Detailed Solution:**

A pi ($\pi$) bond is formed by the lateral or sideways overlapping of half-filled atomic orbitals (like p or d orbitals) after the formation of a sigma ($\sigma$) bond.

Because the extent of overlapping in a $\pi$ bond is less than that in a $\sigma$ bond, a $\pi$ bond is weaker than a $\sigma$ bond.

The presence of a $\pi$ bond between two atoms increases the bond order (e.g., from single to double or triple bond). An increase in bond order leads to a stronger attractive force between the atoms, which decreases the bond length. For example, the C-C single bond length is $1.54$ \AA, whereas the C=C double bond length is $1.34$ \AA.

Therefore, the statement that the presence of a $\pi$ bond increases the bond length is incorrect.

Answer: The presence of Pi $(\pi)$ bond increases the bond length of covalent bond.

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What type of bond and type of overlapping present in $\text{N}_2$ molecule ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Single bond and s$-$s overlap
- **(B)** Double bond and s$-$p overlap
- **(C)** Triple bond and p$-$p overlap
- **(D)** Double bond and p$-$p overlap

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Triple bond and p$-$p overlap**

**Detailed Solution:**

The electronic configuration of a nitrogen atom is $1s^2 2s^2 2p^3$.

Each nitrogen atom has three half-filled $2p$ orbitals ($2p_x, 2p_y, 2p_z$). To achieve a stable octet configuration, two nitrogen atoms share three pairs of electrons, forming a triple bond ($N \equiv N$).

The triple bond consists of one $\sigma$ bond and two $\pi$ bonds. The $\sigma$ bond is formed by the axial overlapping of one set of $p$ orbitals (e.g., $2p_z - 2p_z$), while the two $\pi$ bonds are formed by the lateral overlapping of the remaining two sets of $p$ orbitals (e.g., $2p_x - 2p_x$ and $2p_y - 2p_y$).

Since all the orbitals involved in the bond formation are $p$ orbitals, the overlapping is of the p$-$p type.

Therefore, the $N_2$ molecule contains a triple bond and exhibits p$-$p overlap.

Answer: Triple bond and p$-$p overlap

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the different types of bonds present in quaternary ammonium salts.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Covalent
- **(B)** Ionic
- **(C)** Covalent and co-ordinate
- **(D)** covalent, co-ordinate and ionic

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) covalent, co-ordinate and ionic**

**Detailed Solution:**

Quaternary ammonium salts have the general formula $R_4N^+ X^-$.

The bonds present within the alkyl or aryl groups ($R$) and three of the nitrogen-carbon bonds are covalent.

The fourth nitrogen-carbon bond is formed by the donation of the lone pair of electrons from the nitrogen atom to an electron-deficient carbon atom, which is a co-ordinate (dative) bond.

The electrostatic force of attraction between the complex quaternary ammonium cation ($R_4N^+$) and the counter anion ($X^-$) constitutes an ionic bond.

Thus, quaternary ammonium salts contain covalent, co-ordinate, and ionic bonds.

Answer: covalent, co-ordinate and ionic

</details>

Which of the following molecules does not obey octet rule?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CO}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CHCl}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{ClF}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NH}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Detailed Solution:**

What different types of bonds are formed by chlorine with oxygen in perchloric acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2-single bonds and 2-double bonds
- **(B)** 3 -single bonds and 1-double bond
- **(C)** 2-single bonds and 3-double bonds
- **(D)** 1 -single bond and 3-double bonds

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 1 -single bond and 3-double bonds**

**Detailed Solution:**

In its structure, the central chlorine atom is bonded to four oxygen atoms. One of the oxygen atoms is further bonded to a hydrogen atom, forming a hydroxyl ($-OH$) group. This oxygen is attached to the chlorine atom by a single bond ($Cl-O$).

### Q13. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

What is the number of Lewis structures for $\mathrm{NO}_2^{-}$?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

The total number of valence electrons in $\mathrm{NO}_2^{-}$ is $5 \text{ (from N)} + 2 \times 6 \text{ (from O)} + 1 \text{ (from negative charge)} = 18$ electrons.

The skeletal structure is $\text{O}-\text{N}-\text{O}$. Placing a single bond between N and each O uses 4 electrons, leaving 14 electrons.

Distributing the remaining electrons to complete the octets of the oxygen atoms requires 12 electrons (3 lone pairs on each O). The remaining 2 electrons are placed as a lone pair on the central nitrogen atom.

At this point, the nitrogen atom has only 6 electrons in its valence shell. To complete the octet of nitrogen, one lone pair from an oxygen atom is shared to form a double bond.

Since there are two equivalent oxygen atoms, the double bond can be formed with either of them. This results in two resonance structures:

$[\text{O}=\text{N}-\text{O}]^{-}$ and $[\text{O}-\text{N}=\text{O}]^{-}$

Thus, there are 2 Lewis structures for $\mathrm{NO}_2^{-}$.

Answer: 2

</details>
