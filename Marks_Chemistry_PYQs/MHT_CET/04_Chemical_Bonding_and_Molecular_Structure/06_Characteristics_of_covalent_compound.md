### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following ethers is gaseous at room temperature ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1 - Methoxypropane
- **(B)** 1 - Ethoxypropane
- **(C)** Methoxyethane
- **(D)** Ethoxyethane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Methoxyethane**

**Detailed Solution:**

Dimethyl ether and ethyl methyl ether (methoxyethane) are gases at room temperature.

Other lower homologues of ethers, such as diethyl ether (ethoxyethane), 1-methoxypropane, and 1-ethoxypropane, are colourless, highly volatile liquids at room temperature.

Therefore, methoxyethane is gaseous at room temperature.

Answer: Methoxyethane

</details>
