### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements is correct about temperature and internal energy ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both are extensive properties of the system.
- **(B)** Both are the intensive properties of the system.
- **(C)** Intensive and extensive properties of the system respectively.
- **(D)** Extensive and intensive properties of the system respectively.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Intensive and extensive properties of the system respectively.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Intensive properties are those which do not depend on the mass or size of the system. Temperature is independent of the amount of substance present, so it is an intensive property.

Extensive properties are those which depend on the mass or size of the system. Internal energy is directly proportional to the amount of substance, so it is an extensive property.

Therefore, temperature and internal energy are intensive and extensive properties of the system respectively.

Answer: Intensive and extensive properties of the system respectively.

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is NOT the state function?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Gibbs energy
- **(B)** Temperature
- **(C)** Heat
- **(D)** Heat transfer at constant volume ($Q_v$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Heat**

**Detailed Solution:**

State functions are properties whose values depend only on the initial and final states of the system and not on the path taken.

Gibbs energy ($G$) and temperature ($T$) are state functions.

Heat transfer at constant volume ($Q_v$) is equal to the change in internal energy ($\Delta U$), which is a state function.

Heat ($Q$) and work ($W$) are path functions because their values depend on the path followed during a process.

Therefore, heat is not a state function.

Answer: Heat

</details>

- **(B)** Pressure
- **(C)** Work
- **(D)** Temperature

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Work**

**Detailed Solution:**

State functions are properties whose values depend only on the state of the system and are independent of the path followed to reach that state.

Pressure, volume, and temperature are state functions because they depend only on the initial and final states of the system.

Work and heat are path functions because their values depend on the specific path taken to go from the initial state to the final state.

Therefore, work is not a state function.

Answer: Work

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following properties of a system depends upon the amount of matter and path?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Heat
- **(B)** Free energy
- **(C)** Enthalpy
- **(D)** Entropy

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Heat**

**Detailed Solution:**

Properties that depend on the amount of matter present in the system are known as extensive properties.

Properties whose values depend on the specific path taken to go from the initial state to the final state are known as path functions.

Free energy, enthalpy, and entropy are state functions, meaning their values depend only on the initial and final states of the system and are independent of the path followed.

Heat is a path function because the amount of heat exchanged depends on the process or path taken. It also depends on the amount of matter (extensive in nature), as the heat required to change the state of a system is proportional to its mass or number of moles ($q = mc\Delta T$).

Answer: Heat

</details>
