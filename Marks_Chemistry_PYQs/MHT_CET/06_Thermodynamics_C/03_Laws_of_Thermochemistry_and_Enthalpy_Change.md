### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Find the entropy change of the surrounding if the standard enthalpy of formation of a substance is $-447$ kJ mol$^{-1}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1500$ J K$^{-1}$
- **(B)** $-1500$ J K$^{-1}$
- **(C)** $1000$ J K$^{-1}$
- **(D)** $-1000$ J K$^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $1500$ J K$^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The standard enthalpy of formation of the substance is given as $\Delta H_{sys} = -447$ kJ mol$^{-1} = -447000$ J mol$^{-1}$.

The standard temperature is $T = 298$ K.

The heat absorbed by the surrounding is equal to the heat released by the system:

$q_{surr} = -\Delta H_{sys} = -(-447000) = 447000$ J mol$^{-1}$

The entropy change of the surrounding is given by:

$\Delta S_{surr} = \dfrac{q_{surr}}{T}$

$\Delta S_{surr} = \dfrac{447000}{298} = 1500$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$

Answer: $1500$ J K$^{-1}$

</details>

### Q54. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 2)

Which of the following is true for the value of $\Delta H - \Delta U$ for an isothermal process of an ideal gas?

- **(D)** $\dfrac{5}{2} R$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Zero**

For an ideal gas, enthalpy $H$ and internal energy $U$ are functions of temperature only.

This gives $\Delta H = \Delta U$, which implies $\Delta H - \Delta U = 0$.
