### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of following reactions occur at zone of reduction in blast furnace for extraction of iron?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{C} + \dfrac{1}{2}\text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_{(g)}$
- **(B)** $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + 3\text{CO} \rightarrow 2\text{Fe} + 3\text{CO}_2$
- **(C)** $\text{CaCO}_3 \text{ (heat)} \rightarrow \text{CaO} + \text{CO}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{CaO} + \text{SiO}_2 \rightarrow \text{CaSiO}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + 3\text{CO} \rightarrow 2\text{Fe} + 3\text{CO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In the blast furnace for the extraction of iron, different reactions take place in different temperature zones.

The zone of reduction is located in the upper part of the furnace where the temperature is relatively low, around $500 - 800 \text{ K}$. In this zone, iron oxides are reduced by carbon monoxide ($\text{CO}$) to form spongy iron.

The primary reduction reaction is:
$\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + 3\text{CO} \rightarrow 2\text{Fe} + 3\text{CO}_2$

The other reactions occur in different zones:
$\text{C} + \dfrac{1}{2}\text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}$ occurs in the zone of combustion (lower part).
$\text{CaCO}_3 \rightarrow \text{CaO} + \text{CO}_2$ and $\text{CaO} + \text{SiO}_2 \rightarrow \text{CaSiO}_3$ occur in the zone of slag formation (middle part).

Answer: $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + 3\text{CO} \rightarrow 2\text{Fe} + 3\text{CO}_2$

</details>
