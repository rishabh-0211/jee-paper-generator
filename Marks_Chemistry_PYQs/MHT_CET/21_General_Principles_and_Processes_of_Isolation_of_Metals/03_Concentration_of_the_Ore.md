### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (16 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following ore is concentrated by froth floatation process?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Diaspore
- **(B)** Bauxite
- **(C)** Dolomite
- **(D)** Galena

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Galena**

**Detailed Solution:**

Galena
Galena is also known as the lead glance. It is present in the form of Lead (II) sulphide, PbS.
As the galena ore contains sulphide group, therefore galena is best concentrated by the froth flotation method. Galena is the correct answer.

</details>
