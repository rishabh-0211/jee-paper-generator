### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which following compound acts as a flux in extraction of copper from copper pyrites

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CaSiO}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{FeO}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{FeSiO}_{3}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Copper is extracted from the ore copper pyrite by smelting in a blast furnace.
The flux used is $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$ and slag formed is $\mathrm{FeSiO}_{3}$.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2020 (13 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among following processes occurs at $1500 \mathrm{~K}$ in blast furnace for extraction of iron?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Reduction of ore
- **(B)** Ore loses moisture
- **(C)** Combustion of coke
- **(D)** Slag formation

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Slag formation**

**Detailed Solution:**

The slag formed in the extraction of iron from haematite in the blast furnace is $\mathrm{CaSiO}_{3}$. The reaction in the blast furnace is as follows:
$\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{SiO}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{CaSiO}_{3} \text { (slag) }$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2019 (Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Limestone is used as a flux in the extraction of

**Options:**

- **(A)** Iron
- **(B)** Aluminium
- **(C)** Zinc
- **(D)** Copper

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Iron**

**Detailed Solution:**

Limestone is used as a flux in the extraction of iron because it decomposes in hot furnace to form calcium oxide and carbon dioxide. The formed calcium oxide actually acts as flux and combined with silica to form fusible calcium silicate slag.

</details>
