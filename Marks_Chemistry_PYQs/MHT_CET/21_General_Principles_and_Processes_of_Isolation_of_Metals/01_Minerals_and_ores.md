### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following is a mineral of copper?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Chalcopyrite
- **(B)** Zincite
- **(C)** Limonite
- **(D)** Siderite

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Chalcopyrite**

**Detailed Solution:**

Chalcopyrite has the chemical formula $\text{CuFeS}_2$ and is a principal mineral of copper.

Zincite is a mineral of zinc ($\text{ZnO}$).

Limonite is a mineral of iron ($\text{FeO(OH)} \cdot n\text{H}_2\text{O}$).

Siderite is a mineral of iron ($\text{FeCO}_3$).

Answer: Chalcopyrite

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following minerals contains radioactive element in it?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Cleveite
- **(B)** Carnallite
- **(C)** Chile salt petre
- **(D)** Baryte

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Cleveite**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cleveite is an impure variety of uraninite, which is a radioactive mineral. It primarily contains uranium, which is a radioactive element, along with lead, thorium, and rare earth elements. It is also known for being a source of helium gas on Earth, which is produced by the alpha decay of uranium.

Carnallite is a potassium magnesium chloride mineral ($KMgCl_3 \cdot 6H_2O$).

Chile salt petre is sodium nitrate ($NaNO_3$).

Baryte is barium sulfate ($BaSO_4$).

Therefore, Cleveite is the mineral that contains a radioactive element.

Answer: Cleveite

</details>
