### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following elements does not form its hydroxide and Dihydrogen with water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Barium
- **(B)** Calcium
- **(C)** Magnesium
- **(D)** Beryllium

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Beryllium**

**Detailed Solution:**

Beryllium does not react with water or steam even at high temperatures due to its high ionization enthalpy and small size.

Other alkaline earth metals like Magnesium, Calcium, Strontium, and Barium react with water to form their respective hydroxides and liberate dihydrogen gas. Magnesium reacts with hot water, while Calcium, Strontium, and Barium react with cold water.

Therefore, Beryllium is the element that does not form its hydroxide and dihydrogen with water.

Answer: Beryllium

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

Which of the following elements is used as a moderator in nuclear reactors ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Mg
- **(B)** Be
- **(C)** Ba
- **(D)** Sr

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Be**

**Detailed Solution:**

A moderator in a nuclear reactor is a substance that slows down fast neutrons to thermal energies, making them more likely to cause fission in uranium-235. Good moderators must have low atomic mass and a low tendency to absorb neutrons.

Commonly used moderators include ordinary water, heavy water, graphite, and beryllium (Be). Beryllium is highly effective as a moderator and reflector due to its low neutron absorption cross-section and high scattering cross-section.

Therefore, among the given options, Beryllium (Be) is used as a moderator in nuclear reactors.

Answer: Be

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which alkaline earth metal carbonate decomposes most easily?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{MgCO}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{CaCO}_3$
- **(C)** $\text{SrCO}_3$
- **(D)** $\text{BaCO}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{MgCO}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The thermal stability of alkaline earth metal carbonates increases down the group from $Be$ to $Ba$.

This is because as the size of the cation increases, its polarizing power decreases. A smaller cation like $Mg^{2+}$ polarizes the large carbonate ion ($CO_3^{2-}$) more effectively, weakening the $C-O$ bond and facilitating its decomposition into the metal oxide and $CO_2$.

The order of thermal stability is $MgCO_3 < CaCO_3 < SrCO_3 < BaCO_3$.

Therefore, $MgCO_3$ has the lowest thermal stability among the given options and decomposes most easily.

Answer: $\text{MgCO}_3$

</details>

Identify the product when magnesium burns in air.

**Options:**

- **(A)** MgO only
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Mg}_3 \mathrm{~N}_2$ only
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Mg}_2 \mathrm{NO}_3$
- **(D)** MgO and $\mathrm{Mg}_3 \mathrm{~N}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) MgO and $\mathrm{Mg}_3 \mathrm{~N}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Air is a mixture containing mainly nitrogen and oxygen.

When magnesium burns in air, it reacts with both oxygen and nitrogen to form magnesium oxide and magnesium nitride, respectively.

Therefore, the products formed are $\mathrm{MgO}$ and $\mathrm{Mg}_3\mathrm{N}_2$.

Answer: MgO and $\mathrm{Mg}_3 \mathrm{~N}_2$

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What products are obtained when beryllium oxide is treated separately with aq. HCl and aq. NaOH solutions respectively?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Be}(\mathrm{OH})_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{BeO}_4$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Be}(\mathrm{OH})_2$ and $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{BeO}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{BeO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Beryllium oxide ($\mathrm{BeO}$) is an amphoteric oxide, meaning it reacts with both acids and bases.

Reaction with aqueous $\mathrm{HCl}$ (acid):
$\mathrm{BeO} + 2\mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow \mathrm{BeCl}_2 + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$
The product is beryllium chloride ($\mathrm{BeCl}_2$).

Reaction with aqueous $\mathrm{NaOH}$ (base):
$\mathrm{BeO} + 2\mathrm{NaOH} \rightarrow \mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{BeO}_2 + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$
The product is sodium beryllate ($\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{BeO}_2$).

Thus, the products obtained are $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{BeO}_2$.

Answer: $\mathrm{BeCl}_2$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{BeO}_2$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the correct statement for following reaction.
$3 \mathrm{Mg}+\mathrm{N}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{Mg}_3 \mathrm{~N}_2$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Mg is reduced
- **(B)** Mg is oxidised
- **(C)** $\mathrm{N}_2$ is oxidised
- **(D)** oxidation state of Mg changes from +2 to zero

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Mg is oxidised**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is $3 \mathrm{Mg} + \mathrm{N}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{Mg}_3\mathrm{N}_2$.

The oxidation state of elemental Mg is $0$.

In $\mathrm{Mg}_3\mathrm{N}_2$, the oxidation state of Mg is $+2$.

Since the oxidation state of Mg increases from $0$ to $+2$, Mg undergoes oxidation.

The oxidation state of elemental $\mathrm{N}_2$ is $0$.

In $\mathrm{Mg}_3\mathrm{N}_2$, the oxidation state of N is $-3$.

Since the oxidation state of N decreases from $0$ to $-3$, $\mathrm{N}_2$ undergoes reduction.

Thus, Mg is oxidised.

Answer: Mg is oxidised

</details>

### Q11. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 2)

Which compound is used in medicine as barium meal for intestinal x-ray?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Barium chloride
- **(B)** Barium sulphate
- **(C)** Barium sulphide
- **(D)** Barium nitrate

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Barium sulphate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Barium sulphate ($BaSO_4$) is used in medicine as a barium meal for intestinal X-rays.

Other barium compounds like barium chloride, barium sulphide, and barium nitrate are soluble in water and are highly toxic.
