### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which compound is used in medicine as barium meal for intestinal x-ray?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Barium chloride
- **(B)** Barium sulphate
- **(C)** Barium sulphide
- **(D)** Barium nitrate

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Barium sulphate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Barium sulphate ($BaSO_4$) is used in medicine as a barium meal for intestinal X-rays. It is highly insoluble in water and gastric juices, which prevents the toxic barium ions from being absorbed into the bloodstream. Its high atomic number makes it an excellent radiocontrast agent that absorbs X-rays, providing clear imaging of the gastrointestinal tract.

Answer: Barium sulphate

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds is formed when chlorine reacts with dry slake lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{CaOCl}_2$
- **(B)** $\text{CaO}_2\text{Cl}_2$
- **(C)** $\text{CaOCl}$
- **(D)** $\text{CaCl}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{CaOCl}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

When chlorine gas is passed over dry slaked lime $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$, bleaching powder is formed.

The chemical reaction is given by:

$\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + \text{Cl}_2 \rightarrow \text{CaOCl}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$

The compound formed is $\text{CaOCl}_2$.

Answer: $\text{CaOCl}_2$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compound is used for preparation of quicklime?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Caustic soda
- **(B)** Sodium carbonate
- **(C)** Calcium carbonate
- **(D)** Magnesium carbonate

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Calcium carbonate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Quicklime is calcium oxide, $CaO$.

It is prepared commercially by heating limestone, which is calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$), in a rotary kiln at $1070-1270\text{ K}$.

The reaction is:
$CaCO_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaO + CO_2$

Therefore, calcium carbonate is used for the preparation of quicklime.

Answer: Calcium carbonate

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following compounds is recovered in solvay's process when $\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{Cl}$ is treated with slaked lime?

**Options:**

- **(A)** NaCl
- **(B)** $\quad \mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{HCO}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NH}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CO}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{NH}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In Solvay's process, ammonia is recovered from the byproduct ammonium chloride by treating it with slaked lime, $\mathrm{Ca(OH)}_2$.

The reaction involved is:

$2\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl} + \mathrm{Ca(OH)}_2 \rightarrow 2\mathrm{NH}_3 + \mathrm{CaCl}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$

Thus, the compound recovered is $\mathrm{NH}_3$.

Answer: $\mathrm{NH}_3$

</details>
