### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Choose a false statement regarding the properties of sodium hydroxide.

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is highly water soluble.
- **(B)** It absorbs atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ to form $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$.
- **(C)** the solution of NaOH turns blue litmus red.
- **(D)** NaOH is a white deliquescent solid.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) the solution of NaOH turns blue litmus red.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sodium hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$) is a strong base. Basic solutions turn red litmus paper blue. Therefore, the statement that the solution of $\text{NaOH}$ turns blue litmus red is false.

The other statements are true:
$\text{NaOH}$ is highly soluble in water.
It reacts with atmospheric $\text{CO}_2$ to form sodium carbonate ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$).
It is a white, deliquescent solid that readily absorbs moisture from the air.

Answer: the solution of NaOH turns blue litmus red.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following compounds is obtained as by product in synthesis of sodium carbonate by Solvay process?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ammonium carbonate
- **(B)** Sodium bicarbonate
- **(C)** Calcium Chloride
- **(D)** Ammonium Chloride.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Calcium Chloride**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Solvay process is used for the industrial preparation of sodium carbonate. The overall reaction for the process can be represented as:

$2NaCl + CaCO_3 \rightarrow Na_2CO_3 + CaCl_2$

During the process, sodium bicarbonate precipitates out and ammonium chloride is formed in the solution:

$NH_3 + H_2O + CO_2 + NaCl \rightarrow NaHCO_3 + NH_4Cl$

Ammonia is recovered for reuse by treating the ammonium chloride solution with calcium hydroxide:

$2NH_4Cl + Ca(OH)_2 \rightarrow 2NH_3 + CaCl_2 + 2H_2O$

In this recovery step, calcium chloride is formed. Since it is not reused in the process, it is obtained as the final byproduct.

Answer: Calcium Chloride

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{LiCl}$
- **(B)** KCl
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CsCl}$
- **(D)** NaCl

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{LiCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Lithium is smallest in size among the alkali metals. Hence $\mathrm{Li}^{+}$ion can polarize water molecules more easily than other alkali metals. As the size of the ions increases, their polarizing power decreases. Hence, other alkali metal ions usually form anhydrous salts.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Zn}(\mathrm{OH})_{2}$

- **(C)** $\mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{ZnO}_{2}$
- **(D)** None of the above

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{ZnO}_{2}$**

When $\mathrm{Zn}$ is reacted with excess of $\mathrm{NaOH}$, sodium zincate is obtained.
$$
\mathrm{Zn}+2 \mathrm{NaOH}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Na}_{2}\left[\mathrm{Zn}(\mathrm{OH})_{4}\right]+\mathrm{H}_{2}
$$

Or
$$
\mathrm{Zn}+2 \mathrm{NaOH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{ZnO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2}
