### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which element from group $2$ of periodic table resembles with lithium for different properties ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ca
- **(B)** Be
- **(C)** Ba
- **(D)** Mg

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Mg**

**Detailed Solution:**

Lithium (Group $1$, Period $2$) shows a diagonal relationship with magnesium (Group $2$, Period $3$) in the periodic table.

This resemblance is due to their similar atomic and ionic radii, as well as their similar polarizing power (charge-to-size ratio).

Because of this diagonal relationship, lithium and magnesium share several similar chemical properties, such as forming normal oxides, reacting directly with nitrogen to form nitrides, and having carbonates that decompose on heating.

Answer: Mg

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is an amphoteric hydroxide?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$
- **(B)** $\text{Be(OH)}_2$
- **(C)** $\text{Sr(OH)}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{Be(OH)}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Beryllium hydroxide, $\text{Be(OH)}_2$, is amphoteric in nature because it reacts with both acids and bases.

$\text{Be(OH)}_2 + 2\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{BeCl}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$

$\text{Be(OH)}_2 + 2\text{NaOH} \rightarrow \text{Na}_2[\text{Be(OH)}_4]$

The hydroxides of other alkaline earth metals ($\text{Mg}$, $\text{Ca}$, $\text{Sr}$, $\text{Ba}$) are basic in nature.

Answer: $\text{Be(OH)}_2$

</details>

### Q15. MHT CET 2019 (Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The diagonal relationship in Be and Al is due to

**Options:**

- **(A)** Similar ionisation enthalpy
- **(B)** Similar metallic character
- **(C)** Similar ionic size and charge/radius ratio
- **(D)** Anthraquinone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Similar ionic size and charge/radius ratio**

**Detailed Solution:**

The diagonal relationship in Be and Al is due to similar ionic size and charge/radius ratio. Generally, on moving from left to right across a period the ionic charge/radius ratio. Period, the ionic charge increase to maximum and then decreases, while the ionic size decreases causing an increase in its polarising power (Fajan’s). on the other hand, on moving down a group the ionic charge remains the same while ionic size increases. Therefore, polarising power decreases. On moving diagonally, therefore, there is no marked change in their properties.

</details>
