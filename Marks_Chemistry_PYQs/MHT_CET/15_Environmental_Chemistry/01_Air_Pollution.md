### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (5 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following gases causes the depletion of ozone layer in upper atmosphere?

**Options:**

- **(A)** NO
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NO}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{N}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{N}_2 \mathrm{O}_5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) NO**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nitric oxide (NO) released from the exhausts of supersonic transport aeroplanes causes depletion of the ozone layer in the upper atmosphere.

The reaction involved is:

$\mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O}_3 \rightarrow \mathrm{NO}_2 + \mathrm{O}_2$

Thus, NO is responsible for the depletion of the ozone layer.

Answer: NO

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following gases causes depletion in ozone layer?

**Options:**

- **(A)** He
- **(B)** NO
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CO}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) NO**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nitric oxide ($\mathrm{NO}$) is a reactive gas that contributes to the depletion of the ozone layer in the stratosphere. It reacts with ozone ($\mathrm{O}_3$) to form nitrogen dioxide ($\mathrm{NO}_2$) and diatomic oxygen ($\mathrm{O}_2$).

The reaction is given by:
$\mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O}_3 \rightarrow \mathrm{NO}_2 + \mathrm{O}_2$

Helium ($\mathrm{He}$), carbon dioxide ($\mathrm{CO}_2$), and hydrogen ($\mathrm{H}_2$) do not directly cause ozone layer depletion.

Answer: NO

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is one of the products of ozonolysis of alkenes in the atmosphere?

**Options:**

- **(A)** alcohol
- **(B)** alkane
- **(C)** aldehyde
- **(D)** ester

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) aldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ozonolysis of alkenes involves the cleavage of the carbon-carbon double bond by ozone.

The reaction produces an ozonide intermediate, which subsequently decomposes to form carbonyl compounds.

Depending on the structure of the original alkene, the products formed are either aldehydes or ketones.

Therefore, an aldehyde is one of the products of the ozonolysis of alkenes in the atmosphere.

Answer: aldehyde

</details>
