### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is NOT a role of green chemistry?

**Options:**

- **(A)** To promote innovative chemical technologies not having pollution.
- **(B)** To eliminate the use of hazardous chemicals.
- **(C)** To protect the ozone layer.
- **(D)** Use of volatile solvents like $\text{CCl}_4$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Use of volatile solvents like $\text{CCl}_4$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Green chemistry is a way of thinking and is about utilizing the existing knowledge and principles of chemistry and other sciences to reduce the adverse impact on the environment.

One of the principles of green chemistry is the use of safer solvents and auxiliaries. The use of volatile, toxic, and environmentally harmful solvents like $\text{CCl}_4$ is discouraged in green chemistry because they can cause pollution and deplete the ozone layer.

Therefore, the use of volatile solvents like $\text{CCl}_4$ is not a role of green chemistry.

Answer: Use of volatile solvents like $\text{CCl}_4$.

</details>

Find principle of green chemistry to show protection of selective group is not advantageous.

- **(C)** Use of catalysis
- **(D)** Design for degradation

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Reduce number of derivatives**

**Detailed Solution:**

### Q12. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

What is the percentage atom economy when formula weight of product obtained is 70 u and the sum of formula weight of reactant is 140 u ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $30 \%$
- **(B)** $35 \%$
- **(C)** $50 \%$
- **(D)** $75 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $50 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The percentage atom economy is calculated using the formula:

$\% \text{ atom economy} = \dfrac{\text{Formula weight of the desired product}}{\text{Sum of formula weights of all reactants}} \times 100$

Substituting the given values:

$\% \text{ atom economy} = \dfrac{70}{140} \times 100$

$\% \text{ atom economy} = 50 \%$

Answer: $50 \%$

</details>

### Q13. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which from following reactions exhibits good atom economy according to the principles of green chemistry?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{H}_9 \mathrm{OH}+\mathrm{NaBr}+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4 \longrightarrow \\ & \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{H}_9 \mathrm{Br}+\mathrm{NaHSO}_4+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}\end{aligned}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_4+\mathrm{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Ni}} \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_6$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{OH}+\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{COOH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{COOC}_2 \mathrm{H}_5+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(D)** $\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{H}_9 \mathrm{OH}+\mathrm{SOCl}_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{H}_9 \mathrm{Cl}+\mathrm{HCl}+ \\ & \mathrm{SO}_2\end{aligned}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_4+\mathrm{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Ni}} \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Atom economy is a measure of the proportion of reactant atoms that become part of the desired product in a chemical reaction. It is calculated as the ratio of the molecular mass of the desired product to the sum of the molecular masses of all reactants, multiplied by $100$.

A reaction with $100\%$ atom economy incorporates all the atoms from the reactants into the final product, producing no byproducts. Addition reactions typically have $100\%$ atom economy.

In the reaction $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_4+\mathrm{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Ni}} \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_6$, all the reactant atoms are converted into the single product $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_6$. There are no byproducts formed. Therefore, this reaction exhibits $100\%$ atom economy.

The other given reactions produce byproducts such as $\mathrm{NaHSO}_4$, $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$, $\mathrm{HCl}$, and $\mathrm{SO}_2$, which lowers their atom economy.

Answer: $\mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_4+\mathrm{H}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Ni}} \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{H}_6$

</details>
