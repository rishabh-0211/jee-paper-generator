### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify one dimensional nanostructure from following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Nanowires
- **(B)** Nanoparticles
- **(C)** Thin films
- **(D)** Quantum dots

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Nanowires**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nanomaterials are classified based on the number of dimensions that are not confined to the nanoscale range ($<100$ nm).

Zero-dimensional (0D) nanostructures have all three dimensions in the nanoscale. Examples include nanoparticles and quantum dots.

One-dimensional (1D) nanostructures have two dimensions in the nanoscale and one dimension outside the nanoscale. Examples include nanowires, nanotubes, and nanorods.

Two-dimensional (2D) nanostructures have one dimension in the nanoscale and two dimensions outside the nanoscale. Examples include thin films and graphene.

Therefore, nanowires are one-dimensional nanostructures.

Answer: Nanowires

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the instrument used to find the structure of surface of material.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Scanning electron microscope
- **(B)** X ray diffractometer
- **(C)** Transmission electron microscope
- **(D)** UV-visible spectrophotometer

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Scanning electron microscope**

**Detailed Solution:**

A Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) produces images of a sample by scanning the surface with a focused beam of electrons. The electrons interact with atoms in the sample, producing various signals that contain information about the surface topography and composition of the material.

X-ray diffractometer is used to determine the crystal structure of a material.

Transmission electron microscope is used to view the internal structure of thin specimens.

UV-visible spectrophotometer is used to measure the absorbance or transmittance of light by a sample.

Answer: Scanning electron microscope

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following nanomaterials has two dimensions less than 100 nm ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Nano wires
- **(B)** Microcapsules
- **(C)** Quantum dots
- **(D)** Nanorings

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Nano wires**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nanomaterials are classified based on the number of dimensions that are in the nanoscale range (typically $1$ to $100$ nm).

Zero-dimensional (0D) nanomaterials have all three dimensions in the nanoscale. Examples include quantum dots and nanoparticles.

One-dimensional (1D) nanomaterials have two dimensions in the nanoscale and one dimension outside the nanoscale. Examples include nanowires, nanotubes, and nanorods.

Two-dimensional (2D) nanomaterials have one dimension in the nanoscale and two dimensions outside the nanoscale. Examples include thin films and graphene.

Since nanowires are 1D nanomaterials, they have exactly two dimensions less than $100$ nm.

Answer: Nano wires

</details>
