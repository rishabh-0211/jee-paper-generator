### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is effectively used instead of DDT?

**Options:**

- **(A)** BHC
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CHCl}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2$
- **(D)** Picric acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) BHC**

**Detailed Solution:**

BHC (Benzene hexachloride), also known as Gammexane or Lindane, is a powerful insecticide.

Due to the adverse environmental effects of DDT, BHC is effectively used as a substitute for DDT in agriculture for pest control.

$\mathrm{CHCl}_3$ (Chloroform) is used as a solvent and was formerly used as an anesthetic.

$\mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2$ (Dichloromethane) is used as a solvent and paint remover.

Picric acid is used in explosives and as a dye.

Answer: BHC

</details>
