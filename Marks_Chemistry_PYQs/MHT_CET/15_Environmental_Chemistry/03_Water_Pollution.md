### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following solvents reduces the environmental pollution?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Water
- **(B)** Dichloromethane
- **(C)** Trichloromethane
- **(D)** Tetrachloromethane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Water**

**Detailed Solution:**

Green chemistry principles advocate the use of safer and environmentally friendly solvents to reduce pollution.

Water is considered a green solvent because it is non-toxic, non-flammable, abundant, and does not contribute to environmental pollution.

On the other hand, dichloromethane, trichloromethane, and tetrachloromethane are chlorinated organic solvents. They are highly volatile, toxic, and contribute significantly to environmental pollution, including ozone layer depletion and groundwater contamination.

Therefore, water is the solvent that reduces environmental pollution.

Answer: Water

</details>
