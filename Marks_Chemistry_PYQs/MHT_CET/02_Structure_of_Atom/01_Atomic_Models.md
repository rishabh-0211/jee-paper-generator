Which of the following statements is NOT true about Rutherford atomic model?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Each atom consists of massive, +vely charged centre.
- **(B)** The electrons are revolving continuously around the nucleus.
- **(C)** This model does not describe the distribution of electrons around the nucleus.
- **(D)** This model describes the energies of electrons.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) This model describes the energies of electrons.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Bohr's atomic model describes the energies of electrons.

### Q2. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following travels with the speed of light ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\alpha$ -rays
- **(B)** $\beta$ -rays
- **(C)** $\gamma$ -rays
- **(D)** X-rays

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\gamma$ -rays**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since $\gamma$ rays have zero charge and zero mass, these travel with the speed of light.

</details>
