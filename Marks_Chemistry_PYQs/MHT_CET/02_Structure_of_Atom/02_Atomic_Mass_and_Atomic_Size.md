### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The number of protons, electrons and neutrons in $\text{P}^{3-}$ ion are, respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $15, 15$ and $16$.
- **(B)** $16, 15$ and $15$.
- **(C)** $15, 18$ and $16$.
- **(D)** $16, 15$ and $18$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $15, 18$ and $16$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The atomic number ($Z$) of phosphorus ($\text{P}$) is $15$ and its mass number ($A$) is $31$.

Number of protons = $Z = 15$

For the $\text{P}^{3-}$ ion, the atom has gained $3$ electrons. Thus, the number of electrons = $15 + 3 = 18$

Number of neutrons = $A - Z = 31 - 15 = 16$

Therefore, the number of protons, electrons, and neutrons are $15$, $18$, and $16$, respectively.

Answer: $15, 18$ and $16$.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Calculate the total number of protons, neutrons and electrons in $1$ mole of $^{20}\text{Ne}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6.022 \times 10^{23}$
- **(B)** $1.8066 \times 10^{25}$
- **(C)** $6.022 \times 10^{24}$
- **(D)** $1.2044 \times 10^{25}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $1.8066 \times 10^{25}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For $^{20}\text{Ne}$, the atomic number is $10$ and the mass number is $20$.

Number of protons $= 10$
Number of electrons $= 10$
Number of neutrons $= 20 - 10 = 10$

Total number of protons, neutrons, and electrons in one atom of $^{20}\text{Ne} = 10 + 10 + 10 = 30$

Number of atoms in $1$ mole of $^{20}\text{Ne} = 6.022 \times 10^{23}$

Total number of protons, neutrons, and electrons in $1$ mole $= 30 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 1.8066 \times 10^{25}$

Answer: $1.8066 \times 10^{25}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

Identify a set of isoelectronic species from the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Na}^+, \text{Cl}^-, \text{O}^-, \text{NO}^+$

### Q6. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following statements is NOT true about isotopes?

**Options:**

- **(A)** These are the atoms of same elements.
- **(B)** They have same chemical properties.
- **(C)** They occupy same position in the modern periodic table.
- **(D)** They have equal number of neutrons.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) They have equal number of neutrons.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Isotopes are atoms of the same element having the same atomic number but different mass numbers.

Since the atomic number is the same, they have the same number of protons and electrons, which means they exhibit the same chemical properties and occupy the same position in the modern periodic table.

Because their mass numbers are different, isotopes of an element have different numbers of neutrons.

Therefore, the statement that they have an equal number of neutrons is incorrect.

Answer: They have equal number of neutrons.

</details>
