### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statement about enzyme is correct ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** They alter the equilibrium constant of reactions
- **(B)** They increase the activation energy of reactions
- **(C)** They are specific in their action
- **(D)** They are consumed in the reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) They are specific in their action**

**Detailed Solution:**

Enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up the rate of biochemical reactions without being consumed in the process.

They do not alter the equilibrium constant of a reaction; they only help attain equilibrium faster.

They work by lowering the activation energy of the reaction, not increasing it.

Enzymes are highly specific in their action, meaning each enzyme typically catalyzes only one specific reaction or acts on a specific substrate.

Thus, the statement that they are specific in their action is correct.

Answer: They are specific in their action

</details>

- **(C)** Ribulose
- **(D)** Benzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Glucose**

**Detailed Solution:**

In green technology, Draths and Frost developed a biocatalytic route to synthesize adipic acid from D-glucose using genetically modified bacteria (E. coli).

This method serves as an environmentally friendly alternative to the traditional industrial synthesis of adipic acid, which relies on toxic and petroleum-derived benzene as the starting material.
