### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the synthetic antioxidant from following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Vitamin E
- **(B)** Thymol
- **(C)** BHT
- **(D)** Chloroxylenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) BHT**

**Detailed Solution:**

Antioxidants are substances that prevent oxidation and are used as food preservatives to delay the spoilage of food. 

Vitamin E is a naturally occurring antioxidant. 

BHT (Butylated hydroxytoluene) and BHA (Butylated hydroxyanisole) are common synthetic antioxidants added to foods to prevent rancidity. 

Thymol is a natural phenolic compound, and Chloroxylenol is an antiseptic (the active ingredient in Dettol). 

Therefore, BHT is the synthetic antioxidant.

Answer: BHT

</details>
