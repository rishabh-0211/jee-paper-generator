### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

Quaternary ammonium salts are used in ___

**Options:**

- **(A)** Anionic detergents.
- **(B)** Cationic detergents.
- **(C)** Nonionic detergents.
- **(D)** Soaps.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Cationic detergents.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Quaternary ammonium salts of amines with acetates, chlorides, or bromides as anions are used as cationic detergents.

The cationic part possesses a long hydrocarbon chain and a positive charge on the nitrogen atom. An example is cetyltrimethylammonium bromide, which is a popular cationic detergent used in hair conditioners.

Answer: Cationic detergents.

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the result when soap is used in hard water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Micelles
- **(B)** Scum
- **(C)** Emulsion
- **(D)** Gel

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Scum**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hard water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium ions ($Ca^{2+}$ and $Mg^{2+}$).

When soap (which is typically a sodium or potassium salt of a long-chain fatty acid, such as sodium stearate) is added to hard water, these ions react with the soap molecules.

The sodium or potassium ions are replaced by calcium and magnesium ions, leading to the formation of insoluble calcium and magnesium salts of the fatty acids.

For example:
$2C_{17}H_{35}COONa + Ca^{2+} \rightarrow (C_{17}H_{35}COO)_2Ca \downarrow + 2Na^+$

These insoluble precipitates separate out from the water and are commonly known as scum.

Answer: Scum

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

What type of information is collected using FTIR fourier transform infrared spectroscopy?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Morphology of nanomaterial
- **(B)** Absorption of functional group
- **(C)** Geometry of particles
- **(D)** Particle size

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Absorption of functional group**

**Detailed Solution:**

Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) is an analytical technique used to identify the presence of specific functional groups in a molecule.

It measures the absorption of infrared radiation by the sample, which causes molecular vibrations such as stretching and bending.

Different functional groups absorb characteristic frequencies of IR radiation, allowing for their identification.

Therefore, FTIR collects information regarding the absorption of functional groups.

Answer: Absorption of functional group

</details>
