### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An antibiotic that contains arsenic is

**Options:**

- **(A)** prontosil
- **(B)** chloramphenicol
- **(C)** salvarsan
- **(D)** thymol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) salvarsan**

**Detailed Solution:**

Salvarsan, also known as arsphenamine, is an antibacterial drug that was the first effective treatment for syphilis. It contains an arsenic-arsenic double bond (historically represented as $-\text{As}=\text{As}-$), making it an arsenic-containing antibiotic.

Prontosil is a sulfa drug containing sulfur and nitrogen.

Chloramphenicol is a broad-spectrum antibiotic containing chlorine and nitrogen.

Thymol is a phenol derivative used as an antiseptic and contains only carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen.

Answer: salvarsan

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following elements is not present in salvarsan?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{As}$
- **(B)** $\text{N}$
- **(C)** $\text{O}$
- **(D)** $\text{P}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\text{P}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Salvarsan, also known as arsphenamine, is an organoarsenic compound historically used as an antisyphilitic drug.

Its chemical structure consists of two benzene rings, each substituted with an amino group ($-\text{NH}_2$) and a hydroxyl group ($-\text{OH}$), linked together by an arsenic-arsenic bond ($-\text{As}=\text{As}-$).

The elements present in salvarsan are Carbon ($\text{C}$), Hydrogen ($\text{H}$), Arsenic ($\text{As}$), Nitrogen ($\text{N}$), and Oxygen ($\text{O}$).

Phosphorus ($\text{P}$) is not present in the structure of salvarsan.

Answer: $\text{P}$

</details>

- **(D)** Antimicrobial

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>
