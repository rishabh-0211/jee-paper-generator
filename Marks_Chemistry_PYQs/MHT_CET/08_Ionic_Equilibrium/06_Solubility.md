### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The solubility product of a sparingly soluble salt $\text{BA}_2$ is $1.08 \times 10^{-10}$. Calculate its solubility in g dm$^{-3}$ if the molar mass of salt is $175$ g mol$^{-1}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5.25 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$.
- **(B)** $1.75 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$
- **(C)** $3.50 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$.
- **(D)** $7.00 \times 10^{-3}$ g dm$^{-3}$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $5.25 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The dissociation of the sparingly soluble salt $\text{BA}_2$ is given by:

$\text{BA}_2 \rightleftharpoons \text{B}^{2+} + 2\text{A}^{-}$

Let the solubility of the salt be $s$ mol dm$^{-3}$.

$K_{sp} = [\text{B}^{2+}][\text{A}^{-}]^2 = (s)(2s)^2 = 4s^3$

Given $K_{sp} = 1.08 \times 10^{-10}$, we have:

$4s^3 = 1.08 \times 10^{-10}$

$s^3 = 0.27 \times 10^{-10} = 27 \times 10^{-12}$

$s = 3 \times 10^{-4}$ mol dm$^{-3}$

The molar mass of the salt is $175$ g mol$^{-1}$.

Solubility in g dm$^{-3}$ = $s \times \text{Molar mass}$

Solubility = $3 \times 10^{-4} \times 175 = 525 \times 10^{-4} = 5.25 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$

Answer: $5.25 \times 10^{-2}$ g dm$^{-3}$.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The $K_{SP}$ of $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ is $1.2 \times 10^{-11}$. Then what will be the highest pH of the $0.1$ M $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ ions from which $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ is not precipitated?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4.96$
- **(B)** $6.96$
- **(C)** $7.54$
- **(D)** $9.04$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $9.04$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For precipitation of $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ not to occur, the ionic product must be less than or equal to $K_{SP}$.

$[\text{Mg}^{2+}][\text{OH}^-]^2 \le K_{SP}$

Substituting the given values:

$0.1 \times [\text{OH}^-]^2 \le 1.2 \times 10^{-11}$

$[\text{OH}^-]^2 \le 1.2 \times 10^{-10}$

$[\text{OH}^-] \le \sqrt{1.2} \times 10^{-5}$

$[\text{OH}^-] \le 1.095 \times 10^{-5}$

Taking the negative logarithm on both sides:

$\text{pOH} \ge -\log(1.095 \times 10^{-5})$

$\text{pOH} \ge 5 - \log(1.095)$

$\text{pOH} \ge 5 - 0.04 = 4.96$

Since $\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14$ at $25^{\circ}\text{C}$:

$14 - \text{pH} \ge 4.96$

$\text{pH} \le 14 - 4.96$

$\text{pH} \le 9.04$

Thus, the highest pH from which $\text{Mg(OH)}_2$ is not precipitated is $9.04$.

Answer: $9.04$

</details>

$\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2(s) \rightleftharpoons 3\text{Ca}^{2+}(aq) + 2\text{PO}_4^{3-}(aq)$

### Q60. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The solubility of $\mathrm{AgCl}$ is $1 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{~mol} / \mathrm{L}$. Its solubility in $0.1$ molar sodium chloride solution is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1 \times 10^{-10}$
- **(B)** $1 \times 10^{-5}$
- **(C)** $1 \times 10^{-9}$
- **(D)** $1 \times 10^{-4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $1 \times 10^{-9}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$K_{s p}$ of $\mathrm{AgCl}=(\text { solubility of } \mathrm{AgCl})^{2}$
$$
=\left(1 \times 10^{-5}\right)^{2}=1 \times 10^{-10}
$$
Suppose its solubility in $0.1$ M $\mathrm{NaCl}$ is $x \mathrm{~mol} / \mathrm{L}$
$$
\begin{array}{c}
\mathrm{AgCl} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{Ag}^{+}+\mathrm{Cl}^{-} \\
\mathrm{NaCl} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{Na}^{+}+\underset{0.1 \mathrm{M}}{\mathrm{Cl}^{-}}_{0.1 \mathrm{M}} \\
{\left[\mathrm{Cl}^{-}\right]=(x+0.1) \mathrm{M}} \\
K_{s p} \text { of } \mathrm{AgCl}=\left[\mathrm{Ag}^{+}\right]\left[\mathrm{Cl}^{-}\right] \\
=x \times(x+0.1) \\
1 \times 10^{-10}=x^{2}+0.1 x
\end{array}
$$
Higher power of $x$ are neglected
$$
\begin{aligned}
1 \times 10^{-10} &=0.1 x \\
x &=1 \times 10^{-9} \mathrm{M}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
