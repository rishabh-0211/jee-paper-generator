### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following salts does not undergo hydrolysis?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ammonium chloride .
- **(B)** Sodium carbonate .
- **(C)** Ammonium cyanide .
- **(D)** Potassium nitrate .

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Potassium nitrate .**

**Detailed Solution:**

Salts formed by the neutralization of a strong acid and a strong base do not undergo hydrolysis because their constituent ions are weak conjugate acids and bases, which do not react with water.

Ammonium chloride ($NH_4Cl$) is a salt of a weak base ($NH_4OH$) and a strong acid ($HCl$), so it undergoes cationic hydrolysis.

Sodium carbonate ($Na_2CO_3$) is a salt of a strong base ($NaOH$) and a weak acid ($H_2CO_3$), so it undergoes anionic hydrolysis.

Ammonium cyanide ($NH_4CN$) is a salt of a weak base ($NH_4OH$) and a weak acid ($HCN$), so it undergoes both cationic and anionic hydrolysis.

Potassium nitrate ($KNO_3$) is a salt of a strong base ($KOH$) and a strong acid ($HNO_3$). Therefore, it does not undergo hydrolysis.

Answer: Potassium nitrate .

</details>

What happens when solid $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ dissolved in water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** an increase in $[\text{OH}^-]$

- **(C)** an increase in $[\text{H}^+]$ ions

**Detailed Solution:**

The carbonate ion undergoes anionic hydrolysis:

$\text{CO}_3^{2-} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightleftharpoons \text{HCO}_3^- + \text{OH}^-$

This hydrolysis produces $\text{OH}^-$ ions, leading to an increase in $[\text{OH}^-]$ concentration and making the solution basic.

Answer: an increase in $[\text{OH}^-]$

### Q9. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What happens when $\text{CuSO}_4$ is dissolved in water?
Identify correct statements from the following

**Options:**

- **(A)** Anion of the salt react with water.
- **(B)** Its solution will turn red litmus into blue.
- **(C)** The pH of the solution will be less than $7$.
- **(D)** The $\text{CuSO}_4$ will not undergo hydrolysis reaction.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) The pH of the solution will be less than $7$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\text{CuSO}_4$ is a salt of a weak base ($\text{Cu(OH)}_2$) and a strong acid ($\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$).

When dissolved in water, the $\text{Cu}^{2+}$ cation undergoes hydrolysis to produce $\text{H}^+$ ions:

$\text{Cu}^{2+} + 2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightleftharpoons \text{Cu(OH)}_2 + 2\text{H}^+$

Due to the generation of $\text{H}^+$ ions, the solution becomes acidic.

Therefore, the pH of the solution will be less than $7$.

The anion $\text{SO}_4^{2-}$ does not undergo hydrolysis as it is derived from a strong acid.

An acidic solution turns blue litmus red, not red litmus blue.

Answer: The pH of the solution will be less than $7$.

### Q33. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Ammonium acetate which is $0.01 \quad \mathrm{M}$, is hydrolysed to $0.001 \mathrm{M}$ concentration. Calculate the change in $\mathrm{pH}$ in $0.001 \mathrm{M}$ solution, if initially $\mathrm{pH}=\mathrm{pK}_{a}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 5
- **(B)** 10
- **(C)** 100
- **(D)** 1

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{array}{c}\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COONH}_{4}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}+\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{OH} \\ 0.01 \mathrm{M} & 0.001 \mathrm{M} \\ \mathrm{pH}=\mathrm{p} K_{a}+\log \frac{\left[\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COONH}_{4}\right]}{\left[\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}\right]} \\ =\mathrm{p} K_{a}+\log \left[\frac{0.01}{0.001}\right] \\ =\mathrm{p} K_{a}+\log 10 \\ =\mathrm{p} K_{a}+1 \\ \therefore \text { Change in } \mathrm{pH}=1\end{array}$
