### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the correct pair of molecule and the heteroatom present in it respectively from following:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Furan-S
- **(B)** Pyrrole-N
- **(C)** Thiophene-O
- **(D)** Piperidine-S

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Pyrrole-N**

**Detailed Solution:**

Furan is a five-membered heterocyclic compound containing oxygen (O) as the heteroatom.

Pyrrole is a five-membered heterocyclic compound containing nitrogen (N) as the heteroatom.

Thiophene is a five-membered heterocyclic compound containing sulphur (S) as the heteroatom.

Piperidine is a six-membered saturated heterocyclic compound containing nitrogen (N) as the heteroatom.

Therefore, the correct pair of molecule and heteroatom is Pyrrole-N.

Answer: Pyrrole-N

</details>
