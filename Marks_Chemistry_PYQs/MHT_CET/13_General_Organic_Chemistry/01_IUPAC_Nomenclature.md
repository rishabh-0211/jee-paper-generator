### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the IUPAC name of $\text{CH}_3\text{-O-CH}_2\text{-CH}_2\text{-CH}_3$?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1-Methoxy propane
- **(B)** 3-Propoxy methane
- **(C)** 2-Propoxy methane
- **(D)** 3-Methoxy propane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1-Methoxy propane**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given compound is an ether with the formula $\text{CH}_3\text{-O-CH}_2\text{-CH}_2\text{-CH}_3$.

The two alkyl groups attached to the oxygen atom are methyl and propyl.

According to IUPAC nomenclature, the smaller alkyl group is named as an alkoxy substituent and the larger alkyl group is taken as the parent alkane.

The smaller group is methyl, so the substituent is methoxy.

The larger group is a three-carbon chain, so the parent alkane is propane.

Numbering the carbon chain from the end closer to the oxygen atom gives the locant 1 for the methoxy group.

Therefore, the IUPAC name is 1-Methoxypropane.

Answer: 1-Methoxy propane

</details>
