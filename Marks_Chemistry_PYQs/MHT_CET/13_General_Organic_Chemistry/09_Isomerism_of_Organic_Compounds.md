### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Number of optically active stereoisomer are possible for butane-2,3-diol

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1$
- **(B)** $2$
- **(C)** $3$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The structure of butane-2,3-diol is $CH_3-CH(OH)-CH(OH)-CH_3$.

It contains two similar chiral carbon atoms ($n=2$).

Since the molecule is symmetrical and has an even number of chiral centers, the number of optically active stereoisomers is given by the formula $2^{n-1}$.

Number of optically active isomers = $2^{2-1} = 2^1 = 2$.

The number of meso (optically inactive) isomers is $2^{(n/2)-1} = 2^{1-1} = 2^0 = 1$.

The two optically active isomers are the (2R, 3R) and (2S, 3S) forms, which exist as a pair of enantiomers.

Answer: $2$

</details>
