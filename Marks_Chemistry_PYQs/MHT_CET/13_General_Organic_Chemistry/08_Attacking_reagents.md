### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which one of the following series contains <em>only</em> electrophiles?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{R-OH}, \text{Cl}^+, \text{NH}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{H}_2\text{O}, \text{R}_2\text{N}^-, \text{H}_3\text{O}^+$
- **(C)** $\text{BF}_3, \text{SO}_3, \text{NO}^+$
- **(D)** $\text{AlCl}_3, \text{R-NH}_2, \text{PCl}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{BF}_3, \text{SO}_3, \text{NO}^+$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electrophiles are electron-deficient species that can accept an electron pair. They are either positively charged or neutral molecules with an incomplete octet or highly polar bonds.

In the first series, $\text{R-OH}$ and $\text{NH}_3$ have lone pairs on oxygen and nitrogen respectively, making them nucleophiles.

In the second series, $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ and $\text{R}_2\text{N}^-$ have lone pairs and a negative charge respectively, making them nucleophiles.

In the third series, $\text{BF}_3$ is an electron-deficient Lewis acid with an incomplete octet. $\text{SO}_3$ has a highly electron-deficient sulfur atom due to three electronegative oxygen atoms. $\text{NO}^+$ is a positively charged ion. All three are electrophiles.

In the fourth series, $\text{R-NH}_2$ has a lone pair on nitrogen, making it a nucleophile.

Answer: $\text{BF}_3, \text{SO}_3, \text{NO}^+$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of following has the highest nucleophilicity ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{F}^-$
- **(B)** $\text{OH}^-$
- **(C)** $\text{CH}_3^-$
- **(D)** $\text{NH}_2^-$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{CH}_3^-$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given species $\text{CH}_3^-$, $\text{NH}_2^-$, $\text{OH}^-$, and $\text{F}^-$ have donor atoms belonging to the same period (Period 2) of the periodic table.

Across a period from left to right, the electronegativity of the atoms increases in the order $\text{C} < \text{N} < \text{O} < \text{F}$.

A more electronegative atom holds its lone pair of electrons more tightly, making it less available for donation to an electrophile. Therefore, nucleophilicity decreases as electronegativity increases.

The order of nucleophilicity is $\text{CH}_3^- > \text{NH}_2^- > \text{OH}^- > \text{F}^-$.

Thus, $\text{CH}_3^-$ has the highest nucleophilicity.

Answer: $\text{CH}_3^-$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is a nucleophile?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{BH}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NH}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{AlCl}_{3}$
- **(D)** All of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{NH}_{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nucleophiles (ie, nucleus loving) are the species that have atleast one lone pair of electrons for donation, ie, they can also be called Lewis bases. Among the given, $\mathrm{BH}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{AlCl}_{3}$, being electron deficient, behave like electrophiles while $\ddot{\mathrm{N}} \mathrm{H}_{3}$, due to the presence of a lone pair over $\mathrm{N}$, is a nucleophile.

</details>
