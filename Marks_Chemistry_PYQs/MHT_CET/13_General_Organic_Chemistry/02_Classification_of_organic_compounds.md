What type of information is collected using FTIR spectroscopy ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Preliminary confirmation of formation of nanoparticles.
- **(B)** Crystal structure.
- **(C)** Structure of surface material.
- **(D)** Absorption by functional groups, Binding nature.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Absorption by functional groups, Binding nature.**

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{CH}_2 = \text{CH} - \text{CH}_2 - \text{X}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Allylic halides are compounds in which the halogen atom is bonded to an $sp^3$ hybridized carbon atom adjacent to a carbon-carbon double bond ($C=C$).

In $\text{CH}_2 = \text{CH} - \text{CH}_2 - \text{X}$, the halogen atom $\text{X}$ is attached to the $sp^3$ hybridized carbon atom which is directly attached to the $C=C$ double bond. Thus, it is an allylic halide.

$\text{CH}_3 - \text{CH}_2 - \text{CH}_2 - \text{X}$ is a primary alkyl halide.

$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_2 - \text{X}$ is a benzylic halide.

$\text{CH}_3 - \text{CH} = \text{CH} - \text{X}$ is a vinylic halide.

Answer: $\text{CH}_2 = \text{CH} - \text{CH}_2 - \text{X}$
