### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which inert gas is used in chromatography?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ar
- **(B)** Ne
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Kr}$
- **(D)** He

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Ar**

**Detailed Solution:**

Carrier gas is an inert gas used to carry samples. Helium $\mathrm{(He)}$, nitrogen $\mathrm{(N_2)}$, hydrogen $\mathrm{(H_2)}$, and argon $\mathrm{(Ar)}$ are often used. Helium and nitrogen are most commonly used and the use of helium is desirable when using a capillary column.

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2020 (12 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Separation of Acetone and Benzene from their mixture is carried out by

**Options:**

- **(A)** Fractional distillation
- **(B)** Simple distillation
- **(C)** Fractional distillation using fractionating column
- **(D)** Distillation under reduced pressure

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Fractional distillation using fractionating column**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetone cannot be easily separated from benzene in high purity by distillation because of the closeness of their vapor pressures. 
Acetone can be readily removed from benzene by azeotropic distillation which is basically fractional distillation under fractionating column using certain aromatic hydrocarbons.

</details>
