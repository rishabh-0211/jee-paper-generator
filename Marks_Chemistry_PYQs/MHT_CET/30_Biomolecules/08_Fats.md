### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is an Omega-3 fatty acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $9, 12, 15$ - octadecatrienoic acid
- **(B)** Octadecanoic acid
- **(C)** Hexadecanoic acid
- **(D)** Octanoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $9, 12, 15$ - octadecatrienoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Omega-3 fatty acids are polyunsaturated fatty acids characterized by the presence of a double bond three atoms away from the terminal methyl group (the omega end) in their chemical structure.

In $9, 12, 15$ - octadecatrienoic acid (also known as $\alpha$-linolenic acid), the carbon chain has $18$ carbon atoms. The double bonds are located at the 9th, 12th, and 15th carbon atoms counting from the carboxyl group. 

Counting from the terminal methyl group (omega carbon, which is C-18), the first double bond is at position $18 - 15 = 3$. Therefore, it is an Omega-3 fatty acid.

The other options (octadecanoic acid, hexadecanoic acid, and octanoic acid) are saturated fatty acids and do not contain any carbon-carbon double bonds.

Answer: $9, 12, 15$ - octadecatrienoic acid

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements is true regarding fats?

**Options:**

- **(A)** These are polyesters of fatty acids and ethylene glycol.

- **(C)** These are esters of inorganic acids and bases.
- **(D)** triester of unsaturated fatty acids and ethyl alcohol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) These are triesters of fatty acids and glycerols.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Answer: These are triesters of fatty acids and glycerols.

- **(D)** Fish oil

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Detailed Solution:**

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following oils is mainly a saturated fats?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Coconut oil
- **(B)** Olive oil
- **(C)** Safflower oil
- **(D)** Fish oil

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Coconut oil**

**Detailed Solution:**

Saturated fats are typically solid at room temperature and contain single bonds between carbon atoms in their fatty acid chains.

Coconut oil is composed of approximately $80\%$ to $90\%$ saturated fat, primarily lauric acid, myristic acid, and palmitic acid.

Olive oil is mainly composed of monounsaturated fats (oleic acid).

Safflower oil and fish oil are rich in polyunsaturated fats (such as linoleic acid and omega-3 fatty acids, respectively).

Therefore, coconut oil is mainly a saturated fat.

Answer: Coconut oil

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

Which of the following is found to raise the HDL level in the blood?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Omega-6 fatty acids
- **(B)** Omega-3 fatty acids
- **(C)** Trans fats
- **(D)** Cis fats

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Omega-3 fatty acids**

**Detailed Solution:**

Omega-3 fatty acids are polyunsaturated fatty acids that are known to increase the level of High-Density Lipoprotein (HDL), which is considered good cholesterol, in the blood. They also help in lowering triglyceride levels, thereby reducing the risk of cardiovascular diseases.

### Q7. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the number of moles of water molecules required for complete hydrolysis of n mole triglyceride ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 n$
- **(B)** 3n
- **(C)** $2 n$
- **(D)** n

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 3n**

**Detailed Solution:**

A triglyceride molecule contains three ester linkages formed by the condensation of one glycerol molecule with three fatty acid molecules.

Complete hydrolysis of one ester linkage requires one molecule of water.

Since there are three ester linkages in one molecule of triglyceride, complete hydrolysis of one mole of triglyceride requires three moles of water.

Therefore, for the complete hydrolysis of $n$ moles of triglyceride, $3n$ moles of water molecules are required.

Answer: 3n

</details>

### Q9. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Among the following, the formula of saturated fatty acid is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{29} \mathrm{COOH}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{35} \mathrm{COOH}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{31} \mathrm{COOH}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{33} \mathrm{COOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{35} \mathrm{COOH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The general formula for saturated fatty acids is $\mathrm{C}_{n} \mathrm{H}_{2 n+1} \mathrm{COOH}$
Among the given acids, only $\mathrm{C}_{17} \mathrm{H}_{35} \mathrm{COOH}$ satisfy this formula, hence it is a saturated fatty acid.

</details>
