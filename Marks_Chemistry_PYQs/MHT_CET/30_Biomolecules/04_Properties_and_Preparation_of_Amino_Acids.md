### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds exists as dipolar ion?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ketones
- **(B)** $\alpha$-Amino acids
- **(C)** Carbohydrates
- **(D)** Fatty acids

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\alpha$-Amino acids**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\alpha$-Amino acids contain both an acidic carboxyl group ($-COOH$) and a basic amino group ($-NH_2$) in the same molecule.

In aqueous solution, the carboxyl group loses a proton and the amino group accepts a proton, forming a dipolar ion known as a zwitterion.

The general structure of the zwitterion is $H_3N^+ - CH(R) - COO^-$.

Therefore, $\alpha$-amino acids exist as dipolar ions.

Answer: $\alpha$-Amino acids

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following amino acids does NOT contain chiral carbon?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Valine
- **(B)** Alanine
- **(C)** Glycine
- **(D)** Isoleucine

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Glycine**

**Detailed Solution:**

The general structure of an $\alpha$-amino acid is $H_{2}N-CH(R)-COOH$.

For an amino acid to be chiral, the $\alpha$-carbon must be attached to four different groups.

In glycine, the side chain $R$ is a hydrogen atom ($H$).

The structure of glycine is $H_{2}N-CH_{2}-COOH$.

Since the $\alpha$-carbon is attached to two identical hydrogen atoms, it does not have four different groups attached to it, making it achiral.

Valine, alanine, and isoleucine have different $R$ groups (isopropyl, methyl, and sec-butyl respectively) and thus contain chiral carbons.

Answer: Glycine

</details>

- **(C)** n
- **(D)** $2 n$
