### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following reactions confirms presence of one carbonyl group in glucose?

**Options:**

- **(A)** When glucose is heated with HI for longer time forms n-Hexane.
- **(B)** When glucose is heated with hydroxyl amine forms glucose oxime.
- **(C)** When glucose is heated with excess acetic Anhydride forms glucose pentaacetate.
- **(D)** When glucose is treated with dilute nitric acid forms saccharic acid.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) When glucose is heated with hydroxyl amine forms glucose oxime.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Reaction of glucose with hydroxylamine ($NH_2OH$) yields an oxime. This reaction is characteristic of aldehydes and ketones, thus confirming the presence of a carbonyl group in glucose.

Heating glucose with HI for a long time forms n-hexane, which confirms that all six carbon atoms are linked in a straight chain.

Reaction of glucose with excess acetic anhydride forms glucose pentaacetate, which confirms the presence of five hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups.

Oxidation of glucose with dilute nitric acid yields saccharic acid, which confirms the presence of a primary alcoholic group.

Answer: When glucose is heated with hydroxyl amine forms glucose oxime.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the product when glucose reacts with bromine water.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Saccharic acid
- **(B)** Cyanohydrin
- **(C)** Gluconic acid
- **(D)** Nucleic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Gluconic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Bromine water is a mild oxidizing agent.

When glucose reacts with bromine water, the aldehyde group ($-CHO$) is oxidized to a carboxylic acid group ($-COOH$), while the hydroxyl groups remain unaffected.

The reaction is given by:

$CHO-(CHOH)_4-CH_2OH + Br_2/H_2O \rightarrow COOH-(CHOH)_4-CH_2OH$

The product formed is gluconic acid.

Answer: Gluconic acid

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Identify glycosidic linkages for formation of chain and branches respectively in amylopectin.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\beta-1,6$ and $\alpha-1,6$
- **(B)** $\alpha-1,6$ and $\beta-1,4$
- **(C)** $\beta-1,4$ and $\alpha-1,6$
- **(D)** $\alpha-1,4$ and $\alpha-1,6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\alpha-1,4$ and $\alpha-1,6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Amylopectin is a highly branched polymer of $\alpha$-D-glucose units.

The linear chain of amylopectin is formed by $\alpha-1,4$-glycosidic linkages between the glucose units.

The branching in amylopectin occurs through $\alpha-1,6$-glycosidic linkages, which typically appear every 24 to 30 glucose units along the chain.

Therefore, the glycosidic linkages for the formation of the chain and branches are $\alpha-1,4$ and $\alpha-1,6$ respectively.

Answer: $\alpha-1,4$ and $\alpha-1,6$

</details>
