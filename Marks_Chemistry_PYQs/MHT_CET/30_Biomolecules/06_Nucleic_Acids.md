### Q13. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the total number of -OH groups present in a nucleoside involved in formation of DNA?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 5
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

A nucleoside involved in the formation of DNA consists of a deoxyribose sugar and a nitrogenous base.

The sugar present in DNA is 2-deoxy-D-ribose. In its free cyclic form, it has three -OH groups located at the C-1, C-3, and C-5 positions.

During the formation of a nucleoside, the -OH group at the C-1 position of the sugar is involved in forming an N-glycosidic linkage with the nitrogenous base, with the elimination of a water molecule.

The nitrogenous bases in DNA (adenine, guanine, cytosine, thymine) exist predominantly in their keto (lactam) forms and do not contain free -OH groups.

Therefore, the resulting deoxyribonucleoside contains only two free -OH groups, which are located at the C-3' and C-5' positions of the sugar moiety.

Answer: 2

</details>

- **(C)** Polynucleotide has free -OH group at $3^{\prime}$ end.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Nucleic acids contain back bone formed of $-\mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{C}-$ linkage.**

**Detailed Solution:**

In nucleic acids, the backbone is formed by alternating sugar and phosphate groups. The linkage connecting the nucleotides is a phosphodiester linkage, which involves a $-\mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{P}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{C}-$ sequence of atoms, not a $-\mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{C}-$ linkage.

The $5^{\prime}$ end of a polynucleotide chain has a free phosphate group, and the $3^{\prime}$ end has a free $-\mathrm{OH}$ group.

Neighbouring nucleotides are joined together by phosphodiester bonds.

Therefore, the statement that nucleic acids contain a backbone formed of $-\mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{C}-$ linkage is incorrect.

Answer: Nucleic acids contain back bone formed of $-\mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{C}-$ linkage.

</details>
