### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is a natural antioxidant?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Aspartame
- **(B)** Salicylic acid
- **(C)** Sucralose
- **(D)** Tocopherol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Tocopherol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Tocopherol (Vitamin E) is a naturally occurring antioxidant found in vegetable oils, nuts, and seeds. It protects lipids from oxidative damage.

Aspartame and sucralose are artificial sweeteners.

Salicylic acid is a plant hormone and is used as a precursor for aspirin, but it is not primarily classified as a natural antioxidant in this context.

Answer: Tocopherol

</details>

- **(B)** Wintergreen
- **(C)** Citrus fruits
- **(D)** Cinnamon

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify medicinaly important compound extracted from citrus fruits?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Antimicrobials
- **(B)** Vit. C
- **(C)** Analgesics
- **(D)** Antiseptics

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Vit. C**

**Detailed Solution:**

Citrus fruits such as oranges, lemons, and grapefruits are rich natural sources of Vitamin C (ascorbic acid).

Vitamin C is a medicinally important compound that acts as an antioxidant, helps in the synthesis of collagen, and prevents scurvy.

Antimicrobials, analgesics, and antiseptics are broad classes of medicinal drugs and are not specifically extracted from citrus fruits.

Answer: Vit. C

</details>
