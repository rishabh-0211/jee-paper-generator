### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following arrangements represents the primary structure of proteins?

**Options:**

- **(A)** The three - dimensional folding of the polypeptide chain.
- **(B)** The sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bond.
- **(C)** The interactions between different polypeptide chains.
- **(D)** The formation of alpha helix and beta pleated sheets,

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) The sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bond.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The primary structure of a protein refers to the exact linear sequence of amino acids connected by peptide bonds in a polypeptide chain.

The secondary structure involves local folding into structures such as $\alpha$-helices and $\beta$-pleated sheets.

The tertiary structure refers to the overall three-dimensional folding of a single polypeptide chain.

The quaternary structure involves the spatial arrangement and interactions of multiple polypeptide chains.

Therefore, the sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bonds represents the primary structure.

Answer: The sequence of amino acids linked by peptide bond.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following forms of proteins is used for transport of cholesterol in our body?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Glycoprotein
- **(B)** Lipoprotein
- **(C)** Chromoprotein
- **(D)** Enzymes

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Lipoprotein**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cholesterol is a lipid and is highly hydrophobic, making it insoluble in the aqueous environment of blood.

To be transported through the bloodstream, cholesterol is packaged into complexes called lipoproteins.

Lipoproteins consist of a core of hydrophobic lipids (such as cholesterol esters and triglycerides) surrounded by a shell of hydrophilic proteins (apolipoproteins), phospholipids, and unesterified cholesterol.

Examples of lipoproteins include Low-Density Lipoprotein (LDL) and High-Density Lipoprotein (HDL), which are primarily responsible for the transport of cholesterol in the human body.

Answer: Lipoprotein

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following statements regarding proteins are correct?
(A) Serum albumin is a globular protein.
(B) Myosin is a globular protein.
(C) Insulin is a fibrous protein.
(D) Legumelin is a globular protein.

**Options:**

- **(A)** (A) and (D) only.
- **(B)** (A), (B) and (C) only.
- **(C)** (A), (B), (C) and (D).
- **(D)** (C) and (D) only.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) (A) and (D) only.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Based on the classification of proteins:

Fibrous proteins have polypeptide chains running parallel, held together by hydrogen and disulphide bonds, forming a fibre-like structure. Examples include keratin and myosin. Thus, myosin is a fibrous protein, making statement (B) incorrect.

Globular proteins have polypeptide chains that coil around to give a spherical shape and are usually soluble in water. Examples include insulin and albumins. Therefore, serum albumin is a globular protein, making statement (A) correct. Insulin is also a globular protein, making statement (C) incorrect.

Legumelin is an albumin protein found in leguminous seeds. Since albumins are globular proteins, legumelin is a globular protein, making statement (D) correct.

Therefore, only statements (A) and (D) are correct.

Answer: (A) and (D) only.

</details>
