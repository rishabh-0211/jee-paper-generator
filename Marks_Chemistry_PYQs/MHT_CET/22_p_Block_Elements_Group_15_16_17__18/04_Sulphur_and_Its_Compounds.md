### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Sulfuric acid is used as a dehydrating agent in the following reaction. Identify the term used for carbon left.
$\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{22}\text{O}_{11} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4} 12\text{C} + 11\text{H}_2\text{O}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** sugar charcoal
- **(B)** charring
- **(C)** graphite
- **(D)** fused mixture

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) sugar charcoal**

**Detailed Solution:**

Concentrated sulfuric acid acts as a strong dehydrating agent. When it reacts with sucrose (cane sugar, $\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{22}\text{O}_{11}$), it removes water molecules from it, leaving behind a black, porous mass of carbon.

$\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{22}\text{O}_{11} \xrightarrow{\text{conc. H}_2\text{SO}_4} 12\text{C} + 11\text{H}_2\text{O}$

The black mass of carbon left behind is known as sugar charcoal, and the process is called charring of sugar.

Answer: sugar charcoal

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What are the different types of bonds formed by sulphur with oxygen in thiosulfuric acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** One double bond and two single bond
- **(B)** One double bond and one single bond
- **(C)** Two double bond and one single bond
- **(D)** Two double bond and two single bond

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) One double bond and two single bond**

**Detailed Solution:**

The chemical formula of thiosulfuric acid is $H_2S_2O_3$.

Its structure is derived from sulfuric acid ($H_2SO_4$) by replacing one of the doubly bonded oxygen atoms with a sulfur atom.

In the structure of thiosulfuric acid, the central sulfur atom is bonded to:
1. One sulfur atom via a double bond ($S=S$).
2. One oxygen atom via a double bond ($S=O$).
3. Two oxygen atoms (of the hydroxyl groups) via single bonds ($S-OH$).

Therefore, the bonds formed by the central sulphur atom with oxygen are one double bond and two single bonds.

Answer: One double bond and two single bond

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The thermal stability of hydrides of Group 16 elements decreases down the group.

As the size of the central atom increases from oxygen to tellurium, the E-H bond length increases.

This results in a decrease in the bond dissociation enthalpy of the E-H bond.

Therefore, the thermal stability decreases in the order: $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se} > \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te}$.

The correct increasing order is $\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$.

Answer: $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Te} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} < \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$

</details>

### Q9. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the angle $\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{S}-\mathrm{O}$ in $\mathrm{SO}_2$ molecule.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $119.5^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $180^{\circ}$
- **(C)** $109^{\circ}$
- **(D)** $107.5^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $119.5^{\circ}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In $\mathrm{SO}_2$ molecule, the central sulfur atom undergoes $sp^2$ hybridization.

It has two bond pairs and one lone pair of electrons.

The expected bond angle for $sp^2$ hybridization is $120^{\circ}$.

Due to lone pair-bond pair repulsion being greater than bond pair-bond pair repulsion, the $\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{S}-\mathrm{O}$ bond angle is slightly reduced to $119.5^{\circ}$.

Answer: $119.5^{\circ}$

</details>
