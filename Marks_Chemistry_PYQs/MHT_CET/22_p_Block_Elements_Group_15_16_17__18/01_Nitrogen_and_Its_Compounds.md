### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following catalysts is used in the manufacture of ammonia by Haber's process?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{Fe-Cr}$
- **(B)** $\text{Mo/Fe}$
- **(C)** $\text{MnO}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{Pt}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{Mo/Fe}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In Haber's process for the manufacture of ammonia, the reaction between nitrogen and hydrogen is given by:

$\text{N}_2(g) + 3\text{H}_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2\text{NH}_3(g)$

Finely divided iron ($\text{Fe}$) is used as the catalyst to increase the rate of reaction, and molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$) is used as a promoter to enhance the efficiency of the iron catalyst. Therefore, $\text{Mo/Fe}$ is the correct catalyst-promoter combination used in this process.

Answer: $\text{Mo/Fe}$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the number of moles of Cl atoms and N atoms respectively present in n moles of chloropicrin?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3n and n
- **(B)** 2n and 2n
- **(C)** n and n
- **(D)** n and 2n

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 3n and n**

**Detailed Solution:**

The chemical formula of chloropicrin is $CCl_3NO_2$.

One molecule of chloropicrin contains $3$ chlorine (Cl) atoms and $1$ nitrogen (N) atom.

Therefore, $1$ mole of chloropicrin contains $3$ moles of Cl atoms and $1$ mole of N atoms.

For $n$ moles of chloropicrin, the number of moles of Cl atoms is $3n$ and the number of moles of N atoms is $n$.
