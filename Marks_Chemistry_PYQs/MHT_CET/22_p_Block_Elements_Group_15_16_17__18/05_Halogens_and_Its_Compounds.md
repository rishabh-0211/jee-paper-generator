### Q17. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 2)

What is shape of interhalogen compound so that central halogen exhibits +3 oxidation state?

- **(C)** Square pyramidal
- **(D)** Square planer

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Bent ' $T$ ' shape**

**Detailed Solution:**

Interhalogen compounds with the central halogen in a $+3$ oxidation state have the general formula $XX'_3$ (e.g., $ClF_3$, $BrF_3$).

Total number of electron pairs around the central atom = $3$ bond pairs + $2$ lone pairs = $5$.

The hybridization of the central atom is $sp^3d$, which corresponds to a trigonal bipyramidal electron geometry.

According to VSEPR theory, to minimize repulsion, the two lone pairs occupy the equatorial positions. The three bond pairs occupy the remaining two axial positions and one equatorial position.

This arrangement results in a bent 'T' shape for the molecule.

Answer: Bent ' $T$ ' shape

</details>

### Q29. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Identify the correct decreasing order of thermal stability from following.
[Note: The question has been modified to get the correct answer.]

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{ClF}\gt\mathrm{ICl}\gt\mathrm{IBr}\gt\mathrm{BrCl}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{ClF}\gt\mathrm{ICl}\gt\mathrm{IBr}\gt\mathrm{BrCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The thermal stability of interhalogen compounds is directly proportional to the electronegativity difference ($\Delta EN$) between the two halogen atoms. A greater electronegativity difference leads to a more polar and stronger bond, which increases thermal stability.

The approximate electronegativity values of halogens on the Pauling scale are:
$\mathrm{F} = 4.0$
$\mathrm{Cl} = 3.0$
$\mathrm{Br} = 2.8$
$\mathrm{I} = 2.5$

Calculating the electronegativity difference ($\Delta EN$) for the given compounds:
$\mathrm{ClF}$: $\Delta EN = 4.0 - 3.0 = 1.0$
$\mathrm{ICl}$: $\Delta EN = 3.0 - 2.5 = 0.5$
$\mathrm{IBr}$: $\Delta EN = 2.8 - 2.5 = 0.3$
$\mathrm{BrCl}$: $\Delta EN = 3.0 - 2.8 = 0.2$

Since thermal stability increases with the electronegativity difference, the correct decreasing order of thermal stability is:
$\mathrm{ClF} \gt \mathrm{ICl} \gt \mathrm{IBr} \gt \mathrm{BrCl}$

Answer: $\mathrm{ClF}\gt\mathrm{ICl}\gt\mathrm{IBr}\gt\mathrm{BrCl}$
