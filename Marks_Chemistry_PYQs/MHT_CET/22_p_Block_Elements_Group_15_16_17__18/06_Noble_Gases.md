### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following noble gases exhibits higher oxidation states ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ne
- **(B)** Ar
- **(C)** Kr
- **(D)** Xe

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Xe**

**Detailed Solution:**

As we move down the group of noble gases, the atomic size increases and the ionization enthalpy decreases.

Xenon has a relatively low ionization enthalpy, which allows its valence electrons to be excited to empty d-orbitals.

This enables Xenon to form compounds with highly electronegative elements like fluorine and oxygen.

Xenon exhibits higher oxidation states such as $+2$, $+4$, $+6$, and $+8$ in compounds like $\text{XeF}_2$, $\text{XeF}_4$, $\text{XeF}_6$, $\text{XeO}_3$, and $\text{XeO}_4$.

Neon and Argon do not form stable compounds, and Krypton only forms $\text{KrF}_2$ with an oxidation state of $+2$.

Therefore, Xenon exhibits higher oxidation states.

Answer: Xe

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is oxidation state of xenon in xenonmonooxytetrafluoride?

**Options:**

- **(A)** +2
- **(B)** +4
- **(C)** +6
- **(D)** +8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) +6**

**Detailed Solution:**

The chemical formula of xenon monooxytetrafluoride is $XeOF_4$.

Let the oxidation state of xenon be $x$.

The oxidation state of oxygen is $-2$ and that of fluorine is $-1$.

The sum of oxidation states of all atoms in a neutral molecule is zero.

$x + (-2) + 4(-1) = 0$

$x - 6 = 0$

$x = +6$

Answer: +6

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following xenon compound has chlorine pentafluoride like structure?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{XeF}_4$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{XeF}_6$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{XeO}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Chlorine pentafluoride ($\mathrm{ClF}_5$) has $7$ valence electrons on the central chlorine atom. It forms $5$ single bonds with fluorine atoms, leaving $1$ lone pair. The steric number is $5 + 1 = 6$, corresponding to $sp^3d^2$ hybridization. Its shape is square pyramidal.

Let us examine the given xenon compounds:

For $\mathrm{XeF}_4$: Xenon has $8$ valence electrons. It forms $4$ single bonds with fluorine, leaving $2$ lone pairs. The shape is square planar.

For $\mathrm{XeF}_6$: Xenon forms $6$ single bonds, leaving $1$ lone pair. The shape is distorted octahedral.

For $\mathrm{XeO}_3$: Xenon forms $3$ double bonds with oxygen, leaving $1$ lone pair. The shape is trigonal pyramidal.

For $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$: Xenon forms $1$ double bond with oxygen and $4$ single bonds with fluorine. Electrons used in bonding $= 2 + 4 = 6$. This leaves $1$ lone pair. The steric number is $5$ (sigma bonds) $+ 1$ (lone pair) $= 6$, corresponding to $sp^3d^2$ hybridization. Its shape is square pyramidal.

Thus, $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$ has a square pyramidal structure similar to $\mathrm{ClF}_5$.

Answer: $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify from following compounds that valence shell of 'Xe' consists one lone pair of electrons.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{XeF}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{XeF}_4$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{XeOF}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The central atom Xenon (Xe) has 8 valence electrons.

For $\mathrm{XeF}_2$: Xe forms 2 single bonds with F atoms, using 2 electrons. Remaining electrons = $8 - 2 = 6$, which corresponds to 3 lone pairs.

For $\mathrm{XeF}_4$: Xe forms 4 single bonds with F atoms, using 4 electrons. Remaining electrons = $8 - 4 = 4$, which corresponds to 2 lone pairs.

For $\mathrm{XeOF}_2$: Xe forms 1 double bond with O (using 2 electrons) and 2 single bonds with F (using 2 electrons). Remaining electrons = $8 - 4 = 4$, which corresponds to 2 lone pairs.

For $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$: Xe forms 1 double bond with O (using 2 electrons) and 4 single bonds with F (using 4 electrons). Remaining electrons = $8 - 6 = 2$, which corresponds to 1 lone pair.

Therefore, $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$ has exactly one lone pair of electrons on the central Xe atom.

Answer: $\mathrm{XeOF}_4$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which group element from following achieves noble gas configuration after gaining two electrons?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Group-14
- **(B)** Group-15
- **(C)** Group-16
- **(D)** Group-13

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Group-16**

**Detailed Solution:**

Elements of Group 16 have the general valence shell electronic configuration $ns^2 np^4$.

To achieve the stable noble gas configuration ($ns^2 np^6$), they need to gain two electrons.

For example, Oxygen (Group 16) has the configuration $1s^2 2s^2 2p^4$. By gaining two electrons, it forms $O^{2-}$ with the configuration $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6$, which is the stable noble gas configuration of Neon.

Answer: Group-16

</details>
