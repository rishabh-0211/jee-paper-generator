### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

Which of the following polymers is obtained from-hexamethylene diammonium adipate?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Nylon 6
- **(B)** Nylon 6,6
- **(C)** Dacron
- **(D)** Melamine

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Nylon 6,6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hexamethylene diammonium adipate is the salt formed by the reaction of hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid.

Answer: Nylon 6,6
