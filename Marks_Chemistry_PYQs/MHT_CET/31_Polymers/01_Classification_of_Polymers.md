### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

Which of the following polymers belongs elastomers?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Polystyrene
- **(B)** Polythene
- **(C)** Polyvinyls
- **(D)** Neoprene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Neoprene**

**Detailed Solution:**

Elastomers are rubber-like solid polymers with elastic properties. In these polymers, the polymer chains are held together by the weakest intermolecular forces, which permit the polymer to be stretched.

### Q18. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

- **(D)** Structure

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Number of monomers**
