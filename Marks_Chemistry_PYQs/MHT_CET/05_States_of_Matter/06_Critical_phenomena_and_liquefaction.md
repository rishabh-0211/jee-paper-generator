### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the value of critical temperature of $\text{CO}_2$ in $^\circ$C?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $33.2^\circ\text{C}$
- **(B)** $30.98^\circ\text{C}$
- **(C)** $154.3^\circ\text{C}$
- **(D)** $126.0^\circ\text{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $30.98^\circ\text{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The critical temperature of a gas is the temperature above which it cannot be liquefied, regardless of the amount of pressure applied.

From Andrews' isotherms for carbon dioxide, the critical temperature ($T_c$) of $\text{CO}_2$ is experimentally determined to be $30.98^\circ\text{C}$.

Answer: $30.98^\circ\text{C}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which among the following gases is difficult to liquefy ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Chlorine gas
- **(B)** Nitrogen gas
- **(C)** Ammonia gas
- **(D)** Sulphur dioxide gas

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Nitrogen gas**

**Detailed Solution:**

The ease of liquefaction of a gas depends on the strength of intermolecular forces and its critical temperature. Gases with higher critical temperatures are easier to liquefy.

Ammonia ($NH_3$) has strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding.

Sulphur dioxide ($SO_2$) is a polar molecule with strong dipole-dipole interactions.

Chlorine ($Cl_2$) is non-polar but has a large molecular size, leading to strong London dispersion forces.

Nitrogen ($N_2$) is a small, non-polar molecule with very weak London dispersion forces and a very low critical temperature ($126$ K).

Therefore, nitrogen gas is the most difficult to liquefy among the given options.

Answer: Nitrogen gas

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 2)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{SO}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cl}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NH}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{O}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{O}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The ease of liquefaction of a gas depends on the strength of its intermolecular forces, which is reflected in its critical temperature. Gases with higher critical temperatures are easier to liquefy.

$\mathrm{NH}_3$ exhibits strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding.

$\mathrm{SO}_2$ is a polar molecule with strong dipole-dipole interactions.

$\mathrm{Cl}_2$ is a non-polar molecule but has a relatively large molecular mass, resulting in significant London dispersion forces.

$\mathrm{O}_2$ is a small, non-polar diatomic molecule with very weak London dispersion forces and a low critical temperature. Therefore, it is the most difficult to liquefy among the given gases.

Answer: $\mathrm{O}_2$

</details>

- **(B)** helium
- **(C)** chlorine
- **(D)** nitrogen

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>
