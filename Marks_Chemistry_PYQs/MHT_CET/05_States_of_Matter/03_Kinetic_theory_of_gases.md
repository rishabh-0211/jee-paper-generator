### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following statements is true about ideal gas ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It shows deviation from Boyle's law and Charles' law.
- **(B)** Its molecules are not perfectly elastic.
- **(C)** It does not exist.
- **(D)** Intermolecular attraction is present in it, hence a collision takes place with loss of kinetic energy.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) It does not exist.**

**Detailed Solution:**

An ideal gas is a hypothetical concept and does not exist in reality. Real gases behave as ideal gases only under conditions of low pressure and high temperature.

According to the kinetic theory of gases, an ideal gas strictly obeys Boyle's law, Charles' law, and other gas laws under all conditions of temperature and pressure. The collisions between its molecules are perfectly elastic, and there are no intermolecular forces of attraction between its molecules.

Thus, the statement that it does not exist is true.

Answer: It does not exist.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

According to the kinetic molecular theory of gases, the average kinetic energy of gas molecules:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Increases with increase in pressure
- **(B)** Decreases with increase in volume
- **(C)** Is directly proportional to the absolute temperature
- **(D)** Is the same for all gases at the same volume

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Is directly proportional to the absolute temperature**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the kinetic molecular theory of gases, the average translational kinetic energy of a gas molecule is given by $E = \dfrac{3}{2}kT$, where $k$ is the Boltzmann constant and $T$ is the absolute temperature.

For one mole of an ideal gas, the average kinetic energy is $E = \dfrac{3}{2}RT$, where $R$ is the universal gas constant.

This shows that the average kinetic energy of gas molecules depends only on the absolute temperature and is directly proportional to it. It is independent of pressure, volume, and the nature of the gas.

Answer: Is directly proportional to the absolute temperature

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Argon possesses

**Options:**

- **(A)** translational motion only
- **(B)** translational + rotational motion
- **(C)** translational + vibrational motion
- **(D)** translational $+$ rotational $+$ vibrational motion

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) translational motion only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Argon is a monatomic gas, thus its molecules can move in any direction in space. Hence, it can have three independent motions (all translational). Therefore, argon possesses only translational motion.

</details>
