### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify geometry of $[\text{Ni(Cl)}_4]^{2-}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Square planar
- **(B)** Tetrahedral
- **(C)** Octahedral
- **(D)** Trigonal bipyramidal

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Tetrahedral**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given complex is $[\text{Ni(Cl)}_4]^{2-}$.

Let the oxidation state of Ni be $x$.

$x + 4(-1) = -2 \Rightarrow x = +2$

The electronic configuration of Ni is $[\text{Ar}] 3d^8 4s^2$.

The electronic configuration of $\text{Ni}^{2+}$ is $[\text{Ar}] 3d^8$.

The ligand $\text{Cl}^-$ is a weak field ligand, so it does not cause pairing of the unpaired electrons in the $3d$ orbitals.

To accommodate four pairs of electrons from the four $\text{Cl}^-$ ligands, the complex undergoes $sp^3$ hybridization using one $4s$ and three $4p$ empty orbitals.

The $sp^3$ hybridization corresponds to a tetrahedral geometry.

Answer: Tetrahedral

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What type of hybridization is present in $[\text{CoF}_6]^{3-}$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $d^2sp^3$
- **(B)** $sp^3d^2$
- **(C)** $dsp^2$
- **(D)** $sp^3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $sp^3d^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given complex is $[\text{CoF}_6]^{3-}$.

Let the oxidation state of Co be $x$.

$x + 6(-1) = -3 \Rightarrow x = +3$

The atomic number of Co is 27. The electronic configuration of Co is $[\text{Ar}] 3d^7 4s^2$.

The electronic configuration of $\text{Co}^{3+}$ is $[\text{Ar}] 3d^6$.

Fluoride ($\text{F}^-$) is a weak field ligand, so it does not cause the pairing of $3d$ electrons against Hund's rule.

Since the inner $3d$ orbitals are not available for hybridization, the complex utilizes the outer $4d$ orbitals.

For a coordination number of 6, one $4s$, three $4p$, and two $4d$ orbitals undergo hybridization to form six $sp^3d^2$ hybrid orbitals.

Thus, the hybridization is $sp^3d^2$ and it forms an outer orbital (high spin) octahedral complex.

Answer: $sp^3d^2$

</details>
