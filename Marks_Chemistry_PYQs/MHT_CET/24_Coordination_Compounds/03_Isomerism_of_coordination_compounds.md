### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following coordination compounds exhibits optical isomerism?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Pentaamminenitrocobalt (III) iodide
- **(B)** Diamminedichloroplatinum (II)
- **(C)** trans-Dicyanobis (ethyleneddiamine) chromium (III) chloride
- **(D)** Tris(ethylenediamine)cobalt(III) bromide.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Tris(ethylenediamine)cobalt(III) bromide.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Pentaamminenitrocobalt (III) iodide is $[Co(NH_3)_5(NO_2)]I_2$, which is an octahedral complex of the type $MA_5B$. It has a plane of symmetry and is optically inactive.

Diamminedichloroplatinum (II) is $[Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]$, which is a square planar complex. All square planar complexes have a plane of symmetry (the molecular plane) and are optically inactive.

trans-Dicyanobis(ethylenediamine)chromium (III) chloride is $trans-[Cr(en)_2(CN)_2]Cl$. The trans isomer of an $M(AA)_2B_2$ complex has a plane of symmetry and a center of inversion, making it optically inactive.

Tris(ethylenediamine)cobalt(III) bromide is $[Co(en)_3]Br_3$, which is an octahedral complex of the type $M(AA)_3$. It lacks any plane of symmetry or center of inversion, so it exists as non-superimposable mirror images (d and l forms) and exhibits optical isomerism.

Answer: Tris(ethylenediamine)cobalt(III) bromide.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the isomerism exhibited by complex compounds $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]\text{Br}$ and $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]\text{SO}_4$

**Options:**

- **(A)** coordination isomers
- **(B)** geometrical isomers
- **(C)** optical isomers
- **(D)** ionisation isomers

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) ionisation isomers**

**Detailed Solution:**

The complex compounds $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]\text{Br}$ and $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]\text{SO}_4$ have the same molecular formula but yield different ions in aqueous solution.

$[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]\text{Br} \rightleftharpoons [\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]^+ + \text{Br}^-$

$[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]\text{SO}_4 \rightleftharpoons [\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]^{2+} + \text{SO}_4^{2-}$

This type of isomerism, which arises due to the exchange of ions between the coordination sphere and the ionization sphere, is known as ionisation isomerism.

Answer: ionisation isomers

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following coordination compounds exhibits linkage isomerism?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $[\text{Co(en)}_3]\text{Cl}_3$
- **(B)** $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{Cl}_2]\text{Cl}$
- **(C)** $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{NO}_2\text{Cl}]\text{Br}$
- **(D)** $[\text{Co(NH}_3)_5\text{Cl}]\text{Br}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{NO}_2\text{Cl}]\text{Br}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Linkage isomerism arises in a coordination compound containing an ambidentate ligand.

An ambidentate ligand can bind to the central metal ion through more than one type of donor atom.

Among the given options, $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{NO}_2\text{Cl}]\text{Br}$ contains the $\text{NO}_2^-$ ligand.

The $\text{NO}_2^-$ ligand is ambidentate and can coordinate to the cobalt ion either through the nitrogen atom ($-\text{NO}_2$, nitrito-N) or through an oxygen atom ($-\text{ONO}$, nitrito-O).

Hence, $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{NO}_2\text{Cl}]\text{Br}$ exhibits linkage isomerism.

The other complexes do not contain any ambidentate ligand.

Answer: $[\text{Co(en)}_2\text{NO}_2\text{Cl}]\text{Br}$

</details>

What type of isomerism is exhibited by the following coordinate complexes?
$[\text{CO(NH}_3)_6][\text{Cr(CN)}_6]$ and $[\text{Cr(NH}_3)_6][\text{CO(CN)}_6]$

- **(D)** Linkage Isomerism.

- **(B)** $[\text{Co(NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]\text{Br}$ and $[\text{Co(NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]\text{SO}_4$
- **(C)** $[\text{Co(NH}_3)_6][\text{Cr(CN)}_6]$ and $[\text{Cr(NH}_3)_6][\text{Co(CN)}_6]$
- **(D)** $[\text{Cr(H}_2\text{O})_6]\text{Cl}_3$ and $[\text{Cr(H}_2\text{O})_5\text{Cl}]\text{Cl}_2 \cdot \text{H}_2\text{O}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Detailed Solution:**

Solvate isomers differ in the number of solvent molecules directly bonded to the metal ion as ligands versus those present as free solvent molecules in the crystal lattice.

$[\text{Co(NH}_3)_5\text{SO}_4]\text{Br}$ and $[\text{Co(NH}_3)_5\text{Br}]\text{SO}_4$ are ionization isomers.

$[\text{Co(NH}_3)_6][\text{Cr(CN)}_6]$ and $[\text{Cr(NH}_3)_6][\text{Co(CN)}_6]$ are coordination isomers.

Answer: $[\text{Cr(H}_2\text{O})_6]\text{Cl}_3$ and $[\text{Cr(H}_2\text{O})_5\text{Cl}]\text{Cl}_2 \cdot \text{H}_2\text{O}$

### Q7. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following type of isomerism is found in compounds $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{NO}_2)]$ and $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{ONO})]$?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Geometrical isomerism
- **(B)** linkage isomerism
- **(C)** Optical isomerism
- **(D)** Coordination isomerism

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) linkage isomerism**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given complexes are $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{NO}_2)]$ and $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{ONO})]$.

Both complexes have the same molecular formula but differ in the mode of attachment of the $\text{NO}_2^{-}$ ligand to the central metal atom.

The $\text{NO}_2^{-}$ ion is an ambidentate ligand, meaning it can bind to the central metal atom through either the nitrogen atom or the oxygen atom.

In $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{NO}_2)]$, the ligand is coordinated through the nitrogen atom.

In $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_5(\text{ONO})]$, the ligand is coordinated through the oxygen atom.

Isomerism arising due to the different donor atoms of an ambidentate ligand is known as linkage isomerism.

Answer: linkage isomerism

### Q8. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

- **(A)** $[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)(\text{H}_2\text{O})\,\text{Cl}_2]$
- **(B)** $[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{ClBr}]^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_2\,\text{Cl}_2$
- **(D)** $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{Cl}_2]^+$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{ClBr}]^{2+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The general formula $\text{MA}_4\text{BC}$ represents an octahedral complex with one central metal atom $\text{M}$, four identical ligands $\text{A}$, and two different ligands $\text{B}$ and $\text{C}$.

Analyzing the given options:

$[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)(\text{H}_2\text{O})\,\text{Cl}_2]$ is of the type $\text{MA}_2\text{BC}$ (where $\text{A} = \text{Cl}$, $\text{B} = \text{NH}_3$, $\text{C} = \text{H}_2\text{O}$).

$[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{ClBr}]^{2+}$ is of the type $\text{MA}_4\text{BC}$ (where $\text{A} = \text{NH}_3$, $\text{B} = \text{Cl}$, $\text{C} = \text{Br}$).

$\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_2\,\text{Cl}_2$ is of the type $\text{MA}_2\text{B}_2$ (where $\text{A} = \text{NH}_3$, $\text{B} = \text{Cl}$).

$[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{Cl}_2]^+$ is of the type $\text{MA}_4\text{B}_2$ (where $\text{A} = \text{NH}_3$, $\text{B} = \text{Cl}$).

Thus, $[\text{Pt}(\text{NH}_3)_4\,\text{ClBr}]^{2+}$ is the example of $\text{MA}_4\text{BC}$ type complex, which exhibits geometrical isomerism (cis and trans forms), and these geometrical isomers are diastereoisomers.

### Q9. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the ionization isomer of $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}(\text{NO}_2)]\,\text{Cl}$ from the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,(\text{O}_2\text{N})]\,\text{Cl}_2$
- **(B)** $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}_2]\,(\text{NO}_2)$
- **(C)** $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}(\text{ONO})]\,\text{Cl}$
- **(D)** $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}_2(\text{NO}_2)]\,\text{H}_2\text{O}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}_2]\,(\text{NO}_2)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ionization isomerism occurs when a ligand inside the coordination sphere is exchanged with an anion outside the coordination sphere (counter ion), resulting in a complex that yields different ions in solution.

In the given complex $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}(\text{NO}_2)]\,\text{Cl}$, the counter ion is $\text{Cl}^-$ and the ligands are $\text{H}_2\text{O}$, $\text{Cl}^-$, and $\text{NO}_2^-$.

By exchanging the $\text{NO}_2^-$ ligand inside the coordination sphere with the $\text{Cl}^-$ counter ion outside, we get the ionization isomer $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}_2]\,(\text{NO}_2)$.

The original complex yields $\text{Cl}^-$ ions in aqueous solution, whereas its ionization isomer yields $\text{NO}_2^-$ ions.

Answer: $[\text{Cr}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4\,\text{Cl}_2]\,(\text{NO}_2)$

</details>
