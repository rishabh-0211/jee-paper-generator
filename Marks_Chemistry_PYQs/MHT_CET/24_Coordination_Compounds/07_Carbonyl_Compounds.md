### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the number of electrons lost by Cr in a complex $\left[\mathrm{Cr}(\mathrm{CO})_6\right]$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the oxidation state of Cr in the complex $[\mathrm{Cr}(\mathrm{CO})_6]$ be $x$.

The ligand CO is a neutral molecule, so its charge is $0$.

The overall charge on the complex is $0$.

Therefore, $x + 6(0) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 0$.

Since the oxidation state of Cr is $0$, it has not lost any electrons.

Answer: 0

</details>
