### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the formula of Bis(ethylenediamine)dithiocyanatoplatinum(IV)ion from following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $[\text{Pt(en)}_2(\text{NCS})]^{2+}$
- **(B)** $\text{Pt}[(\text{en})_2(\text{SCN})_2]^{4+}$
- **(C)** $[\text{Pt(en)}_2(\text{SCN})_2]^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\text{Pt}[(\text{en})_3(\text{SCN})_2]^{4+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $[\text{Pt(en)}_2(\text{SCN})_2]^{2+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The central metal ion is Platinum in the $+4$ oxidation state, denoted as $\text{Pt}^{4+}$.

The ligands are:
Bis(ethylenediamine) $\rightarrow$ two ethylenediamine (en) molecules. Since 'en' is a neutral ligand, its charge contribution is $0$.
dithiocyanato $\rightarrow$ two thiocyanato ($\text{SCN}^{-}$) ions. Each $\text{SCN}^{-}$ has a charge of $-1$, so their total charge contribution is $-2$.

The net charge on the complex ion is calculated as:
Net charge = Charge of Pt + Charge of $2(\text{en})$ + Charge of $2(\text{SCN}^{-})$
Net charge = $+4 + 0 + 2(-1) = +2$

Writing the formula by placing the central metal atom followed by the ligands in alphabetical order inside the coordination sphere, we get:
$[\text{Pt(en)}_2(\text{SCN})_2]^{2+}$

Answer: $[\text{Pt(en)}_2(\text{SCN})_2]^{2+}$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following is a formula of sodium hexafluoroaluminate(III)?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}_3\left[\mathrm{AlF}_6\right]$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Na}\left[\mathrm{AlF}_6\right]$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Na}_2\left[\mathrm{AlF}_6\right]^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\quad\left[\mathrm{Al}(\mathrm{NaF})_6\right]^{2+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Na}_3\left[\mathrm{AlF}_6\right]$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The oxidation state of aluminium is $+3$.

The ligand is fluoride, $\mathrm{F}^{-}$, with a charge of $-1$.

The complex anion is hexafluoroaluminate(III), which can be represented as $[\mathrm{AlF}_6]^{x}$.

The net charge on the complex anion is $x = +3 + 6(-1) = -3$.

Thus, the complex anion is $[\mathrm{AlF}_6]^{3-}$.

To balance the $-3$ charge, three sodium ions ($\mathrm{Na}^{+}$) are required.

The formula of sodium hexafluoroaluminate(III) is $\mathrm{Na}_3[\mathrm{AlF}_6]$.

Answer: $\mathrm{Na}_3\left[\mathrm{AlF}_6\right]$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is IUPAC name of Ethylmethylisopropylamine?

**Options:**

- **(A)** N -Methyl- N -isopropylethanamine
- **(B)** N-Ethyl-N-methyl propan-1-amine
- **(C)** N-Ethyl-N-methylpropan-2-amine
- **(D)** N-Ethyl-N-isopropylmethanamine

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) N-Ethyl-N-methylpropan-2-amine**

**Detailed Solution:**

The structure of ethylmethylisopropylamine is $CH_3-CH_2-N(CH_3)-CH(CH_3)_2$.

The longest continuous carbon chain attached to the nitrogen atom is the isopropyl group, which contains 3 carbon atoms. Thus, the parent chain is propane, and since the nitrogen is attached to the second carbon of this chain, the parent amine is propan-2-amine.

The other two groups attached to the nitrogen atom are an ethyl group and a methyl group. According to IUPAC nomenclature rules, substituents on the nitrogen atom are indicated by the prefix 'N-' and are listed in alphabetical order.

Therefore, the IUPAC name of the compound is N-Ethyl-N-methylpropan-2-amine.

Answer: N-Ethyl-N-methylpropan-2-amine

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left[\mathrm{Fe}\left(\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}\right)_5(\mathrm{NCS})\right]^{2+}$**

The name of the complex ion is pentaaquaisothiocyanatoiron(III) ion.

Pentaaqua indicates the presence of five water molecules, $(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O})_5$, as ligands.

Isothiocyanato indicates the presence of an $\mathrm{NCS}^{-}$ ligand, where nitrogen is the donor atom.

Iron(III) indicates that the central metal atom, $\mathrm{Fe}$, is in the $+3$ oxidation state.

The overall charge on the complex ion is the sum of the oxidation state of the metal and the charges of the ligands:
Charge $= +3 + 5(0) + (-1) = +2$

Therefore, the formula of the complex ion is $\left[\mathrm{Fe}\left(\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}\right)_5(\mathrm{NCS})\right]^{2+}$.
