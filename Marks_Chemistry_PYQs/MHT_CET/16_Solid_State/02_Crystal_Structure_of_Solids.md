### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the number of unit cells present in tetragonal crystal systems?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1$
- **(B)** $2$
- **(C)** $3$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The seven crystal systems give rise to $14$ Bravais lattices.

For the tetragonal crystal system, the axial distances are $a = b \neq c$ and the axial angles are $\alpha = \beta = \gamma = 90^{\circ}$.

There are $2$ possible variations of unit cells in the tetragonal crystal system: Primitive (or Simple) tetragonal and Body-centered tetragonal.

Thus, the number of unit cells present in the tetragonal crystal system is $2$.

Answer: $2$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A compound is formed by two elements $A$ and
B. The atoms of element B form ccp structure.
The atoms of A occupy $\frac{1}{3}$ of tetrahedral voids.
What is the formula of the compound?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{A}_2 \mathrm{~B}_3$
- **(B)** AB
- **(C)** $\mathrm{AB}_2$
- **(D)** $\quad \mathrm{AB}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{A}_2 \mathrm{~B}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the number of atoms of element B forming the ccp structure be $N$.

The number of tetrahedral voids in the ccp structure is $2N$.

The atoms of element A occupy $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the tetrahedral voids.

Number of atoms of A $= \dfrac{1}{3} \times 2N = \dfrac{2N}{3}$.

The ratio of atoms of A to B is $\dfrac{2N}{3} : N = 2 : 3$.

The formula of the compound is $A_2B_3$.

Answer: $\mathrm{A}_2 \mathrm{~B}_3$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following is an example of one dimensional nanostructure?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Nano rings
- **(B)** Nano tubes
- **(C)** Layers and coatings
- **(D)** Quantum dots

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Nano tubes**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nanomaterials are classified based on the number of dimensions that are in the nanoscale range ($1$ to $100$ nm):

Zero-dimensional ($0$D) nanostructures have all three dimensions in the nanoscale. Examples include quantum dots, nanoparticles, and nanorings.

One-dimensional ($1$D) nanostructures have two dimensions in the nanoscale and one dimension outside it. Examples include nanotubes, nanowires, and nanorods.

Two-dimensional ($2$D) nanostructures have one dimension in the nanoscale and two dimensions outside it. Examples include thin films, layers, and coatings.

Therefore, nanotubes are an example of a one-dimensional nanostructure.

Answer: Nano tubes

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Calculate the number of atoms in 0.3 gram metal if it forms bec structure $\left[\rho \times \mathrm{a}^3=3 \times 10^{-22} \mathrm{~g}\right]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.0 \times 10^{21}$
- **(B)** $2.0 \times 10^{21}$
- **(C)** $3.0 \times 10^{21}$
- **(D)** $4.0 \times 10^{21}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2.0 \times 10^{21}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mass of one unit cell is given by $\rho \times a^3 = 3 \times 10^{-22} \text{ g}$.

Number of unit cells in $0.3 \text{ g}$ of the metal is $\dfrac{0.3}{3 \times 10^{-22}} = 10^{21}$.

For a body-centered cubic (bcc) structure, the number of atoms per unit cell is $Z = 2$.

Total number of atoms is $2 \times 10^{21}$.

Answer: $2.0 \times 10^{21}$

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{CaCO}_3, \mathrm{NaNO}_3$**

Two or more substances having the same crystal structure called isomorphous.
