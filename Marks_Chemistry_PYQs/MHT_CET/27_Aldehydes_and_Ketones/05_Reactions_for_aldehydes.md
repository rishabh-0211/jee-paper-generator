### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the test from following so that aldehyde when boiled with ammoniacal silver nitrate solution deposts silver.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Schiff's test
- **(B)** Tollen's test
- **(C)** Gehling test
- **(D)** Iodoform test

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Tollen's test**

**Detailed Solution:**

Tollen's reagent is an ammoniacal silver nitrate solution, which contains the complex ion $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$.

When an aldehyde is heated with Tollen's reagent, the aldehyde is oxidized to the corresponding carboxylate anion, and the silver ions are reduced to metallic silver.

$RCHO + 2[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+ + 3OH^- \rightarrow RCOO^- + 2Ag \downarrow + 4NH_3 + 2H_2O$

The metallic silver deposits on the inner walls of the test tube, forming a silver mirror. This test is known as Tollen's test.

Answer: Tollen's test

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the product obtained in the reaction?
\(\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CHO} \xrightarrow[\text { (i) } \mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{O}^{+}]{\text {(i) } \mathrm{LAH}_4} \text { product }\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{OH}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{COOH}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CHO}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{OH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Lithium aluminium hydride ($\mathrm{LiAlH}_4$) is a strong reducing agent that reduces aldehydes to primary alcohols.

It generally does not reduce isolated carbon-carbon double bonds ($\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{C}$).

In the given reactant $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CHO}$, the double bond is isolated from the aldehyde group. Therefore, only the $-\mathrm{CHO}$ group is reduced to $-\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{OH}$ while the double bond remains intact.

The reaction is:

$\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CHO} \xrightarrow[\text{(ii) } \mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{O}^{+}]{\text{(i) } \mathrm{LiAlH}_4} \mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{OH}$

Answer: $\mathrm{CH}_3-\mathrm{CH}=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{OH}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following compounds does not undergo haloform reaction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethanal
- **(B)** Propanal
- **(C)** Propanone
- **(D)** Butanone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Propanal**

**Detailed Solution:**

The haloform reaction is given by compounds containing a $CH_3-CO-$ group (methyl ketones) or compounds that can be oxidized to such a group (like $CH_3-CH(OH)-$ groups).

Ethanal ($CH_3-CHO$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group.

Propanone ($CH_3-CO-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group.

Butanone ($CH_3-CO-CH_2-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group.

Propanal ($CH_3-CH_2-CHO$) does not contain a $CH_3-CO-$ group and cannot be oxidized to one. Thus, it does not undergo the haloform reaction.

Answer: Propanal

</details>
