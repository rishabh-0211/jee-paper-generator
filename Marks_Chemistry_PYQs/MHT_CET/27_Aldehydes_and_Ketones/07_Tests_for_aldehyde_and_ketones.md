### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds shows Silver mirror test ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetone
- **(B)** Acetaldehyde
- **(C)** Ethanol
- **(D)** Benzophenone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Acetaldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Silver mirror test is given by Tollens' reagent, which is an ammoniacal silver nitrate solution.

Aldehydes are oxidized by Tollens' reagent to their corresponding carboxylate ions, and $Ag^+$ is reduced to metallic silver, which forms a silver mirror on the inner walls of the test tube.

Ketones and alcohols generally do not give this test.

Among the given options, Acetaldehyde is an aldehyde and thus gives a positive Silver mirror test. Acetone and Benzophenone are ketones, and Ethanol is an alcohol.

The reaction is:
$CH_3CHO + 2[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+ + 3OH^- \rightarrow CH_3COO^- + 2Ag \downarrow + 4NH_3 + 2H_2O$

Answer: Acetaldehyde

</details>

- **(D)** Ethers

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Aldehydes**

Ketones, carboxylic acids, and ethers do not respond to this test.

Therefore, aldehydes exhibit the Schiff test.

Answer: Aldehydes

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of following observations is found in Tollens test for aldehydes?

**Options:**

- **(A)** The formation of pink or magenta color upon the addition of the reagent to the sample solution.
- **(B)** Formation of an Ag layer on sides of test tube upon addition and warming of the reagent.
- **(C)** Formation of brick-red precipitate upon heating with reagent.
- **(D)** Appearance of red color upon shaking with reagent.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Formation of an Ag layer on sides of test tube upon addition and warming of the reagent.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Tollens' reagent is an ammoniacal silver nitrate solution, $[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+$.

When an aldehyde is warmed with Tollens' reagent, the aldehyde is oxidized to the corresponding carboxylate ion, while the silver ions are reduced to metallic silver.

$RCHO + 2[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+ + 3OH^- \rightarrow RCOO^- + 2Ag\downarrow + 4NH_3 + 2H_2O$

The metallic silver deposits on the inner walls of the test tube, forming a characteristic silver mirror or an Ag layer. This observation confirms the presence of an aldehyde group.

The formation of a pink or magenta color is observed in Schiff's test, and a brick-red precipitate is observed in Fehling's test.

Answer: Formation of an Ag layer on sides of test tube upon addition and warming of the reagent.

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following compounds dose NOT respond positively to the iodoform test ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetaldehyde
- **(B)** Propan-2-ol
- **(C)** Propanone
- **(D)** Pentan-3-one

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Pentan-3-one**

**Detailed Solution:**

The iodoform test is given by compounds containing a methyl ketone group ($CH_3-CO-$) or a secondary alcohol group with a terminal methyl ($CH_3-CH(OH)-$), which gets oxidized to a methyl ketone by the reagent.

Acetaldehyde ($CH_3-CHO$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group and gives a positive test.

Propan-2-ol ($CH_3-CH(OH)-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CH(OH)-$ group and gives a positive test.

Propanone ($CH_3-CO-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group and gives a positive test.

Pentan-3-one ($CH_3-CH_2-CO-CH_2-CH_3$) does not contain a $CH_3-CO-$ group. Therefore, it does not respond positively to the iodoform test.

Answer: Pentan-3-one

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following compounds can not form iodoform?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyl methyl ketone
- **(B)** Isopropyl alcohol
- **(C)** 3-Methyl - 2- butanone
- **(D)** Isobutyl alcohol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Isobutyl alcohol**

**Detailed Solution:**

The iodoform test is given by compounds containing either a methyl ketone group ($CH_3-CO-$) or a secondary alcohol group with a terminal methyl ($CH_3-CH(OH)-$ group), as well as ethanol and acetaldehyde.

Ethyl methyl ketone ($CH_3-CO-CH_2-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group, so it gives a positive iodoform test.

Isopropyl alcohol ($CH_3-CH(OH)-CH_3$) contains a $CH_3-CH(OH)-$ group, so it gives a positive iodoform test.

3-Methyl-2-butanone ($CH_3-CO-CH(CH_3)_2$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group, so it gives a positive iodoform test.

Isobutyl alcohol ($(CH_3)_2CH-CH_2-OH$) is a primary alcohol (other than ethanol) and does not contain a $CH_3-CH(OH)-$ group. Upon oxidation, it forms isobutyraldehyde, which lacks a $CH_3-CO-$ group. Therefore, it does not give the iodoform test.

Answer: Isobutyl alcohol

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Acetaldehyde, when heated in the presence of sodium hydroxide and iodine forms

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sodium formate
- **(B)** Sodium acetate
- **(C)** Ethanol
- **(D)** Iodoethane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Sodium formate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetaldehyde ($CH_3CHO$) contains a $CH_3-CO-$ group, so it undergoes the iodoform reaction when heated with iodine and sodium hydroxide.

The reaction proceeds as follows:

$CH_3CHO + 3I_2 + 4NaOH \xrightarrow{\Delta} CHI_3 + HCOONa + 3NaI + 3H_2O$

The products formed are iodoform ($CHI_3$) as a yellow precipitate and sodium formate ($HCOONa$).

Answer: Sodium formate

</details>

### Q11. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds when treated with ammoniacal silver nitrate exhibits silver mirror test?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethanol
- **(B)** Ethanal
- **(C)** Ethoxyethane
- **(D)** Ethanoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ethanal**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ammoniacal silver nitrate is known as Tollens' reagent. It is a mild oxidizing agent used to distinguish aldehydes from ketones and other functional groups.

Tollens' reagent oxidizes aldehydes to their corresponding carboxylate ions and gets reduced to metallic silver, which deposits on the inner walls of the test tube to form a silver mirror.

Among the given options, ethanal is an aldehyde. Ethanol (an alcohol), ethoxyethane (an ether), and ethanoic acid (a carboxylic acid) do not react with Tollens' reagent.

The reaction for ethanal is:
$CH_3CHO + 2[Ag(NH_3)_2]^+ + 3OH^- \rightarrow CH_3COO^- + 2Ag\downarrow + 4NH_3 + 2H_2O$

Thus, ethanal exhibits the silver mirror test.

Answer: Ethanal

</details>
