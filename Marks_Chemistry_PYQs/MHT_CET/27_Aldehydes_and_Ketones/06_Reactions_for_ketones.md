### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the reagent ' R ' used in the following reaction.
Ketone $\xrightarrow{\mathrm{R}}$ Semicarbazone

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{NH}_2 \mathrm{OH}$.
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ketones react with semicarbazide to form semicarbazones.

The chemical formula of semicarbazide is $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2$.

The reaction is given by:

$\mathrm{R}_2\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{N}-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2 \rightarrow \mathrm{R}_2\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{N}-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2 + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}$

Answer: $\mathrm{NH}_2-\mathrm{NH}-\mathrm{CONH}_2$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2019 (Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following does not given yellow soild on treatment with sodium hypoiodite?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetophenone
- **(B)** Pentan – 3 – one
- **(C)** Pentan – 2 –one
- **(D)** Ethanal

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Pentan – 3 – one**

**Detailed Solution:**

Pentan – 3 – one does not give yellow solid on treatment with sodium hypoiodite because this reaction is given by only methyl ketones. Ketones that have at least one methyl group linked to the carbonyl carbon atom are oxidised by sodium hypoiodite to sodium salts of corresponding carboxylic acids. This reaction is called iodoform reaction.

</details>
