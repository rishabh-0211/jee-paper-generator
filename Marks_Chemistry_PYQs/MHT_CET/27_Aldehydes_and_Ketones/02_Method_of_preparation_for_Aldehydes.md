### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds is used to prepare aliphatic aldehydes by Rosenmund reduction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $R-\text{COOH}$
- **(B)** $R-\text{CN}$
- **(C)** $R-\text{COCl}$
- **(D)** $R-O-R$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $R-\text{COCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Rosenmund reduction is the catalytic hydrogenation of an acyl chloride (acid chloride) to an aldehyde.

The reaction is given by:
$R-\text{COCl} + H_2 \xrightarrow{Pd/BaSO_4} R-\text{CHO} + HCl$

Thus, $R-\text{COCl}$ is used to prepare aliphatic aldehydes by Rosenmund reduction.

Answer: $R-\text{COCl}$

</details>

- **(B)** $\text{R}-\text{CN} \xrightarrow[\large{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+}]{\large{\text{SnCl}_2,\text{HCl}}} \text{R}-\text{CHO} + \text{NH}_4\text{Cl}$
- **(C)** $\text{R}-\text{CHO} \xrightarrow[\large{\Delta}]{\large{\text{Zn}-\text{Hg conc.HCl}}} \text{R}-\text{CH}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
- **(D)** $\text{R}-\text{CO}-\text{R} \xrightarrow[\large{\text{ii) KOH, HO}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{OH},\ \Delta}]{\large{\text{i) H}_2\text{N}-\text{NH}_2}} \text{R}-\text{CH}_2-\text{R}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{R}-\text{CO}-\text{Cl} \xrightarrow[\large{\text{Pd}-\text{BaSO}_4}]{\large{\text{H}_2}} \text{R}-\text{CHO} + \text{HCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Rosenmund reduction is the catalytic hydrogenation of an acid chloride to an aldehyde.

The catalyst used is palladium on barium sulfate ($\text{Pd}-\text{BaSO}_4$), which is partially poisoned to prevent further reduction of the aldehyde to an alcohol.

$\text{R}-\text{CO}-\text{Cl} \xrightarrow[\text{Pd}-\text{BaSO}_4]{\text{H}_2} \text{R}-\text{CHO} + \text{HCl}$

Wolff-Kishner reduction: Reduction of aldehydes or ketones to alkanes using hydrazine and a strong base like $\text{KOH}$ in ethylene glycol.

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the 'product' in the following reaction.
Pent - 3 - enenitrile $\xrightarrow[\large{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+}]{\large{\text{Al H (i-Bu)}_2}}$ Product

**Options:**

- **(A)** Pent - 3 - en - 1 - amine
- **(B)** Pentanal
- **(C)** Pent - 3 - enal
- **(D)** Pent - 3 - en -1 - ol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Pent - 3 - enal**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reactant is pent-3-enenitrile, which has the structure $\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CN}$.

Diisobutylaluminium hydride (DIBAL-H), represented as $\text{AlH(i-Bu)}_2$, is a selective reducing agent. It reduces nitriles to imines at low temperatures, which upon subsequent acidic hydrolysis ($\text{H}_3\text{O}^+$) yield aldehydes.

DIBAL-H does not reduce isolated carbon-carbon double bonds.

The reaction proceeds as follows:
$\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{C}\equiv\text{N} \xrightarrow{\text{AlH(i-Bu)}_2} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}=\text{N}-\text{Al(i-Bu)}_2$

$\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}=\text{N}-\text{Al(i-Bu)}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CHO}$

The final product is pent-3-enal.

Answer: Pent - 3 - enal

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Benzonitrile on reduction with stannous chloride in presence of hydrochloric acid followed by acid hydrolysis forms,

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzal chloride
- **(B)** Benzoyl chloride
- **(C)** Benzophenone
- **(D)** Benzaldehyde

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Benzaldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reduction of benzonitrile with stannous chloride ($SnCl_2$) and hydrochloric acid ($HCl$) followed by acid hydrolysis is known as the Stephen reaction.

In the first step, benzonitrile is reduced to an imine hydrochloride intermediate:
$C_6H_5-C\equiv N + 2[H] + HCl \xrightarrow{SnCl_2/HCl} C_6H_5-CH=NH\cdot HCl$

In the second step, acid hydrolysis of the imine hydrochloride yields benzaldehyde:
$C_6H_5-CH=NH\cdot HCl + H_2O \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} C_6H_5-CHO + NH_4Cl$

Thus, the final product is benzaldehyde.

Answer: Benzaldehyde

</details>

Which reagent from following is used for preparation of aliphatic aldehyde from nitriles?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CrO}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2$ in $\mathrm{CS}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CrO}_3$ in acetic anhydride

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{SnCl}_2, \mathrm{HCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The preparation of aliphatic aldehydes from nitriles is carried out using Stephen reaction.

In this reaction, nitriles are reduced to the corresponding imine hydrochloride by stannous chloride ($\mathrm{SnCl}_2$) in the presence of hydrochloric acid ($\mathrm{HCl}$). The imine hydrochloride on acid hydrolysis gives the corresponding aliphatic aldehyde.

$R-C \equiv N + 2[H] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{SnCl}_2, \mathrm{HCl}} R-CH=NH \cdot \mathrm{HCl} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{O}^+} R-CHO + \mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl}$

$\mathrm{CrO}_2\mathrm{Cl}_2$ in $\mathrm{CS}_2$ and $\mathrm{CrO}_3$ in acetic anhydride are used for the oxidation of toluene to benzaldehyde (Etard reaction and related oxidation).

$\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{HCl}$ with anhydrous $\mathrm{AlCl}_3$ is used for the preparation of benzaldehyde from benzene (Gattermann-Koch reaction).
