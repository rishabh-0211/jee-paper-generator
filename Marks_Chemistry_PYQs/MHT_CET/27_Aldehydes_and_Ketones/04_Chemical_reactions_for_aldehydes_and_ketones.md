Which of the following reactions is an example of a disproportionation reaction?

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Cannizzaro reaction.**

**Detailed Solution:**

A disproportionation reaction is a redox reaction in which the same substance undergoes both oxidation and reduction simultaneously.

In the Cannizzaro reaction, aldehydes lacking $\alpha$-hydrogens undergo self-oxidation and self-reduction in the presence of a concentrated base.
