### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the pair of ketone having both members as mixed ketones.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzophenone, acetone
- **(B)** Butanone, acetophenone
- **(C)** Butanone, acetone
- **(D)** Benzophenone, acetophenone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Butanone, acetophenone**

**Detailed Solution:**

A mixed (or unsymmetrical) ketone is one in which the two alkyl or aryl groups attached to the carbonyl carbon are different.

Benzophenone is diphenyl ketone ($C_6H_5-CO-C_6H_5$), which has two identical phenyl groups. It is a simple (symmetrical) ketone.

Acetone is dimethyl ketone ($CH_3-CO-CH_3$), which has two identical methyl groups. It is a simple (symmetrical) ketone.

Butanone is methyl ethyl ketone ($CH_3-CO-CH_2CH_3$), which has a methyl group and an ethyl group. It is a mixed ketone.

Acetophenone is methyl phenyl ketone ($C_6H_5-CO-CH_3$), which has a methyl group and a phenyl group. It is a mixed ketone.

Therefore, the pair in which both members are mixed ketones is butanone and acetophenone.

Answer: Butanone, acetophenone

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Propyne on reaction with water in presence of $40\%$ sulphuric acid and $1\%$ mercuric sulphate forms

**Options:**

- **(A)** Propan-1-ol
- **(B)** Propan-2-ol
- **(C)** Propanone
- **(D)** Propanal

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Propanone**

**Detailed Solution:**

Propyne undergoes hydration in the presence of dilute sulphuric acid and mercuric sulphate according to Markovnikov's rule.

The addition of a water molecule to the triple bond initially yields an unstable enol intermediate, prop-1-en-2-ol.

$CH_3-C \equiv CH + H_2O \xrightarrow{Hg^{2+}, H^+} CH_3-C(OH)=CH_2$

The enol intermediate rapidly undergoes tautomerization to form the more stable keto form, propanone.

$CH_3-C(OH)=CH_2 \rightleftharpoons CH_3-CO-CH_3$

Answer: Propanone

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Ethyl methyl ketone is obtained by heating calcium salts of

**Options:**

- **(A)** formic acid $+$ propionic acid
- **(B)** acetic acid + propionic acid
- **(C)** acetic acid only
- **(D)** acetic acid + methanoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) acetic acid + propionic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ethyl methyl ketone is an unsymmetric ketone, so two different acids (in the form of calcium salt) are used. The structure of ethyl methyl ketone is
$$
\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COCH}_{3}
$$
The structure shows the presence of 4 carbon atoms, thus the calcium salts must be of acetic acid and propionic acid to form $\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COCH}_{3}$.
$$
\begin{array}{r}
\left(\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COO}\right)_{2} \mathrm{Ca}+\left(\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COO}\right)_{2} \mathrm{Ca} \stackrel{\text { Distill }}{\longrightarrow} \\
\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COCH}_{3}+\mathrm{CaCO}_{3}
\end{array}
$$

</details>
