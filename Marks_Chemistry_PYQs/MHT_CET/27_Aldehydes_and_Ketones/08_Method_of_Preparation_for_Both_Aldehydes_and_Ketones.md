### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the name of the above reaction.
<img style="width: 300px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mht_cet_pyqs_2026/april/watermarked_images/15_april_shift_2__q92_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** Etard reaction
- **(B)** Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction
- **(C)** Stephen reaction
- **(D)** Rosenmund reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction shows the conversion of benzene to benzaldehyde using carbon monoxide ($CO$) and hydrogen chloride ($HCl$) in the presence of anhydrous aluminum chloride ($AlCl_3$) under high pressure.

This specific transformation is known as the Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction. In this reaction, a formyl cation is generated in situ from $CO$, $HCl$, and the Lewis acid catalyst ($AlCl_3$), which then undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution with benzene to form benzaldehyde.

Let us review the other options:
1. Etard reaction: Involves the oxidation of toluene to benzaldehyde using chromyl chloride ($CrO_2Cl_2$).
2. Stephen reaction: Involves the reduction of nitriles to aldehydes using tin(II) chloride ($SnCl_2$) and $HCl$, followed by hydrolysis.
3. Rosenmund reaction: Involves the catalytic hydrogenation of acid chlorides to aldehydes using palladium on barium sulfate ($Pd/BaSO_4$).

Therefore, the correct name for the given reaction is the Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction.

Answer: Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In Gatterman reaction, which of the following catalyst is used?

**Options:**

- **(A)** CO, HCl and anhyd. $\text{AlCl}_3$
- **(B)** Copper powder and HBr
- **(C)** Cu + Aq. $\text{NaNO}_2$
- **(D)** KOH + $\text{Br}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Copper powder and HBr**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Gatterman reaction is a modification of the Sandmeyer reaction used for the synthesis of aryl halides from aryl diazonium salts. In this reaction, finely divided copper powder is used as a catalyst along with a halogen acid such as $\text{HCl}$ or $\text{HBr}$.

$\text{Ar-N}_2^+ \text{X}^- \xrightarrow{\text{Cu powder / HX}} \text{Ar-X} + \text{N}_2$

Option (318007) represents the reagents for the Gatterman-Koch formylation reaction.

Option (318010) represents the reagents for the Hoffmann bromamide degradation reaction.

Therefore, copper powder and $\text{HBr}$ are used in the Gatterman reaction.

Answer: Copper powder and HBr

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following reagents is used in Gatterman-Koch formylation of arene?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{AlH}(\mathrm{i}-\mathrm{Bu})_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{HCl}$ (anhyd. $\mathrm{AlCl}_3$ )
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CrO}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2\left(\mathrm{CS}_2\right)$
- **(D)** DIBAI - H

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{HCl}$ (anhyd. $\mathrm{AlCl}_3$ )**

**Detailed Solution:**

Gatterman-Koch formylation is a reaction in which an arene (such as benzene) is treated with carbon monoxide ($\mathrm{CO}$) and hydrogen chloride ($\mathrm{HCl}$) in the presence of a Lewis acid catalyst like anhydrous aluminum chloride ($\mathrm{AlCl}_3$) and cuprous chloride ($\mathrm{CuCl}$) to form benzaldehyde.

$\mathrm{CrO}_2\mathrm{Cl}_2$ in $\mathrm{CS}_2$ is used in the Etard reaction to convert toluene to benzaldehyde.

DIBAL-H ($\mathrm{AlH}(\mathrm{i}-\mathrm{Bu})_2$) is a reducing agent used to reduce nitriles or esters to aldehydes.

Therefore, the reagent used in Gatterman-Koch formylation is $\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{HCl}$ (anhyd. $\mathrm{AlCl}_3$).

Answer: $\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{HCl}$ (anhyd. $\mathrm{AlCl}_3$ )

</details>
