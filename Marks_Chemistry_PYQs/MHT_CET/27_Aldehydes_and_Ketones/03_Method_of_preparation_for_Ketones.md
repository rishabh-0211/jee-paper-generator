### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product obtained when acetonitrile reacts with methyl magnesium chloride in dry ether followed by acid hydrolysis.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Propion aldehyde.
- **(B)** Ethyl methyl Ketone.
- **(C)** Acetone.
- **(D)** Acetaldehyde.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Acetone.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetonitrile is $CH_3CN$.

When acetonitrile reacts with methyl magnesium chloride ($CH_3MgCl$) in the presence of dry ether, a nucleophilic addition reaction takes place. The methyl carbanion from the Grignard reagent attacks the electrophilic carbon of the cyano group, forming an imine complex.

$CH_3-C \equiv N + CH_3MgCl \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} CH_3-C(CH_3)=NMgCl$

Upon subsequent acid hydrolysis, the imine complex is converted into a ketone.

$CH_3-C(CH_3)=NMgCl \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} CH_3-C(=O)-CH_3 + NH_3 + Mg(OH)Cl$

The final product is acetone.

Answer: Acetone.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Benzonitrile on reaction with a reagent in dry ether followed by hydrolysis forms benzophenone along with ammonia and hydroxymagnesium bromide. Identify the reagent used in above transformation.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgCl}$
- **(B)** $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{MgBr}$
- **(C)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgI}$
- **(D)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgBr}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgBr}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Benzonitrile ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CN}$) reacts with a Grignard reagent to form an imine salt, which on hydrolysis gives a ketone.

Since the product is benzophenone ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COC}_6\text{H}_5$), the Grignard reagent must provide a phenyl group ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5^-$).

The formation of hydroxymagnesium bromide ($\text{Mg(OH)Br}$) as a by-product indicates that the halogen in the Grignard reagent is bromine.

The reaction is:

$\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CN} + \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgBr} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} (\text{C}_6\text{H}_5)_2\text{C=NMgBr}$

$(\text{C}_6\text{H}_5)_2\text{C=NMgBr} + 2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow (\text{C}_6\text{H}_5)_2\text{C=O} + \text{NH}_3 + \text{Mg(OH)Br}$

The reagent used is $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgBr}$.

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product 'Y' in the following reaction $\text{Benzonitrile} \xrightarrow{\large{\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{MgBr} / \text{dry ether}}} X \xrightarrow{\large{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+}} Y + \text{MgBr(OH)} + \text{NH}_3$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzophenone
- **(B)** Benzylalcohol
- **(C)** Benzaldehyde
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Benzophenone**

**Detailed Solution:**

Benzonitrile is $C_6H_5CN$.

When benzonitrile reacts with phenylmagnesium bromide ($C_6H_5MgBr$) in the presence of dry ether, a nucleophilic addition takes place at the electrophilic carbon of the cyanide group, forming an imine salt intermediate ($X$).

$C_6H_5CN + C_6H_5MgBr \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} (C_6H_5)_2C=NMgBr$

Upon acid hydrolysis ($H_3O^+$), the imine salt intermediate ($X$) is converted into a ketone.

$(C_6H_5)_2C=NMgBr \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} (C_6H_5)_2C=O + NH_3 + MgBr(OH)$

The product $Y$ is $(C_6H_5)_2C=O$, which is benzophenone.

Answer: Benzophenone

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product obtained when Grignard reagent reacts with alkyl cyanide followed by acid hydrolysis.

**Options:**

- **(A)** an aldehyde
- **(B)** a ketone
- **(C)** a primary alcohol
- **(D)** a secondary alcohol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) a ketone**

**Detailed Solution:**

Grignard reagents ($R'MgX$) react with alkyl cyanides ($R-C\equiv N$) to form an imine salt intermediate.

$R-C\equiv N + R'MgX \rightarrow R-C(R')=NMgX$

Upon acid hydrolysis, the imine salt is converted into a ketone.

$R-C(R')=NMgX + H_3O^+ \rightarrow R-C(=O)-R' + NH_3 + Mg(OH)X$

Thus, the final product obtained is a ketone.

Answer: a ketone

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify substrate 'S' in the following reaction.
$\mathrm{S} \xrightarrow{\text { Dimethyl cadmium }} \text { Propanone }+ \text { Cadmium chloride }$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyl chloride
- **(B)** Ethylene dichloride
- **(C)** Ethanoyl chloride
- **(D)** Ethylidene dichloride

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Ethanoyl chloride**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of an acyl chloride with dialkyl cadmium yields a ketone.

The general reaction is:
$2 \mathrm{R-COCl} + \mathrm{R'_2Cd} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{R-CO-R'} + \mathrm{CdCl_2}$

Given that the product is propanone ($\mathrm{CH_3-CO-CH_3}$) and the reagent is dimethyl cadmium ($\mathrm{(CH_3)_2Cd}$), the alkyl group $\mathrm{R'}$ is $\mathrm{CH_3}$.

Thus, the acyl chloride must have $\mathrm{R} = \mathrm{CH_3}$, which corresponds to $\mathrm{CH_3-COCl}$.

$\mathrm{CH_3-COCl}$ is ethanoyl chloride (acetyl chloride).

The balanced chemical equation is:
$2 \mathrm{CH_3-COCl} + \mathrm{(CH_3)_2Cd} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{CH_3-CO-CH_3} + \mathrm{CdCl_2}$

Therefore, the substrate 'S' is ethanoyl chloride.

Answer: Ethanoyl chloride

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product ' B ' in the following sequence of ractions.
Methyl magnesium bromide $\xrightarrow{\mathrm{CdCl}_2} \mathrm{~A} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COCl}} \mathrm{B}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Dimethyl cadmium
- **(B)** Propanone
- **(C)** Butanone
- **(D)** Propanal

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Propanone**

**Detailed Solution:**

Methyl magnesium bromide reacts with cadmium chloride to form dimethyl cadmium (A).

$2\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{MgBr} + \mathrm{CdCl}_2 \rightarrow (\mathrm{CH}_3)_2\mathrm{Cd} + 2\mathrm{Mg(Br)Cl}$

Dimethyl cadmium (A) then reacts with acetyl chloride ($\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{COCl}$) to form propanone (B).

$2\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{COCl} + (\mathrm{CH}_3)_2\mathrm{Cd} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{COCH}_3 + \mathrm{CdCl}_2$

Thus, product B is propanone.

Answer: Propanone

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following is a simple ketone?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Acetophenone
- **(B)** Butanone
- **(C)** Benzophenone
- **(D)** Pentan-2-one

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Benzophenone**

**Detailed Solution:**

A simple (or symmetrical) ketone is one in which the two alkyl or aryl groups attached to the carbonyl carbon are identical.

Acetophenone is methyl phenyl ketone, which has different groups (methyl and phenyl) and is a mixed ketone.

Butanone is ethyl methyl ketone, which has different groups (ethyl and methyl) and is a mixed ketone.

Benzophenone is diphenyl ketone, which has identical groups (two phenyl groups) and is a simple ketone.

Pentan-2-one is methyl propyl ketone, which has different groups (methyl and propyl) and is a mixed ketone.

Answer: Benzophenone

</details>
