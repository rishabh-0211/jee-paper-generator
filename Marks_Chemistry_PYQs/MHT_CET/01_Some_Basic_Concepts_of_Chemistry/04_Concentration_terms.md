### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is value of percent atom economy when reactants having sum of formula weight 78 u results in the formation of a product with formula weight 65 u ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $70 \%$
- **(B)** $78 \%$
- **(C)** $83 \%$
- **(D)** $65 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $83 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The formula for percent atom economy is given by:

$\text{Percent atom economy} = \dfrac{\text{Formula weight of the desired product}}{\text{Sum of formula weights of all reactants}} \times 100$

Substituting the given values:

$\text{Percent atom economy} = \dfrac{65}{78} \times 100$

$\text{Percent atom economy} = \dfrac{5}{6} \times 100 = 83.33 \%$

Rounding to the nearest integer, the percent atom economy is $83 \%$.

Answer: $83 \%$

</details>
