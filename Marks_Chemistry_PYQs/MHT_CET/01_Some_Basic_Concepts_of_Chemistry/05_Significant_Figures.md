### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the SI unit of luminous intensity ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ampere
- **(B)** Coulomb
- **(C)** Candela
- **(D)** Ohm

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Candela**

**Detailed Solution:**

Luminous intensity is one of the seven fundamental base quantities in the International System of Units (SI).

The SI unit of luminous intensity is the candela (cd).

Ampere is the SI unit of electric current, Coulomb is the SI unit of electric charge, and Ohm is the SI unit of electrical resistance.

Answer: Candela

</details>
