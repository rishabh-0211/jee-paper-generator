### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the reagent used to distinguish primary and secondary amines by Hinsberg test.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{NaNO}_2$ and HCl
- **(B)** $\text{FeCl}_3$ (alc)
- **(C)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{SO}_2\text{Cl}$
- **(D)** $\text{CHCl}_3$ and KOH (alc)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{SO}_2\text{Cl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hinsberg test is used to distinguish between primary, secondary, and tertiary amines.

The reagent used in this test is benzenesulfonyl chloride, which has the chemical formula $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{SO}_2\text{Cl}$.

Primary amines react with Hinsberg reagent to form an N-alkylbenzenesulfonamide, which is soluble in alkali due to the presence of an acidic hydrogen attached to nitrogen.

Secondary amines react to form an N,N-dialkylbenzenesulfonamide, which is insoluble in alkali as it lacks an acidic hydrogen.

Tertiary amines do not react with Hinsberg reagent.

Answer: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{SO}_2\text{Cl}$

</details>
