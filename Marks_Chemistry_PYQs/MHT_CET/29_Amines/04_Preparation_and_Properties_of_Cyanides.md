### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds is obtained when alkyl cyanides are reacted with sodium and ethanol?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Primary amine
- **(B)** Secodary amine
- **(C)** Tertiary amine
- **(D)** Amide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Primary amine**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkyl cyanides ($R-C\equiv N$) on reduction with sodium and ethanol yield primary amines. This reaction is known as the Mendius reduction.

$R-C\equiv N + 4[H] \xrightarrow{Na/C_2H_5OH} R-CH_2-NH_2$

The product formed is a primary amine.

Answer: Primary amine

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify 'B' in the following reaction
Ethyl cyanide $\xrightarrow{\large{\text{H}_2\text{O}}} \text{A} \xrightarrow{\large{\text{H}_2\text{O / dil HCl}}} \text{B} + \text{NH}_3$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethanoic acid
- **(B)** Ethanamide
- **(C)** Propanoic acid
- **(D)** Propanamide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Propanoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ethyl cyanide is $\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CN}$.

Partial hydrolysis of ethyl cyanide yields propanamide (A).

$\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CN} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{O}} \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CONH}_2$

Complete hydrolysis of propanamide in the presence of dilute $\text{HCl}$ yields propanoic acid (B) and ammonia.

$\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CONH}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{O} / \text{dil HCl}} \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{COOH} + \text{NH}_3$

Therefore, B is propanoic acid.

Answer: Propanoic acid

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product 'B' in the following reaction
$\text{CH}_3 - \text{I} \xrightarrow{\text{KCN}} \text{A} \xrightarrow{\text{Na/C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}} \text{B}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-CN}$
- **(B)** $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-CH}_3$
- **(C)** $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-NH}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{CH}_3\text{-NH-C}_2\text{H}_5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-NH}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Methyl iodide reacts with potassium cyanide to undergo nucleophilic substitution, forming methyl cyanide (acetonitrile) as product A.

$\text{CH}_3\text{I} + \text{KCN} \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{CN} \text{ (A)} + \text{KI}$

Methyl cyanide undergoes Mendius reduction in the presence of sodium and ethanol to form ethylamine as product B.

$\text{CH}_3\text{CN} + 4[\text{H}] \xrightarrow{\text{Na/C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}} \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{NH}_2 \text{ (B)}$

Thus, product B is $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-NH}_2$.

Answer: $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_2\text{-NH}_2$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from the following statement is NOT correct regarding Mendius reduction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** It is useful for preparation of $\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{NH}_2$.
- **(B)** In this alkyl cyanides are reduced.
- **(C)** In this sodium and ethanol is used for reduction.
- **(D)** In this method one carbon atom is lost in the form of $\mathrm{CO}_2$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) In this method one carbon atom is lost in the form of $\mathrm{CO}_2$.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mendius reduction is the reduction of alkyl cyanides (nitriles) to primary amines using sodium and ethanol as the reducing agent.

The reaction is given by:
$\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{C}\equiv\mathrm{N} + 4[\mathrm{H}] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Na}/\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{OH}} \mathrm{R}-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{NH}_2$

In this reaction, the carbon atom of the cyanide group is retained and converted into a $-\mathrm{CH}_2-$ group. No carbon atom is lost as $\mathrm{CO}_2$.

Thus, the statement that one carbon atom is lost in the form of $\mathrm{CO}_2$ is incorrect.

Answer: In this method one carbon atom is lost in the form of $\mathrm{CO}_2$.

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (10 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is Mendius reduction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{CONH}_2 \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\mathrm{Br}_2, \mathrm{KOH}_{(\mathrm{aq})}} \mathrm{R}-\mathrm{NH}_2$
- **(B)** $\quad \mathrm{R}-\mathrm{NH}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{RX} \text { (execess) }} \mathrm{R}_4 \mathrm{NX}$
- **(C)** $\quad \mathrm{R}_4 \mathrm{NX} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text { Moist } \mathrm{Ag}_2 \mathrm{O}}$ Alkene $+\mathrm{R}_3 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $R - CN \xrightarrow[\text{or } \mathrm{LiAlH_4}]{\mathrm{Na/C_2H_5OH}} R - \mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $R - CN \xrightarrow[\text{or } \mathrm{LiAlH_4}]{\mathrm{Na/C_2H_5OH}} R - \mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mendius reduction is the chemical reaction where an alkyl cyanide (nitrile) is reduced to a primary amine using sodium and ethanol or lithium aluminium hydride.

The reaction is given by:
$R - CN \xrightarrow[\text{or } \mathrm{LiAlH_4}]{\mathrm{Na/C_2H_5OH}} R - \mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$

The other reactions are:
$\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{CONH}_2 \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\mathrm{Br}_2, \mathrm{KOH}_{(\mathrm{aq})}} \mathrm{R}-\mathrm{NH}_2$ is Hoffmann bromamide degradation.
$\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{NH}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{RX} \text{ (excess)}} \mathrm{R}_4 \mathrm{NX}$ is exhaustive alkylation.
$\mathrm{R}_4 \mathrm{NX} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\text{Moist } \mathrm{Ag}_2 \mathrm{O}} \text{Alkene} + \mathrm{R}_3 \mathrm{N}$ is Hoffmann elimination.

Answer: $R - CN \xrightarrow[\text{or } \mathrm{LiAlH_4}]{\mathrm{Na/C_2H_5OH}} R - \mathrm{CH_2NH_2}$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the product obtained when benzonitrile is treated with $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{MgBr}$ in dry ether and then hydrolyzed?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phenol
- **(B)** Benzophenone
- **(C)** Benzyl amine
- **(D)** Benzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Benzophenone**

**Detailed Solution:**

Benzonitrile is $\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{CN}$.

When benzonitrile reacts with phenylmagnesium bromide ($\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{MgBr}$) in the presence of dry ether, a nucleophilic addition takes place at the electrophilic carbon of the cyano group to form an imine complex.

$\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{C}\equiv\mathrm{N} + \mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{MgBr} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} (\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5)_2\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{NMgBr}$

Upon acid hydrolysis, the imine complex is converted into a ketone.

$(\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5)_2\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{NMgBr} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{O}^+} (\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_5)_2\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{NH}_3 + \mathrm{Mg}(\mathrm{OH})\mathrm{Br}$

The final product is benzophenone.

Answer: Benzophenone

</details>
