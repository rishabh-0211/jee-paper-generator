### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify product P in following reaction.
$\text{R-NH}_2 + \text{CHCl}_3 + 3\text{KOH (heat)} \rightarrow P$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{CH}_3\text{-NH-R}$
- **(B)** $\text{R-NC}$
- **(C)** $(\text{CH}_3)_2\text{N-R}$
- **(D)** $\text{R-Cl}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{R-NC}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is the carbylamine reaction, which is a characteristic test for primary amines.

When a primary amine ($\text{R-NH}_2$) is heated with chloroform ($\text{CHCl}_3$) and alcoholic potassium hydroxide ($\text{KOH}$), it forms an isocyanide or carbylamine ($\text{R-NC}$), which has a characteristic foul smell.

The balanced chemical equation is:
$\text{R-NH}_2 + \text{CHCl}_3 + 3\text{KOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{R-NC} + 3\text{KCl} + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$

Thus, the product P is $\text{R-NC}$.

Answer: $\text{R-NC}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following amines exhibits the Carbylamine test?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Primary amine
- **(B)** Secondary amine
- **(C)** Tertiary amine
- **(D)** Quaternary ammonium salt

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Primary amine**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Carbylamine test, also known as the isocyanide test, is used specifically for the detection of primary amines.

When a primary amine (aliphatic or aromatic) is heated with chloroform ($CHCl_3$) and alcoholic potassium hydroxide ($KOH$), it forms an isocyanide (carbylamine) which has a characteristic foul smell.

The general reaction is:
$R-NH_2 + CHCl_3 + 3KOH \rightarrow R-NC + 3KCl + 3H_2O$

Secondary and tertiary amines do not give this test because they lack the two hydrogen atoms on the nitrogen required to form the isocyanide group.

Answer: Primary amine

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following amines when heated with ethanolic KOH and chloroform does NOT produce foul smell ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzenamine
- **(B)** N , N - Dimethyl aniline
- **(C)** Propan - 2 - amine
- **(D)** Phenylmethanamine

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) N , N - Dimethyl aniline**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of an amine with chloroform and ethanolic KOH to produce a foul-smelling compound is known as the carbylamine test or isocyanide test.

This test is given only by primary aliphatic and primary aromatic amines. Secondary and tertiary amines do not respond to this test.

Benzenamine is a primary aromatic amine.

Propan-2-amine is a primary aliphatic amine.

Phenylmethanamine is a primary aliphatic amine.

N,N-Dimethyl aniline is a tertiary amine, so it does not give the carbylamine test and will not produce a foul smell.

Answer: N , N - Dimethyl aniline

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following reagents is used to prepare alkyl isocyanides from RX?

**Options:**

- **(A)** AgCN (alco)
- **(B)** $\text{NH}_3$ (alco)
- **(C)** $\text{KNO}_2$
- **(D)** $\text{AgNO}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) AgCN (alco)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkyl halides react with alcoholic silver cyanide ($\text{AgCN}$) to form alkyl isocyanides as the major product.

$\text{AgCN}$ is predominantly covalent in nature, so only the nitrogen atom has a lone pair of electrons available for nucleophilic attack. This leads to the formation of an alkyl isocyanide ($\text{RNC}$).

$\text{R-X} + \text{AgCN} \rightarrow \text{R-NC} + \text{AgX}$

In contrast, $\text{KCN}$ gives alkyl cyanides, $\text{KNO}_2$ gives alkyl nitrites, and $\text{AgNO}_2$ gives nitroalkanes.

Answer: AgCN (alco)

</details>
