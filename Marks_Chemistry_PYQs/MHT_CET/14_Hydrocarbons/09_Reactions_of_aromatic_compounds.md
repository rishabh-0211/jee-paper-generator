### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following reagents is used for acetylation of benzene?
(A) $(\text{CH}_3\text{CO})_2\text{O}$
(B) $\text{H}_2/\text{Pt}$
(C) $\text{CO}/\text{Ni}$
(D) $\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$
Choose the CORRECT option from the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** (A), (B) and (D) only.
- **(B)** (A), (B) and (C) only.
- **(C)** (A) and (D) only.
- **(D)** (B), (C) and (D) only.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) (A) and (D) only.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetylation of benzene is an electrophilic aromatic substitution reaction, specifically known as Friedel-Crafts acylation.

The reagents used for introducing an acetyl group ($-\text{COCH}_3$) to the benzene ring are acetyl chloride ($\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$) or acetic anhydride ($(\text{CH}_3\text{CO})_2\text{O}$) in the presence of a Lewis acid catalyst such as anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$.

$\text{H}_2/\text{Pt}$ is a reducing agent and $\text{CO}/\text{Ni}$ is not used for acetylation.

Thus, (A) and (D) are the correct reagents.

Answer: (A) and (D) only.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the the major product of bromination of anisole in presence of acetic acid.

**Options:**

- **(A)** o-bromoanisole
- **(B)** p-bromoanisole
- **(C)** m-bromoanisole
- **(D)** di-orthobromoanisole

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) p-bromoanisole**

**Detailed Solution:**

Anisole contains a methoxy group ($-OCH_3$) attached to the benzene ring, which is an activating group and directs the incoming electrophile to the ortho and para positions due to its $+R$ effect.

When anisole is treated with bromine in the presence of ethanoic acid (acetic acid), it undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution to form a mixture of ortho-bromoanisole and para-bromoanisole.

Due to steric hindrance caused by the bulky methoxy group at the ortho position, the para isomer is formed in a much larger amount.

Therefore, p-bromoanisole is the major product.

Answer: p-bromoanisole

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following statements is correct regarding aromatic ethers?

**Options:**

- **(A)** These are ortho directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.
- **(B)** These are meta directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.
- **(C)** These are para directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.
- **(D)** These are ortho and para directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) These are ortho and para directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.**

**Detailed Solution:**

In aromatic ethers, the alkoxy group ($-OR$) is attached to the benzene ring. The oxygen atom has lone pairs of electrons which it donates to the benzene ring through resonance ($+R$ effect).

This resonance effect increases the electron density specifically at the ortho and para positions of the benzene ring.

Therefore, the incoming electrophile is directed to the ortho and para positions, making aromatic ethers ortho and para directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.

Answer: These are ortho and para directing compounds towards electrophilic aromatic substitution.

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following reagents is used to carry out aromatic electrophilic substitution reaction with iodine?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{HNO}_3$
- **(B)** $\text{HI}$
- **(C)** $\text{HCl}$
- **(D)** $\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{HNO}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Iodination of aromatic rings is a reversible process because the hydroiodic acid ($\text{HI}$) formed during the reaction is a strong reducing agent and reduces the aryl iodide back to the aromatic hydrocarbon.

To prevent this backward reaction and drive the reaction in the forward direction, an oxidizing agent such as $\text{HNO}_3$ or $\text{HIO}_3$ is added.

The oxidizing agent oxidizes the $\text{HI}$ formed into iodine ($\text{I}_2$).

$2\text{HNO}_3 + 2\text{HI} \rightarrow \text{I}_2 + 2\text{NO}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$

Thus, $\text{HNO}_3$ is used as a reagent to carry out aromatic electrophilic substitution with iodine.

Answer: $\text{HNO}_3$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product obtained when toluene is brominated in the presence of iron in dark.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Mixture of o-Bromotoluene and p-Bromotoluene
- **(B)** o-Bromotoluene
- **(C)** p-Bromotoluene
- **(D)** Benzyl bromide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Mixture of o-Bromotoluene and p-Bromotoluene**

**Detailed Solution:**

Toluene undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution when treated with bromine in the presence of iron (which acts as a Lewis acid catalyst, forming $\text{FeBr}_3$) in the dark.

The methyl group ($-\text{CH}_3$) attached to the benzene ring is an electron-donating group through hyperconjugation and inductive effect. It is an ortho, para-directing group.

Therefore, the incoming electrophile ($\text{Br}^+$) attacks the ortho and para positions of the benzene ring. This results in the formation of a mixture of o-bromotoluene and p-bromotoluene.

Carrying out the reaction in the dark prevents free radical substitution at the benzylic position, which would otherwise yield benzyl bromide.

Answer: Mixture of o-Bromotoluene and p-Bromotoluene

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the catalyst used in the Friedel crafts acylation reaction.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$
- **(B)** Anhydrous $\text{FeCl}_3$
- **(C)** $\text{NaNO}_2$
- **(D)** Alcoholic KOH

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Friedel-Crafts acylation is an electrophilic aromatic substitution reaction in which an acyl group is introduced into an aromatic ring.

The reaction requires a strong Lewis acid catalyst to facilitate the generation of the electrophilic acylium ion from an acyl halide or acid anhydride.

Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$ is the standard and most widely used Lewis acid catalyst for this purpose.

The general reaction is:
$\text{ArH} + \text{RCOCl} \xrightarrow{\text{Anhydrous AlCl}_3} \text{ArCOR} + \text{HCl}$

Answer: Anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

What are the reactants involved in Friedal Crafts alkylation?

**Options:**

- **(B)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$
- **(C)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$
- **(D)** $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Friedel-Crafts alkylation is an electrophilic aromatic substitution reaction in which an alkyl group is introduced into an aromatic ring.

The typical reactants are an aromatic compound, such as benzene ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$), and an alkyl halide, such as methyl chloride ($\text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$). The reaction is carried out in the presence of a Lewis acid catalyst like anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$.

$\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{Cl} \xrightarrow{\text{Anhydrous AlCl}_3} \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3 + \text{HCl}$

The reaction of benzene with acetyl chloride ($\text{CH}_3\text{COCl}$) corresponds to Friedel-Crafts acylation.

Thus, the reactants involved in Friedel-Crafts alkylation are $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$ and $\text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$.

Answer: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + \text{CH}_3\text{Cl}$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following is obtained as major product when methoxybenzene is treated with nitrating mixture?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2-Nitroanisole
- **(B)** 4-Nitroanisole
- **(C)** 4-Nitrophenol
- **(D)** 2-Nitrophenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 4-Nitroanisole**

**Detailed Solution:**

Methoxybenzene (anisole) undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution when treated with a nitrating mixture (conc. $\text{HNO}_3$ and conc. $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$).

The $-\text{OCH}_3$ group is an electron-donating group through the $+R$ effect, which increases the electron density at the ortho and para positions of the benzene ring. Thus, it acts as an ortho, para-directing group.

Nitration of methoxybenzene yields a mixture of 2-nitroanisole (ortho product) and 4-nitroanisole (para product).

Due to steric hindrance at the ortho position caused by the bulky methoxy group, the para product, 4-nitroanisole, is formed as the major product.

Answer: 4-Nitroanisole

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzenediazonium chloride
- **(B)** Fluoroarene
- **(C)** P-Hydroxyazobenzene
- **(D)** N -Ethylbenzene sulfonamide

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) P-Hydroxyazobenzene**

**Detailed Solution:**

Azo coupling reaction involves the reaction of an aromatic diazonium salt with an electron-rich aromatic compound such as phenol or aniline to form an azo compound.

When benzenediazonium chloride reacts with phenol in a mildly alkaline medium, it undergoes an azo coupling reaction to form p-hydroxyazobenzene.

The reaction is:
$C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^- + C_6H_5OH \xrightarrow{OH^-} p-HOC_6H_4-N=N-C_6H_5 + H_2O + Cl^-$

Thus, p-hydroxyazobenzene is obtained by an azo coupling reaction.

Benzenediazonium chloride is a reactant in this reaction.
Fluoroarene is obtained by the Balz-Schiemann reaction.
N-Ethylbenzene sulfonamide is obtained by the Hinsberg reaction.

Answer: P-Hydroxyazobenzene

</details>
