### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (18 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is NOT terminal alkyne ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyne
- **(B)** Propyne
- **(C)** But - 1 - yne
- **(D)** But - 2 - yne

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) But - 2 - yne**

**Detailed Solution:**

Terminal alkynes have a triple bond at the end of the carbon chain, meaning at least one hydrogen atom is directly attached to a triply bonded carbon atom ($R-C \equiv C-H$).

Ethyne is $H-C \equiv C-H$, which is a terminal alkyne.

Propyne is $CH_3-C \equiv C-H$, which is a terminal alkyne.

But-1-yne is $CH_3-CH_2-C \equiv C-H$, which is a terminal alkyne.

But-2-yne is $CH_3-C \equiv C-CH_3$, where the triple bond is between the second and third carbon atoms. It does not have a hydrogen atom attached to the triply bonded carbon atoms. Thus, it is an internal alkyne, not a terminal alkyne.

Answer: But - 2 - yne

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the 'A' in the following reaction.
$\text{CaC}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{A} + \text{Ca(OH)}_2$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethene
- **(B)** Ethyne
- **(C)** Propene
- **(D)** Propyne

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ethyne**

**Detailed Solution:**

Calcium carbide reacts with water to form ethyne and calcium hydroxide.

The balanced chemical equation is:
$\text{CaC}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{C}_2\text{H}_2 + \text{Ca(OH)}_2$

Thus, the product 'A' is ethyne ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_2$).

Answer: Ethyne

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (23 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the alkyne formed by reaction of calcium carbide with water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyne
- **(B)** Propyne
- **(C)** But-1-yne
- **(D)** But-2-yne

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Ethyne**

**Detailed Solution:**

Calcium carbide reacts with water to form ethyne (acetylene) gas and calcium hydroxide.

$CaC_2 + 2H_2O \rightarrow Ca(OH)_2 + C_2H_2$

The alkyne formed is ethyne.

Answer: Ethyne

</details>
