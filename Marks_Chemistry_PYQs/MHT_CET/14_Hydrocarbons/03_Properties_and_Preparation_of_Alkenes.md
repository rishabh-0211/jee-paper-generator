### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following alkenes is highly stabilized ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{R}_2\text{C} = \text{CR}_2$
- **(B)** $\text{R}_2\text{C=CH}_2$
- **(C)** $\text{RCH=CHR}$
- **(D)** $\text{RCH=CH}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\text{R}_2\text{C} = \text{CR}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The stability of an alkene increases with the number of alkyl groups attached to the double-bonded carbon atoms due to hyperconjugation and the +I effect.

The order of stability of alkenes based on substitution is:
Tetrasubstituted $>$ Trisubstituted $>$ Disubstituted $>$ Monosubstituted.

Among the given options, $\text{R}_2\text{C}=\text{CR}_2$ is a tetrasubstituted alkene, having the maximum number of alkyl groups attached to the double bond. Thus, it has the maximum number of hyperconjugative structures and is the most highly stabilized alkene.

Answer: $\text{R}_2\text{C} = \text{CR}_2$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the reagent R used in the following conversion? tert-butyl bromide $\xrightarrow{\mathrm{R}}$ Isobutylene

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Sn} / \mathrm{HCl}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Na}-\mathrm{Hg} / \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NH}_3$ (alc)
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NaBH}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{NH}_3$ (alc)**

**Detailed Solution:**

tert-butyl bromide is a tertiary alkyl halide.

When tertiary alkyl halides are treated with alcoholic ammonia, they undergo dehydrohalogenation (elimination reaction) rather than nucleophilic substitution due to severe steric hindrance.

Ammonia acts as a base, abstracting a $\beta$-proton to form an alkene.

Thus, tert-butyl bromide reacts with alcoholic $\mathrm{NH}_3$ to give isobutylene.

The other reagents ($\mathrm{Sn} / \mathrm{HCl}$, $\mathrm{Na}-\mathrm{Hg} / \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$, $\mathrm{NaBH}_4$) are reducing agents and would yield the corresponding alkane (isobutane) instead of an alkene.

Answer: $\mathrm{NH}_3$ (alc)

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Identify the correct stability order of following alkenes.
I) $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{C}\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_2$
II) $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{CH}_2$
III) $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{CHCH}_3$

**Options:**

- **(A)** III $\gt$ II $\gt$ I
- **(B)** I $\gt$ III $\gt$ II
- **(C)** II $\gt$ I $\gt$ III
- **(D)** I $\gt$ II $\gt$ III

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) I $\gt$ III $\gt$ II**

**Detailed Solution:**

The stability of an alkene is directly proportional to the number of $\alpha$-hydrogen atoms due to hyperconjugation.

For alkene I, $(\mathrm{CH}_3)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{C}(\mathrm{CH}_3)_2$, there are 4 methyl groups attached to the $C=C$ double bond, giving $12$ $\alpha$-hydrogen atoms.

For alkene II, $(\mathrm{CH}_3)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{CH}_2$, there are 2 methyl groups attached to the $C=C$ double bond, giving $6$ $\alpha$-hydrogen atoms.

For alkene III, $(\mathrm{CH}_3)_2 \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{CHCH}_3$, there are 3 methyl groups attached to the $C=C$ double bond, giving $9$ $\alpha$-hydrogen atoms.

Since the number of $\alpha$-hydrogen atoms follows the order I $\gt$ III $\gt$ II, the stability order is I $\gt$ III $\gt$ II.

Answer: I $\gt$ III $\gt$ II

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the major product formed when 2-Methylhexan-3-ol is heated with concentrated sulphuric acid.
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/i5sFyInx4fAuJUq6R7VAGip_98uuEZNg97gfUSlAKe8.original.fullsize.png"/>
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/-2LmPbtGgeNwMh3qGsGrNLPMeqwFU9D7T-ke0L4DgnU.original.fullsize.png"/>
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/R7Qaj4RMxaa6lUsMxxbRqf316eIZs23qdy6kg0ikzMQ.original.fullsize.png"/>
<img src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/mhtcet/py35bdsq/NApXDl699uZ4S7q5EW94O7Q7So2zgnXGl3UEUuJTIUk.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** (A)
- **(B)** (B)
- **(C)** (C)
- **(D)** (D)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) (A)**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of an alcohol with concentrated sulphuric acid upon heating proceeds via an E1 elimination mechanism, which involves the formation of a carbocation intermediate.

Protonation of the hydroxyl group in 2-methylhexan-3-ol followed by the loss of a water molecule generates a secondary carbocation at C-3.

$\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)-\text{CH}(\text{OH})-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3 \xrightarrow{\text{H}^+} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)-\text{CH}(\text{O}^+\text{H}_2)-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3$

$\xrightarrow{-\text{H}_2\text{O}} \text{CH}_3-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)-\text{C}^+\text{H}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3$ (Secondary carbocation)

The secondary carbocation undergoes a 1,2-hydride shift from the adjacent C-2 position to form a more stable tertiary carbocation.

$\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)-\text{C}^+\text{H}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3 \xrightarrow{1,2-\text{hydride shift}} \text{CH}_3-\text{C}^+(\text{CH}_3)-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3$ (Tertiary carbocation)

Elimination of a proton ($\text{H}^+$) from the tertiary carbocation follows Zaitsev's rule, yielding the most highly substituted and stable alkene as the major product. Loss of a proton from C-3 gives the trisubstituted alkene, 2-methylhex-2-ene.

$\text{CH}_3-\text{C}^+(\text{CH}_3)-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3 \xrightarrow{-\text{H}^+} \text{CH}_3-\text{C}(\text{CH}_3)=\text{CH}-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_2-\text{CH}_3$

Comparing this structure with the given options, option (A) corresponds to 2-methylhex-2-ene.

Answer: (A)

</details>
