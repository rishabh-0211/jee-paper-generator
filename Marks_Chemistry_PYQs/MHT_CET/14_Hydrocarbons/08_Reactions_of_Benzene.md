### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the reaction when toluene is converted to benzaldehyde by using chromyl chloride.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Etard reaction
- **(B)** Gattermann-Koch reaction
- **(C)** Cannizzaro reaction
- **(D)** Wurtz reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Etard reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

The conversion of toluene to benzaldehyde using chromyl chloride ($CrO_2Cl_2$) in a non-polar solvent like $CS_2$, followed by acid hydrolysis, is known as the Etard reaction.

The reaction proceeds via the formation of a brown chromium complex, which upon hydrolysis yields benzaldehyde.

Gattermann-Koch reaction is used to convert benzene to benzaldehyde using $CO$ and $HCl$ in the presence of anhydrous $AlCl_3$.

Cannizzaro reaction is a disproportionation reaction of aldehydes lacking alpha-hydrogen atoms.

Wurtz reaction involves the coupling of alkyl halides to form higher alkanes using sodium metal.

Thus, the correct reaction is the Etard reaction.

Answer: Etard reaction

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the reagent used in Etard reaction.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{SnCl}_2\text{.HCl}$
- **(B)** $\text{CrO}_3$
- **(C)** $\text{Pd-BaSO}_4$
- **(D)** $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Etard reaction involves the oxidation of a methyl group attached to an aromatic ring (such as toluene) to an aldehyde group (such as benzaldehyde).

The reagent used for this specific oxidation is chromyl chloride, which has the chemical formula $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$. The reaction proceeds via the formation of a brown chromium complex, which upon hydrolysis yields the corresponding aldehyde.

$\text{SnCl}_2/\text{HCl}$ is used in the Stephen reduction.
$\text{Pd-BaSO}_4$ is used in the Rosenmund reduction.

Answer: $\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following reagents is used to convert benzene into glyoxal?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\text{O}_3$ followed by Fe and $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$
- **(B)** $\text{O}_3$ followed by Zn and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$
- **(C)** $\text{H}_2$ with nickel at 473K
- **(D)** $\text{KMnO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\text{O}_3$ followed by Zn and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Benzene undergoes reductive ozonolysis to form glyoxal. 

When benzene is treated with ozone ($\text{O}_3$), it forms an intermediate called benzene triozonide. 

This triozonide is then cleaved by hydrolysis in the presence of zinc dust and water ($\text{Zn/H}_2\text{O}$) to yield three molecules of glyoxal ($\text{CHO}-\text{CHO}$). 

Zinc is used to prevent the further oxidation of glyoxal to oxalic acid by the hydrogen peroxide ($\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$) formed during the hydrolysis step. 

$\text{C}_6\text{H}_6 + 3\text{O}_3 \rightarrow \text{Benzene triozonide}$

$\text{Benzene triozonide} + \text{Zn/H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow 3\text{CHO}-\text{CHO} + 3\text{ZnO}$

Answer: $\text{O}_3$ followed by Zn and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (17 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product 'B' in the following reaction
$\text{Toluene} \xrightarrow{\large{\text{Chromyl chloride} / \text{CS}_2}} A \xrightarrow{\large{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+}} B$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzal chloride
- **(B)** Benzaldehyde
- **(C)** Benzyl alcohol
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Benzaldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is the Etard reaction.

Toluene reacts with chromyl chloride ($\text{CrO}_2\text{Cl}_2$) in a non-polar solvent like $\text{CS}_2$ to form a brown chromium complex $A$.

The chromium complex $A$ on hydrolysis with $\text{H}_3\text{O}^+$ gives benzaldehyde $B$.

Therefore, the product $B$ is benzaldehyde.

Answer: Benzaldehyde

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product formed in the following reaction.
$\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_3 \xrightarrow[\text { ii) } \mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{O}^{+}]{\text {i) alk. } \mathrm{KMnO}_4}$ Product

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{COOH}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{COOH}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{OH}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COOH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkylbenzenes having at least one benzylic hydrogen atom are oxidized to benzoic acid on heating with strong oxidizing agents like alkaline $\mathrm{KMnO}_4$.

In ethylbenzene ($\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_3$), the benzylic carbon has two hydrogen atoms. Therefore, the entire alkyl side chain is oxidized to a carboxyl group ($-\mathrm{COOH}$) irrespective of the length of the carbon chain.

The reaction proceeds as follows:
$\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{CH}_2-\mathrm{CH}_3 \xrightarrow{\text{alk. } \mathrm{KMnO}_4} \mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COO}^{-} \mathrm{K}^{+} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{O}^{+}} \mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COOH}$

The final product formed is benzoic acid ($\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COOH}$).

Answer: $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5-\mathrm{COOH}$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2024 (09 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which from following reactions is not possible for benzene due to reversible nature?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Hydrogenation
- **(B)** Chlorination
- **(C)** . Bromination
- **(D)** Iodination

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Iodination**

**Detailed Solution:**

Iodination of benzene is a reversible reaction.

$C_6H_6 + I_2 \rightleftharpoons C_6H_5I + HI$

The hydroiodic acid (HI) formed during the reaction is a strong reducing agent. It reduces iodobenzene back to benzene and iodine, making the reaction reversible.

To carry out iodination, an oxidizing agent such as $HNO_3$ or $HIO_3$ is required to oxidize the HI formed into $I_2$, thereby driving the reaction forward according to Le Chatelier's principle.

Therefore, direct iodination is not possible due to its reversible nature.

Answer: Iodination

</details>
