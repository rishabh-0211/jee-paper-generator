### Q6. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the major product of following reaction.
$\text{Propene} \xrightarrow[\large{\text{Peroxide}}]{\large{\text{HBr}}} \,?$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1 - Bromopropane
- **(B)** 2 - Bromopropane
- **(C)** 3 - Bromopropene
- **(D)** 2 - Bromopropan-1-ol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1 - Bromopropane**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of propene with $HBr$ in the presence of peroxide proceeds via a free radical mechanism, known as the peroxide effect or anti-Markovnikov addition.

The peroxide undergoes homolytic cleavage to form alkoxy radicals, which then abstract a hydrogen atom from $HBr$ to generate a bromine radical ($Br^{\bullet}$).

The bromine radical adds to the less substituted carbon atom of the double bond in propene to generate a more stable secondary free radical:

$CH_3-CH=CH_2 + Br^{\bullet} \rightarrow CH_3-\dot{C}H-CH_2Br$

The secondary free radical then abstracts a hydrogen atom from another molecule of $HBr$ to form the final product:

$CH_3-\dot{C}H-CH_2Br + HBr \rightarrow CH_3-CH_2-CH_2Br + Br^{\bullet}$

The major product formed is 1-bromopropane.

Answer: 1 - Bromopropane

</details>

### Q13. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$200$ mL of ethylene gas and $150$ mL of HCl gas were allowed to react at $1$ bar. Pressure to form gaseous ethyl chloride. What is the work done during the reaction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $15$ J
- **(B)** $30$ J
- **(C)** $150$ J
- **(D)** $300$ J

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $15$ J**

**Detailed Solution:**

The balanced chemical equation for the reaction is:

$C_2H_4(g) + HCl(g) \rightarrow C_2H_5Cl(g)$

Initial volumes:
$V_{C_2H_4} = 200$ mL
$V_{HCl} = 150$ mL

Total initial volume $V_i = 200 + 150 = 350$ mL.

Since the reaction occurs at constant pressure and temperature, the volumes of gases are directly proportional to their number of moles. Here, $HCl$ is the limiting reagent.

Volume of $C_2H_4$ reacted = $150$ mL
Volume of $C_2H_4$ remaining = $200 - 150 = 50$ mL
Volume of $C_2H_5Cl$ formed = $150$ mL

Total final volume $V_f = 50 + 150 = 200$ mL.

Change in volume $\Delta V = V_f - V_i = 200 - 350 = -150$ mL = $-0.15$ L.

The work done during the reaction at constant pressure is given by:

$W = -P \Delta V$

Substituting the values:

$W = -1 \text{ bar} \times (-0.15 \text{ L}) = 0.15 \text{ L bar}$

Since $1 \text{ L bar} = 100 \text{ J}$, we get:

$W = 0.15 \times 100 \text{ J} = 15 \text{ J}$

Answer: $15$ J

</details>
