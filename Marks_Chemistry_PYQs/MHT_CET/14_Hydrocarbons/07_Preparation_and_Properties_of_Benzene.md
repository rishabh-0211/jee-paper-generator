### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (13 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the product of decarboxylation of anhydrous sodium benzoate?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Benzene
- **(B)** Cyclohexane
- **(C)** Benzene hexachloride
- **(D)** Chlorobenzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Benzene**

**Detailed Solution:**

Decarboxylation of anhydrous sodium benzoate is carried out by heating it with soda lime, which is a mixture of sodium hydroxide and calcium oxide.

The reaction proceeds as follows:

$C_6H_5COONa + NaOH \xrightarrow{CaO, \Delta} C_6H_6 + Na_2CO_3$

The main product formed in this reaction is benzene.

Answer: Benzene

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product when ethylbenzene reacts with dil nitric acid.

**Options:**

- **(A)** o-Nitroethylbenzene
- **(B)** p-Nitroethylbenzene
- **(C)** Ethoxybenzene
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Benzoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkylbenzenes are oxidized to aromatic carboxylic acids by strong oxidizing agents such as alkaline $KMnO_4$, acidified $K_2Cr_2O_7$, or dilute $HNO_3$.

When ethylbenzene is treated with dilute nitric acid, the entire alkyl side chain is oxidized to a carboxyl group, irrespective of the length of the carbon chain.

Therefore, the oxidation of ethylbenzene with dilute $HNO_3$ yields benzoic acid.

Answer: Benzoic acid

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 2)

The common name of Benzene-1, 2, 3-triol is

**Options:**

- **(A)** Phloroglucinol
- **(B)** Pyrogallol
- **(C)** Quinol
- **(D)** Catechol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Pyrogallol**

**Detailed Solution:**

Benzene-1,2,3-triol has three hydroxyl ($-OH$) groups attached to adjacent carbon atoms of the benzene ring. Its common name is pyrogallol.

Other common names for reference:
Benzene-1,3,5-triol is phloroglucinol.
Benzene-1,4-diol is quinol (or hydroquinone).
Benzene-1,2-diol is catechol.

Answer: Pyrogallol
