### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (02 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following compounds is obtained when cyclohexene is oxidized using $\mathrm{KMnO}_4$ in dilute $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Cyclohexanol
- **(B)** Cyclohexanone
- **(C)** Benzoic acid
- **(D)** Adipic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Adipic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cyclohexene is a cyclic alkene with a double bond between two secondary carbon atoms.

Potassium permanganate ($\mathrm{KMnO}_4$) in dilute $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$ acts as a strong oxidizing agent.

Reaction of cyclohexene with acidic $\mathrm{KMnO}_4$ results in the oxidative cleavage of the carbon-carbon double bond.

The ring opens up, and the two carbon atoms of the double bond are oxidized to carboxylic acid groups.

Since cyclohexene contains six carbon atoms, the resulting product is a six-carbon dicarboxylic acid, which is hexanedioic acid, commonly known as adipic acid.

The reaction can be represented as:
Cyclohexene $\xrightarrow{\mathrm{KMnO}_4, \mathrm{H}^+}$ $\mathrm{HOOC}-(\mathrm{CH}_2)_4-\mathrm{COOH}$

Answer: Adipic acid

</details>
