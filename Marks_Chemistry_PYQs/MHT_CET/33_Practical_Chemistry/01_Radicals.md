### Q1. MHT CET 2016

Brown ring test is used for the detection of which radical?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ferrous
- **(B)** Nitrite
- **(C)** Nitrate
- **(D)** Ferric

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Nitrate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Brown ring test is used for the detection of nitrate.
