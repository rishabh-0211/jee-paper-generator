### Q1. MHT CET 2023 (11 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which from following cations in their respective oxidation states develops colourless aqueous solution?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{Cu}^{+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline Ion & \begin{array}{c} 
Outer electronic \\
configuration
\end{array} & \begin{array}{c} 
No. of unpaired \\
electrons
\end{array} \\
\hline \mathrm{Fe}^{3+} & 3 \mathrm{~d}^5 & 5 \\
\hline \mathrm{Fe}^{2+} & 3 \mathrm{~d}^6 & 4 \\
\hline \mathrm{Cu}^{2+} & 3 \mathrm{~d}^9 & 1 \\
\hline \mathrm{Cu}^{+} & 3 \mathrm{~d}^{10} & 0 \\
\hline
\end{array}
$\mathrm{Cu}^{+}$is colourless because of the absence of unpaired electrons.

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2023 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among following salts shows decrease in solubility with increase in temperature?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{KNO}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NaNO}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{KBr}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Dissolution of $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$ in water is an exothermic process. When a substance dissolves in water by an exothermic process, its solubility decreases with an increase in temperature. Hence, solubility of $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$ in water decreases with increase in temperature.

</details>
