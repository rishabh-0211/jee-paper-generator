### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which among the following is vinylic halide?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Haloalkane
- **(B)** Haloalkene
- **(C)** Halaoalkyne
- **(D)** Haloarene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Haloalkene**

**Detailed Solution:**

Vinylic halides are compounds in which the halogen atom is bonded directly to an $sp^2$ hybridized carbon atom of a carbon-carbon double bond (C=C).

Since they contain a carbon-carbon double bond, vinylic halides are classified as haloalkenes.

Answer: Haloalkene

</details>

**Detailed Solution:**

The full name of DDT is p,p'-dichlorodiphenyltrichloroethane.

Its IUPAC name is 1,1,1-trichloro-2,2-bis(4-chlorophenyl)ethane.

The structure consists of an ethane backbone where the first carbon is attached to three chlorine atoms (forming a $-\text{CCl}_3$ group) and the second carbon is attached to one hydrogen atom and two 4-chlorophenyl (para-chlorophenyl) groups.

### Q6. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Chloroform on reduction with zinc dust and water gives

**Options:**

- **(A)** methyl chloride
- **(B)** dichloro methane
- **(C)** chloro methane
- **(D)** methane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) methane**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\underset{\text { chloroform }}{\mathrm{CHCl}_{3}} \frac{6[\mathrm{H}] ; \mathrm{Zn} / \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}}{\underset{-\mathrm{ZnCl}_{2}}{\mathrm{HCl}}}{\longrightarrow} \underset{\text { methane }}{\mathrm{CH}_{4}}$
Chloroform on reduction with Zn and HCl gives dichloro methane $\left(\mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}_{2}\right)$.

</details>
