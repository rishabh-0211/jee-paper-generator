### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Identify the product obtained when alkyl magnesium halides are reacted with water.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Haloforms
- **(B)** Alkyl halides
- **(C)** Alcohols
- **(D)** Alkanes

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Alkanes**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkyl magnesium halides ($RMgX$), commonly known as Grignard reagents, are highly reactive organometallic compounds. The carbon-magnesium bond is highly polar, giving the alkyl group a partial negative charge ($R^{\delta-} - Mg^{\delta+}X$).

Due to this carbanionic character, the alkyl group acts as a strong base. When reacted with a compound containing acidic hydrogen, such as water ($H_2O$), the alkyl group rapidly abstracts a proton to form an alkane.

The chemical reaction is given by:
$R-MgX + H_2O \rightarrow R-H + Mg(OH)X$

Here, $R-H$ represents an alkane. Therefore, the product obtained when alkyl magnesium halides are reacted with water is an alkane.

Answer: Alkanes

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is used as solvent for Grignard reagent?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alcohol
- **(B)** Ether
- **(C)** Phenol
- **(D)** Ketone

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ether**

**Detailed Solution:**

Grignard reagents ($RMgX$) are highly reactive compounds that act as strong bases and nucleophiles.

Solvents with acidic protons, such as alcohols and phenols, react with Grignard reagents to form alkanes, thereby destroying the reagent.

Similarly, solvents with electrophilic carbonyl groups, such as ketones, undergo nucleophilic addition with Grignard reagents.

Ether is an aprotic solvent that does not react with Grignard reagents.

Furthermore, the lone pairs of electrons on the oxygen atom of ether coordinate with the electron-deficient magnesium atom, stabilizing the reagent.

Therefore, ether is the suitable solvent for Grignard reagents.

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2025 (20 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product ' $Z$ ' in the following series of reactions.
Ethanol $\xrightarrow[\Delta]{\mathrm{SOCl}_2} \mathrm{X} \xrightarrow[\text { Dry ether }]{\mathrm{Mg}} \mathrm{Y} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NH}_3} \mathrm{Z}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Ethyl chloride
- **(B)** Ethyl magnesium chloride
- **(C)** Ethyl amine
- **(D)** Ethane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Ethane**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ethanol reacts with thionyl chloride ($\mathrm{SOCl}_2$) to form ethyl chloride ($\mathrm{X}$).

$\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{OH} + \mathrm{SOCl}_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{Cl} + \mathrm{SO}_2 \uparrow + \mathrm{HCl} \uparrow$

Ethyl chloride reacts with magnesium in the presence of dry ether to form ethyl magnesium chloride ($\mathrm{Y}$), which is a Grignard reagent.

$\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{Cl} + \mathrm{Mg} \xrightarrow{\text{Dry ether}} \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{MgCl}$

Grignard reagents react with compounds containing active hydrogen, such as ammonia ($\mathrm{NH}_3$), to form alkanes. The alkyl group of the Grignard reagent abstracts a proton from ammonia to yield ethane ($\mathrm{Z}$).

$\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{MgCl} + \mathrm{NH}_3 \rightarrow \mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{CH}_3 + \mathrm{Mg}(\mathrm{NH}_2)\mathrm{Cl}$

Thus, the final product $\mathrm{Z}$ is ethane.

Answer: Ethane

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the product ' B ' in the following reaction sequence.
$\text { Alkyl halide } \frac{\mathrm{Mg}}{\text { Dry ether }} \mathrm{A} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NH}_3} \mathrm{~B}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** Alkyl magnesium halide
- **(B)** Alkyl amine
- **(C)** Hydrocarbon
- **(D)** Alkyl nitrile

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Hydrocarbon**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkyl halide ($R-X$) reacts with magnesium in the presence of dry ether to form alkyl magnesium halide ($R-Mg-X$), which is a Grignard reagent. Thus, A is $R-Mg-X$.

Grignard reagents react with compounds containing active hydrogen, such as ammonia ($NH_3$), to form hydrocarbons (alkanes).

The reaction is given by:
$R-X \xrightarrow{\text{Mg / Dry ether}} R-Mg-X$
$R-Mg-X + NH_3 \rightarrow R-H + Mg(NH_2)X$

Therefore, product B is a hydrocarbon ($R-H$).

Answer: Hydrocarbon

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the compound formed by action of chromyl chloride on toluene in presence of $\mathrm{CS}_2$ and hydrolysed further?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Chlorobenzene
- **(B)** Benzal Chloride
- **(C)** Benzaldehyde
- **(D)** Benzoic acid

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Benzaldehyde**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction of toluene with chromyl chloride ($\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2}$) in the presence of a non-polar solvent like $\mathrm{CS_2}$ or $\mathrm{CCl_4}$ is known as the Etard reaction.

In this reaction, toluene is oxidized to form a brown chromium complex as an intermediate.

$\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_3} + 2\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CS_2}} \mathrm{C_6H_5CH(OCrOHCl_2)_2}$

Upon subsequent acid hydrolysis, the chromium complex decomposes to yield benzaldehyde.

$\mathrm{C_6H_5CH(OCrOHCl_2)_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_3O^+}} \mathrm{C_6H_5CHO} + \text{Byproducts}$

Thus, the final compound formed is benzaldehyde.

Answer: Benzaldehyde

</details>
