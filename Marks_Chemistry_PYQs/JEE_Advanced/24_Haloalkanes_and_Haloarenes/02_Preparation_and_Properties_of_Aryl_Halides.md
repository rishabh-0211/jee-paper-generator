**Correct Option:** **(C) Compounds <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi mathvariant="normal">P</mi></math> and <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi mathvariant="normal">S</mi></math> react with hydroxylamine to give the corresponding oximes.**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>Benzene undergoes friedel craft&#39;s reaction with succinic anhydride in presence of anhydrous AlCl<sub>3</sub> to form P.The mechanism of the reaction is electrophilic substitution.</p><p>The keto group in P gets reduced on reaction Zn/Hg in HCl to form Q that is 4-phenylbutanoic acid.</p><p>This reacts with thionyl chloride to form R, that is </p><p>4-Phenylbutanoyl chloride.</p><p>R again undergoes friedel craft&#39;s reaction to form S.</p><p>S gets reduced on reaction Zn/Hg in HCl to form a hydrocarbon as given in the reactions below.</p><p><img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/f8018820-3055-42b2-b219-fcb8e77b8df7-image.png" style="width: 484px; height: 300px;" /></p><p>Compound <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mfenced><mi mathvariant="normal">S</mi></mfenced></math> can not decolorizes bromine water.</p><p><img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/bfbb566c-ced0-4e1d-9193-98a4443b7aed-image.png" style="width: 461px; height: 110px;" /></p><p><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">R</mi><mn>2</mn></msub><mi>Cd</mi></math> is a less reactive nucleophile so reaction stops at carbonyl group.</p><p>Due to the presence of carbonyl group both P and S forms oxime with hydroxyl amine.</p>
