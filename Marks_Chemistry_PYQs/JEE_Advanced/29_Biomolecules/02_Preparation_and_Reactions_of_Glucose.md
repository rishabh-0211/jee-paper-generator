### Q3. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Statement I Glucose gives a reddish-brown precipitate with Fehling's solution. 
Statement II Reaction of glucose with Fehling's solution gives $\mathrm{CuO}$ and gluconic acid.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement I is true, Statement II is true, Statement II is a correct explanation for Statement I
- **(B)** Statement I is true, Statement II is true; Statement II is not a correct explanation for Statement I
- **(C)** Statement I is true, Statement II is false
- **(D)** Statement I is false, Statement II is true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is true, Statement II is false**

**Detailed Solution:**

Glucose gives a reddish brown precipitate with Fehling's solution. Solution consists of $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ which gets reduced to $\mathrm{Cu}^{+}\left(\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{O}\right)$.

</details>
