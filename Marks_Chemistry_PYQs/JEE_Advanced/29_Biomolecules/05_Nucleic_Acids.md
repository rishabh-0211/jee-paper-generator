### Q1. JEE Advanced 2024 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

For a double strand DNA, one strand is given below:
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/e53XGV6r-Y-VKbXwU2G4A49Q1dcSnhGAC7PG0EA5PWA.original.fullsize.png">
The amount of energy required to split the double strand DNA into two single strands is _______ kcal $\mathrm{mol}^{-1}$.
[Given: Average energy per $\mathrm{H}$-bond for A-T base pair $=1.0 \mathrm{kcal} \mathrm{mol}^{-1}, \mathrm{G}-\mathrm{C}$ base pair $=1.5 \mathrm{kcal}$ $\mathrm{mol}^{-1}$, and A-U base pair $=1.25 \mathrm{kcal} \mathrm{mol}^{-1}$. Ignore electrostatic repulsion between the phosphate groups.]

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **41**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{T} \quad 2 \mathrm{H}$-bond
$\mathrm{G} \equiv \mathrm{C} \quad 3 \mathrm{H}$-bond
Number of $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{T}$ pair $=7$
Number of $\mathrm{G} \equiv \mathrm{C}$ pair $=6$
Number of H-bond involve in $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{T}=7 \times 2=14$
Number of $\mathrm{H}$-bond involve in $\mathrm{G} \equiv \mathrm{C}=6 \times 3=18$
Energy required for $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{T}=14 \times 1=14$
Energy required for $\mathrm{G} \equiv \mathrm{C}=18 \times 1.5=27$
Total energy required $14+27=41$
