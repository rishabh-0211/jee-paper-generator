### Q1. JEE Advanced 2025 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

The monomer (X) involved in the synthesis of Nylon 6,6 gives positive carbylamine test. If 10 moles of $\mathbf{X}$ are analyzed using Dumas method, the amount (in grams) of nitrogen gas evolved is ______ .
Use: Atomic mass of N (in amu) = 14

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **280**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/qePGdVj_8bx3f9b1wDMdqPIL6saGfCd04CiyzpDufCU.original.fullsize.png">
Hexamethylene diamine series give $(+)$ carbylamine test.
$\mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{x}} \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{y}} \mathrm{N}_{\mathrm{z}}+(2 \mathrm{x}+1 / 2) \mathrm{CuO} \rightarrow \mathrm{xCO}_2+\frac{\mathrm{y}}{2} \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}+\frac{\mathrm{z}}{2} \mathrm{~N}_2$ (Dumas method)
$\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_{16} \mathrm{~N}_2$ then $\frac{\mathrm{z}}{2} \mathrm{~N}_2=\frac{2}{2} \mathrm{~N}_2=1$ mole
Given 10 mole in this reaction so $\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{N}_2}=10 \times 28=280 \mathrm{gm}$

</details>
