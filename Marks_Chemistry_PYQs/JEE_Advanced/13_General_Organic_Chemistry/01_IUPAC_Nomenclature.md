### Q3. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The IUPAC name of the following compound, is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/w6azp8ZVwNJRA1UqThrHxICtM2Jrt8nnrnaVDxHR0ak.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4-bromo-3-cyanophenol
- **(B)** 2-bromo-5-hydroxybenzonitrile
- **(C)** 2-cyano-4-hydroxybromobenzene
- **(D)** 6-bromo-3-hydroxybenzonitrile

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 2-bromo-5-hydroxybenzonitrile**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cyano group has the highest priority therefore, parent name must be benzonitrile. $\mathrm{Br}$ occurs at 2-position, and hydroxyl at 3-position, hence clockwise numbering will be the correct one.

</details>
