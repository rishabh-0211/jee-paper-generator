### Q3. JEE Advanced 2017 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a bimolecular reaction, the steric factor <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi mathvariant="normal">P</mi></math> was experimentally determined to be <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mn>4</mn><mo>.</mo><mn>5</mn></math>. The correct option(s) among the following is(are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** The value of the frequency factor predicted by the Arrhenius equation is higher than that determined experimentally.
- **(B)** The activation energy of the reaction is unaffected by the value of the steric factor.
- **(C)** Since <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mi mathvariant="normal">P</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>4</mn><mo>.</mo><mn>5</mn></math>, the reaction will not proceed unless an effective catalyst is used.
- **(D)** The experimentally determined value of the frequency factor is higher than that predicated by the Arrhenius equation.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) The experimentally determined value of the frequency factor is higher than that predicated by the Arrhenius equation.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The Arrhenius equation is

\(\mathrm{k}=\mathrm{Ae}^{-\mathrm{Ea} / \mathrm{RT}}\)

where A = Pre-exponential factor

A is not directly related with temperature and activation energy.

Where \(A=\underset{\text { (Frequency factor) }}{Z} \times \underset{\text { (Steric factor) }}{P}\)

Hence we can say the \(\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{a}}\) is independent of steric factor

\(\mathrm{P}=\frac{\mathrm{K}_{\text {actual }}}{\mathrm{K}_{\text {theoretical }}}\)

So, \(A_{\text {actual }}>A_{\text {theoretical }}\)

### Q6. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

For a first order reaction, $A \rightarrow P$, the temperature $(T)$ dependent rate constant $(k)$ was found to follow the equation, $\log k=-(2000) / T+6.0$
The pre-exponential factor $A$ and the activation energy $\left(E_a\right)$, respectively, are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.0 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $9.2 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $6.0 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $16.6 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $1.0 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $16.6 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $1.0 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $38.3 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $1.0 \times 10^6 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$ and $38.3 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Comparing the slope and intercept of the given equation with the following Arrhenius equation :
$$
\log k=-\frac{E_a}{2303 R T}+\log A
$$
Hence, $\log A=6$ i.e. $A=10^6 \mathrm{~s}^{-1}$.
Comparing slope gives $E_a=38.3 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$.

</details>
