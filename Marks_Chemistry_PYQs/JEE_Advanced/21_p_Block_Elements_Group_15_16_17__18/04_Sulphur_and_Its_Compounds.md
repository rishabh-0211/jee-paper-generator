### Q3. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Passing $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}$ gas into a mixture of $\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}, \mathrm{Ni}^{2+}, \mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Hg}^{2+}$ ions in an acidified aqueous solution precipitates

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CuS}$ and $\mathrm{HgS}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{MnS}$ and $\mathrm{CuS}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{MnS}$ and $\mathrm{NiS}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NiS}$ and $\mathrm{HgS}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{CuS}$ and $\mathrm{HgS}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

In acidic medium, $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S}$ is very feebly ionized giving very small concentration of sulphide ion for precipitation. Therefore, the most insoluble salts $\mathrm{CuS}$ and $\mathrm{HgS}$ are precipitated only.

### Q5. JEE Advanced 2008 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$$
\text { Aqueous solution of } \mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3 \text { on reaction with } \mathrm{Cl}_2 \text { gives }
$$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_4 \mathrm{O}_6$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NaHSO}_4$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NaCl}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NaOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{NaHSO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sodium thiosulphate, $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ gets oxidised by chlorine water.
$$
\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+4 \mathrm{Cl}_2+5 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{NaHSO}_4+8 \mathrm{HCl}
$$
$\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ gets oxidised by $\mathrm{FeCl}_3$ into $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_4 \mathrm{O}_6$
$$
2 \mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+2 \mathrm{FeCl}_3 \longrightarrow \mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_4 \mathrm{O}_6+2 \mathrm{FeCl}_2+2 \mathrm{NaCl}
$$
Further, $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ decomposes on treatment with dilute acids
$$
\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+2 \mathrm{HCl} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{NaCl}+\mathrm{SO}_2+\mathrm{S}+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}
$$

</details>
