### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Correct statement(s) about the compounds $\textbf{P}$, $\textbf{Q}$ and $\textbf{R}$ is(are)
<img style="width: 600px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p2_english__q42_d1.png" />

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\textbf{P}$ has two lone pairs of electrons on the central atom.
- **(B)** $\textbf{Q}$ has a perfect octahedral geometry.
- **(C)** $\textbf{Q}$ can act as a fluorinating agent.
- **(D)** The molecular structure of $\textbf{R}$ is trigonal pyramidal.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) The molecular structure of $\textbf{R}$ is trigonal pyramidal.**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given reaction conditions:

$\text{Xe(g)} + 2\text{F}_2\text{(g)} \xrightarrow[\text{1 : 5 ratio}]{\text{873 K, 7 bar}} \text{XeF}_4$
Thus, $\textbf{P}$ is $\text{XeF}_4$.

$\text{XeF}_4 + \text{O}_2\text{F}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{143 K}} \text{XeF}_6 + \text{O}_2$
Thus, $\textbf{Q}$ is $\text{XeF}_6$.

$\text{XeF}_6 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{\text{complete hydrolysis}} \text{XeO}_3 + 6\text{HF}$
Thus, $\textbf{R}$ is $\text{XeO}_3$.

Evaluating the given options:
(A) $\textbf{P}$ ($\text{XeF}_4$) has $8$ valence electrons on the central Xe atom. It forms $4$ single bonds with F, leaving $4$ electrons which form $2$ lone pairs. This statement is correct.

(B) $\textbf{Q}$ ($\text{XeF}_6$) has $6$ bond pairs and $1$ lone pair on the central Xe atom. Its geometry is distorted octahedral, not perfect octahedral. This statement is incorrect.

(C) $\textbf{Q}$ ($\text{XeF}_6$) is a strong fluorinating agent. This statement is correct.

(D) $\textbf{R}$ ($\text{XeO}_3$) has $3$ double bonds and $1$ lone pair on the central Xe atom. Its molecular structure is trigonal pyramidal. This statement is correct.

Answer: $\textbf{P}$ has two lone pairs of electrons on the central atom.; $\textbf{Q}$ can act as a fluorinating agent.; The molecular structure of $\textbf{R}$ is trigonal pyramidal.

### Q7. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
The noble gases have closed-shell electronic configuration and are monoatomic gases under normal conditions. The low boiling points of the lighter noble gases are due to weak dispersion forces between the atoms and the absence of other interatomic interactions.
The direct reaction of xenon with fluorine leads to a series of compounds with oxidation numbers $+2,+4$ and $+6 . \mathrm{XeF}_4$ reacts violently with water to give $\mathrm{XeO}_3$. The compounds of xenon exhibit rich stereochemistry and their geometries can be deduced considering the total number of electron pairs in the valence shell.<strong>Question:</strong>
$\mathrm{XeF}_4$ and $\mathrm{XeF}_6$ are expected to be

**Options:**

- **(A)** oxidising
- **(B)** reducing
- **(C)** unreactive
- **(D)** strongly basic

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) oxidising**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{XeF}_4$ oxidises potassium iodide
$$
\mathrm{XeF}_4+4 \mathrm{I}^{-} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{I}_2+4 \mathrm{~F}^{-1}+\mathrm{Xe}
$$
$\mathrm{XeF}_6$ oxidises hydrogen like other xenon fluorides.

</details>
