### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

In the following reaction sequence, $\textbf{Q}$, $\textbf{R}$, $\textbf{S}$ and $\textbf{T}$ are the major products.
<img style="width: 600px" src="https://cdn.quizrr.in/question-assets/jee_adv/2026/watermarked_images/p1_english__q40_d1.png" />
The correct statement(s) about $\textbf{Q}$, $\textbf{R}$, $\textbf{S}$ and $\textbf{T}$ is(are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\textbf{S}$ on warming with ammoniacal $\text{AgNO}_3$ results in the formation of silver mirror.
- **(B)** $\textbf{Q}$ on treatment with $\text{Cl}_2$(excess)/UV gives gammaxane.
- **(C)** $\textbf{T}$ is a heterocyclic compound.
- **(D)** $\textbf{R}$ on acid catalyzed intramolecular cyclization followed by treatment with Zn-Hg/HCl gives 9,10-dihydroxyanthracene.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\textbf{T}$ is a heterocyclic compound.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Kolbe's electrolysis of sodium butyrate ($\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{COONa}$) yields n-hexane.

Aromatization of n-hexane using $\text{V}_2\text{O}_5$ at $500^\circ\text{C}$ produces benzene ($Q$).

Benzene ($Q$) reacts with phthalic anhydride in the presence of anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$ to form o-benzoylbenzoic acid ($R$).

Treatment of $R$ with $\text{PCl}_5$ gives o-benzoylbenzoyl chloride, which upon Rosenmund reduction ($\text{H}_2-\text{Pd/BaSO}_4$) yields o-benzoylbenzaldehyde ($S$).

Condensation of $S$ with hydrazine ($\text{NH}_2\text{NH}_2$) forms 1-phenylphthalazine ($T$).

Evaluating the options:

(A) $S$ contains an aldehyde group and gives a positive Tollens' test (silver mirror).

(B) $Q$ (benzene) reacts with excess $\text{Cl}_2$ under UV light to form benzene hexachloride (gammaxane).

(C) $T$ (1-phenylphthalazine) is a heterocyclic compound.

(D) Acid-catalyzed cyclization of $R$ yields anthraquinone, which on Clemmensen reduction (Zn-Hg/HCl) gives anthracene, not 9,10-dihydroxyanthracene.

Answer: $\textbf{S}$ on warming with ammoniacal $\text{AgNO}_3$ results in the formation of silver mirror.; $\textbf{Q}$ on treatment with $\text{Cl}_2$(excess)/UV gives gammaxane.; $\textbf{T}$ is a heterocyclic compound.

</details>

$$
\text { Hydrazone formation occurs effectively at } \mathrm{pH} \approx 4.5 \text {. The reaction proceeds in that }
$$

As $\mathrm{H}^{+}$concentration raises, sufficient molecule of hydrazone gets converted into hydrazonium which is not nucleophilic and reaction becomes impossible. Further, low concentration of $\mathrm{H}^{+}$(in the case of hydrolysis of 2, 4-dinitrophenyl hydrazonium bromide) is not effective to proceed elimination (rate determining step).
