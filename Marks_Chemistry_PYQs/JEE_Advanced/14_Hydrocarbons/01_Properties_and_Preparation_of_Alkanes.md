### Q4. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In allene $\left(\mathrm{C}_{3} \mathrm{H}_{4}\right)$, the type(s) of hybridisation of the carbon atoms is (are):

**Options:**

- **(A)** $s p$ and $s p^{3}$
- **(B)** $s p$ and $s p^{2}$
- **(C)** only $s p^{3}$
- **(D)** $s p^{2}$ and $s p^{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $s p$ and $s p^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Allene $\left(\mathbf{C}_{3} \mathbf{H}_{4}\right)$ is $\stackrel{s p^{2}}{\mathrm{H}_{2}} \mathrm{C}=\stackrel{s p}{\mathrm{C}}=\stackrel{s p^{2}}{\mathrm{CH}_{2}}$

</details>
