### Q2. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

The total number of alkenes possible by dehydrobromination of 3-bromo-3-cyclopentylhexane using alcoholic $\mathrm{KOH}$ is

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **5**

**Detailed Solution:**

The substrate has three different types of $\mathrm{B}-\mathrm{H}$, therefore, first, three structural isomers of alkenes are expected as
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/2E-6lbXzWSzSfeC6dpZeGVGXsO8Qk5ByuknVh5D7qCA.original.fullsize.png">

The last two alkenes II and III are also capable of showing geometrical isomerism hence two geometrical isomers for each of them will be counted giving a total of five isomers.

</details>
