### Q3. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Oxidation states of the metal in the minerals haematite and magnetite, respectively, are

**Options:**

- **(A)** II, III in haematite and III in magnetite
- **(B)** II, III in haematite and II in magnetite
- **(C)** II in haematite and II, III in magnetite
- **(D)** III in haematite and II, III in magnetite

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) III in haematite and II, III in magnetite**

**Detailed Solution:**

Haematite is $\mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$, in which oxidation number of iron is III. Magnetite is $\mathrm{Fe}_3 \mathrm{O}_4$ which is infact a mixed oxide (FeO $\cdot \mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$ ) hence, iron is present in both II and III oxidation state.

</details>
