### Q1. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Copper is the most noble of the first row transition metals and occurs in small deposits in several countries. Ores of copper include chalcanthite $\left(\mathrm{CuSO}_4 \cdot 5 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}\right)$, atacamite $\left(\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{Cl}(\mathrm{OH})_3\right)$, cuprite $\left(\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{O}\right)$, copper glance $\left(\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{~S}\right)$ and malachite $\left(\mathrm{Cu}_2(\mathrm{OH})_2 \mathrm{CO}_3\right)$. However, 80\% of the world copper production comes from the ore chalcopyrite $\left(\mathrm{CuFeS}_2\right)$. The extraction of copper from chalcopyrite involves partial roasting, removal of iron and self-reduction.<strong>Question:</strong>
Iron is removed from chalcopyrite as

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{FeO}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{FeS}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{FeSiO}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{FeSiO}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Iron is removed in the form of slag of $\mathrm{FeSiO}_3$

</details>
