The pair(s) of diamagnetic ions is(are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{La}^{3+}, \mathrm{Ce}^{4+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}, \mathrm{Lu}^{3+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{La}^{2+}, \mathrm{Ce}^{3+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Yb}^{3+}, \mathrm{Lu}^{2+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}, \mathrm{Lu}^{3+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{La}^{+3} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^0 & \text { -diamagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Yb}^{+2} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^{14} & \text { -diamagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Lu}^{+3} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^{14} & \text { -diamagnetic } \\ \mathrm{La}^{+2} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 5 \mathrm{~d}^1 & \text { -paramagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Ce}^{+4} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^0 & \text { -diamagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Ce}^{+3} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^1 & \text { -paramagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Yb}^{+3} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^{13} & \text { -paramagnetic } \\ \mathrm{Lu}^{+2} \rightarrow\left[{ }_{54} \mathrm{Xe}\right] 4 \mathrm{f}^{14} 5 \mathrm{~d}^1 & \text { -paramagnetic }\end{aligned}$
