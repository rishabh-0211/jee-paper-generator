### Q1. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The compound(s) formed upon combustion of sodium metal in excess air is (are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{O}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NaO}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NaOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{O}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sodium on heating in air forms both normal oxide and peroxides.

</details>

### Q2. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Statement I Alkali metals dissolve in liquid ammonia to give blue solutions. Statement II Alkali metals in liquid ammonia give solvated species of the type $\left(M\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)_n\right]^{+}(M=$ alkali metals $)$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement I is true, Statement II is true, Statement II is a correct explanation for Statement I
- **(B)** Statement I is true, Statement II is true, Statement II is not a correct explanation for Statement I
- **(C)** Statement I is true, Statement II is false
- **(D)** Statement $\mathrm{I}$ is false, Statement $\mathrm{II}$ is true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Statement I is true, Statement II is true, Statement II is not a correct explanation for Statement I**

**Detailed Solution:**

Alkali metals in ammonia gives blue colour due to ammoniated $e^{-}$

</details>
