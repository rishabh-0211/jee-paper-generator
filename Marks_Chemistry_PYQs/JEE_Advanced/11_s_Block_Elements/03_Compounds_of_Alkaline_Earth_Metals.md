### Q1. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\mathrm{MgSO}_4$ on reaction with $\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH}$ and $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{HPO}_4$ forms a white crystalline precipitate. What is its formula?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Mg}\left(\mathrm{NH}_4\right) \mathrm{PO}_4$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Mg}_3\left(\mathrm{PO}_4\right)_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{MgCl}_2 \cdot \mathrm{MgSO}_4$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{MgSO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Mg}\left(\mathrm{NH}_4\right) \mathrm{PO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{MgSO}_4$ on reaction with $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{HPO}_4$ (disodium hydrogen phosphate) in presence of $\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH}$ to give white precipitate of magnesium ammonium phosphate $\left(\mathrm{Mg}\left(\mathrm{NH}_4\right) \mathrm{PO}_4\right)$.
