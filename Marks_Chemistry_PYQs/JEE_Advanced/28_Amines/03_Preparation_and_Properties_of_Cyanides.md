### Q1. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The smallest ketone and its next homologoue are reacted with $\mathrm{NH}_2 \mathrm{OH}$ to form oxime

**Options:**

- **(A)** two different oximes are formed
- **(B)** three different oximes are formed
- **(C)** two oximes are optically active
- **(D)** all oximes are optically active

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) three different oximes are formed**

**Detailed Solution:**

Lowest ketone is $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COCH}_3$ (which form an oxime) and its next homologue is $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{COCH}_3$ which form oxime with $\mathrm{NH}_2 \mathrm{OH}$. Such oxime shows the property of geometrical isomerism, i.e., it shows two isomers, symmetric and antisymmertic. (These are differ w.r.t. arrangement towards the space of $-\mathrm{C}=\mathrm{N}$, their different behaviour, can be confirmed with the help of Beckmann's rearrangement.
