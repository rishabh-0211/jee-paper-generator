### Q1. JEE Advanced 2025 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The correct statements (s) about intermolecular forces is(are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** The potential energy between two point charges approaches zero more rapidly than the potential energy between a point dipole and a point charge as the distance between them approaches infinity.
- **(B)** The average potential energy of two rotating polar molecules that are separated by a distance $r$ has $1 / r^3$ dependence.
- **(C)** The dipole-induced dipole average interaction energy is independent of temperature.
- **(D)** Nonpolar molecules attract one another even though neither has a permanent dipole moment.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Nonpolar molecules attract one another even though neither has a permanent dipole moment.**

**Detailed Solution:**

(i) Ion - Ion $\rightarrow$ Interaction energy $\propto \frac{1}{r}$
Ion - dipole $\rightarrow$ Interaction energy $\propto \frac{1}{r^2}$
Ion - dipole Interaction energy approaches zero more rapidly as ' $r$ ' increases
(ii) Rotating Polar molecules $\rightarrow$ Interaction energy $\propto \frac{1}{\mathrm{r}^6}$.
(iii) Dipole - induced dipole forces are independent of temperature.
(iv) Non-polar species show London dispersion forces.
