- **(C)** 1 and paramagnetic

$$
\sigma 1 s^2 \sigma^* 1 s^2 \sigma 2 s^2 \sigma^* 2 s^2 \quad \pi 2 p_x^2=\pi 2 p_y^0
$$
So, diamagnetic

$$
