### Q3. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Given, that the abundances of isotopes, ${ }_{54} \mathrm{Fe},{ }_{56} \mathrm{~F}$ and ${ }_{57} \mathrm{Fe}$ are $5 \%$, $90 \%$ and $5 \%$, respectively, the atomic mass of $\mathrm{Fe}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $55.85$
- **(B)** $55.95$
- **(C)** $55.75$
- **(D)** $56.05$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $55.95$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Average atomic weight
$$
\frac{54 \times 5+56 \times 90+57 \times 5}{100}=55.95
$$

</details>
