### Q1. JEE Advanced 2024 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a metal deficient oxide sample, $\mathbf{M}_{\mathbf{x}} \mathbf{Y}_2 \mathbf{O}_4$ ( $\mathbf{M}$ and $\mathbf{Y}$ are metals), $\mathbf{M}$ is present in both +2 and +3 oxidation states and $\mathbf{Y}$ is in +3 oxidation state. If the fraction of $\mathbf{M}^{2+}$ ions present in $\mathbf{M}$ is $\frac{1}{3}$, the value of $\mathbf{X}$ is ________.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.25
- **(B)** 0.33
- **(C)** 0.67
- **(D)** 0.75

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 0.75**

**Detailed Solution:**

Average oxidation state of $\mathrm{M}=\frac{1}{3} \times 2+\frac{2}{3} \times 3=+\frac{8}{3}$
$\begin{aligned} & \therefore \text { For } \mathrm{M}_{\mathrm{X}} \mathrm{M}_{2} \mathrm{Y}_4 \\ & \frac{8}{3} \times \mathrm{x}+3 \times 2+4(-2)=0 \\ & \frac{8}{3} \times \mathrm{x}=2 \\ & \mathrm{x}=\frac{3}{4}=0.75\end{aligned}$

</details>
