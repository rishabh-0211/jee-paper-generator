### Q2. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Among the following, the state function(s) is (are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** internal energy
- **(B)** irreversible expansion work
- **(C)** reversible expansion work
- **(D)** molar enthalpy

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) molar enthalpy**

**Detailed Solution:**

State function are internal energy and molar enthalpy. Work is a path dependent function, however in reversible process the path is fixed i.e. between a given initial and final state there is just on reversible path and one work. Hence, reversible work is also a state function.

</details>
