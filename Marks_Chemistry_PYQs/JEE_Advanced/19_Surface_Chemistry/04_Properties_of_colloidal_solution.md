### Q2. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Among the electrolytes $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4, \mathrm{CaCl}_2 \quad \mathrm{Al}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$ and $\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{Cl}$, the most effective coagulating agent for $\mathrm{Sb}_2 \mathrm{~S}_3$ sol is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CaCl}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Al}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{Cl}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{Al}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Sb}_2 \mathrm{~S}_3$ is an anionic $\mathrm{sol}_2$, therefore cation of highest valency ( $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$ in the present case) would be most effective coagulating agent.

</details>
