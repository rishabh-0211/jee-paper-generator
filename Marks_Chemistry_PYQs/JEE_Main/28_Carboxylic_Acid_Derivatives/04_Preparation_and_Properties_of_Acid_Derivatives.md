### Q10. JEE Main 2022 (29 Jun Shift 2)

In Friedel-Crafts alkylation of aniline, one gets:

**Options:**

- **(A)** alkylated product with ortho and para substitution.
- **(B)** secondary amine after acidic treatment.
- **(C)** an amide product.
- **(D)** positively charged nitrogen at benzene ring.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) positively charged nitrogen at benzene ring.**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>Aniline does not undergo Friedel-crafts reaction due to salt formation with aluminium chloride, the Lewis acid, which is used as a catalyst, whereas aniline is a strong base. So, an acid-base reaction will take place. Thus, aniline reacts with <math><mi mathvariant="normal">A</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>3</mn></mrow></msub></math> to form a salt.

Due to the positive charge on the nitrogen atom (shows <math><mo>-</mo><mi mathvariant="normal">I</mi></math> effect and it is meta directing), electrophilic substitution in the benzene ring is deactivated. Hence, the Friedel-Crafts reaction will not take place in aniline.</p>
