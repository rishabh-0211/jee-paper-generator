### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Complete the following reaction sequence and give the name of major product 'P'.
$CH_3-CH_2-C\equiv N \xrightarrow[\text{(ii) } H_3O^+]{\text{(i) } OH^-/H_2O/\Delta} \xrightarrow[\text{(iv) } H_2O]{\text{(iii) } Cl_2/\text{Red P}} P \text{ (Major product)}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2-Chloropropanoic acid
- **(B)** 3-Chloropropanoic acid
- **(C)** 1-Chloropropane
- **(D)** 2-Chloropropane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 2-Chloropropanoic acid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Step 1: Hydrolysis of the nitrile.
Propanenitrile on basic hydrolysis followed by acidic workup gives propanoic acid:
$CH_3-CH_2-C \equiv N \xrightarrow[(ii)\ H_3O^+]{(i)\ OH^-/H_2O/\Delta} CH_3-CH_2-COOH$

Step 2: Hell$-$Volhard$-$Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction.
Propanoic acid has an $sp^3$ $\alpha$-carbon bearing two $\alpha$-hydrogens. In the HVZ reaction, $Cl_2$ in the presence of red phosphorus selectively replaces one $\alpha$-hydrogen by chlorine, giving the $\alpha$-chloro acid after aqueous workup:
$CH_3-CH_2-COOH \xrightarrow[(iv)\ H_2O]{(iii)\ Cl_2/\text{Red P}} CH_3-CH(Cl)-COOH$

Hence, the major product $P$ is $2$-chloropropanoic acid, which corresponds to option $(1)$.

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The descending order of acidity among the following compounds is:
A. Phenol
B. $4$-nitrophenol
C. $4$-methoxyphenol
D. $4$-nitrobenzoic acid
E. Benzoic acid
<img style="width:200px; "src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/04APRS2__q66_d1.jpg" />
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $B>D>E>A>C$
- **(B)** $D>B>E>A>C$
- **(C)** $C>A>B>D>E$
- **(D)** $D>E>B>A>C$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $D>E>B>A>C$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Carboxylic acids are stronger acids than phenols because the carboxylate anion is stabilized by equivalent resonance structures. Thus, D and E are more acidic than A, B, and C.

For the carboxylic acids, the $-NO_2$ group in D is an electron-withdrawing group ($-I, -R$), which stabilizes the conjugate base, making D more acidic than E.

For the phenols, electron-withdrawing groups increase acidity while electron-donating groups decrease it. The $-NO_2$ group in B is electron-withdrawing, making it more acidic than A. The $-OCH_3$ group in C is electron-donating ($+R > -I$), making it less acidic than A. Thus, the order for phenols is B $>$ A $>$ C.

Combining these, the overall descending order of acidity is D $>$ E $>$ B $>$ A $>$ C.

Answer: $D>E>B>A>C$

</details>

 D. $\mathrm{PhCH}_{2} \mathrm{MgBr}+\mathrm{CO}_{2} \xrightarrow[\mathrm{H}_{3} \mathrm{O}^{+}]{\text {Dry ether }}[\mathrm{D}]$ 
 Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

- **(A)** $A>D>B>C$
- **(B)** $C>A>D>B$
- **(C)** $C>B>A>D$
- **(D)** $A>D>C>B$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $C>A>D>B$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Product [A]: From PhNH₂, steps (1) NaNO₂ + HCl forms diazonium salt, (2) CuCN gives benzonitrile, (3) H₃O⁺/Δ hydrolyzes to benzoic acid (PhCOOH), pKa ≈ 4.2.
