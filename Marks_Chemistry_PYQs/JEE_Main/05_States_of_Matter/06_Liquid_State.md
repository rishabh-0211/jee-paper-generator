### Q1. JEE Main 2016 (09 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Which intermolecular force is most responsible in allowing xenon gas to liquefy?

**Options:**

- **(A)** London forces
- **(B)** Ion-dipole
- **(C)** Ionic
- **(D)** Dipole-dipole

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) London forces**

**Detailed Solution:**

London dispersion Force (LDF), also known as london forces, or instantaneous dipole-induced dipole forces, is the weakest Vander-waal&#39;s force which arises in non-polar molecules due to temporary rearrangement of electrons.

</details>
