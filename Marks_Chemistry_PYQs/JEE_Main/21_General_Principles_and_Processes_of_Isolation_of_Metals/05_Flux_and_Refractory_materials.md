### Q2. JEE Main 2013 (22 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

In Goldschmidt aluminothermic process which of the following reducing agents is used :

**Options:**

- **(A)** calcium
- **(B)** coke
- **(C)** Al-powder
- **(D)** sodium

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Al-powder**

**Detailed Solution:**

Reduction by powdered aluminium is known as Gold-Schmidt aluminothermic process. This process is employed in cases where metals have very high m.p. and are to be extracted from their oxides.

</details>
