### Q1. JEE Main 2022 (24 Jun Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The highest industrial consumption of molecular hydrogen is to produce compound of element :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Carbon
- **(B)** Oxygen
- **(C)** Chlorine
- **(D)** Nitrogen

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Nitrogen**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>The largest single use of dihydrogen is in the synthesis of ammonia (compound of nitrogen) which is used in the manufacture of nitric acid and and nitrogenous fertilizers like urea.</p><p>Around 55% of hydrogen around the world would goes to ammonia production.</p>
