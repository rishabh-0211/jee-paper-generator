- **(A)** <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>BeH</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math>

**Correct Option:** **(A) <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>BeH</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math>**

**Detailed Solution:**

<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>BeH</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math> is indeed considered to be less stable compared to other hydrides among the given due to its significant covalent character and electron deficiency.
