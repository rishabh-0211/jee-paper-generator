### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Sucrose hydrolyses in acidic medium into glucose and fructose by first order rate law with $t_{1/2} = 3$ hour. The percentage of sucrose remaining after $6$ hours is _______. (Nearest integer) (Given: $\log 2 = 0.3010$ and $\log 3 = 0.4771$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **25**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a first order reaction, the amount of reactant remaining after $n$ half-lives is given by $N_t = \dfrac{N_0}{2^n}$.

Given $t_{1/2} = 3$ hours and total time $t = 6$ hours.

Number of half-lives, $n = \dfrac{t}{t_{1/2}} = \dfrac{6}{3} = 2$.

The amount of sucrose remaining is $N_t = \dfrac{N_0}{2^2} = \dfrac{N_0}{4}$.

Percentage of sucrose remaining $= \dfrac{N_t}{N_0} \times 100 = \dfrac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25$.

Answer: $25$

</details>

- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false
