### Q1. JEE Main 2023 (10 Apr Shift 2)

The reaction used for preparation of soap from fat is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** An addition reaction
- **(B)** Reduction reaction
- **(C)** Alkaline hydrolysis reaction
- **(D)** An oxidation reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Alkaline hydrolysis reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>The reaction used for preparation of soap from fat is not alkaline hydrolysis, but rather saponification. In saponification, a triglyceride (fat or oil) is reacted with a strong base, such as sodium hydroxide (or potassium hydroxide, in the presence of heat to produce soap molecules and glycerol as a byproduct. The reaction involves the hydrolysis of the ester bond in the triglyceride by the base to form carboxylate ions (the soap molecules) and glycerol.<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/344eabbd-ae3a-4ab3-bac0-779d90d07511.png" style="width: 500px; height: 259px;" /></p>
