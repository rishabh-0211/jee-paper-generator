### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Given below are two statements:
Statement I: On the basis of inductive effect, the order of stability of alkyl carbanions is CH$_3^- >$ CH$_3$-CH$_2^- >$ (CH$_3$)$_2$CH$^- >$ (CH$_3$)$_3$C$^-$.
Statement II: Allyl and benzyl carbanions are more stabilised by inductive effect and not by resonance effect.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both Statement I and Statement II are correct
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
- **(C)** Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
- **(D)** Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement I is correct because alkyl groups exert a +I effect (electron-donating inductive effect). This increases the electron density on the negatively charged carbon atom, destabilizing the carbanion. Thus, as the number of alkyl groups increases, the stability of the carbanion decreases. The order of stability is $\text{CH}_3^- > \text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2^- > (\text{CH}_3)_2\text{CH}^- > (\text{CH}_3)_3\text{C}^-$.

Statement II is incorrect because allyl and benzyl carbanions are highly stabilized by the resonance effect, where the negative charge is delocalized over the adjacent $\pi$ bonds or the benzene ring, rather than by the inductive effect.

Answer: Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Increasing order of electron withdrawing power of following functional groups is:
a. $-CN$
b. $-COOH$
c. $-NO_2$
d. $-I$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $c < b < d < a$
- **(B)** $c < a < b < d$
- **(C)** $d < b < a < c$
- **(D)** $a < b < c < d$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $d < b < a < c$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The electron withdrawing power ($-I$ effect) of various functional groups follows the standard experimental order:

$-NO_2 > -CN > -COOH > -F > -Cl > -Br > -I$

Based on this series, the increasing order of electron withdrawing power for the given groups is:

$-I < -COOH < -CN < -NO_2$

This corresponds to the sequence $d < b < a < c$.

Answer: $d < b < a < c$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

CORRECT order of stability for the following is $\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}, \mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}, \mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}>\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}>\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CH}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{3}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}^{-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Carbanion stability is determined by the s-character of the hybrid orbital bearing the negative charge.
The acetylide anion $CH \equiv C^-$ has the carbanion on an sp hybridized carbon (50% s-character), providing optimal stabilization through efficient overlap with the charged orbital.
The vinyl anion $CH_2 = CH^-$ has the carbanion on an sp² hybridized carbon (33% s-character), offering moderate stabilization.
The primary alkyl anion $CH_3 - CH_2^-$ has the carbanion on an sp³ hybridized carbon (25% s-character), providing the least stabilization.
Greater s-character enables better penetration of the nucleus and enhanced stabilization of the negative charge.
Therefore, the order is $CH \equiv C^- > CH_2 = CH^- > CH_3 - CH_2^-$.

</details>

 <b>Statement II</b>: $\stackrel{\oplus}{\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{H}_{3}$ is less stable than $\left(\mathrm{CH}_{3}\right)_{3} \stackrel{\oplus}{\mathrm{C}}$ as only three hyperconjugation interactions are possible in $\stackrel{\oplus}{\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{H}_{3}$.

- **(A)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true
- **(B)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(C)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true
- **(D)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Statement I is true but Statement II is false**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement I: In the tert-butyl carbocation $(CH_3)_3C^+$, there are three methyl groups attached to the carbocationic carbon. Conclusion: Statement I is true but Statement II is false.

The order of basicity is I $>$ III $>$ II $>$ IV The lone pair of electrons on $\mathrm{N}$ is more readily available for protonation in I and III then in II.
