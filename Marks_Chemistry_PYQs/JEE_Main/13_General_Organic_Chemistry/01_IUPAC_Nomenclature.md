### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Identify the correct IUPAC name of hydrocarbon ($x$) containing three primary carbon atoms and with molar mass $72\text{ g mol}^{-1}$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1,1-Dimethylcyclopropane
- **(B)** 2,2-Dimethylpropane
- **(C)** 2-Methylbutane
- **(D)** n-pentane

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 2-Methylbutane**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the hydrocarbon be an alkane with general formula $C_nH_{2n+2}$.

Molar mass $= 14n + 2 = 72 \Rightarrow 14n = 70 \Rightarrow n = 5$

The molecular formula is $C_5H_{12}$.

The structural isomers of $C_5H_{12}$ are:

n-pentane: $CH_3-CH_2-CH_2-CH_2-CH_3$ (2 primary carbons)

2-methylbutane: $CH_3-CH(CH_3)-CH_2-CH_3$ (3 primary carbons)

2,2-dimethylpropane: $C(CH_3)_4$ (4 primary carbons)

The hydrocarbon with exactly three primary carbon atoms is 2-methylbutane.

Answer: 2-Methylbutane

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

In IUPAC nomenclature, the <b>correct</b> order of decreasing priority of functional group is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-CONH_2, >C=O, -CHO, -NH_2, -C\equiv C-$
- **(B)** $-CONH_2, -COOCH_3, -CHO, -NH_2, -OH$
- **(C)** $-CONH_2, -CHO, >C=O, -NH_2, -C\equiv C-$
- **(D)** $-CONH_2, -CHO, -CN, -NH_2, -C\equiv C-$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $-CONH_2, -CHO, >C=O, -NH_2, -C\equiv C-$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The correct order of decreasing priority for functional groups in IUPAC nomenclature is:

Carboxylic acids ($-COOH$) $>$ Sulphonic acids ($-SO_3H$) $>$ Esters ($-COOR$) $>$ Acid halides ($-COX$) $>$ Amides ($-CONH_2$) $>$ Nitriles ($-CN$) $>$ Aldehydes ($-CHO$) $>$ Ketones ($>C=O$) $>$ Alcohols ($-OH$) $>$ Thiols ($-SH$) $>$ Amines ($-NH_2$) $>$ Alkynes ($-C\equiv C-$).

Checking the given options:
Option (A) is incorrect because $-CHO$ has a higher priority than $>C=O$.
Option (B) is incorrect because $-COOCH_3$ has a higher priority than $-CONH_2$.
Option (C) correctly follows the priority order: $-CONH_2 > -CHO > >C=O > -NH_2 > -C\equiv C-$.
Option (D) is incorrect because $-CN$ has a higher priority than $-CHO$.

Answer: $-CONH_2, -CHO, >C=O, -NH_2, -C\equiv C-$

</details>
