**Options:**

- **(C)** $\mathrm{CsCl}$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

### Q7. JEE Main 2012 (12 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The radius of a calcium ion is $94 \mathrm{pm}$ and of the oxide ion is $146 \mathrm{pm}$. The possible crystal structure of calcium oxide will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** tetrahedral
- **(B)** trigonal
- **(C)** octahedral
- **(D)** pyramidal

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) octahedral**

**Detailed Solution:**

As per formula,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text { radius ratio } & =\frac{\text { radius of cation }}{\text { radius of anion }} \\
& =\frac{94}{146}=0.643
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the value is between $0.414-0.732$ hence the coordination no. will be 6 and geometry will be octahedral.

</details>
