### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

One mole of $\mathrm{Cl}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})$ was passed into 2 L of cold 2 M KOH solution. After the reaction, the concentrations of $\mathrm{Cl}^{-}, \mathrm{ClO}^{-}$and $\mathrm{OH}^{-}$are respectively (assume volume remains constant)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.5 \mathrm{M}, 0.5 \mathrm{M}, 1 \mathrm{M}$
- **(B)** $0.75 \mathrm{M}, 0.75 \mathrm{M}, 1 \mathrm{M}$
- **(C)** $0.5 \mathrm{M}, 0.5 \mathrm{M}, 0.5 \mathrm{M}$
- **(D)** $1 \mathrm{M}, 1 \mathrm{M}, 1 \mathrm{M}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $0.5 \mathrm{M}, 0.5 \mathrm{M}, 1 \mathrm{M}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cl₂ undergoes disproportionation in cold KOH: $Cl_2 + 2OH^- \rightarrow Cl^- + ClO^- + H_2O$
Initial: 1 mole Cl₂, 2 L × 2 M KOH = 4 moles OH⁻
Molar ratio Cl₂:OH⁻ = 1:2 (stoichiometric)
Products formed: 1 mole Cl⁻, 1 mole ClO⁻; Excess OH⁻ = 4 - 2 = 2 moles
In 2 L solution: [Cl⁻] = 0.5 M, [ClO⁻] = 0.5 M, [OH⁻] = 1 M

</details>
