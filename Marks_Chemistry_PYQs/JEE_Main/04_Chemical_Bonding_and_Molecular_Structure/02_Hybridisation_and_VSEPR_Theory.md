### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Bromine trifluoride autoionizes to form $\text{BrF}_2^{\oplus}$ and $\text{BrF}_4^{\ominus}$. The shapes of the cation and anion are respectively ________, and ________.

**Options:**

- **(A)** bent, square planar
- **(B)** linear, square planar
- **(C)** bent, see-saw
- **(D)** linear, tetrahedral

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) bent, square planar**

**Detailed Solution:**

The autoionization of bromine trifluoride is given by the reaction:

$2\text{BrF}_3 \rightleftharpoons \text{BrF}_2^{\oplus} + \text{BrF}_4^{\ominus}$

For the cation $\text{BrF}_2^{\oplus}$:
The central atom Br has $7$ valence electrons. Due to the positive charge, it has $7 - 1 = 6$ electrons available.
It forms $2$ single bonds with F atoms, leaving $4$ electrons, which constitute $2$ lone pairs.
Steric number = $2$ (bond pairs) + $2$ (lone pairs) = $4$.
The hybridization is $sp^3$. With $2$ bond pairs and $2$ lone pairs, the shape is bent (or V-shaped).

For the anion $\text{BrF}_4^{\ominus}$:
The central atom Br has $7$ valence electrons. Due to the negative charge, it has $7 + 1 = 8$ electrons available.
It forms $4$ single bonds with F atoms, leaving $4$ electrons, which constitute $2$ lone pairs.
Steric number = $4$ (bond pairs) + $2$ (lone pairs) = $6$.
The hybridization is $sp^3d^2$. To minimize repulsion, the $2$ lone pairs occupy opposite (trans) positions in the octahedral geometry, resulting in a square planar shape.

Thus, the shapes of the cation and anion are bent and square planar, respectively.

Answer: bent, square planar

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Given below are two statements :
Statement I : F$_2$O $<$ H$_2$O $<$ Cl$_2$O is the correct trend in terms of bond angle.
Statement II : SiF$_4$, SnF$_4$ and PbF$_4$ are ionic in nature.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false
- **(C)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(D)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is true but Statement II is false**

**Detailed Solution:**

In $Cl_2O$, steric hindrance between the large chlorine atoms increases the bond angle. Thus, Statement I is true.

In Statement II, $SiF_4$ is a covalent compound (it is a gas at room temperature), whereas $SnF_4$ and $PbF_4$ are ionic solids. Thus, Statement II is false.

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Consider the following species:
BrF$_5$, XeF$_5^-$, BF$_4^-$, ICl$_4^-$, XeF$_4$, SF$_4$, NH$_4^+$, ClF$_3$, XeF$_2$, ICl$_2^-$
Number of species having sp$^3$d hybridized central atom is __________.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **4**

**Detailed Solution:**

The steric number $H$ is calculated as $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(V + M - C + A)$, where $V$ is the number of valence electrons on the central atom, $M$ is the number of monovalent atoms, $C$ is the cationic charge, and $A$ is the anionic charge.

For $sp^3d$ hybridization, $H = 5$.

BrF$_5$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(7 + 5) = 6$ ($sp^3d^2$)

XeF$_5^-$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(8 + 5 + 1) = 7$ ($sp^3d^3$)

BF$_4^-$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(3 + 4 + 1) = 4$ ($sp^3$)

ICl$_4^-$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(7 + 4 + 1) = 6$ ($sp^3d^2$)

XeF$_4$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(8 + 4) = 6$ ($sp^3d^2$)

SF$_4$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(6 + 4) = 5$ ($sp^3d$)

NH$_4^+$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(5 + 4 - 1) = 4$ ($sp^3$)

ClF$_3$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(7 + 3) = 5$ ($sp^3d$)

XeF$_2$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(8 + 2) = 5$ ($sp^3d$)

ICl$_2^-$: $H = \dfrac{1}{2}(7 + 2 + 1) = 5$ ($sp^3d$)

The species with $sp^3d$ hybridization are SF$_4$, ClF$_3$, XeF$_2$, and ICl$_2^-$.

The number of such species is $4$.

Answer: $4$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

According to Lewis theory, the total number of $\sigma$ bond-pairs and lone pair of electrons around the central atom of $XeO_6^{4-}$ ion is _____.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **6**

**Detailed Solution:**

The central atom in $XeO_6^{4-}$ is Xenon (Xe).

The number of valence electrons in Xe is $8$.

Let the oxidation state of Xe in $XeO_6^{4-}$ be $x$.

$x + 6(-2) = -4$

$x = +8$

Since the oxidation state of Xe is $+8$, all $8$ of its valence electrons are involved in bonding with the $6$ oxygen atoms. There are no non-bonding electrons left on the central atom.

Number of lone pairs on Xe $= 0$

Xe forms $6$ $\sigma$ bonds with the $6$ oxygen atoms (and $2$ $\pi$ bonds to satisfy its $+8$ oxidation state and minimize formal charge).

Number of $\sigma$ bond-pairs $= 6$

Total number of $\sigma$ bond-pairs and lone pairs around the central atom $= 6 + 0 = 6$.

Answer: $6$

</details>
