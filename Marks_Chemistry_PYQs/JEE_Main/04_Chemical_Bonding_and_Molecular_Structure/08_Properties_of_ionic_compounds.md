### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (29 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `3`

The correct option with order of melting points of the pairs \((\mathrm{Mn}, \mathrm{Fe}),(\mathrm{Tc}, \mathrm{Ru})\) and \((\mathrm{Re}, \mathrm{Os})\) is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\mathrm{Fe} \lt \mathrm{Mn}, \mathrm{Ru} \lt \mathrm{Tc}\) and \(\mathrm{Re} \lt \mathrm{Os}\)
- **(B)** \(\mathrm{Mn} \lt \mathrm{Fe}, \mathrm{Tc} \lt \mathrm{Ru}\) and \(\mathrm{Os} \lt \mathrm{Re}\)
- **(C)** \(\mathrm{Mn} \lt \mathrm{Fe}, \mathrm{Tc} \lt \mathrm{Ru}\) and \(\mathrm{Re} \lt \mathrm{Os}\)
- **(D)** \(\mathrm{Fe} \lt \mathrm{Mn}, \mathrm{Ru} \lt \mathrm{Tc}\) and \(\mathrm{Os} \lt \mathrm{Re}\)

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\mathrm{Mn} \lt \mathrm{Fe}, \mathrm{Tc} \lt \mathrm{Ru}\) and \(\mathrm{Os} \lt \mathrm{Re}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

### Q4. JEE Main 2004

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `3`

Which one of the following aqueous solutions will exhibit highest boiling point?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$
- **(B)** $0.015 \mathrm{M}$ glucose
- **(C)** $0.015 \mathrm{M}$ urea
- **(D)** $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{KNO}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Von't Hoffs factor (i) for $\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4$ is maximum i.e. 3( maximum no of particles)
$$
\mathrm{Na}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4 \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{Na}^{+}+\mathrm{SO}_4^{-}
$$
