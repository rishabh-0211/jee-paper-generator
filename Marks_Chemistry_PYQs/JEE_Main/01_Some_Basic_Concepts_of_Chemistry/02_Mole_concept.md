### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Which of the following contain the same number of atoms ? (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of H, He, O and S are $1, 4, 16$ and $32$ respectively)
A. $2$ g of O$_2$ gas
B. $4$ g of SO$_2$ gas
C. $1400$ mL of O$_2$ at STP
D. $0.05$ L of He at STP
E. $0.0625$ mol of H$_2$ gas
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** A and B only
- **(B)** B and C only
- **(C)** C and D only
- **(D)** A, C and E only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) A, C and E only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Number of atoms is calculated as the product of the number of moles, atomicity, and Avogadro's number ($N_A$).

For A: Moles of O$_2$ = $\dfrac{2}{32} = \dfrac{1}{16}$ mol. Atomicity of O$_2$ is $2$.
Number of atoms = $\dfrac{1}{16} \times 2 \times N_A = \dfrac{1}{8} N_A$

For B: Moles of SO$_2$ = $\dfrac{4}{64} = \dfrac{1}{16}$ mol. Atomicity of SO$_2$ is $3$.
Number of atoms = $\dfrac{1}{16} \times 3 \times N_A = \dfrac{3}{16} N_A$

For C: Moles of O$_2$ at STP = $\dfrac{1400}{22400} = \dfrac{1}{16}$ mol. Atomicity of O$_2$ is $2$.
Number of atoms = $\dfrac{1}{16} \times 2 \times N_A = \dfrac{1}{8} N_A$

For D: Moles of He at STP = $\dfrac{0.05}{22.4} = \dfrac{1}{448}$ mol. Atomicity of He is $1$.
Number of atoms = $\dfrac{1}{448} \times 1 \times N_A = \dfrac{1}{448} N_A$

For E: Moles of H$_2$ = $0.0625 = \dfrac{1}{16}$ mol. Atomicity of H$_2$ is $2$.
Number of atoms = $\dfrac{1}{16} \times 2 \times N_A = \dfrac{1}{8} N_A$

Comparing the number of atoms, A, C, and E contain the same number of atoms.

Answer: A, C and E only

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

How many grams of residue is obtained by heating $2.76$ g of silver carbonate?
(Given: Molar mass of C, O and Ag are $12$, $16$ and $108$ g mol$^{-1}$ respectively)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.08$ g
- **(B)** $2.16$ g
- **(C)** $3.24$ g
- **(D)** $4.32$ g

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2.16$ g**

**Detailed Solution:**

Molar mass of $Ag_2CO_3$ = $2 \times 108 + 12 + 3 \times 16 = 276$ g mol$^{-1}$

Number of moles of $Ag_2CO_3$ = $\dfrac{2.76}{276} = 0.01$ mol

The decomposition reaction of silver carbonate on heating is:

$Ag_2CO_3(s) \rightarrow 2Ag(s) + CO_2(g) + \dfrac{1}{2}O_2(g)$

From the stoichiometry of the reaction, $1$ mole of $Ag_2CO_3$ gives $2$ moles of Ag as residue.

Number of moles of Ag formed = $2 \times 0.01 = 0.02$ mol

Mass of Ag residue = $0.02 \times 108 = 2.16$ g

Answer: $2.16$ g

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The correct order of total number of atoms present in
(A) $2$ moles of cyclohexane
(B) $684$ g of sucrose
(C) $90.8$ L of dihydrogen at STP
is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $C>A>B$
- **(B)** $C>B>A$
- **(C)** $B>C>A$
- **(D)** $B>A>C$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $B>A>C$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For (A):

Formula of cyclohexane is $C_6H_{12}$.

Number of atoms per molecule = $6 + 12 = 18$.

Total number of atoms in $2$ moles = $2 \times 18 \times N_A = 36 N_A$.

For (B):

Formula of sucrose is $C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}$.

Molar mass of sucrose = $342$ g/mol.

Number of moles = $\dfrac{684}{342} = 2$ moles.

Number of atoms per molecule = $12 + 22 + 11 = 45$.

Total number of atoms = $2 \times 45 \times N_A = 90 N_A$.

For (C):

Formula of dihydrogen is $H_2$.

Number of moles at STP = $\dfrac{90.8}{22.7} = 4$ moles.

Number of atoms per molecule = $2$.

Total number of atoms = $4 \times 2 \times N_A = 8 N_A$.

Comparing the total number of atoms:

$90 N_A > 36 N_A > 8 N_A$

Therefore, the correct order is $B > A > C$.

Answer: $B>A>C$

</details>

Number of moles and number of molecules in $1.4187$ L of $SO_2$ at STP respectively are:

- **(B)** $0.0633; 3.812 \times 10^{22}$
- **(C)** $0.1266; 7.6238 \times 10^{22}$

Number of molecules $= n \times N_A$

Number of molecules $= 0.0633 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 3.812 \times 10^{22}$

Answer: $0.0633; 3.812 \times 10^{22}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The ratio of mass percentage (w/w) of C : H in a hydrocarbon is $12 : 1$. It has two carbon atoms. The weight (in g) of $CO_2(g)$ formed when $3.38$ g of this hydrocarbon is completely burnt in oxygen is : (Given : Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ C : 12, H : 1, O : 16)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5.68$
- **(B)** $11.44$
- **(C)** $22.74$
- **(D)** $17.05$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $11.44$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ratio of mass percentage of C : H = $12 : 1$

Ratio of moles of C : H = $\dfrac{12}{12} : \dfrac{1}{1} = 1 : 1$

The empirical formula is $CH$. Since the hydrocarbon has two carbon atoms, its molecular formula is $C_2H_2$.

Molar mass of $C_2H_2 = 2 \times 12 + 2 \times 1 = 26$ g mol$^{-1}$

Moles of $C_2H_2$ in $3.38$ g = $\dfrac{3.38}{26} = 0.13$ mol

The combustion reaction is:

$C_2H_2 + \dfrac{5}{2}O_2 \rightarrow 2CO_2 + H_2O$

From the stoichiometry, $1$ mole of $C_2H_2$ produces $2$ moles of $CO_2$.

Moles of $CO_2$ produced = $2 \times 0.13 = 0.26$ mol

Molar mass of $CO_2 = 12 + 2 \times 16 = 44$ g mol$^{-1}$

Weight of $CO_2$ formed = $0.26 \times 44 = 11.44$ g

Answer: $11.44$

</details>
