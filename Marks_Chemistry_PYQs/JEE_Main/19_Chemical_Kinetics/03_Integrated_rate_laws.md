### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

If the half life of a first order reaction is $6.93$ minutes then the time required for completion of $99$% of the reaction will be _____ minutes.
(Given : $\log 2 = 0.3010$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **46.06**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a first-order reaction, the rate constant $k$ is given by:

$k = \dfrac{2.303 \log 2}{t_{1/2}}$

Using the standard approximation $2.303 \log 2 \approx 0.693$:

$k = \dfrac{0.693}{6.93} = 0.1 \text{ min}^{-1}$

The time required for $99\%$ completion of the reaction is given by the integrated rate law:

$t = \dfrac{2.303}{k} \log \left( \dfrac{a}{a - x} \right)$

Here, the initial concentration $a = 100$ and the amount reacted $x = 99$, so $a - x = 1$.

$t = \dfrac{2.303}{0.1} \log \left( \dfrac{100}{1} \right)$

$t = 23.03 \times \log(10^2)$

$t = 23.03 \times 2 = 46.06 \text{ minutes}$

Answer: $46.06$

</details>

$p_i =$ initial pressure of gas A, $p_t =$ total pressure of the reaction mixture at time $t$
Expression of rate constant ($k$) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{2p_i}{p_i - p_t}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{3p_i - 2p_t}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{3p_i}{4p_i - p_t}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given reaction is $A(g) \rightarrow B(g) + C(g)$

At $t = 0$, the initial pressure of A is $p_i$ and the pressures of B and C are $0$.

At time $t$, let the decrease in pressure of A be $x$.
Pressure of A $= p_i - x$
Pressure of B $= x$
Pressure of C $= x$

Total pressure at time $t$ is $p_t = (p_i - x) + x + x = p_i + x$

From this, we get $x = p_t - p_i$

The partial pressure of A at time $t$ is $P_A = p_i - x = p_i - (p_t - p_i) = 2p_i - p_t$

$k = \dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{P_{A,0}}{P_A}$

Substituting the values, we get

$k = \dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$

Answer: $\dfrac{1}{t}\ln\dfrac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

$t_{100\%}$ is the time required for the $100\%$ completion of the reaction while $t_{1/2}$ is the time required for $50\%$ of the reaction to be completed. Which of the following option correctly represents the relation between $t_{100\%}$ and $t_{1/2}$ for zero and first order reactions respectively?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^2$ and $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^{-\infty}$
- **(B)** $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ and $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^\infty$
- **(C)** $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ and $t_{100\%} = (2t_{1/2})^2$
- **(D)** $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^\infty$ and $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ and $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^\infty$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For a zero-order reaction:
The integrated rate law is given by $t = \dfrac{[A]_0 - [A]_t}{k}$.

For $50\%$ completion ($t_{1/2}$), $[A]_t = \dfrac{[A]_0}{2}$:
$t_{1/2} = \dfrac{[A]_0 - \dfrac{[A]_0}{2}}{k} = \dfrac{[A]_0}{2k}$

For $100\%$ completion ($t_{100\%}$), $[A]_t = 0$:
$t_{100\%} = \dfrac{[A]_0 - 0}{k} = \dfrac{[A]_0}{k}$

Comparing the two, we get:
$t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$

For a first-order reaction:
The integrated rate law is given by $t = \dfrac{1}{k} \ln\left(\dfrac{[A]_0}{[A]_t}\right)$.

For $100\%$ completion ($t_{100\%}$), $[A]_t = 0$:
$t_{100\%} = \dfrac{1}{k} \ln\left(\dfrac{[A]_0}{0}\right) = \infty$

A first-order reaction takes infinite time for $100\%$ completion. Among the given choices, this is symbolically represented as $\infty$ or $(t_{1/2})^\infty$.

Thus, the correct relation is $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ for zero-order and $t_{100\%} = \infty$ for first-order.

Answer: $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ and $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^\infty$

</details>

### Q7. JEE Main 2026 (28 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

An organic compound undergoes first order decomposition. The time taken for decomposition to $\left(\frac{1}{8}\right)^{\text {th }}$ and $\left(\frac{1}{10}\right)^{\text {th }}$ of its initial concentration are $\mathrm{t}_{1 / 8}$ and $\mathrm{t}_{1 / 10}$ respectively. What is the value of $\frac{\mathrm{t}_{1 / 8}}{\mathrm{t}_{1 / 10}} \times 10$ ? ($\log 2=0.3$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 9
- **(C)** 0.9
- **(D)** 30

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 9**

**Detailed Solution:**

For first-order decomposition with rate equation $[A] = [A]_0 e^{-kt}$, the time for concentration to reduce to fraction f is: $t = \frac{1}{k}\ln\frac{1}{f} = \frac{\ln(1/f)}{k}$
