### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Given below are two statements:
$R=8.314$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ and $1$ cal $=4.2$ J
Statement I: When $E_a=12.6$ kcal/mol, the room temperature rate constant is doubled by a $10\,^\circ$C increase in temperature ($298$ K to $308$ K)
Statement II: For a first order reactions A $\rightarrow$ B,
<img style="width: 250px" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_april_watermarked/08APRS2__q57_d1.jpg" />
Here $[A]_o$ is the initial concentration of A and $t_{1/2}$ is half life of reaction.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false
- **(C)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(D)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is true but Statement II is false**

**Detailed Solution:**

For Statement I:
According to the Arrhenius equation:
$\log \left( \dfrac{k_2}{k_1} \right) = \dfrac{E_a}{2.303 R} \left( \dfrac{T_2 - T_1}{T_1 T_2} \right)$

Given that the rate constant doubles, $k_2 = 2k_1$. The temperatures are $T_1 = 298$ K and $T_2 = 308$ K.
The gas constant $R = 8.314$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1} = \dfrac{8.314}{4.2}$ cal K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1} \approx 1.98$ cal K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$.

Substituting the values:
$\log(2) = \dfrac{E_a}{2.303 \times 1.98} \left( \dfrac{308 - 298}{298 \times 308} \right)$

$0.301 = \dfrac{E_a}{4.56} \left( \dfrac{10}{91784} \right)$

$E_a = \dfrac{0.301 \times 4.56 \times 91784}{10} \approx 12598$ cal/mol $\approx 12.6$ kcal/mol.
Thus, Statement I is true.

For Statement II:
For a first order reaction, the half-life is given by:
$t_{1/2} = \dfrac{0.693}{k}$
This indicates that the half-life of a first order reaction is independent of the initial concentration $[A]_o$. The graph of $t_{1/2}$ versus $[A]_o$ should be a horizontal line parallel to the concentration axis.
The given graph shows $t_{1/2}$ directly proportional to $[A]_o$, which corresponds to a zero order reaction ($t_{1/2} = \dfrac{[A]_o}{2k}$).
Thus, Statement II is false.

Therefore, Statement I is true but Statement II is false.

Answer: Statement I is true but Statement II is false

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

Decomposition of a hydrocarbon follows the equation $k = (5.5 \times 10^{11}\,\text{s}^{-1})\,e^{\frac{-28000\,\text{K}}{T}}$. The activation energy of reaction is __________ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest Integer) Given : R $= 8.3$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **232**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to the Arrhenius equation, $k = A e^{-\dfrac{E_a}{RT}}$.

The given equation is $k = (5.5 \times 10^{11} \text{ s}^{-1}) e^{-\dfrac{28000}{T}}$.

Comparing the exponent of $e$ in both equations:

$\dfrac{E_a}{RT} = \dfrac{28000}{T}$

$E_a = 28000 \times R$

Substituting the value of $R = 8.3 \text{ J K}^{-1} \text{ mol}^{-1}$:

$E_a = 28000 \times 8.3 \text{ J mol}^{-1}$

$E_a = 232400 \text{ J mol}^{-1}$

$E_a = 232.4 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

Rounding to the nearest integer, the activation energy is $232$.

Answer: $232$

Given:
$k_1 = 1.5 \times 10^3 \text{ s}^{-1}$
$k_2 = 4.5 \times 10^3 \text{ s}^{-1}$
$T_1 = 27^\circ\text{C} = 300 \text{ K}$
$E_a = 60 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1} = 60000 \text{ J mol}^{-1}$
$R = 8.3 \text{ J K}^{-1} \text{ mol}^{-1}$

Substituting the values into the equation:

$\log \left( \dfrac{4.5 \times 10^3}{1.5 \times 10^3} \right) = \dfrac{60000}{2.3 \times 8.3} \left( \dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1}{T_2} \right)$

$\log 3 = \dfrac{60000}{19.09} \left( \dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1}{T_2} \right)$

$0.48 = \dfrac{60000}{19.09} \left( \dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1}{T_2} \right)$

$\dfrac{1}{300} - \dfrac{1}{T_2} = \dfrac{9.1632}{60000} = 1.5272 \times 10^{-4}$

$\dfrac{1}{T_2} = \dfrac{1}{300} - 1.5272 \times 10^{-4}$

### Q55. JEE Main 2012 (26 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The activation energy for a reaction which doubles the rate when the temperature is raised from $298 \mathrm{~K}$ to $308 \mathrm{~K}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $59.2 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $39.2 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $52.9 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $29.5 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $52.9 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Activation energy can be calculated from the equation.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{\log K_2}{\log K_1}=\frac{-E_a}{2.303 R}\left(\frac{1}{T_2}-\frac{1}{T_1}\right) \\
& \text { Given } \frac{\log K_2}{\log K_1}=2 \quad T_2=308 ; \quad T_1=298 \\
& \therefore \quad \log 2=\frac{-E_a}{2.303 \times 8.314}\left(\frac{1}{308}-\frac{1}{298}\right) \\
& E_a=52.9 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}
\end{aligned}
$$
