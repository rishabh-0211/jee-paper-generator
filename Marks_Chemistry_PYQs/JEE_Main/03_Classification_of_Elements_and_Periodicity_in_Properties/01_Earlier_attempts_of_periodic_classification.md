### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (3 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

Match the LIST-I with LIST-II.
$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}\hline & \begin{array}{c} \text{LIST-I} \\ \text{(Family)} \end{array} & & \begin{array}{c} \text{LIST-II} \\ \text{(Symbol of Element)} \end{array} \\ \hline \text{A.} & \begin{array}{l} \text{Pnicogen} \\ \text{(group 15)} \end{array} & \text{I.} & \text{Ts} \\ \hline \text{B.} & \text{Chalcogen} & \text{II.} & \text{Og} \\ \hline \text{C.} & \text{Halogen} & \text{III.} & \text{Lv} \\ \hline \text{D.} & \text{Noble gas} & \text{IV.} & \text{Mc} \\ \hline\end{array}$
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III
- **(B)** A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II
- **(C)** A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II
- **(D)** A-II, B-III, C-IV, D-I

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) A-IV, B-III, C-I, D-II**

**Detailed Solution:**

(A) Pnictogen $\Rightarrow$ Mc (Moscovium),
Atomic No. $=115$
(B) Chalcogen $\Rightarrow \mathrm{Lv}$ (Livermorium),
Atomic No. $=116$
(C) Halogen $\Rightarrow \mathrm{Ts}$ (Tennessine),
Atomic No. $=117$
(D) Noble gas $\Rightarrow \mathrm{Og}$ (Oganesson),
Atomic No. $=118$

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (28 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

Given below are two statements :
<strong> Statement (I):</strong> According to the Law of Octaves, the elements were arranged in the increasing order of their atomic number.
<strong> Statement (II):</strong>  Meyer observed a periodically repeated pattern upon plotting physical properties of certain elements against their respective atomic numbers.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both <strong> Statement (I)</strong> and<strong> Statement (II)</strong> are false.
- **(B)** Both <strong> Statement (I)</strong> and<strong> Statement (II)</strong> are true.
- **(C)** <strong> Statement (I)</strong> is false but<strong> Statement (II)</strong> is true.
- **(D)** <strong> Statement (I)</strong> is true but<strong> Statement (II)</strong> is false.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Both <strong> Statement (I)</strong> and<strong> Statement (II)</strong> are false.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Law of octaves was arranged in the increasing order of their atomic weight.
Lothar Meyer plotted the physical properties such as atomic volume, melting point and boiling point against atomic weight.

</details>
