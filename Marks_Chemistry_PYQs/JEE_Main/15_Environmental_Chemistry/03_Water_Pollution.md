- **(A)** more than $17 \mathrm{ppm}$
- **(B)** equal to $10 \mathrm{ppm}$
- **(C)** equal to $5 \mathrm{ppm}$
- **(D)** less than $5 \mathrm{ppm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) more than $17 \mathrm{ppm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Water sample is reported to be highly polluted if BOD value of sample is more than 17 ppm.
