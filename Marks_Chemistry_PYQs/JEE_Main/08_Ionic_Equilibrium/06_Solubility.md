### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `3`

$M_3 A_2$ is a sparingly soluble salt of molar mass $y$ g mol$^{-1}$ and solubility $x$ g L$^{-1}$. The ratio of the molar concentration of the anion ($A^{3-}$) to the solubility product of the salt is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{1}{54} \cdot \dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{y^5}{108 x^4}$
- **(C)** $108 \cdot \dfrac{x^5}{y^5}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{1}{108} \cdot \dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\dfrac{1}{54} \cdot \dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The molar solubility $S$ of the salt is given by $S = \dfrac{x}{y}$ mol L$^{-1}$.

The dissociation of the sparingly soluble salt $M_3A_2$ is:

$M_3A_2 \rightleftharpoons 3M^{2+} + 2A^{3-}$

The molar concentration of the anion $A^{3-}$ is:

$[A^{3-}] = 2S$

The solubility product $K_{sp}$ of the salt is:

$K_{sp} = [M^{2+}]^3 [A^{3-}]^2 = (3S)^3 (2S)^2 = 27S^3 \times 4S^2 = 108S^5$

The ratio of the molar concentration of the anion to the solubility product is:

$\dfrac{[A^{3-}]}{K_{sp}} = \dfrac{2S}{108S^5} = \dfrac{1}{54S^4}$

Substituting $S = \dfrac{x}{y}$ into the expression, we get:

$\text{Ratio} = \dfrac{1}{54 \left(\dfrac{x}{y}\right)^4} = \dfrac{1}{54} \cdot \dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$

Answer: $\dfrac{1}{54} \cdot \dfrac{y^4}{x^4}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The solubility product constants of $Ag_2CrO_4$ and $AgBr$ are $32x$ and $4y$ respectively at $298$ K. The value of $\left(\dfrac{\text{molarity of } Ag_2CrO_4}{\text{molarity of } AgBr}\right)$ can be expressed as :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{2\sqrt[3]{x}}{y}$
- **(B)** $2\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{y}}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the solubility of $Ag_2CrO_4$ be $S_1$.

For $Ag_2CrO_4 \rightleftharpoons 2Ag^+ + CrO_4^{2-}$

$K_{sp} = (2S_1)^2(S_1) = 4S_1^3$

$4S_1^3 = 32x \Rightarrow S_1^3 = 8x \Rightarrow S_1 = 2\sqrt[3]{x}$

Let the solubility of $AgBr$ be $S_2$.

For $AgBr \rightleftharpoons Ag^+ + Br^-$

$K_{sp} = S_2^2$

$S_2^2 = 4y \Rightarrow S_2 = 2\sqrt{y}$

The ratio of their molarities is:

$\dfrac{S_1}{S_2} = \dfrac{2\sqrt[3]{x}}{2\sqrt{y}} = \dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$

Answer: $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `3`

Consider two Group IV metal ions $\mathrm{X}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Y}^{2+}$. A solution containing $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{X}^{2+}$ and $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{Y}^{2+}$ is saturated with $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$. The pH at which the metal sulphide YS will form as a precipitate is $\_\_\_\_$. (Nearest integer) 
 (Given: $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}(\mathrm{XS})=1 \times 10^{-22}$ at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}, \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}(\mathrm{YS})=4 \times 10^{-16}$ at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, $\left[\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}\right]=0.1 \mathrm{M}$ in solution, $\mathrm{K}_{a 1} \times \mathrm{K}_{a 2}\left(\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}\right)=1.0 \times 10^{-21}, \log 2=0.30$, $\log 3=0.48, \log 5=0.70$)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **4**

**Detailed Solution:**

$XS(s) \rightleftharpoons X^{+2}(aq.) + S^{2-}(aq.)$
For precipitation of $XS(s)$
$[X^{+2}][S^{2-}] \geq K_{sp}(XS)$
$[S^{2-}] \geq \dfrac{1 \times 10^{-22}}{0.01} = 10^{-20}$
$YS(s) \rightleftharpoons Y^{+2}(aq) + S^{2-}(aq)$
For precipitation of $YS(s)$
$[Y^{+2}][S^{2-}] \geq K_{sp}(YS)$
$[S^{2-}] \geq \dfrac{4 \times 10^{-16}}{10^{-2}} = 4 \times 10^{-14}$
Now, $H_2S(aq) \rightleftharpoons 2H^+(aq) + S^{2-}(aq)$
$\dfrac{[S^{2-}][H^+]^2}{H_2S} = K_{a_1} \times K_{a_2} = 1 \times 10^{-21}$
$[S^{2-}] = \dfrac{1 \times 10^{-21} \times [H_2S]}{[H^+]^2} \geq 4 \times 10^{-14}$
$[H^+]^2 \leq \dfrac{1}{4} \times 10^{-7} \times 10^{-1}$
$[H^+] \leq \dfrac{1}{2} \times 10^{-4} \Rightarrow pH \geq 4.3$
Nearest integer pH = 4.

</details>

Which one of the following arrangements represents the correct order of solubilities of sparingly soluble salts $\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2, \mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$, $\mathrm{BaSO}_4$ and $\mathrm{CrCl}_3$ respectively?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{BaSO}_4>\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2>\mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3>\mathrm{CrCl}_3$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{BaSO}_4>\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2>\mathrm{CrCl}_3>\mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{BaSO}_4>\mathrm{CrCl}_3>\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2>\mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2>\mathrm{BaSO}_4>\mathrm{CrCl}_3>\mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{BaSO}_4>\mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2>\mathrm{CrCl}_3>\mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\text { } \begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{Cr}_2\left(\mathrm{SO}_4\right)_3 \leftrightharpoons 2 \mathrm{Cr}^{+++}+3 \mathrm{SO}_4^{--} \\
& \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}=(2 s)^2(3 s)^3=4 \mathrm{~s}^2 \times 27 s^3=108 s^5 \\
& \mathrm{~s}=\left(\frac{\mathrm{K}_{s p}}{108}\right)^{1 / 5} \\
& \mathrm{Hg}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2 \leftrightharpoons 2 \mathrm{Hg}^{2+}+2 \mathrm{Cl}^{-} \\
& \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}=(2 s)^2 \times(2 s)^2=16 s^4 \\
& \mathrm{~s}=\left(\frac{\mathrm{K}_{s p}}{16}\right)^{1 / 4} \\
& \mathrm{BaSO}_4 \leftrightharpoons \mathrm{Ba}^{++}+\mathrm{SO}_4^{-} \\
& \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}=s^2 \\
& \mathrm{~s}=\sqrt{\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}} \\
& \mathrm{CrCl}_3 \leftrightharpoons \mathrm{Cr}^{3+}+3 \mathrm{Cl}^{-} \\
& s
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{sp}}=s \times(3 s)^3=27 s^4 \\
& s=\left(\frac{\mathrm{K}_{s p}}{27}\right)^{1 / 4}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
