### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (31 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Give below are two statements:</p><p>Statement-I : Noble gases have very high boiling points.</p><p>Statement-II: Noble gases are monoatomic gases. They are held together by strong dispersion forces. Because of this they are liquefied at very low temperature. Hence, they have very high boiling points.</p><p>In the light of the above statements. choose the correct answer from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true
- **(C)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(D)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Both Statement I and Statement II are false**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>As noble gases do not form compounds, hence they cannot occur in the form of diatomic molecules. </p><p>Noble gases, being monoatomic, have no interatomic forces except weak dispersion forces and therefore, they are liquefied at very low temperatures. Hence, they have low boiling points.</p><p>Noble gases are held together by weak dispersion forces.</p><p>Statement I and II are False</p>
