### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Find the correct statements related to group 15 hydrides.
A. Reducing nature increases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$
B. Tendency to donate lone pair of electrons decreases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$
C. The stability of hydrides decreases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$
D. HEH bond angle decreases from NH$_3$ to SbH$_3$ (E = Elements of group 15)
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** A and B only
- **(B)** B and C only
- **(C)** A, B, C and D
- **(D)** A, C and D Only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) A, B, C and D**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement A: The reducing nature of group 15 hydrides depends on the E-H bond strength. As the size of the central atom increases down the group, the E-H bond dissociation enthalpy decreases. Hence, the reducing character increases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$. This statement is correct.

Statement B: The basic character or the tendency to donate a lone pair of electrons depends on the electron density on the central atom. As the size of the central atom increases down the group, the electron density decreases. Thus, the basicity decreases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$. This statement is correct.

Statement C: Due to the decrease in E-H bond dissociation enthalpy down the group, the thermal stability of the hydrides decreases from NH$_3$ to BiH$_3$. This statement is correct.

Statement D: The H-E-H bond angle decreases down the group because the electronegativity of the central atom decreases and the size increases, causing the bond pairs to move further away from the central atom. This decreases the bond pair-bond pair repulsion. The bond angles are NH$_3$ ($107.8^{\circ}$), PH$_3$ ($93.6^{\circ}$), AsH$_3$ ($91.8^{\circ}$), and SbH$_3$ ($91.3^{\circ}$). This statement is correct.

All statements A, B, C, and D are correct.

Answer: A, B, C and D

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (06 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Treatment of a gas '$X$' with a freshly prepared ferrous sulphate solution gives a compound '$Y$' as a brown ring. The compounds $X$ and $Y$ are.

**Options:**

- **(A)** NO and $[\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$
- **(B)** NO$_2$ and $[\text{Fe(NO}_2)]\text{SO}_4$
- **(C)** N$_2$O and $[\text{Fe(N}_2\text{O})]\text{SO}_4$
- **(D)** N$_2$O$_4$ and $[\text{Fe(N}_2\text{O}_4)]\text{SO}_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) NO and $[\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The brown ring test is used for the detection of nitrate ($\text{NO}_3^-$) or nitrite ($\text{NO}_2^-$) ions.

In this test, nitric oxide (NO) gas is evolved which reacts with the freshly prepared ferrous sulphate ($\text{FeSO}_4$) solution to form a brown ring of nitroso ferrous sulphate.

The gas $X$ is NO.

The reaction is given by:

$\text{FeSO}_4 + \text{NO} \rightarrow [\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$

The actual composition of the brown ring complex is $[\text{Fe(H}_2\text{O})_5(\text{NO})]\text{SO}_4$, which is often represented as $[\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$.

Thus, $X$ is NO and $Y$ is $[\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$.

Answer: NO and $[\text{Fe(NO)}]\text{SO}_4$

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (05 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Correct statements from the following are:
A. Nitrogen in oxidation states from $+1$ to $+4$ disproportionates in acid medium.
B. Nitrogen has the ability to form $d\pi - p\pi$ multiple bonds with itself and other elements with small size and high electronegativity.
C. N-N single bond is stronger than P-P single bond.
D. Nitrogen has highest density in its group due to small size.
E. The maximum covalency of nitrogen is four since it has only four valence orbitals for bonding.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

**Options:**

- **(A)** B, C and D Only
- **(B)** C, D and E Only
- **(C)** A, C and E Only
- **(D)** A and E Only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) A and E Only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement A is correct. Nitrogen in oxidation states from $+1$ to $+4$ tends to disproportionate in acidic medium. For example, $3HNO_2 \rightarrow HNO_3 + H_2O + 2NO$.

Statement B is incorrect. Nitrogen does not have d-orbitals in its valence shell, so it cannot form $d\pi - p\pi$ multiple bonds. It forms $p\pi - p\pi$ multiple bonds.

Statement C is incorrect. The N-N single bond is weaker than the P-P single bond due to high interelectronic repulsion between the non-bonding electrons, which arises from the small N-N bond length.

Statement D is incorrect. Density increases down the group in group 15. Nitrogen is a gas and has the lowest density in its group, while bismuth has the highest.

Statement E is correct. Nitrogen has only four valence orbitals (one 2s and three 2p orbitals) available for bonding. Therefore, its maximum covalency is restricted to four.

Thus, only statements A and E are correct.

Answer: A and E Only

</details>
