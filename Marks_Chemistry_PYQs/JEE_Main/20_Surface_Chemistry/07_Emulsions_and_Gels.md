### Q2. JEE Main 2022 (24 Jun Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

<p>Given below are two statements:</p><p><strong>Statement I</strong> : Emulsions of oil in water are unstable and sometimes they separate into two layers on standing.</p><p><strong>Statement II</strong> : For stabilisation of an emulsion, excess of electrolyte is added.</p><p>In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :</p>

**Options:**

- **(A)** Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
- **(C)** Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
- **(D)** Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>Oil and water are not soluble in each other. since oils are non-polar organic compounds and water is a polar compound. So, they will form hydrophobic type of colloid.</p><p>Hydrophobic collides are unstable and required some stabilisers like proteins, gums, natural and synthetic soaps, etc. Excess of electrolyte can not act as stabilisers.</p><p>So, statement 1 is true but statement 2 is false.</p>

</details>
