### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

At 298 K, the mole percentage of $\mathrm{N}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})$ in air is $80 \%$. Water is in equilibrium with air at a pressure of 10 atm. What is the mole fraction of $\mathrm{N}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})$ in water at 298 K ? ($\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{H}}$ for $\mathrm{N}_{2}$ is $6.5 \times 10^{7} \mathrm{~mm} \mathrm{Hg}$)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1.23 \times 10^{-7}$
- **(B)** $1.17 \times 10^{-4}$
- **(C)** $9.35 \times 10^{5}$
- **(D)** $9.35 \times 10^{-5}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $9.35 \times 10^{-5}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using Henry's Law: Mole fraction = $\frac{P}{K_H}$
N₂ mole percentage in air = 80%, total pressure = 10 atm
Partial pressure of N₂ = 0.80 × 10 = 8 atm = 8 × 760 = 6080 mmHg
$K_H$ for N₂ = 6.5 × 10⁷ mmHg
Mole fraction of N₂ = $\frac{6080}{6.5 \times 10^7} = \frac{6.08 \times 10^3}{6.5 \times 10^7} = 0.935 \times 10^{-4} = 9.35 \times 10^{-5}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Given below are two statements: 
 <b>Statement I</b>: The Henry's law constant $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{H}}$ is constant with respect to variations in solution's concentration over the range for which the solution is ideally dilute. 
 <b>Statement II</b>: $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{H}}$ does not differ for the same solute in different solvents. 
 In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement I is false but Statement II is true
- **(B)** Both Statement I and Statement II are false
- **(C)** Statement I is true but Statement II is false
- **(D)** Both Statement I and Statement II are true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Statement I is true but Statement II is false**

**Detailed Solution:**

Statement I: $K_H$ is constant with respect to concentration variations in ideally dilute solutions.
TRUE - Henry's law constant depends only on temperature and the nature of the solute-solvent interaction. For ideally dilute solutions, $K_H$ = $P_\text{gas}/x_\text{solute}$ remains constant regardless of solute concentration within the dilute range.

Statement II: $K_H$ does not differ for the same solute in different solvents.
FALSE - Henry's law constant is solvent-dependent. The same gas or solute has different $K_H$ values in different solvents at the same temperature because the solute-solvent interactions differ. For example, CO₂ dissolves differently in water versus ethanol, giving different $K_H$ values.

Therefore, Statement I is true but Statement II is false.

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

Consider a solution of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}(\mathrm{~g})$ dissolved in water in a closed container. Which one of the following plots correctly represents variation of log (partial pressure of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ in vapour phase above water) $[y$-axis] with $\log$ (mole fraction of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ in water) $[x$-axis $]$ at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** <img style="width:100px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_o_53_1_1.png">
- **(B)** <img style="width:100px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_o_53_2_1.png">
- **(C)** <img style="width:100px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_o_53_3_1.png">
- **(D)** <img style="width:100px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_o_53_4_1.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) <img style="width:100px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/22S1_o_53_2_1.png">**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Henry's Law, the partial pressure of a gas in the vapour phase ($p$) is proportional to the mole fraction of the gas in the solution ($x$).
The mathematical expression is: $p = K_H \cdot x$
where $K_H$ is the Henry's law constant.
Taking logarithm on both sides:
$\log(p) = \log(K_H \cdot x)$
$\log(p) = \log(K_H) + \log(x)$
Comparing this with the equation of a straight line $y = mx + c$:
$y = \log(p)$ (y-axis)
$x = \log(x)$ (x-axis)
$m = 1$ (slope)
$c = \log(K_H)$ (y-intercept)
Since $K_H$ for $CO_2$ in water is a positive constant at $25^{\circ}C$, $\log(K_H)$ will be a constant intercept.
The plot of $\log(p)$ vs $\log(x)$ is a straight line with a positive slope and a positive y-intercept.
This corresponds to graph (2).

</details>
