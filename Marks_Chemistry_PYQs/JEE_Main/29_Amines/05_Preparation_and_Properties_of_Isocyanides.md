### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Match List - I with List - II.
<table style="border: 1px solid black; border-collapse: collapse; width: auto;"><tr><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">List - I
Mixture of Compounds</th><th style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">List - II
Reagent used to distinguish</th></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">A. Diethyl amine + Ethyl amine</td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">I. Bromine water</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">B. Acetaldehyde + Acetone</td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">II. $CHCl_3 + KOH, \Delta$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">C. Ethanol + Phenol</td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">III. Neutral $FeCl_3$</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">D. Benzoic acid + Cinnamic acid</td><td style="border: 1px solid black; padding: 5px;">IV. Ammonical silver nitrate</td></tr></table>
Choose the <b>correct</b> answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III
- **(B)** A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
- **(C)** A-II, B-IV, C-I, D-III
- **(D)** A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I**

**Detailed Solution:**

Diethyl amine is a secondary amine and ethyl amine is a primary amine. Primary amines give the carbylamine test with $CHCl_3$ and $KOH$ on heating, while secondary amines do not. Thus, A matches with II.

Acetaldehyde is an aldehyde and acetone is a ketone. Aldehydes reduce ammoniacal silver nitrate (Tollens' reagent) to form a silver mirror, while ketones do not. Thus, B matches with IV.

Phenol reacts with neutral $FeCl_3$ to form a violet-colored complex, whereas ethanol does not react. Thus, C matches with III.

Cinnamic acid contains a carbon-carbon double bond which decolorizes bromine water, whereas benzoic acid does not react with bromine water. Thus, D matches with I.

The correct matching is A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I.

Answer: A-II, B-IV, C-III, D-I

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (23 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Compound ' P ' undergoes the following sequence of reactions : 
 <img style="width:500px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_q_64_1.png"> 
 ' $\mathrm{P}^{\prime}$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** <img style="width:150px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_o_64_1_1.png">
- **(B)** <img style="width:150px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_o_64_2_1.png">
- **(C)** <img style="width:150px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_o_64_3_1.png">
- **(D)** <img style="width:135px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_o_64_4_1.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) <img style="width:135px;" src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main_2026_watermark_improved/23S1_o_64_4_1.png">**

**Detailed Solution:**

The final product is cyclohexyl isocyanide ($C_6H_{11}NC$).
Working backwards from the final product:
The last step involves $CHCl_3$ and $KOH$ (alc), which is the Carbylamine reaction.
This reaction converts a primary amine ($R-NH_2$) into an isocyanide ($R-NC$). Thus, the intermediate before this step must be cyclohexylamine ($C_6H_{11}NH_2$).
The step before cyclohexylamine involves $Br_2$ and $KOH$, which is the Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation reaction.
This reaction converts an amide ($R-CONH_2$) into a primary amine ($R-NH_2$) with one less carbon atom.
Therefore, the amide 'Q' must be cyclohexanecarboxamide ($C_6H_{11}CONH_2$).
The first step converts 'P' into 'Q' using $NH_3$ and heat.
Carboxylic acids react with $NH_3$ followed by heating to form amides.
Thus, 'P' must be cyclohexanecarboxylic acid ($C_6H_{11}COOH$).
Reaction sequence:
$C_6H_{11}COOH$ (P) $\xrightarrow{NH_3, \Delta} C_6H_{11}CONH_2$ (Q)
$C_6H_{11}CONH_2$ (Q) $\xrightarrow{Br_2, KOH} C_6H_{11}NH_2$ (Cyclohexylamine)
$C_6H_{11}NH_2 \xrightarrow{CHCl_3, KOH} C_6H_{11}NC$ (Cyclohexyl isocyanide)
Comparing with the options, 'P' is cyclohexanecarboxylic acid, which corresponds to option (4).

</details>
