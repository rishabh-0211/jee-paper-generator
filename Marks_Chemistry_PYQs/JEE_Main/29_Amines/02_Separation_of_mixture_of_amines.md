### Q9. JEE Main 2016 (09 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

The test to distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary amines is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sandmeyer's reaction
- **(B)** Carbylamine reaction
- **(C)** Idoform test
- **(D)** <math><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>6</mn></mrow></msub><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">H</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>5</mn></mrow></msub><mi mathvariant="normal">S</mi><msub><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">O</mi></mrow><mrow><mn>2</mn></mrow></msub><mi mathvariant="normal">C</mi><mi mathvariant="normal">l</mi></math>

<p>The Hinsberg test, which can distinguish primary, secondary, and tertiary amines, is based upon sulfonamide formation. In the Hinsberg test, an amine is reacted with benzene sulfonyl chloride.
