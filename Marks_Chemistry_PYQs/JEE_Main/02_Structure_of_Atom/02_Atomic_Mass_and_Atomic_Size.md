### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

A monoatomic anion $(A^-)$ has $45$ neutrons and $36$ electrons. Atomic mass, group in the periodic table and physical state at room temperature of the element $(A)$ respectively are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $80, 17$, liquid
- **(B)** $81, 16$, solid
- **(C)** $80, 16$, gas
- **(D)** $81, 15$, gas

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $80, 17$, liquid**

**Detailed Solution:**

Number of electrons in $A^{-}$ is $36$.

Number of electrons in neutral atom $A = 36 - 1 = 35$.

Atomic number of $A = 35$.

Number of protons in $A = 35$.

Atomic mass of $A = \text{Number of protons} + \text{Number of neutrons} = 35 + 45 = 80$.

The element with atomic number $35$ is Bromine ($\text{Br}$).

Bromine belongs to Group $17$ of the periodic table.

The physical state of Bromine at room temperature is liquid.

Answer: $80, 17$, liquid

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (29 Jan Shift 1)

Statement (II): The magnitude of electron gain enthalpy of halogen decreases in the order.
\(\mathrm{Cl}\gt\mathrm{F}\gt\mathrm{Br}\gt\mathrm{I}\)
In the light of the above statements, choose the most appropriate answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct
- **(B)** Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect
- **(C)** Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect
- **(D)** Both Statement I and Statement II are correct

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{r} \propto \mathrm{q}^{-}$(for isoelectronic species)
$\propto \frac{1}{q^{+}}$
$\therefore$ Statement I is correct
Magnitude of electron gain enthalpy
$\mathrm{Cl}>\mathrm{F}>\mathrm{Br}>\mathrm{I}$
