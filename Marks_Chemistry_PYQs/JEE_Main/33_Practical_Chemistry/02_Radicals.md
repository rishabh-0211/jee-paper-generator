When a salt is treated with sodium hydroxide solution it gives gas $\mathrm{X}$. On passing gas $\mathrm{X}$ through reagent $\mathrm{Y}$ a brown coloured precipitate is formed. $\mathrm{X}$ and $\mathrm{Y}$ respectively, are

**Options:**

### Q3. JEE Main 2025 (2 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `3`

Choose the correct tests with respective observations.
(A) \(\mathrm{CuSO}_4\) (acidified with acetic acid) \(+ \mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \rightarrow\) Chocolate brown precipitate.
(B) \(\mathrm{FeCl}_3+\mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \rightarrow\) Prussian blue precipitate.
(C) \(\mathrm{ZnCl}_2+\mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]\), neutralised with \(\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH}\) \(\rightarrow\) White or bluish white precipitate.
(D) \(\mathrm{MgCl}_2+\mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \rightarrow\) Blue precipitate.
(E) \(\mathrm{BaCl}_2+\mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]\), neutralised with NaOH \(\rightarrow\) White precipitate.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

**Options:**

- **(A)** A, D and E only
- **(B)** B, D and E only
- **(C)** A, B and C only
- **(D)** C, D and E only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) A, B and C only**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(2 \mathrm{CuSO}_4+\mathrm{K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}}\underset{\text{(Chocolate brown ppt.)}}{\mathrm{Cu}_2\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]+2 \mathrm{~K}_2 \mathrm{SO}_4}\)
\(4 \mathrm{FeCl}_3+3 \mathrm{~K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \longrightarrow \underset{\text { (Prussian Blue ppt.) }}{\mathrm{Fe}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]_3}+12 \mathrm{KCl}\)
\(3 \mathrm{ZnCl}_4+2 \mathrm{~K}_4\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH}} \underset{\text { (White or bluish white ppt.) }}{\mathrm{K}_2 \mathrm{Zn}_3\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_6\right]_2}+6 \mathrm{KCl}\)

</details>
