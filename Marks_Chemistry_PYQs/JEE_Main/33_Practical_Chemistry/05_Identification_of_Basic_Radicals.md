### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (04 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Among $Fe^{3+}$, $Pb^{2+}$, $Cu^{2+}$ and $Mn^{2+}$, identify the one that gets precipitated out while passing $H_2S$ in presence of $NH_4OH$ as group reagent. The highest possible oxidation state of the corresponding metal is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $+3$
- **(B)** $+4$
- **(C)** $+2$
- **(D)** $+7$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $+7$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The group reagent $H_2S$ in the presence of $NH_4OH$ is used for the precipitation of Group IV cations in qualitative analysis.

Among the given ions, $Mn^{2+}$ belongs to Group IV and is precipitated as $MnS$.

$Pb^{2+}$ and $Cu^{2+}$ belong to Group II and are precipitated by $H_2S$ in an acidic medium.

$Fe^{3+}$ belongs to Group III and is precipitated as $Fe(OH)_3$ by $NH_4OH$ in the presence of $NH_4Cl$.

The metal corresponding to the precipitated ion is Manganese ($Mn$).

The electronic configuration of $Mn$ is $[Ar] 3d^5 4s^2$. By losing all its valence electrons, it can exhibit a maximum oxidation state of $+7$ (for example, in $KMnO_4$).

Answer: $+7$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

**Options:**

- **(A)** sulphide & sulphide
- **(B)** chromate & sulphide
- **(C)** carbonate & carbonate
- **(D)** hydroxide & carbonate

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) carbonate & carbonate**

**Detailed Solution:**

In Group V of cation analysis, both Ba²⁺ and Ca²⁺ are precipitated by adding Na₂CO₃ or (NH₄)₂CO₃ solution. ### Q3. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `2`

Consider three metal chlorides $\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}$ and z, where x is water soluble at room temperature, y is sparingly soluble in water at room temperature and z is soluble in hot water. $\mathrm{x}, \mathrm{y}$ and z are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CuCl}_{2}, \mathrm{AgCl}$ and $\mathrm{PbCl}_{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{AlCl}_{3}, \mathrm{PbCl}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{BaCl}_{2}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{MgCl}_{2}, \mathrm{AgCl}$ and $\mathrm{AlCl}_{3}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{AgCl}, \mathrm{Hg}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{PbCl}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{CuCl}_{2}, \mathrm{AgCl}$ and $\mathrm{PbCl}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Metal chloride solubility characteristics:
x must be water soluble at room temperature, y must be sparingly soluble at room temperature, and z must be soluble in hot water but not in cold water.
CuCl₂ is highly soluble in water (x).
AgCl is notoriously insoluble in water and sparingly soluble at any temperature, classifying it as sparingly soluble (y).
PbCl₂ exhibits unusual solubility behavior—it is sparingly soluble in cold water but its solubility increases with temperature, making it soluble in hot water (z).
This combination perfectly matches all three criteria.
