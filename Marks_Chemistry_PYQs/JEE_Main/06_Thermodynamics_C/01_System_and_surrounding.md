### Q4. JEE Main 2023 (10 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

<p>The number of incorrect statement/s about the black body from the following is______</p><p>(A) Emit or absorb energy in the form of electromagnetic radiation.</p><p>(B) Frequency distribution of the emitted radiation depends on temperature.</p><p>(C) At a given temperature, intensity vs frequency curve passes through a maximum value.</p><p>(D) The maximum of the intensity vs frequency curve is at a higher frequency at higher temperature compared to that at lower temperature.</p>

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **0**

**Detailed Solution:**

<blockquote><p>A black body is an idealised object that absorbs all the radiation that falls on it and emits radiation over a broad range of frequencies. </p><p>Black bodies emit and absorb energy in the form of electromagnetic radiation.</p><p>The frequency distribution of the emitted radiation from a black body depends on its temperature and is given by Planck&#39;s law.</p><p>At a given temperature, the intensity vs frequency curve for a black body radiation passes through a maximum value. This frequency is known as the peak frequency or the frequency of maximum emission.</p><p>At higher temperatures, the peak frequency of the black body radiation shifts towards higher frequencies (shorter wavelengths). This means that the maximum of the intensity vs frequency curve is at a higher frequency at higher temperature compared to that at lower temperature.</p></blockquote>

</details>
