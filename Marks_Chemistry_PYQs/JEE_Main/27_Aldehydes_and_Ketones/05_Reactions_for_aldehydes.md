### Q3. JEE Main 2012 (26 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Difficulty Level:* `1`

Tollen's reagent and Fehling solutions are used to distinguish between

**Options:**

- **(A)** acids and alcohols
- **(B)** alkanes and alcohols
- **(C)** ketones and aldehydes
- **(D)** n-alkaens and branched alkanes

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) ketones and aldehydes**

**Detailed Solution:**

All aldehydes and ketones show reaction with Tollen's reagent and Fehling solutions, but ketones do not show this reaction.
Note :- Benzaldehyde do not give reaction with Fehling solution.

</details>
