**Options:**

- **(A)** Fries rearrangement and Kolbe
- **(B)** Cumene and Reimer-Tiemann
- **(C)** Dow and Reimer-Tiemann
- **(D)** Dow and Sandmeyer.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Dow and Reimer-Tiemann**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>Phenol is manufactured by heating chlorobenzene with 10% aqueous NaOH solution at about 623K under 300 atm and in the presence of copper salt acting as a catalyst to form sodium phenoxide. The sodium salt when treated with dil. HCl, gives phenol. This method is known as Dow&#39;s process. </p><p>When phenol is heated with <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>CHCl</mi><mn>3</mn></msub></math> in the presence of aqueous NaOH, an aldehydic group (&minus;CHO) get introduced in the ring at a position ortho to the phenolic group. This reaction is known as the Reimer-Tiemann reaction.</p><p><img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/6d6bb09a-1ad7-45bc-911a-55f8587d5688-image.png" style="width: 450px; height: 163px;" /></p>

</details>
