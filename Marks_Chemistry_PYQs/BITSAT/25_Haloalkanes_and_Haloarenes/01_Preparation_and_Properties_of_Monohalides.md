### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In $\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{N}} 2$ substitution reaction of the type
$R-\mathrm{Br}+\mathrm{Cl}^{-} \xrightarrow[\mathrm{BMF}]{ } R-\mathrm{Cl}+\mathrm{Br}^{-}$
Which one of the following has highest relative rate?

**Options:**

- **(A)** <img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/5DkfSdrI89o8xV90DR-NfZhcDUAVonwJOVuYGxVT2I0.original.fullsize.png"/>
- **(B)** <img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/egGBdcTy0UDKIAnCZwYInqqERryoN2iU0wIWKOYXxMw.original.fullsize.png"/>
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The rate of $S_n 2$ reaction is the maximum with $1^{\circ}$ allyl halide having least steric hindrance. Out of
$\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}$ and $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}, \mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{Br}$ has the least steric hindrance and hence has the highest relative rate.

</details>

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{CH}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{OH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{H}_{3} \mathrm{C}-\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{CH}_{3} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{Cl}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{CH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{2}=\mathrm{CHCl}$

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{OH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Cl}$**

& \mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2}-\mathrm{Cl}+\mathrm{HCl}+\mathrm{POCl}_{3}

$$

$\mathrm{MeCH}_{2} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{Br} .\left(\mathrm{E}_{2}\right)$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{PhCH}_{2} \mathrm{Br}>\mathrm{Ph} \mathrm{CHBrMe}>\mathrm{PhCBrMe}_{2}>$
$\operatorname{PhCBrMePh}\left(\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{N}} 1\right)$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{MeI}>\mathrm{MeBr}>\mathrm{MeCl}>\mathrm{MeF}\left(\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{N}} 2\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{PhCH}_{2} \mathrm{Br}>\mathrm{Ph} \mathrm{CHBrMe}>\mathrm{PhCBrMe}_{2}>$
$\operatorname{PhCBrMePh}\left(\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{N}} 1\right)$**

The more is the stability of intermediate carbonium ion, the more is the chance of $\mathrm{S}_{\mathrm{N}} 1$ mechanism. The intermediates obtained will be 
$\mathrm{Ph}^{+} \mathrm{C} \mathrm{H}_{2}(\mathrm{i}), \mathrm{Ph} \mathrm{C} \mathrm{H}-\mathrm{Me}(\mathrm{ii})$
$\mathrm{Ph} \mathrm{C}-\mathrm{Me}_{2}\left(\right.$ iii) $, \mathrm{Ph} \mathrm{C}^{+} \mathrm{MePh}$ (iv)
The stabilty is of the order iv $>\mathrm{iii}>\mathrm{ii}>\mathrm{i}$
