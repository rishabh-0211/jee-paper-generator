### Q1. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following ions can be separated by aq. $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{OH}$ in presence of $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{Cl}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$ and $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}$ and $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ is of group II and $\mathrm{Al}^{3+}$ is of group III of cation analysis.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A mixture of chlorides of copper, cadmium, chromium, iron and aluminium was dissolved in water acidified with $\mathrm{HCl}$ and hydrogen sulphide gas was passed for sufficient time. It was filtered, boiled and a few drops of nitric acid were added while boiling. To this solution ammonium chloride and sodium hydroxide were added and filtered. The filterate shall give test for.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sodium and iron
- **(B)** Sodium and aluminium
- **(C)** Aluminium and iron
- **(D)** Sodium, iron, cadmium and Al

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Sodium and aluminium**

**Detailed Solution:**

CuS and CdS are precipitated by $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$. Hydroxide of Al will pass into the solution in the form of $\mathrm{NaAlO}_{2}$ being amphoteric in nature. Hence filtrate will give test for sodium and aluminium.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An aqueous solution of a substance gives a white precipitate on treatment with dil. HCl which dissolves on heating. When $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$ is passed through the hot acidic solution, a black precipitate is obtained. The substance is a

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Hg}_{2}^{2+}$ salt
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$ salt
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Ag}^{+}$ salt
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$ salt

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$ salt**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{PbCl}_{2}$ is insoluble in cold water, soluble in hot water and $\mathrm{PbS}$ is black ppt in acidic medium.

</details>
