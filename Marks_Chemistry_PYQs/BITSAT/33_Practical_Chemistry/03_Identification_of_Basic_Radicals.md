### Q1. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\mathrm{K}_{4}\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_{6}\right]$ is used in detecting.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+} \mathrm{ion}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{+} \mathrm{ion}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{3+}$ ion
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}$ ion

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+} \mathrm{ion}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ ion can be detected by $\mathrm{K}_{4}\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_{6}\right]$

$$

\begin{aligned}

4 \mathrm{Fe}^{3+}+3 \mathrm{~K}_{4}\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_{6}\right] \\

& \longrightarrow \mathrm{Fe}_{4}\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{CN})_{6}\right]_{3}+12 \mathrm{~K}^{+}

\end{aligned}

$$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$ gas when passed through a solution of cations containing $\mathrm{HCl}$ precipitates the cations of second group of qualitative analysis but not those belonging to the fourth group. It is because

**Options:**

- **(A)** presence of HC1 decreases the sulphide ion concentration.
- **(B)** solubility product of group II sulphides is more than that of group IV sulphides.
- **(C)** presence of HCl increases the sulphide ion concentration.
- **(D)** sulphides of group IV cations are unstable in $\mathrm{HC} 1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) presence of HC1 decreases the sulphide ion concentration.**

**Detailed Solution:**

IV $^{\text {th }}$ group needs higher $\mathrm{S}^{2-}$ ion concentration. In presence of $\mathrm{HCl}$, the dissociation of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$ decreases hence produces less amount of sulphide ions due to common ion effect, thus $\mathrm{HCl}$ decreases the solubility of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$ which is sufficient to precipitate II $^{\text {nd }}$ group radicals.

</details>
