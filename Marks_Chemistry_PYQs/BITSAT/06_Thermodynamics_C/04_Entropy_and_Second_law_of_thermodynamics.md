### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Standard entropy of $X_2, Y_2$ and $X Y_2$ are 60,40 and $50 \mathrm{JK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$, respectively. For the reaction, $\frac{1}{2} X_2+\frac{3}{2} Y_2 \longrightarrow X Y_3, \Delta H=-30 \mathrm{~kJ}$, to be at equilibrium, the temperature will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1250 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(B)** $500 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(C)** $750 \mathrm{~K}$
- **(D)** $1000 \mathrm{~K}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $750 \mathrm{~K}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\Delta S=S\left(X Y_3\right)-\frac{1}{2} S\left(X_2\right)-\frac{3}{2} S\left(Y_2\right)$
$=50-30-60=-40 \mathrm{~J} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}$
$\Delta H=-30 \mathrm{~kJ}=-3000 \mathrm{~J}$
$\Delta G=\Delta H-T \Delta S$
At equilibrium,
$\Delta G=0$
$T=\frac{\Delta H}{\Delta S}=\frac{-3000}{-40}=750 \mathrm{~K}$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the enthalpy of vaporization of water is 186.5 $\mathrm{kJmol}^{-1}$, the entropy if its vaporization will be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0.5 \mathrm{kJK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $1.0 \mathrm{kJK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $1.5 \mathrm{kJK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $2.0 \mathrm{kJK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $0.5 \mathrm{kJK}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given enthalpy of vaporization,

$$

\Delta \mathrm{H}=186.5 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}

$$

Boiling point of water

$$

=100^{\circ} \mathrm{C}=100+273=373 \mathrm{~K}

$$

Entropy change,

$$

\begin{array}{r}

\Delta \mathrm{S}=\frac{\Delta \mathrm{H}}{\mathrm{T}}=\frac{186.5 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}}{373 \mathrm{~K}} \\

=0.5 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1} \mathrm{~K}^{-1}

\end{array}

$$

</details>
