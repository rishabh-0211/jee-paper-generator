### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In an adiabatic process, no transfer of heat takes place between system and surrounding. Choose the correct option for free expansion of an ideal gas under adiabatic condition from the following.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $q=0, \Delta T \neq 0, W=0$
- **(B)** $q \neq 0, \Delta T=0, W=0$
- **(C)** $q=0, \Delta T=0, W=0$
- **(D)** $q=0, \Delta T < 0, W \neq 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $q=0, \Delta T=0, W=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Free expansion, $W=0$
Adiabatic process, $q=0$
$\Delta U=q+W=0$, this means that internal energy remains constant. Therefore, $\Delta T=0$ in ideal gas there is no intermolecular attraction.
Hence, when such a gas expands under adiabatic conditions into a vacuum, no heat is absorbed or evolved, since no external work is done to separate the molecules.

</details>
