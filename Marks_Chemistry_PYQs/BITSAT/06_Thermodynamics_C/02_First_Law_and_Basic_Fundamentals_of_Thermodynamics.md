### Q3. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Work out the heat change (cal) when $40 \mathrm{~g}$ of He gas at $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ undergoes isothermal and reversible compression from initial pressure of $1 \mathrm{~atm}$ to $10 \mathrm{~atm}\left(\mathrm{R}=2 \mathrm{cal} \mathrm{K}^{-1} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}\right)$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $13.818 \mathrm{kcal}$
- **(B)** $-13.818 \mathrm{kcal}$
- **(C)** $55.272 \mathrm{kcal}$
- **(D)** $-55.272 \mathrm{k} \mathrm{cal}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $-13.818 \mathrm{kcal}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{q} &=-\mathrm{w}_{\mathrm{rev}}=-\left(-2.303 \mathrm{nRT} \log \frac{\mathrm{P}_{1}}{\mathrm{P}_{2}}\right) \\ &=2.303 \times \frac{40}{4} \times 2 \times 300 \log \frac{1}{10}=-13.82 \mathrm{kcal} \end{aligned}$

### Q4. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For the combustion reaction at $298 \mathrm{~K}$

$2 \mathrm{Ag}(\mathrm{s})+1 / 2 \mathrm{O}_{2}(\mathrm{~g}) \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{Ag}_{2} \mathrm{O}(\mathrm{s})$

Which of the following alternatives is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{U}$
- **(B)** $\Delta \mathrm{H}>\Delta \mathrm{U}$
- **(C)** $\Delta \mathrm{H}<\Delta \mathrm{U}$
- **(D)** $\Delta \mathrm{H}$ and $\Delta \mathrm{U}$ has no relation with each other

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\Delta \mathrm{H}<\Delta \mathrm{U}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{U}+\Delta \mathrm{nRT}$

$\Delta \mathrm{n}=\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{p}}-\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{R}}$

Now, $\Delta \mathrm{n}=2-\frac{5}{2}=-\frac{1}{2}$

$\therefore \Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{U}-\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{RT}$

Thus, $\Delta \mathrm{U}=\Delta \mathrm{H}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{RT}$

$\therefore \Delta \mathrm{U}>\Delta \mathrm{H}$

</details>

Monoatomic gas

### Q8. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which one of the following is correct regarding $\Delta \mathrm{H}:$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\quad \Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}+2 \mathrm{RT}$
- **(B)** $\quad \Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}-2 \mathrm{RT}$
- **(C)** $\Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}+\mathrm{RT}$
- **(D)** $\quad \Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}-\mathrm{RT}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\quad \Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}-2 \mathrm{RT}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{N}_{2}+3 \mathrm{H}_{2} \rightleftharpoons 2 \mathrm{NH}_{3}$
According to thermodynamics's Ist law
$$
\Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}+\mathrm{nRT}
$$
Where $\Delta \mathrm{H}=$ enthalpy of reaction at constant pressure $\Delta \mathrm{E}=$ heat of reaction at constant volume $\mathrm{R}=$ molar gas constant
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mathrm{T}=\text { temperature of the reaction } \\
\mathrm{n}=(\text { no. of moles of product })-(\text { no. of moles }
\end{array}
$$
of reactant.) From reaction, $\mathrm{n}=\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{p}}-\mathrm{n}_{\mathrm{R}}=2-4=-2$
Hence, $\Delta \mathrm{H}=\Delta \mathrm{E}-2 \mathrm{RT}$.

</details>
