### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Ge (II) compounds are powerful reducing agents whereas $\mathrm{Pb}$ (IV) compounds are strong oxidants. It can be because

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Pb}$ is more electropositive than $\mathrm{Ge}$.
- **(B)** ionisation potential of lead is less than that of Ge.
- **(C)** ionic radii of $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Pb}^{4+}$ are larger than that of $\mathrm{Ge}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Ge}^{4+}$
- **(D)** more pronounced inert pair effect in lead has.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) more pronounced inert pair effect in lead has.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Inert pair effect is more pronounced in heavier members like Pb.
Hence, $\mathrm{Pb}$ (IV) compounds act as strong oxidising agents and are reduced to more stable $\mathrm{Pb}$ (II) compounds.

</details>
