### Q1. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is the most basic oxide?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Sb}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Bi}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{SeO}_{2}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Al}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Bi}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

More the oxidation state of the central atom (metal) more is its acidity. Hence $\mathrm{SeO}_{2}(\mathrm{O} . \mathrm{S} .$ of $\mathrm{Se}=+4)$ is acidic. Further for a given O.S., the basic character of the oxides increases with the increasing size of the central atom. Thus $\mathrm{Al}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{Sb}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$ are amphoteric and $\mathrm{Bi}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$ is basic.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Three separate samples of a solution of a single salt gave these results. One formed a white precipitate with excess ammonia solution, one formed a white precipitate with dil. HCl solution and one formed a black precipitate with $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$. The salt could be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{AgNO}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Hg}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{MnSO}_{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}+2 \mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{OH}$
$$
\begin{array}{r}
\rightarrow \mathrm{Pb}(\mathrm{OH})_{2} \downarrow+2 \mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{NO}_{3}(\text { white ppt }) \\
\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}+2 \mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow \mathrm{PbCl}_{2} \downarrow+2 \mathrm{HNO}_{3}(\text { whiteppt }) \\
\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{~S} \rightarrow \mathrm{PbS} \downarrow+2 \mathrm{HNO}_{3} \text { (black) }
\end{array}
$$

</details>
