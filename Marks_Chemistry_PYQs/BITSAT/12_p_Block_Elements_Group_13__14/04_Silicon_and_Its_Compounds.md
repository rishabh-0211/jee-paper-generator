### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Ordinary glass is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sodium silicate
- **(B)** Calcium silicate
- **(C)** Sodium and calcium silicate
- **(D)** Mixed salt of $\mathrm{Na}$ and $\mathrm{Ca}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Sodium silicate**

**Detailed Solution:**

Normal glass is calcium alkali silicate glass made by fusing the alkali metal carbonate, $\mathrm{CaCO}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{SiO}_{2}$

</details>
