### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

20 mL of 0.1 M acetic acid is mixed with 50 mL of potassium acetate. $K_\alpha$ of acetic acid $=1.8 \times 10^{-5}$ at $27^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. Calculate the concentration of potassium acetate if pH of mixture is 4.8 .

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.1 M
- **(B)** 0.04 M
- **(C)** 0.03 M
- **(D)** 0.02 M

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 0.04 M**

**Detailed Solution:**

For an acidic buffer,
$\mathrm{pH}=\mathrm{p} K_\alpha+\log \frac{[\text { Salt }]}{[\text { Acid }]}\ldots(i)$
Let the concentration of potassium acetate solution $=x \mathrm{M}$ 20 mL of 0.1 M acetic acid $=20 \times 0.1 \mathrm{millimol}$ $=2$ millimol
50 mL of $x \mathrm{M}$ potassium acetate $=x \times 50 \mathrm{millimol}$
$\begin{aligned}
& =50 x \text { milimol } \\
\mathrm{pH} & =4.8
(Given)\end{aligned}$
Substitute all value in eq. (i),
$\begin{aligned}
4.8 & =-\left(\log 1.8+10^{-5}\right)+\log \frac{50 x}{2} \\
\log 25 x & =0.06 \\
x & =0.04 \mathrm{M}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is $\left[\mathrm{NH}_4^{+}\right]$in a solution that is $0.02 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{NH}_3$ $0.01 \mathrm{M} \mathrm{KOH} ?\left[K_b\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)=1.8 \times 10^{-5}\right]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3.6 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$
- **(B)** $1.8 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$
- **(C)** $0.9 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$
- **(D)** $7.2 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3.6 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{NH}_4^{+}+\mathrm{OH}^{-}$
$K_b=\frac{\left[\mathrm{NH}_4^{+}\right]\left[\mathrm{OH}^{-}\right]}{\left[\mathrm{NH}_4 \mathrm{OH}\right]}$
$1.8 \times 10^{-5}=\frac{\left[\mathrm{NH}_4^{+}\right][0.01]}{[0.02]}$
$\therefore \quad\left[\mathrm{NH}_4^{+}\right]=3.6 \times 10^{-5} \mathrm{M}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following combinations would not result in the formation of a buffer solution?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{NH}_{3}+\mathrm{HCl}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{Cl}+\mathrm{NH}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}+\mathrm{NaCl}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NaOH}+\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{NaOH}+\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Combination of $\mathrm{NaOH}$ and $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}$ is the mixture of alkali and acetic acid. Therefore this combination can not be buffer forming solution.

</details>
