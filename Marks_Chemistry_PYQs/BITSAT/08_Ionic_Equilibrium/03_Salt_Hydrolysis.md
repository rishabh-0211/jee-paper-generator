### Q1. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The percentage hydrolysis of $0.15 \mathrm{M}$ solution of ammonium acetate, $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{a}}$ for $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}$ is $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$

and $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{b}}$ for $\mathrm{NH}_{3}$ is $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.556
- **(B)** 4.72
- **(C)** 9.38
- **(D)** 5.56

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0.556**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\alpha=\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{w}}}{\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{a}} \times \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{b}}}}=\sqrt{\frac{1 \times 10^{-14}}{1.8 \times 10^{-5} \times 1.8 \times 10^{-5}}}=0.55$

</details>
