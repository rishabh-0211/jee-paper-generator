### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The pH of 1 N aqueous solutions of HCl , $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}$ and HCOOH follows the order:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{HCl}\gt\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{OOH}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{HCl}=\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}\gt\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{HCl}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}=\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{HCl}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}\gt\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{HCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Stronger the acid, lower is the pH or weaker the acid, higher the pH .
The strength of the given acid is in the order
$\mathrm{HCl}\gt\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}$
Hence, their pH is in the order
$\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{COOH}\gt\mathrm{HCOOH}\gt\mathrm{HCl}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$100 \mathrm{~mL}$ of $2 \mathrm{M}$ of formic acid $\left(\mathrm{p} K_a=3.74\right)$ is neutralise by $\mathrm{NaOH}$, at the equivalence point $\mathrm{pH}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $7$
- **(B)** $6$
- **(C)** $9.5$
- **(D)** $8.87$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $8.87$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sodium formate is present at the equivalence point. It is the salt of weak acid $+$ strong base. So, final solution will be basic in nature.
As we know
$\mathrm{pH}=7+\frac{\mathrm{p} K_a}{2}+\frac{\log C}{2}$
where, $C$ is concentration of salt.
Total volume of solution $=100+100=200 \mathrm{~mL}$
Concentration of salt $(C)=2 \times \frac{100}{200}=1 \mathrm{M}$
$\mathrm{pH}=7+\frac{3.74}{2}+\frac{\log [1]}{2}$
$\mathrm{pH}=7+1.87+0$
$\mathrm{pH}=8.87$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ goes to air, causes green house effect and gets dissolved in water. What will be the effect on soil fertility and $\mathrm{pH}$ of the water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Increases
- **(B)** Decreases
- **(C)** Remain same
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Decreases**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{CO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{CO}_{3} \rightarrow \mathrm{H}^{+}+\mathrm{HCO}_{3}^{-}$
Here $\left[\mathrm{H}^{+}\right]$ increases hence, $\mathrm{pH}$ decreases due to which soil fertility will also decreases.

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The pOH value of a solution whose hydroxide ion concentration is $6.2 \times 10^{-9} \mathrm{~mol} /$ litre is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 8.21
- **(B)** 6.21
- **(C)** 7.75
- **(D)** 7.21

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 8.21**

**Detailed Solution:**

$-\log (\mathrm{OH})=\mathrm{pOH} ;-\log 6.2 \times 10^{-9}=\mathrm{pOH}$
$\therefore \mathrm{pOH}=8.21$

</details>

### Q7. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The pH of a solution is increased from 3 to 6 ; its $\mathrm{H}^{+}$ ion concentration will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** reduced to half
- **(B)** doubled
- **(C)** reduced by 1000 times
- **(D)** increased by 1000 times

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) reduced by 1000 times**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{pH}=3 \ldots\left[\mathrm{H}^{+}\right]=10^{-3} ; \quad \mathrm{pH}=6$
$\therefore\left[\mathrm{H}^{+}\right]=10^{-6}$. Hence $\left[\mathrm{H}^{+}\right]$ is reduced by
$10^{-3}$ times.

</details>
