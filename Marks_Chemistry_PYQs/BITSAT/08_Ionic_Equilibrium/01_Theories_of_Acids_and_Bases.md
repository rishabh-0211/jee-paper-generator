### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following molecules acts as a Lewis acid?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_2 \mathrm{O}$
- **(B)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_3 \mathrm{P}$
- **(C)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_3 \mathrm{~N}$
- **(D)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_3 \mathrm{~B}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left(\mathrm{CH}_3\right)_3 \mathrm{~B}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Lewis concept "Acids are electron acceptor and bases are electron donor" Since, electron deficient compounds also have an ability to accept electrons, these are regarded as acids.
Trimethylborane \((\mathrm{CH})_3\) B have incomplete octet thus, act as Lewis acid.

### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What is the correct increasing order of Bronsted bases?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}>\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}>\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{ClO}^{-}>\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}>\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$ is the correct

increasing order of Bronsted base. With increase in the number of oxygen atoms in the conjugate bases, the delocalisation of the $\pi$ bond becomes more and more extended. This results in decrease in the electron density. Consequently basicity also decreases.

</details>
