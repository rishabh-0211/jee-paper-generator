### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The sol formed in the following unbalanced equation is
$\mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} \rightarrow$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{~S}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{~S}_3$
- **(C)** As
- **(D)** S

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{~S}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+3 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{~S} \rightarrow \mathrm{As}_2 \mathrm{~S}_3+3 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}$

### Q4. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Identify the incorrect statement:

**Options:**

- **(A)** The $S-S-S$ bond angles in the $S_{8}$ and $S_{6}$ rings are the same.
- **(B)** Rhombic and monoclinic sulphur have $\mathrm{S}_{8}$ molecules.
- **(C)** $\mathrm{S}_{2}$ is paramagnetic like oxygen
- **(D)** $\mathrm{S}_{8}$ ring has a crown shape.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) The $S-S-S$ bond angles in the $S_{8}$ and $S_{6}$ rings are the same.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The $\mathrm{S}_{6}$ molecule has a chair-form hexagon ring with the approx same bond length as that in $\mathrm{S}_{8}$, but with some what smaller bond angles i.e. bond lengths are approx same but bond angles are different.
