### Q4. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following is less acidic among the given halogen compounds?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CHF}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CHCl}_{3}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CHBr}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{CHF}_{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Due to stronger-I-effect of $\mathrm{F}$ than that of Cl, $\mathrm{CHF}_{3}$ should be more acidic than $\mathrm{CHCl}_{3} .$ But actually reverse is true.

This is due to : $\mathrm{CCl}_{3}^{-}$ left after the removal of a proton from $\mathrm{CHCl}_{3}$ is stabilised due to presence of $d$ -orbitals in

Cl than: $\mathrm{CF}_{3}^{-}$ left after the removal of a

proton from $\mathrm{CHF}_{3}$ which is not stabilised due to the absence of $d$ -orbitals on $\mathrm{F}$.

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is incorrect with respect to property indicated?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{E} \cdot \mathrm{N}: \mathrm{F}>\mathrm{Cl}>\mathrm{Br}$
- **(B)** $\quad$ E.A: $\mathrm{Cl}>\mathrm{F}>\mathrm{Br}$
- **(C)** Oxidising power : $\mathrm{F}_{2}>\mathrm{Cl}_{2}>\mathrm{Br}_{2}$
- **(D)** Bond energy : $\mathrm{F}_{2}>\mathrm{Cl}_{2}>\mathrm{Br}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Bond energy : $\mathrm{F}_{2}>\mathrm{Cl}_{2}>\mathrm{Br}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{F}_{2}$ is expected to have highest bond energy but the correct decreasing order is $\mathrm{Cl}_{2}>\mathrm{Br}_{2}>\mathrm{F}_{2}$ because of fluorine atom has very small size due to which there is a high inter electronic repulsion between two fluorine atoms so the bond between two fluorine gets weaker and need less energy.

</details>

### Q8. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is used in the preparation of chlorine?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Only $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$
- **(B)** $\quad$ Only $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$
- **(C)** Both $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$
- **(D)** Either $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$, or $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Both $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Both $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$ used for the preparation of chlorine by the action of conc. $\mathrm{HCl}$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mathrm{MnO}_{2}+4 \mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow \mathrm{MnCl}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}+\mathrm{Cl}_{2} \\
2 \mathrm{KMnO}_{4}+16 \mathrm{HCl} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{KCl}+2 \mathrm{MnCl}_{2} \\
+8 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}+5 \mathrm{Cl}_{2}
\end{array}
$$
Chlorine is not obtained by dil. $\mathrm{HCl}$
$$
\mathrm{Ag}_{2} \mathrm{~S}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ag}_{2} \mathrm{~S}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{SO}_{4}
$$

</details>
