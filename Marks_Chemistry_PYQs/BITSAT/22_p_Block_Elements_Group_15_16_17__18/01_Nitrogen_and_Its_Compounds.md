### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Nitrogen is obtained by the thermal decomposition of:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{NO}_{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{NO}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{AgNO}_{3}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Pb}\left(\mathrm{NO}_{3}\right)_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{NO}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{NH}_{4} \mathrm{NO}_{2} \stackrel{\Delta}{\rightarrow} \mathrm{N}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$

</details>
