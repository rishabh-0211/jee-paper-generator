### Q5. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Strong reducing behaviour of $\mathrm{H}_{3} \mathrm{PO}_{2}$ is due to

**Options:**

- **(A)** presence of one $-\mathrm{OH}$ group and two $\mathrm{P}-\mathrm{H}$ bonds
- **(B)** high electron gain enthalpy of phosphorus
- **(C)** high oxidation state of phosphorus
- **(D)** presence of two -OH groups and one $\mathrm{P}-\mathrm{H}$ bond

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) presence of one $-\mathrm{OH}$ group and two $\mathrm{P}-\mathrm{H}$ bonds**

**Detailed Solution:**

The acids which contain $\mathrm{P}-\mathrm{H}$ bond have strong reducing properties. Thus $\mathrm{H}_{3} \mathrm{PO}_{2}$ acid is good reducing agent as it contains two P-H bonds. For example, it reduces $\mathrm{AgNO}_{3}$ to metallic silver.
