### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

IUPAC name of $\left[\mathrm{Pt}\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)_2 \mathrm{Cl}\left(\mathrm{NH}_2 \mathrm{CH}_3\right)\right] \mathrm{Cl}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** (Amino methane) chloro (diammine) platinum (II) chloride.
- **(B)** Chlorodiammine (methanamine) platinum(II) chloride.
- **(C)** Diamminechloro(methanamine) platinum(II) chloride.
- **(D)** Diamminechloro(methylamine) platinum(IV) chloride.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Diamminechloro(methanamine) platinum(II) chloride.**

**Detailed Solution:**

The complex is a cationic complex with two ammine $\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)$, one chloro $(\mathrm{Cl})$ and one methanamine ligand.
Thus, the name will be :Diamminechloro (methanamine) platinum (II) chloride.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The common name of $\mathrm{K}\left[\mathrm{PtCl}_{3}\left(\eta^{2} \cdot \mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4}\right)\right]$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** potassium salt
- **(B)** Zeise's salt
- **(C)** complex salt
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Zeise's salt**

**Detailed Solution:**

Zeise's salt is common name of $\mathrm{K}\left[\mathrm{P} \mathrm{C}_{3}\left(\mathrm{\eta}^{2}=\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4}\right)\right]$

</details>
