### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The type of isomerism present in nitropentammine chromium (III) chloride is

**Options:**

- **(A)** optical
- **(B)** linkage
- **(C)** ionization
- **(D)** polymerisation.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) linkage**

**Detailed Solution:**

The chemical formula of nitropenta ammine chromium(III) chloride is:
$\left[\mathrm{Cr}\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)_5\left(\mathrm{NO}_2\right)\right] \mathrm{Cl}_2$
It can exist in following two structures
$\left[\mathrm{Cr}\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)_5\left(\mathrm{NO}_2\right)\right] \mathrm{Cl}_2$ and
Pentaamminenitrochromium (III) chloride
$\left[\mathrm{Cr}\left(\mathrm{NH}_3\right)_5 \mathrm{ONO}\right] \mathrm{Cl}_2$
Pentaamminenitritochromium (III) chloride
Therefore, the type of isomerism found in this compound is linkage isomerism as $\mathrm{NO}_2$ group is linked through N as $-\mathrm{NO}_2$ or through O as ONO.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{en})_{2}\left(\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}\right)_{2}\right]^{2+}+$ en $\rightarrow$ complex $(\mathrm{X})$. The correct statement about the complex (X) is -

**Options:**

- **(A)** it is a low spin complex
- **(B)** it is diamagnetic
- **(C)** it shows geometrical isomerism
- **(D)** (a) and (b) both

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) (a) and (b) both**

**Detailed Solution:**

Complex $\mathrm{X}$ is $\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{en})_{3} \mathrm{right}\right]^{2+} ;$ as 'en' is a strong field ligand pairing of electrons will take place. $\left[\mathrm{Fe}(\mathrm{en})_{3}\right]^{2+}$ :

$d^{2} s p^{3}$ hybridisation

Hence, hybridisation is $d^{2} s p^{3}$ and complex is diamagnetic. As it has 3 bidentate symmetrical 'en' ligands so it will not show geometrical isomerism.

</details>
