### Q10. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following has square planar geometry?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left[\mathrm{PtCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$
- **(B)** $\left[\mathrm{NiCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$
- **(C)** $\left[\mathrm{ZnCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$
- **(D)** $\left[\mathrm{CoCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left[\mathrm{PtCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left[\mathrm{PtCl}_{4}\right]^{2-}$ has square planar geometry. $\mathrm{Pt}: 5 d^{9} 6 s^{1}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/-gdRq6NOhCl92znyLCzO5N9qVC0JmexUVrElhAzON38.original.fullsize.png">

Two electrons are removed from $5 d$ shell and $6 s$ shell. So, hybridisation takes place is $d s p^{2}$ i.e. square planar geometry.

</details>
