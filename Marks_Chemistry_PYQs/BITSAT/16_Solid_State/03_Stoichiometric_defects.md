### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In a solid lattice, the cation has left a lattice site and is located at an interstitial position, the lattice defect is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Interstitial defect
- **(B)** Valency defect
- **(C)** Frenkel defect
- **(D)** Schottky defect

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Frenkel defect**

**Detailed Solution:**

Frenkel defect is due to dislocation of ion from its usual lattice site to interstitial position.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Frenkel and Schottky defects are :

**Options:**

- **(A)** nucleus defects
- **(B)** non-crystal defects
- **(C)** crystal defects
- **(D)** nuclear defects

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) crystal defects**

**Detailed Solution:**

Frenkel and Schottky defects are crystal defects. It arises due to dislodgement of cation or anion from their places in the crystal lattice.

</details>
