When the sample of copper with zinc impurity is to be purified by electrolysis, the appropriate electrodes are

**Options:**

- **(A)** (a)
- **(B)** (b)
- **(C)** (c)
- **(D)** (d)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) (b)**

**Detailed Solution:**

In electrolytic purification cathode is of pure metal and anode is of impure metal. At anode: $\mathrm{M} \rightarrow \mathrm{M}^{\mathrm{n}+}+\mathrm{n} \overline{\mathrm{e}}$
At cathode: $\mathrm{M}^{\mathrm{n}+}+\mathrm{n} \overline{\mathrm{e}} \rightarrow \mathrm{M}$.
The pure metal is thus deposited at cathode.
