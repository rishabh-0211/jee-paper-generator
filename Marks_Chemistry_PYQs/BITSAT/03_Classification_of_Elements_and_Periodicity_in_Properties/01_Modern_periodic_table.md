### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The element, with atomic number $118,$ will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** alkali
- **(B)** noble gas
- **(C)** lanthanide
- **(D)** transition element

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) noble gas**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electronic configuration of element with atomic number 118 will be $[\mathrm{Rn}] 5 \mathrm{f}^{14} 6 \mathrm{~d}^{10}$ $7 \mathrm{~s}^{2} 7 \mathrm{p}^{6} .$ Since its elctronic configuration in the outer moot or bit (ns $^{2} n p^{6}$ ) resemble with that of inert or noble gases, therefore it will be noble gas element.

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following pairs has both members from the same period of periodic table:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}, \mathrm{Ca}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Na}, \mathrm{Cl}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Ca}, \mathrm{Cl}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Cl}, \mathrm{Br}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Na}, \mathrm{Cl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$_{11} \mathrm{Na} \Rightarrow 2,8,1 ;{ }_{17} \mathrm{Cl} \Rightarrow 2,8,7$
These have same number of shells. Hence, they are the elements of the same period.

</details>
