### Q2. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Semiconductor materials like Si and Ge are usually purified by

**Options:**

- **(A)** distillation
- **(B)** zone refining
- **(C)** liquation
- **(D)** electrolytic refining

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) zone refining**

**Detailed Solution:**

Semiconductor materials like Si and Ge are usually purified by zone refining. Zone refining is based on the principle of fractional crystallisation i.e. difference in

states of metal, so that the zones of impurities are formed and finally removed.

</details>
