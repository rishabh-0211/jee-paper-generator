### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\Delta_{f} \mathrm{G}^{-}$ vs $\mathrm{T}$ plot in the Ellingham diagram slopes downward for the reaction

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Mg}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{MgO}$
- **(B)** $2 \mathrm{Ag}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ag}_{2} \mathrm{O}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{CO}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CO}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{CO}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{C}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \rightarrow \mathrm{CO}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{C}(\mathrm{s})+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}(\mathrm{~g}) \rightarrow \mathrm{CO}(\mathrm{g}) ; \Delta \mathrm{S}$ increases.

Hence, as the temperature increases, $\mathrm{T} \Delta \mathrm{S}$ increases and hence $\Delta \mathrm{G}(\Delta \mathrm{H}-\mathrm{T} \Delta \mathrm{S})$ decreases. In other words, the slope of the curve for formation of CO decreases. However, for all other oxides, it increases.

</details>
