### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The chemical composition of 'slag' formed during the smelting process in the extraction of copper is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{O}+\mathrm{FeS}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{FeSiO}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CuFeS}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Cu}_2 \mathrm{~S}+\mathrm{FeO}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{FeSiO}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

During the extraction of copper, iron is present in the ore as impurity (FeS).
The ore together with a little coke and silica is smelted; FeS present as impurity in the ore is oxidized to iron oxide, which then reacts with silica to form fusible ferrous silicate which is removed as slag.
$2 \mathrm{FeS}+3 \mathrm{O}_2 \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{FeO}+2 \mathrm{SO}_2 \uparrow$

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The reaction that does NOT take place in a blast furnace between $900 \mathrm{~K}$ to $1500 \mathrm{~K}$ temperature range during extraction of iron is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+\mathrm{CO} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{FeO}+\mathrm{CO}_2$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{FeO}+\mathrm{CO} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fe}+\mathrm{CO}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}+\mathrm{CO}_2 \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{CO}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{SiO}_2 \rightarrow \mathrm{CaSiO}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+\mathrm{CO} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{FeO}+\mathrm{CO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

At $900-1500 \mathrm{~K}$ (higher temperature range in the blast furnace)
Reaction which take place are:
$\mathrm{C}+\mathrm{CO}_2 \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{CO}$
$\mathrm{FeO}+\mathrm{CO} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Fe}+\mathrm{CO}_2$
$\mathrm{CaO}+\mathrm{SiO}_2 \longrightarrow \mathrm{CaSiO}_3$ (Slag formation)
$\mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3+\mathrm{CO} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{FeO}+\mathrm{CO}_2$ at $500-800 \mathrm{~K}$

</details>
