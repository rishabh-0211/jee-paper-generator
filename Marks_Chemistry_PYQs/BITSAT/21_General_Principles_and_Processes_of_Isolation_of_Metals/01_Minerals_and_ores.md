### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Calamine, malachite, magnetite and cryolite, respectively, are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{ZnCO}_3, \mathrm{CuCO}_3 \cdot \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{OH})_2, \mathrm{Fe}_3 \mathrm{O}_4, \mathrm{Na}_3 \mathrm{AlF}_6$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{ZnSO}_4, \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{OH})_2, \mathrm{Fe}_3 \mathrm{O}_4, \mathrm{Na}_3 \mathrm{AlF}_6$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{ZnSO}_4, \mathrm{CuCO}_3, \mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3, \mathrm{AlF}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{ZnCO}_3, \mathrm{CuCO}_3, \mathrm{Fe}_2 \mathrm{O}_3, \mathrm{Na}_3 \mathrm{AlF}_6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{ZnCO}_3, \mathrm{CuCO}_3 \cdot \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{OH})_2, \mathrm{Fe}_3 \mathrm{O}_4, \mathrm{Na}_3 \mathrm{AlF}_6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \text { Calamine } \rightarrow \mathrm{ZnCO}_3 \\ & \text { Malachite } \rightarrow \mathrm{CuCO}_3 \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{OH})_2 \\ & \text { Magnetite } \rightarrow \mathrm{Fe}_3 \mathrm{O}_4 \\ & \text { Cryolite } \rightarrow \mathrm{Na}_3 \mathrm{AlF}_6\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In the manufacture of iron from haematite, limestone is added to act as :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Flux
- **(B)** Slag
- **(C)** A reducing agent
- **(D)** An oxidizing agent

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Flux**

**Detailed Solution:**

Limestone $\left(\mathrm{CaCO}_{3}\right)$ is mixed with $\mathrm{Fe}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{3}$ and it acts as flux to form slag $\left(\mathrm{CaSiO}_{3}\right)$.

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Among the following statements the incorrect one is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Calamine and siderite are carbonates.
- **(B)** Argentite and cuprite are oxides.
- **(C)** Zinc blende and iron pyrites are sulphides.
- **(D)** Malachite and azurite are ores of copper.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Argentite and cuprite are oxides.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Cuprite is $\mathrm{Cu}_{2} \mathrm{O}$ and Argentite is $\mathrm{Ag}_{2} \mathrm{~S}$.

</details>
