### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Calculate the activation energy of a reaction, whose rate constant doubles on raising the temperature from 300 K to 600 K .

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3.45 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$
- **(B)** $6.90 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$
- **(C)** $9.68 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$
- **(D)** $19.6 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $3.45 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\log \left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right)=\frac{E_a}{2.303 R}\left[\frac{1}{T_1}-\frac{1}{T_2}\right]$
or, $\log \left(\frac{2 k}{k}\right)=\frac{E_a}{2.303 \times 8.3} \times \frac{[600-300]}{300 \times 600}$
$E_a \approx 3.45 \mathrm{~kJ} / \mathrm{mol}$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The activation energy for a simple chemical reaction $\mathrm{A} \rightarrow \mathrm{B}$ is $E_{a}$ in forward direction. The activation energy for reverse reaction

**Options:**

- **(A)** is always less than $E_{a}$
- **(B)** can be less than or more than $E_{a}$
- **(C)** is always double of $E_{a}$
- **(D)** is negative of $E_{a}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) can be less than or more than $E_{a}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since the nature of reaction (i.e. exothermic or endothermic) is not given, $\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{a}}$ for reverse reaction can be more or less.
