### Q1. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The chemical reaction $2 \mathrm{O}_{3} \longrightarrow 3 \mathrm{O}_{2}$ proceeds as follows:
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/q-k5qeKWP9rRCW7R4z4KEsX-sdRQd33HtNSxGsuFyD4.original.fullsize.png">

rate law expression should be:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]^{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]^{2}\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]^{-1}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]^{2}\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]^{2}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]^{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{r}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]^{2}\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{O}_{3} \stackrel{\text { Fast }}{\longrightarrow} \mathrm{O}_{2}+\mathrm{O}$
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\mathrm{O}+\mathrm{O}_{3} \stackrel{\text { Slow }}{\longrightarrow} 2 \mathrm{O}_{2} \\
\mathrm{k}=\frac{\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right][\mathrm{O}]}{\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]} \\
\text { Rate }=\mathrm{k}^{\prime}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right][\mathrm{O}]
\end{array}
$$
put [O] from (ii)
$$
\mathrm{r}=\frac{\mathrm{k}^{\prime}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right] \mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]}{\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]}=\mathrm{k}\left[\mathrm{O}_{3}\right]^{2}\left[\mathrm{O}_{2}\right]^{-1}
$$
Note: Intermediates are never represented in rate law equation.

</details>
