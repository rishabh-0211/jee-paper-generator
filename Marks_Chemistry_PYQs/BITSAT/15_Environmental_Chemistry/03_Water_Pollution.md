### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Biochemical oxygen demand(BOD) value can be a measure of water pollution caused by the organic matter. Which of the following statements is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Polluted water has BOD value higher than $10 \mathrm{ppm}$
- **(B)** Aerobic bacteria decreases the BOD value
- **(C)** Anaerobic bacteria increases the BOD value
- **(D)** Clean water has $B O D$ value higher than $10 \mathrm{ppm}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Polluted water has BOD value higher than $10 \mathrm{ppm}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Clean water has BOD value less than $5 \mathrm{ppm}$. Polluted water has BOD value higher than $10 \mathrm{ppm}$.

</details>
