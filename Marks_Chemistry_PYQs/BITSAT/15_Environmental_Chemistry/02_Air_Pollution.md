### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The greenhouse effect is because of the

**Options:**

- **(A)** presence of gases, which in general are strong infrared absorbers, in the atmosphere
- **(B)** presence of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$, only in the atmosphere
- **(C)** pressure of $\mathrm{O}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{CH}_{4}$ in the atmosphere
- **(D)** $\mathrm{N}_{2} \mathrm{O}$ and chlorofluorohydrocarbons in the atmosphere

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) presence of gases, which in general are strong infrared absorbers, in the atmosphere**

**Detailed Solution:**

Green house gases such as $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$, ozone, methane, the chlorofluorocarbon compounds and water vapour form a thick cover around the earth which prevents the IR rays emitted by the earth to escape. It gradually leads to increase in temperature of atmosphere.

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

When rain is accompanied by a thunderstorm, the collected rain water will have a $\mathrm{pH}$ value:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Slightly lower than that of rain water without thunderstorm.
- **(B)** Slightly higher than that when the thunderstorm is not there.
- **(C)** Uninfluenced by occurrence of thunderstorm.
- **(D)** Which depends upon the amount of dust in air.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Slightly lower than that of rain water without thunderstorm.**

**Detailed Solution:**

</details>
