### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Dinitrogen is a robust compound, but reacts at high altitude to form oxides. The oxide of nitrogen that can damage plant leaves and retard photosynthesis is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** NO
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NO}_3{ }^{-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NO}_2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NO}_2{ }^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{NO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{N}_2(\mathrm{~g})+\mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{~g}) \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{NO}(\mathrm{g})$
$2 \mathrm{NO}(\mathrm{g})+\mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{~g}) \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{NO}_2(\mathrm{~g})$
Higher concentration of $\mathrm{NO}_2$ damage the leaves and retard the rate of photosynthesis.

</details>

- **(D)** Wet scrubbers are used to wash away all types of particulates.

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) In electrostatic precipitator, the particulates are made to acquire positive charge which are then attracted by the negative electrode and removed.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Particulates acquire negative charge and are attracted by the positive electrode.
