### Q1. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Phosphate pollution is caused by:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sewage and agricultural fertilizers.
- **(B)** Weathering of phosphate rocks only.
- **(C)** $\quad$ Agriculutral fertilizers only.
- **(D)** Phosphate rocks and sewage.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Sewage and agricultural fertilizers.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Phosphate pollution is caused by sewage and agricultural fertilizers.

</details>
