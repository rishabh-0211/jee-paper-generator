### Q1. BITSAT 2018

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A penicillin is a member of a family of drugs that have a

**Options:**

- **(A)** four membered cyclic amide fused to a five membered thiazole ring.
- **(B)** three membered cyclic amide fused to a five-membered thiazole ring.
- **(C)** four-membered cyclic amide fused to have a four membered thiazole ring.
- **(D)** five-membered cyclic amide fused to have a five membered thiazole ring.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) four membered cyclic amide fused to a five membered thiazole ring.**

**Detailed Solution:**

<p>A penicillin is a member of a family of drugs that have a four membered cyclic amide fused to a five membered thiazole ring. It is narrow spectrum antibiotic.</p><p>Penicillin is an antibiotic or group of antibiotics produced naturally by certain blue moulds, now usually prepared synthetically. </p><p><img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/7b96c52e-e034-41d9-8765-0cfb3c98f85c-image.png" style="width: 283px; height: 184px;" /></p>
