### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following chemicals can be added for sweetening of food items at cooking temperature and does not provides calories?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Sucrose
- **(B)** Glucose
- **(C)** Aspartame
- **(D)** Sucralose

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Sucralose**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sucralose is trichloro derivative of sucrose. It is stable at cooking temperture. It does not provide calories.

</details>
