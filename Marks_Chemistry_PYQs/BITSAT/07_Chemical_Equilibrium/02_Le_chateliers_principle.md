### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Le-Chateliers' principle is not applicable to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_2(\mathrm{~g})+\mathrm{I}_2(\mathrm{~g}) \rightleftharpoons 2 \mathrm{HI}(\mathrm{g})$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Fe}(s)+\mathrm{S}(s) \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{FeS}(s)$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{N}_2(\mathrm{~g})+3 \mathrm{H}_2(\mathrm{~g}) \rightleftharpoons 2 \mathrm{NH}_3(\mathrm{~g})$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{N}_2(\mathrm{~g})+\mathrm{O}_2(\mathrm{~g}) \rightleftharpoons 2 \mathrm{NO}(\mathrm{g})$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Fe}(s)+\mathrm{S}(s) \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{FeS}(s)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Le-Chatelier principle is not applicable to pure solids and liquids because they experience negligible change in concentration during chemical equilibrium.

### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following according to LeChatelier's principle is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Increase in temperature favours the endothermic reaction
- **(B)** Increase in temperature favours the exothermic reaction
- **(C)** Increase in pressure shifts the equilibrium in that side in which number of gaseous moles increases
- **(D)** All of the above are true

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Increase in temperature favours the endothermic reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to Le-Chatelier's principle increase in temperature favours the endothermic reaction while decrease in temperature favour the exothermic reaction. Increase in pressure shifts the equilibrium in that side in which number of gaseous moles decreases.

</details>
