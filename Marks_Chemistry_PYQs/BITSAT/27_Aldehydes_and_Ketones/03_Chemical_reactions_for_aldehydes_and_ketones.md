### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following compounds will undergo self aldol condensation in the presence of cold dilute alkali?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_2=\mathrm{CH}-\mathrm{CHO}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH} \equiv \mathrm{C}-\mathrm{CHO}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}_6 \mathrm{H}_5 \mathrm{CHO}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{CHO}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{CHO}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Aldehydes which contain $\alpha$-hydrogen on a saturated carbon, i.e. $\mathrm{CH}_3 \mathrm{CH}_2 \mathrm{CHO}$ will undergo aldol condensation.
Although $\mathrm{CH}_2=\stackrel{\alpha}{\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{H}-\mathrm{CHO}$ also contain a $\alpha$-hydrogen atom but cannot be easily abstracted by a base to form a carbanion and hence does not undergo aldol condensation.

</details>

### Q7. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Nucleophilic addition reaction will be most favoured in

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_{3}\right)_{2} \mathrm{C}=\mathrm{O}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CHO}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CHO}$
- **(D)** <img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/f7G9zN15UtWp76yPv3ADyg08mr5Nei9lHPHhr7FoSz8.original.fullsize.png">

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CHO}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Aldehydes are more reactive than ketones due to $+\mathrm{I}$ effect of $-\mathrm{CH}_{3}$ group. There are two $-\mathrm{CH}_{3}$ group in acetone which reduces +ve charge density on carbon atom of carbonyl group. More hindered carbonyl group becomes less reactive. So in the given case $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CHO}$ is the right choice.

</details>
