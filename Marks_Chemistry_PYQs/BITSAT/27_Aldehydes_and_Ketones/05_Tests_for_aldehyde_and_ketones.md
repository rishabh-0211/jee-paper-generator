### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

What will happen when D-(+)-glucose is treated with methanolic - HCl followed by Tollens' reagent?

**Options:**

- **(A)** A black ppt. will be formed
- **(B)** A red ppt. will be formed
- **(C)** A green colour will appear
- **(D)** No characteristic colour or ppt. will be formed.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) No characteristic colour or ppt. will be formed.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Reaction of $\mathrm{D}-(+)$ -glucose with methanolic $-\mathrm{HCl}$ leads to formation of methyl glucoside $\left(\mathrm{C}_{1}-\mathrm{OH}\right.$ group is methylated) which, being acetal, is not hydrolysable by base, so it will not respond Tollens'reagent.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The reagent (s) which can be used to distinguish acetophenone from benzophenone is (are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2,4 - dinitrophenylhydrazine
- **(B)** aqueous solution of $\mathrm{NaHSO}_{3}$
- **(C)** benedict reagent
- **(D)** $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{NaOH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{NaOH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{I}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{NaOH}$ react with acetophenone $\left(\mathrm{C}_{6} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COCH}_{3}\right)$ to give yellow ppt. of $\mathrm{CHI}_{3}$
but benzophenone $\left(\mathrm{C}_{6} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{COC}_{6} \mathrm{H}_{5}\right)$ does not
give haloform test.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following will not form a yellow precipitate on heating with an alkaline solution of iodine?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}(\mathrm{OH}) \mathrm{CH}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CH}(\mathrm{OH}) \mathrm{CH}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{OH}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{OH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{OH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{OH}$ does not have $-\mathrm{CH}(\mathrm{OH}) \mathrm{CH}_{3}$ group
hence it will not form yellow precipitate with an alkaline solution of iodine (haloform reaction).

</details>
