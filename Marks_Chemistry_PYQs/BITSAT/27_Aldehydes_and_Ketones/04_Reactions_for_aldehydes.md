### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Acetaldehyde and acetone can be distinguished by :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Iodoform test
- **(B)** Nitroprusside test
- **(C)** Fehlings solution test
- **(D)** $C$ \& P test

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Fehlings solution test**

**Detailed Solution:**

Acetaldehyde is easily oxidised to acetic acid by a mild oxidising agent like Fehling solution. Acetone is not easily oxidised. Both acetone and acetaldehyde give iodoform test. Other two conditions are not relevant to aldehydes and ketones.

</details>
