### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following catalyst is used in preparation of high density polythene?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Peroxide catalyst
- **(B)** Ziegler - Natta catalyst
- **(C)** Wilkinson's catalyst
- **(D)** Pd - catalyst

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Ziegler - Natta catalyst**

**Detailed Solution:**

High density polythene is formed when addition polymerisation of ethene takes place in a hydrocarbon solvent in presence of catalyst such as Ziegler-Natta catalyst.
