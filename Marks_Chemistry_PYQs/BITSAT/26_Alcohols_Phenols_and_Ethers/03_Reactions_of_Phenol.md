### Q3. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Consider the following phenols:

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/wjOwd10OlJX_gS42YBvfer87xI3DdTVD2t2chJYufcU.original.fullsize.png">


The decreasing order of acidity of the above phenols is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\quad \mathrm{III}>\mathrm{IV}>\mathrm{II}>\mathrm{I}$
- **(B)** $\quad \mathrm{II}>\mathrm{I}>\mathrm{IV}>\mathrm{III}$
- **(C)** $\quad \mathrm{I}>\mathrm{IV}>\mathrm{II}>\mathrm{III}$
- **(D)** $\quad \mathrm{III}>\mathrm{IV}>\mathrm{I}>\mathrm{II}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\quad \mathrm{III}>\mathrm{IV}>\mathrm{II}>\mathrm{I}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electron withdrawing group $\left(-\mathrm{NO}_{2}\right)$ increases the acidity while electron releasing group $\left(-\mathrm{CH}_{3},-\mathrm{H}\right)$ decreases acidity. Also effect will be more if functional group is present at para position than ortho and meta position.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The following reaction is known as
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/n-Y_LjspTwG3TKHAJstC7iydoaPcjSNmAibfPHjWQKE.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** Friedel-Craft's reaction
- **(B)** Kolbe reaction
- **(C)** Reimer-Tiemann reaction
- **(D)** Witting reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) Kolbe reaction**

**Detailed Solution:**

At $120-140^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ temperature and 1.5 atm pressure, sodium phenoxide reacts with $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ to yield sodium salicylate which on further hydrolysis give to salicylic acid. This reaction is known as Kolbe's reaction.

</details>
