### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Magnesium burns in $\mathrm{CO}_2$ to form

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{MgO}+\mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{MgO}+\mathrm{CO}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{MgCO}_3$
- **(D)** MgO

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{MgO}+\mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Mg}$ burns in $\mathrm{CO}_2$ to give $\mathrm{MgO}$ and $\mathrm{C}$.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Thomas slag is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Ca}_{3}\left(\mathrm{PO}_{4}\right)_{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CaSiO}_{3}$
- **(C)** Mixture of (a) and (b)
- **(D)** $\mathrm{FeSiO}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Mixture of (a) and (b)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Calcium silicophosphate (a mixture of $\left.\mathrm{Ca}_{3}\left(\mathrm{PO}_{4}\right)_{2} \& \mathrm{Ca}_{2} \mathrm{SiO}_{4}\right)$ is called Thomas slag.

</details>
