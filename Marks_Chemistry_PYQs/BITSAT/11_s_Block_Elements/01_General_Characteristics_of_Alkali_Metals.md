\end{array}$

### Q4. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following has minimum melting point?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CsF}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{HCl}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{HF}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{LiF}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{HCl}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ionic compounds have high melting point. Greater the ionic character, more is melting point. $\mathrm{HCl}$ has least ionic character because of maximum electronegativity difference between the two constituent elements, ( $\mathrm{H}$ and $\mathrm{Cl}$ ) among $\mathrm{CsF}, \mathrm{HCl}, \mathrm{HF}$ and $\mathrm{LiF}$

$\therefore \mathrm{HCl}$ has minimum melting point.

</details>

### Q8. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The alkali metals form salt-like hydrides by the direct synthesis at elevated temperature. The thermal stability of these hydrides decreases in which of the following orders?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CsH}>\mathrm{RbH}>\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{LiH}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{LiH}>\mathrm{CsH}>\mathrm{RbH}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{LiH}>\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{RbH}>\mathrm{CsH}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{LiH}>\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{RbH}>\mathrm{CsH}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{LiH}>\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{RbH}>\mathrm{CsH}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The stability of alkali metal hydrides decreases from Li to Cs. It is due to the fact that $\mathrm{M}-\mathrm{H}$ bonds becomes weaker with increase in size of alkali metals as we move down the group from $\mathrm{Li}$ to $\mathrm{Cs}$. Thus the order of stability of hydrides is $\mathrm{LiH}>\mathrm{NaH}>\mathrm{KH}>\mathrm{RbH}>\mathrm{CsH}$

</details>
