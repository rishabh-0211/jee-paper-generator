### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following will precipitate first when aqueous solution containing sulphate ions are added?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Mg}^{2+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Sr}^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Down the group solubility of sulphate decreases. Thus, $\mathrm{Ba}^{2+}$ ions will precipitate out most easily.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\mathrm{BeF}_2,$ is soluble in water whereas fluorides of other alkaline earth metals are insoluble because of

**Options:**

- **(A)** ionic nature of $\mathrm{BeF}_{2}$.
- **(B)** covalent nature of $\mathrm{BeF}_{2}$
- **(C)** greater hydration energy of $\mathrm{Be}^{2+}$ ion as compared to its lattice energy.
- **(D)** none of these.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) greater hydration energy of $\mathrm{Be}^{2+}$ ion as compared to its lattice energy.**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Be}^{2+}$ being small in size is heavily hydrated and heat of hydration exceeds the lattice energy. Hence $\mathrm{BeF}_{2}$ is soluble in water.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following has correct increasing basic strength?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{MgO}<\mathrm{BeO}<\mathrm{CaO}<\mathrm{BaO}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{BeO}<\mathrm{MgO}<\mathrm{CaO}<\mathrm{BaO}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{BaO}<\mathrm{CaO}<\mathrm{MgO}<\mathrm{BeO}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CaO}<\mathrm{BaO}<\mathrm{BeO}<\mathrm{MgO}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{BeO}<\mathrm{MgO}<\mathrm{CaO}<\mathrm{BaO}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The basic character of oxides increases down the group.

</details>
