### Q3. BITSAT 2018

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Which of the following property of alkaline earth metal increases with their atomic number?

**Options:**

- **(A)** electronegativity
- **(B)** solubility of their hydroxides in water
- **(C)** solubility of their sulphate in water
- **(D)** ionisation energy

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) solubility of their hydroxides in water**

**Detailed Solution:**

The lattice energy of the hydroxides of alkaline earth metal decreases more rapidly than their hydration energy leading to more negative value of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>&#916;H</mi><mtext>sol</mtext></msub></math> down the group. More negative is <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><msub><mi>&#916;H</mi><mtext>sol</mtext></msub></math>, more is the solubility of compounds. Hence the solubility of hydroxides of alkaline earth metal increases with their atomic number.

</details>
