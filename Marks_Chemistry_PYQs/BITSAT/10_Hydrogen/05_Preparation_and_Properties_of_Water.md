### Q1. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which one the following removes temporary hardness of water?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Slaked lime
- **(B)** $\quad$ Plaster of Paris
- **(C)** Epsom
- **(D)** Hydrolith

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Slaked lime**

**Detailed Solution:**

This method is known as Clark's process. In this method temporary hardness is removed byadding lime water or milk of lime.

$$

\mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{OH})_{2}+\mathrm{Ca}\left(\mathrm{HCO}_{3}\right)_{2} \longrightarrow \underset{\mathrm{ppt} .}{2 \mathrm{CaCO}_{3}} \downarrow+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}

$$

</details>
