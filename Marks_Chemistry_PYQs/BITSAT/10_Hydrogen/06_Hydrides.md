### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The strongest reducing agent among the following is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{SbH}_3$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{NH}_3$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{BiH}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{PH}_3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{BiH}_3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The stability of hydrides decreases from $\mathrm{NH}_3$ to $\mathrm{BiH}_3$ but their reducing character increases down the group.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Among the following the correct statements are
I. $\mathrm{LiH}, \mathrm{BeH}_2$ and $\mathrm{MgH}_2$ are saline hydrides with significant covalent character
II. Saline hydrides are volatile
III. Electron - precise hydrides are Lewis bases
IV. The formula for chromium hydride is CrH

**Options:**

- **(A)** I, III only
- **(B)** II, IV only
- **(C)** I, IV only
- **(D)** III, IV only

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) I, IV only**

**Detailed Solution:**

Saline hydrides are not volatile as the strong ionic bonds keep the constituent ions together.
Thus, statement II is incorrect.
Electron-precise hydrides like $\mathrm{CH}_4, \mathrm{SiH}_4$, act as pH - neutral species.
Thus, statement 3 is incorrect.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Ionic hydrides reacts with water to give

**Options:**

- **(A)** acidic solutions
- **(B)** hydride ions
- **(C)** basic solutions
- **(D)** electrons

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) basic solutions**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ionic hydrides give the basic solution when it reacts with water, e.g.,
$\mathrm{LiH}+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{LiOH}+\mathrm{H}_2$

</details>
