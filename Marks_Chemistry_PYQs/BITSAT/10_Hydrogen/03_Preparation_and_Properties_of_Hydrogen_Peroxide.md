### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The structure of $\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** planar, linear
- **(B)** non-planar, linear
- **(C)** planar, non-linear
- **(D)** non-planar, non-linear

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) non-planar, non-linear**

**Detailed Solution:**

Hydrogen peroxide $\left(\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2\right)$ is a non-planar molecule with twisted symmetrical structure. It has non-linear structure with an open book structure.
