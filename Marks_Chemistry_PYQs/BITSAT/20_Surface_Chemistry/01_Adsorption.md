### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Methylene blue, from its aqueous solution, is adsorbed on activated charcoal at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$. For this process, which ofthe following statement is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** The adsorption requires activation at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** The adsorption is accompanied by a decrease in enthalpy
- **(C)** The adsorption increases with increase of temperature
- **(D)** The adsorption is irreversible

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) The adsorption is accompanied by a decrease in enthalpy**

**Detailed Solution:**

The adsorption of methylene blue on activated charcoal is an example of physiosorption which is exothermic, multilayer and does not have energy barrier.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following gas molecules have maximum value of enthalpy of physisorption?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{6}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Ne}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The more the liquifiable nature of a gas, the more is the enthalpy of adsorption. Water is more liquifiable.

</details>
