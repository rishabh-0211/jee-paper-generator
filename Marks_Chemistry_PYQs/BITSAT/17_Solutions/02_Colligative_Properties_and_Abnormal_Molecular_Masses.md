### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

58.5 g of NaCl and 180 g of glucose were separately dissolved in 1000 mL of water. Identify the correct statement regarding the elevation of boiling point of the resulting solution.

**Options:**

- **(A)** NaCl solution will show higher elevation of boiling point.
- **(B)** Glucose solution will show higher elevation of boiling point.
- **(C)** Both the solution will show equal elevation of boiling point.
- **(D)** None will show boiling point elevation.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) NaCl solution will show higher elevation of boiling point.**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
\text {  } 58.5 \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{NaCl} & =\frac{58.5}{58.5} \mathrm{~mol} \\
& =1 \mathrm{~mol} \\
180 \mathrm{~g} \text { of glucose } & =\frac{180}{180}=1 \mathrm{~mol}
\end{aligned}$
Thus, we have 1 M NaCl and 1 M glucose solution.
But NaCl dissociates in the solution and glucose does not dissociates.
Thus particle concentration is more in NaCl .
Hence, NaCl solution will have higher elevation in boiling point.

</details>

### Q8. BITSAT 2012

If the elevation in boiling point of a solution of $10 \mathrm{~g}$ of solute $(\mathrm{mol} . \mathrm{wt}=100)$ in $100 \mathrm{~g}$ of water is $\Delta T b$, the ebullioscopic constant of water is

### Q14. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which one of the following statements is false?

**Options:**

- **(A)** The correct order of osmotic pressure for 0.01 M aqueous solution of each compound is $\mathrm{BaCl}_{2}>\mathrm{KCl}>\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{COOH}>$ sucrose.
- **(B)** The osmotic pressure $(\pi)$ of a solution is given by the equation $\pi=\mathrm{MRT}$, where $\mathrm{M}$ is the molarity of the solution.
- **(C)** Raoult's law states that the vapour pressure of a component over a solution is proportional to its mole fraction.
- **(D)** Two sucrose solutions of same molality prepared in different solvents will have the same freezing point depression.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Two sucrose solutions of same molality prepared in different solvents will have the same freezing point depression.**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\Delta \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{f}}=\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{f}} \times \mathrm{m} \times \mathrm{i}$. Since $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{f}}$ has different values
for different solvents, hence even if the $\mathrm{m}$ is the same $\Delta \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{f}}$ will be different.
