### Q1. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The ratio of oxidation states of $\mathrm{Cl}$ in potassium chloride to that in potassium chlorate is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{+1}{5}$
- **(B)** $\frac{-1}{5}$
- **(C)** $\frac{-2}{5}$
- **(D)** $\frac{+3}{5}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{-1}{5}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

\mathrm{KCl} x & =-1 & \mathrm{KClO}_{3}

potassium chloride $+1+x-6=0$

$$

x=+5

$$

potassium chlorate.

$\therefore$ Ratio of oxidation state of $\mathrm{Cl}=\frac{-1}{5}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The oxidation state of sulphur in $\mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{~S}_{4} \mathrm{O}_{6}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6
- **(B)** $\frac{+3}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{+5}{2}$
- **(D)** -2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{+5}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{~S}_{4} \mathrm{O}_{6}$

$2+4 x-12=0$

$4 x-10=0$

$x=\frac{10}{4}=\frac{+5}{2}$

Oxidation state of $\mathrm{S}$ is $=\frac{+5}{2}$

</details>
