### Q1. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following cannot give iodometric titration?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Pb}^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Ag}^{2+}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

There is no reaction between $\mathrm{I}^{-}$ and $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$.

</details>
