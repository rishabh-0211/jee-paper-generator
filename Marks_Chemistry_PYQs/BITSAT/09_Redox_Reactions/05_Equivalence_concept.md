### Q1. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Volume of $3 \%$ solution of sodium carbonate necessary to neutralise a litre of $0.1 \mathrm{~N}$ sulphuric acid

**Options:**

- **(A)** $176.66 \mathrm{ml}$
- **(B)** $156.6 \mathrm{ml}$
- **(C)** $116.0 \mathrm{ml}$
- **(D)** $196.1 \mathrm{ml}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $176.66 \mathrm{ml}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Normality of $3 \% \mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{CO}_{3}$

$\mathrm{N}=\frac{3 \times 1000}{53 \times 100}=0.566 \mathrm{~N}$

For $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{SO}_{4}$ sol. $\mathrm{N}_{1}=0.1, \mathrm{~V}_{1}=100 \mathrm{~mL}$

For $\mathrm{Na}_{2} \mathrm{CO}_{3}$ sol. $\mathrm{N}_{2}=0.566$

Now apply $\mathrm{N}_{1} \mathrm{~V}_{1}=\mathrm{N}_{2} \mathrm{~V}_{2}$

$\mathrm{V}_{2}=\frac{\mathrm{N}_{1} \mathrm{~V}_{1}}{\mathrm{~N}_{2}}=\frac{0.1 \times 1000 \mathrm{~mL}}{0.566}=176.66 \mathrm{~mL}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$0.45 \mathrm{~g}$ of acid molecular weight 90 is neutralised by $20 \mathrm{ml}$ of $0.5 \mathrm{~N}$ caustic potash. The basicity of acid is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Eq. of acid = Eq of base,
$\begin{array}{l}
\therefore \frac{0.45}{\mathrm{E} \cdot \mathrm{wt}}=\frac{20 \times 0.5}{1000}=\mathrm{E} \mathrm{wt}=45 \\
\text { Basicity }=\frac{\mathrm{M} \cdot \mathrm{wt}}{\mathrm{E} \cdot \mathrm{wt}}=\frac{90}{45}=2
\end{array}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

$1.520 \mathrm{~g}$ of hydroxide of a metal on ignition gave $0.995 \mathrm{~g}$ of oxide. The equivalent weight of metal is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1.52
- **(B)** 0.995
- **(C)** 190
- **(D)** 9

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 9**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $E$ be the equivalent weight of the metal
$$
\text { So, } \frac{E+17}{E+8}=\frac{1.52}{0.995}
$$
[17 is equivalent weight of $\mathrm{OH}$ and 8 is equivalent weight of oxygen]
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\Rightarrow 0.995 \mathrm{E}+17 \times 0.995=\mathrm{E} \times 1.52+8 \times 1.52 \\
\Rightarrow 0.525 \mathrm{E}=16.915-12.16=4.755 \\
\therefore \quad \mathrm{E}=\frac{4.755}{0.525}=9
\end{array}
$$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In redox reaction $1 \mathrm{~g}$ -eq of reducing agent requires $\mathrm{Pgm}-\mathrm{eq} .$ of oxidising agent. The value of $\mathrm{P}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** Depends on reaction

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

In redox reaction, $g$ equivalent of reducing agent $=\mathrm{g}$. equivalent of oxidising agent Hence lg equ. of reducing agent $=\mathrm{Pg}$ equ. of oxidising agent.

</details>
