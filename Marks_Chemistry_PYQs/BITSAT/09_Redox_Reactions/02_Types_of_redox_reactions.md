### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following is only a redox reaction but not a disproportionation reaction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 \mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{PO}_3 \rightarrow 3 \mathrm{H}_3 \mathrm{PO}_4+\mathrm{PH}_3$
- **(B)** $2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}_2 \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O}+\mathrm{O}_2$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{P}_4+3 \mathrm{NaOH}+3 \mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \rightarrow 3 \mathrm{NaH}_2 \mathrm{PO}_2+\mathrm{PH}_3$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{P}_4+8 \mathrm{SOCl}_2 \rightarrow 4 \mathrm{PCl}_3+2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2+4 \mathrm{SO}_2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{P}_4+8 \mathrm{SOCl}_2 \rightarrow 4 \mathrm{PCl}_3+2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2+4 \mathrm{SO}_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The reaction $\mathrm{P}_4+8 \mathrm{SOCl}_2 \rightarrow 4 \mathrm{PCl}_3+2 \mathrm{~S}_2 \mathrm{Cl}_2$ $+4 \mathrm{SO}_2$ involves change of oxidation state of P from 0 to +3 and that of $S$ from +4 to +2 .
Thus, it is a redox reaction but not a disproportionation reaction.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Which of the following is not a disproportionation reaction?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{P}_{4}+5 \mathrm{OH}^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{PO}_{2}^{-}+\mathrm{PH}_{3}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Cl}_{2}+\mathrm{OH}^{-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Cl}^{-}+\mathrm{ClO}$
- **(C)** $2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}+\mathrm{O}_{2}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{PbO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{PbO}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{PbO}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{PbO}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Disproportionation involves simultaneous oxidation and reduction of the same atom in a molecule.

</details>
