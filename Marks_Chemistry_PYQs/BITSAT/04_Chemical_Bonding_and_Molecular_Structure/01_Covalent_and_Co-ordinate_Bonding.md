### Q4. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following represents the correct order of $\mathrm{Cl}-\mathrm{O}$ bond lengths in

$\mathrm{ClO}^{-}, \mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}, \mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}, \mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-} \text {? }$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}=\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-}=\mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-}=\mathrm{ClO}^{-}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{ClO}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}^{-}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{ClO}_{4}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{3}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}_{2}^{-} < \mathrm{ClO}^{-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The b.o in $\mathrm{Cl}-\mathrm{O}^{-}$is 1

The b.o in $\mathrm{O}=\mathrm{Cl}-\mathrm{O}^{-}$is $1.5$

The b.o. in $\mathrm{O}=\mathrm{Cl}=\mathrm{O}$ is $\frac{5}{3}=1.66$

The b.o. in $\mathrm{O}=\underset{\mathrm{O}}{\stackrel{\mathrm{O}}{\|}} \mathrm{l}-\mathrm{O}^{-}$is $\frac{7}{4}=1.75$

The bond length increases as b.o. decreases.

</details>
