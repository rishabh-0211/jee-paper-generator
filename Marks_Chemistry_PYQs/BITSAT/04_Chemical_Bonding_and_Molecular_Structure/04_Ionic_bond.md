### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Energy required to completely separate one mole of a solid ionic compound into gaseous. constituent ions is called

**Options:**

- **(A)** Interstitial defect
- **(B)** Valency defect
- **(C)** Frenkel defect
- **(D)** Schottky defect

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Frenkel defect**

**Detailed Solution:**

Frenkel defect is due to dislocation of ion from its usual lattice site to interstitial position.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which of the following pairs will form the most stable ionic bond?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{Na}$ and $\mathrm{Cl}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{Mg}$ and $\mathrm{F}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{Li}$ and $\mathrm{F}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{Na}$ and F

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{Mg}$ and $\mathrm{F}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The stability of the ionic bond depends upon the lattice energy which is expected to be more between $\mathrm{Mg}$ and $\mathrm{F}$ due to +2 charge on Mg atom.

</details>
