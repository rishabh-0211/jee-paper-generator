### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The spin only magnetic moment of $\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}$ ion (in BM) is approximately.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4
- **(B)** 5
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 7

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electronic configuration of Fe .
$\begin{aligned}
{ }_{26} \mathrm{Fe} & =[\mathrm{Ar}] 3 d^6 4 s^2 \\
\mathrm{Fe}^{3+} & =[\mathrm{Ar}] 3 d^5
\end{aligned}$
Number of unpaired electrons, $n=5$
Spin only magnetic moment, $\mu_s=\sqrt{n(n+2)} \mathrm{BM}$
$\begin{gathered}
\mu_s=\sqrt{5(5+2)} \\
\mu_s=\sqrt{35} \\
\mu_s=5.85 \mathrm{BM} \text { or } \mu_s \simeq 6 \mathrm{BM}
\end{gathered}$

(iv) $\mathrm{Sc}^{2+}$
(v) $\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}$

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 3
- **(C)** 2
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 2**

Among the given species, $\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}$ and $\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}$ have same number of unpaired electrons.
$\begin{aligned}
& \text { Unpaired electrons, } n=4 \\
& \text { Magnetic moment, } \begin{aligned}
\mu & =\sqrt{n(n+2)}=\sqrt{4(4+2)} \\
& =\sqrt{24} \mathrm{BM}
\end{aligned}
\end{aligned}$

### Q8. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Bond enthalpies of $\mathrm{H}_{2}, \mathrm{X}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{HX}$ are in the ratio 2: 1: 2 . If enthalpy of formation of $\mathrm{HX}$ is $-50 \mathrm{~kJ}$ $\mathrm{mol}^{-1},$ the bond enthalpy of $\mathrm{X}_{2}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $100 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(B)** $300 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(C)** $200 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$
- **(D)** $400 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $100 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{H}_{2}+\frac{1}{2} \mathrm{X}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{HX}$
Let the bond enthalpy of $\mathrm{X}-\mathrm{X}$ bond be $x$.
$\Delta \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{f}}(\mathrm{HX})=-50$
$=\frac{1}{2} \Delta \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{H}-\mathrm{H}}+\frac{1}{2} \Delta \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{X}-\mathrm{X}}-\Delta \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{H}-\mathrm{X}}$
$=\frac{1}{2} 2 x+\frac{1}{2} x-2 x=\frac{-x}{2}$
$\therefore x=50 \times 2=100 \mathrm{~kJ} \mathrm{~mol}^{-1}$

</details>

### Q9. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The correct bond order in the following species is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}$ ion - Total number of electrons
$(16-1)=15$
E.C.:
$$
\begin{array}{r}
\sigma 1 s^{2} \sigma^{*} 1 s^{2} \sigma 2 s^{2} \sigma^{*} 2 s^{2} \sigma 2 p_{z}^{2} \\
\pi 2 p_{x}^{2}=\pi 2 p_{y}^{2} \pi^{*} 2 p_{x}^{1}=\pi^{*} 2 p_{y} \\
\text { Bond order }=\frac{\mathrm{N}_{\mathrm{b}}-\mathrm{N}_{\mathrm{a}}}{2}=\frac{10-5}{2}=\frac{5}{2}=2 \frac{1}{2}
\end{array}
$$
$\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}$ (Super oxide ion): Total number of
electrons $(16+1)=17$
E.C.:
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\text { \sigma1s }^{2} \sigma^{*} 1 s^{2} \sigma 2 s^{2} \sigma^{*} 2 s^{2} \sigma 2 p_{z}^{2} \\
\pi 2 p_{x}^{2}=\pi 2 p_{y}^{2} \pi^{*} 2 p_{x}^{2}=\pi^{*} 2 p_{y}^{1} \\
\text { Bond order }=\frac{\left(\mathrm{N}_{\mathrm{b}}-\mathrm{N}_{\mathrm{a}}\right)}{2}=\frac{10-7}{2}=\frac{3}{2}=1 \frac{1}{2}
\end{array}
$$
$\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}$ ion: Total number of electrons $=(16-2)=14$
E.C.
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\sigma 1 s^{2} \sigma^{*} 1 s^{2} \sigma 2 s^{2} \sigma^{*} 2 s^{2} \pi 2 p_{x}^{2}=\pi 2 p_{y}^{2} \sigma 2 p_{z}^{2} \\
\text { Bond order }=\frac{\left(N_{b}-N_{a}\right)}{2}=\frac{10-4}{2}=\frac{6}{2}=3
\end{array}
$$
So bond order: $\mathrm{O}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{+}<\mathrm{O}_{2}^{2+}$

</details>
