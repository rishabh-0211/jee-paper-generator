### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

**Options:**

- **(A)** number of lone pairs are same
- **(B)** all have same hybridisation of centre of atom
- **(C)** the bond angles are same
- **(D)** number of bond pairs are same

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) all have same hybridisation of centre of atom**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{CH}_4 ; \mathrm{bp}=4 ; s p^3$-hybridisation
$\mathrm{NF}_3 ; \mathrm{bp}=3 ; l p=1 ; s p^3$-hybridisation

$\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} ; \mathrm{bp}=2 ; l p=2 ; s p^3$-hybridisation
Hence, all have the same slope.

</details>
