### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Intramolecular hydrogen bonding is found in

**Options:**

- **(A)** o-nitrophenol
- **(B)** m-nitrophenol
- **(C)** $p$-nitrophenol
- **(D)** phenol

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) o-nitrophenol**

**Detailed Solution:**

In o-nitrophenol, hydrogen of $\mathrm{O}-\mathrm{H}$ group and oxygen of nitro group form intramolecular hydrogen bonding producing a six membered ring structure while in $m$-nitrophenol, $p$-nitrophenol and phenol, intermolecular hydrogen bonding are present.
