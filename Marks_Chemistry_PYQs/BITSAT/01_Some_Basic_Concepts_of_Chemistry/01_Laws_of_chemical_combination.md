### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

A certain metal sulphide, $M S_2$, is used extensively as a high temperature lubricant. If $\mathrm{MS}_2$ is $40.06 \%$ by mass sulphur, metal $M$ has atomic mass.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $160 \mathrm{u}$
- **(B)** $64 \mathrm{u}$
- **(C)** $40 \mathrm{u}$
- **(D)** $96 \mathrm{u}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $96 \mathrm{u}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$M S_2=M+32 \times 2=M+64$
$\%$ of sulphur $=\left(\frac{64}{M+64}\right) \times 100=40.06$
$M+64=\frac{6400}{40.06}$
$M+64=160$
$M=160-64=96$ u

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The formation of $\mathrm{CO}$ and $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ illustrates the law of

**Options:**

- **(A)** reciprocal proportion
- **(B)** conservation of mass
- **(C)** multiple proportion
- **(D)** constant composition

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) multiple proportion**

**Detailed Solution:**

Formation of $\mathrm{CO}$ and $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ illustrates the law of multiple proportion that is constant mass of $\mathrm{C}$ reacts with different masses of oxygen. These masses here bears simple ratio of 1: 2 .

</details>
