### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In which mode of expression, the concentration of a solution remains independent of temperature?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Molarity
- **(B)** Normality
- **(C)** Fermality
- **(D)** Molality

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Molality**

**Detailed Solution:**

Molality-Moles of solute/mass of solvent in kg
$\because \quad$ Molality does not involve volume term.
$\therefore \quad$ It is independent of temperature.

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The amount of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$ present in 1 litre of $1.5 \mathrm{~N} \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$ solution, is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $25.5 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(B)** $3.0 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(C)** $8.0 \mathrm{~g}$
- **(D)** $2.5 \mathrm{~g}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $25.5 \mathrm{~g}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Molecular weight of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}=34$ Equivalent weight of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}=17$

$\therefore 1 \mathrm{~L}$ of $1 \mathrm{~N} \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2} \mathrm{has}=17 \mathrm{~g}$ of $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$

$\therefore 1$ Lof $1.5 \mathrm{NH}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$ has $=1.5 \times 17=25.5 \mathrm{~g} \mathrm{of} \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}_{2}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An ideal gaseous mixture of ethane $\left(\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{6}\right)$ and ethene $\left(\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4}\right)$ occupies 28 litre at 1 atm and $273 \mathrm{~K}$. The mixture reacts completely with $128 \mathrm{~g}$ $\mathrm{O}_{2}$ to produce $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} .$ Mole fraction at $\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{6}$ in the mixture is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.6
- **(B)** 0.4
- **(C)** 0.5
- **(D)** 0.8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 0.4**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{6}+3.5 \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{CO}_{2}+3 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} ;$

$\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4}+3 \mathrm{O}_{2} \longrightarrow 2 \mathrm{CO}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O}$

Let volume of ethane is $\mathrm{x}$ litre, $22.4 \times 4=3.5 \mathrm{x}+3(28-\mathrm{x})$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{x}=11.2$ litre

at constant $\mathrm{T}$ and $\mathrm{P}, \mathrm{V} \propto \mathrm{n} ;$

$\Rightarrow$ Mole fraction of $\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{6}$ in mixture $=\frac{11.2}{28}=0.4$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Equal weights of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ and $\mathrm{KCl}$ are dissolved separately in equal volumes of solutions. Molarity of the two solutions will be :

**Options:**

- **(A)** Equal
- **(B)** That of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ will be less than that of $\mathrm{KCl}$
- **(C)** That of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ will be more than that of $\mathrm{KCl}$ solution
- **(D)** That of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ will be about half of that of $\mathrm{KCl}$ solution

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) That of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ will be more than that of $\mathrm{KCl}$ solution**

**Detailed Solution:**

When equal weights of different solutes are present in equal volumes of solution the molarity is inversely related to molecular mass of the solute. Mol. mass of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ is less than $\mathrm{KCl}$. Hence, molarity of $\mathrm{NaCl}$ solution will be more.

</details>

### Q7. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

How many grams of concentrated nitric acid solution should be used to prepare $250 \mathrm{~mL}$ of $2.0 \mathrm{MHNO}_{3} ?$ The concentrated acid is $70 \% \mathrm{HNO}_{3}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $90.0 \mathrm{~g}$ conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$
- **(B)** $70.0 \mathrm{~g}$ conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$

- **(D)** $45.0 \mathrm{~g}$ conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $45.0 \mathrm{~g}$ conc. $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\quad$ Molarity $(\mathrm{M})=\frac{\mathrm{wt} \times 1000}{\text { mol. wt. } \times \mathrm{vol}(\mathrm{mL})}$

$2=\frac{\mathrm{wt} .}{63} \times \frac{1000}{250}$

$\mathrm{wt}=\frac{63}{2} \mathrm{~g}$

wt. of $70 \%$ acid $=\frac{100}{70} \times 31.5=45 \mathrm{~g}$

</details>
