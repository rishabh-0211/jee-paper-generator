### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If 0.2 gram of an organic compound containing carbon, hydrogen and oxygen on combustion, yielded 0.147 gram carbon dioxide and 0.12 gram water. What will be the content of oxygen in the substance?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $73.29 \%$
- **(B)** $78.45 \%$
- **(C)** $83.23 \%$
- **(D)** $89.50 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $73.29 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\%$ of $\mathrm{C}=\frac{12}{44} \times \frac{0.147}{0.2} \times 100=20.045 \%$

$\%$ of $\mathrm{H}=\frac{2}{18} \times \frac{0.12}{0.2} \times 100=6.666 \%$

$\%$ of $\mathrm{O}=100-(20.045+6.666)=73.29 \%$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Formation of polyethylene from calcium carbide takes place as follows

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\mathrm{CaC}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{OH})_{2}+\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{2} \\

\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4} \\

\mathrm{nC}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4} \longrightarrow\left(-\mathrm{CH}_{2}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}-\right)_{\mathrm{n}}

\end{array}

$$

The amount of polyethylene obtained from $64.1 \mathrm{~kg}$ of $\mathrm{CaC}_{2}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $7 \mathrm{~kg}$
- **(B)** $14 \mathrm{~kg}$
- **(C)** $21 \mathrm{~kg}$
- **(D)** $28 \mathrm{~kg}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $28 \mathrm{~kg}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The concerned chemical reactions are

(i) $\underset{64 \mathrm{~kg}}{\mathrm{CaC}_{2}+2 \mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ca}(\mathrm{OH})_{2}}+\underset{\text { Ethyne, } 26 \mathrm{~kg}}{\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{2}}$

(ii) $\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{2}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \rightarrow \underset{\text { Ethylene }, 28 \mathrm{~kg}}{\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{4}}$

$$

\text { (iii) } \mathrm{nC}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{2} \rightarrow\left[-\mathrm{CH}_{2}-\mathrm{CH}_{2}-\right]_{\mathrm{n}}

$$

$\begin{array}{ll}\mathrm{n} \times 28 \mathrm{~kg} & \mathrm{n} \times 28 \mathrm{~kg} \text { polythene } \\ \text { or } 28 \mathrm{~kg} & \text { or } 28 \mathrm{~kg}\end{array}$

Thus $64 \mathrm{~kg}$ of $\mathrm{CaC}_{2}$ gives $26 \mathrm{~kg}$ of acetylene which in turn gives $28 \mathrm{~kg}$ of ethylene whose $28 \mathrm{~kg}$ gives $28 \mathrm{~kg}$ of the polymer, polythene.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$3.92 \mathrm{~g}$ of ferrous ammonium sulphate react completely with $50 \mathrm{ml} \frac{\mathrm{N}}{10} \mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$ solution. The percentage purity of the sample is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 50
- **(B)** 78.4
- **(C)** 80
- **(D)** 39.2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 50**

**Detailed Solution:**

Eq of $\mathrm{KMnO}_{4}$ used $=\frac{50 \times 1}{1000 \times 10}=0.005$

$\therefore$ Eq of FAS reacted $=0.005$

$\therefore$ weight of FAS needed

$$

=0.005 \times 392=1.96 \mathrm{~g}

$$

Thus percentage purity of FAS is $50 \%$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The vapour density of ozone is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 16
- **(B)** 32
- **(C)** 24
- **(D)** 48

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 24**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know that, Molecular weight of compound or molecules $=2 \times \mathrm{V}$. $\mathrm{D}$
Vapour density (V. D.) of ozone molecules
$=\frac{\mathrm{M} . \text { wt. of } \mathrm{O}_{3}}{2}=\frac{48}{2}=24$
Hence, $\mathrm{V} . \mathrm{D}$. of $\mathrm{O}_{3}$ is 24

</details>
