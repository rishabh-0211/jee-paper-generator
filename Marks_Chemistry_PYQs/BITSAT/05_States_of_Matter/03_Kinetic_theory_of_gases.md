### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The average kinetic energy of an ideal gas per molecule in SI unit at $25^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6.17 \times 10^{-21} \mathrm{~kJ}$
- **(B)** $6.17 \times 10^{-21} \mathrm{~J}$
- **(C)** $6.17 \times 10^{-20} \mathrm{~J}$
- **(D)** $7.16 \times 10^{-20} \mathrm{~J}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $6.17 \times 10^{-21} \mathrm{~J}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\overline{\mathrm{KE}}=\frac{3}{2} \mathrm{kT}=\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{8.313}{6.023 \times 10^{23}} \times 298=6.17 \times 10^{-21} \mathrm{~J}$

(Average Kinetic energy $\left.\overline{\mathrm{KE}}=\frac{3}{2} \mathrm{k} \mathrm{T}=\frac{3}{2} \frac{\mathrm{R}}{\mathrm{N}} \mathrm{T}\right)$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Increasing order of rms velocities of $\mathrm{H}_{2}, \mathrm{O}_{2}, \mathrm{~N}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{HBr}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_{2}>\mathrm{O}_{2}>\mathrm{N}_{2}>\mathrm{HBr}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{HBr}<\mathrm{O}_{2}<\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{H}_{2}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{H}_{2}>\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{O}_{2}>\mathrm{HBr}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{HBr}>\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{O}_{2}<\mathrm{H}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{HBr}<\mathrm{O}_{2}<\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{H}_{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

RMS velocity of molecules depends on mass. If mol. wt. increases, rms velocity of melocules decreases. $\mathrm{rms} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mathrm{m} \cdot \mathrm{wt}}}$

The order of increasing $\mathrm{m} . \mathrm{wt}$. is

$\mathrm{H}_{2}<\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{O}_{2}<\mathrm{HBr}$

Order of $V_{\mathrm{mx}}$ of molecules. $\mathrm{HBr}<\mathrm{O}_{2}<\mathrm{N}_{2}<\mathrm{H}_{2}$

</details>
