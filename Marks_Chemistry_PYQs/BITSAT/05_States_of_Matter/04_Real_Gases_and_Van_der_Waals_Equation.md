### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In van der Waal's equation of state for a nonideal gas, the term that accounts for intermolecular forces is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(V-b)$
- **(B)** $R T$
- **(C)** $\left(P+\frac{a}{V^2}\right)$
- **(D)** $(R T)^{-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\left(P+\frac{a}{V^2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left(P+\frac{a}{V^2}\right)(V-b)=R T$; Here $\left(P+\frac{a}{V^2}\right)$ represents the intermolecular forces.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The given graph represents the variation of compressibility factor $(Z)=\frac{p V}{n R T}$, for three real gases $A, B$ and $C$. Identify the only incorrect statement.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/oyRA5keONN_o_pg7YjXunRyP45aYxtUbrNo4PgoPv30.original.fullsize.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** For the gas $A, a=0$ and its dependence on $p$ is linear at all pressure.
- **(B)** For the gas $B, b=0$ and its dependence on $p$ is linear at all pressure.
- **(C)** For the gas $C$, which is typical real gas for which neither. $a$ nor $b=0$. By knowing the minima and point of the intersection, with $Z=1, a$ and $b$ can be calculated.
- **(D)** At high pressure the slope is positive for all real gases.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) For the gas $B, b=0$ and its dependence on $p$ is linear at all pressure.**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the graph it is clear that, the value of ' $Z$ ' decreases with increase of pressure. We can explain as follows on the basis of van der Waals' equation.
At high pressure, when ' $p$ ' is large, $V$ will be small and one cannot ignore ' $b$ ' in comparison to $V$. However, the team $a / V^2$ may be considered negligible in comparison to ' $p$ ' in van der Waals' equation.
$\left(p+\frac{a}{V^2}\right)(V-b)=n R T$
$p(V-b)=n R T$
$\Rightarrow \quad p V-p b=n R T$
or    $\frac{p V}{n R T}=1+\frac{p b}{n R T}$
or $Z=1+\frac{p b}{n R T}$
Thus, $Z$ is greater than 1. As pressure is increased (at constant $T$ ), the factor $\frac{p b}{n R T}$ increases. This explains why after minima in the curves, $Z$ increase continuously with pressure. Hence, the only incorrect statement is (b).

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** More heat is used
- **(B)** High pressure cooks the food

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) The boiling point of water increases in the cooker**

In pressure cooker, pressure is high thus, the boiling point of water increases, resulting cooking time is less than other open pots.
