### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Which one of the following reactions is expected to readily give a hydrocarbon product in good yields?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{RCOOK} \frac{\text { Electrolytic }}{\text { oxidation }}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{RCOO}^{-} \mathrm{Ag}^{+} \stackrel{\mathrm{Br}_{2}}{\longrightarrow}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{3} \frac{\mathrm{Cl}_{2}}{\mathrm{hu}}$
- **(D)** $\left(\mathrm{CH}_{3}\right)_{3} \mathrm{CCl} \stackrel{\mathrm{C}_{2} \mathrm{H}_{5} \mathrm{OH}}{\longrightarrow}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{RCOOK} \frac{\text { Electrolytic }}{\text { oxidation }}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Electrolysis of a concentrated aqueous solution of either sodium or potassium salts of saturated carboxylic acids yields higher alkane at anode.

$$

2 \mathrm{RCOOK} \underset{\text { Oxidation }}{\text { Electrolytic }}{\longrightarrow} \underset{\text { Anode }}{2 \mathrm{RCOO}^{-}}+\underset{\text { Cathod }}{2 \mathrm{~K}^{+}}

$$

At anode $2 \mathrm{RCOO}^{-} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{RCOO}+2 \mathrm{e}^{-} \longrightarrow$

$$

\mathrm{R}-\mathrm{R}+2 \mathrm{CO}_{2}

$$

At Cathode $2 \mathrm{~K}^{+}+2 \mathrm{e}^{-} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{~K}$

$$

2 \mathrm{~K}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \mathrm{O} \rightarrow 2 \mathrm{KOH}+\mathrm{H}_{2} \uparrow

$$

(Kolbe's Method)

</details>
