**Options:**

### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The correct nucleophilicity order is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-}<\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}<\mathrm{HO}^{-}<\mathrm{F}^{-}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-} \simeq \mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{HO}^{-} \simeq \mathrm{F}^{-}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-}>\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{HO}^{-}>\mathrm{F}^{-}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{F}^{-}>\mathrm{HO}^{-}>\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-}>\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{HO}^{-}>\mathrm{F}^{-}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Nucleophilicity increases with the decrease in electronegativity of the central atom. Since electronegativity follows the order:
$\mathrm{F}>\mathrm{O}>\mathrm{N}>\mathrm{C} ;$ nucleophilicity of the
concerned group will follow the reverse order i.e.,
$\mathrm{CH}_{3}^{-}>\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{-}>\mathrm{OH}^{-}>\mathrm{F}^{-}$

</details>
