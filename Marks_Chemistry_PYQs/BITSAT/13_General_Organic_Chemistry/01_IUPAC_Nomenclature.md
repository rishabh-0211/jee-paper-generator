### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The IUPAC name of the following molecule is
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/1z-ZP9Emm55Sf2CzXk6tNR9-O7rXDxqVNtQ7zhxd9Ys.original.fullsize.png"/>

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2-Methyl-5 nitro-1-chlorobenzene
- **(B)** 3-Chloro-4 methyl-1-nitrobenzene
- **(C)** 2-Chloro-1 methyl-4-nitorbenzene
- **(D)** 2-Chloro-4 nitro-1-methylbenzene

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 2-Chloro-1 methyl-4-nitorbenzene**

**Detailed Solution:**

$-\mathrm{NO}_2$ and -Cl are written as prefixes with $\mathrm{CH}_3$ being given the highest preference for numbering $(\mathrm{C}-1)$.
Thus, the name of the compound will be $2-$ chloro-1-methyl-4-nitrobenzene.

</details>
