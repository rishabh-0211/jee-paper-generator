### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Choose the correct stability order of the given free radicals.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/bchdufy-bjhh.png">

**Options:**

- **(A)** I > II > III > IV
- **(B)** II > I > IV > III
- **(C)** II = I > IV > III
- **(D)** III > IV > II > I

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) II > I > IV > III**

**Detailed Solution:**

In compounds (I), (III) and (IV) the -M effect and hyperconjugation effect are inoperative at meta positions. So we should consider the inductive effect of the groups. -I effect will decrease the stability of the free radical by withdrawing the electrons toward it itself. The order of -I effect is \(\mathrm{NO}_2>\mathrm{CN}>\mathrm{OH}\). Therefore (III) is most destabilised.
In compound (II) the OH act as electron donating group through +M effect which is operative at para position. This increase the electron density at para position and stabilise the free radical. Hence compound (1) is most stabilised. The order of stability of free radical is
\(\text { II }>\text { I }>\text { IV }>\text { III }\)

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The intermediate formed during the addition of $\mathrm{HCl}$ to propene in the presence of peroxide is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CHCH}_{2} \mathrm{CI}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CHCH}_{3}$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CH}_{2}$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{CH}_{3} \mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{C} \mathrm{H}_{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\mathrm{CH}_{2} \mathrm{CHCH}_{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The addition of HCl to propene proceeds by ionic mechanism and not by free radical mechanism. Hence it forms intermediate carbonium ion.

</details>
