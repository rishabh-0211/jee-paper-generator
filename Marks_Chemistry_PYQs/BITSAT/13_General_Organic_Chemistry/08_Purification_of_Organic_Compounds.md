Which of the following acts as the stationary phase in paper chromatography?

**Options:**

- **(A)** Water
- **(B)** Alumina
- **(C)** Silica gel
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) Water**

**Detailed Solution:**

### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The anthracene is purified by

**Options:**

- **(A)** crystallisation
- **(B)** filtration
- **(C)** distillation
- **(D)** sublimation

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) sublimation**

**Detailed Solution:**

Anthracene is purified by sublimation. In sublimation, a solid is converted directly into gaseous state on heating without passing through liquid phase.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

In paper chromatography:

**Options:**

- **(A)** Mobile phase is liquid and stationary phase is solid.
- **(B)** Mobile phase is solid and stationary phase is liquid.
- **(C)** Both phases are liquids.
- **(D)** Both phases are solids.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) Both phases are liquids.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Paper chromatography is a special case of partition chromatography where the special quality paper containing water trapped in it acts as a stationary phase and solvent as a mobile phase. Thus, both phases are liquids.

</details>
