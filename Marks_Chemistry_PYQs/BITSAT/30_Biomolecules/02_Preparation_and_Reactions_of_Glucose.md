### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

An organic compound with the formula $\mathrm{C}_{6} \mathrm{H}_{12} \mathrm{O}_{6}$ forms a yellow crystalline solid with phenylhydrazine and gives a mixture of sorbitol and mannitol when reduced with sodium. Which among the following could be the compound?

**Options:**

- **(A)** fructose
- **(B)** glucose
- **(C)** mannose
- **(D)** sucrose

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) fructose**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since the compound forms a yellow crystalline solid, i.e. osazone with phenylhydrazine, it may be an aldohexose or a ketohexose. Further, since on reduction, compound forms a mixture of sorbitol and mannitol, it must be a ketohexose, i.e. fructose. Recall that glucose on reduction gives only one alcohol glucitol (Sorbitol)

</details>
