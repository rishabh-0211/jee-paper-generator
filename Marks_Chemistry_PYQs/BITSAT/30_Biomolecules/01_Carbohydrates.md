### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/KBcdhcuUGW5ruwW422lOf7Sw9wfrbkJohUBgBp_FXO0.original.fullsize.png">


Compounds I and II may be grouped as

**Options:**

- **(A)** diastereomers
- **(B)** epimers
- **(C)** $\mathrm{C}-2$ epimers
- **(D)** all of the three.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) all of the three.**

**Detailed Solution:**

When structures I and II are $\mathrm{C}-2$ epimers, it implies that these are epimers and diastereomers too.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Fructose on reduction gives a mixture of two alcohols which are related as

**Options:**

- **(A)** diastereomers
- **(B)** epimers
- **(C)** both (a) and (b)
- **(D)** anomers.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) both (a) and (b)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Ketoses on reduction produce a new chiral carbon leading to the formation of two isomeric alcohols which are diastereomeric as well as $\mathrm{C}-2$ epimers.

</details>
