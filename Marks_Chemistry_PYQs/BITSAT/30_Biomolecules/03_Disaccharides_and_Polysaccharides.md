### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Glycosidic linkage between $C_1$ of $\alpha$-glucose and $C_2$ of $\beta$-fructose is found in

**Options:**

- **(A)** maltose
- **(B)** sucrose
- **(C)** lactose
- **(D)** amylose

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) sucrose**

**Detailed Solution:**

- Maltose is composed of two glucose units.
- Sucrose has glycosidic linkage between $\mathrm{C}_1$ of $\alpha$-glucose and $C_2$ of $\beta$-fructose.
- Lactose is composed of galactose and glucose
- Amylose has glycosidic linkage between $C_1$ of $\alpha$-glucose $C_4$ of $\alpha$-glucose.

</details>
