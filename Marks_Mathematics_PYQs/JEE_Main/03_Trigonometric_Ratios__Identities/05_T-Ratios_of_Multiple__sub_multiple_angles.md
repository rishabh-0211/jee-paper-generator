### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (09 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let $|\cos \theta \cos (60-\theta) \cos (60+\theta)| \leq \frac{1}{8}, \theta \epsilon[0,2 \pi]$. Then, the sum of all $\theta \epsilon[0,2 \pi]$, where $\cos 3 \theta$ attains its maximum value, is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $15 \pi$
- **(B)** $18 \pi$
- **(C)** $6 \pi$
- **(D)** $9 \pi$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $6 \pi$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know that
$(\cos \theta)\left(\cos( 6 0 ^ { \circ } - \theta ) \left(\cos \left(60^{\circ}+\theta\right)=\frac{1}{4} \cos 3 \theta\right.\right.$
So equation reduces to $\left|\frac{1}{4} \cos 3 \theta\right| \leq \frac{1}{8}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow|\cos 3 \theta| \leq \frac{1}{2} \\
& \Rightarrow-\frac{1}{2} \leq \cos 3 \theta \leq \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}$
$\Rightarrow$ maximum value of $\cos 3 \theta=\frac{1}{2}$, here
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow 3 \theta=2 \mathrm{n} \pi \pm \frac{\pi}{3} \\
& \theta=\frac{2 \mathrm{n} \pi}{3} \pm \frac{\pi}{9}
\end{aligned}$
As $\theta \in[0,2 \pi]$ possible values are
$\theta=\left\{\frac{\pi}{9}, \frac{5 \pi}{9}, \frac{7 \pi}{9}, \frac{11 \pi}{9}, \frac{13 \pi}{9}, \frac{17 \pi}{9}\right\}$
Whose sum is
$\frac{\pi}{9}+\frac{5 \pi}{9}+\frac{7 \pi}{9}+\frac{11 \pi}{9}+\frac{13 \pi}{9}+\frac{17 \pi}{9}=\frac{54 \pi}{9}=6 \pi$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2024 (08 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the value of $\frac{3 \cos 36^{\circ}+5 \sin 18^{\circ}}{5 \cos 36^{\circ}-3 \sin 18^{\circ}}$ is $\frac{a \sqrt{5}-b}{c}$, where $a, b, c$ are natural numbers and $\operatorname{gcd}(a, c)=1$, then $a+b+c$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 40
- **(B)** 52
- **(C)** 50
- **(D)** 54

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 52**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{\frac{3(\sqrt{5}+1)}{4}+5\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4}\right)}{5\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{4}\right)-3\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4}\right)}=\frac{8 \sqrt{5}-2}{2 \sqrt{5}+8} \\ & =\frac{4 \sqrt{5}-1}{\sqrt{5}+4} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}-4}{\sqrt{5}-4}\end{aligned}$
$\begin{aligned} & =\frac{20-16 \sqrt{5}-\sqrt{5}+4}{-11} \\ & =\frac{17 \sqrt{5}-24}{11} \Rightarrow a=17, b=27, c=11 \\ & a+b+c=52\end{aligned}$

</details>
