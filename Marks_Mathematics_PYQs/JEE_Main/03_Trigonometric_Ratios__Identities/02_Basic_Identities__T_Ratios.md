### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (29 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\sin x+\sin ^2 x=1, x \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$, then $\left(\cos ^{12} x+\tan ^{12} x\right)+3\left(\cos ^{10} x+\tan ^{10} x+\cos ^8 x+\tan ^8 x\right)+\left(\cos ^6 x+\tan ^6 x\right)$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& \sin x+\sin ^2 x=1 \\
& \Rightarrow \sin x=\cos ^2 x \Rightarrow \tan x=\cos x
\end{aligned}$
$\therefore$ Given expression
$\begin{aligned}
& =2 \cos ^{12} x+6\left[\cos ^{10} x+\cos ^8 x\right]+2 \cos ^6 x \\
& =2\left[\sin ^6 x+3 \sin ^5 x+3 \sin ^4 x+\sin ^3 x\right] \\
& =2 \sin ^3 x\left[(\sin x+1)^3\right] \\
& =2\left[\sin ^2 x+\sin x\right]^3 \\
& =2
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q6. JEE Main 2019 (11 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $f_{k}(x)=\frac{1}{k}\left(\sin ^{k} x+\cos ^{k} x\right)$ for $\mathrm{k}=1,2,3, \ldots$ Then for all $\mathrm{x} \in \mathrm{R},$ the value of $f_{4}(x)-f_{6}(x)$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{12}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{4}$
- **(C)** $\frac{-1}{12}$
- **(D)** $\frac{5}{12}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{12}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$f_{k}(x)=\frac{1}{k}\left(\sin ^{k} x+\cos ^{k} x\right)$

$f_{4}(x)=\frac{1}{4}\left[\sin ^{4} x+\cos ^{4} x\right]$

$=\frac{1}{4}\left[\left(\sin ^{2} x+\cos ^{2} x\right)^{2}-\frac{(\sin 2 x)^{2}}{2}\right]$

$=\frac{1}{4}\left[1-\frac{(\sin 2 x)^{2}}{2}\right]$

$f_{6}(x)=\frac{1}{6}\left[\sin ^{6} x+\cos ^{6} x\right]$

$=\frac{1}{6}\left[\left(\sin ^{2} x+\left(\cos ^{2} x\right)-\frac{3}{4}\left(\sin ^{2} x\right)^{2}\right]\right.$

$=\frac{1}{6}\left[1-\frac{3}{4}(\sin 2 x)^{2}\right]$

Now $f_{4}(x)-f_{(6)}(x)=\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{6}-\frac{(\sin 2 x)^{2}}{8}+\frac{1}{8}(\sin 2 x)^{2}$

$=\frac{1}{12}$

</details>
