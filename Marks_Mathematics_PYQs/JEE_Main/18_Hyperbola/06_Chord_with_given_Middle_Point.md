### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (02 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let O be the origin, and P and Q be two points on the rectangular hyperbola $xy = 12$ such that the mid point of the line segment PQ is $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, -\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$. Then the area of the triangle OPQ equals:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{3}{2}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{5}{2}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{7}{2}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{9}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\dfrac{7}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the chord of the hyperbola $xy = 12$ with midpoint $(x_1, y_1)$ is given by $T = S_1$.

$\dfrac{x y_1 + y x_1}{2} - 12 = x_1 y_1 - 12$

$x y_1 + y x_1 = 2 x_1 y_1$

Substituting the midpoint $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, -\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$:

$x\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) + y\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

$-x + y = -1 \Rightarrow y = x - 1$

To find the coordinates of P and Q, substitute $y = x - 1$ into the equation of the hyperbola $xy = 12$:

$x(x - 1) = 12$

$x^2 - x - 12 = 0$

$(x - 4)(x + 3) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 4, -3$

For $x = 4$, $y = 3$.

For $x = -3$, $y = -4$.

Thus, the coordinates of P and Q are $(4, 3)$ and $(-3, -4)$.

The area of triangle OPQ with vertices $(0,0)$, $(4,3)$, and $(-3,-4)$ is:

$\text{Area} = \dfrac{1}{2} |x_P y_Q - x_Q y_P|$

$\text{Area} = \dfrac{1}{2} |(4)(-4) - (-3)(3)|$

$\text{Area} = \dfrac{1}{2} |-16 + 9| = \dfrac{7}{2}$

Answer: $\dfrac{7}{2}$
