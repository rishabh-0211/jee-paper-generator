### Q7. JEE Main 2019 (11 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Equation of a common tangent to the parabola $y^{2}=4 x$ and the hyperbola $x y=2$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x+y+1=0$
- **(B)** $x-2 y+4=0$
- **(C)** $x+2 y+4=0$
- **(D)** $4 x+2 y+1=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $x+2 y+4=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of a tangent to parabola $y^{2}=4 x$ is:

$y=m x+\frac{1}{m}$

This line is a tangent to $x y=2$ $\therefore \quad x\left(m x+\frac{1}{m}\right)=2 \Rightarrow m x^{2}+\frac{1}{m} x-2=0$

$\because$ Tangent is common for parabola and hyperbola. $\therefore \quad D=\left(\frac{1}{m}\right)^{2}-4 \cdot m \cdot(-2)=0$

$\frac{1}{m^{2}}+8 m=0$

$1+8 m^{3}=0$

$m^{3}=-\frac{1}{8} \Rightarrow m=-\frac{1}{2}$

$\therefore$ Equation of common tangent: $y=-\frac{1}{2} x-2$

$\Rightarrow 2 y=-x-4 \Rightarrow x+2 y+4=0$
