### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $7=5+\frac{1}{7}(5+\alpha)+\frac{1}{7^2}(5+2 \alpha)+\frac{1}{7^3}(5+3 \alpha)+\ldots \infty$, then the value of $\alpha$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{6}{7}$
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{7}$
- **(D)** 1

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 6**

**Detailed Solution:**

$S=a+(a+d) r+(a+2 d) r^2+\ldots$
Then $S=\frac{a}{1-r}+\frac{d r}{(1-r)^2},|r| < 1$
Since, $r=\frac{1}{7}$ and $a=5, d=\alpha$
$\begin{aligned}
& 7=\frac{5}{1-\frac{1}{7}}+\frac{\alpha \cdot \frac{1}{7}}{\left(1-\frac{1}{7}\right)^2} \\
& \Rightarrow \alpha=6
\end{aligned}$

</details>
