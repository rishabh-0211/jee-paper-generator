### Q2. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If non-zero numbers $a, b, c$ are in H.P., then the straight line $\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=0$ always passes through a fixed point. That point is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(-1,2)$
- **(B)** $(-1,-2)$
- **(C)** $(1,-2)$
- **(D)** $\left(1,-\frac{1}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $(1,-2)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

a, b, c are in H.P.
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \frac{2}{b}-\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{c}=0 \\
& \frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}+\frac{1}{c}=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{x}{-1} \quad-\frac{1}{-1} \quad \therefore x=1, y=-2
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If in a triangle $\mathrm{ABC}$, the altitudes from the vertices $\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}, \mathrm{C}$ on opposite sides are in H.P., then $\sin A, \sin B, \sin C$ are in

**Options:**

- **(A)** G.P.
- **(B)** A.P.
- **(C)** Arithmetic - Geometric Progression
- **(D)** H.P.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) A.P.**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\Delta=\frac{1}{2} p_1 a=\frac{1}{2} p_2 b=\frac{1}{2} p_3 b$
$p_1, p_2, p_3$ are in H.P.
$\Rightarrow \frac{2 \Delta}{a}, \frac{2 \Delta}{b}, \frac{2 \Delta}{c}$ are in H.P.
$\Rightarrow \frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{b}, \frac{1}{c}$ are in H.P
$\Rightarrow a, b, c$ are in A.P.
$\Rightarrow \sin A, \sin B, \sin C$ are in A.P.

</details>
