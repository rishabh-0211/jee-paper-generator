### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (23 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A spherical chocolate ball has a layer of ice-cream of uniform thickness around it. When the thickness of the ice-cream layer is 1 cm , the ice-cream melts at the rate of $81 \mathrm{~cm}^3 / \mathrm{min}$ and the thickness of the ice-cream layer decreases at the rate of $\frac{1}{4 \pi} \mathrm{~cm} / \mathrm{min}$. The surface area (in $\mathrm{cm}^2$ ) of the chocolate ball (without the ice-cream layer) is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $196 \pi$
- **(B)** $256 \pi$
- **(C)** $225 \pi$
- **(D)** $128 \pi$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $256 \pi$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/eEyo4cuvUYAjpcUqguTA1yCdC3YuZCyT93mPTeD0Tq8.original.fullsize.png">



$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{v}=\frac{4}{3} \pi \mathrm{r}^3 \\
& \frac{\mathrm{dv}}{\mathrm{dt}}=4 \pi \mathrm{r}^2 \frac{\mathrm{dr}}{\mathrm{dt}} \\
& 81=4 \pi \mathrm{r}^2 \times \frac{1}{4 \pi} \\
& \mathrm{r}^2=81 \\
& \mathrm{r}=9
\end{aligned}$
surface area of chocolate $=4 \pi(r-1)^2=256 \pi$

</details>

### Q11. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\mathrm{x}$ is so small that $\mathrm{x}^3$ and higher powers of $\mathrm{x}$ may be neglected, then $\frac{(1+x)^{3 / 2}-\left(1+\frac{1}{2} x\right)^3}{(1-x)^{1 / 2}}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1-\frac{3}{8} x^2$
- **(B)** $3 x+\frac{3}{8} x^2$
- **(C)** $-\frac{3}{8} x^2$
- **(D)** $\frac{x}{2}-\frac{3}{8} x^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $-\frac{3}{8} x^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& (1-x)^{1 / 2}\left[1+\frac{3}{2} x+\frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{3}{2}-1\right) x^2-1-3\left(\frac{1}{2} x\right)-3(2)\left(\frac{1}{2} x\right)^2\right] \\
& =(1-x)^{1 / 2}\left[-\frac{3}{8} x^2\right]=-\frac{3}{8} x^2
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
