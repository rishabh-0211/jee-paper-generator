### Q21. JEE Main 2002

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two common tangents to the circle $x^2+y^2=2 a^2$ and parabola $y^2=8 a x$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x=\pm(y+2 a)$
- **(B)** $y=\pm(x+2 a)$
- **(C)** $x=\pm(y+a)$
- **(D)** $y=\pm(x+a)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $y=\pm(x+2 a)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Any tangent to the parabola $y^2=8 a x$ is
$$
y=m x+\frac{2 a}{m} 
$$
If (i) is a tangent to the circle, $x^2+y^2=2 a^2$ then, $\quad \sqrt{2} a=\pm \frac{2 a}{m \sqrt{m^2+1}}$
$$
\Rightarrow m^2\left(1+m^2\right)=2 \Rightarrow\left(m^2+2\right)\left(m^2-1\right)=0 ; \Rightarrow m=\pm 1
$$
So, $y=\pm(x+2 a)$
