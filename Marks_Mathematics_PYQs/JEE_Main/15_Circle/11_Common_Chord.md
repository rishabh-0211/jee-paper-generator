### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (09 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

Let the centre of a circle, passing through the points $(0,0),(1,0)$ and touching the circle $x^2+y^2=9$, be $(h, k)$. Then for all possible values of the coordinates of the centre $(h, k), 4\left(h^2+k^2\right)$ is equal to_________

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **9**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/X6ZDAbgntZzldqdmtHoabAG8UD8Gk_ry9VnblQ3-Dac.original.fullsize.png">
$\begin{aligned} & (\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{h})^2+(\mathrm{y}-\mathrm{k})^2=\mathrm{h}^2+\mathrm{k}^2 \\ & \mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{y}^2-2 \mathrm{hx}-2 \mathrm{ky}=0 \\ & \because \text { passes through }(1,0) \\ & \Rightarrow 1+0-2 \mathrm{~h}=0 \\ & \Rightarrow \mathrm{h}=1 / 2 \\ & \because \mathrm{OC}=\frac{\mathrm{OP}}{2} \\ & \sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2+\mathrm{k}^2}=\frac{3}{2}\end{aligned}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{1}{4}+\mathrm{k}^2=\frac{9}{4} \\
& \mathrm{k}^2=2 \\
& \mathrm{k}= \pm \sqrt{2}
\end{aligned}$
$\therefore$ Possible coordinate of
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{c}(\mathrm{h}, \mathrm{k})\left(\frac{1}{2}, \sqrt{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{2},-\sqrt{2}\right) \\
& 4\left(\mathrm{~h}^2+\mathrm{k}^2\right)=4\left(\frac{1}{4}+2\right)=4\left(\frac{9}{4}\right)=9
\end{aligned}$

### Q3. JEE Main 2024 (05 Apr Shift 2)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2

**Correct Option:** **(A) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**
