### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (22 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let the set of all values of $r$, for which the circles $(x+1)^{2}+(y+4)^{2}=r^{2}$ and $x^{2}+y^{2}-4 x-2 y-4=0$ intersect at two distinct points be the interval $(\alpha, \beta)$. Then $\alpha \beta$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 21
- **(B)** 24
- **(C)** 20
- **(D)** 25

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 25**

**Detailed Solution:**

Circle 1: center $C_1 = (-1, -4)$, radius $r$.
Circle 2: $(x-2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 9$, center $C_2 = (2, 1)$, radius $3$.
Distance $d = \sqrt{9+25} = \sqrt{34}$.
For two intersection points: $|r-3| < \sqrt{34} < r+3$.
$r + 3 > \sqrt{34} \Rightarrow r > \sqrt{34}-3$ and $|r-3| < \sqrt{34} \Rightarrow r < \sqrt{34}+3$.
$r \in (\sqrt{34}-3,\ \sqrt{34}+3)$, so $\alpha = \sqrt{34}-3$, $\beta = \sqrt{34}+3$.
$\alpha\beta = (\sqrt{34})^2 - 9 = 34 - 9 = 25$.

</details>

### Q6. JEE Main 2019 (11 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Two circles with equal radii are intersecting at the points (0,1) and (0,-1) . The tangent at the point (0,1) to one of the circles passes through the centre of the other circle. Then the distance between the centres of these circles is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** $2 \sqrt{2}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\because$ Two circles of equal radii intersect each other orthogonally. Then $R$ is mid point of $P Q$.

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/It98ThH9yhsWCG9y11iji5ubWkYTbSxAuT0axtFY03s.original.fullsize.png">


and $P R=O_{1} R=O_{2} R$

$P R=\frac{1}{2} \sqrt{(0-0)^{2}+(1+1)^{2}}=1$

$\therefore$ Distance between centres $=1+1=2$.

### Q8. JEE Main 2004

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If a circle passes through the point $(a, b)$ and cuts the circle $x^2+y^2=4$ orthogonally, then the locus of its centre is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 a x+2 b y+\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$
- **(B)** $2 a x+2 b y-\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$
- **(C)** $2 a x-2 b y+\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$
- **(D)** $2 a x-2 b y-\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2 a x+2 b y-\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the circle be $x^2+y^2+2 g x+2 f y+c=0 \Rightarrow c=4$ and it passes through $(a, b)$ $\Rightarrow \mathrm{a}^2+\mathrm{b}^2+2 \mathrm{ga}+2 \mathrm{fb}+4=0$
Hence locus of the centre is $2 a x+2 b y-\left(a^2+b^2+4\right)=0$

</details>

### Q9. JEE Main 2003

If the two circles $(x-1)^2+(y-3)^2=r^2$ and $x^2+y^2-8 x+2 y+8=0$ intersect in two distinct point, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $r>2$
- **(B)** $2 < \mathrm{r} < 8$
- **(C)** $r < 2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{r}=2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $2 < \mathrm{r} < 8$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left|r_1-r_2\right|=C_1 C_2$ for intersection

and $\mathrm{r}_1+\mathrm{r}_2>\mathrm{C}_1 \mathrm{C}_2, \mathrm{r}+3>5 \Rightarrow \mathrm{r}=2$<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/VrlxA7Koxe6479MEM7PEccGdyQNYP4ZnydEl0lX7lag.original.fullsize.png">
