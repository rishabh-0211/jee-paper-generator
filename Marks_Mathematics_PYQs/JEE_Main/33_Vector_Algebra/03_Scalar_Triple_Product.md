### Q15. JEE Main 2013 (09 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The vector $(\hat{i} \times \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}) \hat{i}+(\hat{j} \times \vec{a} \vec{b}) \hat{j}+(\hat{k} \times \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}) \hat{k}$ is equal to:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\vec{b} \times \vec{a}$
- **(B)** $\vec{a}$
- **(C)** $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$
- **(D)** $\vec{b}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$(\hat{i} \times \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}) \hat{i}+(\hat{j} \times \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}) \hat{j}+(\hat{k} \times \vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}) \hat{k}$
$=(\hat{i} \cdot \vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{i}+(\hat{j} \cdot \vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{j}+(\hat{k} \cdot \vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{k}$
$(\because \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \cdot \vec{c}=\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} \times \vec{c})$
$=(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{i}+(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{j}+(\vec{a} \times \vec{b}) \hat{k}$
$==\vec{a} \times \vec{b}$

</details>

### Q16. JEE Main 2012 (12 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\vec{u}=\hat{j}+4 \hat{k}, \vec{v}=\hat{i}+3 \hat{k}$ and $\vec{w}=\cos \theta \hat{i}+\sin \theta \hat{j}$ are vectors in 3-dimensional space, then the maximum possible value of $|\vec{u} \times \vec{v} \cdot \vec{w}|$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(B)** 5
- **(C)** $\sqrt{14}$
- **(D)** 7

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 5**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\vec{u}=\hat{j}+4 \hat{k}, \vec{v}=\hat{i}-3 \hat{k}$ and
$$
\vec{w}=\cos \theta \hat{i}+\sin \theta \hat{j}
$$
Now, $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 0 & 1 & 4 \\ 1 & 0 & -3\end{array}\right|$
$$
=\hat{i}(-3+\hat{j}-4(+\hat{k})-1 \quad(\quad)
$$
$$
=-3 \hat{i}+4 \hat{j}-\hat{k}
$$
Now,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& (\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) \cdot \vec{w}=(-3 \hat{i}+4 \hat{j}-\hat{k}) \cdot(\cos \theta \hat{i}+\sin \theta \hat{j}) \\
& =-3 \cos \theta+4 \sin \theta
\end{aligned}
$$
Now, maximum possible value of
$$
|-3 \cos \theta+4 \sin \theta|=\sqrt{(-3)^2+(4)^2}=\sqrt{25}=5
$$

</details>

### Q17. JEE Main 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\vec{u}, \vec{v}, \bar{w}$ are non-coplanar vectors and $p, q$ are real numbers, then the equality $\left[\begin{array}{llll}3 \vec{u} & p \vec{v} & p \vec{w}\end{array}\right]-\left[\begin{array}{lll}p \vec{v} & \vec{w} & q \vec{u}\end{array}\right]-\left[\begin{array}{lll}2 \vec{w} & q \vec{v} & q \vec{u}\end{array}\right]=0$ holds for

**Options:**

- **(A)** exactly one value of $(p, q)$
- **(B)** exactly two values of $(p, q)$
- **(C)** more than two but not all values of $(p, q)$
- **(D)** all values of $(p, q)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) exactly one value of $(p, q)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(3 p^2-p q+2 q^2\right)\left[\begin{array}{lll}
\vec{u} & \vec{v} & \bar{w}
\end{array}\right]=0 \\
& \text { But }\left[\begin{array}{lll}
\vec{u} & \vec{v} & \bar{w}
\end{array}\right] \neq 0 \\
& 3 p^2-p q+2 q^2=0 \\
& 2 p^2+p^2-p q+\left(\frac{q}{2}\right)^2+\frac{7 q^2}{4}=0 \Rightarrow 2 p^2+\left(p-\frac{q}{2}\right)^2+\frac{7}{4} q^2=0
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow p=0, q=0, p=\frac{q}{2}
$$
This possible only when $p=0, q=0$ exactly one value of $(p, q)$

</details>

### Q18. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $\vec{a}=\hat{i}-\hat{k}, \vec{b}=x \hat{i}+\hat{j}+(1-x) \hat{k}$ and $\vec{c}=y \hat{i}+x \hat{j}+(1+x-y) \hat{k}$. Then $[\vec{a}, \vec{b}, \vec{c}]$ depends on

**Options:**

- **(A)** only $y$
- **(B)** only $\mathrm{x}$
- **(C)** both $x$ and $y$
- **(D)** neither $x$ nor $y$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) neither $x$ nor $y$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\vec{a}=\hat{i}-\hat{k}, \vec{b}=x \hat{i}+\hat{j}+(1-x) \hat{k}$ and $\vec{c}=y \hat{i}+x \hat{j}+(1+x-y) \hat{k}$
$[\vec{a} \vec{b} \vec{c}]=\vec{a} \cdot(\vec{b} \times \vec{c})$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \vec{b} \times \vec{c}=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\
x & 1 & 1-x \\
y & x & 1+x-y
\end{array}\right|=\hat{i}\left(1+x-x-x^2\right)-\hat{j}\left(x+x^2-x y-y+x y\right)+\hat{k}\left(x^2-y\right) \\
& \vec{a} .(\vec{b} \times \vec{c})=1
\end{aligned}
$$
which does not depend on $\mathrm{x}$ and $\mathrm{y}$

</details>

### Q19. JEE Main 2003

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A tetrahedron has vertices at $\mathrm{O}(0,0,0), \mathrm{A}(1,2,1) \mathrm{B}(2,1,3)$ and $\mathrm{C}(-1,1,2)$. Then the angle between the faces $\mathrm{OAB}$ and $\mathrm{ABC}$ will be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $90^{\circ}$
- **(B)** $cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{19}{35}\right)$
- **(C)** $\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{17}{31}\right)$
- **(D)** $30^{\circ}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{19}{35}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Vector perpendicular to the face $\mathrm{OAB}$
$=\overrightarrow{\mathrm{OA}} \times \overrightarrow{\mathrm{OB}}=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}\mathrm{i} & \mathrm{j} & \mathrm{k} \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & 3\end{array}\right|=5 \mathrm{i}-\mathrm{j}-3 \mathrm{k}$
Vector perpendicular to the face $\mathrm{ABC}$
$=\overrightarrow{\mathrm{AB}} \times \overrightarrow{\mathrm{AC}}=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}\mathrm{i} & \mathrm{j} & \mathrm{k} \\ 1 & -1 & 2 \\ -2 & -1 & 1\end{array}\right|=\mathrm{i}-5 \mathrm{j}-3 \mathrm{k}$
Angle between the faces $=$ Angle between their normals
$\cos \theta=\left|\frac{5+5+9}{\sqrt{35} \sqrt{35}}\right|=\frac{19}{35}$ or $\theta=\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{19}{35}\right)$

</details>
