### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (29 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\alpha x+\beta y=109$ is the equation of the chord of the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1$, whose mid point is $\left(\frac{5}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)$, then $\alpha+\beta$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 58
- **(B)** 46
- **(C)** 37
- **(D)** 72

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 58**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/nGmTmZywgw-6gPr6nZXNJQp7btkkJa_WBQP-uhL4sRs.original.fullsize.png">





Equation of chord T $=\mathrm{S}_1$
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{5}{2}\left(\frac{x}{9}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\mathrm{y}}{4}\right)=\frac{25}{36}+\frac{1}{16} \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{5 \mathrm{x}}{18}+\frac{\mathrm{y}}{8}=\frac{100+9}{144}=\frac{109}{144} \\
& \Rightarrow 40 \mathrm{x}+18 \mathrm{y}=109 \\
& \Rightarrow \alpha=40, \beta=18 \\
& \Rightarrow \alpha+\beta=58
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (28 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the midpoint of a chord of the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1$ is $(\sqrt{2}, 4 / 3)$, and the length of the chord is $\frac{2 \sqrt{\alpha}}{3}$, then $\alpha$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $20$
- **(B)** $22$
- **(C)** $18$
- **(D)** $26$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $22$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/k_l-GFDwK4D88eWZDtopwVsF7fWTPygZXGzNh1FARMM.original.fullsize.png">

$\begin{aligned}
& E: \frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1 \\
& T=S_1 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{\sqrt{2} x}{9}+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{4}{3} y\right)-1=\frac{2}{9}+\frac{16}{9(4)}-1 \\
& \frac{\sqrt{2} x}{9}+\frac{y}{3}=\frac{2}{9}+\frac{4}{9} \\
& \frac{\sqrt{2} x}{9}+\frac{y}{3}=\frac{2}{3} \quad \Rightarrow \sqrt{2 x}+3 y=6
\end{aligned}$
Now point of intersection of chord and ellipse is
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{(6-3 y)^2}{18}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1 \\
& \frac{(2-y)^2}{2}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1 \\
& 2\left(4+y^2-4 y\right)+y^2=4 \\
& \Rightarrow 3 y^2-8 y+4=0 \\
& \Rightarrow y=2, \frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}$

So, points are $(0,2)$ are $\left(2 \sqrt{2}, \frac{2}{3}\right)$
Length of chord $=\sqrt{(2 \sqrt{2})^2+\left(\frac{2}{3}-2\right)^2}$
$\begin{aligned}
& =\sqrt{8+\frac{16}{9}} \\
& =\frac{\sqrt{88}}{3}=\frac{2 \sqrt{22}}{3}
\end{aligned}$

On comparing $\alpha=22$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the chord, of the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{16}=1$, whose mid-point is $(3,1)$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $48 x+25 y=169$
- **(B)** $5 x+16 y=31$
- **(C)** $25 x+101 y=176$
- **(D)** $4 x+122 y=134$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $48 x+25 y=169$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of chord with given middle point
$\begin{aligned}
& T=S_1 \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{3 x}{25}+\frac{\mathrm{y}}{16}-1=\frac{9}{25}+\frac{1}{16}-1 \\
& 48 \mathrm{x}+25 \mathrm{y}=144+25 \\
& 48 \mathrm{x}+25 \mathrm{y}=169 \text { Ans. }
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2025 (23 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The length of the chord of the ellipse $\frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{y^2}{2}=1$, whose mid-point is $\left(1, \frac{1}{2}\right)$, is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{5}{3} \sqrt{15}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{3} \sqrt{15}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2}{3} \sqrt{15}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{15}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{2}{3} \sqrt{15}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& T=S_1 \\
& \frac{x \cdot 1}{4}+\frac{y}{4}=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{8} \\
& \Rightarrow 2 x+2 y=3 \\
& \frac{x^2}{4}+\frac{\left(\frac{3-2 x}{2}\right)^2}{2}=1 \\
& \Rightarrow x=\frac{12 \pm \sqrt{120}}{12} \Rightarrow y=\frac{1}{2} \mp \frac{\sqrt{120}}{12}
\end{aligned}$

So length of chord
$=\frac{2 \sqrt{15}}{3}$

</details>
