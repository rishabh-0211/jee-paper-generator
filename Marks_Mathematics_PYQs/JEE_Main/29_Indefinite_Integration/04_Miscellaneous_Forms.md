<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

$$
-\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{16-(x+3)^2}} d x
$$

$$
=-2 \sqrt{7-6 x-x^2}-\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{x+3}{4}\right)+C

### Q5. JEE Main 2018 (15 Apr Shift 1 Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $f\left(\frac{x-4}{x+2}\right)=2 x+1,(x \in R=\{1,-2\})$, then $\int f$ ( $x$ ) $d x$ is equal to (where $C$ is a constant of integration)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $12 \log _e|1-x|-3 x+c$
- **(B)** $-12 \log _e|1-x|-3 x+c$
- **(C)** $-12 \log _e|1-x|+3 x+c$
- **(D)** $12 \log _e|1-x|+3 x+c$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $-12 \log _e|1-x|-3 x+c$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Suppose, $\frac{x-4}{x+2}=y \Rightarrow x-4=y(x+2)$
$$
\Rightarrow x(1-y)=2 y+4 \Rightarrow x=\frac{2 y+4}{1-y}
$$
So, $f(y)=2\left(\frac{2 y+4}{1-y}\right)+1$
Now, $f(x)=2\left(\frac{2 x+4}{1-x}\right)+1=\frac{3 x+9}{1-x}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
&=\frac{3(x+3)}{1-x}=\frac{3(x-1+4)}{1-x}=-3+\frac{12}{1-x} \\
&\therefore \int f(x) d x=-12 \log _e|1-x|-3 x+c
\end{aligned}
$$

### Q11. JEE Main 2013 (22 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\int \frac{x^2-x+1}{x^2+1} e^{\cot ^{-1} x} d x=A(x) e^{\cot ^{-1} x}+C$, then $A(x)$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-x$
- **(B)** $x$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{1-x}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{1+x}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $x$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\mathrm{I}=\int \frac{x^2-x+1}{x^2+1} \cdot e^{\cot ^{-1} x} d x$
Put $x=\cot t \Rightarrow-\operatorname{cosec}^2 t d t=d x$
Now, $1+\cot ^2 t=\operatorname{cosec}^2 t$
$\therefore$
$\mathrm{I}=\int \frac{e^t\left(\cot ^2 t-\cot t+1\right)}{\left(1+\cot ^2 t\right)}\left(-\operatorname{cosec}^2 t\right) d t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& =-\int e^t\left(\operatorname{cosec}{ }^2 t-\cot t\right) d t \\
& =\int e^t\left(\cot t-\operatorname{cosec}^2 t\right) d t \\
& =e^t \cot t+\mathrm{C} \\
& =e^{\cot ^{-1} x}(x)+\mathrm{C} \equiv \mathrm{A}(x) \cdot e^{\cot ^{-1} x}+\mathrm{C} \\
\Rightarrow \mathrm{A}(x) & =x
\end{aligned}
$$
