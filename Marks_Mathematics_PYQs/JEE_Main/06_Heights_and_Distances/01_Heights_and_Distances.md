$$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/svbdBQle0gWepePzNmIBW6KL31B0ksE4P033i896-uM.original.fullsize.png">

- **(B)** $\frac{\sin \alpha \sin \beta}{\cos (\beta-\alpha)}$

- **(D)** $\frac{\cos (\beta-\alpha)}{\sin \alpha \sin \beta}$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{2 \sin \alpha \sin \beta}{\sin (\beta-\alpha)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\text { Let } \mathrm{AB} \text { be the tower of height ' } \mathrm{h} \text { '. }

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/u7lfRTtIcMOnyutjTiwA7o_qnP_8c_wMwEmMnfbzM9I.original.fullsize.png">

or $\frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha}=\frac{h}{x+2}$
$\Rightarrow(x+2) \sin \alpha=\mathrm{h} \cos \alpha$
$\Rightarrow h=\frac{x \sin \alpha+2 \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha}$
Now, In $\Delta \mathrm{ABC}, \tan \beta=\frac{\mathrm{AB}}{\mathrm{BC}}$
$$
\Rightarrow \frac{\sin \beta}{\cos \beta}=\frac{h}{x} \Rightarrow x=\frac{h \cos \beta}{\sin \beta}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&h=\frac{\frac{h \cos \beta \sin \alpha}{\sin \beta}+\frac{2 \sin \alpha}{1}}{\cos \alpha} \\
&\Rightarrow h=\frac{h \cos \beta \cdot \sin \alpha+2 \sin \alpha \sin \beta}{\sin \beta \cdot \cos \alpha} \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
$\Rightarrow h(\sin \beta \cdot \cos \alpha-\cos \beta \cdot \sin \alpha)=2 \sin \alpha \cdot \sin \beta$
$\Rightarrow h[\sin (\beta-\alpha)]=2 \sin \alpha \cdot \sin \beta$

**Correct Option:** **(A) 16**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/3asF4DrczZXto64437itNbpvqeq3NWS2tUxdhTF2d1A.original.fullsize.png">

We put one pole at origin. $B C=80 \mathrm{~m}, O A=20 \mathrm{~m}$ Line $O C$ and $A B$ intersect at $M$. To find: Length of $M N$.
Eqn of $O C: y=\left(\frac{80-0}{a-0}\right) x$ $\Rightarrow y=\frac{80}{a} x$
Eqn of $A B: y=\left(\frac{20-0}{0-a}\right)(x-a)$ $\Rightarrow y=\frac{-20}{a}(x-a)$
At $M:(1)=(2)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad \frac{80}{a} x=\frac{-20}{a}(x-a) \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \frac{80}{a} x=\frac{-20}{a} x+20 \Rightarrow x=\frac{a}{5} \\
& \therefore \quad y=\frac{80}{a} \times \frac{a}{5}=16
\end{aligned}
$$
