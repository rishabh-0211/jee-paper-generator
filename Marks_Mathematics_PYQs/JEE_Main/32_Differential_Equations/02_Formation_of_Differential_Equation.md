### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (05 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The differential equation of the family of circles passing through the origin and having centre at the line $y=x$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(x^2-y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} x=\left(x^2-y^2-2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} y$
- **(B)** $\left(x^2+y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} x=\left(x^2+y^2-2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} y$
- **(C)** $\left(x^2+y^2-2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} x=\left(x^2+y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} y$
- **(D)** $\left(x^2-y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} x=\left(x^2-y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} y$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left(x^2-y^2+2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} x=\left(x^2-y^2-2 x y\right) \mathrm{d} y$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& C \equiv x^2+y^2+g x+g y=0....(1)\\
& 2 x+2 y y^{\prime}+g+g y^{\prime}=0 \\
& g=-\left(\frac{2 x+2 y y^{\prime}}{1+y^{\prime}}\right)
\end{aligned}$
Put in (1)
$\begin{aligned}
& x^2+y^2-\left(\frac{2 x+2 y y^{\prime}}{1+y^{\prime}}\right)(x+y)=0 \\
& \left(x^2-y^2-2 x y\right) y^{\prime}=x^2-y^2+2 x y
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q9. JEE Main 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The differential equation which represents the family of curves $y=c_1 e^{c_2 x}$, where $c_1$ and $c_2$ are arbitrary constants is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $y^{\prime}=y^2$
- **(B)** $y^{\prime \prime}=y^{\prime} y$
- **(C)** $y y^{\prime \prime}=y^{\prime}$
- **(D)** $y y^{\prime \prime}=\left(y^{\prime}\right)^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $y y^{\prime \prime}=\left(y^{\prime}\right)^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& y=c_1 e^{c_2 x} \\
& y^{\prime}=c_2 c_1 e^{c_2 x} \\
& y^{\prime}=c_2 y \\
& y^{\prime \prime}=c_2 y^{\prime}
\end{aligned}
$$
From (2)
$$
c_2=\frac{y^{\prime}}{y}
$$
So, $y^{\prime \prime}=\frac{\left(y^{\prime}\right)^2}{y} \Rightarrow y y^{\prime \prime}=\left(y^{\prime}\right)^2$

</details>

**Detailed Solution:**

$2 x+2 y y^{\prime}-2 a y^{\prime}=0$
$a=\frac{x+y y^{\prime}}{y^{\prime}} \quad$ (eliminating a)
$\Rightarrow\left(x^2-y^2\right) y^{\prime}=2 x y$
