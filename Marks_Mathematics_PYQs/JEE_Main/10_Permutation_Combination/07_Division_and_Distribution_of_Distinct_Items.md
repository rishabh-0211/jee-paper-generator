### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (08 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A person has three different bags and four different books. The number of ways, in which he can put these books in the bags so that no bag is empty, is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $18$
- **(B)** $36$
- **(C)** $39$
- **(D)** $72$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $36$**

**Detailed Solution:**

To ensure no bag is empty, exactly one bag must contain 2 books and the other two bags must contain 1 book each.

The number of ways to choose 2 books out of 4 to be kept together is $^{4}C_{2} = 6$

Now, we have 3 units (one pair of books and two single books) to be distributed into the 3 different bags.

The number of ways to distribute these 3 units into 3 bags is $3! = 6$

Total number of ways = $6 \times 6 = 36$

Answer: $36$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

This question has Statement $-1$ and Statement $-2$. Of the four choices given after the statements, choose the one that best describes the two statements.
Statement - 1 : The number of ways of distributing 10 identical balls in 4 distinct boxes such that no box is empty is ${ }^9 \mathrm{C}_3$
Statement-2: The number of ways of choosing any 3 places from 9 different places is ${ }^9 \mathrm{C}_3$.

**Options:**

- **(A)** Statement $-1$ is true, Statement $-2$ is true; Statement $-2$ is not a correct explanation for Statement $-1$
- **(B)** Statement $-1$ is true, Statement $-2$ is false.
- **(C)** Statement $-1$ is false, Statement- 2 is true.
- **(D)** Statement $-1$ is true, Statement $-2$ is true; Statement $-2$ is a correct explanation for Statement $-1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) Statement $-1$ is true, Statement $-2$ is true; Statement $-2$ is a correct explanation for Statement $-1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
{ }^{(n-1)} C_{(r-1)}={ }^{(10-1)} C_{(4-1)}={ }^9 C_3
$$
Statement 1 is correct
Statement 2 is also correct
From 9 we can select 3 in ${ }^9 \mathrm{C}_3$ ways. It is correct explanation.

</details>
