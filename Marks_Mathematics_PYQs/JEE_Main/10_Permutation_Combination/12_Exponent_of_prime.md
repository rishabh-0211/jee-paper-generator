### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (24 January Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The largest value of $n$, for which $40^{n}$ divides $60!$, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 11
- **(B)** 12
- **(C)** 14
- **(D)** 13

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 14**

**Detailed Solution:**

$40^n = 2^{3n} \times 5^n$. We need the highest power of $40$ dividing $60!$.
Power of $5$ in $60! = \lfloor 60/5 \rfloor + \lfloor 60/25 \rfloor = 12 + 2 = 14$.
Power of $2$ in $60! = \lfloor 60/2 \rfloor + \lfloor 60/4 \rfloor + \lfloor 60/8 \rfloor + \lfloor 60/16 \rfloor + \lfloor 60/32 \rfloor = 30 + 15 + 7 + 3 + 1 = 56$.
We need $n \leq 14$ and $3n \leq 56$ (i.e., $n \leq 18$).
The binding constraint is $n \leq 14$, so the largest value is $n = 14$.

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (2 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The largest $\mathrm{n} \in \mathrm{N}$ such that $3^{\mathrm{n}}$ divides 50 ! is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** 21
- **(B)** 22
- **(C)** 20
- **(D)** 23

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 22**

**Detailed Solution:**

$2^\alpha \cdot 3^\beta \cdot 5^\gamma$
$\mathrm{B}=\left[\frac{50}{3}\right]+\left[\frac{50}{3^2}\right]+\left[\frac{50}{3^3}\right]+\left[\frac{50}{3^4}\right]$
$=16+5+1$
$=2$
Maximum value of $n$ is 22

</details>
