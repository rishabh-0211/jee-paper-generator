### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (06 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

In a triangle $\mathrm{ABC}, \mathrm{BC}=7, \mathrm{AC}=8, \mathrm{AB}=\alpha \in \mathrm{N}$ and $\cos \mathrm{A}=\frac{2}{3}$. If $49 \cos (3 \mathrm{C})+42=\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{n}}$, where $\operatorname{gcd}(\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{n})=1$, then $\mathrm{m}+\mathrm{n}$ is equal to________

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **39**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \cos \mathrm{A}=\frac{\mathrm{b}^2+\mathrm{c}^2-\mathrm{a}^2}{2 \mathrm{bc}} \\ & \frac{2}{3}=\frac{8^2+\mathrm{c}^2-7^2}{2 \times 8 \times \mathrm{c}} \\ & \mathrm{C}=9 \\ & \cos \mathrm{C}=\frac{7^2+8^2-9^2}{2 \times 7 \times 8}=\frac{2}{7} \\ & 49 \cos 3 \mathrm{C}+42 \\ & 49\left(4 \cos ^3 \mathrm{C}-3 \cos \mathrm{C}\right)+42 \\ & 49\left(4\left(\frac{2}{7}\right)^3-3\left(\frac{2}{7}\right)\right)+42 \\ & =\frac{32}{7} \\ & \mathrm{~m}+\mathrm{n}=32+7=39\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2019 (11 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Given $\frac{\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}}{11}=\frac{\mathrm{c}+\mathrm{a}}{12}=\frac{\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}}{13}$ for a $\Delta \mathrm{ABC}$ with usual

notation. If $\frac{\cos \mathrm{A}}{\alpha}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{B}}{\beta}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{C}}{\gamma},$ then the ordered triad$(\alpha, \beta, \gamma)$ has a value

**Options:**

- **(A)** (7,19,25)
- **(B)** (3,4,5)
- **(C)** (5,12,13)
- **(D)** (19,7,25)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) (7,19,25)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\frac{b+c}{11}=\frac{c+a}{12}=\frac{a+b}{13}=k$ (Say).

$\therefore \quad b+c=11 k, c+a=12 k, a+b=13 k$

$\therefore \quad a+b+c=18 k$

$\therefore \quad a=7 k, b=6 k$ and $c=5 k$

$\therefore \quad \cos A=\frac{36 k^{2}+25 k^{2}-49 k^{2}}{2.30 k^{2}}=\frac{1}{5}$

and $\cos B=\frac{49 k^{2}+25 k^{2}-36 k^{2}}{2.35 k^{2}}=\frac{19}{35}$

and $\cos C=\frac{49 k^{2}+36 k^{2}-25 k^{2}}{2.42 k^{2}}=\frac{5}{7}$

$\therefore \quad \cos A: \cos B: \cos C=7: 19: 25$

$\therefore \quad \frac{\cos A}{7}=\frac{\cos B}{19}=\frac{\cos C}{25}$

Hence, required ordered triplet is (7,19,25)

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2012 (12 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If in a triangle $A B C, \frac{b+c}{11}=\frac{c+a}{12}=\frac{a+b}{13}$, then $\cos \mathrm{A}$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 / 7$
- **(B)** $1 / 5$
- **(C)** $35 / 19$
- **(D)** $19 / 35$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $1 / 5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\frac{b+c}{11}=\frac{c+a}{12}=\frac{a+b}{13}=K$ in a triangle $A B C$. $\Rightarrow b+c=11 K, c+a=12 K, a+b=13 K$ On solving these, we get $a=7 K, b=6 K, c=5 K$
Now we know,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \cos A=\frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2 b c} \\
& =\frac{36 K^2+25 K^2-49 K^2}{2(6 K) 5 K}=\frac{1}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2003

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If in a triangle $\mathrm{ABC} \mathrm{a} \cos ^2\left(\frac{\mathrm{C}}{2}\right)+\mathrm{c}^2 \cos ^2\left(\frac{\mathrm{A}}{2}\right)=\frac{3 \mathrm{~b}}{2}$, then the sides $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}$ and $\mathrm{c}$

**Options:**

- **(A)** satisfy $a+b=c$
- **(B)** are in A.P.
- **(C)** are in G.P.
- **(D)** are in H.P.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) are in A.P.**

**Detailed Solution:**

If $a \cos ^2\left(\frac{\mathrm{C}}{2}\right)+\cos ^2\left(\frac{\mathrm{A}}{2}\right)=\frac{3 \mathrm{~b}}{2}$
$\mathrm{a}[\cos \mathrm{C}+1]+\mathrm{c}[\cos \mathrm{A}+1]=3 \mathrm{~b}$
$(\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{c})+(\mathrm{a} \cos \mathrm{C}+\mathrm{c} \cos \mathrm{B})=3 \mathrm{~b}$
$\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{c}+\mathrm{b}=3 \mathrm{~b}$ or $\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{c}=2 \mathrm{~b}$ or $\mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}$ are in A.P.

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2002

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

In a triangle with sides $a, b, c, r_1>r_2>r_3$ (which are the ex-radii) then

**Options:**

- **(A)** a > b > c
- **(B)** a  < b < c
- **(C)** a > b and b  < c
- **(D)** a  < b and b > c

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) a > b > c**

**Detailed Solution:**

$r_1>r_2>r_3 \Rightarrow \frac{\Delta}{s-a}>\frac{\Delta}{s-b}>\frac{\Delta}{s-c} \Rightarrow s-a < s-b < s-c \Rightarrow-a < -b < -c \Rightarrow a>b>c$

</details>
