### Q2. JEE Main 2002

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The equation of a circle with origin as a centre and passing through equilateral triangle whose median is of length 3a is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^2+y^2=9 a^2$
- **(B)** $x^2+y^2=16 a^2$
- **(C)** $x^2+y^2=4 a^2$
- **(D)** $x^2+y^2=a^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $x^2+y^2=4 a^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/4Dk8QnGk_rgxFyJTmn1mcLhAqEiqu3Adriw48JjCq_Y.original.fullsize.png">

Let $A B C$ be an equilateral triangle, whose median is $A D$.
Given $A D=3 a$
In $\triangle \mathrm{ABD}, \mathrm{AB}^2=\mathrm{AD}^2+\mathrm{BD}^2$
$\Rightarrow x^2=9 a^2+\left(x^2 / 4\right)$ where $A B=B C=A C=x . \frac{3}{4} x^2=9 a^2 \Rightarrow x^2=12 a^2$
In $\triangle \mathrm{OBD}, \mathrm{OB}^2=\mathrm{OD}^2+\mathrm{BD}^2$
$\Rightarrow r^2=(3 a-r)^2+\frac{x^2}{4} \Rightarrow r^2=9 a^2-6 a r+r^2+3 a^2 ; \Rightarrow 6 a r=12 a^2 \Rightarrow r=2 a$
So equation of circle is $x^2+y^2=4 a^2$

</details>
