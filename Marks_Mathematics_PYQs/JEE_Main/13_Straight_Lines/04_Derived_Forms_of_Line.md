### Q5. JEE Main 2012 (26 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The line parallel to $x$-axis and passing through the point of intersection of lines $a x+2 b y+3 b=0$ and $b x-2 a y-3 a=0$, where $(a, b) \neq(0,0)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** above $x$-axis at a distance $2 / 3$ from it
- **(B)** above $x$-axis at a distance $3 / 2$ from it
- **(C)** below $x$-axis at a distance $3 / 2$ from it
- **(D)** below $x$-axis at a distance $2 / 3$ from it

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) below $x$-axis at a distance $3 / 2$ from it**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given lines are
$$
a x+2 b y+3 b=0 \text { and }
$$
$$
b x-2 a y-3 a=0
$$
Since, required line is $\|$ to $x$-axis
$$
\therefore x=0
$$
We put $x=0$ in given equation, we get
$$
2 b y=-3 b \Rightarrow y=-\frac{3}{2}
$$
This shows that the required line is below $x$-axis at a distance of $\frac{3}{2}$ from it.

</details>
