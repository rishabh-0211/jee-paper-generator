### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let the points $\left(\frac{11}{2}, \alpha\right)$ lie on or inside the triangle with sides $x+y=11, x+2 y=16$ and $2 x+3 y=29$. Then the product of the smallest and the largest values of $\alpha$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 44
- **(B)** 22
- **(C)** 33
- **(D)** 55

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 33**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/iM9WDDi4qmrkBX2YtLLqEuIW0CodQiJCCWa5vnlX8Mk.original.fullsize.png">

Clearly, $x=\frac{11}{2}$ intersect $x+y-11=0$ at $\left(\frac{11}{2}, \frac{11}{2}\right)$ and $2 x+3 y-29=0$ at $\left(\frac{11}{2}, 6\right) \Rightarrow \alpha=\left[\frac{11}{2}, 6\right]$
$\alpha_{\min } \cdot \alpha_{\max }=\frac{11}{2} \cdot 6=33$
