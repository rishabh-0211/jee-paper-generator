### Q1. JEE Main 2025 (24 Jan Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\operatorname{IfI}(m, n)=\int_0^1 x^{m-1}(1-x)^{n-1} d x, m, n\gt0$, then $I(9,14)+I(10,13)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{I}(19,27)$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{I}(9,1)$
- **(C)** $I(1,13)$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{I}(9,13)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\mathrm{I}(9,13)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{I}(\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{m})=\int_0^1 \mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{m}-1}(1-\mathrm{x})^{\mathrm{n}-1} d \mathrm{x} \\ & \text { Let } \mathrm{x}=\sin ^2 \theta \quad \mathrm{dx}=2 \sin \theta \cos \theta \mathrm{~d} \theta \\ & \mathrm{I}(\mathrm{m}, \mathrm{n})=2 \int_0^{\pi / 2}(\sin \theta)^{2 \mathrm{~m}-1}(\cos \theta)^{2 \mathrm{n}-1} d \theta \\ & \mathrm{I}(9,14)+\mathrm{I}(10,13)=2 \int_0^{\pi / 2}(\sin \theta)^{17}(\cos \theta)^{27} d \theta \\ & +2 \int_0^{\pi / 2}(\sin \theta)^{19}(\cos \theta)^{25} d \theta \\ & =2 \int_0^{\pi / 2}(\sin \theta)^{17}(\cos \theta)^{25} d \theta \\ & =\mathrm{I}(9,13)\end{aligned}$

</details>
