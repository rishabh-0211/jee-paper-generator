### Q1. JEE Main 2012 (07 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The frequency distribution of daily working expenditure of families in a locality is as follows:
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/WtVuYY0tOxvIhBNQ30vp8moJZxOn7__6MND966C8Q6g.original.fullsize.png">

If the mode of the distribution is $₹ 140$, then the value of $b$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 34
- **(B)** 31
- **(C)** 26
- **(D)** 36

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 36**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\text { Frequency distribution is given as }
$$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/W8clFOASCZEK_blaPdliLciWwuazF8By8z3Jpa-KouI.original.fullsize.png">

Clearly, modal class is $100-150$, as the maximum frequency occurs in this class.
Given, Mode $=140$
We have
$$
\text { Mode }=\ell+\frac{f_0-f_{-1}}{2 f_0-f_{-1}-f_1} \times i
$$
where
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \ell=100, f_0=37, f_{-1}=33, f_1=\mathrm{b} \\
& i=50
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, we get
$$
140=100+\left[\frac{37-33}{2(37)-33-b}\right] \times 50
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& =100+\left[\frac{4}{74-33-b}\right] \times 50 \\
& =100+\frac{200}{41-b} \\
\Rightarrow & 5740=4300+40 b \Rightarrow b=36
\end{aligned}
$$
