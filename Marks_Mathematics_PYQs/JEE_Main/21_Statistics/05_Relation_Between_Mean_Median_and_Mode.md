### Q1. JEE Main 2013 (23 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the median and the range of four numbers $\{x, y, 2 x+y, x-y\}$, where $0 < y < x < 2 y$, are 10 and 28 respectively, then the mean of the numbers is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 18
- **(B)** 10
- **(C)** 5
- **(D)** 14

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 14**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since $0 < y < x < 2 y$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \quad y>\frac{x}{2} \Rightarrow x-y < \frac{x}{2} \\
& \therefore x-y < y < x < 2 x+y
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence median $=\frac{y+x}{2}=10$
$\Rightarrow x+y=20$
And range $=(2 x+y)-(x-y)=x+2 y$
But range $=28$
$$
\therefore x+2 y=28
$$
From equations (i) and (ii),
$$
\begin{aligned}
& x=12, y=8 \\
& \therefore \text { Mean } \\
& =\frac{(x-y)+y+x+(2 x+y)}{4}=\frac{4 x+y}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
=x+\frac{y}{4}=12+\frac{8}{4}=14
$$

### Q2. JEE Main 2005

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If in a frequently distribution, the mean and median are 21 and 22 respectively, then its mode is approximately

**Options:**

- **(A)** 22.0
- **(B)** 20.5
- **(C)** 25.5
- **(D)** 24.0

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 24.0**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mode $+2$ Mean $=3$ Median
$$
\Rightarrow \text { Mode }=3 \times 22-2 \times 21=66-42=24 \text {. }
$$

</details>
