### Q1. JEE Main 2013 (07 Apr)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

All the students of a class performed poorly in Mathematics. The teacher decided to give grace marks of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mn>10</mn></math> to each of the students. Which of the following statistical measures will not change even after the grace marks were given ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** mode
- **(B)** variance
- **(C)** mean
- **(D)** median

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) variance**

**Detailed Solution:**

As we know that with the change in origin, the variance does not change.<p>Therefore, variance does not change even after giving grace marks of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><mn>10</mn></math> to each of the students.</p><p>Whereas, mean, mode and median changes upon changing the origin.</p>

</details>

### Q2. JEE Main 2004

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Consider the following statements:
Mode can be computed from histogram
Median is not independent of change of scale
Variance is independent of change of origin and scale.

**Options:**

- **(A)** only (a)
- **(B)** only (b)
- **(C)** only (a) and (b)
- **(D)** (a), (b) and (c)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) only (a) and (b)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mode can be computed from histogram and median is dependent on the scale. Hence statement (a) and (b) are correct

</details>
