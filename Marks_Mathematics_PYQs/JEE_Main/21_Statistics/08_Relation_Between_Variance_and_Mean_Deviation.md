### Q1. JEE Main 2024 (09 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the variance of the frequency distribution
$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline x & c & 2 c & 3 c & 4 c & 5 c & 6 c \\
\hline f & 2 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
\hline
\end{array}$
is 160, then the value of $c \in N$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 7
- **(B)** 8
- **(C)** 5
- **(D)** 6

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 7**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline \mathrm{x} & \mathrm{C} & 2 \mathrm{C} & 3 \mathrm{C} & 4 \mathrm{C} & 5 \mathrm{C} & 6 \mathrm{C} \\
\hline \mathrm{f} & 2 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\
\hline
\end{array}$
$\bar{x}=\frac{(2+2+3+4+5+6) C}{7}=\frac{22 C}{7}$
$\begin{aligned} & \operatorname{Var}(x)=\frac{\mathrm{c}^2\left(2+2^2+3^2+4^2+5^2+6^2\right)}{7} \\ & -\left(\frac{22 \mathrm{c}}{7}\right)^2 \\ & =\frac{92 \mathrm{c}^2}{7}-\mathrm{c}^2 \times \frac{484}{49} \\ & =\frac{(644-484) \mathrm{c}^2}{49}=\frac{160 \mathrm{c}^2}{49} \\ & 160=\frac{160 \times \mathrm{c}^2}{49} \Rightarrow \mathrm{c}=7\end{aligned}$

</details>

$\begin{array}{|l|c|c|c|c|l|l|}
\hline \text{Age} & 15 & 16 & 17 & 18 & 19 & 20 \\
\hline \text{No of Students} & 5 & 8 & 5 & 12 & x & y \\

\end{array}$
If the mean deviation about the median is 1.25, then $4 x+5 y$ is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 46
- **(B)** 43
- **(C)** 44

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 44**

$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|l|}

\hline 16 & 8 & 2 & 16 \\
\hline 17 & 5 & 1 & 5 \\
\hline 18 & 12 & 0 & 0 \\
\hline 19 & \mathrm{x} & 1 & \mathrm{x} \\

### Q3. JEE Main 2024 (08 Apr Shift 2)

Let the mean, the mean deviation about the mean and the variance of the 5 observations $9,25, \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}$ be 18,4 and $\frac{136}{5}$, respectively. Then $2 \mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}-\mathrm{c}$ is equal to__________

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **33**

**Detailed Solution:**

$a, b, c \in N \quad a < b < c$
$\overline{\mathrm{x}}=$ mean $=\frac{9+25+\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}}{5}=18$
$a+b+c=56$
Mean deviation $=\frac{\Sigma\left|\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}-\overline{\mathrm{x}}\right|}{\mathrm{n}}=4$
$\begin{aligned} & =9+7+|18-\mathrm{a}|+|18-\mathrm{b}|+|18-\mathrm{c}|=20 \\ & =|18-\mathrm{a}|+|18-\mathrm{b}|+|18-\mathrm{c}|=4\end{aligned}$
Variance $=\frac{\Sigma\left|\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}-\overline{\mathrm{x}}\right|^2}{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{136}{5}$
$\begin{aligned} & =81+49+|18-a|^2+|18-b|^2+|18-c|^2=136 \\ & =(18-a)^2+(18-b)^2+(18-c)^2=6\end{aligned}$
Possible values $(18-\mathrm{a})^2=1, \quad(18-\mathrm{b})^2=1 \quad(18-\mathrm{c})^2=4$ $\mathrm{a} < \mathrm{b} < \mathrm{c}$
so $\quad \begin{array}{ccc}18-a=1 & 18-b=-1 & 18-c=-2 \\ \mathrm{a}=17 & \mathrm{~b}=19 & \mathrm{c}=20\end{array}$
$a+b+c=56$
$2 \mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}-\mathrm{c} 34=19-20=33$

### Q4. JEE Main 2024 (05 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

Let the mean and the standard deviation of the probability distribution
$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline \mathrm{X}  & \mathrm{\alpha} & \mathrm{1} & 0 & -3 \\
\hline \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}) & \frac{1}{3} & \mathrm{~K} & \frac{1}{6} & \frac{1}{4} \\
\hline
\end{array}$
be $\mu$ and $\sigma$, respectively. If $\sigma-\mu=2$, then $\sigma+\mu$ is equal to________

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **5**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{1}{3}+\mathrm{k}+\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{4}=1 \quad \Rightarrow \mathrm{k}=\frac{1}{4} \\ & \mu=\frac{\alpha}{3}+\frac{1}{4}-\frac{3}{4} \\ & \mu=\frac{\alpha}{3}-\frac{1}{2} \\ & \sigma=\sqrt{\left(\alpha^2 \frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}+9 \frac{1}{4}\right)-\left(\frac{\alpha}{3}-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} \\ & \sigma=\sqrt{\frac{2 \alpha^2}{9}+\frac{\alpha}{3}+\frac{9}{4}} \\ & \sigma=\mu+2 \\ & \sigma^2=(\mu+2)^2 \Rightarrow \frac{2 \alpha^2}{9}+\frac{\alpha}{3}+\frac{9}{4}=\frac{\alpha^2}{9}+\frac{9}{4}+\alpha \\ & \frac{\alpha^2}{9}-\frac{2 \alpha}{3}=0 \\ & \alpha=0,(\text { reject }) \text { or } \alpha=6 \\ & (\because \mathrm{x}=0 \text { is already given) } \\ & \Rightarrow \sigma+\mu=2 \mu+2 \\ & =5\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q5. JEE Main 2024 (05 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

From a lot of 10 items, which include 3 defective items, a sample of 5 items is drawn at random. Let the random variable $\mathrm{X}$ denote the number of defective items in the sample. If the variance of $\mathrm{X}$ is $\sigma^2$, then $96 \sigma^2$ is equal to ______

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **56**

**Detailed Solution:**

$X=$ denotes number of defective
$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline \mathrm{x} & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\
\hline \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{x}) & \frac{7}{15} & \frac{5}{12} & \frac{5}{12} & \frac{1}{12} \\
\hline \mathrm{x}_1{ }^2 & 0 & 1 & 4 & 9 \\
\hline \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_1{ }^2 & 0 & \frac{5}{12} & \frac{20}{12} & \frac{9}{12} \\
\hline \mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}} & 0 & \frac{5}{12} & \frac{10}{12} & \frac{3}{12} \\
\hline
\end{array}$
$\mu=\Sigma \mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}=\frac{18}{12}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Sigma \mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_1^2=\frac{34}{12} \\
& \sigma^2=\Sigma \mathrm{p}_{\mathrm{i}} \mathrm{x}_1^2-(\mu)^2
\end{aligned}$
$\begin{aligned} & =\frac{34}{12}-\left(\frac{18}{12}\right)^2=\frac{17}{6}-\frac{9}{4} \\ & \frac{34-27}{12}=\frac{7}{12} \\ & 96 \sigma^2=96 \times \frac{7}{12}=56\end{aligned}$

### Q6. JEE Main 2024 (04 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the mean of the following probability distribution of a random variable $\mathrm{X}$ :
$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline \mathrm{X} & 0 & 2 & 4 & 6 & 8 \\
\hline \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}) & a & 2 a & a+b & 2 b & 3 b \\
\hline
\end{array}$
is $\frac{46}{9}$, then the variance of the distribution is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{173}{27}$
- **(B)** $\frac{566}{81}$
- **(C)** $\frac{151}{27}$
- **(D)** $\frac{581}{81}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{566}{81}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \sum P_i=1 \\ & a+2 a+a+b+2 b+3 b=1\end{aligned}$
$4 a+6 b=1$ ...(I)
$\mathrm{E}(\mathrm{x})=$ mean $=\frac{46}{9}$
$\begin{aligned} & \sum P_i X_i=\frac{46}{9} \Rightarrow 4 a+4 a+4 b+12 b+24 b=\frac{46}{9} \\ & 8 a+40 b=\frac{46}{9}\end{aligned}$
$4 a+20 b=\frac{23}{9}$ ...(II)
Subtract (I) from (II) we get
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{b}=\frac{1}{9} \& \mathrm{a}=\frac{1}{12} \\
& \text { Variance }=\mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}{ }^2\right)-\mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}\right)^2 \\
& \mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}\right)^2=0^2 \times 9^2+2^2 \times 2 \mathrm{a}+4^2(\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b})+6^2(2 \mathrm{~b})+8^2(3 \mathrm{~b}) \\
& =24 \mathrm{a}+280 \mathrm{~b}
\end{aligned}$
Put $\mathrm{a}=\frac{1}{12} \quad \mathrm{~b}=\frac{1}{9}$
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}^2\right)=2+\frac{280}{9}=\frac{298}{9} \\
& \therefore \sigma^2=\mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}^2\right)-\mathrm{E}\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}\right)^2 \\
& =\frac{298}{9}-\left(\frac{46}{9}\right)^2 \\
& \sigma^2=\frac{298}{9}-\frac{2116}{81} \\
& =\frac{566}{81}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

Let $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbf{R}$. Let the mean and the variance of 6 observations $-3,4,7,-6, \alpha, \beta$ be 2 and 23 , respectively. The mean deviation about the mean of these 6 observations is :

solving we get $\alpha=4, \beta=6$

### Q8. JEE Main 2013 (22 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Mean of 5 observations is 7 . If four of these observations are $6,7,8,10$ and one is missing then the variance of all the five observations is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** 8
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let 5 th observation be $x$.
Given mean $=7$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore 7=\frac{6+7+8+10+x}{5} \\
& \Rightarrow x=4
\end{aligned}
$$
Now, Variance
$$
\begin{gathered}
=\sqrt{\frac{(6-7)^2+(7-7)^2+(8-7)^2+(10-7)^2+(4-7)^2}{5}} \\
=\sqrt{\frac{1^2+0^2+1^2+3^2+3^2}{5}}=\sqrt{\frac{20}{5}}=\sqrt{4}=2
\end{gathered}
$$

</details>
