### Q12. JEE Main 2012 (19 May Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the normal to the parabola, $x^2=8 y$ at $x=4$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x+2 y=0$
- **(B)** $x+y=2$
- **(C)** $x-2 y=0$
- **(D)** $x+y=6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $x+y=6$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$x^2=8 y$
When, $x=4$, then $y=2$
Now $\left.\frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{2 x}{8}=\frac{x}{4}, \frac{d y}{d x}\right]_{x=4}=1$
Slope of normal $=-\frac{1}{\frac{d y}{d x}}=-1$
Euqation of normal at $x=4$ is $y-2=-1(x-4)$
$\Rightarrow y=-x+4+2=-x+6$
$\Rightarrow x+y=6$

</details>
