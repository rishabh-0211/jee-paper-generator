### Q2. JEE Main 2025 (4 Apr Shift 2)

A line passing through the point $\mathrm{A}(-2,0)$, touches the parabola $P: y^2=x-2$ at the point $B$ in the first quadrant. The area, of the region bounded by the line AB , parabola P and the x -axis, is :-

- **(D)** $3$

<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{8}{3}$**

$\begin{aligned}

& m=\frac{1}{4} \\
& y=\frac{1}{4}(n+2)
\end{aligned}$
and point of tangency $(6,2)$

### Q3. JEE Main 2025 (3 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The radius of the smallest circle which touches the parabolas $y=x^2+2$ and $x=y^2+2$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{16}$
- **(C)** $\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{4}$
- **(D)** $\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{8}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{8}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given parabolas are symmetric about the line $y=x$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_main/DFGMFxnnXiuvYPhT62tZtj9juligsHozKB46Y0fNXbw.original.fullsize.png"/>
Tangents at $\mathrm{A} \& \mathrm{~B}$ must be parallel to $\mathrm{y}=\mathrm{x}$ line, so slope of the tangents $=1$
$\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)_{\min A}=1=\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)_{\min B}$
For point $\mathrm{B}, \mathrm{y}=\mathrm{x}^2+2$
$\begin{gathered}
\frac{d y}{d x}=2 x=1 \\
x=\frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow y=\frac{9}{4} \\
\therefore \text { Point } B=\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{9}{4}\right) \Rightarrow \text { Point } \mathrm{A}=\left(\frac{9}{4}, \frac{1}{2}\right) \\
\mathrm{AB}=\sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{9}{4}\right)^2+\left(\frac{9}{4}-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} \\
=\sqrt{\frac{98}{16}}=\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{4}
\end{gathered}$
Radius $==\frac{7 \sqrt{2}}{8}$

</details>
