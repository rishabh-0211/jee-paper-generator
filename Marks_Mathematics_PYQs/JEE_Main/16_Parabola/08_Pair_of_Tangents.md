### Q2. JEE Main 2018 (15 Apr Shift 2 Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Tangents drawn from the point $(-8,0)$ to the parabola $y^2=8 x$ touch the parabola at $P$ and $Q$. If $F$ is the focus of the parabola, then the area of the triangle $P F Q$ (in sq. units) is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 48
- **(B)** 32
- **(C)** 24
- **(D)** 64

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 48**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of the chord of contact $P Q$ is given by:
$$
T=0
$$
or $T \equiv y y_1-4\left(x+x_1\right)$,
where $\left(x_1, y_1\right) \equiv(-8,0)$
$\therefore$ Equation becomes: $x=8$
$\&$ Chord of contact is $x=8$
$\therefore$ Coordinates of point $P$ and $Q$ are $(8,8)$ and $(8,-8)$
and focus of the parabola is $F(2,0)$
$\therefore$ Area of triangle $P Q F=\frac{1}{2} \times(8-2) \times(8+8)$ $=48$ sq. units

</details>

### Q3. JEE Main 2014 (12 Apr Online)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two tangents are drawn from a point $(-2,-1)$ to the curve, $\mathrm{y}^2=4 \mathrm{x}$. If $\alpha$ is the angle between them, then $|\tan \alpha|$ is equal to:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(D)** 3

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 3**

**Detailed Solution:**

The locus of the point of intersection of tangents to the parabola $y^2=4 a x$ inclined at an angle $\alpha$ to each other is $\tan ^2 \alpha(x+a)^2=y^2-4 a x$
Given equation of Parabola
$$
y^2=4 x\{a=1\}
$$
Point of intersection $(-2,-1)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\tan ^2 \alpha(-2+1)^2=(-1)^2-4 \times 1 \times(-2) \\
&\Rightarrow \tan ^2 \alpha=9 \\
&\Rightarrow \tan \alpha=\pm 3 \\
&\Rightarrow|\tan \alpha|=3
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Main 2006

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Angle between the tangents to the curve $y=x^2-5 x+6$ at the points $(2,0)$ and $(3,0)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\pi}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\pi}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\pi}{6}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\pi}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{\pi}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{d y}{d x}=2 x-5$
$\therefore m_1=(2 x-5)_{(2,0)}=-1, m_2=(2 x-5)_{(3,0)}=1$
$\Rightarrow m_1 m_2=-1$

</details>
