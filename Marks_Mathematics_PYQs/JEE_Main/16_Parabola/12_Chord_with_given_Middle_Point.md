### Q1. JEE Main 2026 (21 January Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let O be the vertex of the parabola $x^{2}=4 y$ and Q be any point on it. Let the locus of the point P, which divides the line segment OQ internally in the ratio $2: 3$ be the conic C. Then the equation of the chord of $C$, which is bisected at the point $(1,2)$, is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 x-4 y+3=0$
- **(B)** $5 x-y-3=0$
- **(C)** $4 x-5 y+6=0$
- **(D)** $x-2 y+3=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $5 x-4 y+3=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Parabola $x^2 = 4y$, vertex O = (0, 0), Q = $(2t, t^2)$ on parabola.
P divides OQ in ratio 2:3: $P = \left(\frac{4t}{5}, \frac{2t^2}{5}\right)$.
Eliminating $t$: $x^2 = \frac{8y}{5}$ (conic C with $a = \frac{2}{5}$).
For chord with midpoint $(h, k)$: $hx - 2ay = h^2 - 2ak$.
At $(1, 2)$: $x - \frac{4y}{5} = 1 - \frac{8}{5} = -\frac{3}{5}$.
$5x - 4y + 3 = 0$.

</details>
