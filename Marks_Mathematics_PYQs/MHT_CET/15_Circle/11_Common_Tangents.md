### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the common tangent touching the circle $(x-3)^2 + y^2 = 9$ and the parabola $y^2 = 4x$ above the X-axis is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{3}y = 3x + 1$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{3}y = -(x + 3)$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{3}y = x + 3$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{3}y = -(3x + 1)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{3}y = x + 3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of a tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 4x$ having slope $m$ is given by $y = mx + \dfrac{1}{m}$, which can be written as $mx - y + \dfrac{1}{m} = 0$.

Since this line is also a tangent to the circle $(x-3)^2 + y^2 = 9$, the perpendicular distance from the center of the circle $(3, 0)$ to the line must be equal to its radius $3$.

$\dfrac{\left|3m - 0 + \dfrac{1}{m}\right|}{\sqrt{m^2 + 1}} = 3$

Squaring both sides:

$\left(3m + \dfrac{1}{m}\right)^2 = 9(m^2 + 1)$

$9m^2 + 6 + \dfrac{1}{m^2} = 9m^2 + 9$

$\dfrac{1}{m^2} = 3 \Rightarrow m = \pm \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

The point of contact of the tangent on the parabola is $\left(\dfrac{1}{m^2}, \dfrac{2}{m}\right)$. Since the tangent touches the parabola above the X-axis, the y-coordinate of the point of contact must be positive, which implies $m > 0$.

Therefore, $m = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.

Substituting $m = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ in the equation of the tangent:

$y = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}x + \sqrt{3}$

$\sqrt{3}y = x + 3$

Answer: $\sqrt{3}y = x + 3$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (27 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The number of common tangents to the circles $x^2+y^2-4 x-6 y-12=0$ and $x^2+y^2+6 x+18 y+26=0$, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** 2
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 3**

**Detailed Solution:**

For the first circle $x^2+y^2-4x-6y-12=0$, the center is $C_1(2, 3)$ and the radius is $r_1 = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2 - (-12)} = \sqrt{4+9+12} = 5$.

For the second circle $x^2+y^2+6x+18y+26=0$, the center is $C_2(-3, -9)$ and the radius is $r_2 = \sqrt{(-3)^2 + (-9)^2 - 26} = \sqrt{9+81-26} = 8$.

The distance between the centers $C_1$ and $C_2$ is $d = \sqrt{(-3-2)^2 + (-9-3)^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$.

The sum of the radii is $r_1 + r_2 = 5 + 8 = 13$.

Since $d = r_1 + r_2$, the two circles touch each other externally.

Therefore, the number of common tangents is $3$.

Answer: 3

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (19 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The number of common tangents that can be drawn to the circles $x^2+y^2-6 x=0$ and $x^2+y^2+6 x+2 y+1=0$ is .....

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $2$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

For the first circle $x^2+y^2-6x=0$:
Center $C_1 = (3, 0)$
Radius $r_1 = \sqrt{(-3)^2 + 0^2 - 0} = 3$

For the second circle $x^2+y^2+6x+2y+1=0$:
Center $C_2 = (-3, -1)$
Radius $r_2 = \sqrt{3^2 + 1^2 - 1} = 3$

The distance between the centers is:
$d = \sqrt{(-3 - 3)^2 + (-1 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 1} = \sqrt{37}$

The sum of the radii is:
$r_1 + r_2 = 3 + 3 = 6 = \sqrt{36}$

Since $d > r_1 + r_2$, the two circles lie completely outside each other and do not intersect.

Therefore, the number of common tangents that can be drawn is $4$.

Answer: $4$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2023 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Number of common tangents to the circles $x^2+y^2-6 x-14 y+48=0$ and $x^2+y^2-6 x=0$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0$
- **(B)** $1$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{array}{ll} 
& x^2+y^2-6 x-14 y+48=0 \\
\therefore \quad & \mathrm{C}_1(3,7), \mathrm{r}_1=\sqrt{10} \\
& \text { Again } x^2+y^2-6 x=0 \\
\therefore \quad & \mathrm{C}_2(3,0), \mathrm{r}_2=3
\end{array}
$$

Now $l\left(\mathrm{C}_1 \mathrm{C}_2\right)=$ distance between centres
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \quad l\left(\mathrm{C}_1 \mathrm{C}_2\right)=\sqrt{0^2+7^2}=7 \text { and } \\
& \\
& \mathrm{r}_1+\mathrm{r}_2=\sqrt{10}+3 < l\left(\mathrm{C}_1 \mathrm{C}_2\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
$\Rightarrow$ The given circles are disjoint.
$\Rightarrow$ Number of common tangents is 4 .

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2021 (24 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of common tangent to the circles $\mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{y}^2-4 \mathrm{x}+10 \mathrm{y}+20=0$ and $\mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{y}^2+8 \mathrm{x}-6 \mathrm{y}-24=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 x-4 y+11=0$
- **(B)** $3 x-4 y-11=0$
- **(C)** $-3 x-4 y+11=0$
- **(D)** $3 x+4 y+11=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3 x-4 y-11=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Circle $x^2+y^2-4 x+10 y+20=0$ has centre $C_1=(2,-5)$ and radius
$$
\mathrm{r}_1=\sqrt{4+25-20}=3
$$
Circle $\mathrm{x}^2+\mathrm{y}^2+8 \mathrm{x}-6 \mathrm{y}-24=0$ has centre $\mathrm{C}_2=(2,-5)$ and radius
$$
\mathrm{r}_2=\sqrt{16+9+24}=7
$$
Distance between centres
$$
=\sqrt{(2+4)^2+(5-2)^2}=10
$$
Thus circle touch each other externally at one point only.
$\therefore$ Equation of common tangent is
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \left(x^2+y^2-4 x+10 y+20\right)-\left(x^2+y^2+8 x-6 y-24\right)=0 \text { i.e. } \\
& 12 x-16 y-44=0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 3 x-4 y-11=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
