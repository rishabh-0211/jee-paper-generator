### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The equation of the circle, concentric with the circle $2 x^2+2 y^2-6 x+8 y+1=0$ and double of its area is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 x^2+2 y^2-6 x+8 y+11=0$
- **(B)** $2 x^2+2 y^2-6 x+8 y-11=0$
- **(C)** $4 x^2+4 y^2-12 x+16 y-21=0$
- **(D)** $4 x^2+4 y^2-12 x+16 y+21=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4 x^2+4 y^2-12 x+16 y-21=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the circle is $2x^2 + 2y^2 - 6x + 8y + 1 = 0$.

Dividing by $2$, we get the standard form:

$x^2 + y^2 - 3x + 4y + \dfrac{1}{2} = 0$

The center of this circle is $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, -2\right)$ and the square of its radius is:

$r_1^2 = g^2 + f^2 - c = \left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 + (2)^2 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{9}{4} + 4 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{23}{4}$

The new circle is concentric with the given circle, so its center is also $\left(\dfrac{3}{2}, -2\right)$.

Since the area of the new circle is double the area of the given circle, its radius squared will be double:

$r_2^2 = 2r_1^2 = 2 \times \dfrac{23}{4} = \dfrac{23}{2}$

The equation of the new circle is:

$\left(x - \dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = r_2^2$

$x^2 - 3x + \dfrac{9}{4} + y^2 + 4y + 4 = \dfrac{23}{2}$

$x^2 + y^2 - 3x + 4y + \dfrac{25}{4} - \dfrac{46}{4} = 0$

$x^2 + y^2 - 3x + 4y - \dfrac{21}{4} = 0$

Multiplying the entire equation by $4$, we get:

$4x^2 + 4y^2 - 12x + 16y - 21 = 0$

Answer: $4 x^2+4 y^2-12 x+16 y-21=0$

</details>
