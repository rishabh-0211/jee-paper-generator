### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The equation of the çircle which has its centre at the point $(3,4)$ and touches the line $5 x+12 y-11=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y+9=0$
- **(B)** $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y+25=0$
- **(C)** $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y-9=0$
- **(D)** $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y-25=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y+9=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The radius of the circle is the perpendicular distance from the centre $(3,4)$ to the line $5x + 12y - 11 = 0$.

$r = \dfrac{|5(3) + 12(4) - 11|}{\sqrt{5^2 + 12^2}}$

$r = \dfrac{|15 + 48 - 11|}{\sqrt{25 + 144}}$

$r = \dfrac{52}{13} = 4$

The equation of the circle with centre $(3,4)$ and radius $4$ is given by:

$(x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 4^2$

$x^2 - 6x + 9 + y^2 - 8y + 16 = 16$

$x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 8y + 9 = 0$

Answer: $x^2+y^2-6 x-8 y+9=0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2021 (23 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If a circle passes through the points $(0,0),(0, y)$, then the coordinates of its centre are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\frac{-x}{2}, \frac{y}{2}\right)$
- **(B)** $\left(\frac{x}{2}, \frac{y}{2}\right)$
- **(C)** $\left(\frac{-x}{2}, \frac{-y}{2}\right)$
- **(D)** $\left(\frac{x}{2}, \frac{-y}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left(\frac{x}{2}, \frac{y}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $(h, k)$ be the centre of circle
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \sqrt{(\mathrm{h}-0)^2+(\mathrm{k}-0)^2}=\sqrt{(\mathrm{h}-\mathrm{x})^2+(\mathrm{k}-0)^2}=\sqrt{(\mathrm{h}-0)^2+(\mathrm{k}-\mathrm{y})^2} \\
& \therefore \mathrm{h}^2+\mathrm{k}^2=(\mathrm{h}-\mathrm{x})^2+\mathrm{k}^2 \quad=\mathrm{h}^2+(\mathrm{k}-\mathrm{y})^2 \\
& \therefore-2 \mathrm{hx}+\mathrm{y}^2=0 \quad \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}(\mathrm{x}-2 \mathrm{~h})=0 \text { and } \\
& -2 \mathrm{ky}+\mathrm{y}^2=0 \text { and } \mathrm{y}(\mathrm{y}-2 \mathrm{k})=0 \\
& \therefore \mathrm{x}=0,2 \mathrm{~h} \quad \text { and } \mathrm{y}=0,2 \mathrm{k} \\
& \therefore \mathrm{x}=2 \mathrm{~h} \text { and } \mathrm{y}=2 \mathrm{k}, \Rightarrow \mathrm{h}=\frac{\mathrm{x}}{2}, \mathrm{k}=\frac{\mathrm{y}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The cartesian equation of the curve $x=3+5 \cos \theta, y=2+5 \sin \theta$ is
$(0 \leq \theta \leq 2 \pi)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^{2}+y^{2}-6 x+4 y-12=0$
- **(B)** $x^{2}+y^{2}+6 x+4 y+12=0$
- **(C)** $x^{2}+y^{2}+6 x-4 y+12=0$
- **(D)** $x^{2}+y^{2}-6 x-4 y-12=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $x^{2}+y^{2}-6 x-4 y-12=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $\frac{x-3}{5}=\cos \theta$ and $\frac{y-2}{5}=\sin \theta$ $\therefore \cos ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \theta=1$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left(\frac{x-3}{5}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{y-2}{5}\right)^{2}=1 \\
\therefore & x^{2}-6 x+9+y^{2}-4 y+4=25 \\
\therefore & x^{2}+y^{2}-6 x-4 y-12=0
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2020 (14 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The centre and radius of a circle $x=4 a\left(\frac{1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}}\right), y=\frac{8 a t}{1+t^{2}}$, are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(0,0)$ and $3 a$ units
- **(B)** $(0,0)$ and $4 a$ units
- **(C)** $(0,0)$ and $2 a$ units
- **(D)** $(0,0)$ and $a$ units

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $(0,0)$ and $4 a$ units**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given equation of circle in parametric form is
$$
x=4 a\left(\frac{1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}}\right) \text { and } y=4 a\left(\frac{2 t}{1+t^{2}}\right)
$$
$x=4 a \cos 2 \theta$ and $y=4 a \sin 2 \theta$, where $t=\tan \theta$
Comparing with $x=r \cos \theta, y=r \sin \theta$, we get $r=4 a$, centre is $(0,0)$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2020 (12 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The cartesian equation of the curve given by $x=6 \cos \theta, \quad y=6 \sin \theta$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^{2}+y^{2}=36$
- **(B)** $x^{2}+y^{2}=5$
- **(C)** $x^{2}+y^{2}=25$
- **(D)** $x^{2}+y^{2}=6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x^{2}+y^{2}=36$**

**Detailed Solution:**

On squaring and adding both given equations, we get
$\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+y^{2} &=36 \cos ^{2} \theta+36 \sin ^{2} \theta \\
&=36\left(\cos ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \theta\right) \\
x^{2}+y^{2} &=36
\end{aligned}$

</details>
