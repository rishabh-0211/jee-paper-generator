### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The differential equation of family of circles, whose centres are on the X -axis and also touch the Y -axis is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$
- **(B)** $\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$
- **(C)** $2\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$
- **(D)** $\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=4\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $4\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the centre of the circle be $(a, 0)$.

Since the circle touches the Y-axis, its radius is $a$.

The equation of the family of circles is $(x - a)^2 + y^2 = a^2$.

Expanding this, we get $x^2 - 2ax + a^2 + y^2 = a^2$, which simplifies to $x^2 + y^2 = 2ax$.

Differentiating both sides with respect to $x$, we get $2x + 2y \dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2a$.

Thus, $a = x + y \dfrac{dy}{dx}$.

Substituting the value of $a$ back into the equation $x^2 + y^2 = 2ax$, we get $x^2 + y^2 = 2x \left( x + y \dfrac{dy}{dx} \right)$.

Squaring both sides, we obtain $\left(x^2 + y^2\right)^2 = 4x^2 \left( x + y \dfrac{dy}{dx} \right)^2$.

Answer: $4\left(x+y \dfrac{\mathrm{~d} y}{\mathrm{~d} x}\right)^2 x^2=\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2021 (24 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $y=2 x$ is a chord of circle $x^2+y^2-10 x=0$, then the equation of circle with this chord as diameter is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^2+y^2-2 x-4 y=0$
- **(B)** $x^2+y^2+2 x+4 y=0$
- **(C)** $x^2+y^2-2 x+4 y=0$
- **(D)** $x^2+y^2+2 x-4 y=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x^2+y^2-2 x-4 y=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
& x^2-10 x+y^2=0 \\
& x-10 x+25+y^2=25 \Rightarrow \text { centre }=(5,0) \text { and } r=5
\end{aligned}$
$\mathrm{y}=2 \mathrm{x}$ is a chord of given circle.
Point of intersection of chord and circle is
$\mathrm{x}^2-10 \mathrm{x}+25+4 \mathrm{x}^2=25 \quad \Rightarrow \mathrm{y}=0,4$
Thus end points of the chord are $(0,0)$ and $(2,4)$
Mid point of the chord $=\left(\frac{2}{2}, \frac{4}{2}\right)=(1,2)$ and
length of chord $=\sqrt{(2)^2+(4)^2}=\sqrt{20}$ is the diameter of required circle.

Hence equation of required circle is $(x-1)+(y-2)=\left(\frac{\sqrt{20}}{2}\right)^2$ i.e. $x^2+y^2-2 x-4 y=0$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2020 (16 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The radius of the circle passing through the points $(5,7),(2,-2)$ and $(-2,0)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2 units
- **(B)** 5 units
- **(C)** 4 units
- **(D)** 3 Units

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 5 units**

**Detailed Solution:**

(B)
Let $(\mathrm{h}, \mathrm{k})$ be the center of the circle which passes through $(5,7),(2,-2)$ and $(-2,0)$ $\therefore(h-2)^{2}+(k+2)^{2}=(h+2)^{2}+k^{2}$
$\therefore-4 \mathrm{~h}+4+4 \mathrm{k}+4=4 \mathrm{~h}+4 \quad \Rightarrow 8 \mathrm{~h}-4 \mathrm{k}=4 \quad \Rightarrow 2 \mathrm{~h}-\mathrm{k}=1$...(1)
Also $(\mathrm{h}-5)^{2}+(\mathrm{k}-7)^{2}=(\mathrm{h}+2)^{2}+\mathrm{k}^{2}$
$\therefore-10 \mathrm{~h}+25-14 \mathrm{k}+49=4 \mathrm{~h}+4 \Rightarrow 14 \mathrm{~h}+14 \mathrm{k}=70 \Rightarrow \mathrm{h}+\mathrm{k}=5$...(2)
Solving $(1)$ and $(2)$, we get, $\mathrm{h}=2, \mathrm{k}=3 \Rightarrow$ centre $=(2,3)$
$\therefore$ Radius $=3-(-2)=5$

</details>
