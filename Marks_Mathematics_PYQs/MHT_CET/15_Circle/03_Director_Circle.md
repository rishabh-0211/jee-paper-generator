### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The equation of the director circle of the circle $x^2 + y^2 + 3x - 8y - 3 = 0$ is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4x^2 + 4y^2 + 12x - 32y - 12 = 0$
- **(B)** $4x^2 + 4y^2 + 12x - 32y - 97 = 0$
- **(C)** $x^2 + y^2 + 12x - 32y - \dfrac{97}{4} = 0$
- **(D)** $x^2 + y^2 + 3x - 8y - 12 = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $4x^2 + 4y^2 + 12x - 32y - 97 = 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given circle is $x^2 + y^2 + 3x - 8y - 3 = 0$.

The center of the circle is $\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}, 4\right)$ and its radius is $r = \sqrt{\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 + (-4)^2 - (-3)} = \sqrt{\dfrac{9}{4} + 19} = \sqrt{\dfrac{85}{4}}$.

The director circle of a given circle is concentric with it and has a radius equal to $\sqrt{2}$ times the radius of the given circle.

Radius of the director circle, $R = \sqrt{2}r = \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{\dfrac{85}{4}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{85}{2}}$.

The equation of the director circle is $\left(x + \dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = R^2$

$x^2 + 3x + \dfrac{9}{4} + y^2 - 8y + 16 = \dfrac{85}{2}$

$x^2 + y^2 + 3x - 8y + \dfrac{73}{4} - \dfrac{170}{4} = 0$

$x^2 + y^2 + 3x - 8y - \dfrac{97}{4} = 0$

$4x^2 + 4y^2 + 12x - 32y - 97 = 0$

Answer: $4x^2 + 4y^2 + 12x - 32y - 97 = 0$
