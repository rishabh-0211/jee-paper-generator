### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (20 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The digit present at the unit place of the sum $(1! + 2! + 3! + 4! + \ldots + 2026!)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $0$
- **(B)** $1$
- **(C)** $2$
- **(D)** $3$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $3$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The unit digit of a number is its remainder when divided by $10$.

For $n \ge 5$, $n!$ contains both $2$ and $5$ as factors, so $n!$ is a multiple of $10$.

Thus, the unit digit of $n!$ for $n \ge 5$ is $0$.

The unit digit of the given sum is determined by the sum of the first four terms:

$1! + 2! + 3! + 4! = 1 + 2 + 6 + 24 = 33$

The unit digit of $33$ is $3$.

Therefore, the unit digit of the sum $(1! + 2! + 3! + 4! + \ldots + 2026!)$ is $3$.

Answer: $3$
