### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the combined equation of angle bisectors of the lines $x^2 - 2pxy - y^2 = 0$ is $x^2 - 2qxy - y^2 = 0$, then which of the following is true?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2p + q = 0$
- **(B)** $2p + 3q = 0$
- **(C)** $pq = 1$
- **(D)** $pq + 1 = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $pq + 1 = 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the pair of lines is $x^2 - 2pxy - y^2 = 0$.

Comparing this with the standard equation $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$, we get $a = 1$, $h = -p$, and $b = -1$.

The combined equation of the angle bisectors is given by the formula $\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{a - b} = \dfrac{xy}{h}$.

Substituting the values of $a$, $h$, and $b$, we get:

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{1 - (-1)} = \dfrac{xy}{-p}$

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{2} = \dfrac{xy}{-p}$

$-p(x^2 - y^2) = 2xy$

$px^2 + 2xy - py^2 = 0$

Dividing the entire equation by $p$, we obtain:

$x^2 + \dfrac{2}{p}xy - y^2 = 0$

It is given that the combined equation of the angle bisectors is $x^2 - 2qxy - y^2 = 0$.

Comparing the coefficients of $xy$ in both equations, we get:

$-2q = \dfrac{2}{p}$

$-q = \dfrac{1}{p}$

$pq = -1$

$pq + 1 = 0$

Answer: $pq + 1 = 0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2025 (25 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The joint equation of the bisector of the angle between the lines $2 x^2+11 x y+3 y^2=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $11 x^2+2 x y-11 y^2=0$
- **(B)** $x^2+2 x y-y^2=0$
- **(C)** $3 x^2-11 x y+2 y^2=0$
- **(D)** $11 x^2-2 x y-11 y^2=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $11 x^2+2 x y-11 y^2=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Comparing the given equation $2x^2 + 11xy + 3y^2 = 0$ with the general equation $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$, we get:

$a = 2$

$2h = 11 \Rightarrow h = \dfrac{11}{2}$

$b = 3$

The joint equation of the bisectors of the angles between the lines is given by the formula:

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{a - b} = \dfrac{xy}{h}$

Substituting the values of $a$, $b$, and $h$, we get:

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{2 - 3} = \dfrac{xy}{\dfrac{11}{2}}$

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{-1} = \dfrac{2xy}{11}$

$11(x^2 - y^2) = -2xy$

$11x^2 - 11y^2 = -2xy$

$11x^2 + 2xy - 11y^2 = 0$

Answer: $11 x^2+2 x y-11 y^2=0$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The equation of pair of lines $y=\mathrm{p} x$ and $y=\mathrm{q} x$ can be written as $(y-\mathrm{p} x)(y-\mathrm{q} x)=0$. Then the equation of the pair of the angle bisectors of the lines $x^2-4 x y-5 y^2=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^2-3 x y+y^2=0$
- **(B)** $x^2+4 x y-y^2=0$
- **(C)** $x^2-3 x y-y^2=0$
- **(D)** $x^2+3 x y-y^2=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $x^2+3 x y-y^2=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the pair of lines is $x^2 - 4xy - 5y^2 = 0$.

Comparing this with the standard equation of a pair of straight lines passing through the origin, $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$, we get:
$a = 1$
$2h = -4 \Rightarrow h = -2$
$b = -5$

The equation of the pair of angle bisectors for the lines $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$ is given by the formula:
$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{a - b} = \dfrac{xy}{h}$

Substituting the values of $a$, $h$, and $b$ into the formula:
$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{1 - (-5)} = \dfrac{xy}{-2}$

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{6} = \dfrac{xy}{-2}$

$\dfrac{x^2 - y^2}{3} = \dfrac{xy}{-1}$

$-(x^2 - y^2) = 3xy$

$-x^2 + y^2 = 3xy$

$x^2 + 3xy - y^2 = 0$

Answer: $x^2+3 x y-y^2=0$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2021 (23 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If one of the lines given by $k x^2+x y-y^2=0$ bisect the angle between the co-ordinate axes, then the values of $\mathrm{k}$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1 and 2
- **(B)** 0 and 2
- **(C)** 0 and -2
- **(D)** -1 and 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 0 and 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{kx}^2+\mathrm{xy}-\mathrm{y}^2=0 \\
& \therefore \mathrm{k}+\left(\frac{\mathrm{x}}{\mathrm{y}}\right)-\left(\frac{\mathrm{y}}{\mathrm{x}}\right)^2=0
\end{aligned}
$$
Slope of line is \pm 1
$$
\therefore \mathrm{k}+1-1=0 \text { or } \mathrm{k}-1-1=0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{k}=0,2
$$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the equation $3 x^2-k x y-3 y^2=0$ represents the bisectors of angles between the lines $x^2-3 x y-4 y^2=0$, then value of $k$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** -6
- **(B)** -10
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 10

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) -10**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $x^2-3 x y-4 y^2=0$ and comparing it with standard equation, we write
$$
\mathrm{A}=1, \mathrm{H}=\frac{-3}{2}, \mathrm{~B}=-4
$$
Equation of bisector of angle of this line is
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{x^2-y^2}{A-B}=\frac{x y}{H} \Rightarrow \frac{x^2-y^2}{1+4}=\frac{x y}{\left(\frac{-3}{2}\right)} \\
& \therefore-3 x^2+3 y^2=10 x y \Rightarrow 3 x^2+10 x y-3 y^2=0
\end{aligned}
$$
Comparing with given equation, we get $\mathrm{k}=-10$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $x^{2}-2 p x y-y^{2}=0$ and $x^{2}-2 q x y-y^{2}=0$
bisect angles between each other, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $p+q=1$
- **(B)** $p q=1$
- **(C)** $p q+1=0$
- **(D)** $p^{2}+p q+q^{2}=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $p q+1=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the bisectors of the angles between the lines $x^{2}-2 p x y-y^{2}=0$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{1-(-1)}=\frac{x y}{-p} \\
\Rightarrow & \frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{2}=\frac{-x y}{p} \\
\Rightarrow p x^{2}+2 x y-p y^{2}=0
\end{aligned}
$$
This is same as $x^{2}-2 q x y-y^{2}=0$. Therefore
$$
\frac{p}{1}=\frac{2}{-2 q}=-\frac{p}{-1}
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad p q+1=0
$$

</details>
