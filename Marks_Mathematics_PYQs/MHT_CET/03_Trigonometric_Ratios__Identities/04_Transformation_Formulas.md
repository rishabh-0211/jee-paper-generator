### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\cos A = \dfrac{3}{4}$, then the value of $2 \sin \dfrac{A}{2} \cdot \sin \dfrac{5A}{2} = ...$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{11}{16}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{16}$
- **(C)** $-\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{16}$
- **(D)** $-\dfrac{11}{16}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\dfrac{11}{16}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using the trigonometric identity $2 \sin x \sin y = \cos(x - y) - \cos(x + y)$, we have:

$2 \sin \dfrac{A}{2} \cdot \sin \dfrac{5A}{2} = \cos\left(\dfrac{5A}{2} - \dfrac{A}{2}\right) - \cos\left(\dfrac{5A}{2} + \dfrac{A}{2}\right)$

$= \cos 2A - \cos 3A$

Using the multiple angle formulas:

$\cos 2A = 2 \cos^2 A - 1$

$\cos 3A = 4 \cos^3 A - 3 \cos A$

Substituting $\cos A = \dfrac{3}{4}$:

$\cos 2A = 2 \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2 - 1 = 2 \left(\dfrac{9}{16}\right) - 1 = \dfrac{9}{8} - 1 = \dfrac{1}{8}$

$\cos 3A = 4 \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3 - 3 \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right) = 4 \left(\dfrac{27}{64}\right) - \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{27}{16} - \dfrac{36}{16} = -\dfrac{9}{16}$

Therefore, the value of the given expression is:

$\cos 2A - \cos 3A = \dfrac{1}{8} - \left(-\dfrac{9}{16}\right) = \dfrac{2}{16} + \dfrac{9}{16} = \dfrac{11}{16}$

Answer: $\dfrac{11}{16}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}=225^{\circ}$, then $\frac{\cot \mathrm{A}}{1+\cot \mathrm{A}} \cdot \frac{\cot \mathrm{B}}{1+\cot \mathrm{B}}$, if it exists, is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** 2
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $A + B = 225^{\circ}$

Taking $\cot$ on both sides, we get:

$\cot(A + B) = \cot 225^{\circ}$

$\dfrac{\cot A \cot B - 1}{\cot A + \cot B} = 1$

$\cot A \cot B - 1 = \cot A + \cot B$

$\cot A + \cot B = \cot A \cot B - 1$

Consider the expression:

$\dfrac{\cot A}{1 + \cot A} \cdot \dfrac{\cot B}{1 + \cot B}$

$= \dfrac{\cot A \cot B}{(1 + \cot A)(1 + \cot B)}$

$= \dfrac{\cot A \cot B}{1 + \cot A + \cot B + \cot A \cot B}$

Substituting $\cot A + \cot B = \cot A \cot B - 1$ in the denominator:

$= \dfrac{\cot A \cot B}{1 + (\cot A \cot B - 1) + \cot A \cot B}$

$= \dfrac{\cot A \cot B}{2 \cot A \cot B}$

$= \dfrac{1}{2}$

Answer: $\frac{1}{2}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2023 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $(1+\sqrt{1+x}) \tan x=1+\sqrt{1-x}$, then $\sin 4 x$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x$
- **(B)** $-x$
- **(C)** $4 x$
- **(D)** $-4 x$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$(1+\sqrt{1+x}) \tan x=1+\sqrt{1-x}$
$\Rightarrow \tan x=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-x}}{1+\sqrt{1+x}}$
Put $x=\sin \theta$
$\therefore \quad \tan x=\frac{1+\sqrt{1-\sin \theta}}{1+\sqrt{1+\sin \theta}}$
$\begin{aligned} & =\frac{1+\sqrt{\left(\cos \frac{\theta}{2}-\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\right)^2}}{1+\sqrt{\left(\cos \frac{\theta}{2}+\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\right)^2}} \\ & =\frac{1+\cos \frac{\theta}{2}-\sin \frac{\theta}{2}}{1+\cos \frac{\theta}{2}+\sin \frac{\theta}{2}} \\ & =\frac{2 \cos ^2 \frac{\theta}{4}-2 \sin \frac{\theta}{4} \cos \frac{\theta}{4}}{2 \cos ^2 \frac{\theta}{4}+2 \sin \frac{\theta}{4} \cos \frac{\theta}{4}} \\ & =\frac{2 \cos ^2 \frac{\theta}{4}\left(1-\tan \frac{\theta}{4}\right)}{2 \cos ^2 \frac{\theta}{4}\left(1+\tan \frac{\theta}{4}\right)}\end{aligned}$
$\therefore \quad \tan x=\tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{\theta}{4}\right) \quad \ldots\left[\because \tan \frac{\pi}{4}=1\right]$
$\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow x=\frac{\pi-\theta}{4} \\ & \Rightarrow \sin 4 x=\sin (\pi-\theta)=\sin \theta=x\end{aligned}$

</details>

$\frac{\sin A+\sin 7 A+\sin 13 A}{\cos A+\cos 7 A+\cos 13 A}=$

- **(C)** $\tan 7 A$
- **(D)** $\cot 6 A$

### Q8. MHT CET 2020 (15 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If $\sin (y+z-x), \sin (z+x-y)$ and $\sin (x+y-z)$ are in A.P., then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \tan y=\tan x-\tan z$
- **(B)** $\tan y=\tan x+\tan z$
- **(C)** $2 \tan y=\tan x+\tan z$
- **(D)** $\tan y=\tan x-\tan z$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2 \tan y=\tan x+\tan z$**

**Detailed Solution:**

As $\sin (y+z-x), \sin (z+x-y)$ and $\sin (x+y-z)$ are in A.P.
$\therefore \sin (z+x-y)-\sin (y+z-x)=\sin (x+y-z)-\sin (z+x-y)$
$\Rightarrow 2 \cos z \sin (x-y)=2 \cos x \sin (y-z)$
$\Rightarrow \cos z \sin (x-y)=\cos x \sin (y-z)$
$\Rightarrow \frac{\cos z \sin (x-y)}{\cos x \cos y \cos z}=\frac{\cos x \sin (y-z)}{\cos x \cos y \cos z}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{\sin (x-y)}{\cos x \cos y}=\frac{\sin (y-z)}{\cos y \cos z}$
Using $\frac{\sin (A-B)}{\cos A \cos B}=\tan A-\tan B$
$\Rightarrow \tan x-\tan y=\tan y-\operatorname{taz}$
$\Rightarrow \tan y-\tan x=\tan z-\tan y$
$\therefore \tan x, \tan y \operatorname{and} \tan z \operatorname{are}$ in A.P.

</details>
