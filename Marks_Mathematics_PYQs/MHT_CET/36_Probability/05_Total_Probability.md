### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A fair n faced die is rolled repeatedly until a number less than n appears. If the mean of the number of tosses required is $\frac{\mathrm{n}}{9}$, then $\mathrm{n}=$ (where $\mathrm{n} \in \mathbb{N}$ )

**Options:**

- **(A)** 4
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** 8
- **(D)** 10

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 10**

**Detailed Solution:**

The probability of getting a number less than $n$ in a single throw of an $n$-faced die is $p = \dfrac{n-1}{n}$.

The number of tosses required to get the first success follows a geometric distribution.

The mean number of tosses required is given by $\dfrac{1}{p}$.

Mean $= \dfrac{n}{n-1}$

Given that the mean is $\dfrac{n}{9}$, we have:

$\dfrac{n}{n-1} = \dfrac{n}{9}$

Since $n \neq 0$, we get $n-1 = 9$

$n = 10$

Answer: 10

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2021 (23 Sep Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A die is thrown four times. The probability of getting perfect square in at least one throw is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{58}{61}$
- **(B)** $\frac{16}{81}$
- **(C)** $\frac{65}{81}$
- **(D)** $\frac{23}{81}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{65}{81}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From 1 to 6 , we have 1 and 4 as perfect squares.
Probability of getting perfect square is one throw of a die
$$
=\frac{2}{6}=\frac{1}{3}
$$
$\therefore$ Probability of not getting perfect square in 4 throws of a die
$$
=\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3}=\frac{16}{81}
$$
$\therefore$ Required probability $=1-\frac{16}{81}=\frac{65}{81}$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2021 (22 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

First bag contains 3 red and 5 black balls and second bag contains 6 red and 4 black balls. A ball is drawn from each bag. The probability that one ball is red and the other is black, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{41}{80}$
- **(B)** $\frac{21}{40}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3}{20}$
- **(D)** $\frac{3}{8}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{21}{40}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Probability of drawing red ball from first bag $=\frac{3}{8}$ and black ball $=\frac{5}{8}$
Similarly probability of drawing red ball from second bag $=\frac{6}{10}$ and black ball $\frac{4}{10}$
$\therefore$ Required probability $=\left(\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{4}{10}\right)+\left(\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{6}{10}\right)=\frac{12+30}{80}=\frac{21}{40}$

</details>
