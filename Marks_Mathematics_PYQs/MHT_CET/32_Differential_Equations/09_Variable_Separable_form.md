### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The general solution of the differential equation $x \cos y \mathrm{~d} y=\left(x \mathrm{e}^x \log x+\mathrm{e}^x\right) \mathrm{d} x$ is given by

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sin y=\mathrm{e}^x+\operatorname{c log} x$, where c is a constant of integration.
- **(B)** $\sin y=\mathrm{e}^x \log x+\mathrm{c}$, where c is a constant of integration.
- **(C)** $\mathrm{e}^x \sin y=\log x+\mathrm{c}$, where c is a constant of integration.
- **(D)** $\sin y=\mathrm{ce}^x+\log x$, where c is a constant of integration.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sin y=\mathrm{e}^x \log x+\mathrm{c}$, where c is a constant of integration.**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given differential equation is $x \cos y \mathrm{~d} y=\left(x \mathrm{e}^x \log x+\mathrm{e}^x\right) \mathrm{d} x$

Dividing both sides by $x$, we get

$\cos y \mathrm{~d} y = \left(\mathrm{e}^x \log x + \dfrac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x}\right) \mathrm{d} x$

Integrating both sides, we get

$\int \cos y \mathrm{~d} y = \int \mathrm{e}^x \left(\log x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right) \mathrm{d} x$

Using the standard integral $\int \mathrm{e}^x (f(x) + f'(x)) \mathrm{d} x = \mathrm{e}^x f(x) + \mathrm{c}$, where $f(x) = \log x$ and $f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$

$\sin y = \mathrm{e}^x \log x + \mathrm{c}$

Answer: $\sin y=\mathrm{e}^x \log x+\mathrm{c}$, where c is a constant of integration.
