### Q1. MHT CET 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

From point $P(8,27)$ tangents $P Q$ and $P R$ are drawn to the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$. Then, angle subtended by $Q R$ at origin is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\tan ^{-1} \frac{2 \sqrt{6}}{65}$
- **(B)** $\tan ^{-1} \frac{4 \sqrt{6}}{65}$
- **(C)** $\tan ^{-1} \frac{8 \sqrt{2}}{65}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) None of these**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of chord of contact $Q R$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 8 \cdot \frac{x}{4}+27 \cdot \frac{y}{9}=1 \\
\Rightarrow \quad & 2 x+3 y=1
\end{aligned}
$$
Now, equation of the pair of lines passing through origin and points $Q, R$ given by
$$
\begin{array}{l}
\quad\left(\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}\right)=(2 x+3 y)^{2} \\
\Rightarrow \quad 9 x^{2}+4 y^{2}=36\left(4 x^{2}+12 x y+9 y^{2}\right) \\
\Rightarrow \quad 135 x^{2}+432 x y+320 y^{2}=0
\end{array}
$$
$\therefore$ Required angle is
$$
\begin{array}{l}
=\tan ^{-1} \frac{2 \sqrt{(216)^{2}-135 \cdot 320}}{455} \\
=\tan ^{-1} \frac{8 \sqrt{2916-2700}}{455} \\
=\tan ^{-1} \frac{8 \sqrt{216}}{455} \\
=\tan ^{-1} \frac{48 \sqrt{6}}{455}
\end{array}
$$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of tangent to the curve $9 x^{2}+16 y^{2}=144$ which makes equal intercepts with coordinate axes, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x+y=5$
- **(B)** $x+y=16$
- **(C)** $x+y=15$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x+y=5$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given curve is $9 x^{2}+16 y^{2}=144$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad \frac{x^{2}}{16}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1
$$
Let the equation of tangent is $x+y=k$
$\Rightarrow \quad y=-x+k$
It is a tangent to the given curve if
$$
\begin{aligned}
& c^{2}=a^{2} m^{2}+b^{2} \\
\Rightarrow \quad & k^{2}=16(-1)^{2}+9
\end{aligned}
$$
$\Rightarrow \quad k^{2}=25$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad k=\pm 5
$$
$\therefore$ Required equations of tangent are
$$
x+y=\pm 5
$$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Tangent to the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{32}+\frac{y^{2}}{18}=1$ having slope $-\frac{3}{4}$ meet the coordinate axes in $A$ and $B$. Find the area of the $\Delta A O B$, where $O$ is the origin.

**Options:**

- **(A)** 12 sq unit
- **(B)** $8 \mathrm{sq}$ unit
- **(C)** 24 sq unit
- **(D)** 32 sq unit

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 24 sq unit**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of tangent with slope $-\frac{3}{4}$ is
$$
y=-\frac{3}{4} x+c
$$
According to condition of tengency
$\therefore$
$$
\begin{aligned}
c &=\sqrt{32 \times\left(\frac{-3}{4}\right)^{2}+18} \\
&=\sqrt{18+18} \\
&=6 \\
y &=-\frac{3}{4} x+6
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\Rightarrow 4 y+3 x=24
$$
It meets the coordinate axes in $A$ and $B$. $\therefore A \equiv(8,0)$ and $B \equiv(0,6)$
Required area $=\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6=24$ sq unit

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $4 x-3 y+k=0$ touches the ellipse $5 x^{2}+9 y^{2}=45$, then $k$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pm 3 \sqrt{21}$
- **(B)** $3 \sqrt{21}$
- **(C)** $-3 \sqrt{21}$
- **(D)** $2 \sqrt{21}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\pm 3 \sqrt{21}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

If line $4 x-3 y+k=0$ touches the ellipse $\frac{x^{2}}{9}+\frac{y^{2}}{5}=1$, then
$\frac{k}{3}=\sqrt{9 \times\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^{2}+5}=\pm \sqrt{21}$
$\Rightarrow$
$k=\pm 3 \sqrt{21}$

</details>
