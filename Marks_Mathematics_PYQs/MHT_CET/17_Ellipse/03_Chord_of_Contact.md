### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Tangents are drawn from the point $P(3,4)$ to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{9} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$ touching the ellipse at points A and B. Then length of segment AB is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{4\sqrt{2}}{5}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{16\sqrt{2}}{5}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{5}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{9\sqrt{10}}{5}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{5}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the chord of contact AB from the point $P(3, 4)$ to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{9} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$ is given by $T = 0$.

$\dfrac{3x}{9} + \dfrac{4y}{4} = 1$

$\dfrac{x}{3} + y = 1 \Rightarrow x = 3 - 3y$

To find the coordinates of points A and B, we substitute $x = 3 - 3y$ into the equation of the ellipse:

$\dfrac{(3 - 3y)^2}{9} + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$

$(1 - y)^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$

$1 - 2y + y^2 + \dfrac{y^2}{4} = 1$

$\dfrac{5y^2}{4} - 2y = 0$

$y \left( \dfrac{5y}{4} - 2 \right) = 0$

This gives $y = 0$ or $y = \dfrac{8}{5}$.

When $y = 0$, $x = 3 - 3(0) = 3$. So, one point of contact is $A(3, 0)$.

When $y = \dfrac{8}{5}$, $x = 3 - 3\left(\dfrac{8}{5}\right) = -\dfrac{9}{5}$. So, the other point of contact is $B\left(-\dfrac{9}{5}, \dfrac{8}{5}\right)$.

The length of the segment AB is the distance between $A$ and $B$:

$AB = \sqrt{\left(3 - \left(-\dfrac{9}{5}\right)\right)^2 + \left(0 - \dfrac{8}{5}\right)^2}$

$AB = \sqrt{\left(\dfrac{24}{5}\right)^2 + \left(-\dfrac{8}{5}\right)^2}$

$AB = \sqrt{\dfrac{576}{25} + \dfrac{64}{25}}$

$AB = \sqrt{\dfrac{640}{25}} = \dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{5}$

Answer: $\dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{5}$
