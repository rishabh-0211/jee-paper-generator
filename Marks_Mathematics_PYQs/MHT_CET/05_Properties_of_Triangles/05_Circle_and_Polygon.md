### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The adjacent sides of a cyclic quadrilateral are 2 and 5, and the angle between them is $60^\circ$. If the area of the quadrilateral is $4\sqrt{3}$ sq. units then the perimeter of quadrilateral (in units) is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $14$
- **(B)** $13$
- **(C)** $12$
- **(D)** $10$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $12$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the sides of the cyclic quadrilateral be $a=2$, $b=5$, $c$, and $d$.

Let the angle between $a$ and $b$ be $60^\circ$. Since the quadrilateral is cyclic, the angle between $c$ and $d$ is $180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$.

The area of the quadrilateral is the sum of the areas of the two triangles formed by the diagonal connecting the other two vertices.

$\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin 60^\circ + \dfrac{1}{2}cd\sin 120^\circ = 4\sqrt{3}$

$\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(5)\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{1}{2}cd\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 4\sqrt{3}$

$\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{cd\sqrt{3}}{4} = 4\sqrt{3}$

$10 + cd = 16 \Rightarrow cd = 6$

Let the length of the diagonal opposite to the given angles be $x$. Using the cosine rule in the first triangle:

$x^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos 60^\circ$

$x^2 = 2^2 + 5^2 - 2(2)(5)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = 19$

Applying the cosine rule to the second triangle:

$x^2 = c^2 + d^2 - 2cd\cos 120^\circ$

$19 = c^2 + d^2 - 2(6)\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

$19 = c^2 + d^2 + 6 \Rightarrow c^2 + d^2 = 13$

Using the identity $(c+d)^2 = c^2 + d^2 + 2cd$:

$(c+d)^2 = 13 + 2(6) = 25$

Since side lengths are positive, $c+d = 5$.

The perimeter of the quadrilateral is $a + b + c + d = 2 + 5 + 5 = 12$.

Answer: $12$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The centroid of tetrahedron with vertices $\mathrm{P}(5,-7,0), \mathrm{Q}(\mathrm{a}, 5,3), \mathrm{R}(4,-6, \mathrm{~b})$ and $\mathrm{S}(6, \mathrm{c}, 2)$ is $(4,-3,2)$, then the value of $2 a+3 b+c$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 15
- **(B)** -7
- **(C)** 7
- **(D)** -5

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 7**

**Detailed Solution:**

The centroid of a tetrahedron with vertices $(x_1, y_1, z_1)$, $(x_2, y_2, z_2)$, $(x_3, y_3, z_3)$, and $(x_4, y_4, z_4)$ is given by $\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4}{4}, \dfrac{y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4}{4}, \dfrac{z_1+z_2+z_3+z_4}{4}\right)$.

Equating the coordinates of the centroid:

$\dfrac{5 + a + 4 + 6}{4} = 4 \Rightarrow 15 + a = 16 \Rightarrow a = 1$

$\dfrac{-7 + 5 - 6 + c}{4} = -3 \Rightarrow -8 + c = -12 \Rightarrow c = -4$

$\dfrac{0 + 3 + b + 2}{4} = 2 \Rightarrow 5 + b = 8 \Rightarrow b = 3$

Substituting the values of $a$, $b$, and $c$ into the expression $2a + 3b + c$:

$2(1) + 3(3) + (-4) = 2 + 9 - 4 = 7$

Answer: 7

</details>
