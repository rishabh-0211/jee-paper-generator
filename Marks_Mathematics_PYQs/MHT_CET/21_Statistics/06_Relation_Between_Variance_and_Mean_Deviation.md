### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A box contains 15 green and 10 yellow balls. If 10 balls are randomly drawn, one by one, with replacement, then the variance of the number of green balls drawn is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** $\frac{6}{25}$
- **(D)** $\frac{12}{5}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{12}{5}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total number of balls in the box = $15 + 10 = 25$

Probability of drawing a green ball in a single draw, $p = \dfrac{15}{25} = \dfrac{3}{5}$

Probability of not drawing a green ball, $q = 1 - p = 1 - \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{2}{5}$

Number of trials (balls drawn), $n = 10$

Since the balls are drawn with replacement, the number of green balls drawn follows a binomial distribution.

The variance of a binomial distribution is given by $npq$.

Variance $= 10 \times \dfrac{3}{5} \times \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{60}{25} = \dfrac{12}{5}$

Answer: $\frac{12}{5}$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2023 (13 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Variance of first $2 n$ natural numbers is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{4 n^2+1}{12}$

- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{n}^2}{3}-1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{4 n^2-1}{12}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \sigma^2=\frac{1}{2 n}\left[1^2+2^2+3^2+\ldots\right. & \left.+(2 n)^2\right] \\ & -\left(\frac{1+2+3+\ldots+2 n}{2 n}\right)^2\end{aligned}$

### Q4. MHT CET 2023 (13 May Shift 1)

### Q5. MHT CET 2023 (12 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

For 20 observations of variable $x$, if $\sum\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right)=20$ and $\sum\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right)^2=100$, then the standard deviation of variable $x$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $9$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Note that standard derivation is independent of change of origin.
$\therefore \quad$ S.D. of $x_{\mathrm{i}}=$ S.D. of $\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \quad \text { S.D. of }\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right) & =\sqrt{\frac{1}{\mathrm{n}} \sum_{\mathrm{i}}^{20}\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right)^2-\left[\frac{\sum\left(x_{\mathrm{i}}-2\right)}{\mathrm{n}}\right]^2} \\
& =\sqrt{\frac{100}{20}-(1)^2} \\
& =2 \\
\Rightarrow \text { Required S.D } & =2
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q15. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the sum of mean and variance of a binomial distribution for 5 trials is 1.8 , then probability of a success is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0.2
- **(B)** 0.6
- **(C)** 0.4
- **(D)** 0.8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0.2**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $n p+n p q=1.8$, where $n=5$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \mathrm{np}(1+\mathrm{q})=1.8 \Rightarrow 5 \mathrm{p}[1+(1-\mathrm{p})]=1.8 \\
& \therefore 5 \mathrm{p}(2-\mathrm{p})=1.8 \Rightarrow 10 \mathrm{p}-5 \mathrm{p}^2=1.8 \text { i.e. } \\
& 5 \mathrm{p}^2-10 \mathrm{p}+1.8=0 \Rightarrow 5 \mathrm{p}^2-9 \mathrm{p}-\mathrm{p}+1.8=0 \\
& \therefore 5 \mathrm{p}(\mathrm{p}-1.8)-1(\mathrm{p}-1.8)=0 \Rightarrow(5 \mathrm{p}-1)(\mathrm{p}-1.8)=0 \\
& \therefore \mathrm{p}=\frac{1}{5}, 1.8 \text { but } \mathrm{p} \leq 1 \Rightarrow \mathrm{p}=\frac{1}{5}=0.2
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q16. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 1)

If 1 is added to first 10 natural numbers, then variance of the numbers so obtained is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 8.25
- **(B)** 3.87
- **(C)** 6.5
- **(D)** 2.87

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 8.25**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have to find variance of numbers $2,3,4, \ldots ., 11$.
Here $\overline{\mathrm{x}}=\frac{2+3+4+\ldots+11}{10}=\frac{65}{10}=6.5$
Variance $=\frac{\sum\left(\mathrm{x}_{\mathrm{i}}-\overline{\mathrm{x}}\right)^2}{\mathrm{n}}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{\left[(-4.5)^2+(-3.5)^2+(-2.5)^2+(-1.5)^2+(-0.5)^2+(0.5)^2+(1.5)^2+(2.5)^2+(3.5)^2+(4.5)^2\right]}{10} \\
& =\frac{2(20.25+12.25+6.25+2.25+0.25)}{10}=\frac{41.25}{5}=8.25
\end{aligned}
$$
