### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The mean and variance of seven observations are 8 and 16 respectively. If five of the observations are $2,4,10,12,14$, then the product of remaining two observations is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 45
- **(B)** 44
- **(C)** 48
- **(D)** 40

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 48**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the remaining two observations be $a$ and $b$.

Given the mean of 7 observations is 8:
$\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum x_i}{n} = 8$

$\dfrac{2 + 4 + 10 + 12 + 14 + a + b}{7} = 8$

$\dfrac{42 + a + b}{7} = 8$

$42 + a + b = 56$

$a + b = 14$

Given the variance of the 7 observations is 16:
$\sigma^2 = \dfrac{\sum x_i^2}{n} - (\bar{x})^2 = 16$

$\dfrac{2^2 + 4^2 + 10^2 + 12^2 + 14^2 + a^2 + b^2}{7} - 8^2 = 16$

$\dfrac{4 + 16 + 100 + 144 + 196 + a^2 + b^2}{7} - 64 = 16$

$\dfrac{460 + a^2 + b^2}{7} = 80$

$460 + a^2 + b^2 = 560$

$a^2 + b^2 = 100$

Using the algebraic identity $(a+b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab$:
$(14)^2 = 100 + 2ab$

$196 = 100 + 2ab$

$2ab = 96$

$ab = 48$

The product of the remaining two observations is 48.

Answer: 48

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (16 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the sum of the deviations of 50 observations from 30 is 50 , then the mean of these observations is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 30
- **(B)** 51
- **(C)** 50
- **(D)** 31

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 31**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the observations be $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{50}$.

Given that the sum of the deviations of these 50 observations from 30 is 50.

$\sum_{i=1}^{50} (x_i - 30) = 50$

$\sum_{i=1}^{50} x_i - 50 \times 30 = 50$

$\sum_{i=1}^{50} x_i - 1500 = 50$

$\sum_{i=1}^{50} x_i = 1550$

The mean of the observations is $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{50} x_i}{50}$.

$\bar{x} = \dfrac{1550}{50} = 31$

Answer: 31

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The mean of the numbers $a, b, 8,5,10$ is 6 and the variance is $6.8$. Then which of the following gives possible values of $a$ and $b$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{a}=3, \mathrm{~b}=4$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{a}=0, \mathrm{~b}=7$
- **(C)** $\mathrm{a}=5, \mathrm{~b}=2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{a}=1, \mathrm{~b}=6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{a}=3, \mathrm{~b}=4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given numbers are $a, b, 8, 5, 10$.

Mean $= \dfrac{a + b + 8 + 5 + 10}{5} = 6$

$a + b + 23 = 30$

$a + b = 7$

Variance $= \dfrac{a^2 + b^2 + 8^2 + 5^2 + 10^2}{5} - (\text{Mean})^2 = 6.8$

$\dfrac{a^2 + b^2 + 64 + 25 + 100}{5} - 36 = 6.8$

$\dfrac{a^2 + b^2 + 189}{5} = 42.8$

$a^2 + b^2 + 189 = 214$

$a^2 + b^2 = 25$

We have $a + b = 7$ and $a^2 + b^2 = 25$.

$(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2 + 2ab$

$49 = 25 + 2ab \Rightarrow 2ab = 24 \Rightarrow ab = 12$

Solving $a + b = 7$ and $ab = 12$, we get $a = 3, b = 4$ or $a = 4, b = 3$.

From the given options, $a = 3, b = 4$ is the correct choice.

Answer: $\mathrm{a}=3, \mathrm{~b}=4$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (03 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The mean of n observations is $\bar{x}$. If three observations $\mathrm{n}+1, \mathrm{n}-1,2 \mathrm{n}-1$ are added such that mean remains same, then value of n is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{2 \bar{x}+1}{3}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{3 \bar{x}-1}{4}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{3 \bar{x}+1}{4}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{\vec{x}+1}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\dfrac{3 \bar{x}+1}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The sum of the initial $n$ observations is $n\bar{x}$.

Three new observations $n+1$, $n-1$, and $2n-1$ are added. The sum of these three observations is:

$(n+1) + (n-1) + (2n-1) = 4n-1$

The new sum of all $n+3$ observations is $n\bar{x} + 4n - 1$.

Since the new mean remains $\bar{x}$, we have:

$\dfrac{n\bar{x} + 4n - 1}{n+3} = \bar{x}$

$n\bar{x} + 4n - 1 = \bar{x}(n+3)$

$n\bar{x} + 4n - 1 = n\bar{x} + 3\bar{x}$

$4n - 1 = 3\bar{x}$

$n = \dfrac{3\bar{x} + 1}{4}$

Answer: $\dfrac{3 \bar{x}+1}{4}$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2023 (09 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Two cards are drawn successively with replacement from a well shuffled pack of 52 cards, then mean of number of queens is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{13}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{169}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2}{13}$
- **(D)** $\frac{4}{169}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{2}{13}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total number of cards $=52$
Total number of queens $=4$
Probability of getting a queen
$P(\text { queen })=\frac{4}{52}=\frac{1}{13}$
Probability of not getting a queen
$\mathrm{P}(\text { non queen })=\frac{48}{52}=\frac{12}{13}$
Let $\mathrm{X}$ be a random variable such that $\mathrm{X}=$ number of queens in 2 draws
Case I: No queens are drawn $(X=0)$ $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=0)=\mathrm{P}($ non queen $) \times \mathrm{P}($ non queen $)$
$=\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{12}{13}=\frac{144}{169}$
 Case II: One queen is drawn $(\mathrm{X}=1)$ $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=1)=\mathrm{P}($ non queen and queen $)$ or $\mathrm{P}($ queen and non queen)
$\begin{aligned}
& =\frac{12}{13} \times \frac{1}{13}+\frac{1}{13} \times \frac{12}{13} \\
& =\frac{24}{169}
\end{aligned}$
Case III: Two queens are drawn $(\mathrm{X}=2)$
$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=2) & =\mathrm{P}(\text { queen }) \times \mathrm{P}(\text { queen }) \\
& =\frac{1}{13} \times \frac{1}{13} \\
& =\frac{1}{169}
\end{aligned}$
Required Mean is
$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E}(\mathrm{X}) & =\sum x \cdot \mathrm{P}(x) \\
& =0 \times \frac{144}{169}+1 \times \frac{24}{169}+2 \times \frac{1}{169} \\
& =\frac{26}{169} \\
\mathrm{E}(\mathrm{X}) & =\frac{2}{13}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q6. MHT CET 2022 (05 Aug Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The means of 5 observations is 4.4 and variance is 8.24 . If three of the five observations are 1,2 and 6 , then the values of other two observations are

**Options:**

- **(A)** 5,7
- **(B)** 4,9
- **(C)** 3,9
- **(D)** 4,8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 4,9**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the two observation be $\mathrm{x}$ and $\mathrm{y}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 1+2+6+x+y=5 \times 4.4=22 \\
& \Rightarrow x+y=13
\end{aligned}
$$
Also $\frac{1^2+2^2+6^2+x^2+y^2}{5}-(4.4)^2=8.24$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \frac{41+x^2+y^2}{5}=8.24+19.36=27.60 \\
& \Rightarrow x^2+y^2=138-41 \\
& \Rightarrow x^2+(13-x)^2=97 \\
& \Rightarrow x^2-13 x+36=0 \\
& \Rightarrow x=4,9
\end{aligned}
$$
$\Rightarrow y=9,4$

</details>
