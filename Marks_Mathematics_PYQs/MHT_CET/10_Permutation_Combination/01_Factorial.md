### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (12 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\dfrac{(n+3)!}{(n^2-4)(n+1)!}=\dfrac{11}{6}$, for $n\in N, n\neq 2$, then the value of $3n-20$ is equal to ...

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8$
- **(B)** $10$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given equation is $\dfrac{(n+3)!}{(n^2-4)(n+1)!} = \dfrac{11}{6}$

Expanding the factorial in the numerator and factoring the denominator:

$\dfrac{(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)!}{(n-2)(n+2)(n+1)!} = \dfrac{11}{6}$

Since $n \in N$ and $n \neq 2$, we can cancel $(n+2)$ and $(n+1)!$ from the numerator and denominator:

$\dfrac{n+3}{n-2} = \dfrac{11}{6}$

Cross-multiplying gives:

$6(n+3) = 11(n-2)$

$6n + 18 = 11n - 22$

$5n = 40$

$n = 8$

Substituting $n = 8$ into the required expression:

$3n - 20 = 3(8) - 20 = 24 - 20 = 4$

Answer: $4$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2021 (20 Sep Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\frac{\mathrm{n} !}{2 !(\mathrm{n}-2) !}$ and $\frac{\mathrm{n} !}{4 !(\mathrm{n}-4) !}$ are in the ratio $2: 1$, then $\mathrm{n}=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 5
- **(D)** 3

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 5**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $\frac{n !}{2 !(n-2) !} \times \frac{4 !(n-4) !}{n !}=\frac{2}{1}$
$$
\therefore \frac{(4 \times 3)}{(n-2)(n-3)}=2 \Rightarrow n^2-5 n+6=6
$$

</details>
