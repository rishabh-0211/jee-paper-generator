### Q5. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The chances to fail in Physics are $20 \%$ and the chances to fail in Mathematics are $10 \% .$ What are the chances to fail in at least one subject?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $28 \%$
- **(B)** $38 \%$
- **(C)** $72 \%$
- **(D)** $82 \%$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $28 \%$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $P(A)=\frac{20}{100}=\frac{1}{5}, P(B)=\frac{10}{100}=\frac{1}{10}$
Since, event are independent and we have to
$$
\text { find } \begin{aligned}
P(A&\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A) \cdot P(B) \\
&=\frac{1}{5}+\frac{1}{10}-\frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{10} \\
&=\frac{3}{10}-\frac{1}{50}=\frac{14}{50}=\frac{14}{50} \times 100=28 \%
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
