### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the line $12x - 5y + k = 0$ is tangent to the hyperbola $x^2 - 4y^2 = 100$, then $k =$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pm \sqrt{551}$
- **(B)** $\pm 3\sqrt{551}$
- **(C)** $\pm 5\sqrt{551}$
- **(D)** $\pm 7\sqrt{551}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\pm 5\sqrt{551}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the hyperbola is $x^2 - 4y^2 = 100$, which can be rewritten in standard form as:

$\dfrac{x^2}{100} - \dfrac{y^2}{25} = 1$

Here, $a^2 = 100$ and $b^2 = 25$.

The given equation of the line is $12x - 5y + k = 0$, which can be rewritten in the slope-intercept form $y = mx + c$ as:

$y = \dfrac{12}{5}x + \dfrac{k}{5}$

Here, $m = \dfrac{12}{5}$ and $c = \dfrac{k}{5}$.

The condition for a line $y = mx + c$ to be tangent to the hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ is:

$c^2 = a^2m^2 - b^2$

Substituting the values of $a^2$, $b^2$, $m$, and $c$, we get:

$\left(\dfrac{k}{5}\right)^2 = 100\left(\dfrac{12}{5}\right)^2 - 25$

$\dfrac{k^2}{25} = 100\left(\dfrac{144}{25}\right) - 25$

$\dfrac{k^2}{25} = 4(144) - 25$

$\dfrac{k^2}{25} = 576 - 25$

$\dfrac{k^2}{25} = 551$

$k^2 = 25 \times 551$

$k = \pm 5\sqrt{551}$

Answer: $\pm 5\sqrt{551}$

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the line $y = 2x + \lambda$ is a tangent to the hyperbola $36x^2 - 25y^2 = 3600$, then $\lambda =$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pm 36$
- **(B)** $\pm 25$
- **(C)** $\pm 16$
- **(D)** $\pm 9$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\pm 16$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the hyperbola is $36x^2 - 25y^2 = 3600$.

Dividing by $3600$, we get:

$\dfrac{x^2}{100} - \dfrac{y^2}{144} = 1$

Comparing with the standard equation $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$, we have $a^2 = 100$ and $b^2 = 144$.

The given line is $y = 2x + \lambda$. Comparing with $y = mx + c$, we have $m = 2$ and $c = \lambda$.

The condition for a line to be tangent to a hyperbola is $c^2 = a^2m^2 - b^2$.

Substituting the values, we get:

$\lambda^2 = 100(2)^2 - 144$

$\lambda^2 = 400 - 144$

$\lambda^2 = 256$

$\lambda = \pm 16$

Answer: $\pm 16$

</details>
