### Q1. MHT CET 2024 (04 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\mathrm{f}(x)=\log _{\mathrm{c}}\left(\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}\right),|x| \lt 1$, then $\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2 x}{1+x^2}\right)$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \mathrm{f}\left(x^2\right)$
- **(B)** $(\mathrm{f}(x))^2$
- **(C)** $\quad-2 \mathrm{f}(x)$
- **(D)** $2 \mathrm{f}(x)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2 \mathrm{f}(x)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\mathrm{f}(x) = \log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}\right)$

Substituting $\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}$ in place of $x$:

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = \log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\dfrac{1 - \dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}}{1 + \dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}}\right)$

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = \log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\dfrac{\dfrac{1+x^2-2x}{1+x^2}}{\dfrac{1+x^2+2x}{1+x^2}}\right)$

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = \log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\dfrac{(1-x)^2}{(1+x)^2}\right)$

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = \log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\left(\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}\right)^2\right)$

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = 2\log_{\mathrm{e}}\left(\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}\right)$

$\mathrm{f}\left(\dfrac{2x}{1+x^2}\right) = 2\mathrm{f}(x)$

Answer: $2 \mathrm{f}(x)$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2022 (06 Aug Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If the function $f(x)=x^3-3(a-2) x^2+3 a x+7$, for some $a \in R$ is increasing in $(0,1]$ and decreasing in $[0,5)$, then a root of the equation $\frac{f(x)-14}{(x-1)^2}=0(x \neq 1)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-7$
- **(B)** $-14$
- **(C)** $7$
- **(D)** $14$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $7$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & f(x)=x^3-3(a-2) x^2+3 a x+7 \\ & f^{\prime}(x)=3 x^2-6(a-2) x+3 a\end{aligned}$
$f^{\prime}(x)$ changes its behaving at $x=1$
$\begin{aligned} & f^{\prime}(1)=0 \\ & \Rightarrow 3 \times 1^2-6(a-2) \times 1+3 a=0 \\ & \Rightarrow a=b \Rightarrow f(x)=x^3-9 x^2+15 x+7 \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{f(x)-14}{(x-1)^2}=0 \Rightarrow \frac{\left(x^3-9 x^2+15 x+7\right)-14}{(x-1)^2}=0 \\ & \Rightarrow x^3-9 x^2+15 x-7=0 \\ & \Rightarrow(x-1)(x-1)(x-7) \\ & \Rightarrow x=1,1,7\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $f(x)=e^{x} g(x), g(0)=2, g^{\prime}(0)=1$, then $f^{\prime}(0)$
is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 3
- **(C)** 2
- **(D)** 0

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 3**

**Detailed Solution:**

$f^{\prime}(x)=e^{x} g^{\prime}(x)+e^{x} g(x)$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Rightarrow \quad f^{\prime}(0)=e^{0} \cdot g^{\prime}(0)+e^{0} g(0) \\
=1 \cdot 1+1 \cdot 2 \\
\left[\because\left\{g^{\prime}(0)=1 \text { and } g(0)=2\right\}\right] \\
=1+2=3
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find a polynomial $f(x)$ of degree 2 where $f(0)=8, f(1)=12, f(2)=18$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^{2}+3 x-8$
- **(B)** $x^{2}-3 x+8$
- **(C)** $2 x^{2}-x+3$
- **(D)** $x^{2}+3 x+8$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $x^{2}+3 x+8$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the polynomial is $a x^{2}+b x+c$.
Now, $f(0)=8$
$\Rightarrow$
$c=8$
$\therefore$ Equation is $a x^{2}+b x+8$.
Again, $f(1)=12 \Rightarrow a+b+8=12$
$\Rightarrow \quad a+b=4 \quad \ldots(\mathrm{i})$
and $f(2)=18$
$\Rightarrow \quad 4 a+2 b+8=18$
$\Rightarrow \quad 2 a+b=5 \quad \ldots$ (ii)
On solving Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$a=1, b=3$
$\therefore$ Required equation is $x^{2}+3 x+8$.

</details>

### Q9. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The value of $\Delta \log f(x)+\Delta^{2}\left(3^{x}\right)$ is

- **(A)** $\log \left[1+\frac{\Delta f(x)}{f(x)}\right]+4 \cdot 3^{x}$
- **(B)** $\log \left[1+\frac{\Delta f(x)}{f(x)}\right]+3^{x}$

- **(D)** $\log \left[\frac{\Delta f(x)}{1+f(x)}\right]+3^{x}$

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} \Delta & \log f(x)+\Delta^{2}\left(3^{x}\right) \\ &=\log f(x+h)-\log f(x)+(E-1)^{2} 3^{x} \\ &=\log \left[\frac{f(x+h)}{f(x)}\right]+\left(E^{2}-2 E+1\right) 3^{x} \\ &=\log \left[\frac{E f(x)]}{f(x)}\right]+E^{2}\left(3^{x}\right)-2 E\left(3^{x}\right)+3^{x} \\ &=\log \left[\frac{(1+\Delta) f(x)}{f(x)}\right]+3^{x+2}-2 \cdot 3^{x+1}+3^{x} \\ &=\log \left[1+\frac{\Delta f(x)}{f(x)}\right]+3^{x}(9-6+1) \\ &=\log \left[1+\frac{\Delta f(x)}{f(x)}\right]+4 \cdot 3^{x} \end{aligned}$
