### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the points $A(1, p, 1), B(2, 5, -1), C(q, 11, r)$ are collinear. If the line passing through these points is perpendicular to the vector $4\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + \hat{k}$, then the value of $p + q + r$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-4$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $-1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The direction ratios of the line passing through $A(1, p, 1)$ and $B(2, 5, -1)$ are given by $(2 - 1, 5 - p, -1 - 1) = (1, 5 - p, -2)$.

Since the line is perpendicular to the vector $4\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} + \hat{k}$, the dot product of their direction vectors is zero:

$1(4) + (5 - p)(-2) + (-2)(1) = 0$

$4 - 10 + 2p - 2 = 0$

$2p = 8 \Rightarrow p = 4$

Substituting $p = 4$, the direction ratios of the line are $(1, 1, -2)$.

Since the points $A, B, C$ are collinear, the direction ratios of the line segment $BC$ must be proportional to $(1, 1, -2)$. The direction ratios of $BC$ are $(q - 2, 11 - 5, r - (-1)) = (q - 2, 6, r + 1)$.

Equating the ratios:

$\dfrac{q - 2}{1} = \dfrac{6}{1} = \dfrac{r + 1}{-2}$

From $\dfrac{q - 2}{1} = 6$, we get $q = 8$.

From $\dfrac{r + 1}{-2} = 6$, we get $r + 1 = -12 \Rightarrow r = -13$.

Therefore, the value of $p + q + r$ is $4 + 8 - 13 = -1$.

Answer: $-1$
