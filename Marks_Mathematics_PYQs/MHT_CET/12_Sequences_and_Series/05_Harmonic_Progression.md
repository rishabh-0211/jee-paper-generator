### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (15 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\frac{1}{4}, \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{b}, \frac{1}{19}$ form a H.P. then the values of a and $\mathrm{b}$ are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{12}, \frac{1}{15}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{5}, \frac{1}{7}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{9}, \frac{1}{14}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{11}, \frac{1}{17}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{9}, \frac{1}{14}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{1}{4}, a, b, \frac{1}{19} \longrightarrow$ H.P.
$4, \frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{b}, 19 \rightarrow$ AP
$a=\frac{1}{9}, b=\frac{1}{14}$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2020 (13 Oct Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If for the harmonic progression, $t_{7}=\frac{1}{10}, t_{12}=\frac{1}{25}$, then $t_{20}=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{48}$
- **(B)** 49
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{49}$
- **(D)** 48

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{49}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

First term of an $\mathrm{AP}=10$ and the $12^{\text {th }}$ term $=25$. Considering corresponding AP $a+6 d=10$ and $a+11 d=25 d=3, a=-8$
$\Rightarrow T_{20}=a+19 d=8+57=49$
Hence, the $20^{\text {th }}$ term of the corresponding HP is $1 / 49$.

</details>
