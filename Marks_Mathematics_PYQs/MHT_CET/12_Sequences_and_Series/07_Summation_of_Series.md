### Q1. MHT CET 2020 (20 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The sum to 10 terms of the series $1 \times 3^{2}+2 \times 5^{2}+3 \times 7^{2}+\ldots \ldots \ldots \ldots . .$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 13,495
- **(B)** $\cdot 15,595$
- **(C)** $13,000$
- **(D)** 13,695

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 13,695**

$1 \times 3^{2}+2 \times 5^{2}+3 \times 7^{2}+$

$S=\sum_{\varepsilon=1}^{10} z(2 q+1)^{2}$
$S=\sum_{\varepsilon=1}^{10}\left(4 z^{2}+4 z+1\right) \varepsilon$

$S=\frac{4 \times 10^{2} \times 11^{2}}{4}+\frac{4 \times 10 \times 11 x}{6}$$+\frac{10 \times 11}{2}$
$S=13,695$

### Q4. MHT CET 2020 (12 Oct Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\frac{2+4+6+8-------\text { upto } n \text { terms }}{1+3+5+7-------\text { upto } n \text { terms }}=\frac{37}{36}$, then $n=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 36
- **(B)** 29
- **(C)** 23
- **(D)** 37

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 36**

**Detailed Solution:**

sum of $n$ even natural numbers $=n(n+1)$
sum of $n$ odd natural numbers $=n^{\wedge} 2$
$n(n+1) / n^{\wedge} 2=37 / 36$
$n+1 / n=37 / 36$
cross multiplication
$36(n+1)=37 n$
$36 n+36=37 n$
$37 n-36 n=36$
$n=36$

</details>
