### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The tangent to the parabola $x^2 = 2y$ at point P makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the X-axis, then the coordinates of P are:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(1, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$
- **(B)** $(2, 2)$
- **(C)** $(-2, 2)$
- **(D)** $\left(-1, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left(1, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given the equation of the parabola is $x^2 = 2y$.

Differentiating with respect to $x$, we get:

$2x = 2 \dfrac{dy}{dx}$

$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = x$

The tangent makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the X-axis, so its slope is $\tan(45^\circ) = 1$.

Therefore, $x = 1$.

Substituting $x = 1$ in the equation of the parabola:

$(1)^2 = 2y$

$y = \dfrac{1}{2}$

The coordinates of point P are $\left(1, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.

Answer: $\left(1, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (14 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the tangent to the curve $y^2 = 4x$ at $(4, 4)$ touches the circle $x^2 + y^2 - c = 0$ then the value of $5c$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $64$
- **(B)** $16$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $8$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $16$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 4x$ at the point $(x_1, y_1)$ is given by $y y_1 = 2(x + x_1)$.

Substituting $x_1 = 4$ and $y_1 = 4$, we get $4y = 2(x + 4)$, which simplifies to $x - 2y + 4 = 0$.

This line touches the circle $x^2 + y^2 - c = 0$. The center of the circle is $(0, 0)$ and its radius is $\sqrt{c}$.

For the line to be a tangent to the circle, the perpendicular distance from the center of the circle to the line must be equal to the radius.

The perpendicular distance $d = \dfrac{|0 - 2(0) + 4|}{\sqrt{1^2 + (-2)^2}} = \dfrac{4}{\sqrt{5}}$.

Equating this to the radius, we get $\sqrt{c} = \dfrac{4}{\sqrt{5}}$.

Squaring both sides, $c = \dfrac{16}{5}$.

Therefore, $5c = 16$.

Answer: $16$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2025 (26 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The angle, between the tangents drawn from the point $(1,4)$ to the parabola $\mathrm{y}^2=4 x$, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\pi}{6}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\pi}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\pi}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\pi}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\pi}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of a tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 4ax$ having slope $m$ is $y = mx + \dfrac{a}{m}$.

For the given parabola $y^2 = 4x$, we have $a = 1$. Thus, the equation of the tangent is $y = mx + \dfrac{1}{m}$.

Since the tangent passes through the point $(1, 4)$, we substitute $x = 1$ and $y = 4$ into the equation:

$4 = m(1) + \dfrac{1}{m}$

$m^2 - 4m + 1 = 0$

Let $m_1$ and $m_2$ be the roots of this quadratic equation, which represent the slopes of the two tangents.

$m_1 + m_2 = 4$

$m_1 m_2 = 1$

The angle $\theta$ between the tangents is given by $\tan \theta = \left| \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{1 + m_1 m_2} \right|$.

We know that $(m_1 - m_2)^2 = (m_1 + m_2)^2 - 4m_1 m_2 = (4)^2 - 4(1) = 12$.

$|m_1 - m_2| = \sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$

Substituting the values, we get:

$\tan \theta = \left| \dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{1 + 1} \right| = \sqrt{3}$

$\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$

Answer: $\frac{\pi}{3}$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2024 (15 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the tangent to the parabola $y^2=8 x$, which is parallel to the line $4 x-y+3=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 x-8 y+1=0$
- **(B)** $8 x-2 y+1=0$
- **(C)** $8 x+2 y+1=0$
- **(D)** $2 x-8 y-1=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $8 x-2 y+1=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the parabola is $y^2 = 8x$.

Comparing this with the standard form $y^2 = 4ax$, we get $4a = 8 \Rightarrow a = 2$.

The given line is $4x - y + 3 = 0$, which can be rewritten as $y = 4x + 3$.

The slope of this line is $m = 4$.

Since the required tangent is parallel to the given line, its slope is also $m = 4$.

The equation of the tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 4ax$ having slope $m$ is given by $y = mx + \dfrac{a}{m}$.

Substituting $a = 2$ and $m = 4$, we get:

$y = 4x + \dfrac{2}{4}$

$y = 4x + \dfrac{1}{2}$

Multiplying by $2$, we get:

$2y = 8x + 1$

$8x - 2y + 1 = 0$

Answer: $8 x-2 y+1=0$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the line $b x+m y+n=0$ is tangent to the parabola $y^{2}=4 a x$, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $m n=a l^{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{lm}=\mathrm{an}^{2}$
- **(C)** $\ln =a m^{2}$
- **(D)** None of the above

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\ln =a m^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, parabola, $y^{2}=4 a x$
$\begin{array}{lr}\Rightarrow & \text { 2y } \frac{d y}{d x}=4 a \\ \Rightarrow \quad \frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{2 a}{y}\end{array}$
which is the slope of tangent.
Given, $l x+m y+n=0$
is an equation of tangent of the parabola $y^{2}=4 a x$
$\therefore$ Slope of tangent $=-\frac{l}{m}$
From Eqs. (i) and (ii)
$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2 a}{y} &=-\frac{l}{m} \Rightarrow y=\frac{-2 a m}{l} \\ \Rightarrow \quad & y^{2}=4 a x \\ \Rightarrow \quad \frac{4 a^{2} m^{2}}{l^{2}} &=4 a x \\ \Rightarrow & x=\frac{a m^{2}}{l^{2}} \end{aligned}
$$
On putting the values of $x$ and $y$ in the following equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
b x+m y+n &=0 \\
l\left(\frac{a m^{2}}{l^{2}}\right)+m\left(\frac{-2 a m}{l}\right)+n &=0 \\
\frac{a m^{2}}{l}-\frac{2 a m^{2}}{l}+n &=0 \\
\Rightarrow \quad \quad \frac{a m^{2}}{l}=n \Rightarrow a m^{2} &=n l
\end{aligned}
$$
which is the required relation.

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2007

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation to the line touching both the parabolas $y^{2}=4 x$ and $x^{2}=-32 y$, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x+2 y+4=0$
- **(B)** $2 x+y-4=0$
- **(C)** $x-2 y-4=0$
- **(D)** $x-2 y+4=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $x-2 y+4=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of any tangent to the parabola $y^{2}=4 x$ is
$$
y=m x+\frac{1}{m}
$$
This touches the parabola $x^{2}=-32 y$, therefore the equation $x^{2}=-32\left(m x+\frac{1}{m}\right)$ has equal roots
$\therefore$
$$
(32 \mathrm{~m})^{2}=4\left(\frac{32}{\mathrm{~m}}\right)
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad 8 m^{3}=1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad m=\frac{1}{2}
$$
On putting the value of $m$ in Eq. (i), we get
$$
x-2 y+4=0
$$

</details>
