### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (22 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The line $\mathrm{y}=\mathrm{m} x+3$ is tangent to the parabola $\mathrm{y}^2=4 x$, if the value of m is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(C)** $4$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the given parabola is $y^2 = 4x$.

Comparing with the standard form $y^2 = 4ax$, we get $4a = 4 \Rightarrow a = 1$.

The equation of the given line is $y = mx + 3$.

Comparing with $y = mx + c$, we get $c = 3$.

The condition for the line $y = mx + c$ to be a tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 4ax$ is $c = \dfrac{a}{m}$.

Substituting the values of $a$ and $c$, we get $3 = \dfrac{1}{m}$.

Therefore, $m = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

Answer: $\frac{1}{3}$
