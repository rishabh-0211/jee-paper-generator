### Q1. MHT CET 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The focal distance of a point $P$ on the parabola $y^{2}=12 x$ if the ordinate of $P$ is 6, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 12
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 9

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 6**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given parabola is $y^{2}=12 x$
Here $a=3$
For point $P(x, y), y=6$
This point lie on the parabola $\therefore$
$$
(6)^{2}=12 x \Rightarrow x=3
$$
Now, focal distance of point $P$ is $x+a=6$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2008

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The focal distance of a point on the parabola $y^{2}=16 x$ whose ordinate is twice the abscissa, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 6
- **(B)** 8
- **(C)** 10
- **(D)** 12

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 8**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given curve is $y^{2}=16 x$. Let the point be $(h, k)$. But $2 h=k$, then $k^{2}=16 h$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Rightarrow & & 4 h^{2} &=16 h \\
\Rightarrow & & h &=0, h=4 \\
\Rightarrow & & k &=0, k=8
\end{aligned}
$$
$\therefore$ Points are $(0,0),(4,8)$.
Hence, focal distance are respectively
$$
0+4=4,4+4=8
$$
$(\because$ focal distance $=h-a)$

</details>
