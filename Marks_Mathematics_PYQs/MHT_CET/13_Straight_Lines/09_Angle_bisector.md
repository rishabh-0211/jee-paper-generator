### Q1. MHT CET 2025 (21 Apr Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The joint equation of the bisectors of the angles between the line $x=5$ and $\mathrm{y}=3$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x^2-y^2-10 x+6 y+16=0$
- **(B)** $x^2+y^2-10 x-6 y-16=0$
- **(C)** $x^2+y^2+10 x+6 y-16=0$
- **(D)** $x^2+y^2-5 x-2 y-7=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x^2-y^2-10 x+6 y+16=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given lines are $x - 5 = 0$ and $y - 3 = 0$.

The equations of the bisectors of the angles between the lines are given by:

$\dfrac{x - 5}{\sqrt{1^2 + 0^2}} = \pm \dfrac{y - 3}{\sqrt{0^2 + 1^2}}$

$x - 5 = \pm (y - 3)$

The joint equation of the bisectors is:

$(x - 5)^2 = (y - 3)^2$

$(x - 5)^2 - (y - 3)^2 = 0$

$(x^2 - 10x + 25) - (y^2 - 6y + 9) = 0$

$x^2 - y^2 - 10x + 6y + 16 = 0$

Answer: $x^2-y^2-10 x+6 y+16=0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2024 (11 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $P \equiv(-5,0), Q \equiv(0,0)$ and $R \equiv(2,2 \sqrt{3})$ be three points. Then the equation of the bisector of the angle $P Q R$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} y=0$
- **(B)** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} x-y=0$
- **(C)** $x+\sqrt{3} y=0$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{3} x+y=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\sqrt{3} x+y=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The coordinates of the given points are $P(-5,0)$, $Q(0,0)$, and $R(2,2\sqrt{3})$.

The angle bisector of $\angle PQR$ passes through the origin $Q(0,0)$. The direction of the angle bisector is along the sum of the unit vectors of $\overrightarrow{QP}$ and $\overrightarrow{QR}$.

The vector $\overrightarrow{QP} = -5\hat{i}$.
The unit vector along $\overrightarrow{QP}$ is $\hat{n}_1 = -\hat{i}$.

The vector $\overrightarrow{QR} = 2\hat{i} + 2\sqrt{3}\hat{j}$.
The magnitude of $\overrightarrow{QR}$ is $\sqrt{2^2 + (2\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{4 + 12} = 4$.
The unit vector along $\overrightarrow{QR}$ is $\hat{n}_2 = \dfrac{2\hat{i} + 2\sqrt{3}\hat{j}}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}\hat{i} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{j}$.

The vector along the angle bisector of $\angle PQR$ is given by:
$\hat{n}_1 + \hat{n}_2 = -\hat{i} + \dfrac{1}{2}\hat{i} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{j} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\hat{i} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\hat{j}$

The slope of the angle bisector is $m = \dfrac{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}{-\dfrac{1}{2}} = -\sqrt{3}$.

The equation of the angle bisector passing through $Q(0,0)$ is:
$y - 0 = -\sqrt{3}(x - 0)$

$\sqrt{3}x + y = 0$

Answer: $\sqrt{3} x+y=0$

</details>
