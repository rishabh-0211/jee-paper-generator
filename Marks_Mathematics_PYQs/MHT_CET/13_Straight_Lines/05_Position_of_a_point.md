### Q1. MHT CET 2023 (10 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The vertices of the feasible region for the constraints $x+y \leq 4, x \leq 2, y \leq 1, x+y \geq 1, x$, $y \geq 0$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** (1,0),(2,0),(2,1),(0,4)
- **(B)** (0,1),(4,0),(0,4),(1,0)
- **(C)** (1,0),(2,0),(2,1),(0,1)
- **(D)** (1,0),(4,0),(2,1),(0,4)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) (1,0),(2,0),(2,1),(0,1)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Feasible region lies on origin side of $x+y=4$, $x=2, y=1$ and non-origin side of $x+y=1$ and in first quadrant.
$\therefore \quad$ Vertices of feasible region are $\mathrm{A}(1,0), \mathrm{B}(2,0), \mathrm{C}(2,1), \mathrm{D}(0,1)$
