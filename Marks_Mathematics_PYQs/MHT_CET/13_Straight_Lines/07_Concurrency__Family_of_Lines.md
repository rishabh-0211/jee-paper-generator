### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 May Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the line which passes through the point of intersection of lines $x + 2y + 6 = 0$, $2x - y = 2$ and which makes intercept 5 on Y - axis is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$
- **(B)** $39x + 2y + 10 = 0$
- **(C)** $39x + 2y - 10 = 0$
- **(D)** $39x - 2y - 10 = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given lines are $x + 2y + 6 = 0$ and $2x - y - 2 = 0$.

Solving these two equations, we multiply the second equation by 2 and add it to the first equation:

$x + 2y + 6 + 4x - 2y - 4 = 0$

$5x + 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -\dfrac{2}{5}$

Substituting the value of $x$ in $y = 2x - 2$, we get:

$y = 2\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) - 2 = -\dfrac{14}{5}$

The point of intersection is $\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}, -\dfrac{14}{5}\right)$.

The required line makes an intercept of 5 on the Y-axis, which means it passes through the point $(0, 5)$.

The slope of the line passing through $\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}, -\dfrac{14}{5}\right)$ and $(0, 5)$ is:

$m = \dfrac{5 - \left(-\dfrac{14}{5}\right)}{0 - \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)} = \dfrac{\dfrac{39}{5}}{\dfrac{2}{5}} = \dfrac{39}{2}$

Using the slope-intercept form $y = mx + c$, the equation of the line is:

$y = \dfrac{39}{2}x + 5$

Multiplying by 2, we get:

$2y = 39x + 10$

$39x - 2y + 10 = 0$

Answer: $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$

</details>

### Q2. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The family of straight lines $4ax + 3by + c = 0$ such that $a + b + c = 0$ (where a, b, c are real constants) are concurrent at the point...

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(4, 3)$
- **(B)** $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}, \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- **(C)** $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}, \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$
- **(D)** $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}, \dfrac{1}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}, \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given the family of straight lines $4ax + 3by + c = 0$ and the condition $a + b + c = 0$.

Substituting $c = -(a + b)$ into the equation of the line:

$4ax + 3by - (a + b) = 0$

$a(4x - 1) + b(3y - 1) = 0$

For the lines to be concurrent for all values of $a$ and $b$, the coefficients of $a$ and $b$ must be zero.

$4x - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{1}{4}$

$3y - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{1}{3}$

The point of concurrency is $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}, \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$.

Answer: $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}, \dfrac{1}{3}\right)$

</details>

### Q3. MHT CET 2026 (16 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The line $(2 + k)x + (1 + k)y = 5 + 7k$ passes through the fixed point for different values of k. If 'd' is the distance of a fixed point from the origin, then $d^2 = \ldots$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $29$
- **(B)** $37$
- **(C)** $65$
- **(D)** $85$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $85$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given equation of the line is $(2 + k)x + (1 + k)y = 5 + 7k$.

Rearranging the terms to express it in the form $L_1 + kL_2 = 0$:

$2x + kx + y + ky - 5 - 7k = 0$

$(2x + y - 5) + k(x + y - 7) = 0$

This represents a family of lines passing through the point of intersection of the lines:

$2x + y - 5 = 0$

$x + y - 7 = 0$

Solving these two equations simultaneously, we subtract the second equation from the first:

$(2x + y - 5) - (x + y - 7) = 0$

$x + 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -2$

Substituting $x = -2$ into the second equation:

$-2 + y - 7 = 0 \Rightarrow y = 9$

The fixed point is $(-2, 9)$.

The distance $d$ of this fixed point from the origin $(0, 0)$ is given by:

$d^2 = (-2 - 0)^2 + (9 - 0)^2$

$d^2 = 4 + 81 = 85$

Answer: $85$

</details>

### Q4. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the line passing through the point of intersection of the lines $x + 2y + 6 = 0$ and $2x - y = 2$ and making an intercept 5 on the y-axis is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $39x + 2y - 10 = 0$
- **(B)** $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$
- **(C)** $x + 4y - 20 = 0$
- **(D)** $x - 4y + 20 = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Solving the given equations $x + 2y + 6 = 0$ and $2x - y = 2$.

Multiplying the second equation by $2$ and adding to the first gives $5x + 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -\dfrac{2}{5}$.

Substituting $x$ in the second equation gives $y = 2\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right) - 2 = -\dfrac{14}{5}$.

The point of intersection is $\left(-\dfrac{2}{5}, -\dfrac{14}{5}\right)$.

The required line makes an intercept of $5$ on the y-axis, so it passes through $(0, 5)$.

The slope of the line is $m = \dfrac{5 - \left(-\dfrac{14}{5}\right)}{0 - \left(-\dfrac{2}{5}\right)} = \dfrac{\dfrac{39}{5}}{\dfrac{2}{5}} = \dfrac{39}{2}$.

The equation of the line is $y = mx + c \Rightarrow y = \dfrac{39}{2}x + 5$.

Rearranging the terms gives $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$.

Answer: $39x - 2y + 10 = 0$

</details>

### Q5. MHT CET 2026 (11 April Shift 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The equation of a line passing through the point of the intersection of the lines $x - y + 1 = 0$ and $2x + 3y - 8 = 0$ and having x intercept $3$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x + y + 3 = 0$
- **(B)** $x + y - 3 = 0$
- **(C)** $x - y + 3 = 0$
- **(D)** $-x + y - 2 = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $x + y - 3 = 0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The given lines are:

$x - y + 1 = 0$

$2x + 3y - 8 = 0$

From the first equation, $y = x + 1$. Substituting this into the second equation:

$2x + 3(x + 1) - 8 = 0$

$5x - 5 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 1$

Substituting $x = 1$ into $y = x + 1$, we get $y = 2$.

The point of intersection is $(1, 2)$.

The required line has an x-intercept of $3$, which means it passes through the point $(3, 0)$.

The equation of the line passing through $(1, 2)$ and $(3, 0)$ is given by:

$y - 0 = \dfrac{2 - 0}{1 - 3}(x - 3)$

$y = -1(x - 3)$

$x + y - 3 = 0$

Answer: $x + y - 3 = 0$

</details>

### Q7. MHT CET 2023 (10 May Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The joint equation of a pair of lines passing through the origin and making an angle of $\frac{\pi}{4}$ with the line $3 x+2 y-8=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $5 x^2+24 x y-5 y^2=0$
- **(B)** $5 x^2-24 x y+5 y^2=0$
- **(C)** $5 x^2-24 x y-5 y^2=0$
- **(D)** $5 x^2+24 x y+5 y^2=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $5 x^2+24 x y-5 y^2=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The slope of line $3 x+2 y-8=0$ is
$\mathrm{m}_1=\frac{-3}{2}$
Let $\mathrm{m}$ be the slope of one of the lines making an angle $\frac{\pi}{4}$ with $3 x+2 y-8=0$
$\begin{aligned}
\therefore \quad \tan \frac{\pi}{4} & =\left|\frac{\mathrm{m}-\mathrm{m}_1}{1+\mathrm{mm}_1}\right| \\
\Rightarrow 1 & =\left|\frac{\mathrm{m}-\left(\frac{-3}{2}\right)}{1+\mathrm{m}\left(\frac{-3}{2}\right)}\right| \\
\Rightarrow 1 & =\left|\frac{2 \mathrm{~m}+3}{2-3 \mathrm{~m}}\right|
\end{aligned}$
Squaring on both sides, we get
$\begin{aligned}
& (2-3 m)^2=(2 m+3)^2 \\
& \Rightarrow 5 m^2-24 m-5=0
\end{aligned}$
This is the auxiliary equation of two lines and their joint equation is obtained by putting
$\mathrm{m}=\frac{y}{x}$
$\therefore \quad$ The joint equation of the lines is
$\begin{gathered}
5\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^2-24\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)-5=0 \\
\text { i.e., } 5 x^2+24 x y-5 y^2=0
\end{gathered}$

</details>

### Q8. MHT CET 2023 (10 May Shift 1)

The number of integral values of $p$ in the domain $[-5,5]$, such that the equation $2 x^2+4 x y-\mathrm{p} y^2+4 x+\mathrm{q} y+1=0$ represents pair of lines, are

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 7
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 8**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given equation of pair of lines is
$2 x^2+4 x y-p y^2+4 x+q y+1=0$
Comparing with $\mathrm{a} x^2+2 \mathrm{~h} x y+\mathrm{b} y^2+2 \mathrm{~g} x+2 \mathrm{f} y+\mathrm{c}=0$, we get $\mathrm{a}=2, \mathrm{~h}=2, \mathrm{~b}=-\mathrm{p}$
If the given equation represents a pair of straight lines, then
$\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{h}^2 \geq \mathrm{ab} \\
& \Rightarrow 4 \geq-2 \mathrm{p} \\
& \Rightarrow 2 \geq-\mathrm{p} \\
& \Rightarrow \mathrm{p} \geq-2
\end{aligned}$
$\therefore \quad$ Possible values of $\mathrm{p}$ from domain $[-5,5]$ are $-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4,5$.
$\therefore \quad$ Number of integral values of $p=8$
