### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (19 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Point A$(5, 12)$ rotated about the origin O in the XY-plane through an angle of $30^\circ$ in the anticlockwise direction to a new position B. The ordinate of point B is...

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6\sqrt{3} + \dfrac{5}{2}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2} - 6$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{5\sqrt{3}}{2} + 6$
- **(D)** $6\sqrt{3} - \dfrac{5}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $6\sqrt{3} + \dfrac{5}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the coordinates of point A be $(x, y) = (5, 12)$.

When a point $(x, y)$ is rotated about the origin by an angle $\theta$ in the anticlockwise direction, the new coordinates $(x', y')$ are given by:

$x' = x \cos\theta - y \sin\theta$

$y' = x \sin\theta + y \cos\theta$

Substituting $x = 5$, $y = 12$, and $\theta = 30^\circ$ to find the ordinate $y'$ of the new point B:

$y' = 5 \sin 30^\circ + 12 \cos 30^\circ$

$y' = 5 \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) + 12 \left(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$

$y' = \dfrac{5}{2} + 6\sqrt{3}$

The ordinate of point B is $6\sqrt{3} + \dfrac{5}{2}$.

Answer: $6\sqrt{3} + \dfrac{5}{2}$

</details>
