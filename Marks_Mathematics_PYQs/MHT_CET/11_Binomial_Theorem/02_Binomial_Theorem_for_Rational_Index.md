### Q1. MHT CET 2026 (15 April Shift 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The approximate value of $(0.007)^{\frac{1}{3}}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{21}{120}$
- **(B)** $\dfrac{23}{120}$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{29}{120}$
- **(D)** $\dfrac{31}{120}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\dfrac{23}{120}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $f(x) = x^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$

Let $x = 0.008$ and $\Delta x = -0.001$

$f(x) = (0.008)^{\dfrac{1}{3}} = 0.2 = \dfrac{1}{5}$

$f'(x) = \dfrac{1}{3} x^{-\dfrac{2}{3}}$

$f'(0.008) = \dfrac{1}{3(0.008)^{\dfrac{2}{3}}} = \dfrac{1}{3(0.04)} = \dfrac{1}{0.12} = \dfrac{100}{12} = \dfrac{25}{3}$

Using the approximation $f(x + \Delta x) \approx f(x) + f'(x) \Delta x$

$f(0.007) \approx \dfrac{1}{5} + \left(\dfrac{25}{3}\right)(-0.001)$

$f(0.007) \approx \dfrac{1}{5} - \dfrac{25}{3000}$

$f(0.007) \approx \dfrac{1}{5} - \dfrac{1}{120}$

$f(0.007) \approx \dfrac{24 - 1}{120} = \dfrac{23}{120}$

Answer: $\dfrac{23}{120}$
