### Q1. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The minimum value of \(\frac{x^4+y^4+z^2}{x y z}\) for positive real number \(x, y, z\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\sqrt{2}\)
- **(B)** \(2 \sqrt{2}\)
- **(C)** \(4 \sqrt{2}\)
- **(D)** \(8 \sqrt{2}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(2 \sqrt{2}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

By A.M. \(\geq\) G.M.
\(x^4+y^4 \geq 2 x^2 y^2\) and \(2 x^2 y^2+z^2 \geq \sqrt{8} x y z\).
\(\Rightarrow \frac{x^4+y^4+z^2}{x y z} \geq \sqrt{8}\)

</details>
