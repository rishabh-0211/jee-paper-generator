### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If p and q be the longest and the shortest distance respectively of the point $(-7,2)$ from any point $(\alpha, \beta)$ on the curve whose equation is $x^2+y^2-10 x-14 y-51=0$, then G.M. of p

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \sqrt{11}$
- **(B)** $5 \sqrt{5}$
- **(C)** $13$
- **(D)** $11$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $2 \sqrt{11}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The centre $C$ of the circle $=(5,7)$ and the radius
$=\sqrt{5^2+7^2+51}=5 \sqrt{5}$
$\mathrm{PC}=\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=13 \Rightarrow q=P A=13-5 \sqrt{5}$
and $p=P B=13+5 \sqrt{3}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/wf2Brex5t8NNHNPL_K5j5jggOlMntFYf9ncI-NbY-bM.original.fullsize.png"/>
$\therefore$ G.M. of $p$ and $q$
$=\sqrt{p q}=\sqrt{(13-5 \sqrt{5})(13+5 \sqrt{5})}$
$=\sqrt{169-125}=2 \sqrt{11}$.
