### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The equation of the circle with centre $(0,2)$ and radius 2 is $x^2+y^2-m y=0$. The value of $m$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 4
- **(D)** 3

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 4**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here $h=0, k=2$ and $r=2$. Therefore, the required equation of the circle is :
$(x-0)^2+(y-2)^2=(2)^2$
or $x^2+y^2-4 y+4=4$ or $x^2+y^2-4 y=0$

</details>
