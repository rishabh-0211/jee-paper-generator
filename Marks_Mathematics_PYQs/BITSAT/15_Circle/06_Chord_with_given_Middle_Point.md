### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The locus of the mid-point of the chord if contact of tangents drawn from points lying on the straight line $4 x-5 y=20$ to the circle $x^2+y^2=9$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $20\left(x^2+y^2\right)-36 x+45 y=0$
- **(B)** $20\left(x^2+y^2\right)+36 x-45 y=0$
- **(C)** $36\left(x^2+y^2\right)-20 x+45 y=0$
- **(D)** $36\left(x^2+y^2\right)+20 x-45 y=0$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $20\left(x^2+y^2\right)-36 x+45 y=0$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $P\left(t, \frac{4 t-20}{5}\right)$ be a point on the line
$4 x-5 y=20$. Then the chord of contact of tangents drawn from $P$ to the circle $x^2+y^2=9$ is
$t x+\left(\frac{4 t-20}{5}\right) y=9\quad \quad \dots(i)$
Let $\theta(h, k)$ be the mid-point of this chord of contact, then its equation is also $h x+k y=h^2+k^2\quad \quad \dots(ii)$
$\left[\right.$ using $\left.T=S^{\prime}\right]$
Clearly Eqs. (i) and (ii) represent the same line
$\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \frac{t}{h}=\frac{4 t-20}{5 k}=\frac{9}{h^2+k^2} \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{t}{h}=\frac{9}{h^2+k^2} \text { and } \frac{t}{h}=\frac{4 t-20}{5 k} \\
& \Rightarrow t=\frac{9 h}{h^2+k^2} \text { and } t=\frac{20 h}{4 h-5 k} \\
& \Rightarrow \frac{9 h}{h^2+k^2}=\frac{20 h}{4 h-5 k} \\
& \Rightarrow  h\left\{20\left(h^2+k^2\right)-36 h+45 k\right\}=0 \\
& x=0, \text { or }\left[20\left(x^2+y^2\right)-36 x+45 y\right]=0
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The length of the chord $x+y=3$ intercepted by the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x-2 y-2=0$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{7}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{14}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\sqrt{14}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The centre of the circle is \(\mathrm{C}(1,1)\) and radius of the circle is 2 , perpendicular distance from \(\mathrm{C}\) on \(\mathrm{AB}\), the chord \(\mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y}=3\)

\(\begin{aligned} &\mathrm{CD}=\left|\frac{1+1-3}{\sqrt{2}}\right|=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ &\Rightarrow \mathrm{AD}=\sqrt{4-\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{7}{2}}\left[\mathrm{AD}=\sqrt{\mathrm{AC}^{2}-\mathrm{CD}^{2}}\right] \end{aligned}\)

Hence, the length of the chord \(\mathrm{AB}=2 \mathrm{AD}=2 \sqrt{\frac{7}{2}}=\sqrt{14}\).

### Q3. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The line \(a x+b y=1\) cuts ellipse \(c x^2+d y^2=1\) only once if

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\mathrm{ca}^2+\mathrm{db}^2=1\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{\mathrm{c}}{\mathrm{a}^2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{b}^2}=1\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{\mathrm{a}^2}{\mathrm{c}}+\frac{\mathrm{b}^2}{\mathrm{~d}}=1\)
- **(D)** \(a c^2+b d^2=1\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) \(\frac{\mathrm{a}^2}{\mathrm{c}}+\frac{\mathrm{b}^2}{\mathrm{~d}}=1\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Clearly \(a x+b y=1\)
i.e \(y=-\frac{a}{b} x+\frac{1}{b}\) is tangent to
\(\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{cx}^2+\mathrm{dy}^2=1 \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{x}^2}{\frac{1}{c}}+\frac{\mathrm{y}^2}{\frac{1}{d}}=1 \\
& \therefore\left(\frac{1}{b}\right)^2=\left(\frac{1}{c}\right)\left(-\frac{a}{b}\right)^2+\left(\frac{1}{d}\right) \\
& \Rightarrow 1=\frac{a^2}{c}+\frac{b^2}{d}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
