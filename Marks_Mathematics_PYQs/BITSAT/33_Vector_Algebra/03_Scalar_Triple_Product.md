### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let $\mathbf{a}=\hat{\mathbf{i}}-\hat{\mathbf{k}}, \mathbf{b}=x \hat{\mathbf{i}}+\hat{\mathbf{j}}+(1-x) \hat{\mathbf{k}}$ and $\mathbf{c}=y \hat{\mathbf{i}}+x \hat{\mathbf{j}}+(1+x-y) \hat{\mathbf{k}}$. Then, $[\mathbf{a} \mathbf{b} \mathbf{~ c}]$ depends on

**Options:**

- **(A)** only $y$
- **(B)** only $x$
- **(C)** both $x$ and $y$
- **(D)** neither $x$ nor $y$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) neither $x$ nor $y$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here, $[a b c]=|a \cdot(b \times c)|$
Now, according to question,
$\begin{aligned}
{\left[\begin{array}{lll}
a & b c
\end{array}\right] } & =\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & 0 & -1 \\
x & 1 & 1 - x \\
y & x & 1+x-y
\end{array}\right| \\
& =1(1+x-y-x(1-x))-1\left(x^2-y\right) \\
& =1+x-y-x+x^2-x^2+y \\
& =1 \\
& =\text { Independent of } x \text { and } y .
\end{aligned}$

</details>
