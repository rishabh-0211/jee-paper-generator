### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Girl students constitute $10 \%$ of I year and $5 \%$ of II year at Roorkee University. During summer holidays $70 \%$ of the I year and $30 \%$ of II year students are given a project. The girls take turns on duty in canteen. The chance that I year girl student is on duty in a randomly selected day is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3}{17}$
- **(B)** $\frac{14}{17}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3}{10}$
- **(D)** $\frac{7}{10}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{14}{17}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The desired probability

$=\frac{\frac{10}{100} \times \frac{70}{100}}{\frac{10}{100} \times \frac{70}{100}+\frac{5}{100} \times \frac{30}{100}}=\frac{14}{17}$

</details>
