### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The vertices of the hyperbola $9 x^2-16 y^2-36 x+96 y-252=0$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(6,3),(-2,3)$
- **(B)** $(6,3),(-6,3)$
- **(C)** $(-6,3),(-6,-3)$
- **(D)** $(2,3),(-2,3)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $(6,3),(-2,3)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given hyperbola is
$\begin{aligned}
& 9 x^2-16 y^2-36 x+96 y-252=0 \\
& \Rightarrow 9\left(x^2-4 x\right)-16\left(y^2-6 y\right)=252 \\
& \Rightarrow 9\left(x^2-4 x+4\right)-16\left(y^2-6 y+9\right) \\
& =252+36-144 \\
& \Rightarrow 9(x-2)^2-16(y-3)^2=144
\end{aligned}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/jLY8-6clWoBW7M9aqoLEdPXjxPnZv_kpDKECzHGVaUY.original.fullsize.png">

Put $x=X+2$ and $y=Y+3$
$\therefore$ Equation (i) becomes $\frac{X^2}{16}-\frac{Y^2}{9}=1$
Now, vertices are $X= \pm a$ wehre $a=4$ and $Y=0$
Hence, vertices are $(6,3),(-2,3)$.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Find the eccentricity of the conic represented by $x^{2}-y^{2}-4 x+4 y+16=0$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** $\sqrt{2}$
- **(C)** $2 \sqrt{2}$
- **(D)** $3 \sqrt{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have $x^{2}-y^{2}-4 x+4 y+16=0$
$\Rightarrow\left(x^{2}-4 x\right)-\left(y^{2}-4 y\right)=16$
$\Rightarrow\left(x^{2}-4 x+4\right)-\left(y^{2}-4 y+4\right)=-16$
$\Rightarrow(x-2)^{2}-(y-2)^{2}=-16$
$\Rightarrow \frac{(x-2)^{2}}{4^{2}}-\frac{(y-2)^{2}}{4^{2}}=1$
This is rectangular hyperbola, whose eccentricity is always $\sqrt{2}$.

</details>
