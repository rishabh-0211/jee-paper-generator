### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $A=1+r^a+r^{2 a}+r^{3 a}+\ldots \infty$
and $\mathrm{B}=1+\mathrm{r}^{\mathrm{b}}+\mathrm{r}^{2 \mathrm{~b}}+\mathrm{r}^{3 \mathrm{~b}}+\ldots \infty$, then $\frac{\mathrm{a}}{\mathrm{b}}$ is
equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\log _B(A)$
- **(B)** $\log _{1-B}(1-A)$
- **(C)** $\log _{\frac{B-1}{B}}\left(\frac{A-1}{A}\right)$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\log _{\frac{B-1}{B}}\left(\frac{A-1}{A}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$A=\frac{1}{1-r^a} \Rightarrow 1-r^a=\frac{1}{A} \Rightarrow r^a=1-\frac{1}{A}=\frac{A-1}{A}$
$B=\frac{1}{1-r^b} \Rightarrow 1-r^b=\frac{1}{B} \Rightarrow r^b=1-\frac{1}{B}=\frac{B-1}{B}$
$\therefore a \log r=\log \left(\frac{A-1}{A}\right)$
and $b \log r=\log \left(\frac{B-1}{B}\right)$
$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{a}}{\mathrm{b}}=\frac{\log \left(\frac{\mathrm{A}-1}{\mathrm{~A}}\right)}{\log \left(\frac{\mathrm{B}-1}{\mathrm{~B}}\right)}=\log _{\frac{\mathrm{B}-1}{\mathrm{~B}}}\left(\frac{\mathrm{~A}-1}{\mathrm{~A}}\right)$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the sum of an infinite GP $a, a r, a r^2, a r^3, \ldots$ is 15 and the sum of the squares of its each term is 150 , then the sum of $a r^2, a r^4, a r^6, \ldots$ is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{5}{2}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{25}{2}$
- **(D)** $\frac{9}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Sum of infinite terms of series a $+a r+a r^2+$ $=15$
$\therefore \frac{a}{1-r}=15$ ...(i)
Sequence formed by square of terms: $a^2, a^2 r^2, a^2 r^4, a^2 r^6 \ldots$
$a^2+a^2 r^2+\cdots=150$
Sum $=\frac{a^2}{1-r^2}=150$
$\Rightarrow \frac{a}{1-r} \cdot \frac{a}{1+r}=15 \Rightarrow 15 \cdot \frac{a}{1+r}=150$ $\left(\because \frac{\mathrm{a}}{1-\mathrm{r}}=15\right)$
$\Rightarrow \frac{a}{1+r}=10$ ...(ii)
On dividing equation (i) by (ii)
$\frac{1+\mathrm{r}}{1-\mathrm{r}}=\frac{15}{10}$
or $r=\frac{1}{5} \Rightarrow a=12$
Now series : $a r^2+a r^4+a r^6+\ldots$
Sum $=\frac{a^2}{1-r^2}=\frac{12 \cdot\left(\frac{1}{25}\right)}{1-\frac{1}{25}}=\frac{1}{2}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Consider an infinite geometric series with first term $a$ and common ratio $r$. If its sum is 4 and the second term is $\frac{3}{4}$, then:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a=\frac{4}{7}, r=\frac{3}{7}$
- **(B)** $a=2, r=\frac{3}{8}$
- **(C)** $a=\frac{3}{2}, r=\frac{1}{2}$
- **(D)** $a=3, r=\frac{1}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $a=3, r=\frac{1}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since, sum $=4$ and second term $=\frac{3}{4}$ $\Rightarrow \frac{a}{1-r}=4$, and $a r=\frac{3}{4}$ $\Rightarrow \frac{a}{1-\frac{3}{4 a}}=4$ $\Rightarrow(a-1)(a-3)=0$ $\Rightarrow a=1$ or $a=3$

$\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow \frac{a}{1-\frac{3}{4 a}}=4 \\

\Rightarrow(a-1)(a-3)=0 \\

\Rightarrow a=1 \text { or } a=3

\end{array}$

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1

- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

$\left[\because \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{c}, \mathrm{b}\right.$ are in $\left.\mathrm{G} . \mathrm{P} . \Rightarrow \mathrm{c}^{2}=\mathrm{ab}\right]$

### Q9. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If \(A\) and \(B\) are \(2 \times 2\) matrices, then which of the following is true?

**Options:**

- **(A)** \((\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B})^2=\mathrm{A}^2+\mathrm{B}^2+2 \mathrm{AB}\)
- **(B)** \((A-B)^2=A^2+B^2-2 A B\)
- **(C)** \((A-B)(A+B)=A^2+A B-B A-B^2\)
- **(D)** \((A-B)(A+B)=A^2-B^2\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) \((A-B)(A+B)=A^2+A B-B A-B^2\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

For two \(2 \times 2\) matrices, \(A ~\&~ B\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& (A-B) \times(A+B) \\
& =A \times A+A \times B-B \times A-B \times B \\
& =A^2-B^2+A B-B A
\end{aligned}\)
Hence, \((A-B)(A+B)=A^2+A B-B A-B^2\)

</details>
