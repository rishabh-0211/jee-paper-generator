### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $a\gt0, b\gt0, c\gt0$ and $a, b, c$ are distinct, then $(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)$ is greater than

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2(a+b+c)$
- **(B)** $3(a+b+c)$
- **(C)** $6 a b c$
- **(D)** $8 a b c$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $8 a b c$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know that $\mathrm{AM} \geq \mathrm{GM}$...(i)
Now, applying Eq. (i) in $a$ and $b$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{a+b}{2} \geq \sqrt{a b}$....(ii)
applying Eq. (i) in $b$ and $c$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{b+c}{2} \geq \sqrt{b c}$....(iii)
and applying Eq. (i) in $c$ and $a$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{c+a}{2} \geq \sqrt{c a}$....(iv)
By E'qs. (ii), (iii) and (iv)
$\begin{array}{cc}
\Rightarrow & \frac{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)}{8} \geq a b c \\
\Rightarrow & (a+b)(b+c)(c+a) \geq 8 a b c
\end{array}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $a>0, b>0, c>0$ and $a, b, c$ are distinct, then $(a+$b) $(b+c)(c+a)$ is greater than

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2(a+b+c)$
- **(B)** $3(a+b+c)$
- **(C)** $6 \mathrm{abc}$
- **(D)** $8 \mathrm{abc}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $8 \mathrm{abc}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{AM}>\mathrm{GM}$

$\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}}{2}>\sqrt{\mathrm{ab}}, \frac{\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}}{2}>\sqrt{\mathrm{bc}}, \frac{\mathrm{c}+\mathrm{a}}{2}>\sqrt{\mathrm{ca}}$

So, $(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)>8 a b c$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $a, b, c$ are positive numbers, then least value of $(a+b+c)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** 9
- **(D)** None

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 9**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{AM} \geq \mathrm{HM}$

$\Rightarrow \frac{a+b+c}{3} \geq \frac{3}{\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}}$

$\Rightarrow(a+b+c)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\right) \geq 9$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The product of $n$ positive numbers is unity, then their sum is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** a positive integer
- **(B)** divisible by $n$
- **(C)** equal to $n+\frac{1}{n}$
- **(D)** never less than $n$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) never less than $n$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since, product of $n$ positive number is unity. $\Rightarrow x_{1} x_{2} x_{3} \ldots \ldots x_{n}=1$

Using A.M. $\geq$ GM

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\ldots . .+x_{n}}{n} \geq\left(x_{1} x_{2} \ldots \ldots x_{n}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} \\

\Rightarrow x_{1}+x_{n}+\ldots \ldots+x_{n} \geq n(1)^{\frac{1}{n}}\left[\text { From eq }^{\mathrm{n}}(\mathrm{i})\right]

\end{array}

$$

</details>
