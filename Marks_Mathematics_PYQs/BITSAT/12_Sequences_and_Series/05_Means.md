### Q1. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The arithmetic mean of numbers a, $\mathrm{b}, \mathrm{c}, \mathrm{d}, \mathrm{e}$ is $\mathrm{M}$. What is the value of $(a-M)+(b-M)+(c-M)+$ $(\mathrm{d}-\mathrm{M})+(\mathrm{e}-\mathrm{M}) ?$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{M}$
- **(B)** $a+b+c+d+e$
- **(C)** 0
- **(D)** $5 \mathrm{M}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $\mathrm{M}=\frac{\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}+\mathrm{d}+\mathrm{e}}{5}$

$\Rightarrow a+b+c+d+e=5 M$

$\Rightarrow a+b+c+d+e-5 m=0$

$$

\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow(a-M)+(b-M)+(c-M)+(d-M) \\

+(e-M)=0

\end{array}

$$

Hence, required value $=0$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The coefficient of \(x^{32}\) in the expansion of \(\left(x^4-\frac{1}{x^3}\right)^{15}\) is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** \({ }^{-15} \mathrm{C}_3\)
- **(B)** \({ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_4\)
- **(C)** \({ }^{-15} \mathrm{C}_5\)
- **(D)** \({ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_2\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \({ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_4\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know by Binomial expansion, that \((x+a)^{\mathrm{n}}\)
\(\begin{aligned}
&={ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_0 x^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{a}^0+{ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_1 \mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{n}-1} \cdot \mathrm{a}+{ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_2 \mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{n}-2} \mathrm{a}^2 \\
&+{ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_3 \mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{n}-3} \mathrm{a}^3+{ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{n}-4} \cdot \mathrm{a}^4+\ldots . .+{ }^{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{x}^0 a^{\mathrm{n}}
\end{aligned}\)
Given expansion is \(\left(x^4-\frac{1}{x^3}\right)^{15}\)
On comparing we get \(\mathrm{n}=15, \mathrm{x}=\mathrm{x}^4\),
\(a=\left(-\frac{1}{x^3}\right)\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& \therefore\left(x^4-\frac{1}{x^3}\right)^{15}={ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_0\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{15}\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)^0 \\
& +{ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_1\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{14}\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)+{ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_2\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{13}\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)^2 \\
& +{ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_3\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{12}\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)^3+{ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_4\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{11}\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)^4+\ldots \\
& \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{r}+1}={ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{r}}\left(\mathrm{x}^4\right)^{15-\mathrm{r}} \cdot\left(-\frac{1}{\mathrm{x}^3}\right)^{\mathrm{r}}=-{ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{r}} \mathrm{x}^{60-7 \mathrm{r}} \\
& \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}{ }^{60-7 \mathrm{r}}=\mathrm{x}^{32} \Rightarrow 60-7 \mathrm{r}=32 \\
& \Rightarrow 7 \mathrm{r}=28 \Rightarrow \mathrm{r}=4
\end{aligned}\)
So, 5 th term, contains \(\mathrm{x}^{32}\)
\(={ }^{15} C_4\left(x^4\right){ }^{11}\left(-\frac{1}{x^3}\right)^4={ }^{15} C_4 x^{44} x^{-12}\)
\(={ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_4 \mathrm{x}^{32} .\)
Thus, coefficient of \(\mathrm{x}^{32}={ }^{15} \mathrm{C}_4\).

</details>
