### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The range of $(8 \sin \theta+6 \cos \theta)^2+2$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(0,2)$
- **(B)** $[2,102]$
- **(C)** $(-\infty, \infty)$
- **(D)** $(2,1)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $[2,102]$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$-10 \leq 8 \sin \theta+6 \cos \theta \leq 10$
$\Rightarrow 0 \leq(8 \sin \theta+6 \cos \theta)^2 \leq 100$
$\Rightarrow 2 \leq(8 \sin \theta+6 \cos \theta)^2+2 \leq 102$

</details>
