### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The modulus of the complex number $z$ such that $|z+3-i|=1$ and $\arg (z)=\pi$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 9
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 3**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $z=x+i y$
Given, $\quad|z+3-i|=1$
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad|(x+3)+(y-1) i|=1 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \sqrt{(x+3)^2+(y-1)^2}=1 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(x+3)^2+(y-1)^2=1\ldots(i)
\end{aligned}$
Above is the equation of circle with centre $\equiv(-3,1)$ and
$\text { radius }=1$
Also, $\arg (z)=\pi \Rightarrow \tan ^{-1}\left|\frac{y}{x}\right|=\pi$
$\Rightarrow \quad\left|\frac{y}{x}\right|=\tan \pi=0 \Rightarrow y=0$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/0Cx_H71UrlvsAaWxnnhhCkMW9pD0N85P8K2TMcRVynI.original.fullsize.png"/>
By Eq. (i),
$\begin{aligned}
\Rightarrow & x & =-3 \\
\therefore & z & =-3+0 i \\
\Rightarrow & \text { Modulus of } z & =3
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $z_1, z_2, \ldots . . z_n$ are complex numbers such that $\left|z_1\right|=\left|z_2\right|=\ldots . .=\left|z_n\right|=1$, then $\left|z_1+z_2+\ldots . .+z_n\right|$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left|z_1 z_2 z_3 \ldots . . z_n\right|$
- **(B)** $\left|z_1\right|+\left|z_2\right|+\ldots . .+\left|z_n\right|$
- **(C)** $\left|\frac{1}{z_1}+\frac{1}{z_2}+\ldots . .+\frac{1}{z_n}\right|$
- **(D)** n

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\left|\frac{1}{z_1}+\frac{1}{z_2}+\ldots . .+\frac{1}{z_n}\right|$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left|z_1\right|=\left|z_2\right|=\left|z_3\right|=\ldots \ldots .=\left|z_n\right|=1$
$\Rightarrow z_1 \bar{z}_1=z_2 \bar{z}_2=z_3 \bar{z}_3=\ldots \ldots=z_n \bar{z}_n=1$
$\overline{\mathrm{z}}_1=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}}, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_2=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_2}, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_3=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_3}, \ldots \ldots, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{n}}}$
Now, $\left|z_1+z_2+z_3+\ldots \ldots .+z_n\right|$
$=\left|\bar{z}_1+\bar{z}_2+\ldots \ldots+\bar{z}_n\right|$
$\left|\bar{z}_1+\bar{z}_2+\bar{z}_3+\ldots .+\bar{z}_n\right|=\left|\frac{1}{z_1}+\frac{1}{z_2}+\frac{1}{z_3}+\ldots \ldots+\frac{1}{z_n}\right|$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\left|z_1\right|=2,\left|z_2\right|=3,\left|z_3\right|=4$ and $\left|2 z_1+3 z_2+4 z_3\right|=$ 4 , then absolute value of $8 z_2 z_3+27 z_3 z_1+$ $64 z_1 z_2$ equals

**Options:**

- **(A)** $24$
- **(B)** $48$
- **(C)** $72$
- **(D)** $96$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $96$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\left|8 z_2 z_3+27 z_3 z_1+64 z_1 z_2\right|=\left|z_1\right|\left|z_2\right|\left|z_3\right|$
$\left|\frac{8}{z_1}+\frac{27}{z_2}+\frac{64}{z_3}\right|$
$=(2)(3)(4)\left|\frac{8 \bar{z}_1}{\left|z_1\right|^2}+\frac{27 \bar{z}_2}{\left|z_2\right|^2}+\frac{64 \bar{z}_3}{\left|z_3\right|^2}\right|$
$=24\left|2 \bar{z}_1+3 \bar{z}_2+4 \bar{z}_3\right|$
$=24\left|\overline{2 z_1+3 z_2+4 z_3}\right|$
$=24\left|2 z_1+3 z_2+4 z_3\right|=(24)(4)=96$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The modulus of $\frac{(1+i \sqrt{3})(2+2 i)}{(\sqrt{3}-i)}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** $3 \sqrt{2}$
- **(D)** $2 \sqrt{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $2 \sqrt{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \frac{(1+i \sqrt{3})(2+2 i)}{\sqrt{3}-i}=\frac{2+2 \sqrt{3} i+2 i-2 \sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}-i} \\ & =\frac{(2-2 \sqrt{3})+(2 \sqrt{3}+2) i}{\sqrt{3}-i} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}+i}{\sqrt{3}+i} \\ & =\frac{2 \sqrt{3}-6+2 i-2 \sqrt{3} i+6 i+2 \sqrt{3} i-2 \sqrt{3}-2}{3+1} \\ & =\frac{8 i-8}{4}=-2+2 i \\ & \therefore \text { Modulus }=\sqrt{(-2)^2+(2)^2}=2 \sqrt{2}\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\left|z_{1}\right|=\left|z_{2}\right|=\ldots \ldots \ldots . .\left|z_{n}\right|=1,$ then the

value of $\left|z_{1}+z_{2}+\ldots \ldots . z_{n}\right|-$

$\left|\frac{1}{z_{1}}+\frac{1}{z_{2}}+\ldots \ldots .+\frac{1}{z_{n}}\right|$ is,

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** -1
- **(D)** None

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

$z_{1} \bar{z}_{1}=z_{2} \bar{z}_{2}=\ldots .=z_{n} \bar{z}_{n}=1$

$\Rightarrow \overline{\mathrm{z}}_{1}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_{1}}, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_{2}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_{2}}, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_{3}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_{3}}, \ldots \ldots, \overline{\mathrm{z}}_{\mathrm{n}}=\frac{1}{\mathrm{z}_{\mathrm{n}}}$

$\therefore\left|z_{1}+z_{2}+\ldots .+z_{n}\right|-\left|\frac{1}{z_{1}}+\frac{1}{z_{2}}+\ldots .+\frac{1}{z_{n}}\right|$

$=\left|z_{1}+z_{2}+\ldots+z_{n}\right|-\left|\bar{z}_{1}+\bar{z}_{2}+\ldots .+\bar{z}_{n}\right|=0$

</details>

The amplitude of $\sin \frac{\pi}{5}+i\left(1-\cos \frac{\pi}{5}\right)$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pi / 5$
- **(B)** $2 \pi / 5$
- **(C)** $\pi / 10$
- **(D)** $\pi / 15$

<details>

**Detailed Solution:**

$=2 \sin \frac{\pi}{10} \cos \frac{\pi}{10}+\mathrm{i} 2 \sin ^{2} \frac{\pi}{10}$
$=2 \sin \frac{\pi}{10}\left(\cos \frac{\pi}{10}+\mathrm{i} \sin \frac{\pi}{10}\right)$
For amplitude, $\tan \theta=\frac{\pi}{\sin \frac{\pi}{10}}{\cos \frac{\pi}{10}}=\tan \frac{\pi}{10}$
$\Rightarrow \theta=\frac{\pi}{10}$

### Q11. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For the equation \(\frac{1}{x+a}-\frac{1}{x+b}=\frac{1}{x+c}\), if the product of roots is zero, then the sum of roots is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** \(\frac{2 \mathrm{ab}}{\mathrm{b}+\mathrm{c}}\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{2 b c}{b+c}\)
- **(D)** \(\frac{-2 b c}{b+c}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) \(\frac{-2 b c}{b+c}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\frac{1}{x+a}-\frac{1}{x+b}=\frac{1}{x+c}\)
or \(x^2+(a+b) x+a b=(b-a) x+(b-a) c\) or \(\mathrm{x}^2+2 \mathrm{ax}+\mathrm{ab}+\mathrm{ca}-\mathrm{bc}=0\) Since product of the roots \(=0\)
\(a b+c a-b c=0 ; a=\frac{b c}{b+c}\)
Thus sum of roots \(=-2 a=\frac{-2 b c}{b+c}\)

</details>
