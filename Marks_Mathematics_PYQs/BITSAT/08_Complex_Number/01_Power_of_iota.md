### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$i^{57}+\frac{1}{i^{25}}$, when simplified has the value

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** $2 i$
- **(C)** $-2 i$
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

$i^{57}+\frac{1}{i^{25}}=\left(i^4\right)^{14} . i+\frac{1}{\left(i^4\right)^6  .i}=i+\frac{1}{i}=i-i=0$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$i^{57}+\frac{1}{i^{25}},$ when simplified has the value

**Options:**

- **(A)** 0
- **(B)** $2 i$
- **(C)** $-2 i$
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} i^{57}+\frac{1}{i^{25}}=\left(i^{4}\right)^{14} \cdot i &+\frac{1}{\left(i^{4}\right)^{6} \cdot i} \\=i+\frac{1}{i}\left(\because i^{4}=1\right) &=i-i\left(\because \frac{1}{i}=-i\right) \\ &=0 \end{aligned}$

</details>
