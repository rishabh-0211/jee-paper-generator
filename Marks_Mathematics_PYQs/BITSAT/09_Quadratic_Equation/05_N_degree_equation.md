### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Rational roots of the equation $2 x^4+x^3-11 x^2+$ $x+2=0$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2}$ and 2
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}, 2, \frac{1}{4},-2$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}, 2,3,4$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2}, 2, \frac{3}{4},-2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{2}$ and 2**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given equation can be reduced to a quadratic equation.
$\therefore 2 x^2+x-11+\frac{1}{x}+\frac{2}{x^2}=0$
$\Rightarrow 2\left(x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}\right)+\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)-11=0$
Put $x+\frac{1}{x}=y$
$2\left(y^2-2\right)+y-11=0$
$\Rightarrow 2 y^2+y-15=0$
$\Rightarrow y=-3$ and $\frac{5}{2}$
$\Rightarrow x+\frac{1}{x}=-3, x+\frac{1}{x}=\frac{5}{2}$
$\Rightarrow x^2+3 x+1=0,2 x^2-5 x+2=0$
Only 2nd equation has rational roots as $\mathrm{D}=9$ and roots are $\frac{1}{2}$ and 2 .

</details>
