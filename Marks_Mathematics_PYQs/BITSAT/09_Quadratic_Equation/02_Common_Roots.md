### Q1. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

\(10^n+3\left(4^{n+2}\right)+5\) is divisible by \((n \in N)\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 7
- **(B)** 5
- **(C)** 9
- **(D)** 17

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 9**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& 10^n+3\left(4^{n+2}\right)+5 \text { Taking } n=2, \\
& 10^2+3 \times 4^4+5=100+768+5=873
\end{aligned}\)
Therefore this is divisible by 9.

</details>
