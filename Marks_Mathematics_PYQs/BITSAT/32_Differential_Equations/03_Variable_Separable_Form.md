### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The number of solutions of $\frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{y+1}{x-1}$, when $y(1)=2$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** none
- **(B)** one
- **(C)** two
- **(D)** infinite

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) one**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since, $\frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{y+1}{x-1}$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{d y}{y+1}=\frac{d x}{x-1}$
After integrating on both sides, we have
$\log (y+1)=\log (x-1)-\log C$
$C(y+1)=(x-1)$
$C=\frac{x-1}{y+1}$
If $x=1$, then $y=2$, so $C=0$ 
Therefore, $x-1=0$ 
Hence, there is only one solution.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The solutions of $(x+y+1) d y=d x$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x+y+2=C e^{y}$
- **(B)** $x+y+4=C \log y$
- **(C)** $\quad \log (x+y+2)=C y$
- **(D)** $\log (x+y+2)=C-y$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x+y+2=C e^{y}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Putting $x+y+1=u,$ we have $d u=d x+d y$ and the given equation reduces to $u(d u-d x)=$ $d x$

$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{u d u}{u+1}=d x \quad \Rightarrow \quad u-\log (u+1)=x$

$\Rightarrow \log (x+y+2)=y+$ constant

$\Rightarrow x+y+2=C e^{y}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Solution of differential equation

$$

x^{2}=1+\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{-1} \frac{d y}{d x}+\frac{\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{-2}\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)^{2}}{2 !}+\frac{\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{-3}\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)^{3}}{3 !}+\ldots \text { is }

$$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $y^{2}=x^{2}\left(\ln x^{2}-1\right)+C$
- **(B)** $y=x^{2}(\ln x-1)+C$
- **(C)** $y^{2}=x(\ln x-1)+\mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** $y=x^{2} e^{x^{2}}+\mathrm{C}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $y^{2}=x^{2}\left(\ln x^{2}-1\right)+C$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$x^{2}=e^{\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{-1}\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)} \Rightarrow x^{2}=e^{\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\left(\frac{d y}{d x}\right)}$

$\Rightarrow \ln x^{2}=\frac{y}{x} \frac{d y}{d x}$ or $\int x \ln x^{2} d x=\int y d y$

Put $x^{2}=t \Rightarrow 2 x d x=d t \therefore \frac{1}{2} \int \ln t d t=\frac{y^{2}}{2}$

$C+t \ln t-t=y^{2}$ or $y^{2}=x^{2}\left(\ln x^{2}-1\right)+C$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The general solution of the differential equation $\frac{d y}{d x}+\sin (x+y)=\sin (x-y)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\log \tan y+\sin x=\mathrm{C}$
- **(B)** $\quad \log \tan \frac{y}{2}+2 \sin x=\mathrm{C}$
- **(C)** $\tan \frac{y}{2}+\log \sin x=\mathrm{C}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\quad \log \tan \frac{y}{2}+2 \sin x=\mathrm{C}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation is,

$\frac{d y}{d x}=\sin (x-y)-\sin (x+y)=2 \cos x \sin (-y)$

$\Rightarrow \frac{d y}{\sin y}+2 \cos x d x=0$

$\Rightarrow \int \cos e c y d y+2 \int \cos x d x=\mathrm{C}$

$\Rightarrow \log \tan \frac{y}{2}+2 \sin x=\mathrm{C}$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the solution of \(\frac{d y}{d x}+2 y=1\) satisfying \(y(0)=0\) ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(y=\frac{1-e^{-2 x}}{2}\)
- **(B)** \(y=\frac{1+e^{-2 x}}{2}\)
- **(C)** \(y=1+e^x\)
- **(D)** \(y=\frac{1+e^x}{2}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) \(y=\frac{1-e^{-2 x}}{2}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \frac{d y}{d x}+2 y=1 \Rightarrow \frac{d y}{d x}=1-2 y \\
& \int \frac{d y}{1-2 y}=\int d x-\frac{1}{2} \log |1-2 y|=x+C \\
& \text { at } x=0, y=0 ;-\frac{1}{2} \log 1=0+C \Rightarrow C=0 \\
& 1-2 y=e^{-2 x} \Rightarrow y=\frac{1-e^{-2 x}}{2}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
