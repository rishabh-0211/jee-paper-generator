### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$A B C$ is a triangular park with $A B=A C=100 \mathrm{~m}$. A TV tower stands at the mid-point of $B C$. The angles of elevation of the top of the tower at $A$, $B, C$ are $45^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}$ respectively. The height of the tower is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 50 m
- **(B)** $50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(C)** $50 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(D)** $50(3-\sqrt{3}) \mathrm{m}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

According to question,
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/5y1wsg7K9bwn0ZuQXOhXNn-7N9PFZP4Ui3BT9LeBulw.original.fullsize.png"/>
Let $D E=h$ and $C D=D B=x$
In $\triangle E B D, \quad \tan 60^{\circ}=\frac{h}{x} \Rightarrow x=\frac{h}{\sqrt{3}}$
Now, in $\triangle A D E$
$\begin{aligned}
& \tan 45^{\circ}=\frac{E D}{D A} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad D A=h
\end{aligned}$
In $\triangle A B D$, by applying Pythagoras theorem
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad\left(\frac{h}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^2+h^2=100^2 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \frac{4 h^2}{3}=10000 \quad \Rightarrow h=50 \sqrt{3}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

From the top of a cliff 50 m high, the angles of depression of the top and bottom of a tower are observed to be $30^{\circ}$ and $45^{\circ}$. The height of tower is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 50 m
- **(B)** $50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(C)** $50(\sqrt{3}-1) \mathrm{m}$
- **(D)** $50\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right) \mathrm{m}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $50\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right) \mathrm{m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Accoding to condition,
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/S-za6gK9MOlwEMop04Bh1Vi8uXSmp7yxhyO-lxrmfco.original.fullsize.png"/>
Let, height of tower $=h$
In $\triangle A B D, \quad \tan 45^{\circ}=\frac{A B}{B D} \Rightarrow B D=50 \mathrm{~m}$
Now, In $\triangle A C C^{\prime}$
$\begin{aligned}
& & \tan 30^{\circ} & =\frac{A C^{\prime}}{C^{\prime} C} \\
\Rightarrow & & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & =\frac{50-h}{50} \\
\Rightarrow & & 50 & =50 \sqrt{3}-h \sqrt{3} \\
\Rightarrow & & h \sqrt{3} & =50(\sqrt{3}-1) \\
& & h & =50\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) \\
& & & =50\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right) \mathrm{m}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

A house subtends a right angle at the window of a opposite house and the angle of elevation of the window from the bottom of the first house is $60$. If the distance between two houses be $6 \mathrm{~m}$, then the height of the first house is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(B)** $6 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(C)** $4 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $8 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $P Q$ be the house subtending a right angle at the window $B$ of opposite house $A B$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/0V7dsPYHXJPSlfmOThYsY38RGsVYM4qnm7QqqLpWp0s.original.fullsize.png">

In $\triangle P A B$, we have 
$\tan 60^{\circ}=\frac{A B}{6}=A B=6 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
In $\triangle C B Q$, we have
$\tan 30^{\circ}=\frac{h-C P}{B C}$
$\begin{array}{ll}\Rightarrow & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{h-6 \sqrt{3}}{6} \quad[\because A B=C P, B C=A P] \\ \Rightarrow & h=6\left(\sqrt{3}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) \\ \Rightarrow & h=8 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}\end{array}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Let $\alpha$ be the solution of $16^{\sin ^2 \theta}+16^{\cos ^2 \theta}=10$ in $\left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$. If the shadow of a vertical pole is $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ of its height, then the altitude of the sun is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\alpha$
- **(B)** $\frac{\alpha}{2}$
- **(C)** $2 \alpha$
- **(D)** $\frac{\alpha}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $2 \alpha$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have,
$16^{\sin ^2 \theta}+16^{\cos ^2 \theta}=10$
$\Rightarrow \quad 16^{\sin ^2 \theta}+16^{1-\sin ^2 \theta}=10$
$\Rightarrow x+\frac{16}{x}=10$, where $x=16^{\sin ^2 \theta}$
$\Rightarrow \quad x^2-10 x+16=0 \Rightarrow x=2,8$
$\therefore \quad 16^{\sin ^2 \theta}=2,8 \Rightarrow 2^{4 \sin ^2 \theta}=2,2^3$
$\Rightarrow \quad 4 \sin ^2 \theta=1,3 \Rightarrow \sin ^2 \theta=\frac{1}{4},\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2$
$\Rightarrow \quad \sin \theta=\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \Rightarrow \theta=\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{3}$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/S5pOq2wkF-enUh0phx2RJpTLusnmaGSnw_vsT1ydx70.original.fullsize.png">

Let the altitude of the sun be $\theta$. Then,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& & \tan \theta & =\frac{h}{h / \sqrt{3}} \Rightarrow \tan \theta=\sqrt{3} \\
\Rightarrow \quad & & \theta & =\pi / 3 \Rightarrow \theta=2 \alpha
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\mathrm{ABC}$ is a triangular park with $\mathrm{AB}=\mathrm{AC}=100 \mathrm{~m} . \mathrm{A}$ TV tower stands at the mid-point of $\mathrm{BC}$. The angles of elevation of the top of the tower at A, B, C are $45^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}, 60^{\circ}$ respectively. The height of the tower is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $50 \mathrm{~m}$
- **(B)** $50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(C)** $50 \sqrt{2} \mathrm{~m}$
- **(D)** $50(3-\sqrt{3}) \mathrm{m}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $50 \sqrt{3} \mathrm{~m}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tan 45^{\circ}=\frac{\mathrm{PQ}}{\mathrm{AQ}}=\frac{\mathrm{h}}{\mathrm{AQ}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{h}=\mathrm{AQ}$

Where $\mathrm{PQ}$ is tower and $\mathrm{ABC}$ is the park, with $\mathrm{Q}$ being mid point of the side $\mathrm{BC}$ and $\mathrm{PQ}=\mathrm{h}$ Also, $A Q^{2}+B Q^{2}=100^{2}$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{h}^{2}+\mathrm{h}^{2} \cot ^{2} 60^{\circ}=100^{2}$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/tuAM6FNFOsmKk75aDThfd9hkBNGpcAj1vMCBk-QdOjY.original.fullsize.png">


$\Rightarrow \mathrm{h}^{2}\left[1+\frac{1}{3}\right]=100^{2}$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{h}^{2}=\frac{3 \times 100^{2}}{4} \Rightarrow \mathrm{h}=50 \sqrt{3}$

</details>

### Q7. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a triangle \(\mathrm{ABC}, \angle \mathrm{C}=90^{\circ}\), then \(\frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2+b^2}\) is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\sin (\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B})\)
- **(B)** \(\sin (A-B)\)
- **(C)** \(\cos (\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B})\)
- **(D)** \(\sin \left(\frac{\mathrm{A}-\mathrm{B}}{2}\right)\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\sin (A-B)\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}=180^{\circ}-\mathrm{C}=90^{\circ} \\
& \mathrm{a}=2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{A}, b=2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{B}, c=2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{C} \\
& \begin{array}{l}
\therefore \frac{a^2-b^2}{a^2+b^2}=\frac{\sin ^2 \mathrm{~A}-\sin ^2 \mathrm{~B}}{\sin ^2 \mathrm{~A}+\sin ^2 \mathrm{~B}} \\
\quad=\frac{\sin (\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}) \sin (\mathrm{A}-\mathrm{B})}{\sin ^2 \mathrm{~A}+\sin ^2\left(90^{\circ}-\mathrm{A}\right)} \\
{\left[\because \mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}=90^{\circ}\right]} \\
=\frac{\sin 90^{\circ} \sin (\mathrm{A}-\mathrm{B})}{\sin ^2 \mathrm{~A}+\cos ^2 \mathrm{~A}}=\sin (\mathrm{A}-\mathrm{B})
\end{array}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
