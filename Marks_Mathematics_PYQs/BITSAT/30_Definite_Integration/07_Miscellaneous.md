### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\int_0^{\infty} \frac{d x}{\left(x^2+a^2\right)\left(x^2+b^2\right)}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\pi a b}{a+b}$
- **(B)** $\frac{a b}{2(a+b)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\pi}{2 a b(a+b)}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\pi(a+b)}{2 a b}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\pi}{2 a b(a+b)}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $\begin{aligned} I & =\int_0^{\infty} \frac{1}{\left(x^2+a^2\right)\left(x^2+b^2\right)} d x \\ I & =\frac{1}{a^2-b^2} \int_0^{\infty} \frac{\left(x^2+a^2\right)-\left(x^2+b^2\right)}{\left(x^2+a^2\right)\left(x^2+b^2\right)} d x \\ I & =\frac{1}{a^2-b^2} \int_0^{\infty} \frac{1}{\left(x^2+b^2\right)}-\frac{1}{\left(x^2+a^2\right)} d x \\ I & =\frac{1}{a^2-b^2}\left[\frac{1}{b} \tan ^{-1} \frac{x}{b}-\frac{1}{a} \tan ^{-1} \frac{x}{a}\right]_0^{\infty} \\ I & =\frac{1}{a^2-b^2}\left[\frac{1}{b} \times \frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{1}{a} \times \frac{\pi}{2}\right] \\ I & =\frac{1}{(a+b)(a-b)}\left[\frac{a-b}{a b}\right] \times \frac{\pi}{2} \\ I & =\frac{\pi}{2 a b(a+b)}\end{aligned}$

</details>
