### Q1. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find the value of \(\int_0^{4 \pi}|\sin x| d x\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 8
- **(B)** 6
- **(C)** 4
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 8**

**Detailed Solution:**

We know that \(|\sin x|\) is a periodic function of \(\pi\)
\(\text { Hence } \begin{array}{r}
\int_0^{4 \pi}|\sin x| d x=4 \int_0^\pi|\sin x| d x=4 \int_0^\pi \sin x d x \\
=4[-\cos x]_0^\pi=8
\end{array}\)

</details>
