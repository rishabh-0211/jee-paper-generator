### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the $17^{\text {th }}$ and the $18^{\text {th }}$ terms in the expansion of $(2+a)^{50}$ are equal, then the coefficient of $x^{35}$ in the expansion of $(a+x)^{-2}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-35$
- **(B)** $3$
- **(C)** $36$
- **(D)** $-36$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-36$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, $17^{\text {th }}$ and $18^{\text {th }}$ terms in the expansion $(2+a)^{50}$ are equal
$\therefore T_{17}=T_{18}$
$\Rightarrow{ }^{50} C_{16}(2)^{34}(a)^{16}={ }^{50} C_{17}(2)^{33}(a)^{17}$
$\Rightarrow a=\frac{{ }^{50} C_{16}}{{ }^{50} C_{17}} \times 2=1$
$\therefore$ Now, coefficient of $x^{35}$ in the expansion
$(1+x)^{-2}=-36$
$\because$ Coefficient of $x^r$ is $(r+1)$ in $(1-x)^{-2}$.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\mathrm{T}_{0}, \mathrm{~T}_{1}, \mathrm{~T}_{2} \ldots \ldots \mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{n}}$ represent the terms in the

expansion of $(x+a)^{n}$, then $\left(\mathrm{T}_{0}-\mathrm{T}_{2}+\mathrm{T}_{4}-\ldots \ldots .\right)^{2}+$

$\left(\mathrm{T}_{1}-\mathrm{T}_{3}+\mathrm{T}_{5}-\ldots . .\right)^{2}=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)$
- **(B)** $\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)^{n}$
- **(C)** $\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)^{1 / n}$
- **(D)** $\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)^{-1 / n}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)^{n}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

From the given condition, replacing $a$ by a and $-a i$ respectively, we get

$(x+a i)^{n}=\left(T_{0}-T_{2}+T_{4}-\ldots \ldots\right)+i\left(T_{1}-T_{3}+T_{5}-\ldots .\right)$ ...(i)

and

$(x-a i)^{n}=\left(T_{0}-T_{2}+T_{4}-\ldots \ldots\right)-i\left(T_{1}-T_{3}+T_{5}-\ldots\right)$...(ii)

Multiplying (ii) and

(i) we get required result i.e.,

$$

\left(x^{2}+a^{2}\right)^{n}=\left(T_{0}-T_{2}+T_{4}-\ldots .\right)^{2}+\left(T_{1}-T_{3}+T_{5}-\ldots\right)^{2}

$$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2013

$+\left(C_{0}+C_{1}+\ldots . .+C_{n-1}\right)$

- **(A)** $\mathrm{n} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{n}-1}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{n} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{n}}$
- **(C)** $(n-1) \cdot 2^{n-1}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{n} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{n}-1}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{C}_{0}+\left(\mathrm{C}_{0}+\mathrm{C}_{1}\right)+\left(\mathrm{C}_{0}+\mathrm{C}_{1}+\mathrm{C}_{2}\right)+\ldots \ldots \ldots \ldots \ldots \\ &+\left(\mathrm{C}_{0}+\mathrm{C}_{1}+\ldots \ldots \ldots . \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{n}-1}\right) \\ &=\mathrm{n} \mathrm{C}_{0}+(\mathrm{n}-1) \mathrm{C}_{1}+(\mathrm{n}-2) \mathrm{C}_{2}+\ldots \ldots \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{n}-1} \\ &=\mathrm{C}_{1}+2 \mathrm{C}_{2}+3 \mathrm{C}_{3}+4 \mathrm{C}_{4} \ldots \ldots \mathrm{n} \mathrm{C}_{\mathrm{n}}=\mathrm{n} .2^{\mathrm{n}-1} \end{aligned}$

</details>
