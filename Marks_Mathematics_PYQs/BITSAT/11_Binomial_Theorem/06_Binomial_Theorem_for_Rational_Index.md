### Q1. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the third term in the expansion of $\left[\mathrm{x}+\mathrm{x}^{\log _{10} \mathrm{x}}\right]^{5}$

is $10^{6},$ then $\mathrm{x}$ may be

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** $\sqrt{10}$
- **(C)** 10
- **(D)** $10^{-2 / 5}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 10**

**Detailed Solution:**

Put $\log _{10} x=y,$ the given expression becomes $\left(x+x^{y}\right)^{5}$

$\mathrm{T}_{3}={ }^{5} \mathrm{C}_{2} \cdot \mathrm{x}^{3}\left(\mathrm{x}^{\mathrm{y}}\right)^{2}=10 \mathrm{x}^{3+2 \mathrm{y}}=10^{6}$ (given)

$\Rightarrow(3+2 \mathrm{y}) \log _{10} \mathrm{x}=5 \log _{10} 10=5$

$\Rightarrow(3+2 \mathrm{y}) \mathrm{y}=5$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{y}=1,-\frac{5}{2}$

$\Rightarrow \log _{10} \mathrm{x}=1$ or $\log _{10} \mathrm{x}=-\frac{5}{2}$

$\therefore \mathrm{x}=10$ or $\mathrm{x}=(10)^{-5 / 2}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the sum of odd numbered terms and the sum of even numbered terms in the expansion of $(x+a)^{n}$ are $A$ and $B$ respectively, then the value of $\left(x^{2}-\right.$ $\left.a^{2}\right)^{n}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{A}^{2}-\mathrm{B}^{2}$
- **(B)** $\mathrm{A}^{2}+\mathrm{B}^{2}$
- **(C)** $4 \mathrm{AB}$
- **(D)** None

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\mathrm{A}^{2}-\mathrm{B}^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$$

\begin{array}{r}

(x+a)^{n}={ }^{n} C_{0} x^{n}+{ }^{n} C_{1} x^{n-1} a+{ }^{n} C_{2} x^{n-2} a^{2} \\

+{ }^{n} C_{3} x^{n-3} a^{3}+{ }^{n} C_{4} x^{n-4} a^{4}+\ldots . \\

=\left({ }^{n} C_{0} x^{n}+{ }^{n} C_{2} x^{n-2} a^{2}+{ }^{n} C_{4} x^{n-4} a^{4}+\ldots .\right)+ \\

+\left({ }^{n} C_{1} x^{n-1} a+{ }^{n} C_{3} x^{n-3} a^{3}+{ }^{n} C_{5} x^{n-5} a^{5}\right)+\ldots .

\end{array}

$$

$=A+B$ ....(i)

Similarly, $(x-a)^{n}=A-B$ ....(ii)

Multiplying eqns. (1) and (2), we get

$$

\left(\mathrm{x}^{2}-\mathrm{a}^{2}\right)^{\mathrm{n}}=\mathrm{A}^{2}-\mathrm{B}^{2}

$$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The coefficient of $x^{20}$ in the expansion of
$\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{40} \cdot\left(x^{2}+2+\frac{1}{x^{2}}\right)^{-5}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** ${ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{10}$
- **(B)** ${ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{25}$
- **(C)** 1
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) ${ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{25}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Expression $=\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{40} \cdot\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)^{-10}$
$=\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{30} \cdot x^{10}$
The coefficient of $x^{20}$ in $x^{10}\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{30}$ $=$ the coefficient of $\mathrm{x}^{10}$ in $\left(1+\mathrm{x}^{2}\right)^{30}$
$={ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{5}={ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{30-5}={ }^{30} \mathrm{C}_{25}$

</details>
