### Q1. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Evaluate \(\int \frac{x^2}{x^2-1} d x\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(x-\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{\mathrm{x}-1}{\mathrm{x}+1}\right)+\mathrm{c}\)
- **(B)** \(x+\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{x+1}{x-1}\right)+c\)
- **(C)** \(x+\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{\mathrm{x}-1}{\mathrm{x}+1}\right)+\mathrm{c}\)
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) \(x+\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{\mathrm{x}-1}{\mathrm{x}+1}\right)+\mathrm{c}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given integral \(I=\int\left(1+\frac{1}{x^2-1}\right) d x\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& =\int d x+\int \frac{d x}{(x-1)(x+1)} \\
& =x+\frac{1}{2} \int\left(\frac{1}{x-1}-\frac{1}{x+1}\right) d x=x+\frac{1}{2} \log \left(\frac{x-1}{x+1}\right)+c
\end{aligned}\)

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The slope of the tangent to the hyperbola \(2 x^2-3 y^2=6\) at \((3,2)\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** -1
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** 0
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

Differentiating the given equation of the curve
\(\begin{aligned}
& 4 x-6 y \cdot(\mathrm{dy} / \mathrm{dx})=0 \quad \therefore \mathrm{dy} / \mathrm{dx}=2 \mathrm{x} / 3 \mathrm{y} \\
& \left(\frac{\mathrm{dy}}{\mathrm{dx}}\right)_{(3,2)}=\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2}=1
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
