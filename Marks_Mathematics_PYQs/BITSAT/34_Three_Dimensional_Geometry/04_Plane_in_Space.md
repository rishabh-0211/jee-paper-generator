### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let the acute angle bisector of the two planes $x-2 y-2 z+1=0$ and $2 x-3 y-6 z+1=0$ be the plane $P$. Then which of the following points lies on P?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(3,1,-\frac{1}{2}\right)$
- **(B)** $\left(-2,0,-\frac{1}{2}\right)$
- **(C)** $(0,2,-4)$
- **(D)** $(4,0,-2)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\left(-2,0,-\frac{1}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that $P_1: x-2 y-2 z+1=0$
$P_2: 2 x-3 y-6 z+1=0$
Equation of plane bisectors
$\begin{aligned}
& \left|\frac{x-2 y-2 z+1}{\sqrt{1+4+4}}\right|=\left|\frac{2 x-3 y-6 z+1}{\sqrt{2^2+3^2+6^2}}\right| \\
& \frac{x-2 y-2 z+1}{3}= \pm \frac{2 x-3 y-6 z+1}{7}
\end{aligned}$
Since $\mathrm{a}_1 \mathrm{a}_2+\mathrm{b}_1 \mathrm{~b}_2+\mathrm{c}_1 \mathrm{c}_2=20\gt0$
$\therefore$ Negative sign will be taken for acute bisector.
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow 7 x-14 y-14 z+7=-[6 x-9 y-18 z+3] \\
& \Rightarrow 13 x-23 y-32 z+10=0 \\
& \left(-2,0,-\frac{1}{2}\right) \text { satisfy it }
\end{aligned}$

</details>

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\cos ^{-1}(4 / 21)$
- **(B)** $\cos ^{-1}(2 / 21)$
- **(C)** $\cos ^{-1}(1 / 21)$
- **(D)** $\cos ^{-1}(5 / 21)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\cos ^{-1}(4 / 21)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The angle between two planes is the angle between their normals. From the equation of the planes, the normal vectors are $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{1}=2 \hat{\mathrm{i}}+\hat{\mathrm{j}}-2 \hat{\mathrm{k}}$ and $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{2}=3 \hat{\mathrm{i}}-6 \hat{\mathrm{j}}-2 \hat{\mathrm{k}}$

Therefore, $\cos \theta=\left|\frac{\overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{1} \cdot \overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{2}}{\left|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{1}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{N}}_{2}\right|}\right|$

$$

=\left|\frac{(2 \hat{i}+\hat{j}-2 \hat{k}) \cdot(3 \hat{i}-6 \hat{j}-2 \hat{k})}{\sqrt{4+1+4} \sqrt{9+36+4}}\right|=\left(\frac{4}{21}\right)

$$

Hence, $\theta=\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{4}{21}\right)$
