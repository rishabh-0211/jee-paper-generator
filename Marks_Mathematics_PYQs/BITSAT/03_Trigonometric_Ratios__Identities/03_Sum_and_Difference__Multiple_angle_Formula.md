### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The sum of all values of $x$ in $[0,2 \pi]$, for which $\sin x+\sin 2 x+\sin 3 x+\sin 4 x=0$, is equal to :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $8 \pi$
- **(B)** $11 \pi$
- **(C)** $12 \pi$
- **(D)** $9 \pi$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $9 \pi$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$(\sin x+\sin 4 x)+(\sin 2 x+\sin 3 x)=0$
$\Rightarrow 2 \sin \frac{5 x}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{3 x}{2}+2 \sin \frac{5 x}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{x}{2}=0$
$\Rightarrow 2 \sin \frac{5 x}{2}\left\{\cos \frac{3 x}{2}+\cos \frac{x}{2}\right\}=0$
$\Rightarrow 2 \sin \frac{5 x}{2}\left\{2 \cos x \cos \frac{x}{2}\right\}=0$
$2 \sin \frac{5 x}{2}=0 \Rightarrow \frac{5 x}{2}=0, \pi, 2 \pi, 3 \pi, 4 \pi, 5 \pi, \ldots$
$\Rightarrow x=0, \frac{2 \pi}{5}, \frac{4 \pi}{5}, \frac{6 \pi}{5}, \frac{8 \pi}{5}, 2 \pi$
$\cos \frac{x}{2}=0 \Rightarrow \frac{x}{2}=\frac{\pi}{2} \Rightarrow x=\pi ;$
$\cos x=0 \Rightarrow x=\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3 \pi}{2}$
So, sum $=6 \pi+\pi+2 \pi=9 \pi$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\cos ^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}+\theta\right)-\sin ^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}-\theta\right)=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2} \cos 2 \theta$
- **(B)** 0
- **(C)** $-\frac{1}{2} \cos 2 \theta$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{2} \cos 2 \theta$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \cos ^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}+\theta\right)-\sin ^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}-\theta\right) \\ &=\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6}+\theta+\frac{\pi}{6}-\theta\right) \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6}+\theta-\frac{\pi}{6}+\theta\right) \\ &=\cos \frac{2 \pi}{6} \cos 2 \theta=\frac{1}{2} \cos 2 \theta \end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

An object is obseved from the points $A, B$ and C lying in a horizontal straight line which passes directly underneath the object. The angular elevation at $\mathrm{B}$ is twice that at $\mathrm{A}$ and at $\mathrm{C}$ three times that at $\mathrm{A} .$ It $\mathrm{AB}=a, \mathrm{BC}=b$, then the height of the object is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{b}{2 a} \sqrt{(a+b)(3 b-a)}$
- **(B)** $\frac{a}{b} \sqrt{(a+b)(3 b-a)}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{a}}{2 \mathrm{~b}} \sqrt{(\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b})(3 \mathrm{~b}-\mathrm{a})}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2 a}{b} \sqrt{(a+b)(3 b-a)}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\mathrm{a}}{2 \mathrm{~b}} \sqrt{(\mathrm{a}+\mathrm{b})(3 \mathrm{~b}-\mathrm{a})}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/rwdKoQrfKyk9Z0EX5JJEz95pGwrs8huU3BDOAEBcdXk.original.fullsize.png">


Let $\mathrm{ED}=h, \angle \mathrm{EAB}=\alpha$

$\therefore \quad \angle \mathrm{EBD}=2 \alpha, \angle \mathrm{ECD}=3 \alpha$

Now, $\angle \mathrm{DBE}=\angle \mathrm{EAB}+\angle \mathrm{BEA}$

$\Rightarrow \quad 2 \alpha=\alpha+\angle \mathrm{BEA}$

$\Rightarrow \quad \angle \mathrm{BEA}=\alpha=\angle \mathrm{EAB}$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{AB}=\mathrm{EB}=a$

Similarly, $\angle \mathrm{EBC}=\alpha$ $\operatorname{In} \Delta \mathrm{EBC}, \frac{\mathrm{BC}}{\sin \alpha}=\frac{\mathrm{EB}}{\sin \left(180^{\circ}-3 \alpha\right)}$

$\Rightarrow \frac{b}{\sin \alpha}=\frac{a}{\sin 3 \alpha} \Rightarrow \frac{a}{b}=\frac{\sin 3 \alpha}{\sin 3 \alpha}$

$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{a}{b}=\frac{3 \sin \alpha-4 \sin ^{3} \alpha}{\sin \alpha}=3-4 \sin ^{2} \alpha$

$\Rightarrow 4 \sin ^{2} \alpha=3-\frac{a}{b}=\frac{3 b-a}{b}$

$\Rightarrow \sin \alpha=\sqrt{\frac{3 b-a}{4 b}}$

In $\Delta \mathrm{EBD}, \sin 2 \alpha=\frac{E D}{E B}$

$\Rightarrow \quad \mathrm{ED}=\mathrm{a} \cdot 2 \sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha$

$\Rightarrow h=2 a \sqrt{\frac{3 b-a}{4 b}}, \sqrt{1-\frac{3 b-a}{4 b}}$

$=2 a \sqrt{\frac{3 b-a}{4 b}} \sqrt{\frac{b-a}{4 b}}$

$=\frac{a}{2 b} \sqrt{(a+b)(3 b-a)}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If $\sin ^{2} \theta+\sin ^{2} \phi=1 / 2, \cos ^{2} \theta+\cos ^{2} \phi=3 / 2,$ then $\cos ^{2}(\theta-\phi)$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3 / 8$
- **(B)** $5 / 8$
- **(C)** $3 / 4$
- **(D)** $5 / 4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $5 / 8$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using cosine formula 
$2 \sin (\theta+\phi) \cos (\theta-\phi)=1 / 2$ ...(i)
$2 \cos (\theta+\phi) \cos (\theta-\phi)=3 / 2$ ...(ii)
Squaring (1) and (2) and then adding $\cos ^{2}(\theta-\phi)=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{9}{4}=\frac{5}{2} \Rightarrow \cos ^{2}(\theta-\phi)=\frac{5}{8}$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If, \(\tan \mathrm{A}=\frac{1}{2}\) and \(\tan B=\frac{1}{3}\), then find the value of \(\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\pi\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{\pi}{4}\)
- **(D)** \(\frac{-\pi}{4}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) \(\frac{\pi}{4}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \tan (A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A \tan B}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}}{1-\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3}}=1 \\
& \therefore A+B=45^{\circ}=\frac{\pi}{4}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
