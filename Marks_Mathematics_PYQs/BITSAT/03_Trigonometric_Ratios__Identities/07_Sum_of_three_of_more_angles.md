### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let $A, B$ and $C$ are the angles of a triangle and $\tan \frac{A}{2}=\frac{1}{3}, \tan \frac{B}{2}=\frac{2}{3}$. Then, $\tan \frac{C}{2}$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{7}{9}$
- **(B)** $\frac{2}{9}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{7}{9}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}+\mathrm{C}=180^{\circ} \text { (Angle sum property of a triangle) } \\ & \Rightarrow \mathrm{A}+\mathrm{B}=180^{\circ}-\mathrm{C} \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{A+B}{2}=90^{\circ}-\frac{C}{2} \\ & \Rightarrow \tan \left(\frac{A}{2}+\frac{B}{2}\right)=\tan \left(90^{\circ}-\frac{C}{2}\right) \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{\tan A / 2+\tan B / 2}{1-\tan A / 2 \tan B / 2}=\cot C / 2 \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{\frac{1}{3}+\frac{2}{3}}{1-\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{2}{3}}=\cot C / 2 \Rightarrow \frac{1}{1-\frac{2}{9}}=\cot C / 2 \\ & \Rightarrow \frac{9}{7}=\cot C / 2 \Rightarrow \tan C / 2=7 / 9\end{aligned}\)

</details>
