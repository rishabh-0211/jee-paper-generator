### Q1. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If \(\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A} \cup \mathrm{B})=\frac{2}{3}, \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A} \cap \mathrm{B})=\frac{1}{6}\) and \(\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A})=\frac{1}{3}\) then

**Options:**

- **(A)** A and \(\mathrm{B}\) are independent events
- **(B)** A and \(\mathrm{B}\) are disjoint events
- **(C)** A and \(\mathrm{B}\) are dependent events
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) A and \(\mathrm{B}\) are independent events**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A} \cup \mathrm{B})=\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A})+\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{B})-\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{AB})\)
\(\Rightarrow \frac{2}{3}=\frac{1}{3}+\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{B})-\frac{1}{6} \Rightarrow \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{B})=\frac{1}{2}\)
Now, \(\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{AB})=\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{A})~ \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{B}), \mathrm{A}\) and \(\mathrm{B}\) are independent events.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If the function \(f:(-\infty, \infty) \rightarrow B\) defined by \(f(x)=-x^2+6 x-8\) is bijective, then \(B=\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** \([1, \infty)\)
- **(B)** \((-\infty, 1]\)
- **(C)** \((-\infty, \infty)\)
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \((-\infty, 1]\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since the function \(f\) is bijective, therefore \(f\) is onto. Therefore range of \(f=B\).
Let \(y=-x^2+6 x-8\)
\(\Rightarrow \mathrm{x}^2-6 \mathrm{x}+(8+\mathrm{y})=0\)
\(\Rightarrow x=\frac{6 \pm \sqrt{36-4(8+y)}}{2}=\frac{6 \pm \sqrt{4-4 y}}{2}\)
For \(x\) to be real, \(4-4 y \geq 0 \Rightarrow y \leq 1\)
\(\therefore \mathrm{B}=\) range of \(\mathrm{F}=(-\infty, 1]\)

</details>
