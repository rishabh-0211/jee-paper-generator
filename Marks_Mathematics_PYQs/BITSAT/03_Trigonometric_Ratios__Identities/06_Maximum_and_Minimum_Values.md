### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation $\sin ^{4} x-(k+2) \sin ^{2} x-(k+3)=0$ possesses a solution if

**Options:**

- **(A)** $k>-3$
- **(B)** $k<-2$
- **(C)** $-3 \leq \mathrm{k} \leq-2$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{k}$ is any positive integer

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $-3 \leq \mathrm{k} \leq-2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $\sin ^{4} x-(k+2) \sin ^{2} x-(k+3)=0$

$\Rightarrow \sin ^{2} x=\frac{(k+2) \pm \sqrt{(k+2)^{2}+4(k+3)}}{2}$

$=\frac{(k+2) \pm(k+4)}{2}$

$\Rightarrow \sin ^{2} x=k+3\left(\because \sin ^{2} x=-1\right.$ is not possible)

Since $0 \leq \sin ^{2} x \leq 1$

$\therefore 0 \leq \mathrm{k}+3 \leq 1$ or $-3 \leq \mathrm{k} \leq-2$

</details>
