### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If $\cot \left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)=\sec \left\{\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{a}{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}\right)\right\}$
$b \gt a$, then $\mathrm{x}=$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{b}{\sqrt{2 b^2-a^2}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}{a b}$
- **(C)** $\frac{a}{\sqrt{2 b^2-a^2}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}{a}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{b}{\sqrt{2 b^2-a^2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that,
$\cot \left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)=\sec \left\{\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{a}{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}\right)\right\}$
Since, $\cos ^{-1} x=\cot ^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)$ and $\tan ^{-1} x=\sec ^{-1}\left(\sqrt{1+x^2}\right)$
$\Rightarrow \cot \left(\cot ^{-1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)=$
$\sec \left\{\sec ^{-1} \sqrt{1+\left(\frac{a}{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}\right)^2}\right\}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\sqrt{\frac{b^2-a^2+a^2}{b^2-a^2}}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2-a^2}}$
On squaring both the sides, we get
$\Rightarrow \frac{x^2}{1-x^2}=\frac{b^2}{b^2-a^2}$
$\begin{aligned} \Rightarrow & x^2 b^2-x^2 a^2=b^2-b^2 x^2 \\ & x^2 b^2+b^2 x^2-x^2 a^2=b^2\end{aligned}$
$\Rightarrow \quad 2 x^2 b^2-x^2 a^2=b^2 \Rightarrow x^2\left(2 b^2-a^2\right)=b^2$
$\Rightarrow \quad x=\frac{b}{\sqrt{2 b^2-a^2}}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The value of $\cos \left(2 \cos ^{-1} x+\sin ^{-1} x\right)$ at $x=\frac{1}{5}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-\frac{2 \sqrt{6}}{5}$
- **(B)** $-2 \sqrt{6}$
- **(C)** $-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{5}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $-\frac{2 \sqrt{6}}{5}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \cos \left[2 \cos ^{-1} x+\sin ^{-1} x\right] \\ & =\cos \left[\cos ^{-1} x+\cos ^{-1} x+\sin ^{-1} x\right] \\ & =\cos \left[\cos ^{-1} x+\pi / 2\right]=-\sin \left[\cos ^{-1} x\right] \\ & =-\sin \left[\sin ^{-1} \sqrt{1-x^2}\right]=-\sqrt{1-x^2} \\ & =-\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^2}=-\sqrt{\frac{24}{25}}=-\frac{2 \sqrt{6}}{5} \\ & \end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $x \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ then the value of $\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{7}{2}(1+\cos 2 x)+\sqrt{\left(\sin ^2 x-48 \cos ^2 x\right) \sin x}\right)$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $x-\cos ^{-1}(7 \cos x)$
- **(B)** $x+\sin ^{-1}(7 \cos x)$
- **(C)** $x+\cos ^{-1}(6 \cos x)$
- **(D)** $x+\cos ^{-1}(7 \cos x)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $x-\cos ^{-1}(7 \cos x)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$y=\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{7}{2}(1+\cos 2 x)+\sqrt{\left(\sin ^2 x-48 \cos ^2 x\right)} \sin x\right)$
$=\cos ^{-1}\left((7 \cos x)(\cos x)+\sqrt{1-49 \cos ^2 x} \sqrt{1-\cos ^2 x}\right)$
$=\cos ^{-1}(\cos x)-\cos ^{-1}(7 \cos x) \quad[\because \cos x < 7 \cos x]$
$=x-\cos ^{-1}(7 \cos x)$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)+\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{9}\right)$ equal to-

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2} \cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2} \sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2} \tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(D)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)+\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{9}\right)$
$\quad=\tan ^{-1}\left[\frac{\frac{1}{4}+\frac{2}{9}}{1-\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{2}{9}}\right]=\tan ^{-1}\left[\frac{1}{2}\right]$

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2011

The value of \(\sin \left(4 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{3}\right)-\cos \left(2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{7}\right)\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(3 / 7\)
- **(B)** \(7 / 8\)
- **(C)** \(8 / 21\)
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) None of these**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\sin \left(4 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{3}\right)\)
\(\begin{aligned}
&= 2 \sin \left(2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{3}\right) \cos \left(2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{3}\right) \\
&=2 \sin \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{3}{4}\right)=\cos \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{3}{4}\right)=2 \cdot \frac{3}{5} \cdot \frac{4}{5}=\frac{24}{25} \\
& \cos \left(2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{7}\right)=\cos \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{7}{24}\right)=\frac{24}{25} .
\end{aligned}\)
The given expression \(=0\)

</details>
