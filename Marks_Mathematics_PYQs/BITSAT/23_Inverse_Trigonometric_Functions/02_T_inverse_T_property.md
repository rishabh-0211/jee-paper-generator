### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\cos \cot ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\cot \left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)$, then the value of $x$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{-1}{\sqrt{12}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2}{\sqrt{6}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{-2}{\sqrt{6}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have
$\cos \left(\cot ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\right)=\cot \left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)$
Let $\cot ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\alpha \Rightarrow \cot \alpha=\frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow \cos ^{-1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$
$\Rightarrow \cos \left(\cos ^{-1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\right)=\cot \left(\cot ^{-1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\right)$
$\Rightarrow \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \Rightarrow \sqrt{1-x^2}=\sqrt{5} x$
On squaring both sides, we get,
$1-x^2=5 x^2 \Rightarrow 1=6 x^2$
$\Rightarrow x= \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}}$
$\begin{array}{ll}& x=\frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \quad \text { [neglecting - ve sign] }\end{array}$

### Q2. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find the value of $\tan \left[2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{5}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right]$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-1 / 3$
- **(B)** $-7 / 17$
- **(C)** $-1 / 2$
- **(D)** $-1 / 4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $-7 / 17$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & 2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{5}=\tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{5}+\tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{5} \\ &=\tan ^{-1} \frac{\frac{1}{5}+\frac{1}{5}}{1-\frac{1}{5} \cdot \frac{1}{5}}=\tan ^{-1} \frac{2 / 5}{24 / 25}=\tan ^{-1} \frac{5}{12} \\ &=\tan \left(2 \tan ^{-1} \frac{1}{5}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\tan \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{5}{12}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \\ &=\frac{\tan \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{5}{12}\right)-\tan \frac{\pi}{4}}{1+\tan \left(\tan ^{-1} \frac{5}{12}\right) \tan \frac{\pi}{4}}=\frac{-\frac{7}{12}}{\frac{17}{12}}=-\frac{7}{17} \end{aligned}$

</details>
