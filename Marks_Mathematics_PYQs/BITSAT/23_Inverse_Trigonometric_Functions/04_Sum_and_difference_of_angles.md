### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)+\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{9}\right)$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2} \cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2} \sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2} \tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$
- **(D)** $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)+\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2}{9}\right)=\tan ^{-1}\left\{\frac{\frac{1}{4}+\frac{2}{9}}{1-\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{2}{9}}\right\}$
$=\tan ^{-1}\left\{\frac{9+8}{36-2}\right\}=\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{2 a}{1+a^{2}}\right)-\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{1-b^{2}}{1+b^{2}}\right)=\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2 x}{1-x^{2}}\right)$,

then what is the value of $x$ ?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a / b$
- **(B)** $a b$
- **(C)** $b / a$
- **(D)** $\frac{a-b}{1+a b}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{a-b}{1+a b}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given,

$\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{2 a}{1+a^{2}}\right)-\cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{1-b^{2}}{1+b^{2}}\right)=\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{2 x}{1-x^{2}}\right)$

$\therefore \quad 2 \tan ^{-1} a-2 \tan ^{-1} b=2 \tan ^{-1} x$

$\Rightarrow \tan ^{-1} a-\tan ^{-1} b=\tan ^{-1} x$

$\Rightarrow \quad \tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{a-b}{1+a b}\right)=\tan ^{-1} x$

$\Rightarrow \quad x=\frac{a-b}{1+a b}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $\cos ^{-1} x-\cos ^{-1} \frac{y}{2}=\alpha$,

then $4 x^{2}-4 x y \cos \alpha+y^{2}$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2 \sin 2 \alpha$
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** $4 \sin ^{2} \alpha$
- **(D)** $-4 \sin ^{2} \alpha$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4 \sin ^{2} \alpha$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\cos ^{-1} x-\cos ^{-1} \frac{y}{2}=\alpha$

$\Rightarrow \cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{x y}{2}+\sqrt{\left(1-x^{2}\right)\left(1-\frac{y^{2}}{4}\right)}\right)=\alpha$

$\Rightarrow \cos ^{-1}\left(\frac{x y+\sqrt{4-y^{2}-4 x^{2}+x^{2} y^{2}}}{2}\right)=\alpha$

$\Rightarrow 4 x^{2}+y^{2}-4 x y \cos \alpha=4 \sin ^{2} \alpha$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The greatest and least values of $\left(\sin ^{-1} x\right)^{2}+\left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)^{2}$ are respectively

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}$ and 0
- **(B)** $\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\frac{-\pi}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{5 \pi^{2}}{4}$ and $\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}$
- **(D)** $\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}$ and $\frac{-\pi^{2}}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{5 \pi^{2}}{4}$ and $\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $\left(\sin ^{-1} x\right)^{2}+\left(\cos ^{-1} x\right)^{2}$

$=\left(\sin ^{-1} x+\cos ^{-1} x\right)^{2}-2 \sin ^{-1} x \cdot \cos ^{-1} x$

$=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}-2 \sin ^{-1} x\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\sin ^{-1} x\right)$

$=\frac{\pi^{2}}{4}-\pi \sin ^{-1} x+2\left(\sin ^{-1} x\right)^{2}$

$=2\left[\left(\sin ^{-1} x\right)^{2}-\frac{\pi}{2} \sin ^{-1} x+\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}\right]$

$=2\left[\left(\sin ^{-1} x-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^{2}+\frac{\pi^{2}}{16}\right]$

Thus, the least value is $2\left(\frac{\pi^{2}}{16}\right)$ i.e. $\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}$

and the greatest value is $2\left[\left(\frac{-\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)^{2}+\frac{\pi^{2}}{16}\right]$ i.e. $\frac{5 \pi^{2}}{4} .$

</details>
