### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If the function $f(x)$, defined below, is continuous on the interval $[0,8]$, then
$f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{cc}x^2+a x+b & , 0 \leq x \lt 2 \\ 3 x+2, & 2 \leq x \leq 4 \\ 2 a x+5 b, & 4 \lt x \leq 8\end{array}\right.$

**Options:**

- **(A)** $a=3, b=-2$
- **(B)** $a=-3, b=2$
- **(C)** $a=-3, b=-2$
- **(D)** $a=3, b=2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $a=3, b=-2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here, $f(x)$ is continuous on the interval $[0,8]$.
So it will also be continuous on 2 and 4.
At $x=2$,
$\lim _{x \rightarrow 2^{-}} f(x)=\lim _{x \rightarrow 2^{+}} f(x)$
$\lim _{x \rightarrow 2^{-}}\left(x^2+\mathrm{ax}+\mathrm{b}\right)=\lim _{x \rightarrow 2^{+}}(3 x+2)$
$4+2 a+b=3(2)+2$
$\therefore 2 a+b=4$    ...(i)
At $x=4$
$\lim _{x \rightarrow 4^{-}} f(x)=\lim _{x \rightarrow 4^{+}} f(x)$
$\lim _{x \rightarrow 4^{-}}(3 x+2)=\lim _{x \rightarrow 4^{+}} 2 a x+5 b$
$3(4)+2=2 a(4)+5 b$
$\therefore 8 a+5 b=14$  .......(ii)
On solving Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$a=3$ and $b=-2$.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The mean of n items is X . If the first item is increased by 1 , second by 2 and so on, the new mean is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\bar{X}+\frac{x}{2}$
- **(B)** $\bar{X}+x$
- **(C)** $\overline{\mathrm{X}}+\frac{\mathrm{n}+1}{2}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\overline{\mathrm{X}}+\frac{\mathrm{n}+1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the items be $\mathrm{a}_1, \mathrm{a}_2, \ldots \ldots . ., \mathrm{a}_{\mathrm{n}}$
then $\bar{X}=\frac{a_1+a_2+\ldots \ldots .+a_n}{n}$
Now, according to the given condition:
$\bar{X}=\frac{\left(a_1+1\right)+\left(a_2+2\right)+\ldots \ldots \ldots+\left(a_n+n\right)}{n}$
$=\overline{\mathrm{X}}+\frac{1+2+3+\ldots \ldots \ldots+n}{n}=\bar{X}+\frac{n(n+1)}{2 n}$
(using sum of n natural nos.)
$=\overline{\mathrm{X}}+\frac{\mathrm{n}+1}{2}$.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The mean of five observations is $4$ and their variance is $5.2$. If three of these observations are $1$,$2$ and $6$ , then the other two are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $2$ and $9$
- **(B)** $3$ and $8$
- **(C)** $4$ and $7$
- **(D)** $5$ and $6$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $4$ and $7$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let the two unknown items be $x$ and $y$. Then,
$\operatorname{Mean}=4 \Rightarrow \frac{1+2+6+x+y}{5}=4$
$\Rightarrow \quad x+y=11\dots(i)$
and variance $=5.2$
$\Rightarrow \frac{1^2+2^2+6^2+x^2+y^2}{5}-(\text { mean })^2=5.2$
$\Rightarrow \quad 41+x^2+y^2=5(5.2+16)$
$\begin{array}{ll}\Rightarrow & 41+x^2+y^2=106 \\ \Rightarrow & x^2+y^2=65\end{array}$
On solving Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$x=4, y=7 \text { or } x=7, y=4$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Find the value of \(\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sqrt{\left(1+x^2\right)}-\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x^2}\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 5

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}-\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x^2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{1+x^2}+\sqrt{1-x^2}}{\sqrt{1+x^2}+\sqrt{1-x^2}} \\
\Rightarrow & \lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{1+x^2-1+x^2}{x^2\left(\sqrt{1+x^2}+\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)} \\
\Rightarrow & \lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{2 x^2}{x^2\left(\sqrt{1+x^2}+\sqrt{1-x^2}\right)}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{1}+\sqrt{1}}=\frac{2}{2}=1
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
