### Q1. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Mean of 25 observations was found to be 78.4. But later on it was found that 96 was misread 69 . The correct mean is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 79.24
- **(B)** 79.48
- **(C)** 80.10
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 79.48**

**Detailed Solution:**

Mean \(\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{n}\) or \(\Sigma x=n \bar{x}\)
\(\Sigma \mathrm{x}=25 \times 78.4=1960\)
But this \(\Sigma \mathrm{x}\) is incorrect as 96 was misread as 69 .
\(\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \text { correct } \Sigma \mathrm{x}=1960+(96-69)=1987 \\
& \therefore \text { correct mean }=\frac{1987}{25}=79.48
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
