### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Find the mean deviation about the mean for the data $4,7,8,9,10,12,13,17$

**Options:**

- **(A)** 3
- **(B)** 24
- **(C)** 10
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 3**

**Detailed Solution:**

Arithmetic mean $\bar{x}$ of $4,7,8,9,10,12,13$, 17 is
$\bar{x}=$ $\frac{4+7+8+9+10+12+13+17}{8}=\frac{80}{8}=10$
$\Sigma\left|x_i-\bar{x}\right|=6+3+2+1+0+2+3+7=24$
$\therefore$ Mean deviation about mean
$=\text { M.D. }(\bar{x})=\frac{\sum\left|x_i-\bar{x}\right|}{n}=\frac{24}{8}=3$

</details>
