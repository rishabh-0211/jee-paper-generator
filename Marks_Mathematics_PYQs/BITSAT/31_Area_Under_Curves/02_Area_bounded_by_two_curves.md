### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The area enclosed by the curves $y=x^3$ and $y=\sqrt{x}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{5}{3}$ sq. units
- **(B)** $\frac{5}{4}$ sq. units
- **(C)** $\frac{5}{12}$ sq. unit
- **(D)** $\frac{12}{5}$ sq. units

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{5}{12}$ sq. unit**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given curves are $y=x^3$ and $y=\sqrt{x}$
Both curves intersects at $x=0$ and $x=1$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/z_KznZMe4vfzCzaJXSNJ_XvekF3NnRaPnjhDPnsu5nk.original.fullsize.png"/>
Shaded region is required area
$\begin{aligned}
A & =\left|\int_0^1\left(x^3-\sqrt{x}\right) d x\right|=\left|\left[\frac{x^4}{4}-\frac{2 x^{3 / 2}}{3}\right]_0^1\right| \\
& =\left|\left[\frac{1}{4}-\frac{2}{3}\right]\right| \\
& =\frac{5}{12} \text { sq. unit }
\end{aligned}$

</details>

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, $x=y^2-2$ and $x=y$.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/cC7sDC8y5-9eTwJAFhGUAwnXFfpFeZcB1zZr6N7rFhM.original.fullsize.png"/>

$\begin{aligned}

### Q3. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If the area bounded by the curves $y=a x^2$ and $x$ $=a y^2,(a\gt0)$ is 3 sq units, then the value of $a$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{2}{3}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(C)** $1$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have given, $y=a x^2$    ....(i)
and $x=a y^2$      ......(ii)
Put the value of $y$ by Eq. (i) in Eq. (ii), we get
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/AYIkM7DV4l9eiCiqDpgUdJvdN-otyvL0SyszpeihR4s.original.fullsize.png"/>
$x=a \times a^2 x^4 \Rightarrow x^4 a^3-x=0$
$x\left(x^3 a^3-1\right)=0 \Rightarrow x=0, \frac{1}{2}$
When, $x=0 \Rightarrow y=0$ and $x=\frac{1}{a} \Rightarrow y=\frac{1}{a}$
Here, points of intersection of curves $y=a x^2$
and $x=a y^2$ are $(0,0)$ and $\left(\frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{a}\right)$.
$\therefore \quad$ Required area
$A=\int_{x=a}^{x=b}\left[f_2(x)-f_1(x)\right] d x$
$3=\int_0^{1 / a}\left(\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{a}}-a x^2\right) d x$
$3=\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{a}} \times \frac{2}{3} x^{3 / 2}-\frac{a x^3}{3}\right]_0^{1 / a}$
$3=\frac{2}{3 \sqrt{a}}\left[\left(\frac{1}{a}\right)^{3 / 2}\right]-\frac{a}{3}\left[\left(\frac{1}{a}\right)^3\right]$
$3=\frac{2}{3 \sqrt{a}} \times \frac{1}{a \sqrt{a}}-\frac{a}{3} \times \frac{1}{a^3}$
$3=\frac{2}{3 a^2}-\frac{1}{3 a^2} \Rightarrow 3=\frac{2-1}{3 a^2} \Rightarrow 9 a^2=1$
$a^2=\frac{1}{9} \Rightarrow a=\frac{1}{3}$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The area bounded by curves $(x-1)^{2}+y^{2}=1$ and $x^{2}+y^{2}=1$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\left(\frac{2 \pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$
- **(B)** $\frac{2 \pi}{3}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2 \pi}{3}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\left(\frac{2 \pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given circles are $x^{2}+y^{2}=1 \ldots$ (i) and $(x-1)^{2}+y^{2}=1 \ldots$ (ii)

Centre of (i) is $\mathrm{O}(0,0)$ and radius $=1$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/h-abKd3JCjcK1BeOZKRiAZ6yaZvRisMxeLCXMht6Nb0.original.fullsize.png">


Both these circle are symmetrical about $x$-axis solving (i) and (ii), we get, $-2 x+1=0$

$\Rightarrow x=\frac{1}{2}$

$\begin{array}{l}

\text { then } y^{2}=1-\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}=34 \\

\Rightarrow y=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}

\end{array}$

$\therefore$ The points of intersection are

$\mathrm{P}\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ and $\mathrm{Q}\left(\frac{1}{2},-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$

It is clear from the figure that the shaded portion in region whose area is required.

$\therefore$ Required area = area OQAPO

$=2 \times$ area of the region OLAP

$=2 \times($ area of the region $O L P O+$ area of $L A P L$ )

$=2\left[\int_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}} \sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}} d x+\int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1} \sqrt{1-x^{2}} \mathrm{dx}\right]$

$=2\left[\frac{(x-1) \sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}}{2}+\frac{1}{2} \sin ^{-1}(x-1)\right]_{0}^{\frac{1}{2}}+2\left[\frac{x \sqrt{1-x^{2}}}{2}+\frac{1}{2} \sin ^{-1} x\right]_{\frac{1}{2}}^{1}$

$=-\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}+\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{-1}{2}\right)-\sin ^{-1}(-1)+0+\sin ^{-1}(1)-\left(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}+\sin ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\right)$

$=\left(\frac{2 \pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ sq. units.

</details>

### Q6. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The area bounded by the curves $x+2 y^{2}=0$ and $x+3 y^{2}=1$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1 sq. unit
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{3}$ sq. units
- **(C)** $\frac{2}{3}$ sq. units
- **(D)** $\frac{4}{3}$ sq. units

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{4}{3}$ sq. units**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $x+2 y^{2}=0$

$\Rightarrow y^{2}=-\frac{x}{2} \ldots \text { (i) }$

a parabola with vertex $(0,0)$ and $x+3 y^{2}=1$

$\Rightarrow y^{2}=\frac{1-x}{3}=-\left(\frac{x-1}{3}\right) \ldots$ (ii), a parabola with vertex $(1,0)$

Solving (i) and (ii), we get $y=\pm 1$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/1uUb_Wysw315Rl0DFYCFG6Ly7ChrUi0sAteTJV3PPqo.original.fullsize.png">


$\mathrm{A}=\int_{-1}^{1}\left(\left(1-3 y^{2}\right)-\left(-2 y^{2}\right)\right) d y$

$=2 \int_{0}^{1}\left(1-y^{2}\right) d y=2\left[y-\frac{y^{3}}{3}\right]_{0}^{1}$

$=\frac{4}{3}$ sq.units

</details>

### Q9. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The area of the region $\mathrm{R}=\left\{(x, y):|x| \leq|y|\right.$ and $x^{2}$ $\left.+y^{2} \leq 1\right\}$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3 \pi}{8}$ sq units
- **(B)** $\frac{5 \pi}{8} \mathrm{sq}$ units
- **(C)** $\frac{\pi}{2}$ sq units
- **(D)** $\frac{\pi}{8}$ sq unit

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{\pi}{2}$ sq units**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/5nCmf5KCGMlyNK8_pS9tpwndw7e6OZXR_vf-JKknoYU.original.fullsize.png">


Required area $=4$ (Area of the shaded region in first quadrant)

$$

=4 \int_{0}^{1 \sqrt{2}}\left(y_{1}-y_{2}\right) d x=4 \int_{0}^{1 \sqrt{2}}\left(\sqrt{1-x^{2}}-x\right) d x

$$

$=4\left[\frac{x}{2} \sqrt{1-x^{2}}+\frac{1}{2} \sin ^{-1} x-\frac{x^{2}}{2}\right]_{0}^{1 / \sqrt{2}}$

$=4\left[\frac{1}{2 \sqrt{2}} \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{1}{4}\right]$

$=4\left[\frac{1}{4}+\frac{\pi}{8}-\frac{1}{4}\right]=\frac{4 \pi}{8}=\frac{\pi}{2}$ sq units

</details>
