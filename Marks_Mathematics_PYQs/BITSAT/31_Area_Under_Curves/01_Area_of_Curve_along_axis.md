### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The area enclosed between the curve $y=\log _e(x+e)$ and the coordinate axes is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 3
- **(D)** 4

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/ZM6sWNxcXPEqQvB8_byL2CiWCn7MvniGotvUdZThfPs.original.fullsize.png">

Required area $A=\int_{1-e}^0 y d x=\int_{1-e}^0 \log _e(x+e) d x$
put $x+e=t \Rightarrow d x=d t$ also when $x=1-e, t=$ 1 and when $x=0, t=e$
$\therefore A=\int_1^e \log _e t d t=\left[t \log _e t-t\right]_1^e$
$e-e-0+1=1$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

What is the area bounded by \(y=\tan x, y=0\) and \(x=\frac{\pi}{4} ?\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\ell n ~2~ \mathrm{sq}\). units
- **(B)** \(\frac{\ell n 2}{2} ~\mathrm{sq}\). units
- **(C)** \(2~(\ell n ~2)~ \mathrm{sq}\). units
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\frac{\ell n 2}{2} ~\mathrm{sq}\). units**

**Detailed Solution:**

Required area \(=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \tan x d x\)
\(=\ln |\sec 1|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}=\ln \sqrt{2}=\frac{\ln 2}{2}\)

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2010

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If \(\mathrm{I}_{\mathrm{m}}=\int_1^{\mathrm{e}}(\ln \mathrm{x})^{\mathrm{m}} \mathrm{dx}\), where \(\mathrm{m} \in \mathrm{N}\), then \(I_{10}+10 I_9\) is equal to -

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\mathrm{e}^{10}\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{\mathrm{e}^{10}}{10}\)
- **(C)** \(\mathrm{e}\)
- **(D)** \(\mathrm{e}-1\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) \(\mathrm{e}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \mathrm{I}_{10}=\int_1^{\mathrm{e}} 1 \cdot(\ln \mathrm{x})^{10} \mathrm{dx}=\left[(\ln \mathrm{x})^{10} \mathrm{x}\right]_1^{\mathrm{e}} \\
& -\int_1^{\mathrm{e}} 10(\ln \mathrm{x})^9 \cdot \frac{1}{\mathrm{x}} \cdot \mathrm{xdx}=\mathrm{e}-0-10 \int_1^{\mathrm{e}}(\ln \mathrm{x})^9 \mathrm{dx} \\
& =\mathrm{e}-10 \mathrm{I}_9+10 \mathrm{I}_9=\mathrm{e}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
