### Q1. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In how many ways can 5 prizes be distributed among 4 boys when every boy can take one or more prizes?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1024
- **(B)** 625
- **(C)** 120
- **(D)** 600

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 1024**

**Detailed Solution:**

First prize may be given to any one of the 4 boys, hence first prize can be distributed in 4 ways. similarly every one of second, third, fourth and fifth prizes can also be given in 4 ways. the number of ways of their distribution $=4 \times 4 \times 4 \times 4 \times 4=4^{5}=1024$

</details>
