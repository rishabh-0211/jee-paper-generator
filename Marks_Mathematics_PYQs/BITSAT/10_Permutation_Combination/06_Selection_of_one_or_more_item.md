### Q1. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Total number of ways of selecting 2 white squares on a normal chess board if the squares are not from the same row or column is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 480
- **(B)** 496
- **(C)** 412
- **(D)** 400

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 400**

**Detailed Solution:**

2 white squares can be selected in ${ }^{32} \mathrm{C}_{2}$ ways If they belong to some row or same column then $2\left(8 \cdot{ }^{4} \mathrm{C}_{2}\right)$ ways.
