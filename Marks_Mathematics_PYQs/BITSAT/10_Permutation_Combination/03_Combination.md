### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

At an election a voter may vote for any number of candidates not exceeding the number to be elected. If 4 candidates are to be elected out of the 12 contested in the election and voter votes for at least one candidate, then the number of way of selections

**Options:**

- **(A)** $793$
- **(B)** $298$
- **(C)** $781$
- **(D)** $1585$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $793$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total contested candidates $=12$
4 candidates are to be elected and voter votes for at least one candidate then total number of ways of selections.
$={ }^{12} \mathrm{C}_1+{ }^{12} \mathrm{C}_2+{ }^{12} \mathrm{C}_3+{ }^{12} \mathrm{C}_4$
$=12+\frac{12 \times 11}{2 \times 1}+\frac{12 \times 11 \times 10}{3 \times 2 \times 1}+\frac{12 \times 11 \times 10 \times 9}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1}$
$=12+66+220+495=793$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2016

If three vertices of a regular hexagon are chosen at random, then the chance that they form an equilateral triangle is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{5}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{10}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{10}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Three vertices can be selected in ${ }^{6} \mathrm{C}_{3}$ ways.

$\mathrm{p}=\frac{2}{{ }^{6} \mathrm{C}_{3}}=\frac{2}{20}=\frac{1}{10}$

### Q5. BITSAT 2014

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In how many ways can a committee of 5 made out 6 men and 4 women containing atleast one woman?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 246
- **(B)** 222
- **(C)** 186
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 246**

**Detailed Solution:**

A committee of 5 out of $6+4=10$ can be made in ${ }^{10} \mathrm{C}_{5}=252$ ways. If no woman is to be included, thennumber of ways $={ }^{5} \mathrm{C}_{5}=6$

$\therefore$ the required number $=252-6=246$

### Q7. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A box contains two white balls, three black balls and four red balls. In how many ways can three balls be drawn from the box if at least one black ball is to be included in the draw?

**Options:**

- **(A)** 64
- **(B)** 129
- **(C)** 84
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 64**

**Detailed Solution:**

Atleast one black ball can be drawn in the following ways

(1) one black and two other colour balls

$$

={ }^{3} \mathrm{C}_{1} \times{ }^{6} \mathrm{C}_{2}=3 \times 15=45

$$

(ii) two black and one other colour balls

$$

={ }^{3} \mathrm{C}_{2} \times{ }^{6} \mathrm{C}_{1}=3 \times 6=18

$$

(iii) All the three are black $={ }^{3} \mathrm{C}_{3} \times{ }^{6} \mathrm{C}_{0}=1$

$\therefore$ Req. no. of ways $=45+18+1=64$

</details>
