### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 3)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A person invites a party of 10 friends at dinner and place so that 4 are on one round table and 6 on the other round table. Total number of ways in which he can arrange the guests is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{10!}{6!}$
- **(B)** $\frac{10!}{24}$
- **(C)** $\frac{9!}{24}$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{10!}{24}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Total person $=10$
Two groups are formed containing 4 and 6 persons.
$\therefore$ Total ways of formation of groups $=\frac{10!}{4!6!}$
Now, 4 persons can be arranged on round table
$=(4-1)!=3!$
and 6 persons can be arranged on round table $=(6-1)$ !
$=5!$
$\therefore$ Total ways of arranging the guest
$=\frac{10!}{4!6!} \times 3!\times 5!=\frac{10!}{24}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In how many ways can 5 boys and 5 girls be seated at a round table so that no two girls may be together?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 !$
- **(B)** $5 !$
- **(C)** $4 !+5 !$
- **(D)** $4 ! \times 5 !$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $4 ! \times 5 !$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Leaving one seat vacant between two boys, 5 boys may be seated in $4 !$ ways. Then at remaining 5 seats, 5 girls any sit in $5 !$ ways. Hence the required number $=4 ! \times 5 !$

</details>
