### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If ${ }^{22} P_{r+1}:{ }^{20} P_{r+2}=11: 52$, then $r$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $3$
- **(B)** $5$
- **(C)** $7$
- **(D)** $9$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $7$**

**Detailed Solution:**

${ }^{22} P_{r+1}:{ }^{20} P_{r+2}=11: 52$
or $(21-r)(20-r)(19-r)=52 \times 2 \times 21$
$\Rightarrow(21-r)(20-r)(19-r)=14 \times 13 \times 12$
$\begin{aligned} & \Rightarrow(21-r)(20-r)(19-r)=(21-7)(20-7)(19 \\ & -7)\end{aligned}$
$\Rightarrow r=7$

</details>
