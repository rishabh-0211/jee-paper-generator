### Q1. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The digits \(\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B}\) and \(\mathrm{C}\) are such that the three digit numbers \(A 88,6 \mathrm{~B} 8,86 \mathrm{C}\) are divisible by 72 then the determinant \(\left|\begin{array}{ccc}\text { A } & 6 & 8 \\ 8 & B & 6 \\ 8 & 8 & C\end{array}\right|\) is divisible by

**Options:**

- **(A)** 72
- **(B)** 144
- **(C)** 288
- **(D)** 216

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) 72**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(R_3 \rightarrow 100 R_1+10 R_2+R_3\)
\(\Rightarrow\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
A & 6 & 8 \\
8 & B & 6 \\
8 & 8 & C
\end{array}\right|=\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
A & 6 & 8 \\
8 & B & 6 \\
A 88 & 6 B C & 86 C
\end{array}\right|\)
which is divisible by 72.

</details>
