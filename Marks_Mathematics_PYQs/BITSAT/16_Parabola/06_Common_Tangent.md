### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of a common tangent to the parabolas $y=x^2$ and $y=-(x-2)^2$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $y=4(x-2)$
- **(B)** $y=4(x-1)$
- **(C)** $y=4(x+1)$
- **(D)** $y=4(x+2)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $y=4(x-1)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of tangent of parabola $y=x^2$ be 
$t x=y+a t^2$ ...(i)
$y=t x-\frac{t^2}{4}$
Solve with $\mathrm{y}=-(\mathrm{x}-2)^2$
$t x-\frac{t^2}{4}=-(x-2)^2$
$x^2+x(t-4)-\frac{t^2}{4}+4=0$
Here, Discriminant $=0$.
$(t-4)^2-4 \cdot\left(4-\frac{t^2}{4}\right)=0 \Rightarrow t^2-4 t=0 \Rightarrow t=0$
or $\mathrm{t}=4$
Put value of $t$ in eq. (i), then $y=4(x-1)$.

</details>
