### Q1. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Consider the function $\mathrm{f}$ in $\mathrm{A}=\mathrm{R}-\left\{\frac{2}{3}\right\}$ defined as $f(x)=\frac{4 x+3}{6 x-4}$, then $f^{-1}$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3+4 x}{6 x-4}$
- **(B)** $\frac{6 x-4}{3+4 x}$
- **(C)** $\frac{3-4 x}{6 x-4}$
- **(D)** $\frac{9+2 x}{6 x-4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{3+4 x}{6 x-4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given $f(x)=\frac{4 x+3}{6 x-4}$
Let $y=\frac{4 x+3}{6 x-4}$,
$\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow 6 x y-4 y=4 x+3 \Rightarrow x(6 y-4)=3+4 y \\
& \Rightarrow x=\frac{3+4 y}{6 y-4} \\
& f^{-1}(x)=\frac{3+4 x}{6 x-4}
\end{aligned}$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If \(f(x)=\frac{1-x}{1+x}\) the domain of \(f^{-1}(x)\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\mathrm{R}\)
- **(B)** \(\mathrm{R}-\{-1\}\)
- **(C)** \((-\infty,-1)\)
- **(D)** \((-1, \infty)\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\mathrm{R}-\{-1\}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let \(f(x)=y\). Then \(\frac{1-x}{1+x}=y\)
\(\Rightarrow x=\frac{1-y}{1+y} \Rightarrow f^{-1}(y)=\frac{1-y}{1+y}\)
Thus, \(f^{-1}(x)=\frac{1-x}{1+x}\)
Clearly, \(\mathrm{f}^{-1}(\mathrm{x})\) is defined for \(1+\mathrm{x} \neq 0\).
Hence, domain of \(\mathrm{f}^{-1}(\mathrm{x})\) is \(\mathrm{R}-\{-1\}\)

</details>
