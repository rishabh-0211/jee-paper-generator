### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

$f(x)=\sin x+\cos x, g(x)=x^2-1$, then $g(f(x))$ is invertible if

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{-\pi}{4} \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$
- **(B)** $\frac{-\pi}{2} \leq x \leq 0$
- **(C)** $\frac{-\pi}{2} \leq x \leq \pi$
- **(D)** $0 \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{-\pi}{4} \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that, $f(x)=\sin x+\cos x$
$g(x)=x^2-1$
$g[f(x)]=(\sin x+\cos x)^2-1$
$=\sin ^2 x+\cos ^2 x+2 \sin x \cos x-1$
$=1+\sin 2 x-1=\sin 2 x$
$\left(\because \sin ^2 x+\cos ^2 x=1, \sin x=2 \sin x \cos x\right)$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/das8psdkef8Jpeb0nqSuEub-8U_ijwhvC_pHDla6qhI.original.fullsize.png"/>
Among the given options, $\sin 2 x$ is monotonous
(here strictly increasing) in $-\frac{\pi}{4} \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$.
So, $g(f(x))$ is invertible in $-\frac{\pi}{4} \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$.

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The period of $\tan 3 \theta$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\pi$
- **(B)** $3 \pi 4$
- **(C)** $\pi / 2$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) None of these**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\tan \theta$ is of period $\pi$ so that $\tan 3 \theta$ is of period $\pi / 3$

### Q3. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The value of \(\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\left(4^x-1\right)^3}{\sin \frac{x^2}{4} \log (1+3 x)}\), is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\frac{4}{3}(\ln 4)^2\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{4}{3}(\ln 4)^3\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{3}{2}(\ln 4)^2\)
- **(D)** \(\frac{3}{2}(\ln 4)^3\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\frac{4}{3}(\ln 4)^3\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\left(4^x-1\right)^3}{\sin \frac{x^2}{4} \log (1+3 x)} \\
& =\lim _{x \rightarrow 0} \frac{\left(4^x-1\right)^3}{x^3} \cdot \frac{(x / 2)^2}{\sin x^2 / 4} \cdot \frac{3 x}{\log (1+3 x)} \cdot \frac{4}{3} \\
& =\frac{4}{3}\left(\log _e 4\right)^3 \cdot 1 \cdot \log _e(e)=\frac{4}{3}\left(\log _e 4\right)^3 .
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
