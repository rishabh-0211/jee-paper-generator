### Q2. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let the function $g:(-\infty, \infty) \rightarrow\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ be given by $g(u)=2 \tan ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$. Then, g is

**Options:**

- **(A)** even and is strictly increasing in $(0, \infty)$
- **(B)** odd and is strictly decreasing in $(-\infty, \infty)$
- **(C)** odd and is strictly increasing in $(-\infty, \infty)$
- **(D)** neither even nor odd, but is strictly increasing in $(-\infty, \infty)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) odd and is strictly increasing in $(-\infty, \infty)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given that $g(u)=2 \tan ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$
$\therefore \quad g(-u)=2 \tan ^{-1}\left(e^{-u}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}=$
$2 \tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{e^u}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$
$=2 \cot ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}=2\left[\frac{\pi}{2}-\tan ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)\right]-\frac{\pi}{2}$
$=\pi-2 \tan ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$
$=\frac{\pi}{2}-2 \tan ^{-1}\left(e^u\right)=-g(u)$
$\therefore \quad g$ is an odd function.
Also $g^{\prime}(u)=\frac{2 e^u}{1+e^{2 u}}\gt0, \forall u \in(-\infty, \infty)$
$\therefore g$ is strictly increasing on $(-\infty, \infty)$.

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If $\mathrm{f}$ is an even function and $\mathrm{g}$ is an odd function, then the function fog is

**Options:**

- **(A)** an even function
- **(B)** an odd function
- **(C)** neither even nor odd
- **(D)** a periodic function

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) an even function**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $f o g(-x)=f[g(-x)]=f[-g(x)]$ $(\because g$ is odd $)$
$\begin{array}{l}
=\mathrm{f}[\mathrm{g}(\mathrm{x})] \quad(\because \mathrm{fis} \text { even }) \\
=\operatorname{fog}(\mathrm{x}) \forall \mathrm{x} \in \mathrm{R}
\end{array}$
$\therefore$ fog is an even function.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

If \(f(x)\) is a function that is odd and even simultaneously, then \(\mathrm{f}(3)-\mathrm{f}(2)\) is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** -1
- **(C)** 0
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 0**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{x})=0 ~\forall~ \mathrm{x} \in \mathrm{R} \Rightarrow \mathrm{f}(3)-\mathrm{f}(2)=0\)

</details>
