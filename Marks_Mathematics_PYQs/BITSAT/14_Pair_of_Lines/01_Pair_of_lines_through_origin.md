### Q1. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If \(\int_0^n[x] \mathrm{dx}=66\), then \(n=\)

**Options:**

- **(A)** 24
- **(B)** 9
- **(C)** 12
- **(D)** 7

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) 12**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& \int_0^n[x] d x=\int_0^1 0 d x+\int_1^2 1 d x+\int_2^3 2 d x+\ldots \ldots+ \\
& \int_{n-1}^n(n-1) d x \\
& =1+2+3+\ldots .+(n-1)=\frac{(n-1) n}{2}=66 \\
& \Rightarrow n(n-1)=132 \Rightarrow n=12
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
