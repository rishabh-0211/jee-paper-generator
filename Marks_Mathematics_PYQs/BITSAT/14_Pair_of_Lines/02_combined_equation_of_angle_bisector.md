### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\mathrm{A}(3,2,0), \mathrm{B}(5,3,2), \mathrm{C}(-9,6,-3)$ are three points forming a triangle. AD , the bisector of angle BAC meets BC in D. Find the co-ordinates of D.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{19}{8}, \frac{57}{15}, \frac{57}{15}$
- **(B)** $\frac{19}{8}, \frac{57}{16}, \frac{17}{16}$
- **(C)** $(2,3,0)$
- **(D)** $(4,5,6)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{19}{8}, \frac{57}{16}, \frac{17}{16}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since $A D$ is the bisector of $\angle B A C$.
$\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{BD}}{\mathrm{DC}}=\frac{\mathrm{AB}}{\mathrm{AC}}$    ....(i)
Now,
$\mathrm{AB}=\sqrt{(5-3)^2+(3-2)^2+(2-0)^2}$
$=\sqrt{4+1+4}=\sqrt{9}=3$
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/CSbNXtILB1pcB-LkopQmKyTmNxU6GkvBypJfLzHuKOg.original.fullsize.png"/>
$A C=\sqrt{(-9-3)^2+(6-2)^2+(-3-0)^2}$
$=\sqrt{144+16+9}=13$
$\therefore \quad \frac{\mathrm{BD}}{\mathrm{DC}}=\frac{3}{13}$
Hence, D divides BC in the ratio $3: 13$
The co-ordinates of D are
$\left(\frac{3(-9)+13(5)}{3+13}, \frac{3(6)+13(3)}{3+13}, \frac{3(-3)+13(2)}{3+13}\right)$
$=\left(\frac{19}{8}, \frac{57}{16}, \frac{17}{16}\right)$
