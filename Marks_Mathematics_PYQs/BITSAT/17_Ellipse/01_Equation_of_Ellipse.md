### Q1. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

An ellipse has $\mathrm{OB}$ as semi minor axis, $\mathrm{F}$ and $\mathrm{F}^{\prime}$ its focii and the angle FBF' is a right angle. Then the eccentricity of the ellipse is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{4}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\because \angle \mathrm{FBF}^{\prime}=90^{\circ}$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{FB}^{2}+\mathrm{F}^{\prime} \mathrm{B}^{2}=\mathrm{FF}^{\prime 2}$

$\therefore\left(\sqrt{a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}}\right)^{2}+\left(\sqrt{a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}}\right)^{2}=(2 a e)^{2}$

$\Rightarrow 2\left(a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}\right)=4 a^{2} e^{2}$

$\Rightarrow e^{2}=\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/qqG2CtULR4Qizjzl6dtVQNINLlZsYB1djhzAAe6QstE.original.fullsize.png">


Also $e^{2}=1-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}=1-e^{2}$

$\begin{array}{l}

\Rightarrow 2 e^{2}=1 \\

\Rightarrow e=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}

\end{array}$

</details>

### Q2. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The eccentricity of the ellipse whose major axis is three times the minor axis is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- **(B)** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let a be the major axis and $b$, the minor axis of the ellipse, then 3 minor axis = major axis.

$\Rightarrow 3 b=a$

Eccentricity is given by:

$\begin{array}{l}

b^{2}=a^{2}\left(1-e^{2}\right) \\

\Rightarrow b^{2}=9 b^{2}\left(1-e^{2}\right) \\

\Rightarrow \frac{1}{9}=\left(1-e^{2}\right) \\

\Rightarrow e^{2}=\frac{8}{9} \\

\Rightarrow e=\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}

\end{array}$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The eccentricity of an ellipse, with its centre at the origin, is $1 / 2$. If one of the directrices is $\mathrm{x}=4$ then the equation of the ellipse is:

**Options:**

- **(A)** $4 x^{2}+3 y^{2}=1$
- **(B)** $3 x^{2}+4 y^{2}=12$
- **(C)** $4 x^{2}+3 y^{2}=12$
- **(D)** $3 x^{2}+4 y^{2}=1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $3 x^{2}+4 y^{2}=12$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{e}=\frac{1}{2} .$ Directrix $, \mathrm{x}=\frac{\mathrm{a}}{\mathrm{e}}=4$

$$

\therefore \mathrm{a}=4 \times \frac{1}{2}=2 \quad \therefore \mathrm{b}=2 \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{4}}=\sqrt{3}

$$

Equation of ellispe is

$$

\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1 \Rightarrow 3 x^{2}+4 y^{2}=12

$$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2013

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

$\mathrm{S}$ and $\mathrm{T}$ are the foci of an ellipse and $\mathrm{B}$ is an end of the minor axis. If STB is an equilateral triangle, then the eccentricity of the ellipse is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{4}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(D)** $\frac{2}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let eq. of ellipse be $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$, $\mathrm{S}$ is $(-\mathrm{ae}, 0), \mathrm{T}$ is $(\mathrm{ae}, 0)$ and $\mathrm{B}$ is $(0, \mathrm{~b})$

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/oOGjD6NyVDk2FaWoimoG2G8B4w4uebxeLD-nKEOVzGg.original.fullsize.png">


$\Rightarrow \mathrm{SB}=\sqrt{(0+\mathrm{ae})^{2}+\mathrm{b}^{2}}$

Also $\mathrm{SB}^{2}=\mathrm{ST}^{2} \Rightarrow 4 \mathrm{a}^{2} \mathrm{e}^{2}=\mathrm{a}^{2} \mathrm{e}^{2}+\mathrm{b}^{2}$

$\Rightarrow 3 a^{2} e^{2}=a^{2}\left(1-e^{2}\right)=a^{2}-a^{2} e^{2}$

$\Rightarrow 4 a^{2} e^{2}=a^{2} \Rightarrow e^{2}=\frac{1}{4} \Rightarrow e=\frac{1}{2}$

</details>

- **(D)** $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned} & \because \angle F B F^{\prime}=90^{\circ} \Rightarrow F B^{2}+F^{\prime} B^{2}=F F^{\prime 2} \\ \therefore &\left(\sqrt{a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}}\right)^{2}+\left(\sqrt{a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}}\right)^{2}=(2 a e)^{2} \\ & \Rightarrow 2\left(a^{2} e^{2}+b^{2}\right)=4 a^{2} e^{2} \Rightarrow e^{2}=\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}} \ldots(1) \end{aligned}$

### Q7. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the ellipse with focus at \(( \pm 5,0)\) and \(x=\frac{36}{5}\) as one directrix is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\frac{x^2}{36}+\frac{y^2}{25}=1\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{x^2}{36}+\frac{y^2}{11}=1\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{11}=1\)
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\frac{x^2}{36}+\frac{y^2}{11}=1\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have ae \(=5\) [Since focus is \(( \pm \mathrm{ae}, 0)\)]
and \(\frac{\mathrm{a}}{\mathrm{e}}=\frac{36}{5} \quad\left[\right.\) since directrix is \(\left.\mathrm{x}= \pm \frac{\mathrm{a}}{\mathrm{e}}\right]\)
On solving we get \(\mathrm{a}=6\)
\(\text { and } \mathrm{e}=\frac{5}{6} \Rightarrow \mathrm{b}^2=\mathrm{a}^2\left(1-\mathrm{e}^2\right)=36\left(1-\frac{25}{36}\right)=11\)
Thus, the required equation of the ellipse is
\(\frac{x^2}{36}+\frac{y^2}{11}=1\)

</details>
