### Q1. BITSAT 2024 (Memory Based Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If the angle made by the tangent at the point $\left(x_0, y_0\right)$ on the curve $x=12(t+\sin t \cos t), y=12$
$(1+\sin t)^2, 0 \lt t \lt \frac{\pi}{2}$, with the positive x -axis is $\frac{\pi}{3}$, then $y_0$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $6(3+2 \sqrt{2})$
- **(B)** $3(7+4 \sqrt{3})$
- **(C)** $27$
- **(D)** $48$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $27$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given equation of curve
$x=12(t+\sin t \cos t), y=12(1+\sin t)^2$
Differentiate w.r.t ' $t$ ',
$\begin{aligned}
& \frac{d x}{d t}=12\left(1+\cos ^2 t-\sin ^2 t\right) \\
& \frac{d x}{d t}=12(1+\cos 2 t) \text { and } \frac{d y}{d t} \\
& =24(1+\sin t) \cos t \\
& \frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{2(1+\sin t) \times \cos t}{1+\cos 2 t}
\end{aligned}$

### Q2. BITSAT 2023 (Memory Based Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The curve given by $x+y=e^{x y}$ has a tangent parallel to the Y-axis at the point

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(0,1)$
- **(B)** $(1,0)$
- **(C)** $(1,1)$
- **(D)** None of these

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $(1,0)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\because x+y=e^{x y}$
Differentiating w.r.t. $x$, we get
$1+\frac{d y}{d x}=e^{x y}\left[y+x \frac{d y}{d x}\right]$
$\Rightarrow \quad \frac{d y}{d x}\left(1-x e^{x y}\right)=y e^{x y}-1$
$\Rightarrow \frac{d y}{d x}=\frac{y e^{x y}-1}{1-x e^{x y}} \because \frac{d y}{d x}=\infty$,
as tangent is parallel to $\mathrm{Y}$-axis
$\Rightarrow 1-x e^{x y}=0$
$\therefore \mathrm{xe}^{\mathrm{xy}}=1$
This holds, when $x=1$ and $y=0$

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2021

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

The points at which the tangent passes through the origin for the curve $y=4 x^{3}-2 x^{5}$ are

**Options:**

- **(A)** $(0,0),(2,1)$ and $(-1,-2)$
- **(B)** $(0,0),(2,1)$ and $(-2,-1)$
- **(C)** $(2,0),(2,1)$ and $(-3,1)$
- **(D)** $(0,0),(1,2)$ and $(-1,-2)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $(0,0),(1,2)$ and $(-1,-2)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the given curve is

$\begin{array}{l}

y=4 x^{3}-2 x^{5} \\

\frac{\mathrm{dy}}{\mathrm{dx}}=12 x^{2}-10 x^{4}

\end{array}$

Therefore, the slope of the tangent at point $(x, y)$ is $12 x^{2}-10 x^{4}$. The equation of the tangent at $(x, y)$ is given by

$\mathrm{Y}-y=\left(12 x^{2}-10 x^{4}\right)(\mathrm{X}-x) \ldots \text { (i) }$

When, the tangent passes through the origin $(0,0)$, then $\mathrm{X}=\mathrm{Y}=0$ Therefore, eq. (i) reduce to

$\begin{array}{l}

-y=\left(12 x^{2}-10 x^{4}\right)(-x) \\

\Rightarrow y=12 x^{3}-10 x^{5}

\end{array}$

Also, we have $y=4 x^{3}-2 x^{5}$

$\begin{array}{l}

\therefore 12 x^{3}-10 x^{5} \\

=4 x^{3}-2 x^{5} \\

\Rightarrow 8 x^{5}-8 x^{3}=0 \\

\Rightarrow x^{5}-x^{3}=0 \\

\Rightarrow x^{3}\left(x^{2}-1\right)=0 \\

\Rightarrow x=0, \pm 1

\end{array}$

When, $x=0$

$y=4(0)^{3}-2(0)^{5}=0$

When, $x=1$,

$y=4(1)^{3}-2(1)^{5}=2$

When, $x=-1$,

$y=4(-1)^{3}-2(-1)^{5}=-2$

Hence, the require points are $(0,0),(1,2)$ and $(-1,-2)$.

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The equation of the tangent to the curve $y=b e^{-x/a}$ at the point where it crosses the $y$ -axis is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{x}{a}-\frac{y}{b}=1$
- **(B)** $a x+b y=1$
- **(C)** $a x-b y=1$
- **(D)** $\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=1$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=1$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$y=b e^{-x / a}$ meets the $y$ -axis at $(0, b)$. Again,

$\frac{d y}{d x}=b e^{-x / a}\left(-\frac{1}{a}\right)$

$\operatorname{At}(0, b), \frac{d y}{d x}=b e^{0}\left(-\frac{1}{a}\right)=-\frac{b}{a}$

Therefore, required tangent is

$y-b=-\frac{b}{a}(x-0) \text { or } \frac{x}{a}+\frac{y}{b}=1$

</details>

### Q10. BITSAT 2015

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The slope of the tangent to the curve $y=e^{x}$ cos $x$ is minimum at $x=\alpha, 0 \leq a \leq 2 \pi$, then the value of $\alpha$ is

- **(A)** $\underline{0}$

- **(D)** $3 \pi 2$

Diff. w.r.t 'x'

$$

\begin{array}{r}

\Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{dm}}{\mathrm{dx}}=\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{x}}(\cos \mathrm{x}-\sin \mathrm{x})+\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{x}}(-\sin \mathrm{x}-\cos \mathrm{x}) \\

=-2 \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{x}} \sin \mathrm{x}

\begin{aligned}

\Rightarrow & y-y_{1}=-\sin x_{1}\left(0-x_{1}\right)

\end{aligned}

$$

Since, it is given that equation of tangent passes through origin.

$$

\begin{array}{lr}

\Rightarrow & y_{1}=-x_{1} \sin x_{1}

\end{array}

$$

Also, point $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right)$ lies on $y=\cos x$

$$

\therefore \quad y_{1}=\cos x_{1}

$$

From Eqs. (i), (ii), we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

& \sin ^{2} x_{1}+\cos ^{2} x_{1}=\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{x_{1}^{2}}+y_{1}^{2}=1 \\

\Rightarrow & x_{1}^{2}=y_{1}^{2}+y_{1}^{2} x_{1}^{2}

\end{aligned}

$$

Hence, the locus of $\left(x_{1}, y_{1}\right)$ is

$$

x^{2}=y^{2}+y^{2} x^{2} \Rightarrow x^{2} y^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}

$$
