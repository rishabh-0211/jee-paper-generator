- **(B)** $80 \mathrm{ft}$ and $120 \mathrm{ft}$
- **(C)** $35 \mathrm{ft}$ and $110 \mathrm{ft}$
- **(D)** $35 \mathrm{ft}$ and $120 \mathrm{ft}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $35 \mathrm{ft}$ and $110 \mathrm{ft}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/aibsGJb8SAnd0AlzAIOjNYoNfQTsHZOd1upqzr5sx14.original.fullsize.png">

Perimeter $=440 \mathrm{ft}$

$2 x+2 \pi r=440$
$$
\begin{aligned}
A & =\text { Area of the rectangular portion }=x 2 r \\
A & =x \frac{(440-2 x)}{\pi}=\frac{1}{\pi}\left(440 x-2 x^2\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
Let $\frac{d A}{d x}=\frac{1}{\pi}(440-4 x)=0$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow x=110 \text { for which } \frac{d^2 A}{d x^2} < 0 \\
& \Rightarrow A \text { is maximum when } x=110 \\
& \Rightarrow 2 r=\frac{440-2 x}{\pi}=\frac{440-220}{22 / 7}=70 \\
& \therefore \quad r=35 \mathrm{ft} \text { and } x=110 \mathrm{ft}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Q4. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let $x+y=3-\cos 4 \theta$ and $x-y=4 \sin 2 \theta$ then the greatest of $x y$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3}{4}$
- **(B)** 1
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(D)** 2

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) 1**

**Detailed Solution:**

$x=\frac{3-\cos 4 \theta+4 \sin 2 \theta}{2}$

$=\frac{3-\left(1-\sin ^{2} 2 \theta\right)+4 \sin 2 \theta}{2}=(1+\sin 2 \theta)^{2}$

$y=\frac{3-\cos 4 \theta-4 \sin 2 \theta}{2}$

$=\frac{3-\left(1-\sin ^{2} 2 \theta\right)+4 \sin 2 \theta}{2}=(1-\sin 2 \theta)^{2}$

$\therefore x y=\left(1-\sin ^{2} 2 \theta\right)^{2}=\cos ^{4} 2 \theta \leq 1$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2020

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Consider the function $f(x)=|x-1| /x^{2},$ then $f(x)$ is

**Options:**

- **(A)** increasing in (0,1)$\cup(2, \infty)$
- **(B)** increasing in $(-\infty, 0) \cup(1,2)$
- **(C)** decreasing in (0,1)$\cup(2, \infty)$
- **(D)** decreasing in (0,1)$\cup(2, \infty)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) decreasing in (0,1)$\cup(2, \infty)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$f(x)=\frac{|x-1|}{x^{2}}=\left\{\begin{array}{l}\frac{1-x}{x^{2}}, x<1, x \neq 0 \\ \frac{x-1}{x^{2}}, x>1\end{array}\right.$

Clearly, $f(x)$ is continuous for all $x \in R$ except at $x=0$

$f^{\prime}(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{l}

\frac{x-2}{x^{3}}, x<1, x \pm 0 \\

\frac{2-x}{x^{3}}, x>1

\end{array}\right.$

$f^{\prime}(x)>0 \Rightarrow x<0$ or $1<x<2$

$f^{\prime}(x)<0 \Rightarrow 0<x<1$ or $x>2$

Hence, $f(x)$ is increasing in $(-\infty, 0) \cup(1,2)$ and

decreasing in (0,1)$\cup(2, \infty)$.

</details>

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

If $a^{2} x^{4}+b^{2} y^{4}=c^{4}$, then the maximum value of xy is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{\mathrm{c}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{ab}}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{c^{2}}{2 \sqrt{a b}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\mathrm{c}}{2 \sqrt{\mathrm{ab}}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{c^{2}}{\sqrt{2 a b}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\frac{c^{2}}{\sqrt{2 a b}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

If the sum of two positive quantities is a constant, then their product is maximum, when they are equal. $\therefore \mathrm{a}^{2} \mathrm{x}^{4} \cdot \mathrm{b}^{2} \mathrm{y}^{2}$ is maximum when

$$

a^{2} x^{4}=b^{2} y^{4}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2} x^{4}=\frac{1}{2}\left(a^{2} x^{4}+b^{2} y^{4}\right)=\frac{c^{4}}{2}\right.

$$

$\therefore$ maximum value of $a^{2} x^{4} \cdot b^{2} y^{4}=\frac{c^{4}}{2} \cdot \frac{c^{4}}{2}=\frac{c^{8}}{4}$

Maximum value of $x y=\left(\frac{c^{8}}{4 a^{2} b^{2}}\right)^{1 / 4}=\frac{c^{2}}{\sqrt{2 a b}}$

</details>

### Q19. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The function \(\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{x})=2 \mathrm{x}^3-3 \mathrm{x}^2-12 \mathrm{x}+4\), has

**Options:**

- **(A)** two points of local maximum
- **(B)** two points of local minimum
- **(C)** one maxima and one minima
- **(D)** nomaxima or minima

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) one maxima and one minima**

**Detailed Solution:**

\(\begin{aligned}
& f(x)=2 x^3-3 x^2-12 x+4 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad f^{\prime}(x)=6 x^2-6 x-12=6\left(x^2-x-2\right) \\
& =6(x-2)(x+1)
\end{aligned}\)
For maxima and minima \(\mathrm{f}^{\prime}(\mathrm{x})=0\)
\(\therefore \quad 6(\mathrm{x}-2)(\mathrm{x}+1)=0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}=2,-1\)
Now, \(\mathrm{f}^{\prime \prime}(\mathrm{x})=12 \mathrm{x}-6\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& \text { At } \mathrm{x}=2 ; \mathrm{f}^{\prime \prime}(\mathrm{x})=24-6=18 > 0 \\
& \therefore \quad \mathrm{x}=2, \text { local min. point } \\
& \text { At } \mathrm{x}=-1 ; \mathrm{f}^{\prime \prime}(\mathrm{x})=12(-1)-6=-18  < 0 \\
& \therefore \mathrm{x}=-1 \text { local max. point }
\end{aligned}\)

</details>

### Q20. BITSAT 2011

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

The minimum value of \(f(x)=\sin ^4 x+\cos ^4 x\) in the interval \(\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\frac{1}{2}\)
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** \(\sqrt{2}\)
- **(D)** 1

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) \(\frac{1}{2}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let \(y=\sin ^4 x+\cos ^4 x\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& \frac{d y}{d x}=4 \sin ^3 x \cos x+4 \cos ^3 x(-\sin x) \\
& =4 \sin x \operatorname{cox}\left(\sin ^2 x-\cos ^2 x\right) \\
& =(2 \sin 2 x)(-\cos 2 x)=-\sin 4 x \\
& \therefore \frac{d y}{d x}=0 \Rightarrow \sin 4 x=0 \Rightarrow 4 x=0, \pi, 2 \pi, 3 \pi
\end{aligned}\)
or \(\mathrm{x}=0, \frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3 \pi}{4}, \ldots \ldots \ldots, \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}=\frac{\pi}{4}\)

</details>

### Q22. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

A person standing on the bank of a river observes that the angle subtended by a tree on the opposite bank is \(60^{\circ}\). when he retreats 20 feet from the bank, he finds the angle to be \(30^{\circ}\). The breadth of the river in feet is :

**Options:**

- **(A)** 15
- **(B)** \(15 \sqrt{3}\)
- **(C)** \(10 \sqrt{3}\)
- **(D)** 10

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) 10**

**Detailed Solution:**

et \(h\) be the height of tree PQ and breadth of river PS be \(x \mathrm{~ft}\). Angle of elevation subtended by a tree is \(60^{\circ}\). Also, when he retreats 20 feet, the angle becomes \(30^{\circ}\).
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/bitsat/jOllLNiEMLkHgFUugK12xCQ7Ec8Jb8rofS_ArBzIMR8.original.fullsize.png">

Also, in \(\triangle \mathrm{PQS}\),
\(\begin{aligned}
& \tan 60^{\circ}=\frac{h}{x} \\
& \Rightarrow h=\sqrt{3} x
\end{aligned}\)
and in \(\triangle \mathrm{PQR}\),
\(\begin{aligned}
& \tan 30^{\circ}=\frac{h}{x+20} \Rightarrow \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{h}{x+20} \\
& \Rightarrow x+20=\sqrt{3} h \\
& \Rightarrow x+20=3 x \Rightarrow 2 x=20 \Rightarrow x=10
\end{aligned}\)
Hence breadth of river is \(10 \mathrm{ft}\).

</details>
