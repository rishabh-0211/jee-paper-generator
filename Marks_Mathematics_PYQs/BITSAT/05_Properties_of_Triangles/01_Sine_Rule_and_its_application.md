### Q1. BITSAT 2022

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

If in a $\triangle A B C, 2 b^2=a^2+c^2$, then $\frac{\sin 3B}{\sin B}$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{c^2-a^2}{2 c a}$
- **(B)** $\frac{c^2-a^2}{c a}$
- **(C)** $\frac{\left(c^2-a^2\right)^2}{(c a)^2}$
- **(D)** $\left[\frac{c^2-a^2}{2 c a}\right]^2$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\left[\frac{c^2-a^2}{2 c a}\right]^2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{\sin 3 B}{\sin B}=\frac{3 \sin B-4 \sin ^3 B}{\sin B}$
$=3-4 \sin ^2 B$
$=3-4\left(1-\cos ^2 B\right)$
$=-1+\frac{4\left(a^2+c^2-b^2\right)^2}{4(a c)^2}$
$=-1+\frac{\left(\frac{a^2+c^2}{2}\right)^2}{(a c)^2}$
$=-1+\frac{\left(a^2+c^2\right)^2}{4(a c)^2}$
$=(\frac{c^2-a^2}{2 a c})^2$

</details>

### Q3. BITSAT 2016

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

In a $\triangle \mathrm{ABC},$ if $\frac{\cos A}{\mathrm{a}}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{B}}{\mathrm{b}}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{C}}{\mathrm{c}},$ and the side $\mathrm{a}=2,$ then area of the triangle is

**Options:**

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
- **(D)** $\sqrt{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $\sqrt{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{\cos A}{a}=\frac{\cos B}{b}=\frac{\cos C}{c}$

$\Rightarrow \frac{\cos \mathrm{A}}{2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{A}}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{B}}{2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{B}}=\frac{\cos \mathrm{C}}{2 \mathrm{R} \sin \mathrm{C}}$

$\Rightarrow \cot A=\cot B=\cot C$

$\Rightarrow \mathrm{A}=\mathrm{B}=\mathrm{C}=60^{\circ} \Rightarrow \Delta \mathrm{ABC}$ is equilateral

Hence, area of $\Delta=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \mathrm{a}^{2}=\sqrt{3}$.

</details>

### Q4. BITSAT 2012

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a triangle $\mathrm{ABC},$ if $\mathrm{a}=2, \mathrm{~B}=60^{\circ}$ and $\mathrm{C}=75^{\circ}$, then b equals

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\sqrt{3}$
- **(B)** $\sqrt{6}$
- **(C)** $\sqrt{9}$
- **(D)** $1+\sqrt{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\sqrt{6}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\mathrm{A}=180^{\circ}-60^{\circ}-75^{\circ}=180^{\circ}-135^{\circ}=45^{\circ}$
Now, $\frac{\mathrm{a}}{\sin \mathrm{A}}=\frac{\mathrm{b}}{\sin \mathrm{B}}$
$\Rightarrow \frac{2}{\sin 45^{\circ}}=\frac{\mathrm{b}}{\sin 60^{\circ}} \Rightarrow \mathrm{b}=\frac{2 \cdot(\sqrt{3} / 2)}{1 / \sqrt{2}}=\sqrt{6}$

</details>

### Q5. BITSAT 2009

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The random variable \(\mathrm{X}\) has the following probability distribution
\(\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline \mathrm{x} & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
\hline \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=\mathrm{x}) & \mathrm{k} & 3 \mathrm{k} & 5 \mathrm{k} & 2 \mathrm{k} & \mathrm{k} \\
\hline
\end{array}\)
Then the value of \(P(X \geq 2)\) is

**Options:**

- **(A)** \(\frac{1}{3}\)
- **(B)** \(\frac{2}{3}\)
- **(C)** \(\frac{3}{4}\)
- **(D)** \(\frac{1}{4}\)

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) \(\frac{2}{3}\)**

**Detailed Solution:**

Since, \(\Sigma \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{i}}(\mathrm{X}=\mathrm{x})=1\)
\(\begin{aligned}
& \therefore \mathrm{k}+3 \mathrm{k}+5 \mathrm{k}+2 \mathrm{k}+\mathrm{k}=1 \therefore 12 \mathrm{k}=1 \therefore \mathrm{k}=\frac{1}{12} \\
& \text { Now, } \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X} \geq 2)=\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=2)+\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=3)+\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X}=4) \\
& =5 \mathrm{k}+2 \mathrm{k}+\mathrm{k}=8 \mathrm{k}=8\left(\frac{1}{12}\right)=\frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}\)

</details>
