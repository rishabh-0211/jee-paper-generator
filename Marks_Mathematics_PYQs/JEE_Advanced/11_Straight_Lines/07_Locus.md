### Q4. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

The locus of the orthocentre of the triangle formed by the lines $(1+p) x-p y+p(1+p)=0$, $(1+q) x-q y+q(1+q)=0$ and $y=0$, where $p \neq q$, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** a hyperbola
- **(B)** a parabola
- **(C)** an ellipse
- **(D)** a straight line

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) a straight line**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, lines are
$$
(1+p) x-p y+p(1+p)=0
$$
and $(1+q) x-q y+q(1+q)=0$
On solving Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$$
C\{p q,(1+p)(1+q)\}
$$

$\therefore$ Equation of altitude $C M$ passing through $C$ and perpendicular to $A B$ is
$$
x=p q
$$
$\because$ Slope of line (ii) is $\left(\frac{1+q}{q}\right)$.
$\therefore$ Slope of altitude $B N$ (as shown in figure) is $\frac{-q}{1+q}$.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/KuYdPR9jmFax0y6klYaQcg1EstIIJR540LdVt85WdZk.original.fullsize.png">

$\therefore$ Equation of $B N$ is $y-0=\frac{-q}{1+q}(x+p)$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad y=\frac{-q}{(1+q)}(x+p)
$$
Let orthocentre of triangle be $H(h, k)$, which is the point of intersection of Eqs. (iii) and (iv)
On solving Eqs. (iii) and (iv), we get $x=p q$ and $y=-p q \Rightarrow h=p q$ and $k=-p q \Rightarrow h+k=0$
$\therefore$ Locus of $H(h, k)$ is $x+y=0$.
