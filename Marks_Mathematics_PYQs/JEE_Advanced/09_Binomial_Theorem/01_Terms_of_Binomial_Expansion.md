### Q1. JEE Advanced 2025 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `1`

Let $a_0, a_1, \ldots, a_{23}$ be real numbers such that
$\left(1+\frac{2}{5} x\right)^{23}=\sum_{i=0}^{23} a_i x^i$
for every real number $x$. let $a_r$ be the largest among the numbers $a_j$ for $0 \leq j \leq 23$.
The the value of $r$ is ______

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **6**

**Detailed Solution:**

For $\mathrm{x}=1$
$\left(1+\frac{2}{5}\right)^{23}=a_0+a_1+a_2+\ldots+a_{23}$
for numerically greatest term
$\frac{n+1}{1+\left|\frac{a}{b}\right|}=\frac{23+1}{1+\frac{5}{2}}=\frac{48}{7}$
$\Rightarrow\left[\frac{48}{7}\right]=6=\mathrm{m}$ (where [.] greatest integer function)
so, $\mathrm{T}_7$ is numerical greatest term
Hence $r=6$

</details>

### Q6. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

For $r=0,1, \ldots, 10$, let $A_r, B_r$ and $C_r$ denote, respectively, the coefficient of $x^r$ in the expansions of $(1+x)^{10}$, $(1+x)^{20}$ and $(1+x)^{30}$. Then $\sum_{r=1}^{10} A_r\left(B_{10} B_r-C_{10} A_r\right)$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $B_{10}-C_{10}$
- **(B)** $A_{10}\left(B_{10}^2-C_{10} A_{10}\right)$
- **(C)** 0
- **(D)** $C_{10}-B_{10}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $C_{10}-B_{10}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$A_r=$ Coefficient of $x^r$ in
$$
\begin{aligned}
& (1+x)^{10}={ }^{10} C_r \\
& B_r=\text { Coefficient of } x^r \text { in } \\
& (1+x)^{20}={ }^{20} C_r \\
& C_r=\text { Coefficient of } x^r \text { in } \\
& (1+x))^{30}={ }^{30} C_r \\
& \therefore \sum_{r=1}^{10} A_r\left(B_{10} B_r-C_{10} A_r\right) \\
& =\sum_{r=1}^{10} A_r B_{10} B_r-\sum_{r=1}^{10} A_r C_{10} A_r \\
& =\sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_r{ }^{20} C_{10}{ }^{20} C_r \\
& -\sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_r{ }^{30} C_{10}{ }^{10} C_r \\
& =\sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_{10-r}{ }^{20} C_{10}{ }^{20} C_r \\
& -\sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_{10-r}{ }^{30} C_{10}{ }^{10} C_r \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& ={ }^{20} C_{10} \sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_{10-r} \cdot{ }^{20} C_r \\
& \quad-{ }^{30} C_{10} \sum_{r=1}^{10}{ }^{10} C_{10-r}{ }^{10} C_r \\
& ={ }^{20} C_{10}\left({ }^{30} C_{10}-1\right)-{ }^{30} C_{10}\left({ }^{20} C_{10}-1\right) \\
& ={ }^{30} C_{10}-{ }^{20} C_{10}=C_{10}-B_{10}
\end{aligned}
$$
