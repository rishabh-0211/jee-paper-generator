$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow x^2+(2-y) x+(4-2 y)=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(2-y)^2-4(4-2 y) \geq 0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad y^2+4 y-12 \geq 0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad y \leq-6, y \geq 2 \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
$\therefore$ Minimum value of $y$ is 2 .

&
\end{aligned}
$$
