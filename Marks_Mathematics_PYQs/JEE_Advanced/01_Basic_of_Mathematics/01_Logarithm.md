### Q5. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $\left(x_0, y_0\right)$ be the solution of the following equations $(2 x)^{\log 2}=(3 y)^{\log 3}$, $3^{\log x}=2^{\log y}$, then $x_0$ is equal to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{6}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(D)** 6

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\frac{1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Taking log on both sides,
$$
\log 2 \cdot \log (2 x)=\log 3(\log 3 y)
$$


$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\Rightarrow & \log 2\{\log 2+\log x\} \\
& =\log 3\{\log 3+\log y\} \\
\text { and } & \log x \cdot \log 3=\log y \log 2 \\
& \log y=\frac{\log x \cdot \log 3}{\log 2}
\end{array}
$$
From Eqs. (i) and (ii), we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \log 2\{\log 2+\log x\} \\
&=\log 3 \cdot\left\{\log 3+\frac{\log x \cdot \log 3}{\log 2}\right\} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(\log 2)^2+\log 2 \cdot \log x \\
&=(\log 3)^2+\frac{(\log 3)^2}{(\log 2)} \cdot \log x \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \log x\left\{\frac{(\log 3)^2}{\log 2}-\log 2\right\} \\
&=(\log 2)^2-(\log 3)^2
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad \log x=-\log 2=\log 2^{-1} \\
& \therefore \quad x=\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
