\Rightarrow & a^2+b^2+c^2 \geq(a b+b c+c a) \\
\therefore & \frac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{a b+b c+c a} \geq 1
\end{array}

$$
\begin{array}{lrl}

\end{array}
