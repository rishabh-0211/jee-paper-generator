### Q4. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $P Q R$ be a triangle of area $\Delta$ with $a=2, b=\frac{7}{2}$ and $c=\frac{5}{2} ;$ where $a, b$, and $c$ are the lengths of the sides of the triangle opposite to the angles at $P, Q$ and $R$ respectively. Then $\frac{2 \sin P-\sin 2 P}{2 \sin P+\sin 2 P}$ equals.

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3}{4 \Delta}$
- **(B)** $\frac{45}{4 \Delta}$
- **(C)** $\left(\frac{3}{4 \Delta}\right)^{2}$
- **(D)** $\left(\frac{45}{4 \Lambda}\right)^{2}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\left(\frac{3}{4 \Delta}\right)^{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\frac{2 \sin P-\sin 2 P}{2 \sin P+\sin 2 P}=\frac{2 \sin P-2 \sin P \cos P}{2 \sin P+2 \sin P \cos P}=\frac{1-\cos P}{1-\sin P}$

$=\frac{2 \sin ^{2} \frac{P}{2}}{2 \cos ^{2} \frac{P}{2}}=\tan ^{2} \frac{P}{2}=\frac{(s-b)(s-c)}{s(s-a)}$ where $s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$

$=\frac{(s-b)^{2}(s-c)^{2}}{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}=\frac{(a+c-b)^{2}(a+b-c)^{2}}{16 . \Delta^{2}}$

$=\frac{\left(2+\frac{5}{2}-\frac{7}{2}\right)^{2}\left(2+\frac{7}{2}-\frac{5}{2}\right)^{2}}{16 \Delta^{2}}=\frac{1 \times 9}{16 \Delta^{2}}=\left(\frac{3}{4 \Delta}\right)^{2}$

### Q6. JEE Advanced 2009 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

In a $\triangle A B C$ with fixed base $B C$, the vertex $A$ moves such that $\cos B+\cos C=4 \sin ^2 \frac{A}{2}$. If $a, b$ and $c$ denote the lengths of the sides of the triangle opposite to the angles $A, B$ and $C$ respectively, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $b+c=4 a$
- **(B)** $b+c=2 a$
- **(C)** locus of point $A$ is an ellipse
- **(D)** locus of point $A$ is a pair of straight line

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) locus of point $A$ is an ellipse**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given, $\cos B+\cos C=4 \sin ^2 \frac{A}{2}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow 2 \cos \left(\frac{B+C}{2}\right) \cos \left(\frac{B-C}{2}\right)=4 \sin ^2 \frac{A}{2} \\
& \Rightarrow 2 \sin \frac{A}{2}\left[\cos \left(\frac{B-C}{2}\right)-2 \sin \frac{A}{2}\right]=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \cos \left(\frac{B-C}{2}\right)-2 \cos \left(\frac{B+C}{2}\right)=0 \\
& \Rightarrow-\cos \frac{B}{2} \cos \frac{C}{2}+3 \sin \frac{B}{2} \sin \frac{C}{2}=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \tan \frac{B}{2} \tan \frac{C}{2}=\frac{1}{3} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \sqrt{\frac{(s-a)(s-c)}{s(s-b)} \cdot \frac{(s-b)(s-a)}{s(s-c)}}=\frac{1}{3} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad \frac{s-a}{s}=\frac{1}{3} \Rightarrow 2 s=3 a \\
& \Rightarrow \quad b+c=2 a
\end{aligned}
$$

### Q8. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

Internal bisector of $\angle A$ of $\triangle A B C$ meets side $B C$ at $D$. A line drawn through $D$ perpendicular to $A D$ intersects the side $A C$ at $E$ and side $A B$ at $F$. If $a, b$ and $c$ represent sides of $\triangle A B C$, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $A E$ is $\mathrm{HM}$ of $b$ and $c$
- **(B)** $A D=\frac{2 b c}{b+c} \cos \frac{A}{2}$
- **(C)** $E F=\frac{4 b c}{b+c} \sin \frac{A}{2}$
- **(D)** the $\triangle A E F$ is isosceles

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) the $\triangle A E F$ is isosceles**

**Detailed Solution:**

We have, $\triangle A B C=\triangle A B D+\triangle A C D$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \frac{1}{2} b c \sin A=\frac{1}{2} c A D \sin \frac{A}{2}+\frac{1}{2} b A D \sin \frac{A}{2} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad A D=\frac{2 b c}{b+c} \cos \frac{A}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
Again, $\quad A E=A D \sec \frac{A}{2}=\frac{2 b c}{b+c}$
$\therefore A E$ is $\mathrm{HM}$ of $b$ and $c$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
E F & =E D+D F=2 D E=2 A D \tan \frac{A}{2} \\
& =2 \frac{2 b c}{b+c} \cos \frac{A}{2} \tan \frac{A}{2}=\frac{4 b c}{b+c} \sin \frac{A}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
As $A D \perp E F$ and $D E=D F$ and $A D$ is bisector.
$\Rightarrow \triangle A E F$ is isosceles.
