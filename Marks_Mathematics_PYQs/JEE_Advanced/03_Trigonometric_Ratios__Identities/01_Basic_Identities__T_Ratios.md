### Q2. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $f:(-1,1) \rightarrow I R$ be such that $f(\cos 4 \theta)=\frac{2}{2-\sec ^{2} \theta}$ for $\theta \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \cup\left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$. Then the value (s) of $f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)$ is (are)

**Options:**

- **(A)** $1-\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$
- **(B)** $1+\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$
- **(C)** $1-\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$
- **(D)** $1+\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $1+\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given : $f(\cos 4 \theta)=\frac{2}{2-\sec ^{2} \theta}=\frac{2 \cos ^{2} \theta}{2 \cos ^{2} \theta-1}$

$=\frac{1+\cos 2 \theta}{\cos 2 \theta}=1+\frac{1}{\cos 2 \theta}$

Let $\cos 4 \theta=\frac{1}{3} \Rightarrow 2 \cos ^{2} 2 \theta-1=\frac{1}{3} \Rightarrow \cos 2 \theta=\pm \sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}$

$\therefore f(\cos 4 \theta)=1+\frac{1}{\cos 2 \theta}=1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$ or $f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)=1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

Let $\theta \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ and $t_1=(\tan \theta)^{\tan \theta}, t_2=(\tan \theta)^{\cot \theta}$ and $t_4=(\cot \theta)^{\tan \theta}$, then

**Options:**

- **(A)** $t_1>t_2>t_3>t_4$
- **(B)** $t_4>t_3>t_1>t_2$
- **(C)** $t_3>t_1>t_2>t_4$
- **(D)** $t_2>t_3>t_1>t_4$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $t_4>t_3>t_1>t_2$**

**Detailed Solution:**

As when $\theta \in\left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$
$\tan \theta < \cot \theta$
Since, $\tan \theta < 1$ and $\cot \theta>1$
$\therefore(\tan \theta)^{\cot \theta} < 1$ and $(\cot \theta)^{\tan \theta}>1$
$\therefore t_4>t_1$ which only holds in option (b).

</details>
