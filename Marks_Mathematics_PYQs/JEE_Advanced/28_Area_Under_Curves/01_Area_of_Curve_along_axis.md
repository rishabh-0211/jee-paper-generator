### Q2. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let the straight line $x=b$ divide the area enclosed by $y=(1-x)^2, y=0$ and $x=0$ into two parts $R_1(0 \leq x \leq b)$ and $R_2(b \leq x \leq 1)$ such that $R_1-R_2=\frac{1}{4}$. Then, $b$ equals to

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\frac{3}{4}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **(C)** $\frac{1}{3}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{4}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $\frac{1}{2}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here, area between 0 to $b$ is $R_1$ and $b$ to
$$
\begin{aligned}
& 1 \text { is } R_2 \\
& \therefore \int_0^b(1-x)^2 d x-\int_b^1(1-x)^2 d x=\frac{1}{4} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad\left(\frac{(1-x)^3}{-3}\right)_0^b-\left(\frac{(1-x)^3}{-3}\right)_b^1=\frac{1}{4} \\
& \Rightarrow-\frac{1}{3}\left\{(1-b)^3-1\right\}+\frac{1}{3}\left\{0-(1-b)^3\right\} \\
& =\frac{1}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \Rightarrow \quad-\frac{2}{3}(1-b)^3=-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}=-\frac{1}{12} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(1-b)^3=\frac{1}{8} \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(1-b)=\frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow b=\frac{1}{2} \\
&
\end{aligned}
$$
