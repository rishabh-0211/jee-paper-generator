### Q2. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `1`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Let $A_1, G_1, H_1$ denote the arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means respectively, of two distinct positive numbers. For $n \geq 2$, let $A_{n-1}$ and $H_{n-1}$ has arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means as $A_n, G_n, H_n$, respectively.<strong>Question:</strong>
Which of the following statements is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\mathrm{H}_1>\mathrm{H}_2>\mathrm{H}_3>\ldots$
- **(B)** $H_1 < H_2 < H_3 < \ldots$
- **(C)** $H_1>H_3>H_5>\ldots$ and $H_2 < H_4 < H_6 < \ldots$
- **(D)** $\mathrm{H}_1 < \mathrm{H}_3 < \mathrm{H}_5 < \ldots$ and $\mathrm{H}_2>\mathrm{H}_4>\mathrm{H}_6>\ldots$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(B) $H_1 < H_2 < H_3 < \ldots$**

**Detailed Solution:**

As above $A_1>H_2>H_1, A_2>H_3>H_2$ $\therefore \quad H_1 < H_2 < H_3 < \ldots$

</details>

### Q3. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `3`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Let $A_1, G_1, H_1$ denote the arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means respectively, of two distinct positive numbers. For $n \geq 2$, let $A_{n-1}$ and $H_{n-1}$ has arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means as $A_n, G_n, H_n$, respectively.<strong>Question:</strong>
Which of the following statements is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $A_1>A_2>\ldots$
- **(B)** $A_1 < A_2 < A_3 < \ldots$
- **(C)** $A_1>A_3>A_5>\ldots$ and $A_2 < A_4 < A_6 < \ldots$
- **(D)** $A_1 < A_3 < A_5 < \ldots$ and $A_2>A_4>A_6>\ldots$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(A) $A_1>A_2>\ldots$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Let $f(x)=k e^x-x$
$\begin{aligned} & & f^{\prime}(x) & =k e^x-1=0 \\ & & x & =-\ln k \\ & \therefore & f^{\prime \prime}(x) & =k e^x \\ & \text { Hence, } & {\left[f^{\prime \prime}(x)\right]_{x=-\ln k} } & =1>0 \\ & & f(-\ln k) & =1+\ln k\end{aligned}$
For one root of given equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
1+\ln k & =0 \\
Hence,
k & =\frac{1}{e} .
\end{aligned}
$$
$A_2$ is $A M$ of $A_1$ and $H_1$ and $A_1>H_1$
$$
\Rightarrow \quad A_1>A_2>H_1
$$
$A_3$ is $\mathrm{AM}$ of $\mathrm{A}_2$ and $\mathrm{H}_2$ and $\mathrm{A}_2>\mathrm{H}_2$
$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\Rightarrow & A_2>A_3>H_2 \\
\therefore & A_1>A_2>A_3>\ldots
\end{array}
$$

</details>

### Q4. JEE Advanced 2007 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

<strong>Paragraph:</strong>
Let $A_1, G_1, H_1$ denote the arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means respectively, of two distinct positive numbers. For $n \geq 2$, let $A_{n-1}$ and $H_{n-1}$ has arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means as $A_n, G_n, H_n$, respectively.<strong>Question:</strong>
Which one of the following statements is correct?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $G_1>G_2>G_3>\ldots$
- **(B)** $G_1 < G_2 < G_3 < \ldots$
- **(C)** $G_1=G_2=G_3=\ldots$
- **(D)** $G_1 < G_3 < G_5 < \ldots$ and $G_2>G_4>G_6>\ldots$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $G_1=G_2=G_3=\ldots$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$A_1=\frac{a+b}{2}, G_1=\sqrt{a b}$ and $H_1=\frac{2 a b}{a+b}$ $\begin{aligned} A_n=\frac{A_{n-1}+H_{n-1}}{2}, G_n & =\sqrt{A_{n-1} H_{n-1}}, \\ H_n & =\frac{2 A_{n-1} H_{n-1}}{A_{n-1}+H_{n-1}}\end{aligned}$
Clearly, $\quad G_1=G_2=G_3=\ldots=\sqrt{a b}$.

</details>
