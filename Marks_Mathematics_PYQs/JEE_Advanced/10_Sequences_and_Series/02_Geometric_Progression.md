### Q4. JEE Advanced 2006

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

Let $A_n=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)-\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^2+\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^3+\ldots+(-1)^{n-1}\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^n$ and $B_n=1-A_n$. Find a least odd natural number $n_0$, so that $B_n>A_n, \forall n \geq n_0$.

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **7**

**Detailed Solution:**

$B_n=1-A_n>A_n \Rightarrow A_n < \frac{1}{2}$ Now, $A_n=\frac{\frac{3}{4}\left(1-\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)^n\right)}{1+\frac{3}{4}} < \frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)>-\frac{1}{6}$
Obviously, it is true for all even values of $n$. But, for
$$
\begin{aligned}
n & =1, & \frac{3}{4} & < \frac{1}{6} \\
n & =3, & \frac{27}{64} & < \frac{1}{6} \\
n & =5, & \frac{243}{1024} & >\frac{1}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
which is true for $n=7$.
Obviously, $n_0=7$

</details>
