### Q2. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

The minimum value of the sum of real numbers $a^{-5}, a^{-4}, 3 a^{-3}, 1, a^8$ and $a^{10}$ with $a>0$ is

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **8**

**Detailed Solution:**

Using $A M \geq G M$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{a^{-5}+a^{-4}+a^{-3}+a^{-3}+a^{-3}+1+a^8+a^{10}}{8} \\
& \geq\left(a^{-5} \cdot a^{-4} \cdot a^{-3} \cdot a^{-3} \cdot a^{-3} \cdot 1 \cdot a^8 \cdot a^{10}\right)^{\frac{1}{8}} \\
& \Rightarrow a^{-5}+a^{-4}+3 a^{-3}+1+a^8+a^{10} \geq 8 \cdot 1
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, minimum value is 8 .

</details>
