### Q1. JEE Advanced 2025 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $\mathbb{R}$ denote the set of all real numbers. Let $z_1=1+2 i$ and $z_2=3 i$ be two complex numbers, where $i=\sqrt{-1}$. 
Let $S=\left\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}:\left|x+i y-z_1\right|=2\left|x+i y-z_2\right|\right\}$.
Then which of the following statements is (are) TRUE?

**Options:**

- **(A)** $S$ is a circle with centre $\left(-\frac{1}{3}, \frac{10}{3}\right)$
- **(B)** $S$ is a circle with centre $\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{8}{3}\right)$
- **(C)** $S \text { is a circle with radius } \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$
- **(D)** $S \text { is a circle with radius } \frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $S \text { is a circle with radius } \frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

$\begin{aligned}
&\begin{aligned}
& |x+i y-1-2 i|=2|x+i y-3 i| \\
& \Rightarrow \quad(x-1)^2+(y-2)^2=4\left(x^2+(y-3)^2\right) \\
& \Rightarrow \quad 3 x^2+3 y^2+2 x-20 y+31=0 \\
& \Rightarrow \quad x^2+y^2+\frac{2 x}{3}-\frac{20 y}{3}+\frac{31}{3}=0
\end{aligned}\\
&\therefore \mathrm{S} \text { is a circle with centre }\left(-\frac{1}{3}, \frac{10}{3}\right) \text { and radius }=\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}+\frac{100}{9}-\frac{31}{3}}=\sqrt{\frac{8}{9}}=\frac{2 \sqrt{2}}{3}
\end{aligned}$

</details>
