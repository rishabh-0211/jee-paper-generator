### Q2. JEE Advanced 2011 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `3`

Consider the parabola $y^2=8 x$. Let $\Delta_1$ be the area of the triangle formed by the end points of its latusrectum and the point $P\left(\frac{1}{2}, 2\right)$ on the parabola and $\Delta_2$ be the area of the triangle formed by drawing tangents at $P$ and at the end points of the latusrectum. Then, $\frac{\Delta_1}{\Delta_2}$ is

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **2**

**Detailed Solution:**

As, we know area of triangle formed by three points on parabola is twice the area of triangle formed by corresponding tangents, i.e. area of $\triangle P Q R=2$ area of $\Delta T_1 T_2 T_3$.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/Tn_oNb9XptnCEq3OqpNXGsGE325gb-xi60MgtbaSZlI.original.fullsize.png">

$\therefore \quad \Delta_1=2 \Delta_2$ or $\frac{\Delta_1}{\Delta_2}=2$
