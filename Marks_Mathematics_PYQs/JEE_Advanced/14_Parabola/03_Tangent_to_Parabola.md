### Q1. JEE Advanced 2026 (Paper 2)

*Type:* `singleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $T$ be the tangent to the parabola $y^2 = 16x$ at the point $(64, 32)$. Let $L$ be the tangent to the same parabola at another point $(x_1, y_1)$ on the parabola. If $L$ and $T$ are perpendicular to each other, then the distance between the point $(x_1, y_1)$ and the focus of the parabola, is

**Options:**

- **(A)** $\dfrac{15}{4}$
- **(B)** $4$
- **(C)** $\dfrac{17}{4}$
- **(D)** $5$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(C) $\dfrac{17}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

The equation of the parabola is $y^2 = 16x$. Comparing with $y^2 = 4ax$, we get $a = 4$.

Let the parametric coordinates of the point $(64, 32)$ be $(at_1^2, 2at_1)$.

$2at_1 = 32 \Rightarrow 8t_1 = 32 \Rightarrow t_1 = 4$

The slope of the tangent $T$ at $t_1$ is $m_1 = \dfrac{1}{t_1} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

Let the tangent $L$ be at the point $(x_1, y_1)$ with parameter $t_2$. Its slope is $m_2 = \dfrac{1}{t_2}$.

Since $T$ and $L$ are perpendicular, $m_1 m_2 = -1$.

$\dfrac{1}{4} \times \dfrac{1}{t_2} = -1 \Rightarrow t_2 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$

The $x$-coordinate of the point $(x_1, y_1)$ is $x_1 = at_2^2 = 4\left(-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}$.

The distance of a point $(x_1, y_1)$ on the parabola from the focus is given by its focal distance $x_1 + a$.

Distance $= \dfrac{1}{4} + 4 = \dfrac{17}{4}$

Answer: $\dfrac{17}{4}$

</details>

The tangent $P T$ and the normal $P N$ to the parabola $y^2=4 a x$ at a point $P$ on

**Options:**

- **(A)** vertex is $\left(\frac{2 a}{3}, 0\right)$ it meet its axis at points $T$ and $N$,
- **(B)** directrix is $x=0$ respectively. The locus of the centroid of $\triangle P T N$ is a parabola whose
- **(C)** latusrectum is $\frac{2 a}{3}$
- **(D)** focus is $(a, 0)$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) focus is $(a, 0)$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Equation of tangent and normal at point $P\left(a t^2, 2 a t\right)$ is $t y=x=a t^2$ and $y=-t x+2 a t+a t^2$ Let centroid of $\triangle P T N$ is $R(h, k)$. $\therefore \quad h=\frac{a t^2+\left(-a t^2\right)+2 a+a t^2}{3}$ and $k=\frac{2 a t}{3}$

$$
\begin{array}{rlrl} 
& \Rightarrow & 3 h & =2 a+a \cdot\left(\frac{3 k}{2 a}\right)^2 \\
\Rightarrow & & 3 h & =2 a+\frac{9 k^2}{4 a} \\
\Rightarrow & 9 k^2 & =4 a(3 h-2 a)
\end{array}
$$
$\therefore$ Locus of centroid is
$$
y^2=\frac{4 a}{3}\left(x-\frac{2 a}{3}\right)
$$
$\therefore \operatorname{Vertex}\left(\frac{2 a}{3}, 0\right)$; directrix
$$
x-\frac{2 a}{3}=-\frac{a}{3} \Rightarrow x=\frac{a}{3}
$$
Latusrectum $=\frac{4 a}{3}$
$\therefore$ Focus $\left(\frac{a}{3}+\frac{2 a}{3}, 0\right)$ i.e. $(a, 0)$
