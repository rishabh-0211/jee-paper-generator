### Q4. JEE Advanced 2012 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `numerical` | *Level:* `2`

Let $S$ be the focus of the parabola $y^{2}=8 \mathrm{x}$ and let $P Q$ be the common chord of the circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x-4 y=0$ and the given parabola. The area of the triangle $P Q S$ is

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Value:** **4**

**Detailed Solution:**

Given parabola $y^{2}=8 x$

and circle $x^{2}+y^{2}-2 x-4 y=0$ pass through the origin

$\therefore$ One end of common chord PQ is origin. Say $\mathrm{P}(0,0)$

Let $\mathrm{Q}$ be the point $\left(2 t^{2}, 4 t\right)$, then it will satisfy the equation of circle.

$\therefore \quad 4 t^{4}+16 t^{2}-4 t^{2}-16 t=0$

$\Rightarrow t^{4}+3 t^{2}-4 t=0 \Rightarrow t\left(t^{3}+3 t-4\right)=0$

$\Rightarrow t(t-1)\left(t^{2}+t-4\right)=0 \Rightarrow t=0$ or 1

For $t=0$, we get point $P$, therefore $t=1$ gives point $Q$ as $(2,4)$.

We also observe here that $\mathrm{P}(0,0)$ and $\mathrm{Q}(2,4)$ are end points of diameter of the given circle and focus of the parabola is the point $S(2,0)$.

$\therefore \quad$ area $(\Delta \mathrm{PQS})=\frac{1}{2} \times P S \times Q S=\frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 4=4$

### Q5. JEE Advanced 2010 (Paper 1)

*Type:* `multipleCorrect` | *Level:* `2`

Let $A$ and $B$ be two distinct points on the parabola $y^2=4 x$. If the axis of the parabola touches a circle of radius $r$ having $A B$ as its diameter, then the slope of the line joining $A$ and $B$ can be

**Options:**

- **(A)** $-\frac{1}{r}$
- **(B)** $\frac{1}{r}$
- **(C)** $\frac{2}{r}$
- **(D)** $-\frac{2}{r}$

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer & Solution</b></summary>

**Correct Option:** **(D) $-\frac{2}{r}$**

**Detailed Solution:**

Here, coordinate $M=\left(\frac{t_1^2+t_2^2}{2}, t_1+t_2\right) i e$, mid point of chord $A B$.
<img src="https://cdn-question-pool.getmarks.app/pyq/jee_advanced/5OP5-giT0HF2WPAeV49UhRy4TxSM3EtXoEcLRC-CfFc.original.fullsize.png">

$M P=t_1+t_2=r$ Also, $\quad m_{A B}=\frac{2 t_2-2 t_1}{t_2^2-t_1^2}=\frac{2^{\ldots(i)}}{t_2+t_1}$ (when $A B$ is chord)
$$
\Rightarrow \quad m_{A B}=\frac{2}{r}
$$
[from Eq. (i)]
Also, $\quad m_{A^{\prime} B^{\prime}}=-\frac{2}{r}$ (when $A^{\prime} B^{\prime}$ is chord)
Hence, (c, d) is the correct option.

</details>
